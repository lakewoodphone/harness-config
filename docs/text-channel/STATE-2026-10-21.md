## 1. The queue that could never send (L1, L2, L3, L4)

**Measured 2026-10-21T00:00Z.** `owner_message_queue` held 13 rows; 12 carried
exactly one error:

```
held: urgency 'normal' is below the send threshold 'urgent'
```

The worst was `#2635` at 70 h and 20 attempts. `#2643` was the owner's card
payment (`...5578`, minimum 273.83, statement balance 4,692.89) and its due date
passed while it sat. The last successful owner send was 2026-10-19T09:00Z.

**Cause.** The send pass lived *inside* the autopilot reminder sweep, and its held
selection was `due_only=True`. A threshold refusal therefore wrote a six-hour
exponential backoff, and the class policy that should have ended a machine notice
was only evaluated when the row next came due. The rows were invisible most of the
time, and a failure anywhere in the reminder chain could silence the whole outbox.

**Fixed in the running tree (`/opt/company`):**

* `app/autopilot.py::_loop` now calls `_maybe_flush_owner_message_queue` on the
  loop's own cadence, outside the reminder sweep. The flush keeps its 60 s guard.
* `app/autopilot.py::_triage_owner_message_queue` reads **every** held row, due or
  not. It ends the classes that will never be a text (`urgent_email`,
  `sync_breaker:*`, `freshness_watchdog:*`, and anything `machine_facing_why`
  names) with a sentence about the message, and clears both the backoff *and* the
  spent attempt budget on obligations that were refused only by the threshold.
* `app/services/owner_notifications.py::owner_sms_allowed` does not apply the
  urgency threshold to `reminder:` — a reminder is an obligation the owner asked
  us to hold him to. The text gate, the one-at-a-time rule and the flood ceilings
  still run.
* `app/autopilot.py` logs `owner queue: flush sees N queued and M held due`, so the
  next silence is visible in the journal instead of inferred.

**Proof.** `#2643` sent at 2026-10-21T00:07:01Z, SID
`SM161bd384bd01927486d1eb0f16aec811`; the nine machine/mailbox rows are
`dismissed` with class-specific notes; the three duplicate health reminders were
resolved (one requeued, `#2658`, with its budget reset and the note that the 12
