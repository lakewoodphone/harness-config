# The owner text channel, audited and overhauled — 2026-10-19

**Status:** design + measured evidence. Every number here was read in the session that wrote
it; the command or query that produced it is named beside it. Nothing here is remembered.

The owner's brief, verbatim:

> *"so many things are so stupid, so many of them I don't need text it to me and so many times
> the CFO sends me a question that the answer is obvious or didn't do its due diligence and
> checking emails and different things before sending it to me. This thing really needs to be
> made way better and way smarter."*

|---|---|---|---|---|

| outbound | `sms_service.audit` | failed | 930 | refused, recorded as failure |
| inbound | `orchestrator` | received | 487 | **his** texts, all time |
| outbound | `orchestrator` | sent + delivered | 555 | the conversational agent's replies |
| outbound | `sms_service.audit` | delivered | 105 | the CFO / reminder engine, **delivered** |
| outbound | `family_chat.owner_leg` | delivered | 23 | the family chat, mirrored to his phone |
| outbound | `orchestrator` | failed | 114 | replies that never reached him |

**The trap in the raw table, and I walked into it and corrected it.** I first excluded
`sms_service.audit` as "duplicate audit rows" and concluded the system had sent him 81 texts in
30 days. It was the opposite: the relay tags its **delivered** texts with that same `agent_id`,
so that filter deleted the CFO's entire voice. The two halves of the channel are distinguished
by `status`, not by `agent_id`:

* `sms_service.audit` + `suppressed`/`failed` = a **refusal** (not delivered, and correctly so)
* `sms_service.audit` + `delivered`/`sent` = **the CFO and the reminder engine, on his phone**

### 1.6 So the four things that are actually still broken

1. **Due diligence is not in the path.** The owner's rule of 2026-10-16 — *"before you give me a
   charge, you have to exhaust all of the resources for exploring it"* — has a 708-line
   implementation that was never deployed and is called by nothing. Every ask goes out with
   whatever the asker happened to see first.
2. **Topic identity is recorded and never enforced.** `attempt.topic` and `ask.topic` exist as
   columns; `policy_hash` dedupes on the **body**. Four reminders for one payment have four
   bodies, so all four pass. Nothing anywhere refuses "we already told him about this".
3. **A reminder is never re-measured.** It fires if it is due, not if it is still true. Hence a
   deadline reminder after the deadline, and a balance alarm against a false reading.
4. **The conversational agent cannot see what it just asked him.** "I'm not sure what 'gas'
   refers to here — I don't have the question you're reacting to in front of me. Which one are
   you calling out?" (10-19 05:48), and eight days earlier "I need to know which charge you're
   looking at". It asked, he answered, and the answer had no question to attach to.

## 2. The overhaul

Four builds, ordered by value, each with a measurable acceptance test. Nothing here replaces
what §1.5 lists as live; every one of these is a missing layer.

### A. `owner_topic` — stop saying the same thing twice
A topic ledger in `/root/.owner-text/relay.db` (`owner_topic` table) with `admit(topic,
subsystem, body)` returning `ok | topic_cooldown | topic_closed`, and per-family windows
(reminder 20 h, ask 72 h, alert 1 h, status 6 h). Wired at the two places every producer
already passes through: `owner_text_relay.submit()` (askers) and the app's owner-send function
(reminders, notifications). **Acceptance:** the four Weinberg reminders replay against the
ledger and produce **1 send, 3 refusals**, with the refusal code on each.

### B. Deploy `preask` and make the receipt compulsory
Ship `preask.py` to `/root/bin`, call it from `cfo-ask.py`, `owner-ask.py` and
`card-due-watch.py` before composing, and have the relay refuse an `opens_ask=True` item whose
receipt is missing or whose verdict is `answered` (in which case the asker must record the
answer instead of asking). **Acceptance:** an ask with no receipt is refused by the relay, and
a charge the resolver can answer never reaches the phone.

### C. Re-measure before reminding
Before a reminder or an alert is sent, re-read the condition it names (task open? deadline
future? balance actually negative?). If it no longer holds, close it and stay silent.
**Acceptance:** the two stale reminders in §1.4 do not send when replayed.

### D. Give the conversational agent its own last outbound
The reply path must carry the last message *it* sent him, so "which charge?" and "what gas?"
cannot happen. **Acceptance:** a reply to a one-word answer resolves against the pending ask,
or says what it last asked.

### E. Make the evolution loop close, not just report
`owner-text-audit.py` already runs weekly and files a flag. It must also measure **outcome**
— of the texts that reached him, how many did he answer, how many did he complain about, how
many repeated — and the woken session must land one bounded change with a before/after number.
**Acceptance:** the next audit run shows the metric it targeted moving, and the change is in
git.

## 3. Why this is the right shape

Every defect in §1.3 and §1.4 is a **per-message** judgement being asked to do a **per-topic**
job. The gate asks "is this body worth reading?" — which it now does well — and never asks "did
we already say this?", "is it still true?", or "did we do the work before asking?". Those three
questions are what the owner is actually complaining about, and none of them existed in code.

The one rule this document exists to enforce, in the estate's own words (P4, *the improvement
loop does not close*): **a gate that is written and not wired is not a gate.** `preask.py` is
the proof — 708 lines, tested, mirrored, hash-manifested, and unreachable from the phone.

Both questions he named (`4144` "should I record the Polkadot $299…" and the Newegg one that

**Honest limits.** Two of five rungs are inert (`docs` has no root, `web` has no command) and
the ladder was not exercised end-to-end through `cfo-ask.py --why` today, because no unasked
merchant was above the $25 floor. Reminder staleness (§C) and the conversational agent's
own-outbound blindness (§D) are **not** implemented.

### The number to move

`owner-text-scorecard.py --days 30` → **52% of his texts are ignored or complained about.**
Seven days → 40%. The worst families are the briefings he banned in March (4/4, 2/2 dead) and
one payment reminder (2/2). Any future change to this channel should be judged by whether this
number falls, not by whether a new rule exists.
