**L1641 · An aggregate age printed beside a subset count is a wrong number**

**An aggregate age printed beside a subset count is a wrong number.**

Measured 2026-09-15, minutes after writing it. I added freshness to `check_attention_debt` and the
badge summary came back:

 owner-attention debt: 2 critical message(s) held, newest 0.1d old; 11 urgent message(s) held

`0.1d` was the newest held row of **any** urgency -- the evening briefing, observed 3.4 h earlier --
beside a count of *critical* rows that were 140.6 h (5.9 d) old. The sentence is false in the way
that matters: it tells the owner that the critical alerts are three hours old when they are six
days old. It was caught by reading the output rather than trusting the code that produced it, which
is the only reason it is not on his badge right now.

Fix: ages are tracked per urgency (`newest_critical_age_hours` beside `newest_held_age_hours`) and
the critical line uses the critical one.

Rule: whenever a line pairs a number with a qualifier, both must come from the same subset. Compute
the qualifier *inside* the category you are counting -- an aggregate beside a subset is not an
approximation, it is a different claim. This is the same family as the three hand-typed timestamp
filters and the "nobody verified at the decision point" failures already in this journal: the code
was right about what it computed and wrong about what it said.
