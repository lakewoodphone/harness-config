**D188 · 2026-09-15 · A stale owner-question mirror is refreshed at the reader, and its age is printed.**

**Decision: a stale mirror is refreshed at the reader, and its age is printed.**

`journal.py status` now calls `_maybe_refresh_questions()`: when `state/owner-questions.md` is older
than 60 minutes it is regenerated by running this tool's own `questions` command (one bounded
subprocess, 45 s timeout, and it keeps the existing file when the authority is unreachable), and the
page prints the mirror's age in minutes beside the count. It was deliberately **not** wired into
`scripts/autosync.sh`, which never commits and reports a dirty tree -- writing state from there
would manufacture permanent dirt on every machine. Measured: the first stale read cost 1,462 ms and
reported 12 pending (matching the authority); subsequent reads are fast against a fresh mirror.
