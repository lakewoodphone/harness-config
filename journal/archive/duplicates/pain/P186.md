## P186 — Reminders are marked sent into a dashboard nothing has been shown to render

**P-new — reminders are marked `sent` into a "dashboard" nothing has been shown to render.**

Measured 2026-09-15: `reminders` holds 396 rows, 14 pending, 0 due (so the schedule itself is
fine), and the recent ones carry `status='sent'` with `delivery_channel='dashboard'`.
`personal_ops.process_due_reminders` marks a dashboard reminder delivered and sends nothing --
by design, and safely, since only `sms`/`both` reaches `send_owner_update` (which is subject to the
owner-SMS kill switch). But if nothing renders that surface, then "sent" is a status the system
awards itself for work no one received, which is the same shape as the owner-message queue before
its shelf life and the same shape as the "delivery flowing" reading built on two escaped SMS.

Fix: find the reader (an API route, a page, the badge) or make "delivered to dashboard" mean
something the owner can open. Until one of those is true, the reminder feature reports success for
reaching nobody, and the 14 pending rows will look delivered too.

Removed from P144 (which bundled four faults) and given its own entry; the writer half of P144 is
P183, and the other two halves are fixed and applied (`9943817e`, `b822350`).
