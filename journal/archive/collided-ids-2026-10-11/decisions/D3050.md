**D3050 · 2026-10-11 · Bin photos belong in a catalog that feeds parts_inventory, not in a second inventory store.**

DECISION. The shop-bin photo work keeps its EVIDENCE in lpt-hub/tools/parts-catalog (append-only JSONL + the photo hash ledger) and writes its STOCK into personal-secretary-mvp's existing parts_inventory table. It does not create a new inventory table, and it does not put photos into the parts table.

WHY. parts_inventory already existed with 13 columns, four REST routes at /api/v1/shop/parts and a write-provenance convention (x-ps-source-machine). It held ZERO rows - created lazily and never used. A second store would be the estate's characteristic failure: the same information in two places, neither trusted. Recon also found the website's Prisma InventoryItem/Stock (richer, has a real location column) deliberately unwired, and the one ledger that IS full - purchases, 3,842 rows - is sourcing, not stock.

THE SPLIT IS SEMANTIC, NOT TECHNICAL. The catalog's unit is a photographed BAG (with label text, confidence, evidence, photo name). parts_inventory's unit is a PART TYPE (with quantity, min, supplier, cost). They are not the same thing and the bridge refuses to pretend they are: it will not export an unidentified or low-confidence item, so a guess cannot be laundered into the shop's stock.

ALSO DECIDED, SAME REASONING:
- No shelf/bin vocabulary is asserted. lpt-hub/tools/intake/README.md:162-167 explicitly defers one until real photos show the shop's own words; the catalog keeps his label text verbatim for that promotion instead.
- No prices. There is no parts-pricing convention in the estate (P2854 and P253 are open on exactly this), so every exported row carries unit_cost 0.0 rather than an invented number.
- min_quantity exported as 0: the route flags quantity <= min_quantity, and min_quantity=1 made 11 of 12 parts report as low stock. A photograph cannot know a reorder point.

EVIDENCE: parts_inventory live schema + count 0 (ssh secratary, 2026-10-11); commit fce467b91; live read-back total 12 after export.
