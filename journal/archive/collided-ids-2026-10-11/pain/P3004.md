## P3004 — The daily Shabbat reprogram failed silently on 3 of 7 mornings, with a 24h retry - power could fail to return after a Friday miss

**P — the daily reprogram was failing silently on transient plug timeouts, and
the only retry was 24 hours later. FIXED 2026-10-09.**

## The hurt, from the machine's own log

`journalctl -u secretary-api.service | grep 'Shabbat/Yom Tov schedule'`:

```
Oct 03 10:00 ERROR NOT programmed: ConnectTimeout to 192.168.50.103
Oct 04 10:00 ERROR NOT programmed: ConnectTimeout
Oct 05 10:00 INFO programmed
Oct 06 10:00 ERROR NOT programmed: ConnectTimeout
Oct 08 10:00 INFO programmed
Oct 09 10:00 INFO programmed
```

**Three failures in seven mornings.** The job was a single daily cron, so each
failure meant the next attempt was 24 hours away, and `program_week` returned
`plug unreachable` on the FIRST failed connect with no retry inside the run
either.

## Why that is dangerous rather than untidy

This job writes the **ON** schedule onto the plug — the schedule that brings
power back on motzei. After a cut, HA and every camera are dark, and the device
schedule is the only thing that restores them (that is the whole reason a
device schedule exists: it survives the secretary being down).

So a missed reprogram on a **Friday** would leave the coming Shabbat with no ON
schedule on the device, and power would not come back at all. The owner would
find a dark building and dead cameras, with nothing having alerted anyone —
because a failed reprogram logged an ERROR into journald and nothing read it.

The plug is a small Wi-Fi device that occasionally misses a 3-second connect.
That is a normal, transient condition; treating it as a daily hard failure was
the defect.

## The fix (commit c487f123, deployed)

1. `program_week` retries the connection 4 times with a 5 s gap.
2. The scheduler job became a **15-minute interval with a window guard** —
 `_within_shabbat_program_window`, open for `shabbat_program_window_hours`
 (default 3) from `shabbat_program_time` — instead of a one-shot cron. Inside
 the window it is idempotent, so the first success of the day takes effect.

## What would have caught this sooner

Nothing was watching. The check that finds it is one command:
`journalctl -u secretary-api.service | grep 'NOT programmed'`. That ought to be
part of any health surface for this automation, because the failure is silent by
construction — the automation still "works" for weeks while drifting toward the
day it does not.

## Residual

The window means it now retries over 3 hours rather than at one instant, which
converts a transient miss into a success. It cannot fix a plug that is genuinely
offline for the whole window; that would still leave the device schedule
un-extended, and nothing currently distinguishes "transient" from "the plug has
been gone for three hours" to an observer. Worth a future look if it recurs.
