**L3442 · the Print Label button: 54 passing tests, zero labels - a green suite proves the client, never the other end**

The Phone & Tech "Print Label" button composes a correct label, is covered by 54 passing tests, and
has never printed a label. Measured 2026-10-08.

Chain: `frontend/.../WorkOrderDetails.tsx` -> tRPC -> `backend/src/services/print.service.ts` ->
HTTP POST to `PRINT_SERVICE_URL` -> `http://localhost:8080`
(`docs/operations/backend-env-production.md:49`). On a Heroku dyno `localhost:8080` is the dyno
itself, where nothing listens. The intended target was the Home Assistant add-on in
`ha-config/addons/local/phonetech-printer`, which is also not listening (`192.168.50.34:8080`
refuses the connection).

And that add-on could not have worked on this hardware even if it were up: it provisions CUPS with
`lpadmin -m everywhere` (driverless IPP Everywhere), and the QL-820NWB does not answer IPP - port
631 completes a TCP handshake and then never replies. It also composes ESC/POS, which this printer
does not speak; it speaks Brother's own raster language.

What works, measured the same day: Brother raster over TCP 9100. Sent that way the printer went
READY -> PRINTING and labels came out.

The general lesson, which is the reason this is a lesson and not a bug report: a green test suite
around a client proves the client, never the thing on the other end. Two of the three links here
(URL and protocol) were untestable by unit test and were both wrong.
