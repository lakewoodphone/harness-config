- `app/services/shabbat_safety.py` — `check_persistent_unlock` now DELEGATES to `home_assistant.hold_interior_door_open` (disable the re-locking automations → release → watch → prove) instead of releasing the relay with the owner's guard rail still armed.  - **A defect in the Friday fix, caught before it bit:** restoring the automations does NOT re-lock the door — an HA automation only fires on its trigger. The Friday probe would have left the interior door OPEN for anyone who ran it without following it with a cut. The relock is now explicit and reported as `relocked`.
- `app/services/shabbat_orchestrator.py` — **the device OFF schedule is now gated** behind new `shabbat_device_off_schedule` (default **False**). A device schedule is UNGATED: it fires at candle-lighting whether or not anything held the door. So an armed device OFF turns "the door was not holdable, so we safely did nothing" into "the plug cut power with the door locked" — the 2026-09-11 lockout. The ON schedule is still written, because restoring power cannot lock anyone out. OFF is now software-only, gated on the proven hold; ON is both.
 - `_power_up_critical` no longer sleeps a fixed 90 s and makes one unverified attempt. It waits for HA to ANSWER (bounded by attempts, 18 × 20 s), then relocks, restores the guard rails, and **reads the relay back** — `set_interior_maglock` returns `ok` on a successful `mqtt.publish` whether or not the relay actuated.
- `app/agent_bus.py` — the daily program cycle now logs its outcome either way. A dormant automation that says nothing is indistinguishable from a working one (P334).
- `app/config.py`, `.env.example` — the new setting, documented.
