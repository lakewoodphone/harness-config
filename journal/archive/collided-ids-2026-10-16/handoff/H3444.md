**1. The lockout is fixed, its cause is known, and it cannot recur silently.**
`/root` was mode **777**; with `StrictModes` on, sshd refuses EVERY authentication - key and password -
from a world-writable home directory, which is why restoring `authorized_keys` changed nothing and why
three source addresses were refused identically. The box's own `auth.log` said so:
`Authentication refused: bad ownership or modes for directory /root`. Reached through the provider's
rescue system (mounted, fixed, clean unmount, rebooted); `/root` is 700 now and there are TWO keys -
the estate's and `authority-recovery-2026-10-11`, held only on ZABZ-YOGA. Fail2ban's `ignoreip` now
carries the estate's own /24s, derived from addresses that actually authenticated.

**3. Every ported systemd unit runs, and two silent defects were found doing it.**

now `ExecMainStatus=0`. The dead-man switch's unit had been FAILING (`203/EXEC`, `Permission denied`)

failed run (a stale marker read as "in flight"); a marker older than 8 h is now a FAILURE.
