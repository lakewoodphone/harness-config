### The two things that actually changed the estate

1. **The estate could not read the owner's Gmail, and everyone assumed it could not, and that
 assumption is what produced the false alarm in the first place.** It can:
 `/root/bin/gmail-search.py` (now `scripts/tax/gmail-search.py` in the repo) reads all five
 authenticated accounts read-only. It **must** be run with `cd /home/zabz/personal-secretary-mvp`
 first, or the relative token paths resolve wrong and it reports **zero accounts**, which reads
 exactly like "this person has no mail". That failure mode is itself worth remembering.
2. **A calendar cannot answer "has this been filed?"** The estate had a tool that read a date and
 wrote a conclusion. Two of its rows (five questions) were wrong; four of five were false or moot.
 Fixed by `scripts/tax/filing-register.json` + `filing_register.py`, with three verdicts that are
 not interchangeable — `FILED` / `UNRECORDED` / `N/A` — where **UNRECORDED means UNKNOWN and says
 so, because absence of a record is not evidence of absence**. Both tax tools now read it;
 `tax-deadlines.py` filters proven-filed deadlines out of "DUE NOW" and stamps the portal capture
 with its age; the queue feeder no longer asks the owner anything the register can answer, no
 longer uses `critical` for an unverified date, and writes open questions to
 `/root/.cfo/tax-unverified.json` for an agent to settle first.
 `/root/bin/tax-deadlines.py` and `tax-deadlines-to-queue.py` are now **symlinks into
 `/opt/company/scripts/tax/`**, so the cron entry points are unchanged and git owns the code.

### Bugs I made and caught in my own fix, worth not repeating

- **Settle before you group.** The feeder groups deadlines into one question per form per year,
 keyed on the EARLIEST deadline. I first put the register check *after* grouping, so a group whose
 first member was proven filed was dropped whole — which silently swallowed the genuinely open
 2026-Q3 ST-50 behind four already-filed quarters. Judge each period on its own, then group.
- **`render()` must filter on every settled status, not just `FILED`.** The digest kept printing
 seven "DUE NOW" lines for obligations already determined not to be owed.
