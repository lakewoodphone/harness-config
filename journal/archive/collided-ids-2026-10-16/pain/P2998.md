## P2998 — The owner text line was dead because three modules were never ported at the cloud cutover

Measured 2026-10-13. This is the root cause of the owner's complaint that the texting system
"needs to be ordered and I'm fixed up a lot".

WHAT THE OWNER SAW. His own words, inbound, live while a session was working, 2026-10-13T02:08:34Z:
"That was a confusing and unclear text that had too many things in it. Talk to me like a normal
person and asked me things one at a time." And at 02:12:39Z, an answer nobody consumed:
"Yes, that was a loan from me to the business, and all of it was payment for him with part being
actual payment and part being a bonus." Eleven inbound texts between 2026-10-11 and 2026-10-13 had
no decision. The queue raised them back to HIM as questions (rows #452-#454, "texted your AI line
and I have not answered").

THE MECHANISM, in three layers, each measured.

1. `sms-responder.py` is scheduled NOWHERE: not crontab, not /etc/cron.d, not a systemd unit.
 Owner-queue row #455 (high) already said so and was never actioned.

2. The responder loads its decision modules from `HERE = Path(__file__).resolve().parent`, i.e.
 `/root/bin`. It ran with `modules=store=ok ctx=MISSING decide=MISSING work=MISSING send=ok`.
 With no decider, `safe_decide4` returns NOISE for everything and every text is escalated - so the
 owner's ANSWER was filed back to him as a QUESTION. That is literally the loop he described.

3. `harness-config/scripts/deployed/bin/` is the capture of what ran on the OFFICE box, and its own
 MANIFEST.json lists `textdecide.py` under `exclusions` - "Owned by other workers; capturing them
 would freeze a half-edited state." So `textdecide.py` was never captured, and `textctx.py` /
 `textwork.py` were captured but never copied to the cloud. The cloud's `/root/bin` got the
 responder WITHOUT the three modules it depends on.

THE PART THAT MAKES IT A TRAP. The `textdecide.py` that IS in harness-config (36,311 bytes) is an
older, smaller file that exports `choose`, `classify_text`, `validate_reply` - and **no `decide4`**.
`safe_decide4` checks `hasattr(decide_mod, "decide4")`, so deploying that file makes the module
"present" and still yields NOISE for every message, with the same reason string
`(textdecide unavailable)`. A file that loads and a capability that exists are different claims.

THE FIX, verified. The real files only existed on the frozen office anchor `secratary`
(read-only, never written to): `textdecide.py` 77,165 B with `def decide4` at line 1317,
`textctx.py` 61,572 B, `textwork.py` 41,803 B. Copied to `/root/bin`: then
`modules=store=ok ctx=ok decide=ok work=ok send=ok`, and the reasons changed from
"textdecide unavailable" to real reasoning ("neither a question nor a request: a statement").
Answers are still not correlated to the CFO's questions because the clarification record only
starts existing now - but the pipe is open.

WHAT WOULD FINISH IT: (a) schedule the responder, which is an OUTBOUND behaviour change and is
already owner-queue row #455; (b) a drift test that compares `/root/bin` against the captured set
AND asserts required capabilities, not just file presence. The estate has
`scripts/assert-bin-matches-repo.py`; this incident is the argument for running it.
