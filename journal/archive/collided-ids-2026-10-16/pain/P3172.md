# The alert firehose is not mediated: 51 owner texts in 30 days come from two READ-ONLY hosts the relay cannot see

Found 2026-10-14 while building the owner-text relay. `company-heartbeat.sh` (sha `e37fa87c…`, 54259 B,
2026-10-09) lives on **both** `waze-mdm-01` and `lpt-apps-01` and posts straight to Twilio with
`curl -X POST -u "$TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID:$TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN" .../Messages.json` (live lines 323-328). It
never touches `textsend.py`, `owner_text_turn`, the app gate, or `owner_flood_guard.py`, and writes no DB
row — **the only trace is Twilio itself**. That is why a relay built on the authority sees none of it.

**Measured:** 51 alert texts of 502 owner-bound in 30 days = **10.16 %**, and on 2026-10-06 **37 of 40 =
92.5 %** of everything he received that day, including **17 copies of one "COMPANY IS BACK"** for a single
state transition. Cause, in the deployed file: three cron lines (`*/2`, `*/15`, `*/15`) fire into one
unlocked `STATE_DIR`; `.last-alert-$key` is written **after** the POST (line 329) and `.alert-budget`
**after** success (line 331), so concurrent runs read stale state and send; and the two hosts keep
independent state so neither ceiling constrains the other. The `peer|peer-*)` case is exempt from the
single-sender rule (lines 278-290).

**Still on the wire today:** the literal `ZZ` in `...stopped answering at 2026-10-09T02:04:01ZZ` (format
string at line 762 appends `Z` to `down_since`, which already ends in `Z`; exactly two Z's in all 51
stored rows).

**Why this is open rather than fixed:** `settings/machines/host-posture.tsv` designates `waze-mdm-01` and
`lpt-apps-01` **read-only** ("Waze MDM production. Not the company's box to reconfigure." / "Fleet: Caddy
front door … Do NOT add load"). Editing a file there needs the owner's confirmation. So the honest state
is: the loudest noise class in his transcript is the one the relay most needs to own, and it is the one I
am not permitted to touch without him.

**What would fix it:** one `owner-text submit` call in place of the `curl`, plus a `flock` around the mark,
plus the `ZZ` fix. A one-question ask is parked in the owner decision queue.
