## P3151 — The reconciliation auto-sets READY_FOR_PICKUP with no evidence, and 422 orders carry that status

WHAT HURTS: a robot sets READY_FOR_PICKUP and nothing can tell it from a real one. MEASURED 2026-10-12: order WO26091712718 (Greenfeld Dell) was READY_FOR_PICKUP from 2026-10-01 until I corrected it, set by order_status_history id 2608 with reason "Auto-updated by secretary reconciliation (confidence: 0.9)" - on a machine that had not been repaired, had no part ordered and had no payment row at all (payment_transactions for order 2883: zero rows). The customer had never been told it was ready. Meanwhile the store carries 422 READY_FOR_PICKUP rows, so a staff member scanning the board cannot use readiness as a signal.

WHY IT MATTERS: the failure mode is handing a customer a broken machine or promising a pickup, and the record that would prevent it is the one lying.

WHAT WOULD FIX IT: make the reconciliation refuse to write READY_FOR_PICKUP at all - it is a customer-facing promise, not a derived state - or gate it on evidence (a completion note, a status change made by a human, a payment). Failing that, have audit.py's C07 also read the *setter* of the status and count robot-set READY_FOR_PICKUP rows as their own class, which is cheap and would size the problem: I measured one by hand, not the population. Related: Shlesinger X201 recorded the same class on 2026-10-12 (commit f150742bd) and could not trace a caller either.
