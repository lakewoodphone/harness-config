## P3152 — 191 of 413 sync records cannot validate against their own schema because the root is closed

WHAT HURTS: 191 of 413 sync records carry keys at the JSON ROOT that CUSTOMER_CASE_SYNC_RECORD.schema.json forbids, because that schema sets additionalProperties:false at the root. MEASURED 2026-10-12 with jsonschema 4.25.1 over docs/customer-operations/sync-records/*.json. The common offenders are legacy (98), issue (86), status (86), notes (39), date (37), case_file (33), updated_at (31), supersededBy/supersededReason (5) - a record carrying two of the last is INVALID, measured on surface-laptop-4-1950-greenfeld.json.

NOT MINE AND NOT NEW: the same document already opened `references`, `pricing` and `labEscalation` for exactly this reason (its own comments cite 90 of 397 records carrying ad-hoc `references` keys as of 2026-09-29). The root was left closed.

WHAT WOULD FIX IT: the same decision at the root, or a migration that moves the ad-hoc keys into documented blocks - either way it is a 191-file change and a governance call, not a case fix. audit.py's C12 reports ERROR=0 despite this, so it does not currently validate the root; do not read C12 as "all records conform".
