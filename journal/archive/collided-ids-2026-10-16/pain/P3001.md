## P3001 — Twenty-one account-months still do not reconcile, and they fall into two diagnosable classes

Measured 2026-10-13 with `/usr/local/bin/finance-reconcile.sh`. Recorded because the analysis is
done and the fix is not, so a future session starts from the split rather than from zero.

CLASS A - SMALL TIMING DELTAS, probably not missing money. These are 0.03 to 435.38:
 lpt-boa-checking 2026-10 10.30
 lpt-chase-3929 2026-09 5.04
 lpt-chase-3929 2026-10 78.00
 lpt-chase-7542 2026-09 435.38
 personal-chase-9650 2026-10 3.00
 personal-amex-hysa-3181 2026-09 0.03
Each is small enough to be a transaction posted on the 1st landing in the wrong month, or one
small row missing. Class A wants a per-month row-by-row diff, not a hunt.

CLASS B - A BALANCE READING OF EXACTLY ZERO POISONING THE CHAIN. The clearest:
 personal-amex-22005 2026-08 delta -1,308.46
 "chain from 497490 on 2026-07-01 [manual] plus -366644 of transactions gives 130846, but the
 reading on 2026-08-31 [plaid-api] says 0"
and then 2026-09 inherits it: "chain from 0 on 2026-08-31 plus 58908 of transactions gives 58908,
but the reading on 2026-09-30 says 16192".

WHY I DID NOT SHIP A "ZERO IS SUSPECT" RULE. It is wrong for a third of the accounts that have one.
Measured: `personal-amex-93003` holds 110 zero readings against 2 non-zero (the card is dormant, and
zero is its true state), and `personal-chase-4258` holds 84 zero against 24 non-zero. A blanket rule
would discard the real balance for a dormant account - exactly the failure mode `cfo-balances.py`'s
own R1/R2/R3 rules are written to avoid ("never silently used"). The correct rule is narrower and
needs its own test: a zero reading is suspect only when it is a DIP - non-zero readings from the
same source exist both before and after it within a short window. Accounts where zero is the
surrounding state keep it.

ALSO WORTH KNOWING BEFORE CHASING CLASS B: the 2026-07-01 `manual` anchor that several chains start
from is itself qualified. `cfo-balances.py` prints its own warning on it - "this reading is one of
11 rows written by manual at 2026-07-01T19:00+00:00, of which 7 are demonstrably defective; the
value that survived is not by that fact proven good" - and `personal-amex-94045` carries that same
reading TWICE, at +277,727 and -277,727, twenty-four minutes apart. Any chain anchored there starts
from a number the tool cannot vouch for.
