THE ONE ROW THAT SURVIVES EVERY AUDIT CHECK is the PNY Duo Link V3 1 TB at 129.99: verified Amazon.com buy box, same-day price, and a published measured post-cache floor. It is also the row the other research streams barely mentioned.

CORRECTIONS THAT MATTER MORE THAN THE PICKS (do not reuse the old figures):
 MX500 1 TB "452 MB/s" does not exist (source says 400-420); MX500 dropped entirely.
 870 EVO "513 MB/s" -> 458; and its listing DOES carry an Amazon.com offer at 331.92 (an earlier pass said it failed the seller constraint - that was incomplete).
 BAR Plus / Type-C 256 GB "115 MB/s MEASURED sustained" was a 1 GB BURST, not sustained.
 Silicon Power A55 2 TB "438 MB/s" is cache-resident, not a floor.
 PM893 372.5 MB/s is the 240 GB part; the 960 GB SKU has no measurement.
 SanDisk Extreme PRO Portable V2 2 TB "1541 MB/s post-cache" is a 300 GB AVERAGE, and WD names that exact SKU in its May 2023 hardware-defect advisory (silent total data loss). Rejected.
 Every portable SSD candidate was refuted or weakened; the portable-ssd research stream is truncated mid-JSON, so NO portable SSD is recommended and nothing rests on that stream.
 The Sabrent EC-UASP enclosure was stamped "acceptable" - it is a custom-marketplace seller, i.e. a hard-constraint failure. Every bare-drive total in the research is missing a compliant enclosure line.

WORKLOAD DISCIPLINE: the 16-job table is agent-assembled, not telemetry. Use "nine of sixteen under 30 GB"; the "median 25.6 GiB" moves to ~42 GiB under a different inclusion rule; "largest 1.53 TiB" is a unit error for 1.495 TiB. The six measured job throughputs (3.55-11.1 MB/s, plus one 285.8 MB/s portable-SSD job) are what the record supports - and they are why a 30 GB payload costs the shop 45-140 minutes today.

NEXT MEASUREMENT (the only band with no compliant measured drive): a >=1.3 TB sequential write to a current-production 2 TB 2.5in SATA SSD, throughput logged per 10 GB.
