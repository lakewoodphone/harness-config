# Decision — the DSH Evolution Audit (2026-10-13)

The owner's ask, verbatim: *"it's time for the next evolution… go through every single DSH session…
categorize all my conversations… find all the pain points, find what I want, I want the systems
evolved and fixed. I want the instructions and identities changed to better match what we really
need."* Plus a second pillar: *"go back and look at your memories and make sure that you remember
everything, and document everything properly"* — and a refreshed spec of what this setup needs and
what it does not.

Predecessor: `personal-secretary-mvp/docs/secretary-replacement-audit/` (2026-09-11), built from
1,642 Copilot sessions / 26,950 owner turns. The current `zabz` persona was written from it.

## Decisions taken (dev decisions — mine, per the owner's standing instruction that development
## questions are not his)

**D1 — Scope is the harness and the agent system, not the company.** The deliverable set is the
instruction and identity surface (presets, skills, AGENTS.md), the memory/continuity mechanism, and
the recurring engineering pains. The autonomous company is treated as a *system whose pains are
evidence*, not as a rebuild target. Reason: he asked for "your system" to be optimized and for
"the instructions and identities" to change; a company rebuild is a different project with a
different budget.

**D2 — The DSH corpus is primary; the Copilot corpus is the baseline, not a second audit.** 6,909
DSH sessions / 2.03M events on the authority (`secratary-cloud:/root/authority.db`), newest
2026-10-13T11:05Z. The Copilot corpus's numbers already exist as computed values in the 2026-09-11
audit and in `~/code/_audit/` raw JSON; re-deriving them would cost and add nothing. **The
interesting question the owner actually implied is the delta:** which of the ten measured Copilot
behaviours survived the migration, and which did the new harness fix? That is a stated section of
the output.

**D3 — Deterministic first, judgment second.** One streaming pass over `dsh_session_events` produces
every count; agents judge only what a rule cannot. Reason: the predecessor audit's numbers were
reproducible from scripts and its opinions were not. Every figure in the new set traces to
`/home/zabz/evolution-corpus/stats.json`, a SQL result, or a file.

**D4 — Coverage is stated, never implied.** The deterministic layer covers 100% of the 6,909
sessions. The agent-reading layer covers a stratified sample, and the sample's size, seed and
selection rules are written into `shards/MANIFEST.json`. "We read all of them" will be replaced by
the real number.

**D5 — The fan-out runs on the mesh; the artifact is written by one hand.** `subagent` is the mesh
(a real child turn on another node). Children **do research and return documents**; the manager
writes every file. Reason: mesh children have no shared filesystem, so the worktree-isolation
model does not transfer, and a worker that commits into a repo path on its own machine is a
conflict nobody can see. Where a child must produce code, it returns a unified diff and the manager
applies and tests it. **One writer per path, always.**

**D6 — A transcript read is a context read, so no child dumps a session into its context.** Children
read digests and shards, never raw session dumps; `sessions.mjs show` is for targeted quotes only.
