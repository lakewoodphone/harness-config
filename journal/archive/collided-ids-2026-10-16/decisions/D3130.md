## NJ ST-50 Q3 2026 will be filed on a gross-receipts basis of 25,671.84, tax 1,700.76

**Decided 2026-10-13.** Deadline 2026-10-20. Recorded in
`/opt/company/docs/finance/TAX-CLUSTER-OPEN-2026-10-13.md` §7.3 and in owner-queue #465.

### The decision

The taxable-sales basis for NJ ST-50, 2026-Q3, is **gross receipts of 25,671.84**, giving
**1,700.76** of tax at 6.625%:

| | Q3 total |
|---|---|
| Stripe card charges, gross, per transaction (Jul 6,040.70 + Aug 6,772.78 + Sep 7,347.69) | 20,161.17 |
| less Stripe refunds (346.54 + 150.00 + 494.52) | −991.06 |
| customer Zelle (1,915.00 + 2,170.00 + 159.00) | 4,244.00 |
| in-store sales recorded (607.73 + 620.00 + 340.00) | 1,567.73 |
| cash deposits (315.00 + 225.00 + 150.00) | 690.00 |
| **GROSS RECEIPTS** | **25,671.84** |

### Why this basis and not the old one

**ST-50 line 1 is gross receipts, and a payment processor's fee is not a deduction from it.** The
canonical `lpt-monthly-pnl.py --months 3 --end 2026-09-30` reports 26,202.68 for the same quarter —
within 530.84, which is exactly Stripe fees and payout lag. Filing on the P&L figure would be
1,735.93 and would understate gross receipts by the fee. The gross charge figure is the defensible
one, and the two are shown together so anyone can check the arithmetic.

### Why the old convention is not a convention

`scripts/tax/calculate-sales-tax.py` claims to compute this and is wrong by a factor of 2.2: it
returns **37,118.27** for 2026-Q2 against a filed basis of **16,876.08**, because its rule sums every
credit on every `LPT`-named account and so counts a Stripe charge *and* the payout that settles it.
Its `KNOWN_TAX` table came from a "v9 FINAL P&L" whose generator (`_scratch/_pnl_final.py`) no longer
exists anywhere on the box. **Do not reuse `calculate-sales-tax.py` as a source of a filing figure.**

### The uncertainty, stated

The shop keeps no per-sale taxable/exempt flag, so this is a reconstruction, not a reading. It could
be understated if customers paid the owner personally (the personal accounts took 13,855.52 of Q3
credits that the classifier routes away from revenue as owner funding); out-of-state eBay sales were
not deducted.

### The consequence nobody asked for

The four prior ST-50 filings implied bases of **4,048.75 / 4,456.30 / 5,799.25 / 16,876.08**, and the
last of those sits against a canonical revenue of **23,030.55** for the same quarter — 73% of it.
Those filings look **understated**, consistently. Whether to amend them is a money question and is
**not** decided here; it is on the owner.
