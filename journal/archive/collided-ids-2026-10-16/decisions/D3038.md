**D3038 · 2026-10-09 · Shabbos power-down moves to 5 hours before sunset + ceiling light held ON.**

**Decision 2026-10-09** (owner instruction, this session).

Owner: *"change it that it turns everything off and the automation runs 5 hours
before sunset, not later"* and *"make sure that the ceiling light stays on
always ... before Home Assistant shuts down, it just turns on the [ceiling]
light."*

## What changed

1. **Power OFF is now 300 minutes (5 h) before sunset** — was 180. Power ON
 stays 100 min after sunset.
 - `app/services/shalom_zmanim.py` `DEFAULT_CANDLE_OFFSET_MIN = 300`
 - `app/config.py` `shabbat_candle_offset_min = 300`
 - `secratary:.env` `SHABBAT_CANDLE_OFFSET_MIN=300`
 - Verified with astral: Fri 2026-10-16 sunset 18:15 EDT -> OFF **13:15**.

2. **The ceiling light is forced ON before HA stops.**
 - Entity `switch.deserted_house_lights` ("Control Room Main Light").
 - New `home_assistant.ensure_ceiling_light_on()` — turns it on and reads it
 back (a 200 from `turn_on` is a request, not a lamp).
 - New step in `shabbat_orchestrator._power_down_critical`, ordered **before**
 the graceful stop so the confirming read-back has a live HA.
 - **Not a gate.** The gate for a cut is the interior door; refusing to cut
 over a dark room would strand the released maglock for the whole run.

## Why the light needed explicit handling

The room lights are driven by presence — `automation.phoenix_v2_master_light_controller`
turns them off whenever neither the boss nor a worker reads present. Nobody
registers presence across Shabbat/Yom Tov, so the room went dark for the entire
run. A forced ON, persisted across the reboot, is the only thing that keeps it lit.

## Verified in production, not just in tests

The change was deployed at 19:15:13Z and the executor's own structured result at
19:17:06Z records the real sequence:

```
hold_interior_door_open : held=True samples [off x8], 15s
ceiling_light_on : before {state: off} after {state: on} verified=True
ha_shutdown : homeassistant.stop -> 504, then connection-refused
reverify_door : basis ha_down_after_requested_stop
power_off : ok=True, state=off
ok=True powered_on=False
```

So the ceiling light really did go from `off` to `on` before the cut, with HA
confirming it.

## Residual risks still to check

- **HA must restore the light as ON** when power returns Sat 2026-10-10 ~20:04.
 If the recorder lost the write, the room is dark for Shabbat. Check the
 light's state as soon as HA is back.
- The plug's on-device ON jobs (survive a dead secretary) are correct and were
 verified by hand: `4 20 10 10 *`, `53 19 17 10 *`, `44 19 24 10 *`, all
 `Switch.Set on=true`. The OFF is software-only by design
 (`SHABBAT_DEVICE_OFF_SCHEDULE=false`) so an ungated device OFF can never cut
 power with the door locked.
