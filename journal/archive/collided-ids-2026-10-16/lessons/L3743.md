**L3743 · Hebrew on the shop label tape renders reversed and fails silently (PIL has no bidi)**

MEASURED 2026-10-13 on the shop's Brother QL-820NWB, dry run of a sheet listing the Hebrew
recording names from the Fisher SD card.

Hebrew (any RTL script) CANNOT go on the label tape. PIL's ImageDraw.text has no bidirectional
algorithm, so the renderer draws the characters left to right: "ניגון שמחה בישטנא.wav" came out
as "wav.בישטנא שמחה ניגון" - the words in reverse order and the extension on the wrong side.
There was NO error and no warning. It looked like a plausible label until it was read.

Two rules follow:
1. RTL text for a customer never goes on tape. Put it on screen or on a normal printer.
2. This is the second way the renderer fails silently, the first being the font floor that
 truncates a long line with no warning. So ALWAYS look at the dry-run PNG before printing -
 a successful dry run proves discovery, not correctness, and the machine will happily print
 reversed or chopped text.

Cost of learning it this way: one dry run, no tape.
