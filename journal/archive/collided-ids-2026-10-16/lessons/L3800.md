**L3800 · Dell all-in-one HDD extraction: read the manual's prerequisite chain as a dependency graph - the 2205 drive needs only the back cover (2 screws)**

**L · For a specific FRU, read Dell's own prerequisite chain as a dependency graph — it proves what does NOT have to come off.**

Measured 2026-10-14, Dell **Inspiron One 2205** ("regular Model W03B", the owner's words; Service Tag
`1QL7NQ1`), aunt Raitzik's all-in-one, job = get the 500 GB 3.5" SATA drive out. Family job, no charge.

- **Dell's service manual is the cheapest authority and it is free.** `dl.dell.com` PDFs **403 a plain
 `Invoke-WebRequest`** — send a browser User-Agent and the same URL returns the file (74 pages,
 1,513,836 bytes here). On ZABZ-TECH the PDF readers already installed are **pymupdf** and **pypdf 4.3.1**
 and **pdfminer**; there is **no `pdftotext`**. `fitz` page renders at `dpi=170` make usable diagram
 PNGs, which matters because the callout numbers in these manuals are only meaningful in the drawing.
- **The hard drive's ONLY prerequisite is the back cover.** Manual p25, "Removing the Hard Drive", step 2
 names one thing: *"Remove the back cover"*. Back cover is p2: **2 screws**, place the machine **face
 down on a soft cloth** (glass panel), slide the cover off its tabs, lift.
- **Why that is a proof and not a guess:** Dell's dependency graph is written into the manual's own
 prerequisite lines. Front Stand (p23), Rear Stand (p56) and Rear Stand Cover (p58) *each* name
 **"Remove the back cover"** as their own step — so they are **downstream** of the back cover and cannot
 be requirements of it. A generic "take the stand off first" teardown of this family is extra work on a
 glass panel for nothing.
- **Screw count for the drive, end to end: 8** — 2 back cover, 2 **captive** (loosen only, they stay in
 the chassis), 4 holding the cage to the drive. The drive connects with an ordinary SATA data + SATA
 power pair: no flex cable, no adapter.
- **A 3.5" drive needs 12 V.** Any bus-powered 2.5"-only USB adapter will not spin it; the dock must be
 powered (the shop's Sabrent ASM1153E dual dock qualifies).
- **Generalise it:** before planning any teardown, grep the manual for the FRU's own
 "Remove the … (see Removing the …)" list and treat it as a graph. Two minutes of that beats a video
 that removes four extra assemblies, and it is falsifiable — the claim "the stand does not have to come
 off" has a citation.
