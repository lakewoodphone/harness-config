**L3687 · Two adjacent months disagree by equal and opposite amounts: one defect counted twice**

2026-10-13. A compensating pair of disagreements, one month apart, that cancel to the cent - so two of the
68 are one defect.

WHAT I THOUGHT I HAD. `lpt-chase-7542` has 8 months with 1-2 transactions each (Oct 2025 to Apr 2026,
values 65 to 134) on a card that runs thousands a month in 2026. That looked like a seven-month coverage
gap, and it would have been the largest clause-3 finding of the session.

WHAT IT ACTUALLY IS, read out of the mail rather than inferred. The raw statements say
 'Statement balance | $6837.97' with 'Minimum payment due | $68.00' 2025-09-29
 'Statement balance | $6769.97' with 'Minimum payment due | $67.00' 2025-10-29
 'Statement balance | $6702.97' with 'Minimum payment due | $67.00' 2025-11-29
so the extraction is correct and the balance declines by EXACTLY the minimum payment each month - and the
1-2 recorded transactions ARE those minimum payments. **The card is dormant and being amortised, not
under-synced.** A balance falling by a constant 67 dollars a month was the clue, and it took reading three
messages to dispose of a hypothesis that looked like the biggest finding of the night.

WHAT THE CHECK DID FIND. Those monthly readings are `statement`-sourced and look like this:
 2026-07-29 481833
 2026-08-29 552567 DISAGREE -435.38
 2026-09-29 593122 DISAGREE +435.38
**Two disagreements, adjacent months, equal and opposite to the cent.** One reading is wrong; the pair is
one defect counted twice. Which of the two I cannot say without the statement detail, but the arithmetic is
unambiguous: 435.38 appears as a deficit in August and a surplus in September, and no month can be wrong in
both directions.

WHY THE TOOL DID NOT CATCH IT. `mark_suspects` rule R1 finds a sign mirror and it is bounded to SIX HOURS
apart, which is right for the defect it was written for - a batch that wrote two values minutes apart. A
mirror split across a MONTH BOUNDARY is invisible to it, and so is any pair whose two readings are simply
wrong in opposite directions a month apart. **The estimator already has the shape: look for two adjacent
months whose deltas sum to zero, and treat them as one defect.** That is a cheap check on a structure the
reconciliation already computes, and it is worth having because a pair like this inflates both the count
and the summed magnitude - 68 disagreements and 55,319.21 both contain this defect twice.
