**L3052 · A carrier receipt is not a read receipt, and our own status column was wrong**

**L — A message can be "delivered" at the carrier and still not be on the handset, and our own status column is not evidence of either.**

Measured 2026-09-29 while answering the owner's "Shabsi says he did not get Mommy's siyum text".

WHAT WE COULD PROVE. The siyum fan-out (inbound message id 125282, 2026-09-28 20:05:31Z
from Mommy +17326745878) produced 10 outbound ledger rows, ids 125283-125299, one per
active unmuted member. Read back from Twilio's own API for all 10: status=delivered,
error_code=None, num_segments=2, price=0.01660 USD. Shabsi's copy (id 125297, SID
SM366e9e64302a50888a4d551624ab0379, to +18485257897) left at 20:06:32Z and the carrier
receipt landed at 20:06:33Z. Shabsi was not skipped, not muted, not inactive: the fan-out
predicate is `active and not muted and phone != from_number` (hub.py:1108-1112), and the
roster has exactly 13 members, 1 inactive (Bracha) and 1 muted (Yosef Dov), so 10 is the
complete and correct recipient set.

WHAT THAT DOES *NOT* PROVE. Twilio's "delivered" is the carrier's word. There is no read
receipt anywhere in this system, so no record of ours can show that a human saw a message.
"Delivered" therefore answers "did we hand it off correctly" and never answers "did he get it".

WHAT IT ALMOST CERTAINLY IS NOT. The copy was the only 2-segment message in that 6-minute
burst (277 chars); his other 8 were 1 segment. But that is not the cause: since 2026-09-08
Shabsi has 21 multi-segment messages from this number (up to 4 segments, e.g. 2026-09-25
19:10:12, len=391), all delivered, and he replied to and argued about the content of those
exact messages. Multi-segment SMS reaches his handset. Do not offer that as the explanation -
it does not survive the evidence.

THE REAL FINDING, AND IT IS OURS TO FIX. While tracing, our ledger was found wrong in the
OTHER direction: 40 outbound rows sat at 'queued' or 'sent' for ever while Twilio reported
them delivered - including Shabsi's own copy of Mommy's 18:36 "the sign says 8" message (id
125037) and 7 of the 10 copies of the 17:43 fan-out. Cause: Twilio status callbacks are
best-effort and are NOT retried. When one is lost in transit the row keeps its last-known
status permanently and nothing ever notices, because there was no reconciliation against the
carrier. So anyone (a family member, Chip, or a future session) reading our log to argue
about delivery is arguing from a record that can be wrong in both directions.

RULE. Before claiming anything about delivery, read Twilio's own record for the SID, and
reconcile our status column against it. An empty or non-terminal status is a refusal, not
evidence of loss. Repo tool that does the comparison and repair: /home/zabz/phone/fc_reconcile.py
(dry run by default, `--go` to apply; only moves rows forward on the ladder).
