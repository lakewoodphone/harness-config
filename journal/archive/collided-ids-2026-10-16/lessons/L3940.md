The candidate set is now two, one of them eliminated, and the remaining one has a name.

**Eliminated: the 900 s `run_once` backoff.** I said it was the untested candidate; it is now tested and it is not the cause. `_backoff_multiplier` is **not persisted** — it is not a key in `data/autopilot_state.json` at all — and the state carries `consecutive_failures: 0` and `last_error: None`. With no failure recorded, the effective gap is `interval` itself, not `interval × multiplier`. **So the 900 s gap IS the stored `interval_sec`, and the backoff is cleared.**
