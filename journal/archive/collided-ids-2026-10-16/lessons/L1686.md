Environment facts worth not rediscovering:
- `pdftotext` is NOT installed on this machine. Python 3.14 at C:\Python314\python.exe has `pymupdf` (fitz) and `pypdf`; `python-pptx` is NOT installed.
- Text extraction from JSTOR scans (which carry an OCR text layer) came out clean and correct: verified 5 of 9 table pages by rendering them with pymupdf at 210 dpi grayscale and reading the images. Numbers matched the extracted text every time, so extraction alone is adequate for these PDFs.
