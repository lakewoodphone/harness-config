**L3772 · Correction to L3770: available current never bounds a converter's output voltage - a rail is real when it holds under a load**

## Correction to L3770 — and L3770 taught a rule that is wrong in principle

L3770 says, as a rule, that a 5 V input cannot make 16.8 V because "0.22 A is 1.1 W — far too little to
run the boost that node would need." **That is wrong and must not be carried forward.**

**Power is V x I, so a boost converter trades current for voltage.** 1.1 W can hold 16.8 V at roughly
60 mA. **Available current never bounds the voltage a converter can produce.** In the Klein 5420 case I
used that power argument to conclude a 16.75 V node was "stored, not generated" — the conclusion may
still be right, but that argument does not support it.

**The rule that does hold, and is the only discriminator: a rail is real when it holds its voltage
under a load.** LoZ mode, or a ~10 kohm resistor across the probes. A driven rail holds; a stored node
collapses. Not a bigger number on the meter.

**And the framing to keep instead:** voltage and current are independent. A node sits at a voltage
because of charge on its capacitance (V = Q/C) and that charge can move as microamps, so 16.8 V at
essentially no current is ordinary. The question is *stored or driven*, never *large or small*.

## Two more things from the same bench session

- **An unpowered board makes a meter wander, and that is the instrument reporting the board, not a
 fault.** A 10 Mohm DMM on an undriven node reads residue, coupling and drift; the numbers move with
 probe position and decay while you watch. On a machine with no PD contract and no pack, almost every
 node is undriven, so almost every reading is a ghost. The owner had never seen this before because
 his probes normally land on a board that is running, or on one with its pack fitted.
- **"It changed when X was plugged in" is not "this node is X's net."** The 16 V node moved to 10 V when
 the pack went in, and I called it the battery node and built an inference on it; it later read 16 V
 again with nothing changed. **A node can move because it is loaded, not because it is that circuit's
 node.**
