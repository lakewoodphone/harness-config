## 2026-10-13 — the QL-820NWB DOES report `PRINTING`, if you read it inside the feed window

The `lpt-label-printing` skill says the `READY → PRINTING → READY` transition "is simply not
observable" and tells sessions not to spend calls on it. Measured 2026-10-13 on the shop's
Brother QL-820NWB (`H3G354844`, 29 mm DK-2210 roll), that is **too strong**, and the correction
matters because it is the only positive evidence short of holding the tape.

Method, and it is exact:

1. before the send: `ssh secratary-ts 'lpt-label find'` → `state READY`
2. send the job (`lpt-label print -`, 55.5 mm of tape)
3. **the very next read**, no sleep, still inside the feed window → `state PRINTING`
4. one read a minute later → `state READY`

The skill's own negative measurement came from a **poll loop sampled every 150 ms through the
printer's web status page**, whose `Auto Refresh Interval` is 60 000 ms, and it concluded the state
was unreadable. `lpt-label find` does not read that cached page — it re-reads the device — so the
negative was a property of the sampling route, not of the printer.

**What is still not proven, and must not be claimed:** `PRINTING` is not tape. It is the device
saying it is feeding *something*. The honest report remains: printer identified by model and serial,
`READY` with the 29 mm roll, the transport accepted the job — plus, now, the state observation with
its timing stated. The only proof of tape is the tape in the roll or in a hand.

Practical consequence for the next session: take **one** read immediately after the send and record
it with its timing; do not build a poll loop, and do not treat a missing `PRINTING` as failure,
because a 33 mm job can finish between two reads.
