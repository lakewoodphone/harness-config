**L3686 · Nothing is asked twice - but the gate is doing it, not the selector, and one merchant has two keys**

2026-10-13. Verified the property clause 5 ends with - "so nothing is asked twice" - and it HOLDS for what
reaches him, with two defects in the selector behind it.

THE PROPERTY, as tested. The asks store has two tables and they answer different questions: `ask` is the
ledger of what the SELECTOR chose, `clarification` is the record of what was actually PUT to him.
 clarification: ask-1791862240 Newegg ASKED | ask-1791867617 Polka Dot ASKED
 ask-1791871210/008/811 Pass EXPIRED (three separate rows)
So exactly TWO questions are outstanding, one per purchase, and every duplicate attempt was refused by the
one-at-a-time gate before any network call. **He has never been texted about the same purchase twice.** The
property holds where it matters - on his phone, not in the ledger.

DEFECT 1, in the selector. `ask` holds the same purchase more than once, keyed by merchant:
 newegg 362.51 at 03:11:15 AND 03:30:40
 polka dot 299.00 at 04:42:27 AND 05:00:36
 back market 357.18 at 04:30:09 and
 back-market-inc 357.18 at 04:30:09 AND 04:38:45
Nothing reached him twice only because the gate caught it. **The gate is doing work the selector should
have done**, and a gate is a worse place for it: it can only refuse the whole message, so a re-picked
purchase burns an ask slot rather than being skipped. The wasted attempts are visible - `pass 135.33`
generated three separate clarification rows, all expired - and each one costs a slot out of three a day.

DEFECT 2, merchant normalisation, and it is the one that could actually produce a double question.
`back market` and `back-market-inc` are one merchant under two keys, and the same 357.18 appears against
two different accounts (Blue Business Card and Personal PayPal). If those are one purchase mirrored across
two accounts - which is exactly the pattern this ledger already carries for PayPal, where every
transaction is written as a debit AND a credit - then two different questions about one purchase are
queued and only the gate's own similarity check stands between them and his phone.

WHAT I AM NOT CLAIMING. I have not shown he was ever asked twice, because he was not. I have shown that
the mechanism preventing it is a refusal rather than a rule, and that the merchant key can split one
purchase into two questions. Filed as a ledger item rather than patched tonight: the fix is a
merchant-key canonicalisation plus an exclusion for purchases already queued, and it should not be written
straight into the live ask path without a test that plants a duplicate and watches it be skipped.
