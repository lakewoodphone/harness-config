**L3793 · The 'your repair is ready' notification has never once delivered: 300 attempts to 273 customers, all FAILED, for ten months**

# 394 finished orders, 363 customers, and the "your repair is ready" notification has never once delivered

Follow-on to H3659, on the owner's instruction *"of course keep working, just don't send any
customer-facing texts without permission."*

## The measurement (mine, reproduced twice)

The Schuck investigator found it; I verified it independently. Over `communications`:

- **300 rows mention `REPAIR_COMPLETED` in `metadata`. All 300 are `FAILED`. Zero delivered.**
- 252 of the 300 (84 %) carry the literal error `"Twilio has been removed or is not configured in this
 environment"`. Also FAILED: 24 `REPAIR_AWAITING_PARTS`, 1 `REPAIR_CREATED`.
- Not recent and not one incident: first failure **2025-12-23**, last **2026-10-12**. By month —
 2025-12:7, 2026-01:25, 02:19, 03:6, 05:52, 06:73, 07:76, 08:6, 09:33, 10:3. Chronic for ten months.
- **They are addressed to 273 distinct phone numbers / 273 distinct customers** — not a test number. I
 checked this specifically because the newest one pointed at `+17325691594`, a number this estate has
 used for machine traffic, and I did not want to report 300 customers on the strength of one row.
 It is 273 real recipients.
- **There is no `template` column.** The template name lives in `metadata` JSON. Anyone querying a
 `template` column will get `column does not exist` and may wrongly conclude the claim is unverifiable.

## The population it sits inside

`orders` where `status='READY_FOR_PICKUP'`: **420**. Of those, **401 have neither `pickupReleasedAt`
nor `pickup_notified_at` set** (only 3 released, 16 notified across the whole table — pickups are
essentially not recorded).

Excluding test/self rows: **394 orders, 363 distinct customers, 16,938.37 of attached total.**

When the customer was last actually reached (a `DELIVERED` communication):
**303 last heard from 30-90 days ago, 64 within 30 days, and 33 have NEVER had a delivered contact** —
that last group includes both Felder orders and Shira Schuck.

**268 of the 363 were sent a REPAIR_COMPLETED notice that failed.**

The list is at `/root/lpt-sweep-20261014/uncollected-orders.csv` on the authority and copied to the
owner's work root as `uncollected-orders.csv` (394 rows; id, orderNumber, opened, total, customer,
phone, customerId, ready_at, last_delivered, failed_ready_notices).

## What this is NOT

**Not proof that 394 people are waiting.** `pickupReleasedAt` is set on 3 of 649 orders, so the shop does
not record collection — an order can sit at `READY_FOR_PICKUP` for months after the customer walked out
with it. The honest reading is **"possibly uncollected, and in 268 cases provably never told"**, which
is why this needs triage by a human who knows the shelf, not a blast.

## Where the sender is, and why I have not fixed it

`REPAIR_COMPLETED` appears **nowhere** in `/opt/company/app` or `/opt/company/scripts`. So it is not the
secretary app: it is the **shop's own backend**, the `lpt-backend` container on **`lpt-apps-01`**, whose
env carries both `TWILIO_*` and `DIALPAD_SMS_SEND_ENDPOINT` / `DIALPAD_SMS_TARGET_ID` / `DIALPAD_API_KEY`.
The shape is a notification written against Twilio on a line that has since been decommissioned, with the
row landing FAILED in the shared `communications` table — which is why the dashboard shows a
notification for every order and reads as "customer told".

**`lpt-apps-01` is designated read-only in `settings/machines/host-posture.tsv`** ("Do NOT add load;
service changes here need the owner"). So repointing or repairing that sender is an owner decision, and
it is the ONE thing standing between this shop and every future completion notice working.
