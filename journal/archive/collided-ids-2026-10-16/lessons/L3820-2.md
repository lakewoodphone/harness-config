**L3820 · CORRECTION to L3818: dialpad_sms_cache double-stores since 2026-10-08 under two session_ids, so the auto-reply rate I reported was double**

CORRECTION to L3818. That entry reported the customer-facing auto-reply rate as "16-24/day, roughly a fifth of inbounds". The row count it came from is wrong: `dialpad_sms_cache` stores most recent messages TWICE.

MEASURED 2026-10-15. One message, `message_id` 6086487966523392, to 732-664-4776, stored as two rows:

| session_id | created_at | fetched_at | what it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| `6086487966523392` | 2026-10-15T12:25:10.272Z | 12:25:11.527Z | the live capture, 1.2 s later |
| `ptf-thread-7326644776` | 2026-10-15T12:25:13.191Z | 12:45:06.377Z | a second harvest path, 20 min later |

The unique key is `UNIQUE(session_id, message_id)`, so a second `session_id` defeats it. Two consequences that matter more than the count:
- **The two rows disagree about `created_at` by 2.9 seconds, and the later-fetched row carries the EARLIER `created_at`.** So ordering this table by `created_at` to ask "who spoke last" is not reliable to the second. Dedup by `message_id` before reading direction.
- `message_id` is non-null on all 135,804 rows (0 nulls measured), so `COUNT(DISTINCT message_id)` is the valid count.

DUPLICATION IS RECENT, NOT UNIFORM. Table-wide 135,804 rows / 132,029 distinct = **1.029x**, but every day from 2026-10-08 to 2026-10-15 is **~1.9-1.95x** (10-08: 165 rows for 85 distinct; 10-12: 108 for 56). The second `session_id` path appears to have begun around 2026-10-08. A "how much is duplicated" figure read over the whole table hides it.

CORRECTED RATE, counted DISTINCT `message_id`, by day — inbound / canned template / all outbound:
`10-08 44/8/41 | 10-09 14/11/12 | 10-10 1/1/1 | 10-11 36/10/20 | 10-12 25/12/31 | 10-13 27/8/16 | 10-14 16/10/23 | 10-15 5/3/3`
Eight-day totals: **168 inbound, 63 auto-replies, 147 outbound.** So about **8 auto-replies a day, not 16-24**, and the share of inbounds auto-answered is **63/168 = 37%**, not a fifth.

L3818's conclusion is unchanged and still the point: a customer gets one canned hours message and nothing else. Only its number was double.

RULE: over `dialpad_sms_cache` since 2026-10-08, count `DISTINCT message_id` and dedup before ordering by `created_at`. The comms index already collapses these (`comms_health` reported 13,402 collapsed duplicates on 2026-10-15), so `ps_comms_waiting` and `comms-search.py` are unaffected — raw SQL over the table is. The general form is the measurement rule: **ask what WRITES the field you are filtering on.** Here, two writers, two `session_id`s, one message, and they do not agree on the timestamp.
