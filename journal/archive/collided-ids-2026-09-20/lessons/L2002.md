**L2002 · Pixel 6 screen replacement: the fingerprint sensor is part of the display and calibration is mandatory**

**Durable shop rule: on a Pixel 6, the fingerprint sensor IS part of the display, and calibration is mandatory after any screen replacement. A customer-supplied screen is therefore a fingerprint gamble, not just a glass swap.**

Source: iFixit guides 148750 (screen), 148965 (fingerprint reader), 166753 (calibration), plus GSMArena 2022-02-16 and the Pixel Repair tool itself (HTTP 200, 2026-09-18). Documentation-sourced, NOT verified in our own shop - nobody here has yet done a Pixel 6 screen.

WHAT IS STRUCTURAL (cannot change with tooling improvements):
- Pixel 6 fingerprint is an optical under-display sensor (UDFPS), bonded to the rear of the panel and connected by a ZIF connector on the rear of the screen. Screen and scanner are effectively one assembly, so a screen replacement always replaces the sensor or requires transferring the original onto the new panel.
- iFixit, fingerprint-reader guide: "If you replace the fingerprint reader in the Pixel 6 or switch it between two different displays, you need to recalibrate it to maintain its functionality." Transferring the ORIGINAL sensor to a new panel still needs calibration - the calibration is tied to the panel.
- Replacement fingerprint sensors are not sold by Google or iFixit. If the customer's original sensor is damaged (likely when glass shattered over the sensor area), fingerprint is permanently dead. Guardrail: require the incoming screen to arrive WITH a sensor already attached.

CALIBRATION PROCEDURE (the part that decides the job):
- Tool: https://pixelrepair.withgoogle.com/udfps - verified live 2026-09-18.
- Chrome ONLY (WebUSB), on a Windows or Mac PC, phone in Fastboot mode over USB. ~10-15 min.
- Flow: Connect Device -> Install software -> phone auto-restarts -> **manually restart the phone again afterwards**. Google/iFixit: skipping the manual restart means the fingerprint will not work properly. On failure, just repeat the procedure.
- Windows needs the Google USB driver first; developer options + USB debugging must be on.
- The tool registers hardware IDs with Google's servers, so it needs internet and the serial must be known to Google.
- Known failure: "Couldn't find the unique calibration software for this device" - Google has no calibration profile for that serial. Documented since 2022, including at uBreakiFix (Google's own endorsed partner). A 2026-08 Pixel Repair web-tool update indicates independent techs can now do the panel/sensor pairing over WebUSB without proprietary bench gear, so the situation improved - but it is still reported.

AFTERMARKET RISK (the real reason to be careful):
- iFixit, screen guide: "If you're using an aftermarket screen and fingerprint sensor, you may encounter issues with the fingerprint recalibration tool." A copy panel's optical characteristics may not calibrate, or may calibrate and still read badly.

PARTS TRAP - ASK BEFORE QUOTING:
- Some Pixel 6 replacement screens come WITHOUT the pre-installed frame/chassis, which needs a different and harder removal procedure. Genuine service packs come with the frame. Screen-with-frame is the easy path; screen-only is a much bigger job.

DISASSEMBLY SHAPE (for difficulty judgment):
- Screen-first (display-out). Heat the edges, slice adhesive, FIVE opening picks, plus small plastic clips holding the screen - insert the pick only 3-4 mm or it jams on the clips. Open the panel like a book hinged on the right, then free the metal bracket over the display cable. Battery must be below 25 percent. Class 1 laser caution. IP rating is lost and new adhesive is required. iFixit: test the phone BEFORE applying adhesive and resealing.

SHOP GUARDRAIL for any customer-supplied screen (recommend, owner sets price):
- Labor-only, no parts warranty, fingerprint explicitly excluded in writing and signed before starting.
- Accept only a screen assembly that includes the frame AND a pre-attached fingerprint sensor.
- Enroll a fingerprint on the shop bench BEFORE sealing the phone; if it will not enroll, stop and tell the customer before reassembly.
