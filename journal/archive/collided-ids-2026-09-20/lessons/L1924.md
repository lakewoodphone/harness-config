- Owner himself resolved the last bench fault: the power and volume flexes are mounted on the **old screen assembly** and he transferred them to the new one. Nothing to order.

DURABLE DEVICE FACT (the reason this entry exists)
- **On a Unihertz Jelly Max, the power and volume button flexes are part of the screen/frame assembly.** The $60 replacement touchscreen module from Unihertz ships **without** them, and its product photo shows only a bare frame plus the display flex - which is why the omission is easy to make and impossible to spot from the listing. **On every Jelly Max screen job: transfer the two flexes from the old assembly or the buttons are dead.**
- Failure path if one is torn or lost (neither Unihertz nor any US source had them when I looked, 2026-09-17): SpainSellers, in stock, EUR 6.73 each - power flex ref **70036**, volume flex ref **70030**, `spainsellers.com/en/mobile-spare-parts/unihertz-mobile/unihertz-jelly-max-5g/` (both pages returned HTTP 200 on check; the shop ships from Crevillente, Alicante, Spain).
- Matching a part from a photo is what solved it. The third-party flex listing's own image showed the two round dome contacts and connector tail, and the owner placed them on the old assembly from that alone - cheaper than ordering, faster than a teardown video.

HOW TO READ A UNIHERTZ PRICE (reusable)
- `https://www.unihertz.com/products/<handle>.js` returns the Shopify variant JSON: every part in the collection with its real price in cents. Fetched 2026-09-17 for `replacement-parts-for-jelly-max`: Touchscreen Module **$60** (6000), Back Cover $15, Back Camera $30, Battery $40, Earpiece $8, Speaker Module $8.
