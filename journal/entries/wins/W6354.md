**W6354 · 2026-10-13 · The authority disk emergency was a Hetzner disk upgrade that was never applied - one power cycle turned 0 bytes free into 76 GB.**

RESOLVED 2026-10-14T01:14Z. The authority secratary-cloud had been at LITERAL 0 bytes free CONTINUOUSLY from 2026-10-13T20:15:01Z to 2026-10-14T00:48:03Z (4h33m), measured from /root/.disk-guard/guard.log which recorded 'CRITICAL: / at 96.2% (0.0 GB free, 0 MB)' every 15 minutes across that window. In that window the transcript archive refused to start on its 2.5 GB free-space floor (ALARM file, 2026-10-14T00:23:15Z), swap sat at 4082/4095 MB, and the company database (26.8 GB) had nowhere to grow.
