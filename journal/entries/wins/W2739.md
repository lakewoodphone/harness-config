`cron-wrap.sh` FIXED, PROVEN, AND NOW REACHING THE WHOLE MESH. 2026-10-11 22:35Z (child `subagent-22`,
artifact `161`), plus the follow-up it asked about - which was MINE, not the owner's.

THE DEFECT, quoted: `ERRTAIL="$(tail -c 400 "$ERRFILE" | tr '\n\t' ' ' | cut -c1-160)"` - keeps the last
400 bytes of stderr, then the FIRST 160 of those, so field 5 recorded bytes **N-400..N-240 and discarded the
final 240 - exactly where a traceback's exception line lives.** Every traceback in this estate lost its
cause. That is why several failures today were undiagnosable from the ledger.

THE FIX: field 5 is now the **END** of stderr - the last non-empty line is the anchor and is kept whole; its
FRONT is kept if it exceeds 160 (`ValueError: ` is at the front); earlier frames are back-filled in front,
marked `...`. An `rc != 0` with empty stderr now records `[no stderr; rc=N -- reason is on stdout or in the
job's own log]` instead of a blank. **The 5-field `printf` is byte-for-byte unchanged**, so
`cron-failures.py`'s `parts[4]` still parses: verified over **all 2,323 live rows** - 0 malformed, 0 over 160.
Installed at `/usr/local/bin/cron-wrap.sh`, 156 lines, mode 755, sha `be544850d2168403`.

PROOF, and it is real proof rather than a fixture: 8 old-vs-new fixtures; an in-situ sentinel test; **through
the actual scheduler into the live ledger** (test job fired 22:30:01Z carrying the exception line, crontab
then restored **byte-identical** to its pre-test backup, sha `f6cba2705d66`); and 9 real rows after the swap,
all 5 fields, none blank-on-failure. Rollback is one `cp -p` from a verified backup.
