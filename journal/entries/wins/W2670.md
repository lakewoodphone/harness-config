**W2670 · 2026-10-07 · Retiring a false STALE banner, and JSON for free on the fast path.**

**Retiring a false alarm is worth more than adding a feature, and it is measurable.**

The MCP search surface told every agent `STALE: the index is behind` on a journal index that held
**every** entry (3157 of 3157). A banner that cries wolf teaches readers to ignore banners - the
source comment says exactly that about the previous 300 s ceiling, and the 1200 s one reintroduced it
for the journal. Removing the forced ceiling made the honest answer (`stale:false`, with
`index_entries` and `covers_through` as the evidence) the default.

Second, smaller: fixing the live branch to honour `--json` cost nothing on the fast path, because
hits are collected into one temp file and encoded once - `fa code` went from **27517 ms** (parsing
nothing, returning raw text, and doing a cold full scan) to **179 ms** warm with a real JSON object.
