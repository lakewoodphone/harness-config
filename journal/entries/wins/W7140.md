### The property set, each with a live browser record

1. **One attempt is one record.** 116 answers with a mid-run reload → one `assessment_session_records` row,
 `participant_json->'session'->>'status'` = `complete`, report stored, five recommendations, feedback carrying
 `record_id`. Was 2–10 rows.
2. **A reload's aborted write is merged forward exactly once**, invisibly: 13 aimed reloads + a full run, zero
 conflicts shown, every save confirmed, and a forced-failure control that still surfaces the conflict (81
 samples) without overwriting.
3. **A conflict is a hard pause with one accurate sentence.** 621 samples across four two-context runs: zero
 "Another device" where the gap was ≥2; zero writes for answers given while paused; the other writer's
 answers unchanged at the same revision and values; "keep" issues exactly one write at the refusal's revision
 and clears (zero re-pauses in 386 samples); "use the shared version" writes nothing and the reloaded page
 opens the shared copy; a raw reload re-derives the pause and writes nothing.
4. **The person is told what happened where they are looking.** The resolution confirmation renders in the sync
 box that held the controls — it was measured at 107–298 ms and now carries a **4,000 ms minimum dwell**
 (reading-time arithmetic: ten words at 200–250 wpm = 2.4–3.0 s, +0.3 s to notice the box change, +0.4 s to
 re-read the two meaning-carrying words = 3.7 s, rounded up). At the deadline it yields to whatever is true
 then; a refusal, outage, retrying, sign-out or second conflict takes the box immediately.
5. **No pause without a way out.** The measured 2,548 ms window of "paused" with the controls gone now reads
 "Saving your choice…" and returns the pause with both controls if the write is refused.

### Three costs named, not hidden

- **A second write per answer after a conflict resolution.** Not duplication: session count and cache presence
 never differ and the answers are byte-identical; the extra ~4 KB is the adaptive-question AI call's own
 bookkeeping (`aiHistory` +1, a note, the next item's rendered question) landing on the session, which gives
 the autosave a genuinely different state. About 1.5 writes per answer, each re-sending 67–85 KB. A cost
 defect and the next optimisation target; the data converges correctly.
- **`handleParticipantReset`'s message is still unheard** — "A copy of this session was kept in Archived
 Attempts…" has no mounted reader and no other surface states it. Named in the audit test rather than fixed,
 because the reset moves the attempt's own stamp and needs a different anchor.
- **The 4,000 ms dwell is a reading-time model, not a measurement of comprehension.**

### The last thing not yet observed

the time of writing, because a timer firing and a sentence being readable are precisely what unit tests cannot
prove. If that run shows the sentence surviving ~4 s and the in-flight window saying "Saving your choice…", the
campaign's engineering is complete.
