**W2741b · 2026-10-13 · The owner's answers can finally be recognised: the missing link was an empty question list.**

Fixed and PROVEN 2026-10-13. This is the defect behind "your unified brain is not working".

THE CHAIN, every link measured.
1. `_asks_for()` in `sms-responder.py` returns the questions we put to the owner. It began
 `if ctx_mod is None or not hasattr(ctx_mod, "recent_asks"): return []`.
2. `textctx.recent_asks` DOES NOT EXIST in the module deployed on this host, so it returned `[]`
 for every single message.
3. With no open question in `ctx`, the four-way decider cannot tell an answer from a statement. It
 returned NOISE - correctly given what it was shown - and the responder recorded
 `ignored / decide4:NOISE`. Measured on the owner's own three texts: "Yes, that was a loan from me
 to the business", "Talk to me like a normal person and ask me things one at a time", and "In
 regards to Weinberg ... I paid him $1000 from my personal account" - all three NOISE, two of them
 direct answers to questions the CFO had texted him hours earlier.

THE FIX. `_asks_for()` now falls back to `cfo_clarify.open_clarifications()` - the CFO already
records every question it sends, so read it there instead of depending on a function that is not on
the box. The fallback maps the clarification row onto the ask shape the decider expects
(`reference`, `body`, `merchant_key`, `amount`, `card`, `when`).

PROVEN, not asserted. On a COPY of the store with one synthetic waiting question:
 open_clarifications from the copy: 1
 asks returned to the decider: 1
 {'reference': 'ask-1791861075', 'merchant_key': 'newegg', 'amount': '362.51', ...}
 asks for a text sent BEFORE the question: 0 <- the negative case
 RESULT: PASS

AND I BROKE IT MYSELF, IN THE FIX. The first version of my date guard read
`if asked and sent_at and asked > sent_at` - comparing `asked_at` (ISO-8601, from the CFO store)
against `messages.date_sent` (which holds RFC-2822 from Twilio AND ISO-8601 from the app) as
STRINGS. The negative case returned 1 instead of 0 and I caught it only because I had written the
negative case down. This is the third time this estate has hit mixed date formats in one day, and
the second time inside the hour of filing a lesson about it. The guard now parses both forms to
aware instants and compares those.

THE RULE, sharpened: a comparison involving a timestamp is a claim that both sides are the same
type. Write the NEGATIVE case first - "a question asked after the text must not match" - because a
positive-only test passes just as happily on a comparison that is not comparing anything.

NOW ON A CLOCK. `sms-responder.py run --send` every 5 minutes and `cfo-ask.py --send` every 30
(caps live in the tools: 3 asks/day, 45-minute gap, never the same merchant twice). Only numbers
marked `allow=auto` are auto-answered - the owner's own phone, his brother Shabsi and his father
Totty, each carrying his recorded authorisation with his own words and a date; everyone else is
filed to the owner queue, never replied to.
