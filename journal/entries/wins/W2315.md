## W2409 · 2026-09-24 · The intake pipeline works end to end

From three photographs to three live CHECKED_IN work orders (WO26092412720/721/722) — case file,
schema-valid sync record, dry run, push, and a production read-back — in one session, with two
defects found in the push tool and fixed on the way through.

What made it safe rather than fast: every phone number was matched against production and Dialpad
before it was written; the one ambiguous surname was resolved against the shop's own customer data
instead of guessed; the one device that was never photographed turned out to be identifiable from
the phone record (she called about her iPad's charging port and then asked where the drop-off closet
is); and nothing was quoted because nothing was priced.

Reusable shape: `lptcase.py new <slug> --device … --name … --phone …` -> fill the case file ->
write the sync record -> `--push-only <file> --dry-run` (must say CREATE) -> `--push-only <file>` ->
SELECT the customer and order back. ~5 minutes per intake once the readings are verified.
