**W6491 · 2026-10-15 · Classifying money movement is provably P&L-neutral; classifying spend is not - so the statement lines can be settled without the owner.**

Measured 2026-10-16 on the CFO ledger, round 7. Of 29,862 USD parked as unexplained, a slice were bank statement lines whose descriptor names the CARD ISSUER and whose operation is a payment (AMERICAN EXPRESS DES:ACH PMT INDN:LAKEWOOD LLC, CHASE CRD EPAY). The resolver parked them as no_merchant_name - honest, because there is no merchant string - but the counterparty is not a merchant, it is the business paying its own card. Seven rules were written into the ENGINE's own rules.jsonl rather than writing categories directly (one writer preserved, applied by the same cfo-resolve.py --apply-rules cron runs; rc 0, rules 308, clusters 7, rows written 7). Unexplained 107 -> 100, dollars 33,172.34 -> 27,289.16, exactly the amount covered. The important part is the neutrality test: the statement artifacts before and after are identical where it matters - personal gross receipts 17,047.24 = 17,047.24 and NET INCOME 5,754.22 = 5,754.22; lpt gross receipts 55,578.91 = 55,578.91 and NET INCOME -45,091.09 = -45,091.09 - and the only band that moved is movement, lpt 48,910.97 -> 43,027.79. That is the generalisable rule: a movement classification cannot change net income, so it can be made from a bank descriptor alone, while a spend classification always can, so it needs evidence or the owner. The engine's own guards also fired and were not bypassed: the direction guard refused 2 rows for being spending-shaped against a credit, and one rule was held back by a conflict rather than overwriting. Separately the park store's own headline overstated open work by 6,982.10 (12 of 36 clusters were already fully categorised); cfo-park.py gained a prune that MOVES them to parked-resolved.jsonl with resolved_at and resolved_reason rather than deleting them, taking the store from 36 clusters / 29,862.31 to 24 / 22,880.21. Roughly 17,600 of what remains waits on three owner answers, not on engineering.
