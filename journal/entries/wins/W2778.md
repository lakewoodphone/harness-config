**W2778 · 2026-10-13 · The forward view had never been run: 7 months of runway, LPT cash 291.61, now on a clock.**

2026-10-13. Clause 4's forward view existed, was well built, and had NEVER RUN. It is now on a clock and
its answers are on the record.

WHAT I FOUND. `/opt/company/scripts/finance/forward-view.py` is a 36-MONTH forward statement for both
sides of the owner's life, and its docstring states the three questions it exists to answer: when the
personal funding requirement reaches zero, what the business owes him and when it could be repaid, and
what tax is owed forward and when it falls due. It gets its numbers by CALLING the house tools rather
than re-deriving - `generate_pnl()` from the monthly P&L, `compute_owner_loan_register()` from the loan
register, balances from `balance_snapshots.raw_json` - and it labels every figure MEASURED or MODELLED
with the assumption printed beside it. **The file it writes, `scripts/finance/forward-view.json`, did not
exist, and no cron anywhere mentioned it.** The forward third of the program's own definition of done had
never been produced once.

Two things there are worth the owner's attention and neither is new news to him - #228 already asks the
funding question - but the figures are sharper than anything previously written down: **the business holds
291.61 of cash and 1262.70 of available credit**, and the honest horizon is seven months.

FIXED: `/root/bin/forward-view-cron.sh` + a weekly cron at Sunday 06:31, six minutes after
finance-reconcile, so the forward statement is refreshed on the same day as the reconciliation it depends
on. 55 cron-wrap jobs now.

THE PATTERN, third instance tonight. A tool that exists, works, and has never been run is invisible in
exactly the same way as a tool that runs and writes into silence - both look like nothing is wrong. The
tax calendar (P3010) ran daily and wrote a file nobody read; this one wrote a file nobody had ever asked
it to write. **The check that finds both is the same and it is cheap: for each artifact the CFO is
supposed to produce, ask who reads it - and for each capability, ask when it last ran.**
