**W2734 · 2026-10-11 · 137: a fabricated zero became a measured zero; two latent self-resetting-baseline defects found by reading the second run; and the migration decided without touching the rollback anchor.**

WHAT WORKED, MEASURED. Three things from workstream 137, all 2026-10-11, all on `secratary-cloud`.

1. **A fabricated zero became a measured zero, and the fix is visible in the artefact the job itself
 produces.** Before: `lock_contention_7d … source=systemd journal (secretary-api)`, `journal_events =
 0`, `undercount_factor = 0.0`, while `journalctl -u company-api --since '7 days ago' | wc -l` = 2,615
 lines. After the patch, the report the unit wrote at 06:34:35Z reads:
 source=systemd journal (company-api) + error_log floor
 journal_source = systemd journal: company-api unit
 journal_unit_provenance = resolved by `systemctl cat company-api`
 journal_command = journalctl -u company-api --since '7 days ago' --no-pager -o cat
 journal_events = 0
 The number is still zero and THAT is the win: it is now a zero that was read (`grep -c 'database is
 locked'` = 0 over 2,615 lines) rather than a zero manufactured by a unit name that does not exist.
 **Why it generalises:** the repair is re-runnable, idempotent, exact-anchor, refuses if the deployed
 file is not the revision it was written against, backs the original up, and is kept beside its own
 109-line diff. Every claim in it is checkable by the next session with two commands.

2. **The port found two latent defects in the thing being ported, by reading the second run.** The office
 `estate-monitor`'s `disk_util_pct` had been dead for 38.9 hours (`age=140038s` in its own log) because
 its io_ticks baseline was written only inside the branch that required a baseline. Fixed, and measured
 working on the authority: `disk_util_pct = 56.54 %`. The same self-resetting-baseline shape was caught
 in my own `authority_db_growth_mb_h` before it shipped. **The lesson that produced both: a delta or
 rate metric is proven by its SECOND run; the first is always NODATA and that is correct, which is
 exactly why the defect hides there.**

3. **The whole migration decision was implemented without touching the rollback anchor.** Two ported and
 proven, four retired with evidence, and the ten office unit files and six scripts read with `cat`/
 `grep`/`ls`/`ps`/`df`/`systemctl show` only. The office box load stayed 0.01–0.42 through every
 reading, and its crontab, its database and its five live timers are exactly as they were found. The
 retirement list is written down with exact `disable --now` commands and was deliberately not run.

4. **A single reading justified a port that a translation would have missed.** The monitor's first run on
 the authority immediately produced `oom_kills_24h=1` — the kernel's global OOM kill of `company-api`
 at 05:47:24Z, `constraint=CONSTRAINT_NONE`, `anon-rss 7,544,976 kB` — against
 `MemoryMax=infinity` and `MemoryPeak=6.673 GiB` on a 7.7 GiB box, plus `public_health_ttfb_ms=2422`
 where the laptop had measured 221 ms. Neither is visible from any office-era metric.
