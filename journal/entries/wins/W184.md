**W184 · 2026-09-16 · Proved the phone question card works with a probe against the running app, and made a stale tab self-heal.**

scripts/question-card-live-probe.py mounts the card's real markup in the real app's composer seat, at a phone viewport, with a simulated keyboard band, and reads back the measurement plugin-mobile leaves on window.__dshPhoneCard.

Before the fix: the sheet is anchored to the layout viewport; with a band of 240-660 on a 393x852 screen the title sits at y=39, outside the band — the options are visible and the question is not, which is word-for-word what the owner reported.

After: the inline repair pins the frame to the band and the title lands at y=279 with Submit at y=608 and repaired=true, at both 393x852 and 393x640. passes end to end.

And the fix can no longer be lost to a stale tab: the layer carries a token, plugin-mobile compares it with the served copy every minute and on visibilitychange, re-applies newer bytes in place, and reloads only when no question is pending and nothing is focused or typed. 5 new assertions in packages/plugin-mobile/test/client-smoke.mjs pin that behaviour (32 checks total, all passing).
