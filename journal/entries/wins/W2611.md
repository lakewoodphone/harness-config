**W2611 · 2026-09-30 · The fan-out runs every minute now: a freed gate is claimed in two minutes instead of waiting for the five-minute tick.**

ROUND 17, 2026-09-30. WHY, from a measurement not a hunch: at 16:00:04 the pre-flight guard skipped while wake-nextclaim reported the earliest gate at 16:00:09 - five seconds later - so nothing could claim until the 16:05 tick, and with 11 rows gated and 23 ledger items waiting the tick rate was the binding constraint on how fast a freed row is claimed. Running every minute was expensive before the guard existed, because each idle tick started a dispatcher that claimed nothing and logged a line that read like a failure. HOW IT WAS APPLIED, and this part matters more than the change: the crontab was never piped from a PowerShell-written script again. crontab -l was written to a FILE, sed operated on that file, the proposal was checked (line count unchanged, exactly one every-5 line gone, exactly one every-1 line present) and only then installed with crontab FILE. THE SAFETY GATE CAUGHT MY OWN ERROR: the first proposal was refused because my replacement had nine star fields instead of five - I double-counted by capturing the four time fields and also writing five stars - and the script printed REFUSED and installed nothing, leaving the live crontab untouched. VERIFIED IN PRODUCTION, five minutes later: 2026-09-30T16:03:05Z releasing wake 158 (project:housekeeping:20260930#3), the very subject whose gate expired at 16:00:09 and which under the old schedule would have waited for 16:05; wake 163 released at 16:03:07; fanout: shift #4 pid=2561275 CLAIMED; started 4 this run, 5 in flight at target; and the 16:04 tick correctly reported 5 in flight, at target - starting nothing more. Diff: exactly one line, 59c59, line count 100 before and after. LESSON WORTH KEEPING: a change to a shared file installs only after a diff and a count check, and that gate is what turned a wrong edit into a refused proposal instead of a broken crontab.
