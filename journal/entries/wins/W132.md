**W-new · 2026-09-15 · A reminder the system marked `sent` now actually reaches the owner, and the badge's one line shows what is live.**

Measured end to end with a real due reminder (probe id 398, then cancelled and dismissed with a
note, since it was my test and not his):

| step | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| `reminders` rows with `status='sent'`, channel `dashboard` | marked delivered, rendered by nothing | delivered **and** queued |
| `_run_reminder_poll` result | `{checked, sent, delivered, sms_sent, failed}` | `{..., queued: 1, ...}` |
| what the owner sees | nothing (no surface reads `delivery_channel`) | a `held` `owner_message_queue` row, `reason=reminder:398` |
| the badge's waiting line | two six-day-old Google security alerts, for ever | `[Netlify] Action needed: ableTelSolutions...` \| `🌙 Evening briefing...` -- the live items |

The queue does the rest: it is what the badge renders, it dedups a re-polled reminder into one row
with `repeat_count`, it ages a reminder out after 24 h, and it can never become an outbound SMS
because in-app reminders are enqueued `held`.

The second half is the part that would have been missed: wiring the channel was **not** enough.
The reminder reached the queue and the badge still showed the same two stale criticals, because
the sample ordering was `reason, urgency, created_at` and urgency outranked everything. Only after
freshness was put first did the owner's own reminder appear on the line he reads.
