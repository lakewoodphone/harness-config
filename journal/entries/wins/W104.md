The host code root was a Windows-only literal, so the company could not see his repositories

WHAT: local_context.py held `_CODE_FOLDER = pathlib.Path("C:/Users/ezabz/Code")`.
On the authority that path does not exist, so build_local_context_summary() and
get_repo_file_content() found nothing. The same literal, in one shape or another,
appeared in source_watchers, next_stage_runtime, the _LEGACY_QB_REPO fallback of
five bank services, the company blueprint, managed workflows and the autopilot's two
one-shot utilities. The correct rule was already in the codebase — chat_action_repos.py
has used "%USERPROFILE%/Code on nt, ~/repos otherwise" all along — it was simply not
shared.

FIX: app/paths.py — app_root(), code_root(), accounting_db_path(). code_root() prefers
the platform convention and then PROBES the alternatives, because the failure mode of a
wrong guess is silence: a scanner that finds no repositories and reports success.
12 sites repointed across 11 files; app/accounting_paths.py (created earlier the same
evening) removed in favour of the single module.
