## WHY THIS TOOK FOUR ROUNDS, AND WHAT THAT COST

The fix itself was seven lines. The delay was entirely in **establishing that a failing measurement
meant what it appeared to mean.** Every intermediate reading of `122x20` was a measurement of pre-fix
code, and the only way to know that was to check the served bundle for the class rather than infer from
the number. That check is what stopped me from concluding the fix was broken three separate times.

**L3769 is the rule, and it earned its place twice in one session:** merged is not live. Check that the
artifact you measured is the one you changed, before reading a failing number as a failing fix.

**And the counter-lesson, which is cheaper to apply:** once the trigger is a hash rather than a clock,
stop polling. Three of those four rounds were spent re-measuring old code.
