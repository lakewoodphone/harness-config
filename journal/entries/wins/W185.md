## W185 — the phone question card is proved working against the running app, and a stale tab now heals itself (correction to W184)

at a phone viewport, with a simulated keyboard band, and reads back the measurement `plugin-mobile`
leaves on `window.__dshPhoneCard`.

**Before the fix:** the sheet is anchored to the layout viewport, so with a band of 240–660 on a

question is not, which is word for word what the owner reported.

reloads only when no question card is mounted and nothing is focused or typed. Five new assertions
in `packages/plugin-mobile/test/client-smoke.mjs` pin that behaviour — 32 checks, all passing.
