**W2809 · 2026-10-12 · The label route in lpt-label-printing section 6 worked first try: identify, dry-run, LOOK, print.**

**2026-10-12, ticket 2657 (Schreiber, Dell Inspiron 15 3520).** Printed the first shelf label for this job following `lpt-label-printing` section 6 instead of the deployed wrapper, and it worked on the first attempt: **two ssh calls, no media error, no printer freeze.**

**What was actually run.** One ssh call to `secratary-ts` that (a) ran `lpt-label find` to make the device identify itself, and (b) ran the **checkout** renderer rather than the stale copy on the anchor - `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 /home/zabz/.local/lib/lpt-label/venv/bin/python /home/zabz/code/lpt-ops/bin/lpt-label print - --dry-run --out /tmp/x.png` - with the label JSON piped in over ssh stdin from a local file. The PNG was then copied back and looked at. A second call removed `--dry-run`.

**Measured result.** Printer identified as **Brother QL-820NWB, serial H3G354844, firmware 1.25, at 192.168.50.60, media "Not Empty 29mm", state READY**. Dry run 25.4 mm of tape; the PNG showed the masthead, `#2657` big, customer, device, drop date and three fault lines of 15-22 characters all inside the width with nothing truncated; the print then reported `sent to 192.168.50.60 ... 25.4mm of tape, image /tmp/lpt-label-20261012T201632.png`.

**Why this is the counter-example to P3009** (20 render attempts on the wrong axis while the owner waited): the cheap sequence is **identify by model first, dry-run to a PNG, LOOK at the PNG, then print** - and never route a shop label through a Windows printer driver. Designing the lines to 22 characters and checking them in the image is what prevents the silent truncation, which raises no error and no warning.

**What may be claimed afterwards, and nothing more:** the printer was identified by model and serial, it was READY with the 29 mm roll, and the transport accepted the job. Tape is proven by the tape - the roll in the printer or the person standing next to it. `did_print` is false by construction on this model and proves nothing either way.
