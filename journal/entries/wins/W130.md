**W130 · 2026-09-15 · A parallel sweep's own contradiction was caught before any money moved - quote FRUs from the OEM manual, not the seller.**

Evidence: Shlesinger ThinkPad X201 screen case, 2026-09-15, machine lakewooechsmini.

Two sourcing passes ran in parallel on the same question. The second one recommended a $12 Amazon
cable (ASIN B079GPWVYX) as the cheapest option, while the same report's own caveat note said that
part - FRU 44C9909 - is the CCFL assembly and wrong for an LED X201. The recommendation contradicted
its own finding. Catching it cost one cross-check against Lenovo's HMM and saved a wrong-part order
plus an RMA on a 15-year-old machine.

What worked, in order: (1) take the FRU from the OEM manual, not from the seller; (2) when two sources
disagree, the one that cites the manual wins; (3) label anything read through a relay or search snippet
as "reported", never "verified" - the relay that produced the Amazon figures 403s on a second attempt.

Outcome recorded: nothing ordered, nothing sent to the customer, cable 44C9991 identified in stock at
$21.46 (1 unit) and four purchasable panels at $65.62-$79.48.
