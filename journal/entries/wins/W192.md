**W192 · 2026-09-16 · The Mac Mini is now fleet-placeable, and the reclaim job is reversible with one command.**

The darwin memory reader landed during the hygiene work, so the node went from `freeMiB null`,
`maxChildren 0`, `fleet false` to `freeMiB 7960-8176`, `totalMiB 16384`, `physical 10`,
`swapUsedPct 58.8`, `maxChildren 12`, `fleet true`. By the frozen formula its effective slots
are min(memory, 10 x 0.75) = 7. A third fleet-capable node, from a machine that this morning had
nothing to do with the mesh.
The reclaim job is a launchd daemon (300 s, runs as root, no KeepAlive) with an explicit TARGETS
list, console-user scope only, an idle threshold of 1800 s, idempotency, and a log line on every
path including every decline. Its off switch is ONE command and was demonstrated live: `launchctl
bootout` removed it while the engine and gate stayed running. Both branches were forced and both
logged: acted (idle 4683 s, 993 MB released, reclaimable 7.54 -> 7.94 GiB, compressor 7.38 -> 6.57
GiB of physical) and declined (idle below threshold). Scope proven rather than asserted: another
user's 7 Chrome processes were checked before AND after and were untouched.
