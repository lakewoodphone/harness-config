## WIN — the customer site's seam to the broker, checked from inside the site container

The broker has been verified many times this session; the site less so, and the seam between them not
at all. A site that cannot reach the broker renders perfectly and fails the moment a parent signs in,
which is the kind of breakage that only shows up in front of a customer.

Measured 2026-10-05, in the site container, not from a laptop:

 SITE_BROKER_BASE_URL=http://lpt_filter_ai_api:8443 (the Docker-network name)
 site -> broker /health -> 200 {"status":"ok"}
 site -> broker /client-profile/ios -> 200

And the public surface, from the internet: `/`, `/onboarding`, `/account`, `/terms`, `/privacy`,
`/how-it-works`, `/limitations`, `/parent-notice` all 200; the site container is `Up (healthy)`.

Two notes for the next person:
- The variable is `SITE_BROKER_BASE_URL`. My first probe looked for `LPT_BROKER_BASE_URL`, got
 `(unset)` and reported a failure that did not exist - the same wrong-instrument mistake as the other
 four this session, caught only because the failure text said `unknown url type: '(unset)/health'`
 rather than a connection error.
- `/onboarding` and `/account` return ~1.9 KB unauthenticated and are near-identical in size. That is
 expected (a sign-in shell), and it is also why "the page returns 200" is not evidence that onboarding
 works. Confirming the flow itself needs a signed-in session, which needs the owner's credentials.
