**W2777 · 2026-10-13 · R4: a deposit account cannot be negative - and one savings account reconciles as a result.**

2026-10-13. Added the one invariant the reading judge was missing, and a savings account with 22
impossible months now reconciles.

WHAT WAS MISSING. `cfo-balances.py::mark_suspects` judges every reading before any transaction is
examined, and it had three rules: R1 sign mirrors, R2 two sources disagreeing inside 90 minutes, R3 one
source writing two values at one instant. It had NO rule about a balance that cannot exist.
`personal-amex-hysa-3181` is a SAVINGS account and 22 of its 25 monthly readings were NEGATIVE, from
-8506 to -8429, after which the chain jumped to +1337 on six cents of transactions.

R4, as added:
 a CHECKING or SAVINGS account cannot hold a negative balance. Such a reading is a sign or magnitude
 error, it is marked suspect with that reason, and every chain that passes through it is wrong.
The line is drawn deliberately: negative is legitimate for a credit card (a credit balance) and possible
for a checking account (an overdraft), so R4 applies only where it is IMPOSSIBLE rather than merely odd.

MEASURED EFFECT, which is what makes this a fix and not a theory:
 before anchored 119 derived 164 DISAGREEMENTS 69 warned 2
 after anchored 118 derived 165 DISAGREEMENTS 68 warned 1
and the account itself, which is the part that matters:
 before closings -8506 -8502 -8498 -8494 ... -8429 then +1337 (impossible, 22 bad months)
 after closings 1261 1265 1269 1273 ... (12.61 and rising by pennies)
The chain re-derives from a positive anchor and now describes a small dormant savings account earning
four cents a month - which is exactly what its `Interest Payment` transactions of 0.04 to 0.34 say it is.
Its disagreements fall from two to one.

A CONFOUND I CHECKED BEFORE BELIEVING IT. My first test passed `--mail-max-age-days 400` while the cron
passes `400` by default - so the numbers matched, but I read the wrapper to confirm that rather than
assume it, because a test that differs from production in one argument proves nothing about production.

ADOPTED THROUGH THE SANCTIONED PATH. Ran `/usr/local/bin/finance-reconcile.sh` itself - read-only against
the ledger, the same wrapper the weekly clock uses - which rebuilt `balances-live.json` and
`reconciliation-worklist.md`. Backup of the tool at `/root/cfo-balances.py.bak-r4-20261013T065128Z`.

THE GENERAL POINT, which is why R4 is worth more than one account. The program printed `derived 164`
beside `anchored 119` as if a computed reading and a read one carried the same weight - and this account
showed 21 derived months can be wrong together, silently, because one anchor was impossible and nothing
asked. **An invariant that is cheap and always true is worth more than an analysis that is expensive and
sometimes right.**
