Measured in two steps, each one naming the next:

1. A **per-file descriptor probe** over the whole suite: +8,160 across 199 files, worst
 `test_tool_contracts` +378, `test_lpt_analytics` +368, `test_work_generation` +307.
2. A **descriptor-type probe** at session end, after closing the database connections: **285 sockets and
 139 eventpoll descriptors** — leaked asyncio event loops — with SQLite down to the 14 handles of the
 real database.

So two fixtures in `tests/conftest.py` (`f6abcb1f`, preserved):
- `_close_cached_db_connections` (per test) calls the app's own `close_db_connections()` — the app has
 had that helper all along; the tests never called it, and every `tmp_path` database is a new key in a
 per-`(url, thread)` cache;
- `_close_leaked_event_loops` (per module) closes any loop that is not running, taking its selector,
 self-pipe and client sockets with it.

**Verified end to end**: with the `RLIMIT_NOFILE` raise temporarily neutralised, on the default 1024
limit, the full suite reports 2,721 passed / 16 skipped / **0 failed / 0 errors** in 191 s. The raise
stays in conftest as head-room rather than a crutch.
