BEFORE, measured 2026-10-15 on /root/authority.db read-only, 7 days: 176 sessions carry TOOL_LOOP_DEADLOCK; 36 name an action that is `temporarily disabled` and 28 a `file not found`. THE CAUSE was a MISSING STRING, not a missing branch. `_NON_FATAL_CB_FAILURE_PATTERNS` in app/chat_action_resilience.py drives BOTH `_should_record_circuit_breaker_failure` and `_should_retry_action_failure` -- they are one predicate. So the failure was counted, the action was disabled at ten strikes, and the identical-failure guard spent three strikes on something that can never succeed.

THE FIX: those three strings added to the five `repo_*` pattern tuples. Three strings, no control flow, no new predicate.
