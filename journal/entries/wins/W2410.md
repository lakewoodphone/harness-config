**W2xxx · Two interval-change thresholds written into the owner health record in advance both fired — because the file refused to reassure itself from a stale value.**

Measured 2026-09-25 (ZABZ-YOGA). Evidence: `docs/personal/health/recurrent-febrile-episodes.md` §28.4 (written 2026-09-23) vs §34 (result filed 2026-09-25), branch `docs/owner-health-2026-09-25`.

THE MECHANISM, and it is the whole point. On 2026-09-23 the record acquired three historical lab draws and could easily have written: "we have a normal CBC from ten weeks ago, so the liver is probably fine." It did not, because **the owner pushed back on exactly that sentence** — *"that test was a few months ago, how do you know it's still normal"* — and the paragraph was corrected the same day (§28.4 item 5, documented in its own correction block). What replaced it were **two named, falsifiable thresholds**:

| Written 2026-09-23, before the result existed | 2026-09-25 result |
|---|---|
| neutrophils materially above **73 %** or absolute lymphocytes materially below **1.0** | **80 %** and **0.8** — fired |
| ALT materially above **26**, or **AST rising with it** | **51**, with AST **46** up from 22 — fired |

§28.4 item 5 also named the mechanism in advance: "~650 mg-equivalent days of acetaminophen on minimal food plus dehydrating fever and heavy sweating — **a rising transaminase is a live and plausible possibility, not an academic one.**"

WHY THIS IS A WIN AND NOT A COINCIDENCE. The value was not in being right; it was in **converting a vague reassurance into a number that could be checked**. A record that had said "probably fine" would have had nothing to compare against, and the same 80 % / 0.8 / 46 / 51 would have read as new and alarming instead of as the predicted interval change.

THE TRANSFERABLE RULE. **When a record has a stale baseline in hand, the honest move is not reassurance and not alarm — it is a written threshold with a direction.** "If X moves past Y, that is a real change" survives the arrival of data; "probably fine" does not. And the correction that made it possible came from the owner, not from an agent: **when he contradicts a reassuring paragraph, the correction is the asset — record what he said and rebuild the paragraph around it.**

SCOPE NOTE. The prediction fired on **two of the two** items it named, and the entry also records the reverse: §28.4's other warnings (thyroid normal ×3, no infection signal in July) were **not** contradicted. Both directions are recorded, which is what makes the next prediction worth anything.
