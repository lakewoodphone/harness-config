**W180 · 2026-09-16 · The engine has a health surface, a list_agents that cannot hang, and an admission governor — with the numbers.**

Three defects, one host-plane plugin (harness-config/packages/plugin-health, committed a1c6cfd). All three verified by observation, not by configuration.

1. HEALTH SURFACE — live. `GET /healthz` on an isolated engine (own DSH_HOME, :3098): 200 with loop lag p50=1ms p95=17ms max=8923ms over 194 samples, rss 199 MiB, physical 16024/32373 MiB, commit 20638 free of 44149, 405 processes / 6496 threads, engine 17 threads / 268 handles, tool-call runners 0, MCP 0, sessions 0, plus the listing and governor sections below. Cost of the instrument: 6 probe spawns in 30 s (one per 5 s interval), 1 tick dropped while a probe was in flight, 0 failures, 933 ms per probe, 0-1 ms to read the snapshot. The live engine on :3099 still returns 404 — it predates the bundle link and needs one restart.

2. list_agents DEADLINE + CACHE — built, registered in a real engine, not yet on the live engine. The shipped tool declares no `timeoutMs`, so the timeout policy passes it through untouched and nothing can ever time it out; and every call re-walks the session corpus. Measured from the session LOGS (not a wrapper): 389 dirs / 214 MB, calls at 644/940/4285 ms, one matched call/result pair 21 minutes apart. The replacement keeps the name, arguments, schema and rendering, adds a `Promise.race` deadline (so even a service that ignores the abort cannot hang it), a declared `timeoutMs` for the hard backstop, and a 3 s single-flight cache. Engine-side proof: /healthz reports listAgents.registry=registered, registeredTimeoutMs=10000, registeredByHealthPlugin=true.

3. ADMISSION GOVERNOR — live as a CLI and as a tool. Cross-process proof with 30 real child processes and a budget of 5: exactly 5 GRANTED, 25 QUEUED with a position, no duplicate slot, one reaper wins the rename, slots reusable after the reap (15/15 checks). Cross-surface proof: a CLI-acquired lease appeared in the engine's own health surface as `governor: inUse=1 ... pid 32252 cli-proof`. Budget is derived per host at call time: 15943 MiB free - 3885 reserved / 160 MiB per slot = 75, capped at 24, floored at 4 so it never refuses.

58 unit tests + 15 cross-process checks pass. The one thing that cannot be claimed yet: the live engine's numbers, because it has not been restarted.
