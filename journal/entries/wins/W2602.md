**W — Counting the tells deterministically proved a voice change that the judged score could not.**

2026-09-29, Chip's register. The 33-case golden-set harness returned the register change as
a REGRESSION on the first after-run (87.87% -> 85.2%, hard gates 9 -> 13) and the change was
minutes from being reverted. The harness's own noise (two runs of the same config, 2026-09-25)
turned out to be 21 of 33 cases moving on a repeat, 9 of them by >10 points, worst 66.

What worked instead, and should be reached for first next time: count the specific tells in
the replies the harness had ALREADY generated, with no judge in the loop. Two independent
after-runs against the baseline:

 emoji 2 -> 0 and 0
 exclamation marks 1 -> 0 and 0
 agreement opener 1 -> 0 and 0
 memory-as-flat-fact 2 -> 6 and 5
 basement/hardware device 11 -> 10 and 13
 mean chars when speaking 145 -> 132 and 144

Every cute/helpful tell the independent audit had named went to zero, and the wanted device
roughly tripled, stably across both runs. That is a before/after a future session can
reproduce in seconds from saved JSON, with no API calls and no LLM in the loop.

The general shape: when a change is about a STYLE you can name, you can usually count it.
Reach for the counters before the judge - and if you must use a judged score, measure its
noise floor on a repeat run first, or the number is decoration.
