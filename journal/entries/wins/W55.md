**A sync that needs a login now reaches the owner, verified at the surface he opens**

**What was wrong.** Five syncs — BoA, Chase, Amex, Amazon, eBay — each had a `needs_mfa` branch containing
nothing but a log line: *"will surface in next owner check-in"*. It enqueued nothing. The promise was
false, and the cost was measured earlier this session: `amazon_sync` and `ebay_sync` sat at **42
consecutive failures each** with the real reason — *"Amazon login requires MFA"*, *"Authentication
failed"* — existing only in a log nobody reads. That is why six tasks were created for these syncs and
closed without fixing them: the work needs a human login, no agent can do it, and nobody was told.

**The fix.** `_notify_owner_sync_needs_login()` enqueues one owner message naming the sync and the reason,
with `status="held"` deliberately — a held row is counted by the attention-debt check and rendered by the
badge, and it **cannot become an outbound SMS without a deliberate change**, which matters because the SMS
kill switch is the owner's decision and this code must not manufacture sendable messages on its own. The
queue's dedup keeps a repeatedly-needing sync to one row with a repeat count instead of the 74 rows the
breaker nudge produced.

**Verified at the surface, not at the enqueue — and the first attempt failed that test.** The first
version used `urgency="high"`. The row was created, held, and correctly reasoned… and the owner saw
nothing, because the attention-debt check counts `urgency == 'urgent'` **exactly** and the badge renders
only that summary line. The queue grew 158 → 160 and the badge did not move at all. Changed to
`urgency="urgent"`, the two existing rows flipped, and the badge now reads:

 owner-attention debt: 7 critical message(s) held; 34 urgent message(s) held; ...
 ^^ was 32 before this fix

So the delivery is demonstrated end to end: probe → enqueue → **the number on the surface he opens moved**.
10 tests pass on the authority's own tree; `/health` 200; deployed 16:5x Z.

**The lesson, and it is the sixth instance of one shape in this session.** *A stored row that moves no
surface the owner opens has not delivered anything.* I have now made this mistake in four different
costumes: a log field that could not contain the answer, a status whose writer I never checked, an
instrument written to a variable that is never persisted, and a message enqueued at a priority the summary
does not count. Each time the fix was sound and the **verification was aimed at the wrong layer** —
at the write instead of at the read.

**Also recorded: a check of mine has now tripped on its own prose three times.** `port-sync-breaker.py`,
`test_sync_login_reaches_the_owner.py` and `port-sync-login-notify.py` each asserted that a phrase was
*absent* from a file, while the very comment or docstring explaining the bug quoted that phrase. The
correction is always the same: assert on the **call** (`logger.info(...)` matching a pattern), never on the
bare string. This is L232.
