**W2759 · 2026-10-13 · 14 of the owner's answers existed only in a mutable table; they are in the append-only record now.**

MEASURED AND FIXED 2026-10-13. The owner's own answers were living in a mutable table column.

`owner_decision_queue` is the single source of truth for what is waiting on him, and `answer` writes
his words into a column that a later `amend` can overwrite. The journal is append-only. **Nothing
connected the two**, so a decision he had given survived only as a table cell and was lost exactly
when the agent that recorded it forgot to write the entry -- and silently, because the row still
reads `answered` and every check says it is recorded.

MEASURED: of 488 rows and 67 carrying an answer, **14 had no copy in the journal anywhere.**
`journal/tools/reconcile-owner-answers.py` found them and wrote them through the sanctioned
`journal.py append`, D3129-D3142. Among them:

 * #470 -- 268 payments worth **28,142.70** with no work order, and the corrected recommendation
 that came out of building the back-link;
 * #294 -- a customer quote: *"No, the total will be 6550 goes towards it."*
 * #102, #103, #109 -- three different customers whose texts were to be left unanswered:
 *"Leave the line silent until the owner is back Tuesday."*
 * #231, #233 -- bank charges 639.13 and 632.00 attributed to a card and a store;
 * #256, #257 -- two Kosher Waze product decisions, one of which is a single word: *"APPROVED"*.

VERIFIED IDEMPOTENT: the re-run reports **0 answers with no journal copy**. The tool is dry-run by
default, never writes to the queue, and writes only through `journal.py append`.

WHAT IS STILL MISSING, and it is the other half: the reconciliation is a command a session must
remember to run. **The durable fix is at the moment of answering** -- `owner-queue.py answer` should
append the entry itself, so the copy exists before the row can be edited. Until then, run
`reconcile-owner-answers.py --apply` after any session that answers queue rows, or schedule it.
