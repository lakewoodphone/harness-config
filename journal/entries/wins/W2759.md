**W2759 · 2026-10-13 · His reply would have been filed against the wrong merchant: the matcher never read the merchant.**

2026-10-13. The answer matcher would have filed his reply against the wrong question, and having two
questions open is what exposed it.

THE BUG. `cfo_clarify.match_reply` ordered its decisions as: (1) the reference his reply names, else
(2) the OLDEST waiting question, recorded as `oldest-open-fallback`. There was no step for "his reply
names the merchant". With ONE question open that is harmless. Measured on the live store, TWO were
open - newegg 362.51 (`ask-1791862240`) and polka dot 299.00 (`ask-1791867617`) - so a reply reading
"the polka dot one was shop supplies" would have been recorded as an answer about NEWEGG.

WHY THAT IS WORSE THAN LEARNING NOTHING. An answer writes a durable rule (the objective's own words:
"answers persisted as durable rules so nothing is asked twice"). A rule saying newegg is shop
supplies, learned from a sentence about polka dot, teaches the ledger something false and stops the
merchant ever being asked again. The docstring defended the fallback on the grounds that it is
recorded with the open count "so the ambiguous case is visible in the record" - and that is true, but
visibility is not correctness. The row would have been visible AND wrong.

FIXED, additively. `_merchants_named()` matches his words against the merchant and cluster_key of the
waiting questions on word boundaries, and the fallback now only applies when that finds ZERO or TWO
OR MORE - i.e. a guess is never made. Exactly one match wins and is recorded as `via='merchant-named'`
with the open count.

VERIFIED against the real two open questions, with the controls that matter:
 "the polka dot one was shop supplies" -> via=merchant-named merchant=polka dot
 "newegg was shop supplies" -> via=merchant-named merchant=newegg
 "ask-1791867617 yes that was supplies" -> via=reference merchant=polka dot
 "yes" -> via=oldest-open-fallback merchant=newegg
 "no idea what you mean" -> via=oldest-open-fallback merchant=newegg
The first line is the bug, gone. The last two are the old behaviour deliberately preserved: an
ambiguous reply still falls back to the oldest, still records that it did, and is still visible.

WHAT PRODUCED THIS. The bug existed before today; what made it reachable was a DIFFERENT defect I
fixed in the previous round - the one-at-a-time gate was letting a second question through, so for
the first time in this store's life there were two open questions at once. Fixing one thing exposed
the next, and the second one was only reachable in a state the first one had never allowed. Worth
remembering before assuming the matcher had ever been exercised with two open questions.
