**W2745 · 2026-10-13 · Reconciliation was solving half the problem it could see, because the fast path skipped the mail.**

2026-10-13. Same ledger, same window, one flag different, and the picture nearly doubles.

MEASURED, over the identical 24-month window and the identical ledger:
 `cfo-balances.py --build --no-mail` anchored 59 disagreements 21
 `cfo-balances.py --build` anchored 119 disagreements 69

`balance_snapshots` only starts 2026-07-01, so a snapshot-only reconciliation can judge five months
of a two-year window. The statement and balance mail reaches back much further - it anchored 25
months on `personal-chase-9650` alone - and with those anchors the run sees gaps that were not
merely unreported but INVISIBLE: `personal-chase-9650 2025-03` (5,347.72),
`lpt-chase-7542 2025-07` (2,740.61), `personal-amex-22005 2026-05` (5,556.57).

The `--no-mail` flag was chosen for speed when the tool was first run. That is the trap worth
naming: a cheaper version of a measurement is not the measurement. A reconciliation that can judge
119 of 925 account-months is thin; one that can judge 59 is thinner still, and it also reported a
smaller number that looked like better news.

CHANGED. `/usr/local/bin/finance-reconcile.sh` (the Sunday 06:23 job) now reads mail, bounded by
`FINANCE_RECONCILE_MAIL_MAX_AGE_DAYS` (default 400). The worklist it writes gained an
`anchor_warned` column and the summary line now reports "69 disagreement(s), 119 anchored month(s),
2 from a warned anchor" so the reader can tell a real gap from a chain opening on a number the tool
distrusts.

THE HONEST LIMIT, unchanged and worth repeating: 642 of 925 account-months still have NO anchor at
all, and 164 are derived rather than read. Reconciliation is now the best it has ever been on this
estate and it is still not complete - for most of the window the answer is "cannot be judged", and
that is a different answer from "reconciled".
