**The mesh's last measured wall closed with one appended key line, and the proof is the child's own MESH-HOST line.**

`zabz-tech` could not ssh to itself, so a child the broker placed on it died at `exitCode 255` in 108 ms with no
`MESH-HOST:` line (ledger `remote-59e5ec01-…`, doc 106 §6). The cause was that machine's own public key
(`~/.ssh/zabz-tech-ed25519.pub`, `SHA256:tuQvTkv6fNSyfSjzylJpBkDkJo87YIeN9PEElUhotdE`) was absent from its own

**Two doors this opened, both measured, both worth as much as the fix:**
