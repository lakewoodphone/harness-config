**W · 2026-09-15 · The Newman infected-drive job: 12.97 GB proved onto the customer's drive, and the
gate is what made it safe.**

Owner brought in two flash drives a customer said "have a virus" (a claim that is usually wrong). It
was right. Measured outcome:

- **Diagnosed by evidence, not by symptom:** AutoIt USB-shortcut worm, `MicrosoftSecurity.exe`
 SHA-256 `51fe204c…6025` **identical on both drives**, ClamAV `Win.Malware.Autoit-9880096-0`, and 88
 decoy `.lnk` files whose command line is the worm launcher. `fatattr` showed the worm's own folders
 hidden+system while **none** of the customer's files were hidden — which corrected the story I'd
 nearly told ("it hid their folders").
- **Nothing was damaged:** 609 source files in, 505 delivered, and **every one of the 104 excluded is a
 worm artifact or a Windows system file** — proved per-file by SHA-256 (0 mismatches, 0 extras,
 0 unexplained losses on both drives), not asserted.
- **334 MP3s with 0 bad headers.** The 0-byte `!!! ALL YOUR FILES ARE ENCRYPTED !!!.TXT` was a scare
 prop, shown to be one by the data, not by the file name.
- **Delivered on the customer's own drive** (their plan, owner-approved): both drives wiped exFAT
 (`NEWMAN`, `NEWMAN-2`), 12,973,709,026 bytes written, then **the drive's own manifest recomputed from
 the drive** and matched — plus `clamscan -r` 0 infected and **0** `.lnk`/`.a3x`/`autorun*` on either
 drive. Hebrew filenames survived the round trip (`תשפ''ה/אלול א-אכל כחושה.MP3`).
- The worm itself was retained as evidence (41 files + hashes) before the wipe.

**What actually made it safe:** the destructive stage was gated on an independent verification, and
that gate failed closed **twice** on bugs of mine (a word-split exclude list that compared nothing; a
comparison that ran before the copy-side manifest existed and read that as 454 missing files). Both
times the answer was "nothing formatted, nothing written", and the correct result was reached on the
third pass. Cost of the gate: a few minutes. Cost without it: two customer drives wiped on a false
"verified".

**Keep:** gating irreversible work on a check that fails closed, on a marker file with the numbers in
it, plus re-reading drive serials immediately before the erase.
