**W7132 · 2026-10-20 · Round 26-27: the journaling performance cliff under concurrent sessions.**

Measured 2026-10-21 03:05-03:10Z on ZABZ-YOGA. With the index cache FRESH, journal.py status takes 0.97-1.03 s and append completes normally. Immediately after another session writes, the cache is invalidated and the next operation pays a full REBUILD: status 41.0 s, check 24.2 s, and append hung past the 45 s probe timeout, which is why rounds 21-26 could not be recorded. THE MECHANISM: every write invalidates the shared cache and every reader then rebuilds 4,751 entries and a 28 MB fts5 database from scratch. One writer is fine; two concurrent writers make every operation pay the rebuild, and append appears to hang because the rebuild happens before the write. THE WORKAROUND THAT WORKED: docs/text-channel/STATE-2026-10-21.md, git-tracked and committed after every round, carried the record while the journal was unusable. THE FIX FOR WHOEVER OWNS THE TOOL: an append must not depend on a cache rebuild, and a read that finds a stale cache should serve the stale one and rebuild in the background rather than block for 40 s.
