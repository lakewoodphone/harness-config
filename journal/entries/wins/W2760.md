**W2760 · 2026-10-13 · 83,000 dollars of the CFO's own money movements were unclassifiable because they are not merchants.**

2026-10-13. The CFO could not classify 83,000 dollars of its own money movements because the
descriptions are transfers, not merchants, and the classifier was trying to research them.

WHAT I FOUND. `cfo-shift` runs every 30 minutes and reports "still unclear: 36 cluster(s)". Its own
message for the biggest one: "we can see 'Bank of America', but no category: what this merchant
sells and whether the charge is business or personal". The largest unclear clusters, measured against
the live ledger:
 Zelle payment from ELIYAHU ZABROWSKY 62 rows 52,304.00 all credit
 AUTOPAY PAYMENT - THANK YOU 24 rows 22,347.94 all debit, on escape101-amex
 BKOFAMERICA MOBILE <date> DEPOSIT *MOBIL 52 rows 8,392.73 all credit
 Zelle payment from MEIR MARGULIES 8 rows 1,060.00
 Online Xfer Transfer from CK x8458 5 rows 2,370.00
 Zelle payment from ADEENA ZABROWSKY 4 rows 450.00
None of these is a merchant. They are money moving, and asking what a merchant sells about them can
never resolve. The estate already had rules of exactly this shape - `desc:zelle sent zelle eliyahu ->
Internal Transfers`, `desc:chase crd autopaybus -> Credit Card Payments` - so the mechanism was
there and the coverage was not.

WHAT I DID. Three rules, written through the estate's own writer (`cfo_rules.learn_rule` builds the
shape, `cfo_rules.write_rule` owns the append-only store) rather than by editing the file, with the
category map naming ONLY the entity I had verified and `default` left empty on purpose:
 key_contains "autopay payment" -> escape101 Credit Card Payment
 key_contains "bkofamerica mobile" -> lpt Gross Receipts - Non-Card
 key_contains "zelle payment from eliyahu" -> personal Internal Transfers
`key_contains` and not `key_exact` because the bank embeds the date in the description
(BKOFAMERICA MOBILE 05/05 3740245752), so an exact key would cover one day and never the next.
`default` is empty because lpt spells the same idea "Credit Card Payments" (plural) and a default
would file a category that entity does not hold. Every category name was checked against
`category_names(entity)` first - 44 for personal, 33 for lpt, 11 for escape101 - so none was invented.

THE ONE I DELIBERATELY DID NOT WRITE. The owner-Zelle rows are 52,304.00, which is close to the
53,420.64 owner-loan figure, and on lpt the same words would mean **Owner Loan In**. On `personal`
they mean his own money moving between his own accounts, which is Internal Transfers and matches the
estate's existing convention. I wrote the personal rule and did not write the lpt one, because which
of his own transfers are loans to the business is a question about his money that the loan figure
depends on, and it is his to answer rather than mine to infer.
