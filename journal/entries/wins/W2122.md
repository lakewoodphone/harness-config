2026-09-17, the Bachrach Family Link job. I fanned out three research subagents (the default for a
job with genuinely independent angles) and they were ALL still running when I needed to decide
what the answer was. Rather than idle or write "pending research", I went and verified the two
load-bearing facts myself:

- support.google.com/families/answer/7103028: "System apps can't have app limits placed on them."
 (fetched via firecrawl_scrape with maxAge 0 - the plain web_fetch returned a JS shell, i.e. an
 EMPTY RESULT, which is a refusal and not health)
- support.google.com/families/answer/7086922: the "Google Search" control is SafeSearch
 Filter/Blur/Off, and the same page names browsers and Google Go as bypasses.

Both are (a) vendor-documented. With those two, the answer - Family Link CANNOT do it, by design,
because the Google app is a system app; the fix is ADB - was established, and the procedure, case
file, sync record, commit and push were all completed while the subagents were still working.

It was also worth doing because the agents' value order was unknown: their briefs are
corroboration, not foundation, and I did not have to guess which.

Secondary win, same session: I caught myself shipping an UNVERIFIED claim. I had written a
Circle-to-Search settings path from memory into the procedure; Samsung's own page documents the
TRIGGER (touch and hold the home button / navigation handle) but no kill switch. I corrected the
document to say UNVERIFIED and told the technician to verify it on the phone. A guess that looks
as authoritative as a verified fact is exactly the failure mode this corpus keeps paying for.

Generalisable: delegate the breadth, but verify the ONE fact the deliverable hinges on yourself,
first, and date-stamp which is which.
