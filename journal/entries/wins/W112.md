**W112 · 2026-09-14 · The 53-silent-devices alarm was our own monitoring: 27 answered within seconds once we asked.**

Measured, and it closes the question. The fleet is fine; the alarm was our own monitoring.

WHAT I GOT WRONG AND WHAT IT COST
I told the owner 48 DRN devices had "never reported at all" and that a push to them would do
nothing. He corrected me from his own memory: about 50 of them were set up, profiles and Waze
pushed. He was right. The evidence I had was a gap in a database, and I reported it as a fact
about hardware - the same failure class as the stale-copy incident this project already has on
record. Two things made the mistake possible: I never asked "what has to happen for this number
to move?", and I did not test the alternative (send something and see) before concluding.

WHAT WAS ACTUALLY TRUE
- The MDM's command history on Postgres begins 2026-08-14 06:53:47. Everything before that is
 gone from every surviving store: fleet.db holds only Telnyx rows, NanoMDM's /app/db, /app/dbkv
 and /var/lib/nanomdm volumes are empty, and mdm_gateway.db is a 0-byte file.
- Our own batch records prove the fleet WAS provisioned in July: install-waze 57 device slots,
 lockdown 47, activation-lock 45, renumber 39, all "completed" with zero failures.
- A device only answers when something is SENT to it. The only regular traffic to this fleet was
 one hourly hotspot sweep aimed at ONE device (DRN 26, id_count=1 in NanoMDM's log).
- So `last_seen` was not a liveness measurement at all. It measured whether WE had contacted the
 phone. Untouched phones sat frozen at their enrolment moment and the dashboard called it silence.

THE MEASUREMENT
1. A read-only ProfileList to five devices (three with no replies on record, two controls):
 **DRN 2 answered in seconds** - a device I had labelled "never reported".
2. Then `POST /fleet/heartbeat` (new) sent a read-only DeviceInformation to every non-retired
 device not seen in 24 h: **54 asked, 0 enqueue failures, 27 answered within seconds** - DRN 1,
 8, 9, 14, 16-24, 28, 34-43, 46, 48, 53, 55, 9998, nearly all with no prior replies on record.
3. Fleet-wide: **28 of 65 heard from in the last hour, up from 0.** With the old single-device
 sweep it was impossible for the other 54 to ever look alive.

THE FIX, DEPLOYED
- `POST /fleet/heartbeat` in the fleet-api: read-only, cheap (no profile, no setting, no lock),
 one failing device cannot stop the sweep, and it skips retired devices by default.
- Scheduled every 6 hours on the MDM host with `stale_hours=24`, so every device is asked daily
 and "silent" now means the device did not answer.
- The UI no longer says "Never reported". It says "No reply on record" and states in full that
 this is not the same as never having been set up, names the missing history, and says the real
 consequence: we have no evidence the phone is reachable now.
- Retracted and reworded in the portal (0c8786068) and the fleet-api (b673d17a9); 122 tests pass.

WHAT THIS ENABLES
DRN 26 stopping at 2026-09-11 16:00 is now visible as what it is - one phone that went quiet -
instead of being buried under 53 false positives. The next useful step is an alert when a device
that answered yesterday does not answer today, which the heartbeat now makes computable.
