**W63 · 2026-09-14 · A year of the owner's business conversation is now searchable, and nothing was.**

Measured before any work: the authority held **127,651 SMS**, 12,473 calls, 4,052 transcripts, 3,036
voicemails and 24,000+ message rows — and **not one byte of it was full-text searchable**. The 21
Dialpad tables had no FTS index of any kind; the only indexes in the database covered email drafts,
memories, project knowledge and chat exports. Built `commsindex.py`: ten explicit, auditable extractors
feeding one FTS5 store — **174,622 communications indexed in ~33 s**, covering 2025-04-18 to 2026-09-14 —
with a trigram table so substrings inside phone numbers match. Search quality was treated as part of the
deliverable, so transcript artifact markers (`whole_call_summary_fragment`, `action_item_v2`) are
stripped at ingest: they were fragmenting phrase searches and making every result list look noisy.
A real query now returns the right SMS from a year ago.
