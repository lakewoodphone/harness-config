**W2787 · 2026-10-13 · Marketplace: the 90-day window needed a monthly full pass behind it, and now has one.**

2026-10-13. Clause 2's last durability gap: the daily sync searched 90 days and nothing ever re-ran the
unwindowed pass.

THE GAP. `/usr/local/bin/marketplace-orders-sync.sh` documents its own window:
 "WINDOW. `--window-days 90` searches the last 90 days of mail rather than the whole history. The store
 is a permanent accumulator, so a narrower window loses nothing and keeps the daily run cheap; 90 days
 rather than 30 so a clock that is down for two months still catches up. Set MARKETPLACE_ORDERS_FULL=1
 to force an unwindowed backfill."
The reasoning is sound for mail that arrives while the store exists - and it has one hole. **An order whose
mail arrived outside a 90-day window, or which the parser missed during it, is lost permanently once the
mailbox ages past that window**, because nothing ever ran the unwindowed pass again. And the flag for it
existed, documented, and no cron used it: `grep -c MARKETPLACE_ORDERS_FULL` in the crontab returned 0.

WHY IT MATTERS MORE THAN IT LOOKS. The store's range is 2018-10-21 to 2026-10-12 and holds 671 orders, so a
full pass DID happen at some point - it is how this session found 585 purchases and 725 item rows. But the
sync had NO CRONTAB ENTRY AT ALL until tonight (W2779), which means the 90-day window was, in effect,
indefinite: mail simply accumulated un-ingested until a human ran it. **The window is only a safety margin
if the thing it guards is running.** Now that the daily run is scheduled, the window is real - and a real
window needs a periodic full pass behind it or it silently becomes the whole mechanism.

FIXED: `20 6 1 * *` monthly, `MARKETPLACE_ORDERS_FULL=1`, one day a month, after the daily run and before
the weekly reconcile. 57 cron-wrap jobs. The flag the script already shipped is now used.
