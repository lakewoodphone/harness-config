## Result 2026-10-22 16:15Z: with AllowFailover=false the dictionary download fails — the resolver was the gate, the failover key was the hole

Test on DRN 2004 (serial `F4HCTN35N72K`), the phone the owner had on the bench. Change under test: `AllowFailover` true -> false in `com.abletel.waze.sirifilterdns`, pushed 16:07:14, Acknowledged 16:07:18 (`F93B7BD5-…`), one DNS payload on the device, same UUID (see `D9354`).

**Observed, from the resolver's own log (`docker logs waze-mdm-dns-filter-1`, 25-minute window, read 16:15:52Z):**
- The device kept asking for the payload host and was refused: `blocked updates.cdn-apple.com` at 16:14:22, 16:14:22, 16:14:22, 16:15:02, 16:15:02, 16:15:08 — the same retry cadence seen in yesterday's refused window (`L12252`).
- Everything the kiosk needs still resolved: `allowed gdmf.apple.com` 16:14:03/16:14:21/16:15:02, `allowed mesu.apple.com` 16:14:03/16:15:02, `allowed mesu-cdn.origin-apple.com.akadns.net` 16:15:06, `allowed cl2.apple.com`, `cl3.apple.com`, `gsp-ssl.ls.apple.com`, `gs-loc-new.ls-apple.com.akadns.net`, `configuration.apple.com`, `ocsp2.apple.com`, `init.push.apple.com`, `time.apple.com`, and the Waze hosts.
- The owner's own observation at the bench: "it looks like it's not downloading now."

**So:** DNS is live and the phone is online (allowed names answer in the same seconds), while the one refused name is the one the dictionary needs — and the download stops. The dictionary payload is `updates.cdn-apple.com` (255 of 523 catalog assets; `L12250`), the resolver refuses it, and the only reason every earlier attempt completed was the failover hatch switched on 2026-10-21 00:47:49 (`L12251`).

**This retires `L12196` and the conclusion section of `fc1950f6c`'s investigation doc.** Host lists do block this asset; the earlier tests were uninterpretable for one reason, and the mechanism is now measured rather than argued.

**What the change costs, which must be said next to the win.** With failover off, the phone's only resolver is our DoH endpoint. If that endpoint is unreachable, the device has no DNS at all, and because the DNS payload is `PayloadRemovalDisallowed` + `ProhibitDisablement`, it cannot be recovered from the device — only by us pushing a profile, which itself needs the device to reach us. So failover=false is safe only behind (a) monitoring that fires before a customer notices, and (b) an endpoint that does not depend on one container. Until both exist, it is a fleet-wide single point of failure, and rolling it out is the owner's call.

**Still open:** the PAC still sends those three payload hosts `DIRECT` (`DIRECT_DOMAINS` lines 2-4) — harmless while the resolver refuses them, dangerous the moment it does not; `apply-location-fix.py:69-71` still re-adds `iphone-ld` and the asset family on the next `--apply`; and the card-level fix (`keyboard.settings`, `D9352`) is untouched by this result.
