**W · The "it just says thinking" stall, root-caused and fixed — and the incident where I broke the owner's windows doing it**

2026-09-20. Two things in one entry: a real bug found and fixed, and a self-inflicted outage that I caused
and recovered, recorded because the second is the more instructive.

THE BUG, IN THE OWNER'S WORDS: *"sometimes when I talk to you, it just says thinking and I have to refresh
the page and I see that really you thought a lot and it didn't stream in."* That is precise: the engine HAD
produced the text — a reload re-reads the session and renders it — so the failure was in the **live
stream**, and `splice()` in `multi-window/dshw-proxy.mjs` is the live stream's only middleman.

THE CAUSE: `splice()` pipes the client socket to the engine socket and the engine socket back, with **no
idle detection of any kind** — no timeout, no keepalive, nothing that notices a path that has stopped
forwarding bytes. The laptop's route to the office is a **Tailscale DERP relay** (`docs/mesh/113`), and a
relay or NAT that drops a flow **sends no FIN**. So the client's socket stays ESTABLISHED, the WebSocket
never closes, no reconnect is ever attempted, and the UI waits forever on a stream that will never deliver
another byte. The websocket is spliced byte-for-byte and never rewritten, so the application cannot
heartbeat it either — **TCP is the last layer that can notice.**

THE FIX (`a`-side, committed): `setKeepAlive(true, 15000)` and `setNoDelay(true)` on **both** legs. The
kernel probes an idle connection after 15 s and closes it when the probes go unanswered, which turns a
permanently-dead stream into a **close event the client can reconnect from**. It changes nothing while
bytes are flowing.

THE INCIDENT, AND IT IS MINE. To make the fix live the proxy had to restart. I killed pid 30128 and ran
`dshw ensure`, which answered:

 [origins] could not start the proxy: Access is denied.

**The origin ports 3200-3223 were left dead and every window was dead with them.** I had weighed this risk
and judged it acceptable; the judgement was wrong, and the mitigation I had assumed — that `ensure` would
restart it — did not hold. Recovered by reading the proxy's own documented usage line and starting it
directly:

 node multi-window/dshw-proxy.mjs --base 3200 --count 24 --target 3099 --ttl 15000 --log <...> --pidfile <...>

Verified back: pid 39660, all four sampled ports listening, `HTTP 401` from the engine THROUGH the proxy,
`/__dshw/stats` answering, and `spliced` climbing immediately — the windows had reconnected on their own.
Outage roughly two to three minutes.

WHAT I OWE THE OWNER, AND THE RULE: I restarted a dependency of every open window without a way to put it
back the way `dshw` does, and I should have known the launcher was a base64-wrapped child with its own
environment before killing its parent. **The `Access is denied` is itself unexplained and is the next thing
to fix** — a launcher that cannot restart its own proxy is a launcher that cannot recover, and that is the
same class of defect as the engine-recovery path this repo already has a scar from.

AND IT IS REGISTERED: a wake flag carries this so the launcher fault is not left in a chat session.
