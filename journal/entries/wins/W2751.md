# Round 17: the FIRST optimisation — the phone number on every service page is a 20px tap target on a phone

2026-10-13, ZABZ-YOGA. Goal `goal-a8614123`, round 17. **PR 400.**

## THE FINDING, MEASURED ON THE LIVE SITE

The visitor walk reports `[minor] 13 interactive element(s) smaller than 24x24 on the mobile viewport`
on the public site. A count is not actionable, so I measured it and named what it found:

| element | measured at 393x852 | where |
|---|---|---|
| **`(732) 655-2355`** - a `tel:` link | **122 x 20** | **every service page** |
| Privacy | 42 x 15 | footer, every page |
| Terms | 35 x 15 | footer, every page |

24x24 CSS px is the **WCAG 2.2 Target Size (Minimum)** floor. **The element under it is the phone
number, on the public customer-facing site, on a phone** - for a phone repair shop, the single most
likely thing a customer taps.

## IT IS A CLASS, NOT AN INSTANCE

The identical class string is copy-pasted across **seven** page components
(`BatteryReplacement`, `DataRecovery`, `FlipPhone`, `LaptopRepair`, `PhoneRepair`, `ScreenRepair`,
`TabletRepair`), all rendering `<a href={BUSINESS_PHONE_HREF}>` with
`text-brand-teal hover:text-brand-teal-hover font-semibold`. **All seven fixed in one change** -
`inline-block py-1` - which is the difference between fixing a class and fixing a thing.

**7 files, 7 insertions, 7 deletions.** That is the first optimisation landed from the loop, and it is
the shape the owner asked for: the class, not a drive-by tweak.

## HONEST VERIFICATION STATUS

- **BEFORE: measured**, three pages, on the live site: `122x20 FAIL <24`.
- **AFTER: NOT measured.** Two attempts to render it locally both failed - `Start-Process` left the
 vite server dead by the time the probe ran (*connection refused*), and a `Start-Job` re-run answered
 but reported **"no tel link found"**, so the dev render did not produce the anchor production does.
 **Not diagnosed.** The claim is therefore arithmetic - 20px + `py-1` = 28px - and it is labelled as
 arithmetic in the PR. `node _tap-check.mjs https://test.lakewoodphoneandtech.com` settles it after
 the deploy, and if it still prints `FAIL <24` the fix is wrong and I will say so.

## WHY THIS ONE OUT OF THE WHOLE REPORT

Of the walk's 20 majors: 8 are an unverified lookup class, 8 are the work-order payment design question
(an owner question, not a fix), and 2 are known non-defects (a deliberate side panel; a deliberate `ul`
instead of a table). **This was the only finding that was measured, on the customer-facing site,
unambiguous, and small enough for its safety to be obvious.** The footer's 15px legal links are left
alone - lower value, and arguably inside the "inline in a sentence" exception.
