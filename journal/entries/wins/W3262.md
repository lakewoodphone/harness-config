DELIBERATELY NARROW, AND THE CONTROLS SAY SO: three of the seven probes assert that a rate limit, a 503 and a connection reset on the SAME tools remain RETRYABLE. A fast-fail rule that swallowed transient faults would be worse than the retry it replaces. Two of my own instruments failed on the way and both were caught by their own guards:
