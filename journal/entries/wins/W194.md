**W194 · 2026-09-15 · Pre-event 1440p clips, two restored evidence streams, and silent failure ended.**

**What measurably works after the 2026-09-15 evidence overhaul. Do not "optimise" these away.**

**Clips now contain the approach, at full resolution, in a codec that plays.** Production proof, not a test harness: an `outside_door` event produced 3/3 cameras, each reporting the identical window `14:32:56-14:33:16` (10 s before and 10 s after the trigger) from OSD-titled cameras `Main Entrance` (ch6), `IPC` (ch5) and `Reolink Video Doorbell PoE` (ch8). Clip durations 19.2 / 20.0 / 21.1 s. A frame-level check confirmed real-time playback: at clip 2 s the burned-in clock read 14:15:49 and at clip 38 s it read 14:16:25 — 36 s of scene across 36 s of clip. Output is H.264 2560x1440 with AAC.

**Two evidence streams that had been dead since 2026-09-14 are producing again.** `waiting_room` (NVR ch5) and `control_room_exit` (NVR ch1) stills: HTTP 200 in ~2 s, 205,894 and 333,338 bytes, 2560x1440, written atomically.

**Security history is legible again.** ~50,000 state changes a week of presence-sensor churn no longer enter the recorder; verified 0 rows for both excluded entities in the minutes after the restart while door contacts still record.

**Silent failure is over.** Every capture appends one line to `/media/phoenix/capture.log` with rc, method, window, duration and a reason. On top of that an hourly watchdog (`~/bin/check-phoenix-evidence.py` on secratary, cron at :25) probes the *capability* of all five cameras through the real code path rather than guessing from file timestamps — because "no stills in 30 hours" cannot distinguish a quiet weekend from a dead camera, and the gap days in this archive were exactly that ambiguity. On a fault it writes JSON beside ha-truth/ha-behaviour and raises a task through `POST /ceo/tasks`, de-duplicated to one per 12 h.

Two details in that watchdog that were deliberate and should survive: it ignores failures older than the newest deploy of the capture code (a fault that has been fixed must stop being reported, or the reader learns to ignore the watchdog), and its log scan is time-filtered for the same reason.

**The evidence is off-host.** 858 MB, 3,081 files, 179 clips rsync'd to secratary from the Pi's SD card (73% full, no redundancy) at 03:30 daily. Deliberately **no `--delete`**: the Pi purges after 25 days and keeping everything off-host is the point of a backup. `--exclude='*.part'` so a clip mid-transcode is never copied.
