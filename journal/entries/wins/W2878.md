**W · Whisper Small (English) on the owner's iPhone 15 Pro: ~3 s or better, against ~15 s for Large v3 Turbo — and the first real iPhone dictation latency number in this estate**

HIS WORDS, 2026-10-13: *"I tried the small model and it's way way faster. It takes like the same three
seconds probably even faster than the cloud model. Drops less accurate but I think that's worth it as I'm
changing to it."*

WHAT WORKED, precisely: Superwhisper free tier, mode **Voice to Text**, model **Whisper Small (English)**
(500 MB, old in-app name *Standard*), with notifications, Live Activities and the Shortcut all declined.
Same phone, same session: **Large v3 Turbo (1.6 GB) ~15 s -> Small (500 MB) ~3 s or better.**

THE GENERALISABLE PART, because it was not obvious: the vendor rates both models **8/10 for speed**, so the
speed rating was no guide at all. **The file size was the whole thing** -- a 1.6 GB model on an 8 GB phone is
evicted and re-loaded on every dictation, and the vendor's own documentation names model load time as the
first cause of slow dictation while iOS exposes no control to keep it resident. **When a local speech model
is slow on a phone, change the SIZE, not the speed rating.**

ALSO KEPT: the "subscription" he thought he had was the **3,000-word free Pro allowance**, and the cloud
models exist only inside it -- so the fast cloud experience he missed was never going to last and was never
a plan. He is now on the free tier permanently and has beaten the cloud on latency anyway.

ACCURACY TRADE, accepted by him in the same sentence: Small is 5/10 against Turbo's 8/10. **Do not re-raise
it** -- he decided with the number in front of him. The one operational consequence is already a standing
lesson (L1930): a dictated serial, IMEI or part number is not evidence.
