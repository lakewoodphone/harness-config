**W-new · 2026-09-15 · The third permanently-red alarm in my own kernel is fixed: `tick_completion` now reads INFO.**

| reading | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| badge overall | 6 attention / 8 ok | **5 attention / 9 ok** |
| `tick_completion` | **critical** | **info** ok=True |
| latest day's completion | 93.9% over 244 ticks | unchanged, now *reported* |

The check returned CRITICAL whenever **any** historical collapse window existed in its 120-day window.
Measured: every window had ended by 2026-08-04, the newest span was 2026-08-23..2026-08-24, and the
latest day was **93.9% complete over 244 ticks** — so a healthy system was showing a red light nobody
could clear.

This is the third instance of the same defect in this kernel: `attention_debt` counted a dead channel's
briefings, `check_delivery` measured a channel the owner had closed, and now `tick_completion` shouted
about history. A window is now LIVE while it ends within 7 days of the newest day with data; recent
windows still go CRITICAL, historical ones are INFO naming the count, the newest span and today's rate,
and they stay in `metrics.failure_windows` — the long window exists precisely to catch a collapse a
short one would miss, so the record stays and only the colour changes. A latest rate under 60% with no
window yet is HIGH, so a fresh decline is still caught.
