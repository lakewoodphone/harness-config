The budget extension now fires on a real session, verified before and after

WHAT WAS WRONG: the first version of the progress-earned budget extension was wired into
the pre-tick guard only. It never fired, and one session of live evidence said why:

 75414 task #25118, step 5 was `update_task` (a mutating action, so the progress test
 should have extended it) -> FAILED
 marker: [STEP_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED: reached planned 5/5 work steps without [WORK_DONE]]

"reached planned 5/5 work steps" is the wording of the check INSIDE the step loop, not
of the guard. There are two budget-decision sites in the work loop and I had patched
one. 39 unit tests passed while the feature did nothing, because they tested the helper
rather than the interception.

FIX: one helper, `_maybe_extend_work_session_budget`, called from both sites, committed
e6a28a6b.

VERIFIED LIVE, which is the only thing that settles it:

 75414 before failed 5/5 ext=0
 75415 after active 5/8 ext=1
 [BUDGET-EXTENDED: 5 -> 8 steps. The recent steps took real actions, so the session
 continues rather than being discarded at a limit fixed before the work started.]

Task #25106 had failed three times earlier the same night (75405, 75406, 75407) at 5/5;
the extended attempt was still running with three steps to spare. So the same shape that
was being discarded now has room to finish.

ALSO IN THE SAME COMMIT: the reconnaissance nudge was dead for an analogous reason --
`self._readonly_steps >= 2` could never be reached because the counter resets at the top
of every tick and the queue runs one step per tick. The count now comes from the persisted
step markers, and the per-tick counter is deleted so there is one source of truth. P136
is closed by that.
