**W2758 · 2026-10-13 · One at a time was not holding: the CFO's question was ranked as machine noise.**

2026-10-13. The CFO's one-at-a-time rule was not holding, and a second question had already gone out.

THE EVIDENCE. `cfo_clarify --status` moved from `asked=1` to `asked=2` while the first was still
unanswered: #1 newegg asked 03:30:40 (outbound_ok=1, real Twilio sid), #2 polka dot asked 05:00:17,
also actually sent. The owner's rule is one at a time, and the machinery appeared to implement it -
`open_owner_turn` finds the open turn and `textsend` refuses with "owner conversation open ... one at
a time". So the question was why it did not.

THE CAUSE, traced through the ranking. `may_supersede_open_turn` compares
`owner_text_priority(incoming)` against `owner_text_priority(effective_turn_reason(held))`. Measured:
 a CFO question (reason `cfo-ask`) -> priority 1
 the open turn as recorded -> priority 21
so the new question outranked the open one and TOOK THE FLOOR. And the open turn's recorded reason
was **`aitext-responder`** - a machine-band label - even though its body reads
"CFO [ask-1791862240]: what was 362.51 at newegg ...".

WHY THE BODY WAS IGNORED. `effective_turn_reason()` falls back to reading the body's shape ONLY when
the recorded reason is in `_GENERIC_TURN_REASONS`, which is `{"", "send_message", "owner_update",
"notify", "notify_owner", "sms", "auto"}`. `aitext-responder` was NOT in that set, yet it is this
sender's own DEFAULT (`_get(cfg, "reason", default="aitext-responder")`). So every message sent
without an explicit label was ranked by a transport word rather than by what it is - which is exactly
the failure the set's own comment describes: "the producer that opened it passed a transport word
rather than a class".

FIXED by adding `aitext-responder` to `_GENERIC_TURN_REASONS`. Verified:
 open turn reason : aitext-responder
 effective turn reason: cfo_ask <- inferred from the body now
 incoming cfo-ask -> may_supersede=False <- refused, one at a time
 incoming aitext-responder -> may_supersede=False
Before the change, `cfo-ask` returned True, which is how question #2 got sent.

NOTE ON THE OTHER HALF, which was working: the CFO also declines to ask the SAME merchant twice
(`skipped_waiting_clarification`), so `newegg` could not be re-asked. The defect was specifically
that a DIFFERENT merchant's question may jump the queue, and the owner's instruction is one question
at a time, not one merchant at a time.

CONSEQUENCE FOR ANYONE READING THIS LATER: two of the owner's texts are now outstanding - the newegg
362.51 at 03:30 and the polka dot 299.00 at 05:00 - and from this fix onward a third cannot be sent
until he answers one. The second one was mine to have allowed, so I am not going to quietly withdraw
it; it is a real question with a real answer and it is already on his phone.
