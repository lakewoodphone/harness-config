**W2752b · 2026-10-13 · 28,142.70 of the shop's received money carries no work order, and the terminal flow is why.**

2026-10-13. Follow-through on W2751/L3652, which found 20 orders flagged paid with no payment row.
This round found the cause, and it is 7x larger than the symptom.

THE NUMBER. Live production store `operational` (the RDS `d5oq83veii4rlb`), read-only:
 SUCCEEDED payments 326 rows, 33,220.35
 carrying a work order 58 rows
 **carrying NONE 268 rows, 28,142.70**
 PENDING 34 rows, 28,669.68 (not money received)
So 28,142.70 of money the shop actually took cannot be tied to the repair it paid for. The 20
orders in L3652 are the visible tip: the money exists and is orphaned.

THE CAUSE, read out of the deployed backend on lpt-apps rather than guessed.
 * The pay-before-pickup flow is EXEMPLARY and I want that on the record. Its header rule is
 "NEVER MATCH ON AMOUNT. metadata.workOrderId is the only thing that ties [the payment to the
 order]", it rejects a payment whose metadata lacks a usable workOrderId, and it is idempotent
 three times over keyed on the Stripe event id (`webhook_events.stripeEventId`, then the payment
 row's unique constraints, then releaseForPickup). It also works: `webhook_events` holds two
 processed `checkout.session.completed` rows, the newer on 2026-10-12 carrying
 workOrderId 3071 and producing a linked 2.00 SUCCEEDED payment.
 * The CARD-TERMINAL flow carries none of that. Measured on live charges, its metadata is
 {readerId, staffNote, transactionType: terminal} and a free-text description such as "Moshe
 Ifrah" or "sayag". The work order is simply absent, so the payment is born orphaned and no later
 process can recover it.

ALSO SETTLED, correcting my own uncertainty earlier this session: the Stripe webhook DOES deliver
and the route DOES work. `app.use('/webhooks', webhookRoutes)` at index.ts:349, a POST without a
signature returns 400 as it should, the public URL maps to this app, and two events have been
processed. I had been treating "no webhook lines in the log" as evidence of non-delivery, and the
log turned out to hold 66 lines in 30 days, errors only - the app does not log successful requests.
An absent line in a log that never records successes is not evidence of absence.
Two things could NOT be tested: `POST /v1/events/{id}/retry` is refused by Stripe for live events
("You can only retry Events in test mode"), so a replay test is unavailable in live mode.

RAISED AS #470, high, with the numbers and the recommendation to have the terminal carry the work
order at the point of payment rather than back-linking 268 payments by hand. The one thing I need
from the owner is a product question, not a technical one: should the terminal REQUIRE a work order
or be allowed to ring up a walk-in sale. Back-linking is mine to build either way.
