All three contacts now report, and all three template sensors the security automations read are
live. Before this the estate had exactly one working door contact out of three, and the missing one
included `binary_sensor.phoenix_outside_door_contact` -- the canonical trigger for the
highest-severity scenario the business recognises.

**Neither sensor needed a re-pair.** Both were still in the coordinator's database, so power alone
brought them back; `device/configure` then rebuilt the bindings (all four clusters, each acknowledged
with `AREQ: ZDO - bindRsp`). Reach for the reset button only after power has failed.

**Two notes that matter more than the fix.**
- **Battery readings lag by up to 18 hours.** Both devices republished their *old* value (8% and 21%)
 for minutes after the swap, then reported **100**. `batteryPercentageRemaining` has a maximum
 report interval of 65000 s. Judge a battery change by whether the device is transmitting, never by
 the first number it shows. I nearly called the first swap a failure for this reason.
- **All three contacts currently read `on` (open).** For these SmartThings sensors `contact: true` is
 *closed* (`value_off: true`) and `false` is *open*, so `on` means an open door. Two were just taken
 apart and the third is 73 minutes stale-open. **The acceptance test is that each shows closed once
 the door is shut and the magnet re-seated** -- a sensor that reports but reads open forever is a
 misaligned magnet, which is the failure mode that makes a working sensor useless. Not yet checked
 by the owner.

**Still open:** the third sensor's cell is at **19%** and 15 hours stale -- due for replacement
proactively, since that is the door the apartment lock depends on. And
`lock.boltchecked_apartmentdoor` remains `unavailable` (915 min).
