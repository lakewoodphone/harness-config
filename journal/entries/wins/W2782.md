**W2782 · 2026-10-13 · owner-queue amend: the queue can now keep its rows true, and #228 carries the measured runway.**

2026-10-13. The owner queue had no way to keep its rows true, so I added one and used it on the queue's
own head.

THE GAP. The estate's rule about this is written down, and it was written after a priority-1 item stopped
being true within two hours: "an item is a statement about the world at FILING time. When the measurement
moves, the item moves with it, or the next shift spends its opening minutes chasing a ghost." There was no
way to obey it. `add` refuses what looks like a duplicate, `resolve`/`answer`/`defer` all change the STATE
of a row, and nothing could change its TEXT. So a row either kept a stale figure for ever or had to be
closed and refiled, which loses its age, its position and its history.

WHAT I ADDED. `owner-queue.py amend <id> [--context TEXT] [--recommendation TEXT] [--note TEXT]`. It
updates the CONTEXT and/or the RECOMMENDATION of a PENDING row, **and never the QUESTION** - that is what
the owner was actually asked, and rewriting it would make the queue's own history a lie. Only a pending
row qualifies, because amending a resolved one would rewrite what was answered. A `--note` is APPENDED to
the context with the date, so the change is visible in the row rather than silent, and the write commits
its read snapshot first for the same reason `_close` does (an UPDATE that has to upgrade a stale read
transaction is refused immediately by SQLite, which is why resolve used to fail four times in a row).
Backup: /root/owner-queue.py.bak-amend-<ts>.

USED IT ON #228, the funding question, the queue's genuine head, deferral expired since 2026-10-09. Its
context was dated 2026-10-01 with a hand-extracted figure of roughly 4,500 a month leaving the personal
accounts. It now carries what `scripts/finance/forward-view.py` MEASURES - a tool that had existed and
had never been run until tonight:
 revenue 8205.54/mo, cost 10761.79/mo, gap -2556.25/mo; breakeven 11525.34, so +40.5 per cent needed
 LPT cash 291.61, LPT cards owed 18892.47, LPT available credit 1262.70
 personal cash 769.43, personal available credit 20957.57; owner loan 53440.64
 sales tax 543.62/mo at the statutory rate or 463.88/mo at the IMPLIED rate
 RUNWAY gap 3099.87/mo against 21727.00 of capacity = 7.01 months, exhausted 2027-06
and the amendment says out loud that the two figures are different measures and both true: 4,500 was what
the business DREW from personal accounts, 3099.87 is the net trading gap including the sales-tax set-aside.
**The row had been carrying an old measurement while the measurement moved, which is the exact ghost the
rule exists to prevent.**

AND THE RECOMMENDATION CHANGED WITH THE FACTS. It now reads: stop funding it on a DATE rather than when
the money runs out - with 7.01 months of capacity and a 3099.87 monthly gap, the choice is one made in
October, not one that gets made in June 2027 when the credit is gone. A recommendation that does not move
when the numbers move is the same defect one level up.

A SCRATCH ARTEFACT, DECLARED: `/root/bin/amend-row.py` is a two-line helper that reads /tmp/amend228.json
and calls amend. It is in /root/bin with a name that says what it does, not in /tmp, so that a later
session can see it exists - but it is specific to one row and should be deleted or generalised rather than
reused.
