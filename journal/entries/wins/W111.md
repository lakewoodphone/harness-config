**W111 · 2026-09-14 · The work loop never showed the model its own tool results, so no session could accumulate a fact.**

The work loop never showed the model its own tool results

WHAT: app/autopilot.py, the work-session loop. execute_actions() results were
appended only to `proactive_actions` -- the tick's notes -- while
_build_work_prompt() is built from `accumulated_output`, which carried the model's
prose plus the NAMES of the actions it had called and never their RESULTS. So a
session asked a question, never saw the answer, asked it again on the next step,
and died at the step budget having learned nothing.

WHY IT LOOKED LIKE SOMETHING ELSE: the symptom was 5/5 steps with
progress_pct 99 and logs full of reconnaissance -- [ACTIONS: 1 (qb_status)],
[ACTIONS: 2 (ha_states, list_tasks)]. That reads as a lazy model, and two earlier
fixes treated it that way: making action names visible in the session log, and
prepending STOP SEARCHING after two read-only steps. The instruction was provably
in the prompt at _readonly_steps 2 and 3, and provably ignored. It was ignored
because following it would mean acting while blind.

MEASURED 2026-09-14, work_sessions since 2026-09-12: every failed session at 5/5,
progress_pct 99; task #25095 re-claimed four times (75398-75401) and task #25103
three times (75402-75404), each attempt restarting from zero knowledge.

FIX: AutopilotController._render_tool_results renders action, outcome, detail and a
bounded JSON of data (900 chars per action, 4000 for the block, so one chatty tool
cannot poison the context), and the loop appends that block to accumulated_output.
Deployed 2026-09-14T23:25:01+00:00, health 200, committed b4ef49cd.

PROOF THAT IT IS THE RIGHT LAYER: tests/test_tool_results_reach_the_prompt.py drives
the real _build_work_prompt and asserts that a fact returned by a tool in step 1
appears in the prompt built for step 2. That is the property that was missing, and
it is asserted at the prompt, not at the renderer.
