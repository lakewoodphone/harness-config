# Round 24 — D4 proved by making it fail

A check that has only ever been seen to pass has not been tested. D4 was green because no
overlap exists since the cut-over floor — that is not evidence it can detect one.

## The test, and the first attempt at it was wrong

**Attempt 1 failed to plant a failure.** I copied both ask stores, planted a `sent=1` row for
`polka dot` in the cfo-ask copy, and ran D4 against the copies through the env overrides the
check already supports. Result: `1 merchant(s) sent by cfo-ask and 0 by owner-ask … overlap:
none` — **and D4 was right.** Owner-ask has no SENT purchase after the cut-over floor, so there
was nothing to overlap *with*. A test that cannot plant the failure is not a test, and this one
had silently tested nothing.

**Attempt 2 planted BOTH sides** — a `sent=1` purchase row for `newegg` in each copy, dated now
— and D4 caught it:

 FAIL D4 no charge was put to the owner by both askers
 1 merchant(s) sent by cfo-ask and 1 by owner-ask since 2026-10-02T18:00Z;
 overlap: newegg -- the owner was asked about the same charge by two askers. Each
 reads the other's record (`P2830`); this means one of those reads has broken.

and with the planting removed, the same command against the live stores:

 OK D4 no charge was put to the owner by both askers
 0 merchant(s) sent by cfo-ask and 0 by owner-ask since 2026-10-02T18:00Z; overlap: none

**So D4 is verified in both directions**: it fails on a real overlap with a message that says
what broke and which fix it implicates, it passes when there is none, and the test ran entirely
on copies — no live store was touched.

## Why this round was worth a round

The only thing worse than no check is a check whose green means nothing. This engagement has
already produced five silent fallbacks and three missing imports, all found by making failures
audible; the same discipline applies to a *check* — its failure path has to be exercised, or its
pass is unearned. `P2830` cost the owner a duplicate question about his own vendor; D4 is what
stops that recurring unnoticed.
