**W2754b · 2026-10-13 · PayPal purchases were permanently unaskable: the gate wanted a card number that does not exist.**

2026-10-13. The CFO could not ask about a whole class of purchases, and the fix had to extend the
rule without loosening it.

THE GAP. `cfo-ask` builds a question about an unclear charge, and every body passes
`ask_quality.review()`. For `kind="charge"` that gate requires the body to name the funding source:
`has_card = CARD_RE.search(text) or bool(card)`. The rule is not arbitrary - its docstring quotes the
owner, who answered a card-less question with "That does not contain enough information or card which
card it was". Measured at the 04:30 run, the gate refused this:
 "what was 357.18 at back-market-inc on 5 Oct (Personal PayPal)?"
 GATE REFUSED - it is about a charge and names no card (last four digits); an account name like
 'Blue Cash Everyday' is shared by two cards
A PayPal charge HAS no last four. So every PayPal purchase the CFO was unsure about was permanently
unaskable: 27 charges dropped at that gate in one run, and the objective's "questions about unclear
current or recent purchases" silently excluded an entire funding source.

THE FIX, and why it is an extension rather than a relaxation. The gate's stated concern is AMBIGUITY
- two cards really are both called "Blue Cash Everyday" - not the mere absence of digits. So the rule
becomes: a card, OR an account whose name the ledger says is UNIQUE (`account_is_unambiguous()` in
ask_quality.py, reading `accounts.name` from the accounting store read-only). `cfo-ask.py` now passes
the account through to the gate.

TESTED THREE WAYS, which is the part that matters:
 PayPal body, account supplied -> True [] accepted
 PayPal body, no account -> False [names no card ... and no unique account]
 "Blue Cash Everyday", account arg -> False [same reason] STILL REFUSED
The third line is the control: naming an account is not enough, the account must be one whose name
the ledger can vouch for. The rule now recognises a valid identifier where none existed; it does not
accept any identifier for a card-funded charge.

VERIFIED END TO END. A dry run of `cfo-ask` after the change auto-resolved one merchant by research
(back-market-inc -> Electronics & Devices) and then built a well-formed question for the next
candidate, naming the card: "what was 299.00 at polka dot on 27 Sep (ending 4258)?" Nothing was sent.

NOTE ON THE RUN ITSELF, worth keeping because it is the loop behaving well: the 04:30 run declined to
ask 29 minutes after the last one (floor is 45), then auto-resolved a merchant instead of spending a
question on it, and only then reached for the next candidate. That is the ordering the owner asked
for - resolve it yourself first, ask only what is left.
