**W129 · 2026-09-15 · The control-room interior door contact is fully back on a battery swap alone - and a battery reading is not a battery state.**

**W (2026-09-15) - The control-room interior door contact went from 22 hours blind to fully reporting, on a battery swap alone.**

Measured on the live estate. Owner replaced the CR2450 at ~11:00 on 2026-09-15.

Before: `0x286d97000115c914` had sent **zero** Zigbee messages since the 2026-09-13 21:51 ET host
restart. Six ZDO node-descriptor requests had failed and `device/configure` had failed twice with
`Bind … failed (AREQ - ZDO - bindRsp after 10000ms)` -- the device not answering at all. Last battery
reading: **8%**.

After, read back from the live surfaces:

 z2m 11:00:32 Successfully interviewed 'Control Room Door Sensor', device has successfully been paired
 z2m 11:00:55 Received Zigbee message from 'Control Room Door Sensor' (accelerometer report)
 HA binary_sensor.interior_control_room_door_sensor_contact off, updating live
 HA binary_sensor.phoenix_control_room_interior_door_contact off, live (the template the automations use)
 HA sensor.interior_control_room_door_sensor_temperature 28.17 C, live
 z2m battery: 8 -> 100 (the new cell, reported by the device itself)

All four binds were then applied and acknowledged: `genPollCtrl`, `msTemperatureMeasurement`,
`genPowerCfg`, `manuSpecificSamsungAccelerometer`, each followed by `AREQ: ZDO - bindRsp`. Contact
was observed toggling `true -> false -> true` as the door moved.

**No re-pair was needed.** The device was still in the coordinator's database (nwkAddr 8679), so
power alone brought it back and z2m rebuilt the bindings. Worth knowing before reaching for a reset
button next time.

**Two traps this session paid for, both worth not repeating:**
- **A battery reading is not a battery state.** The device kept publishing `battery: 8` for minutes
 after the swap, because `batteryPercentageRemaining` has a maximum report interval of **65000
 seconds (18 h)**. Anyone reading the cache would have said the swap failed. It then reported
 **100**. Do not judge a battery change by the first reading; wait for the device to report, or
 judge it by whether the device is transmitting at all.
- The `Failed to configure` bridge responses I found on re-checking were **yesterday's**, sitting in
 the same log file. Read the timestamp, not just the line.

**Method note that mattered:** the first attempt to watch for this rejoin reported a false positive,
because it grepped for the word `SUCCESSFUL` and matched a 40 KB `bridge/devices` payload describing
the *historical* interview from three days earlier (L1477). Re-armed by recording the log's line
count first and reading only lines written after it, and by matching only lines a device itself
produces. That version caught the real event.

**Remaining:** `binary_sensor.0x286d97000115ca7a_contact` (the outside-door contact, the canonical
intrusion trigger) is still `unavailable` with a last reading of 21%. Same model, same CR2450.
