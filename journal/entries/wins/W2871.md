**Why this is the signal and not the completion rate.** 3 of 4 completed. But the meaningful fact is that **two of the four hit a tool-loop deadlock, were redirected, and survived** — one to completion, one still working at step 11. That is precisely the behaviour the P3c/P3d work was built to produce: *a repeat is a redirect, not a death sentence.* Before it, those two sessions would have been **closed**.

**And the redirect is a real instruction, not a label.** Read from session 78725's own log:

The session was told what it was doing wrong and what to do instead, and it continued — step 1 *"I'll start by verifying the actual current state of the QB agent and ledger connection — the task description may be stale"* with `[ACTIONS: 1 (qb_status)]`, step 2 continuing the same FINANCE BLOCKER. **That is eleven steps on a task that the day's baseline calls a 64 % failure.**

**And the spend is not the burn the owner warned about.** **0.704 USD for 4 sessions = 0.176 per session**, against the ~0.53 per session the estate has been paying. Four data points is not a rate, but the direction is the one his condition requires: *"productive and not just token and money burn."*

**What I am NOT claiming.** n = 4. Three completions of four is not evidence of a 75 % rate — against the day's 33.9 % it is encouraging and unproven. The comparison that would be wrong to make is the pre-window's 15 %, because 17 of those 20 sessions were `paused` under the cap, so that window measures a stopped company. **The day stands at 115 sessions, 39 completed, 48 failed, 27 paused.** What *is* established is narrower and stronger: the redirect mechanism is firing in production (2 of 4 sessions) and sessions are surviving it, which is a mechanism observed rather than a rate inferred.
