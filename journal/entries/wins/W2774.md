**W2774 · 2026-10-13 · Merged the two item stores: 55 orders and 83 item rows, past a gate that reported success while adding nothing.**

2026-10-13. W2673 found the two item stores. This round merged them, and hit a gate worth recording.

THE MERGE. `/opt/company/scripts/marketplace-backfill-items.py`: for every canonical purchase with no
item rows, read the legacy table's rows for that order, build a `FoldedOrder`, and hand it to
`marketplace_order_sync.upsert_orders` - the same writer the clock uses. No SQL of its own. Dry-run by
default, `--apply` to write, VACUUM INTO backup first. Derived and stated as derived: the scraper left
`quantity` and `line_total` NULL on many rows, so quantity becomes 1 and line_total becomes unit x
quantity, which is what the canonical store's own `item_count()` already assumes.

MEASURED:
 canonical item rows 642 -> 725 (+83)
 purchases with no items 95 -> 40 (-55)
 upsert result {'new': 0, 'changed': 55, 'unchanged': 0}

THE GATE THAT ALMOST MADE THIS A SILENT NO-OP, and it is the part worth keeping. The first `--apply`
reported `{'new': 0, 'changed': 0, 'unchanged': 55}` and added nothing - a green result that did
nothing at all. `upsert_orders` writes items only when
 rank_ok = int(new.amount_rank) >= int(stored.amount_rank)
and my built `FoldedOrder`s defaulted to `amount_rank=0` while the stored orders are ranks 1 to 4, so
every item write was skipped. `amount_rank` is a measure of the TOTAL's quality - 4 for a grand total,
1 for items with no total - and it is being used as a proxy for "is this record at least as good as
what is on file". Passing the STORED rank is the honest answer for a backfill of items, because the
record is not claiming a better total, only adding lines:
 amount_rank=int(o["amount_rank"] or 0)
**A cross-table write gated on a column that means something else will skip silently and report
success.** The tell was `changed: 0` on a run that had something to change, and the check that caught
it was asking the destination how many rows it had before and after, not reading the return value.

WHAT REMAINS. 40 purchases still have items in neither store: the mailbox has none (Amazon's 2026
layout carries a category, not products) and the scraper has no rows for them. An Amazon order export
is the only route, which is the fallback ladder the scraper's own handoff already sets out.
