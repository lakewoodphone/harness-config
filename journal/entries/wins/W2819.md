**W2819 · 2026-10-14 · One repo-internal skill divergence converged by measuring the superset relation; the other is a hand-merge and is recorded as one.**

One of the two skills the repo disagreed with itself about is now converged, and the other is recorded as needing a decision rather than guessed at.

**The defect.** `preset-drift.py` reported `divergent-in-repo` on two shared skills. The catalog `~/.dsh/skills/<name>/SKILL.md` can hold **one** copy, so three presets carrying three different versions of the same skill means whichever preset syncs last wins — a rule that changes depending on the order of an unattended job.

**`parallel-agent-orchestration` — fixed, and the safety of the fix was measured rather than assumed.** Superset analysis across the three presets:

 zabz 13700 B = 152 lines | cordis-bg 11220 B = 124 lines | yocheved 7852 B = 100 lines
 yocheved is a SUBSET of zabz (0 lines lost) and of cordis-bg (0 lines lost)
 adopting zabz into cordis-bg loses exactly 1 line (a reworded authority row)

So zabz's copy was adopted into both, byte-exact: cordis-bg 11,220 → 13,700, yocheved 7,852 → 13,700, each verified identical by sha256, committed `777c1922`. The detector then reported **`divergent-in-repo 2 → 1`** with `match=15 drift=0` unchanged on the loaded surface.

**`lpt-production-case-work` — NOT fixed, deliberately, because no copy is a superset of another.** Measured (non-blank lines): zabz 144, cordis-bg 130, yocheved 138, and ZABZ-TECH's own copy 213. What makes this a hand-merge rather than a pick:

* adopting **zabz** would lose **5 lines** from cordis-bg and **15** from yocheved — and they are one whole section: `## 1. There are TWO production databases. Name which one answered.` with its two-row table (`operational` = the older RDS the authority calls `PHONE_TECH_FULL_DATABASE_URL`; `app` = `lpt_prod` on Hetzner, which `api.lakewoodphoneandtech.com` serves) and the measurement behind it, journal `L3351`.
* adopting **ZABZ-TECH's 213-line copy** would still lose that same 5 and 15 lines: it carries `L3351`, `operational`, `lpt_prod` and `PHONE_TECH_FULL_DATABASE_URL`, but **not the section heading or its table.** Verified by grep against the file, not by reading the summary.

**That section is load-bearing shop knowledge** — it is the rule for *naming which production database answered*, which is precisely the question whose wrong answer produced this estate's worst recorded incident. So the correct action is a hand-merge: take the 213-line copy for its 69 extra lines and restore the missing section from cordis-bg. **Recorded as needing a decision, not deferred silently, and not guessed at.**

**The general rule this bought, and it is the third time today it has paid:** *"the largest copy is the newest"* is a heuristic, not a measurement. On `parallel-agent-orchestration` the largest **was** a superset; on `lpt-production-case-work` the largest was missing a section the smaller copies had. **Compute the superset relation; never infer it from size.** The measurement is five lines of Python — `set(smaller_lines) - set(larger_lines)` — and it is what stood between a tidy convergence and silently deleting the rule for naming the production database.
