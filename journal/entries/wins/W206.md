**W206 · 2026-09-17 · Shop intake turned into a source-dated case record in one hour by running the research pass in background.**

**What worked, measured 2026-09-17.** A one-turn shop intake became a decision-ready, source-dated case record **inside the same shop hour**, because the two halves ran at once instead of in series:

1. **The record was written while a background research pass ran.** `subagent` in the background on the device/part/USB-C/failure-mode questions, filed under `hardware-diagnostics/`, while the case file, the sync record and the local precedent hunt were written in the foreground. The pass returned a per-claim brief with URLs and read dates; folding it in cost one append, not a second session.
2. **The shop's own 188 cases were searched before any web research** (`lptcase.py find`, never a tree-grep). That is where the answer actually came from: the **Weissman Acer Aspire 3 N22C6** case — same brand, **same symptom**, confirmed DC jack at the bench, **$75 quoted**. The price and the diagnosis both had precedent; nothing was invented.
3. **The tooling validated the work rather than a human having to.** `tools/customer/lint.py` (0 findings), `record.validate` + JSON Schema (0 errors), `lptcase.py find` resolving by phone, name and device. Every check ran and passed before the turn ended.

**Why this is worth keeping.** The failure mode it avoids is the one that costs the owner attention: a record written from memory later, a price guessed, or a case that cannot be found by the number the customer rings from. **Background-research-plus-foreground-record is the default shape for a customer intake, not a special effort** — the research pass is free to run and the record gets better, not slower.
