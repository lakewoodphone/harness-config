**W · The whole mesh under simultaneous load, measured for the first time — and only one node's signal beat the noise**

MEASURED 2026-09-18 by workstream F2-r2. The previous attempt died when five children concentrated on one
node; this one ran **2 children on each of three nodes**, spread deliberately, inside a single aligned
window.

ALIGNMENT FIRST, because the numbers are meaningless otherwise: **the mesh clocks are 2.2-3.1 s apart**
(offsets +2,251 to +3,043 ms). Every sampler stamped each tick with the **sampled node's own UTC epoch**,
and each node's offset was measured before and after the window with an RTT/2-corrected bracket, then
binned onto a 1 Hz grid with a ±500 ms tolerance and gaps reported. All four series really do cover one
window: aligned spans 119.6-121.0 s.

| node | children | commit before | during (mean) | peak | delta/child | predicted unit cost | avail low-water | proc delta | CONFIRMED |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `zabz-tech` (32c) | 2 | 32,146.8 | 32,380.8 | 32,764.1 | **117.0** | 295 ×2 = 590 | 37.3 GiB | +9.4 | **2/2** |
| `zabz-tech-linux` (12c) | 2 | 5,297.4 | 5,588.4 | 5,594.9 | **145.5** | 113 plat / 140 peak | 9.79 GiB | +9.8 | **2/2** |
| `lakewooechsmini` (macOS) | 2 | 9,480.9 | 9,688.8 | 9,766.9 | **104.0** | 159 ×2 = 318 | 5.94 GiB | +14.0 | **2/2** |
| `zabz-yoga-1` (laptop) | **0** | 24,732.1 | 25,279.1 | 26,146.4 | — | — | 15.22 GiB | +12.8 | no children |

**6 children, 6/6 confirmed on the node each was aimed at from its own `MESH-HOST:` line, 6/6 alive in
every tick.** Total commit added across the mesh: **733 MiB**; `zabz-tech-linux` took the most. No bound
tripped: the tightest disk never moved and the lowest available-physical reading anywhere was 5.94 GiB
against a 4 GiB floor. Cleanup verified at both ends; `secratary` watched throughout and showed no distress.

**AND THE HONEST HEADLINE IS THE CONTROL, NOT THE RESULTS.** The laptop — which received **zero** children
— moved **+547 MiB of commit and +36 processes in the same window**, which is *more* than either the
desktop or the mac mini added. So of the three loaded nodes, **only `zabz-tech-linux`'s +291 MiB is
distinguishable from ambient mesh churn**, and the process-count delta (+9.4 / +9.8 / +14.0 loaded against
+12.8 unloaded) is **not a per-child signal at all**. Two of this programme's most confident-looking rows
would have been noise presented as measurement if the unloaded node had not been instrumented.

**A UNIT COST THAT DID NOT REPRODUCE, SAID PLAINLY:** the desktop came in at **117 MiB/child commit and
161.2 MiB/child RSS**, far below its documented **295 MiB** — only a single-sample peak excursion (308.7)
reached it. **This run did not land near the desktop's unit cost**, and the child said so rather than
explaining it away. Linux landed on its documented **peak** (140) rather than its plateau (113), and its
commit delta matched its own children's RSS (139.2 MiB/child) almost exactly. The mac mini matched its
documented **229 MiB resident** while its commit-analog under-read it at 104.

**A TRANSPORT FINDING WORTH KEEPING:** on `zabz-tech-linux`, children launched as
`node bin.js --profile headless` **die instantly with `MISSING_CREDENTIAL: llm-deepseek`** — only
`/usr/local/bin/dsh`, which sources `/etc/dsh-worker.env`, authenticates. That is the node's documented
executor rule proving itself under a new measurement.

THE LIMITS, volunteered: every child ran a trivial prompt (one `hostname` plus six sleeps), so these are a
**floor, not a full turn's cost**; and whether the desktop's 295 MiB/turn reproduces under this instrument
at all is now an open question rather than an established number.
