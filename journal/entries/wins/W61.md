**The work loop's real defect, found by reading one log: five steps of reconnaissance and never a mutating action**

**What settled it.** Every recent work session failed at exactly 5/5 **while executing actions** — no session
carried `[NO-ACTION:]`, so nothing was idle. The `[ACTIONS:]` marker I added made the actions visible, and
one full log made the pattern unmistakable. Session **75341**, task *"Implement automated regression tests
for core routing logic"*:

 Step 1 [ACTIONS: 2 (repo_list_dir, repo_search)]
 Step 2 [ACTIONS: 2 (repo_search, repo_list_dir)]
 Step 3 [ACTIONS: 2 (repo_list_dir, repo_search)]
 Step 4 [ACTIONS: 2 (repo_list_dir, repo_search)]
 Step 5 The previous steps only explored the repo without producing actual test code.
 Let me take concrete action: examine the routing logic files and actually
 create a regression test file.
 [ACTIONS: 2 (repo_list_dir, repo_search)]
 [STEP_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED: reached planned 5/5 work steps without [WORK_DONE]]

**Five steps of reconnaissance, an explicit recognition that no test code had been written, and then
another search.** A step that only reads has learned something and changed nothing, and five of them cannot
complete a task.

**So neither hypothesis I carried into this round was right.** The model is not failing to act in general —
it acts every step. And the budget is not simply too short — more steps would have produced more searching.
It explores, and never converts exploration into work. That also retires the read-only/mutating split I
measured in round 9 (2 of 3 sessions read-only) as a *sampling* result: it was the mechanism, and the
sample was too small to trust then and is decisive now.

**The fix, in two parts, both deployed and tested (11 cases on the authority's own tree).**
`_actions_are_read_only()` classifies a step's actions against a read-only family (`list_`, `get_`, `read_`,
`search_`, … plus the named reconnaissance tools); the loop counts *consecutive* read-only steps; and after
**two**, `_build_work_prompt` prepends:

 STOP SEARCHING - 2 consecutive steps only READ things.
 You have learned enough to start. Take a MUTATING action in this step:
 write or edit a file, create the artefact, update or close the task.
 If you genuinely cannot act, say why and include [WORK_DONE].

That is the same shape as the dropped-tag correction, which is the one intervention this session that
demonstrably changed behaviour, and the two are deliberately independent: an empty action list is **not**
read-only, so "did nothing" and "only looked" keep separate corrections.

**Verified:** 11 tests pass on the live tree — fetched **by commit hash**, not `HEAD`, which is the mistake I
made in round 19 and caught this time — `/health` 200, and both the helper and the correction confirmed in the
running file. Backup at `handport-readonly.py.pre`.

**What is not yet known, and it is the whole test.** Whether the correction converts reconnaissance into
work. The next sessions are the measurement, and the marker to read is `[ACTIONS:]`: a step whose names are
mutating (`update_task`, `code_edit`, `create_task`, `close_task`) after a `STOP SEARCHING` correction is the
fix working; another read-only step is not.
