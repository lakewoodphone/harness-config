work. Branch `lpt/period-report-20261009` off the long-term tracking branch
(34366d74). Commits `0c4507da` (the report) and `8856c42f` (the engine fix),
pushed to origin.

THE PROBLEM THIS REPLACES. Three times in one night a report was hand-rolled and
three times it was wrong the same two ways, both invisible in the output:
 (a) a per-day DISTINCT metric summed across days, so one person who texted on
 three days counted three times -- customers.py warns about exactly this;
 (b) rows whose timestamps cannot be trusted bucketed by date anyway, inflating
 Jul-Sep 2026 by up to 3x (P2890).
A hand-rolled answer is not a tool, and the fourth question would have produced a
fourth variation. So the rules now live in one place.

`scripts/finance/lpt-period-report.py` (--grain daily|weekly|biweekly|monthly,
--months N, --group money|counts|people|all, --json, --md PATH):
 * money and counts from the long-term store's day series; each period prints
 measured days, hole days, and the days the STORE ITSELF calls PARTIAL (a
 lower bound). A hole is not a zero and never prints as one;
 * people metrics recomputed from source as a set union of normalized phones
 across the whole period, never summed from days;
 * the shop's own business calendar per period (open / shut / unknown), so a
 fortnight the shop was shut for cannot be read as a decline;
 * people/first deliberately NOT totalled, with the reason printed every run;
 * grain comes from lpt_analytics.periods (the fixed-Monday biweekly epoch),
 never a local reimplementation.

THE ENGINE FIX. `config.UNDATEABLE_SMS_MESSAGE_ID_PREFIX = "ptf-comm-"` plus
`customers._dated_sms_filter()`, applied to `dialpad_events`,
`dialpad_event_counts` and `dialpad_first_contact`, each now taking
`include_undateable: bool = False` so the exclusion is visible at every call site
and reversible. `tests/test_lpt_undateable_sms.py` pins both directions on a
synthetic fixture. Full LPT suite: **328 passed in 76 s**. Live before/after on
2026-08-15..2026-08-28: outreach_people **264 with the fix, 316 raw**.
