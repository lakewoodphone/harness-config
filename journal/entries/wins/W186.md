**W186 · 2026-09-16 · RattleByte DMP-BD84 resolved: wrong-model firmware was the whole defect; correct file + emptied stick + board left connected = flashed and paired in one trip.**

**A multi-day, vendor-blocked customer case closed in one trip to the unit, and the fix was a file, not hardware.**

Panasonic DMP-BD84 + RattleByte region-free board, ticket #188880. The player refused the Initial flash with
"This USB device is incompatible" through every earlier attempt. The cause: the USB stick carried the **wrong model's**
Initial firmware (the BD83 archive, 130,250,740 B) for a unit the vendor says is firmware-wise a BD90 (correct file
77,730,260 B). The stick and the owner's method were both fine.

**What actually made it work, in order of importance:** (1) the correct-model file, hash-verified; (2) a stick emptied to the
vendor's spec - only `PANA_DVD.FRM` at root, which meant relocating a stale `BUDA\BUDA_PROP.INF` and the old file rather than
leaving them; (3) powering the player on to the Home Screen **before** inserting the stick, per the vendor's fitting sheet,
not Panasonic's generic menu path; (4) leaving the board **plugged in** through the pairing.

**Verification that mattered:** the board LED (steady red then off) could not confirm pairing, and the documented brief green
flash was never seen. The confirmation came from *function* - [EJECT] then [1] closed the tray and the unit restarted itself,
which only happens on a paired board. When an indicator is ambiguous, test the behaviour instead of reading the LED.
