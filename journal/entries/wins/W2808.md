**W2808 · 2026-10-11 · Mesh placement now gated on host-posture: the frozen anchor cannot be dispatched to by any of the three paths. The guard for placement, which is what the incident was about. Plus my decision that the authority is not also a compute worker.**

Mesh placement gated on machine posture, 2026-10-11 23:12Z (child `subagent-29`, artifact `167`, commit
`8b5d7db`). **The frozen anchor can no longer be dispatched to.**

THE PATH THAT WAS UNGATED, traced before anything was changed:
`provider.start()` -> `placer.acquire()` (`lib/placement.js`) -> `broker.place()` names ONE node ->
`nodes[placement.node]` (`lib/nodes.js`) -> `createSshTransport({ target: facts.ssh })` -> the child runs.
**`grep -rn host-posture packages/` was EMPTY: nothing under `packages/**` read a posture file at all.** And
`secratary` was reachable **three ways** - the broker roster, `MESH_TARGET_NODE` (fixed mode, broker never
consulted), and the direct-probe failover ranking. **So the guard that protects service state had no
counterpart for placement, which is the thing this whole incident was about.**

THE GATE, and the design choices that matter:
- New `lib/posture.js` reads `settings/machines/host-posture.tsv` - **the file, never a copy, and re-read on
 EVERY acquisition.** That is deliberate and worth keeping: *a cached freeze is a freeze a later decision
 does not reach.* The duplication a cache would introduce is exactly how `brokerSsh` survived a claimed fix.
- Same three refusals as the service-state guard: **frozen refused with no override; read-only refused unless
 `allowReadOnly` (which never unlocks frozen); undesignated refused.** A node matches through every spelling
 and **the strictest wins**, so a frozen alias stays frozen.
- The broker placer offers non-writable nodes as a **soft `exclude` AND then checks the answer** - because
 `exclude` is soft by the broker's own queue-never-amputate rule, so no guarantee lives there.
- On refusal: **the lease is released unspent**, the ledger records `state: posture-refused`, and **no ssh
 transport is ever built.** An unreadable designation refuses **before** the broker is asked.
- All three reach paths gated, including the fixed one - so `MESH_TARGET_NODE=secratary-ts` is closed.

THE `secratary` ENTRY: **kept and gated, not retargeted** - and the reasoning is right. It is the measured
record of a real machine; retargeting `ssh` alone would leave the half-migrated `driver`/`bin`/`cwd` pair that
took a node down on 2026-10-07/08; and **naming a node `secratary` while pointing it at `secratary-cloud` is
the exact alias confusion `AGENTS.md` §5 exists to prevent.** Instead the broker roster
(`packages/mesh-broker/nodes.json`) marks the row `"excluded": true` with the reason in `_excluded_on_purpose`
pointing at `host-posture.tsv` as the authority - the roster's own mechanism, so **the broker spends no lease
on a node the dispatcher must refuse.**

**A DECISION IT LEFT TO THE OWNER THAT IS MINE, AND I AM MAKING IT: no cloud node row.** It wrote none, and
justified it in part with *"secratary-cloud is the sole writer of /root/authority.db - running model turns on
it is the owner's call."* **That is a resource and architecture judgement, not his.** My call, recorded:
**the authority does not also serve as a compute worker.** It is the sole writer of the company database, a
2 vCPU / 7.7 GB box, and the one that was OOM-killed at 15:17Z today when too much ran at once. Model turns
belong on the workstations. It also measured the cloud read-only and found **no verified dsh bin and no
`headless` profile there** (`/root/dsh-current` and `/etc/dsh-worker.env` absent), so a row would have been a
guess - correctly refused.

**THE UNHOOKED GATE IS HOOKED.** `make-preset-rows.mjs --check` - documented as living "in CI" while nothing
ran it - is now part of **`scripts/sync.py`, the 15-minute tick that PUBLISHES those artefacts**, gating the
profile step. Drift and cannot-run both **withhold the artefact and name the remedy**, demonstrated live on a
deliberately-staled file. That is the durable half: the check now sits where the thing it checks is produced,
rather than in a CI that does not exist.

PROOF: `plugin-remote-fanout` **222 tests, 220 pass, 2 skipped, 0 fail**, including 15 new in
`test/posture.test.mjs` - frozen refused **with the lease handed back and no transport constructed**, a
writable placement still succeeding, unreadable refusing with `broker.calls.place.length === 0`, and the
**real** posture file asserted so `secratary` is the only barred node. `mesh-broker` **62 pass, 0 fail.** 5 new
pytest tests including the real repo and the real generator. **Nothing was started, stopped, masked, unmasked,
enabled or restarted anywhere, and `~/.dsh` was not touched.**
