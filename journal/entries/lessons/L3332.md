**L3332 · Lakewood: a community-college credential is plausibly free (OCC 216/credit incl books + CCOG + part-time TAG + Pell) while the frum short courses are not aid-eligible at all**

CREDENTIAL ROUTES FOR A FRUM WOMAN IN LAKEWOOD, 2026-10-05 - the finding that inverts the usual advice.

TUITION CAN BE NEAR ZERO AND THAT IS THE POINT. Lakewood is in Ocean County, so Ocean County College is the in-county college: 216/credit INCLUDING BOOKS. CCOG (HESAA) covers remaining tuition up to 18 credits a term plus approved fees for AGI <= 65,000 at >= 6 credits/semester, IN-COUNTY RATES ONLY. A separate PART-TIME county TAG exists (N.J.A.C. 9A:9-3.5, 6-11 credits, 774-1,161/semester) - the common belief that part-time forfeits TAG is FALSE at a county college. Pell 2026-27 max 7,395, prorated by credits/12. Rule-based estimate (NOT an award letter): at 6 credits/semester with CCOG funded, direct tuition is near zero; without CCOG, Pell plus part-time TAG plausibly covers the ~2,592/yr. THREE CAVEATS: below 6 credits CCOG, part-time TAG and part-time EOF all fall away at once; CCOG was cut 18% in FY2026 with a further 25% proposed for FY2027 (appropriation unverified); and OCC's actual participation must be confirmed with its financial aid office. THE INVERSION: the frum short courses are private, non-degree and NOT Title IV aid-eligible - full cash, up front, largely non-refundable - and the Design Alive NCCRS credit route runs ~220-700+/credit versus OCC's 216 including books. The expensive-looking option is the cheap one.

WHAT THE DEGREE ACTUALLY SAYS: OCC's A.S. is Media, Communication and Design with a Graphic Design CONCENTRATION, and OCC's own catalog states a concentration "is not a separate major or credential and will not appear on the diploma or transcript"; OCC itself says a bachelor's is typically required for graphic-design roles. Stronger career-terminal design programmes are Passaic/Mercer/Middlesex/Camden, but SCHEDULING eliminates several for a part-time working student (Mercer: some courses daytime only and its fully-online degrees exclude design; Middlesex: design degrees not among its six evening programmes). GEORGIAN COURT UNIVERSITY IS AT 900 LAKEWOOD AVENUE, LAKEWOOD NJ - a women's university with a Graphic Design B.A., 840/credit part-time, 648 online, and portfolio-assessment credit at ~136/credit: the natural bachelor's completion target in her own town.

BEST FRUM CREDENTIAL FOUND: WITS Baltimore (Women's Institute of Torah Seminary & College) 25-credit Graphic Design Certificate, 4,990 TOTAL, 12 months, women-only, MSCHE-accredited institution with a flexible ONLINE option, real client projects + internships + portfolio website - the SAME price as TTI's 4,900 five-month course, which is unaccredited and whose own footer says "TTI is not a college or university and does not offer credit bearing courses or degrees in New York State." UNRESOLVED CONFLICT: WITS's own tuition page says 410/credit, so the 167/credit figure appears certificate-specific - resolve by phone. TTI's "up to 15 college credits by exam" is only PARTIALLY verified: the archived 2025 page named Coopersmith Career Consulting; the live 2026 page removed the name; the awarding college is unsourced.

EMPLOYER EVIDENCE CUTS BOTH WAYS: BLS says web/digital interface design grows +6% at a 104,000 median and that requirements "range from a high school diploma to a bachelor's degree" with demonstrated work able to substitute - but the HBS/Burning Glass finding is that after degree requirements were deleted, fewer than 1 IN 700 hires went to a non-four-year-degree candidate (second-hand). Stanford Aug 2026: employment of 22-25 year olds in the most AI-exposed occupations ~19% BELOW trend (15% a year earlier), via reduced hiring not layoffs. Touro LCW has a 54-credit Digital Media Design programme at 1,010/credit; YU/Stern has NO design major at 54,500/yr.
