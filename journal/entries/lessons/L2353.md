**L2353 · Stale tests in the area you are touching hide real regressions: fix or delete them, never leave them red**

Four assertions in tests/test_owner_sms_kill_switch.py had been failing since 2026-09-22 - they asserted the pre-refactor shape of the kill switch (_OWNER_SMS_KILL_SWITCH_PATH in app/sms_service.py, owner_sms_kill_switch_active() with no arguments, no bypass_owner_kill_switch) while the enforcement itself was intact in app/owner_sms_kill_switch.py. Found on 2026-09-23 only because I ran the SMS test selection after changing trim_to_segments, and 4 red tests meant I could not tell my regressions from the noise.

Rule: when you touch an area, the tests in it must be green when you leave, and 'pre-existing' is a finding to resolve, not a label to apply. Verify pre-existence by reverting your own change and re-running (I did: identical 5 failures), then either update the test to the current contract or delete it - a red test that everyone has learned to ignore is worse than no test.

Kept red on purpose, once: tests/test_instrument_truth_w1.py::test_sms_summary_counts_distinct_deduplicated_messages (expects activity_source, an in-flight communications_contract rework owns that file) - left alone to avoid colliding with an active workstream, and recorded in H2468.
