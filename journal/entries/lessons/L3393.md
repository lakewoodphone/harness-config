**L3393 · journal-commit's ten red rows were a real divergence, not a broken job - and the empty stderr column hid it**

THE PACKET'S OWN HEURISTIC FAILED HERE. `sources/cron-failures.py` tells the woken session: "if
the error column is empty, the job exits non-zero on purpose (a check that reports a finding) --
then say so and stop." For `journal-commit` the column is empty for the OPPOSITE reason: the
crontab payload THROWS THE OUTPUT AWAY. `crontab -l | grep journal-commit` decodes to

 /home/zabz/bin/journal-commit.sh >/dev/null 2>&1 && /home/zabz/harness-config/scripts/autosync.sh >/dev/null 2>&1

so both halves' stdout and stderr go to /dev/null and `cron-wrap.sh` has nothing to record. The
job was not reporting a finding; it was FAILING, and the one line that said why had been
redirected. The reason was on disk the whole time, in ~/.harness-config-autosync/autosync.log.

WHAT WAS ACTUALLY WRONG (2026-10-08 01:30Z-03:45Z, 10 consecutive rc=1). The authority's
~/harness-config checkout was genuinely diverged from origin/main: local `main` held generated
`journal/index/*` autocommits made by `journal-commit.sh` / `converge-authority.sh` (the tree is
dirty within a minute of every commit), while origin/main advanced with other machines' journal
entries and code. `scripts/autosync.sh` is fast-forward-only BY DESIGN (L1845), so it did exactly
what it documents: preserved the local commit to refs/heads/diverged-secratary-20261008, refused
to merge, and exited 1 ("attention").

THE REPAIR (D2711 policy: never auto-resolve; a session measures, preserves, then reconciles).
Both sides' changed paths were disjoint, so `git merge --no-edit origin/main` was clean and
conflict-free, and the local commit was already preserved on the diverged ref. Merged and pushed
twice (03:55Z HEAD 64d2d644, 04:02Z HEAD c2998fa5) -- the second time because the 04:00 tick
re-diverged within five minutes. Proof rows in ~/.cron-status/runs.tsv:

 2026-10-08T04:02:08Z journal-commit 0 1
 2026-10-08T04:12:11Z journal-commit 0 3
 2026-10-08T04:15:01Z journal-commit 0 5

RESIDUAL, NOT FIXED. The divergence recurs whenever the authority's generated index churn and
another machine's push land in the same 15-minute window. The real fix is the one already written
down and deliberately deferred: stop TRACKING `journal/index/{journal.db,entries.tsv,stamp.json}`
(converge-authority.sh lines 3-16; P129) so the checkout is not dirty within a minute of every
commit, and reconcile by REGENERATING the index rather than merging it. That changes what
harness-config tracks and needs verification on every machine, so it was not done from a cron
repair.

DO NOT "MAKE IT HONEST" BY MOVING autosync's exit 1 TO 3. It looks like the estate's
condition-not-fault convention (CONDITION_EXIT in sources/cron-failures.py) and it is not:
cron-failures.py treats rc=3 as a job WORKING and files nothing, and this packet is currently the
ONLY thing that escalates a diverged authority. rc=3 would silence it and leave the machine frozen
on old config while looking healthy (L1845, L2852) -- the exact failure those entries exist to
prevent. Escalate divergences; do not quietly relabel them.
