**L2131 · L2139 - before concluding the company is quiet, check that its main loop is completing; a wedged loop looks like idleness**

Measured 2026-09-20. For several rounds I reported that measurements were blocked because "the company
is quiet this evening" - 5 work sessions in an hour, 4 tool calls in 12 minutes, `run_once` traffic
near zero. I treated low traffic as a property of the environment and said I would wait for more.

It was not quiet. **`run_once` had timed out 5 times in 6 hours and completed 0 times** (P2138/P2129).
The autopilot's main loop was wedged by a re-entrancy flag that only a `finally` could clear, and
nothing was running.

**The lesson is about what "not enough data" can mean.** I had two competing explanations for low
traffic - "the company is idle at this hour" and "something is broken" - and I chose the benign one
without testing it, repeatedly, across several rounds. The test was two commands:

 journalctl -u secretary-api --since '6 hours ago' --no-pager | grep -c 'run_once timed out'
 journalctl -u secretary-api --since '6 hours ago' --no-pager | grep -ciE 'run_once.*(complete|ok)'

**Rule: when a measurement is blocked by low volume, the FIRST thing to check is whether the thing
that produces the volume is running at all.** Absence of data is a finding about the producer before
it is a finding about the world. This system already has a rule against treating an empty result as
evidence of health; this is that rule applied to *throughput* rather than to a query.

It also cost real time: several rounds of "waiting for traffic" that were actually waiting for a
wedged loop to unwedge itself, which it could not do by design.

**Corollary for the audit itself.** I fixed a completion predicate, repo paths, a rate limit, a
temp-file leak and a finder of findings - all real, all verified - while the loop that runs them was
executing once every ~72 minutes and completing nothing. A defect that stops the system from running
makes every other defect's measurement meaningless. When several measurement attempts come back
"insufficient traffic", stop and audit the producer before auditing anything else.
