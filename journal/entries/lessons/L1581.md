**L1581 · CORRECTION to L1572: the family chat's real unrecorded-delivery count is 19, not 151 — I counted carrier attempts and retries as lost messages**

L1572 reported "151 of 1448 carrier messages (10.4%) absent from our database" and
called it loss. That was wrong, and the storage workstream caught it. Corrected by
independent measurement, not by accepting the claim:

WHAT WAS WRONG
1. A Twilio message row exists per ATTEMPT, not per delivery. A send the carrier
 rejected (error 21610, "unsubscribed recipient") is in the log with
 status='failed'. It never reached anyone, so our table lacking it is not a lost
 family message. In the 2026-08-19 window, 109 of 117 entries for one member
 were rejections.
2. send_message retries. Eighteen distinct bodies produced 109 carrier entries
 because each was POSTed six times. Counting entries counted one message six
 times.

Twilio's own record for +18482407257 on 2026-08-19..08-20, pulled independently:
 117 entries | statuses {'failed': 109, 'delivered': 8} | codes {'21610': 109}
 27 distinct bodies: 18 attempted six times (all failed), 9 attempted once (all
 delivered, earlier in the day while she was still subscribed)
So nothing was ever delivered to her that evening, and our outbox marking those 18
as 'dropped' was substantively right. My "two tables agree and are both wrong" claim
was wrong too: what was actually wrong was only the MESSAGE row, left at
status='queued' with sid=NULL by a drop that never reconciled it.

CORRECTED NUMBERS (30-day window, instrument v2, arrival-only)
 1429 carrier entries: 1313 arrived, 115 rejected
 1315 our rows
 24 arrived entries with no row by SID -> 5 already stored under another SID
 -> 19 GENUINELY UNRECORDED DELIVERED MESSAGES
Of those 19: 13 are '[Family Chat] ...' admin notices, 6 are Chip replies.
Rejected by code: {'21610': 109, '11205': 5, '30038': 1}, billed .115.
Attempts per logical message: 19 messages needed more than one POST.

MONEY (storage workstream, from Twilio's own price field): billed .53 vs our
recorded .82 - an under-count of .71/month (4.6%) against a cap, not
10.4% of the money. Failed attempts ARE billed ~.001 each, which I had assumed
was free.

THE LESSONS, WHICH MATTER MORE THAN THE NUMBER
- "In the transport log but not in ours" is not a measure of loss. Only entries
 that ARRIVED count. An instrument that reports loss must filter on delivery
 status and de-duplicate by logical message, or it reports the retry policy as
 data loss.
- Twilio's date_created is RFC-2822 ('Tue, 15 Sep 2026 15:50:45 +0000'), NOT ISO.
 A filter on [:10] silently matched nothing and produced a false "0 messages"
 reading. When a filter returns empty, suspect the filter before concluding
 anything about the data - an empty result is a refusal, not a finding. I nearly
 reported "the agent is wrong" off this mistake.
- Two independent records agreeing is not confirmation. Here I had it backwards:
 the two records agreed AND the carrier was right, and it was my reading of the
 carrier that was wrong.
- A subagent contradicting the manager's evidence is the most valuable message in
 the system. Verify it rather than defending the earlier claim, and correct the
 record in public.
