**L3779 · A quiet SMS log is not a healthy channel - the machine got quieter while his experience got worse**

**Measured 2026-10-13, the owner text line.**

Week by week, distinct turns that actually reached his phone:

| week | delivered | his replies | truncated | 00:00-05:59 | reminders | his frustration | refusals logged |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 09-23..09-29 | 22 | 12 | 0 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 5 |
| 09-30..10-06 | 31 | 37 | 0 | 7 | 7 | 7 | 1,481 |
| 10-07..10-13 | 38 | 29 | **8** | **15** | **16** | **9** | 524 |

The raw machine noise FELL by 65% in the week he said *"the system's getting worse, not better"* and
*"it's upsetting"*. Truncation went 0 -> 8, small-hours deliveries 3 -> 15, repeated reminders 2 -> 16,
and his expressions of frustration 2 -> 9.

So: **a metric that counts attempts is not a metric about the recipient.** The thing to count is what
arrived, at what hour, in what shape, and whether he had to say it twice.
