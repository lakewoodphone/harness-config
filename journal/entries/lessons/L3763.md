**L3763 · A second probe that shares the first one's assumption is not independent verification**

MEASURED 2026-10-13. The persona walk reported '[blocker] No work order could be opened from the Work Orders hub' on all four viewports. Before repeating it I wrote a second probe, read the live DOM, got tableRows:0 / totalLinks:3 / elementsMentioningWO:[], called that confirmation, reported it to the owner as serious, and put it at the top of the roadmap.

IT WAS WRONG. The Counter renders 25 rows: a ul[data-testid=staff-open-rows][data-row-count=25] of li > button[data-testid=staff-open-case-1427], each min-h-20 w-full touch-manipulation. The walker looked for 'table tbody tr, [role=row]' and the screen is a ul, so it matched nothing.

WHY MY SECOND PROBE WAS NOT INDEPENDENT: it searched for WO followed by digits because I assumed the rows were work orders. The open queue holds TICKETS (Ticket 1427). Both methods encoded the same two wrong assumptions - the list is a table, and the rows are work orders - so their agreement carried no information. Agreement between methods that share an assumption is the same mistake counted twice.

THE RULE: when verifying a negative, the second method must differ in its ASSUMPTION, not merely its technique. Ask what each method believes about the shape of the thing before it looks. If both believe the same shape, only one test has been run.

THE COST: a false 'the shop cannot see its work orders' reported to the owner as serious. The markup even carried data-row-count=25 the whole time and nothing read it - which is the fix shipped in PR 391: read what the SCREEN says it has, not what the tool found.
