**L12387 · The grading band was not monotonic in usage - 48.7 h of talk graded better than 1.8 h**

2026-10-26, ZABZ-TECH. Two E4810s through the resale flow. Both AUTHORISED, both clean, and the
register showed one of them graded BETTER than the other while being far more used:

 558800571376 317 calls, 175,489 s of talk (48.7 h) -> normal -> grade B
 558800311530 43 calls, 6,448 s of talk (1.8 h), 16 texts -> heavy -> grade C

Cause: usage_profile() in cli/lpt/condition/grade.py scores points - calls>=20 OR talk>=900 gives
one, talk>=3600 gives a second, SMS>=10 a third, photos>=20 a fourth - and "heavy" needs three. Talk
time therefore saturates at two points no matter how extreme, so a handset used for two days of calls
cannot reach "heavy" on talk alone, while a light user with a texting history can. Grade drives price,
so this is a money bug, not a cosmetic one.

Fixed: talk >= 43,200 s (12 h) is a third step, keeping the band monotonic in the wear it describes.
558800571376 now grades C/heavy; the other ten rows are unchanged. 43 condition tests pass.

Rule worth keeping: when a score is built from thresholds, check it is monotonic in the underlying
quantity before a number from it reaches a price. Two handsets on the same bench were enough to show
it was not.
