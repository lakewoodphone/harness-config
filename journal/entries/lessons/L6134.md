**L6134 · Clause 3 consolidated: audited, bounded by the statement PDFs, four explanations disproven**

2026-10-13. Clause 3 after five further rounds: the audit is finished, the recovery is bounded, and I
disproved four of my own explanations doing it. Consolidating so none of them is re-tried.

WHERE CLAUSE 3 STANDS. "Full historical coverage audited and recovered where recoverable":
 AUDITED - yes, and thoroughly. 925 account-months, every reading carrying its source, 119 anchored,
 164 derived, 642 unknown, 69 disagreements summing 55,319.21, measured by `finance-reconcile.sh` on
 a weekly clock. Its docstring states the program's completion bar: (1) explained, (2) reconciled
 against the account's own balance movement, (3) no unnamed exception - and only (1) was ever measured
 before today.
 RECOVERED WHERE RECOVERABLE - not done, and now BOUNDED by one missing input rather than by unknowns:
 the statement PDFs. The alert emails this machine can read carry `Statement balance`, a due date and
 a minimum payment. They carry NO transaction list. I read five of them end to end.

FOUR EXPLANATIONS TESTED AND DISPROVEN, so a future session does not spend the rounds I spent:
 1. STATEMENTS MIS-PARSED. The oscillation of `personal-chase-9650`'s balances (548.64 -> 4,079.69 ->
 714.13 -> 396.72 -> 4,996.74) looked like the reader taking the wrong one of two `Statement balance`
 figures. Checked against the raw message text, five months, five exact to the cent:
 $4079.69, $396.72, $4996.74, $2965.33, $3912.92. **The anchors are right and the balances really do
 swing that far.**
 2. UNMARKED CROSS-SOURCE DUPLICATES in the window. Measured: 37 rows, all live, all one source, ZERO
 groups sharing (date, direction, absolute amount).
 3. A SAME-DATE DUPLICATE has to be tested on the DATE. I first tested this requiring identical dates
 and found nothing - which cannot catch a charge recorded twice five weeks apart. When I widened it
 the pair appeared: two charges of exactly 2,481.72 at `PAYPAL *REEBELOUSAI` inside one month, 93 per
 cent of the gap.
 4. THAT PAIR BEING ONE PURCHASE RECORDED TWICE. Disproven by the PayPal feed: there are TWO separate
 PayPal transactions, 2025-02-18 and 2025-03-17, each mirrored on the card. Two charges, each with
 its own transaction behind it, is two real purchases. The owner bought the same items twice.

THE CASE IS STILL OPEN AND I AM LEAVING IT OPEN. `personal-chase-9650` 2025-03: 37 real unduplicated
rows, anchors verified to the cent, no missing bank-side payment, both large charges PayPal-backed, and
5,347.72 of recorded charges not reflected in the balance movement. Four explanations, four disproofs.
The next step is the statement PDF and there is no substitute for it.

TWO FACTS WORTH KEEPING FROM THE DISPROOFS THEMSELVES:
- **`personal-paypal` nets to ZERO.** It records every transaction as a debit AND a credit, same date,
 same amount, same description. So a PayPal purchase does not move that account's balance in either
 direction, and any flow computed over it measures nothing. It is an itemisation account, not a
 balance-reconcilable one.
- **A rank, a source label and a statement are all provenance, not proof.** I treated `statement` as a
 guarantee of correctness for two rounds. It is now verified for five months of one account - which is
 the only reason I can say the reading is right and stop looking there.
