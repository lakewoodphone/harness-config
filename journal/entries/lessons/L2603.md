**L2603 · origin/test moves under you: ~40 worktrees share one .git, so a sibling's fetch rewrites the ref mid-run**

Two sibling worktrees share one .git, so refs/remotes/origin/test moves under you.

MEASURED 2026-09-28, worktree _worktrees/phone-and-tech-full/lpt-custdetail.
`git fetch origin` at 03:00 gave origin/test = 4ca125d91; I branched from it. At
04:05 `git rev-parse origin/test` was 927644852 ("Merge remote-tracking branch
'origin/test' into lpt/integrate-all-20260925") although I had not fetched again:
a sibling worktree ran its own fetch/push against the SAME shared .git and moved
the shared remote-tracking ref.

CONSEQUENCE: the definition-of-done check `git diff --diff-filter=D --name-only
origin/test HEAD` reported 3 deletions that were not mine -
frontend/src/pages/ContactPage.tsx, its test, and
WazePage.moneyGate.test.tsx. All three are ABSENT from my base and PRESENT only
in the moved ref, i.e. they are a sibling branch's ADDITIONS, not my deletions.
`git diff --diff-filter=D --name-only 4ca125d91 HEAD` (my real fork point) is
empty, which is the true answer.

RULE: in this repo, resolve `origin/test` ONCE at the start, record the sha in
your report, and run every "what did I change" comparison against that recorded
sha - never against the live `origin/test` ref. Prove a deletion claim with
`git cat-file -e <base>:<path>` on both refs; if the file is missing from the
base, the ref moved and the "deletion" is somebody else's addition. Retargeting
onto the newer ref is the integrator's job (see the sibling
lpt/integrate-all-20260925 branch), not each worker's.
