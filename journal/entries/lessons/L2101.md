**L2101 · L2099 - the step-budget gate failed sessions whose task the same session had already closed**

The single largest measured defect in the AI company, fixed 2026-09-20.

**Symptom.** 804 of 1,146 failed work sessions (70 %) in 14 days carried
`STEP_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED: reached planned 5/5 work steps without [WORK_DONE]`.

**Cause.** `app/autopilot.py` judges completion by the model emitting a `[WORK_DONE]` token, and a
record-based fallback (`_task_is_closed`) already existed for when the token never comes - its own
docstring records that on 2026-09-14, 664 of 679 failed sessions (97.8 %) had their task marked
`done`, i.e. the work finished and the session was failed anyway. **But `_task_is_closed` was
consulted only INSIDE the step loop.** A session that closed its task on its *final* step ran out of
budget and fell straight into `_close_unfinished_work_session` at the gate below, without anyone
asking the task row.

**Fix.** At the step-budget gate, before recording a failure, consult the task's own status - exactly
as the in-loop guard does. The artifact gate stays **outermost**, so a task closed without the
required evidence is still blocked rather than completed. Verified: `app/autopilot.py` compiles, and
28 tests pass across the new gate test plus the four pre-existing completion tests
(`test_session_completion_follows_the_task`, `test_work_session_completion_truth`,
`test_work_session_status`, `test_no_false_task_completion`).

**Why it escaped its own tests.** The suite pinned `_task_is_closed` itself and the *in-loop* call
site (`test_the_loop_consults_the_task_record` in `test_session_completion_follows_the_task.py`), but
the gate that actually fires at 5/5 was not covered. The fix's regression test
(`tests/test_step_budget_gate_consults_task_record.py`) pins the ORDER: the record is consulted
before the session is closed as unfinished, and the artifact check comes first. Order is the whole
fix - the same three calls in a different order change nothing.

**Honest limit.** The new test is source-level, not behavioural: driving the real 5-step loop with a
simulated model would require a fake that emits exactly the right replies, which proves nothing about
the real model. The behavioural evidence has to come from the data - watch whether
`STEP_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED` sessions with a `done` task disappear from new rows.
