THE WRONG INFERENCE, and why it looked right: the 05:24:40Z push to `test` ran its deploy jobs to SUCCESS, so I reasoned the account could not be blocked at 05:24Z — and it could not. But 05:24Z was BEFORE the block: the three PR runs died at 10:17Z. "A deploy succeeded earlier today" is not evidence that a later job started. The only evidence that a job started is a step count, and the only evidence for WHY it did not is the annotation.

TWO RULES THIS RE-CONFIRMS
1. A 3-second, zero-step failure is not self-explanatory. Read the annotation before naming a cause; never infer billing, or its absence, from another run's colour.
2. A queue row marked `resolved` records that a condition WAS true and someone acted. It does not prove the condition is false now — the account was funded on 2026-10-20T15:50Z and blocked again by 2026-10-21T10:17Z. A resolution is a dated fact, not a standing state.
