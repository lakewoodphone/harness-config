**L3355 · The keep-alive guard probe reports POSTURE BROKEN on every deploy and is wrong**

deploy-backend.sh exits 3 with 'GUARD POSTURE BROKEN' on a successful deploy, every time. It probes two tRPC MUTATIONS with GET: auth.requestPasswordReset and customerAccount.requestClaimCode. Measured again 2026-10-07 on production: GET -> 405, POST -> 200 and POST -> 400. Both refuse an anonymous caller correctly; the probe is asking with the wrong method and reading 405 as an auth failure.

The cost is real: an operator who trusts the alert rolls back a good deploy, and an agent that trusts it spends a turn investigating a non-incident. Rule: after any production deploy, exit code 3 alone is NOT evidence of a problem. Check that the container is healthy and that /health reports the sha you intended, and treat the two ALERTs as known noise unless the GET->POST relationship changes. Evidence and the exact probe pairs are in H3312/H3313.
