**L3875 · rf-watch is unexercised: built, parser-tested, never run on a handset - don't quote it until it has**

Addendum to H3664, minute later. H3664 says the work is complete and committed. One caveat belongs on the record so the next session does not trust a tool it has not earned:

`rf-watch` (lpt.condition.rf, `lpt rf-watch`) has NEVER run against a live handset. It was built while the phones were on the cable and the moment came to test it after they had been unplugged - all five gone by 20:0x. What is proven: the parser against real `mServiceState`/`mSignalStrength` strings (unit test), and the failure path (`device not found`, exit 1, clean message). What is not proven: the sampling loop, the artifact writer, the in-service percentage, the transition list. Do not quote an rf-watch result until one exists.

Also unexercised: nothing else. The bench app, charge-test, telephony and usage reader all ran on all five.
