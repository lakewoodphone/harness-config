**L3103 · Write down the parking level the moment you park — ten seconds at the start, half an hour at midnight**

MEASURED 2026-09-29: the party parked in the Empire Outlets garage, 55b Richmond Terrace, Staten Island, before an evening in Manhattan. Nothing recorded which level. They returned at 23:55 off the last ferry, went up on P1, and the car was on P3. About 25 minutes lost at midnight, and the drive-home ETA moved from ~01:20 to 01:33 — for the want of a level number. The trip file at the time said only 'Lot — Ferry Terminal South, 3 Ferry Terminal Viaduct, or Empire Outlets garage' as *options*, because the plan could not settle which would be used in advance.

THE RULE: any trip plan that parks in a structure must carry a line for the answer, filled in at the moment of parking: LOT + LEVEL + nearest bay marker (or a photo of the level sign). On a multi-storey garage the level IS the address. Same class of failure as 'take the ferry' without naming the operator (L3102): the plan compressed a real, distinguishing detail out of the instruction because it did not know the answer yet — and the fix in both cases is to leave a blank that gets filled in, rather than a general statement that reads as complete.
