**L1856 · A registration that cannot fail is not an instrument: /healthz agentLoopsRunning is blind to headless children**

MEASURED 2026-09-17 03:58Z + the calibration stream's 83 samples. A real 'node <dsh>/bin.js --profile headless <task>' child ran ON ZABZ-YOGA and sessions.agentLoopsRunning read 10 before, 10 during, 10 after; 20 s later with nothing dispatched it read 11. On the TARGET node the calibration stream saw agentLoopsRunning:0 and governor.inUse:0 in all 83 samples with 8 real headless children (+3.0 GiB commit, 41.5% CPU). Headless children are separate processes and never register in the resident engine. RULE: any acceptance field must be shown to MOVE when the thing it measures happens, and to stay still when it does not - otherwise it certifies nothing. WHAT TO USE INSTEAD: count processes by command line (runners = a node whose line names the dsh entry point and is not the engine; the engine is 'bin.js web', which has NO --profile) and/or the broker's own lease state, which is the only place a dispatched fleet is visible while it runs.
