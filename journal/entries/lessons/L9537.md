**L9537 · Owner-queue probes were answering the wrong question; eight live rows invisible**

Lesson: the owner queue's probes were hiding eight live rows by answering the wrong question.

Measured 2026-10-19 on authority.db, 79 pending rows: five personal Google Voice texts, three
300DDR emails and one insurance notice were reachable ONLY with `queue --all`.

Three distinct defects, one shape (a probe read something adjacent to the row):
1. customer-contact read dialpad_sms_cache for every row. GV is his personal line; Dialpad is the
 shop's. Five texts (Zellie x2, Genack x2, a pidyon-haben invitation) came back unknown -> held.
2. already-sent matched the ACCOUNT as well as the sender, so an inbound vendor mail in our own
 mailbox counted as a sent reply. Ticket 74115 was called "approval moot" while never answered.
 It also matched on snippet: a pasted JotForm prefill looked like a reply.
3. work-order and customer-contact took the phone from the quoted mail underneath the row.
 (323) 230-0622 and 800-437-5556 are signature-block switchboards; production was read for
 them, failed, and held two live rows.

THE RULE, for every future probe: read (a) the record the row NAMES and (b) the row's own words.
An evidence blob pasted under a row is the counterparty's text, not ours, and it is not a record.
Corollaries worth keeping: a toll-free number is never a customer's device; a negative from an
index you just read is a measurement, not "unknown"; and a gate whose only escape is a fresh
verdict turns "no probe exists" into "he never sees it" - which is how these eight disappeared.
