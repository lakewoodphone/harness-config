**L3891 · lpt ticket's comms panel prints FETCH time, not message time - it made an answered case look unanswered**

Measured 2026-10-20 on WO26102012759 (Pollok, 8452636081). The order's COMM communications panel printed 2026-10-20 17:39:05 for five texts, all on the same second. The dialpad_sms_cache message epochs (ps_comms_thread ts) are 1792516369..1792517530 = 17:12:49Z to 17:32:10Z. So 17:39:05 is when the rows were FETCHED, not when the texts were sent: a 26-minute error, uniform across the burst because they were pulled in one fetch. Consequence: the 17:11:41Z FOLLOWUP note on that order (4543) said the customer's 17:05:56Z message was UNANSWERED, and the reply had in fact gone out 17:13:51Z-17:15:10Z. Rule: for any claim about WHEN someone said something, use the dialpad_sms_cache / dialpad_call_full epoch and convert it; never quote the lpt ticket comms-panel clock. Second tell: a group of messages sharing one identical timestamp is the signature of a fetch, not of a conversation.
