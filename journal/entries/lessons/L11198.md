# A payment row is a claim, not money — and the shop's bank statements are on ZABZ-YOGA

Owner, 2026-10-20, on being told a customer had paid twice: *"That is very unusual because customers
do not do that. So I want you to verify both of those end to end."* He was right to doubt it.

## The case

WO2601150002 (order 199). Two `payment_transactions` rows of 140.00 each, both SUCCEEDED: a Stripe
terminal charge (2026-01-19) and an "Offline payment / ZELLE" row (2026-01-21). A 2026-10-18 note on
the order had already declared `DOUBLE CAPTURE CONFIRMED` on the strength of **the two rows compared
with each other**. It never looked at a bank.

End to end, the card was real (Stripe live: captured, settled, no refunds, payout reached the bank).
The Zelle was not. The owner's uploaded statements — BofA 04/2025-10/2026 with 185 Zelle lines and
personal Chase 9730 10/2024-10/2026 with 177 — contain **no 140.00 Zelle in that window at all**
(the only two 140.00 rows in the whole BofA file are a June credit from MBY Seminary and a July debit
to Avi Shapiro). Payment row 305 was one of fifteen offline payments typed by user 101 in a single
catch-up run on 2026-01-20/21.

**The rule this yields:** for money, `payment_transactions` is a claim. The statement is the fact.
Comparing two claim rows to each other cannot detect a duplicate — they agree because they were
entered together.

## How the statements are read (they are not in any database)

The owner uploads bank exports to `C:\Users\ezabz\Downloads` on **ZABZ-YOGA**. On 2026-10-19 those
were `bank of america.csv`, `Chase9730_Activity_20261019.csv`, and three card-activity files.

- `scp` to that host fails with `Connection closed` (its sshd refuses scp).
- `ssh zabz-yoga-ts 'Get-Content ...'` mangles quoting: the remote shell is **PowerShell**, and the
 path contains a space.
- What works, byte-exact, is base64 with the path in single quotes inside a double-quoted local
 argument:
 `ssh zabz-yoga-ts "[Convert]::ToBase64String([IO.File]::ReadAllBytes('C:\Users\ezabz\Downloads\bank of america.csv'))" | base64 -d > boa.csv`

BofA CSV layout: five summary lines, a blank line, then `Date,Description,Amount,Running Bal.`
Chase 9730: `Details,Posting Date,Description,Amount,Type,Balance,Check or Slip #`.

## Why this matters beyond one order

14 of the 27 "ZELLE" rows in production reconcile to no bank row; two of the fifteen in that January
catch-up do. So the offline-payment record is a mix of real receipts and data-entry noise, and a
refund decision taken from it alone can pay out money that was never taken.
