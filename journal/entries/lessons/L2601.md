**L2601 · Owner text is a conversation: one at a time, one thing, no briefings**

The owner's texts are a conversation, and the rules are his, verbatim (2026-09-28):
"you send me one text at a time with a very clear request or piece of information and I
send you back an answer or thank you or don't tell me such information again... I do not
want like these briefings or globs of information or anything of this sort."

Four rules, one enforcement point: `app/owner_text.py` on the authority
(`/home/zabz/personal-secretary-mvp`).
 I1 one at a time -- a second owner-bound message waits until he answers (12h window;
 urgent/critical may interrupt)
 I2 one thing -- <=320 chars, no markdown, no bullet list, no section label,
 at most one question mark
 I3 in a conversation -- his reply closes the open turn; our reply opens a new one
 I4 stop means stop -- "don't tell me about X again" suppresses X; a blanket stop is
 honoured except for a real emergency

MEASURED, and the reason this exists -- `sms_log` on the authority:
 * 2026-09-27 07:30:16 -> 07:41:26, ids 990-1013: the same 1,736-char "Morning briefing"
 submitted to Twilio every 30 seconds, 25 times, every attempt rejected with
 "The concatenated message body exceeds the 1600 character limit";
 * then the LLM variant, then an evening briefing, then
 "URGENT EMAIL / From: / Subj: / Acct:" -- four labelled fragments and no ask;
 * nothing answered him when he replied.

WHY THE OBVIOUS FIX WOULD NOT HAVE WORKED. The authority's `.env` already said
`OWNER_BRIEFINGS_ENABLED=false`. It did not matter: `settings_overrides` held
`owner_briefings_enabled=true` and `owner_briefing_max_chars=2000`, written 2026-05-04,
and `app/main.py:1124 _apply_effective_settings_from_overrides()` copies DB override rows
onto the settings object at every startup. A behaviour guarded only by a settings flag can
be reversed by a row nobody remembers writing. This one is in the send path, and the two
stale rows were deleted (values backed up at
`/tmp/owner-briefing-overrides-backup.json` on the authority).

THE PATTERN, in one line: when the owner complains about what a system SAYS, put the rule
where the message is composed, not where it is configured.
