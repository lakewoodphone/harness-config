**L2338 · L2057 · How to pull up a phone DSH chat: it lives on secratary at ~/phone, session logs are zstd-compressed JSONL**

WHERE: the phone-facing DSH instance does NOT run on this laptop. It runs on `secratary` with cwd `/home/zabz/phone`, preset `zabz`. Its sessions are at `secratary:~/.dsh/sessions/--home-zabz-phone--/` (53 sessions as of 2026-09-18). This laptop's `~/.dsh/sessions` holds only this laptop's sessions - a phone chat is never there.

FORMAT: one directory per session, each containing `session.v3.jsonl.zstd` - zstd-compressed JSONL, one event per line, every line carrying a `type` field: `session`, `session/title`, `user/message`, `assistant/message`, `tool/call`, `tool/result`, `step/start`, `session/title`. The first line is the `session` header (id, createdAt in ms epoch, cwd, agentPreset, parentSession, origin).

GUI SESSIONS vs SUBAGENTS: directories named `session-<uuid>` are GUI chats the owner actually had. Plain `<uuid>` directories are subagents - their header line has `origin: subagent` and a `parentSession`. When he says "the chat I had", read the `session-<uuid>` ones and ignore subagents (a fleet of 10+ subagents is normal and they dominate by count).

HOW TO READ: `zstd -dc <file> | python3` on secratary (both present at /usr/bin). Node on this laptop also has `zlib.zstdDecompressSync`, so local `~/.dsh/sessions` logs can be decompressed and scanned in-process without installing anything.

THE TRAP THAT COST THE MOST TIME: his wording is often dictation of something else. He asked for "the chat about jockish" - the chat is titled "Groin itch powder and ointment" and is about **jock itch**. A literal case-insensitive search for `jockish` across 715 local session files and all 53 phone sessions returned ZERO. Do not conclude "not found" from a literal search of a dictated word. Instead: (a) search the topic he is likely naming, and (b) enumerate sessions by mtime with their `session/title` + first user message, which identifies the chat in one pass (this is what worked).

EVIDENCE: `session-d5868ca1-ef18-488c-aca7-90590947a64b` = the "jockish" chat, extracted to `C:\Users\ezabz\code\phone-transcripts\2026-09-18-groin-itch-powder.md`. The title event arrives twice - the first is a truncated echo of the first user message, the second is the model-generated title; use the last one.
