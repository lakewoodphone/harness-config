**L3121 · Size a mesh wave to the provider's concurrency limit, not to this host's memory: 10 of 12 children died at balance 0, limit 5**

A mesh fleet wider than the provider's own concurrency limit is a fleet that dies, and the failure looks like a hundred different bugs.

MEASURED 2026-09-30, ZABZ-TECH, dispatching 12 verification children in one message:

 subagent-81 failed RATE_LIMIT: "Your current concurrency is 5, which exceeds your
 concurrency limit of 5 ... Please top up your balance to restore your concurrency"
 subagent-c7… failed QUOTA: Insufficient Balance
 subagent-88 failed QUOTA: Insufficient Balance
 subagent-89 failed QUOTA: Insufficient Balance
 … 10 of 12 children died this way in one wave

Every one of them reported as a DIFFERENT symptom at the parent: "the remote one-shot exited 1",
"produced no final message", a stderr tail that was the child's own last reasoning line. The parent
cannot see the provider's reason. Ten children burning a turn each, and the record of what happened
is a fragment of somebody's thinking.

WHAT IS ACTUALLY TRUE

- The harness's own `admission_governor` said `22 free, 0 waiting` and granted 12 slots. It is a
 MEMORY budget on this host. It knows nothing about the model provider's concurrency ceiling or its
 balance, which is where the kill actually came from.
- The provider ties concurrency to balance: at this tier the ceiling was 5 concurrent requests, and
 an empty balance drops it to zero.
- The child's reason travels nowhere. `job_output` on a failed mesh child returns "(no new output)"
 (journal P2249b) — the child crashed at settlement and its last message was lost.

THE RULE THIS PRODUCES

1. Size a mesh wave to the PROVIDER's concurrency limit, not to this host's memory. On this account
 that limit was 5, and it is shared with the session that dispatches. Waves of 4 worked; 12 did not.
2. Before a wave larger than 4, establish the balance and the ceiling, or accept that a dead child is
 indistinguishable from a broken prompt.
3. A fleet that dies at dispatch must be RETRIED, not diagnosed for an hour. The dispatch failure is
 cheaper to test than any hypothesis about it.

AND THE PART THAT SHOULD NOT BE TRUE

The harness already has the place to put this: the governor reports budget and queue, and a mesh
provider that knows its own concurrency limit could report it in the same line. Until it does, the
cap is an operator habit, which is exactly the class of thing this journal exists to convert into a
mechanism.
