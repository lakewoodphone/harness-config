**L2377 · The 'progesterone slows GI motility 30-50%' figure is unsourced — stop repeating it**

Found 2026-09-24 while researching cramping. The claim sits in docs/wedding/aygestin-after-effects.md (the Aug-18 symptom analysis, source line 6: 'PMC Alqudah et al. (2022). Progesterone slows GI motility 30-50%. (via Dr. Amy Killen)') and I repeated it in conversation as the mechanism for gut cramping. A dedicated search found NO primary human study, review or label carrying that number. Evidence: the human literature is contradictory; the best-designed variability study (Degen & Phillips, Gut 1996, n=32) concludes the effect 'must be small and of doubtful clinical significance'; the large percentages that do exist are from PREGNANCY (rat gastric emptying ~+46%, human third-trimester transit ~+83%), not from progestin therapy. What IS labelled and real: bloating and constipation (Utovlan SmPC side-effect table) and 'stomach/abdominal cramps/bloating' as a common patient-reported effect on the US norethindrone 5 mg leaflet.

Lesson beyond this drug: a mechanistic percentage inside an agent-written research file is not evidence. Two agents in this repo's wedding/health docs carry such numbers; when one is repeated into an answer it acquires authority it never had. Before reusing any statistic from a prior session's file, check whether that file cites a retrievable primary source — the source line above named a podcast-adjacent secondary ('via Dr. Amy Killen'), which was the tell.
