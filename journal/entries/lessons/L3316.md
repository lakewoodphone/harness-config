**L3316 · The shop auto-reply is not an answer, and it hid 23 live customer conversations**

MEASURED 2026-10-05, dialpad_sms_cache on the authority.

THE BUG
The shop sends an auto-reply beginning "Thanks for texting Lakewood Phone & Tech". It is an OUTBOUND row and it fires ~0.7 s AFTER the customer's inbound row. Any "unanswered" test of the form "is the newest message per phone inbound?" therefore counts the auto-reply as our answer and silently drops the conversation.

Measured: 108 conversations have a customer message and no real reply. 23 of them had been made invisible by exactly this. Two were live that morning:
 7323306591 inbound "Are you open today?" 13:19:26.627Z, auto-reply 13:19:27.275Z
 8483336341 inbound "Is the laptop in?" 12:16:53.733Z, auto-reply 12:16:56.231Z

THE CORRECT TEST
 conversation = a phone with >=1 inbound in the window
 answered = some OUTBOUND row exists whose body_text does NOT start with
 'Thanks for texting Lakewood Phone & Tech%' AND whose created_at
 is strictly GREATER than that phone''s newest inbound created_at
 unanswered = otherwise
Build in build_queue.py. Never let the auto-reply into the "answered" side.

ALSO: the auto-reply fired at 12:16 and 13:19 on 2026-10-05 while the shop was open
(Mon-Thu 10:30-5:30) and told two customers we were closed. The hours gate in it is
wrong too.

RELATED: 48 phones in the window have outbound traffic but NO inbound. They are not
conversations and must be excluded from the denominator, or "answered" is inflated.

ROWS: window since 2026-09-01 = 10,836 rows / 165 conversations with inbound /
57 answered / 108 unanswered / 23 auto-reply-masked.
