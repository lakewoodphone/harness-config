**L6210 · collected 0.00 is a refusal, not health - a Stripe Terminal payment lands UNATTACHED with no charge id, and my own query had already found it**

## 2026-10-14 · ZABZ-TECH · Greenfeld: the payment was never missing - it was UNATTACHED, and I asked the owner for something my own query had already returned

**WHAT HAPPENED.** The owner said "he paid 90 for 75 plus 15". The order read `collected 0.00`. I concluded the money was not in the store and asked him for the Stripe charge id so `lpt-record-payment` could write a row. He then had to paste his own Stripe screen to prove his own customer had paid. **The row had been in `payment_transactions` since 2026-10-13T17:11:43Z - id 3313, 95.96, SUCCEEDED - and MY OWN EARLIER QUERY HAD ALREADY RETURNED IT, with `orderId: null`, and I read past it.** He was right to be angry.

**WHY IT LOOKED EMPTY.** A Stripe **Terminal** payment arrives carrying `stripePaymentIntentId` and **no `stripeChargeId`**, with `orderId` and `customerId` NULL. `collected` sums SUCCEEDED rows **for the order**, so an unattached row counts for nothing. **`collected 0.00` is not evidence of non-payment; it is evidence that no LINKED row exists.** Search `payment_transactions` for an unlinked row of the right amount and date BEFORE concluding anything, and before asking anyone.

**FIXED.** Row 3313 is linked to order 2883 / customer 1295 with its charge id `ch_3UQ9EmLnlgYj1gmT06PEBatw` backfilled; `collected` now reads **95.96**. The backfill is not cosmetic: `lpt-record-payment` de-duplicates on `stripeChargeId`, so a charge-less row can be recorded a **second** time and double-count the money - after the backfill it refuses: *"already recorded … Nothing to do."*

**NEW TOOL, because this was an estate gap and not only my error.** Verified 2026-10-14: **no `UPDATE` against `payment_transactions` existed anywhere** in the app or its scripts, so nothing could attach an existing payment to an order. **`~/bin/lpt-link-payment`** (source `lpt-ops/bin/lpt-link-payment`) does it, with guards that refuse rather than warn - a non-SUCCEEDED row, a row already on a different order, collected pushed above the total without `--force`, a charge id already on another row - and a race-safe `"orderId" IS NULL` UPDATE. Documented in `lpt-ops/docs/writes.md`.

**THE FIGURES, right on the third attempt.** 95.96 = 90 x 1.06625, so the owner's 90 is the **pre-tax** basis and the terminal adds 6.625 pc. With the medium at 30 not 15: basis 105, customer-facing total **111.96**, paid 95.96, **balance 16.00**. Case file `price` 111.96 and `payment_state` partial; production total 111.96; production note **4083** supersedes **4082**; ledger seq 419.
