**L6462 · A handover is done when the surface the READER sees carries it: queue #530 kept a stale question under a live recommendation**

## Round 3 — the handover itself had a defect, and it is fixed

**The queue row the world was pointed at carried a stale question.** `owner-queue.py amend` can change
a row's `context` and `recommendation` but **not its `question`** — so after I amended #530, the owner
would have read:

> *"Who brings it back, and is it you or should a session be allowed to re-provision it on a machine
> you name?"* — next to a recommendation saying *"Create a Cloudflare API token…"*

A question that no longer describes the world, answered by a recommendation about something else. That
is the exact failure the wake skill names: *"An item is a statement about the world at FILING time.
When the measurement moves, the item moves with it, or the next shift spends its opening minutes
chasing a ghost."* I had moved the measurement and left the item behind.

Worse, it was invisible from the table alone — it only showed up when I read the **mirror**
(`journal/state/owner-questions.md`), which is what a session without authority access reads. The
mirror had the old question and the new recommendation side by side.

FIXED, and the old row was not quietly rewritten:

* **#530 resolved** with `--how` recording that its question IS answered — by solving it, not by
 deferring it. Nobody brings the runner back, because the deploy no longer uses a self-hosted runner.
* **#533 filed** carrying the real remaining decision (create a Pages-scoped `CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN`),
 recommendation, the measurements behind it, and what it blocks. Queue guard: *"passed clean"*. The
 mirror was refreshed: **31 open, 501 closed**.
* **the three live pointers repointed** from #530 to #533 — the status instrument, the lpt-website
 registry note, and this document's actionable line. One occurrence each, refused otherwise, modes
 preserved, idempotent. The doc's *historical* #530 mentions are kept: a record of what happened
 should not be rewritten, only its "what to do now" line.

**THE LESSON, WHICH IS NOT ABOUT THIS ROW.** A handover is not done when the information is recorded;
it is done when the SURFACE THE READER ACTUALLY SEES carries it. Three surfaces existed here — a
queue row, a mirror of that row, and two live scripts that cited its number — and the one the owner
reads was the one that was wrong. **Check the reading path, not the write.** This is the same shape as
pain P2942 ("the owner channel can be closed without saying so"): filing succeeds at every step and
still nothing useful arrives.
