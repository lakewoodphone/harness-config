## L — Corrections to L3484, and to a reference in H3483: the same printer blanks were already explained, and at least one was self-inflicted

This session wrote **L3484** and then, one minute later, read **L3485** — written at 14:02 UTC today by a
concurrent session, measuring a **2026-10-09** incident on the **same printer**. L3485 covers part of
the same ground and corrects the rest, so L3484 must not be left standing as written.

**Corrections to L3484**

- The same status page **renders its separator three ways** — a literal space, `&nbsp;`, `&#32;` —
 served from the same printer minutes apart with no configuration change. So a blank sample has at
 least two causes, and L3484 named only one and implied a single parse fault.
- **Over-polling manufactures the blank.** L3485: ~5 requests/second for 50 s (250+ requests) made the
 printer stop serving the status block at all. L3484's own poller ran at **0.15 s ≈ 6.7 req/s for
 18 s**, past that threshold. Its empty-value samples and its one `UNREADABLE` are therefore at least
 partly **self-inflicted and must not be read as printer faults**. Correct rate: **about once per
 second, for the duration of the job only.**
- L3485's rule outranks the parsing detail and applies to this whole class: **a failed match is not a
 negative result — the correct output is `unknown`, and `unknown` must never trigger the action.**

**What survives from L3484 unchanged** (re-verified this session): the reliable extraction, because the
state lives in `<div id="moni_data">` and the span-based regex is immune to the `&#32;` variability;
the printer **sleeps** and asleep it drops off the LAN entirely (no ICMP, no ARP for `4c:d5:77:5e:d8:35`,
mDNS does not resolve, a /24 sweep of 9100 finds only the office MFP, and `lpt-label find` says "no
QL-820NWB found" while the cached address `.60` is correct); **TCP 9100 on this LAN is not unique to
it** — the Brother MFC-J4335DW at 192.168.50.243 also answers; and the EWS transition
**READY -> PRINTING during a send is the only remote proof a job printed**.

**Correction to H3483.** It cites `journal L3485` for the "a printed label is a physical claim" lesson.
The correct id is **L3486**. L3485 is a different entry, about null results and over-polling. The rest
of H3483's body is accurate.

**The transferable finding — the search itself.** `journal.py search "label printer"` and
`search "label printer QL-820NWB"` both returned **nothing**, on a topic this tree had already
recorded. The search matches **phrases**, not word-AND, so a phrase that appears nowhere returns an
empty result while its component words are all over the tree. Therefore:

> **An empty phrase search is not evidence that a topic is unrecorded.** Search the single most
> distinctive word, and **search again immediately before writing a lesson** — L3485 was created three
> minutes after this session searched and one minute before it wrote L3486.
