**L3641 · The ask_user_question widget is unreadable on the owner's phone - plain text is the standing format (3rd recurrence)**

**L · The ask_user_question widget is dead on his phone — plain text is the standing format, and this is the third time**

RECURRENCE, 2026-10-13. I delivered the one owner question of the session through `ask_user_question` and he
replied: *"I think you forgot that using the ask a question tool on the iPhone doesn't work very well. I
can't see like the question and the answer is properly."* He had already told me twice:
- 2026-09-18 — *"Don't use the ask a question tool, you still haven't resolved and made it work on this
 phone, I can't read it normally — just ask the question plain text."* (that session: FOUR widget calls,
 every one answered blind or not at all — P326)
- 2026-09-23 — *"I can't see the question when you use the answer a question tool on the phone."* (P2189)

**The rule, now written into the shipped preset so it cannot be forgotten again:** never deliver an owner
question with `ask_user_question`. Write it in plain chat text — question in prose, options as numbered
lines, recommendation marked on the one you recommend, **question last**, never a wall of text before it.
On every machine, every session, because he works from the phone most nights.

**Why this is worse than a cosmetic bug:** the widget is the single most important primitive in this seat —
"one question, options, one recommended" is exactly what he asked for on 2026-09-11. If it renders
unreadably, correct reasoning upstream is worth nothing and the turn stalls on a question he cannot answer,
which is the failure rule 1 exists to prevent. The cost is one full round trip per question, plus a lost
turn when he never answers.

**Fixed in:** `presets/zabz/agent.cordis.yml` rule 2 (bolded, with the three dated quotes and a pointer to
P326/P2189 in the text, so a future session reads the rule rather than re-deriving it). The widget's own
phone rendering is still unverified and stays open under P326.

**A future me must not read this as "the tool is bad, avoid it when convenient".** It is unusable **for
him**, which is the only audience that matters here.
