**L1723 · Settings were being applied faithfully and nothing was delivering them**

**L· 2026-09-15 · Settings were being applied faithfully and nothing was delivering them**

MEASURED on DESKTOP-FGV6KMH (Yocheved's laptop), 2026-09-15 22:20-22:42 EDT. This is the reason a
launcher fix from the morning did not exist on her machine in the evening, and it is a different failure
from the one it caused.

WHAT WAS WORKING: `PersonalSecretary-HarnessSync` runs every 15 minutes and runs `scripts/harness-sync.mjs`
in APPLY mode. Its log is healthy and specific --

 harness-config sync (portable)
 repo : C:\Users\cheve\code\harness-config
 machine : DESKTOP-FGV6KMH.yaml
 mode : APPLY
 settings: settings.yaml: written (1451 bytes, self-check passed)
 verify: preset=danger-full-access provider=deepseek-proxy model=deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp

WHAT WAS NOT WORKING: the `repo` that line names is a copy with **no `.git` at all** (`Test-Path
.git` -> False). It cannot pull. Nothing refreshed it. Its newest non-journal file was **2026-09-14
22:45** while the repo moved on all day. So the applier faithfully applied a frozen input, every fifteen
minutes, and reported success every time.

WHY THE MACHINE COULD NOT PULL, which is the honest part: `harness-config`'s origin is
`secretary-ts:/home/zabz/harness-config.git`, her laptop is on a separate network with no Tailscale, and
`repo-sync.mjs` -- which does exactly this job for her other repos -- never had the harness-config repo in
its list. Three separate mechanisms existed (sync.py, sync.ps1, harness-sync.mjs) and **all three apply;
none of them deliver.** Applying and delivering are different jobs and the fleet had only one of them.

THE FIX, and it uses the one channel that reaches her box: `scripts/push-harness-config-to-laptop.ps1`
builds a `git archive` of origin/master, pushes it with the exec API's `/write`, pushes
`scripts/apply-harness-config-archive.ps1`, runs it, runs her own `harness-sync.cmd`, and then **reads
back `HARNESS-CONFIG-APPLIED.json` from her machine and refuses to record the commit as delivered unless
the tree id matches**. Scheduled as `PersonalSecretary-PushHarnessConfig` every 15 minutes.

THREE THINGS THAT WERE WRONG IN THE FIRST ATTEMPTS, all worth keeping:
1. **`journal/` must not travel, but `journal/tools/` must.** Excluding the whole directory froze
 `journal.py` and `rehash.py` on every receiving machine; the first scheduled delivery reported
 `updated=1` of 261 files. The record is per-machine; the tools are code.
2. **60-second exec cap.** The first applier hashed every file twice with cmdlets and did not finish
 1,584 files inside it, so no delivery could ever complete. .NET `[IO.File]::Copy` plus a single
 `SHA256` instance, and an archive without `journal/` (~260 files), finish in about five seconds.
3. **An equal length is not equal content.** The fast version compared lengths and called a file
 unchanged; a same-length edit would have been silently skipped forever. It hashes on the length-match
 path -- the check is the point of the mechanism.

*Rule:* when a machine's job is to apply a configuration, ask separately **how that configuration gets
there**, and make the delivery prove itself with an artifact the receiving machine wrote. "The task
exited 0" is not evidence -- hers exited 0 every fifteen minutes for a day against a frozen input.
