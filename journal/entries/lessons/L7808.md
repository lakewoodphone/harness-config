**L7808 · A match that does not contain the thing asked about is not evidence**

A match that does not contain the thing being asked about is not evidence.

Building the pre-ask ladder (`preask.py`, 2026-10-18) I wrote "did he already answer this?" as a
substring search. First live run: a 418.77 charge at "spider corp" -- an amount no record contains
-- was reported **solved** by a memory about a 25.00 co-pay, and a 114.04 charge matched his own
text "I don't know. I want you to figure this out", which is the opposite of an answer.

Two guards fixed it, and both generalise:

1. `_discriminates(text, amount, terms)` -- an amount question is only answered by text that
 contains the amount AND a merchant term. No amount, no evidence.
2. `deflected` -- "I don't know / figure it out / check my emails" is a distinct outcome from
 `not_found`. He was asked and told us to work; re-asking is the defect, and quoting a deflection
 back as "you told me" is worse than asking again.

The same defect was already in the estate: `merchant_resolve.resolve()` resolves by merchant NAME
and ignores the amount, so it "solved" the same 418.77 charge at conf 0.92 from a text about
25.00. It was written to answer "what merchant is this", and it is being called to answer "what was
this charge". Two questions, one function, no amount in the predicate.
