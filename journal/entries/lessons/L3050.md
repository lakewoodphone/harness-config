**L3050 · In RC aircraft the letter code decides whether the box is a gift or a parts kit**

Measured 2026-09-28, HobbyTown Toms River, advising the owner in the aisle by phone photos.

**The trap:** the bold **2** in a yellow circle on an E-flite box is **BNF Basic - Bind-N-Fly.** There is **no transmitter in the box.** Several are also missing the battery and charger. The owner photographed **fifteen** models carrying it: T-28 Trojan 1.2m and 1.1m, UMX Waco, Night Vapor, Timber X, Viper 30mm EDF, UMX P-51D, UMX Pitts, Apprentice STS 1.5m, Micro Scrappy, Ultrix, Micro DRACO, Gee Bee R-2, UMX Cirrus, UMX Me 262.

**The big BNF boxes look like the better product, and often are. They are just not complete ones.** Buying the T-28 as a gift would have walked out with an aircraft that cannot fly.

| Marking | Included | Must add |
|---|---|---|
| **RTF** Ready To Fly | radio, battery, charger | nothing |
| **BNF / BNF Basic** | receiver only | **transmitter**, battery, charger |
| **PNF / PNP** | airframe, motor, ESC, servos | transmitter, receiver, battery, charger |

**GENERALISE: in any hobby where the same model is sold at several completion levels, the price tag does not tell you what is in the box, and the model number usually does.** RTF / BNF / PNP / ARF / kit are completion levels, and a 130 BNF next to a 160 RTF of a similar plane is not the same purchase at a discount - it is two-thirds of a purchase. **Read the completion level before the price.** Same class of failure as reading a price without reading the size, or a kashrus claim without an authority.

**Also worth keeping:** the two tags that were actually checked both came in **below list** - Traxxas T-Maxx 3.3 at 349.95 against a 549.95 list, Sport Cub S 2 RTF at 159.99 against a 206.99 stated retail. Both Traxxas and Horizon Hobby enforce **minimum advertised pricing**, so 'Amazon will be a quarter of the price' is false for new product in MAP-controlled hobbies - and that is worth saying out loud, because the instinct to check is right even when the answer is 'the shop is fine'. What is cheap used is someone else's problem.
