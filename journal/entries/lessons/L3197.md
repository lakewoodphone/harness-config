**L3197 · A plan document is not a measurement - the 123.8 GB free that had become 44.3 GB**

WHAT HAPPENED: `play-tonight.md`, `pc-gaming-profile.md` and `top-4-games.md` were written on
2026-09-30 as a PLAN, then read on 2026-10-01 as if they were current state. The controlling number
in all three was "the Yoga has 123.8 GB free". Measured on ZABZ-YOGA 2026-10-01 ~00:55 EDT the real
figure was **44.3 GB**. An intermediate handoff (H3109) recorded 52.8 GB. So the number had moved
twice and nobody re-measured.

THE CONSEQUENCE WAS NOT COSMETIC: those documents' number-one recommendation is Mass Effect
Legendary Edition, which needs about 120 GB. At 123.8 GB it fitted exactly; at 44.3 GB it does not
fit at all. A recommendation that had silently become impossible was still being presented as the
top pick. The same files also still instructed the owner to "download It Takes Two first" when it
was already installed (44.80 GB), and to "buy the Xbox Wireless Controller at Walmart" when two had
been bought, delivered and paired.

RULE: when a document contains a number its conclusion depends on — free space, a driver version,
whether something is installed, whether something was bought — that number is a measurement, and it
needs a timestamp and a source. Read the plan, then MEASURE, then quote. Never quote a plan's number
as current state.

HOW TO CORRECT IT: a dated correction block, not a rewrite. Leave the original planning text intact
so the distance between what was planned and what is true stays on the record. Pushed as lpt-hub
`10bbd03cb`.

EVIDENCE: 2026-10-01 ZABZ-YOGA — `Get-PSDrive C` 44.3 GB free; `Get-ChildItem 'C:\Program Files\EA
Games'` 44.80 GB and 63.67 GB; `Win32_VideoController` and `Win32_PnPSignedDriver` both reporting
32.0.101.9033.
