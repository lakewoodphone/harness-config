**L3410 · Dreame HHR32A floor washer (owner's unit): hard-floors-only, mode list, and the rotten-egg fix — manuals cached locally**

What it is: Dreame T12 Pro / HHR32A cordless floor washer (US sibling 'Aero Pro' = HHR32B, same platform, same controls). Owner's unit: production date 01/2026, North American label (CSA, EN/FR).
Durable facts from the official manual (his model, PL manual PDF for HHR32A + EN manual for HHR32B, both cached in ~/phone/manuals/dreame-hhr32a/):
1. Surfaces: 'use only indoors, on surfaces NOT covered with carpet, such as vinyl, tiles, protected/sealed wood, etc.' => tile yes, sealed hardwood yes, carpet/rug NO (no carpet mode exists on this model), unsealed/waxed wood NO.
2. Modes: Smart (auto suction+water by dirt level), Eco/Quiet, Turbo (max), Suction (picks up large amounts of water), plus self-clean and drying modes. Resumes last mode.
3. Controls: power switch; mode switch (short press cycles); self-clean button on top of handle (short press = hot-water self-clean, needs battery >=20%; long-press 3 s = standalone drying; short press again within 3 s of start = 5-min quick dry instead of 30-min default; short press during drying ends it); voice-prompt button (short press = voice/volume, hold 3 s = language).
4. NEVER lift, tilt, shove or move the running machine — used water flows into the motor (manual's own warning + 'water leaking from the vent' troubleshooting row). Refitting a damp filter does the same. That is the one smell/corrosion failure that is NOT owner-fixable.
5. Hygiene = the whole game: empty + rinse the used-water tank after every use, self-clean + dry every use, roller rinsed and fully dried, filter rinsed and bone dry before refit, wipe the tube (never rinse), never rinse the brush/head assembly. Filter and roller every 3-6 months. Only the approved Dreame fluid (AWH10, ~10 ml per capful) — nothing else, no vinegar/bleach.
6. Smell = H2S from anaerobic bacteria in used-water tank + filter + roller + tube, i.e. stagnant dirty water, not a defect — unless it survives a full deep clean, in which case it is water in the motor and a support claim (unit is 9 months old).
