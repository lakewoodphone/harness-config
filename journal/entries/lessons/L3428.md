**L3428 · the + click was disk queueing, not launcher logic: InventorySvc at 25-82 MB/s and my own runaway rg at 23 MB/s**

Diagnosed ZABZ-YOGA 2026-10-09 02:10-02:20 UTC, while the owner's "+ new window" click measured 25-32 s.

THE MACHINE, not the launcher: CPU 87-93 % on 16 cores, and DISK BUSY 372-2,862 % across five samples
(`\PhysicalDisk(_Total)\% Disk Time`), 420 processes, 6,780 threads, memoryLoad 71 %. Every cold
process start on this box queues behind that disk: the same 204 KB script parse measured 284 ms in one
process and 7,195 ms in another eleven minutes later, and `pwsh -NoProfile -Command exit` ranged
1,230 ms to over 12 s inside my measurements.

NAMED CAUSES, by measured read throughput (`Win32_PerfFormattedData_PerfProc_Process`, IODataBytesPersec):
 - MY OWN runaway ripgrep, pid 23068: 23 MB/s, 169 % CPU, 21 minutes old, from an aborted search of
 mine over C:\Users\ezabz. KILLED. A leaked pwsh from a 73-minute-old aborted scan was killed with
 it. Neither had finished because I had asked for an unbounded recursive search on a machine whose
 disk was already busy - the fast-search skill exists to prevent exactly this, and I did not use it.
 - `InventorySvc` ("Inventory and Compatibility Appraisal service"), svchost pid 13984: 25-82 MB/s of
 READS, continuously, the largest single reader after my rg was killed. It is Windows compatibility
 telemetry (launched by the WinSAT task). It cannot be stopped from a non-elevated shell - measured:
 "Cannot open 'InventorySvc' service on computer '.'" - so it needs the owner's admin approval.
 - Edge: 31 processes, 5,881 MB, ~16 MB/s across windows, 61,812 s of CPU lifetime.
 - The DSH fleet: 14 agent loops executing, 32 sessions, 15 tool-call runners (633 MB), engine loop
 lag p95 2,298 ms / max 15,806 ms.

SO: a click is not slow because the launcher is slow. It is slow because four cold starts (pwsh, a
204 KB parse, WMI, HttpClient) each wait behind a disk that is 4-29 times saturated. Making the click
fast therefore needs BOTH a warm launcher (no cold starts per click) AND less disk contention. The
first is mine and is designed (H3358); the second is partly mine (do not scan unbounded on this host)
and partly his (a Windows service and 31 browser processes).
