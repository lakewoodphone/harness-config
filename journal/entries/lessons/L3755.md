**L3755 · A migrated subsystem with no scheduler is not migrated: the wake loop sat staged on the cloud authority for 2d18h**

THE CLASS: a subsystem migrated to a new host where its SCRIPTS arrived but its SCHEDULER did not. It
looks installed, it looks configured, and it produces exactly zero evidence forever.

MEASURED, 2026-10-13 01:03-01:20Z, on the cloud authority `secratary-cloud`, after the 2026-10-11
05:43:36Z cutover moved the company off the office box:

 * wake-heartbeat frozen at 2026-10-11T06:39:19Z -- 2 d 18 h stale, and it is the file that answers
 "can anything revive me?".
 * The last dispatch attempt in wake-dispatch.log is 2026-10-11T06:39:19Z and it FAILED
 ("prompt transfer to zabz-tech-ts failed ... no session was started"). It was the only attempt.
 * ZERO wake lines in `crontab -l` on the authority. Not disabled, not masked -- never installed.
 * `run-wake-sources.sh` (the orchestrator that runs all 25 sources) existed ONLY at
 /root/_staging/files/run-wake-sources.sh. The port had copied it and stopped there.
 * 11 wake-loop scripts existed NOWHERE on the authority: run-wake-sources.sh, autonomy-status.sh,
 continuation.py, work-outcomes.py, shift-packet.py, shift-report.py, wake-value.py,
 seed-discovery-items.py, register-projects.py, migrate-stale-flags.py, raise-project-throughput.py.
 * /root/work/work.db was a 0-BYTE file. A woken shift claims from that ledger; empty ledger ->
 `nothing-todo` -> the project is woken and advances nothing.
 * /root/bin/sources/projects.json was ABSENT, so even the sources that existed had no registry.

WHY IT WAS DELIBERATE, WHICH IS THE PART THAT MATTERS: docs/mesh/139-PORT-OWNER-CHANNEL.md marked
run-wake-sources and wake-fanout "NOT ENABLED", and docs/mesh/150-AUDIT-AUTONOMY.md section 3 says
"the cutover's own note says wake was left out on purpose". The named blocker was SPEND: the office box
held ~/.sms-inbox/BALANCE_WARNED with {"state":"paused","balance":5.44} written 2026-10-11T03:41Z.
So the correct reading was never "someone forgot" -- it was "someone stopped this for money and the
money recovered, and nobody re-measured".

RESOLVED BY MEASUREMENT 2026-10-13T01:16Z: `wake-balance.py --guard` on the authority -> balance 30.55,
floor 10.00, warn 75.00 -> not paused, above the floor. At 0.036-0.068 USD per release that is
roughly 300-500 releases of runway.

THE LESSON, AND IT IS GENERAL: after ANY host migration, the acceptance test is not "are the files
there" -- it is "did this subsystem RUN on the new host, and when". For anything scheduled, that means
crontab/systemd AND a fresh heartbeat AND one real end-to-end unit of work. Grep the OLD host's crontab
for every line, and for each one ask which of: installed / deliberately withheld with a written reason
/ silently dropped. And when a reason was "money", RE-MEASURE the money rather than inheriting the
decision -- a paused guard is a statement about a balance at a moment, not a policy.

Sibling evidence: docs/mesh/000-SYSTEMS-REGISTER.md trap 11 -- "a pinned comment is not a fix".
