**L3799 · The company's budget fix moved the failure class, not the outcome: budget exhaustion down, stalling up 9% to 38%**

The company's work-budget change moved the failure, not the outcome — and the next fix is now named by measurement.

Context: edit **P1** of `docs/dsh-evolution-v2/55-company-design.md` (raise the work budget from a hard 5 steps) was applied to the LIVE company at **2026-10-14T07:23:44Z** by a session that was not this program (`/opt/company/app/autopilot.py`, md5 `30a4a185a8e28395092032fc4d367f36`, backup `autopilot.py.bak-item80-20261014T072225Z`). Live now reads `_WORK_MAX_STEPS = 20`, `_WORK_ABSOLUTE_MAX_STEPS = 30`, `_WORK_ABSOLUTE_MAX_TURNS = 60`, and it carries the **P4** evidence-based completion markers.

Measured by me from `/root/authority.db`, read-only, 2026-10-14 ~12:15Z — `work_sessions` started today, split at 07:23:00:

| window | sessions | completed | failed | completed % | STEP_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED | TOOL_LOOP_DEADLOCK | STALLED |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| before 07:23 | 65 | 25 | 34 | 38.5 % | 32 (49 %) | 13 (20 %) | 6 (9 %) |
| after 07:23 | 26 | 8 | 14 | 30.8 % | 5 (19 %) | 7 (27 %) | 10 (38 %) |

Read this carefully, because two different stories fit it and only one is supported:

- **Supported: the budget fix worked at what it targeted.** Budget exhaustion fell from 32 sessions to 5, and as a *share* from 49 % to 19 %.
- **Supported: it did not improve the outcome.** Completion share went 38.5 % → 30.8 % on 26 sessions. At n=26 a difference of 2 sessions is inside noise, so the honest statement is **"no measured improvement", not "a regression"**. Anyone quoting 30.8 % vs 38.5 % as a regression is over-reading 26 rows.
- **Supported and actionable: the failure MOVED.** `STALLED_REPEATING_OUTPUT` rose from 9 % to 38 % of sessions. Removing the budget wall exposed the wall behind it.

**The lesson is the shape, not the number: when you remove the binding constraint from a control-flow failure, the next constraint becomes binding, and the completion rate barely moves. Measure the failure-class MIX, not just the outcome rate — the mix is what tells you which fix is next.** Here it says **P3 (a repeated identical tool call or two empty turns changes strategy instead of ending the session)** is now the dominant residual failure, ahead of P2 and P5, and that P3 is what to build.

Supporting figures from the same reading, all from `model_usage`/`work_sessions` grouped by day: evidence-backed completions (`output_log LIKE '%TASK-CLOSED-BY-SESSION%'`) are **24 today and 0 on every one of the preceding 8 days** — so a qualitative change did land today even though the rate did not move. Spend is flat at 12-23 USD/day (~17 average, ~123 per 7 days); an earlier claim of mine that spend was "accelerating" was a rolling-window artefact and is corrected in `PROGRAM.md`.
