## What this is

Research done 2026-09-16 for a family purchase (owner's mother, ~55, all-day two-screen
office work, presbyopia, eye strain, older small 1080p screens). Recorded because the same
criteria apply to any monitor recommended to a person, not just this purchase.

## What actually reduces eye strain, in order of effect

1. **Physical text size** — the dominant variable. Age-related accommodation loss means the
 fix is bigger glyphs, not a "better" panel. Buy headroom to scale up.
2. **Pixel density** — 163 PPI (27" 4K) vs 109 PPI (27" QHD) vs ~92 PPI (24" 1080p).
 27" 4K at Windows 150% scaling = crisp text at the same layout size as QHD 100%, and
 can go to 175% for genuinely larger text without softening.
3. **Hardware low-blue-light, not software.** Software "low blue light"/night modes yellow
 the whole image — that is exactly what a colour-sensitive user complains about.
 Dell ComfortView Plus is hardware and TUV-certified. Check the certification tier
 (Dell S2725QC = TUV Eye Comfort 3.0 4-star; U2724D = 5-star).
4. **Flicker-free backlight** (no PWM dimming) — RTINGS scores this explicitly.
5. **Matte anti-glare coating.** Reflections are a bigger strain source than resolution.
6. **120 Hz over 60 Hz** — small but real, and it is now cheap.
7. **Ambient light sensor / auto-brightness** — prevents the too-bright-screen-in-a-dim-room
 mismatch. Present on UltraSharp, absent on Plus series.
8. Ergonomics: height so the top bezel is at eye level, pushed to arm's length (~24").

## Picks, with prices as read 2026-09-16 (US)

- **Value: Dell 27 Plus 4K USB-C S2725QC** — 27" 3840x2160, 163 PPI, IPS 1500:1, 350 nits,
 120 Hz, TUV Eye Comfort 3.0 4-star, flicker-free, anti-glare, height/tilt/swivel/pivot,
 2x5W speakers, USB-C 65W PD. Amazon $302.99 (list $349.99), dell.com $349.99.
 **Box contains only a power cable + USB-C-to-C 100W cable — no HDMI/DP cable.**
- **Premium: Dell UltraSharp 27 4K U2725QE** — adds IPS Black 3000:1, 450 nits, ambient
 light sensor, Thunderbolt 4 140W, KVM, 2.5GbE, 3-yr Advanced Exchange + Premium Panel
 Exchange. $799.99 dell.com. RTINGS: "excellent for office", "fantastic text clarity".
 Known defect: multiple coil-whine reports on this model.
- **QHD middle: Dell UltraSharp U2724D** — 27" 1440p 120 Hz IPS Black, TUV 5-star, ambient
 light sensor, DP-in AND DP-out (daisy chain two monitors off one port), includes DP cable.
 $439.99 dell.com. Only 109 PPI, so it is bigger but not much sharper than 22-24" 1080p.
- **Cheap-but-solid add-on: VIVO STAND-V002 dual monitor arm, ~$35** (Amazon B009S750LA) —
 gets both panels to eye level and lets them be pushed back; PCWorld's budget dual-arm pick.

## Trap to avoid

Do not recommend a 27" 1080p "eye-care" monitor (e.g. BenQ GW2790) for text work at this
age: at 82 PPI it is *less* legible than the screens it replaces, regardless of its
eye-care marketing.
