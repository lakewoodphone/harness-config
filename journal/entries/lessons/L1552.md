**L1552 · IAM access-key deletion is eventually consistent: a probe run too early reports the opposite of the truth**

MEASURED 2026-09-15 on secratary. After deleting AKIA4T4OBZYQJVFWTWXJ (account 867344436768), I immediately probed STS with that exact credential from a fresh client. It still returned a valid caller identity: arn:aws:iam::867344436768:user/phone-tech-dev-user. Had I stopped there I would have reported 'the deletion did not work'. I waited 45 seconds, probed again with identical code, and got InvalidClientTokenId. The deletion had worked the whole time; IAM change propagation was the lag.

RULE
- After deleting or rotating an AWS credential, sleep (45s is enough here) and re-probe BEFORE claiming it is dead or alive. A safety claim made in the propagation window is a guess wearing the clothes of a measurement.
- Always pair the probe with a CONTROL: the same call using a deliberately wrong secret. Both probes returning InvalidClientTokenId is what proves the probe can fail; a lone 'it failed' could be a bad request. Both were run here.
- This is the same failure family as P3 (reading the wrong data and believing it): the reading was real, the timing made it wrong.
