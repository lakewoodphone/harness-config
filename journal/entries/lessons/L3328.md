**L3328 · Cheap course vs paid school: 3.13% vs 46% completion settles it, and the school's premium is a critique session worth ~150**

MEASUREMENT THAT SETTLES "cheap course vs paid school". The best completion evidence is MIT/Harvard's edX data in Reich & Ruiperez-Valiente, "The MOOC Pivot", Science 363(6423), 2019: 5.63M learners / 12.67M registrations, 3.13% of ALL participants completed in 2017-18 (down from ~6% in 2014-15), while 46% of PAYING certificate-seeking students completed. Paying plus a deadline moves completion from ~3% to ~46%, and more than half still do not finish. Therefore a course purchase in this field is mostly buying supervision, not information.

PRICE OF THE PARTS (verified 2026-10-05): Udemy list USD 20-200 but promotional 12-25 with lifetime access, Personal Plan from 13/month, 30-day refund that is VOIDED if "a notable amount of the course has been consumed" and does not apply to subscriptions at all. Coursera ~49/month (Google UX ~150-300 total); the FREE AUDIT route no longer exists for the specializations people actually want - both Michigan and CalArts carry the verbatim FAQ "No, you cannot take this course for free... apply for financial aid". IxDF 16.50/month annual. ADPList is genuinely FREE 1:1 with senior designers but the median session is 30 minutes and continuity depends on re-booking. MentorCruise USD 100-300/month is the only reliable continuous human critique. So: a school charging USD 2,450 for ~60 hours is roughly USD 150 per weekly critique session - the same critique costs 100-350 a month bought directly. Pay the school only if the person has demonstrably failed to finish self-guided work; what they are buying then is supervision and they should say so out loud.

ALSO: Coursera completed its combination with Udemy on 11 May 2026 (Udemy is now a wholly owned Coursera subsidiary, delisted from NASDAQ) - "Udemy vs Coursera" is no longer a choice between competitors. And premium bootcamp placement does NOT vindicate the price: Springboard's own state-filed California BPPE School Performance Fact Sheet (published 15 Nov 2025, read myself) reports placement IN FIELD for its career tracks in 2024 between about 3% and 12% with on-time completion 3.2-9%, while its marketing advertises 79-91.5%. CareerFoundry still advertises a 6-month money-back job guarantee and ceased operations on 28 Feb 2026.
