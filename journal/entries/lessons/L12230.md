**L12230 · Cascade audit: nothing needs training, but it emits no per-attribute output, early-exits at 0.15, and the tzniut gap was never measured; A-P0-03 spend withdrawn**

## The cascade audit — owner was right about training, wrong about capability

Mesh brief (ZABZ-TECH, 199 s). Read-only. Settles the A-P0-03 money question without spending anything.

**He was right:** nothing wired needs training. Three pretrained artefacts, downloaded at runtime and
sha256-pinned (server/app/model_manifest.py:99-134). android/app/src/main/assets contains only
tessdata/heb.traineddata — no model ships in the APK — and there is no training script in the repo.

**He was wrong:** the cascade cannot "say what is exposed". It emits ONE fused scalar plus a Decision
(SAFE/BLOCK/WARN/ESCALATE) and category strings (CascadeOrchestrator.kt:56-78, 348-356). There is no
per-attribute output channel anywhere; the scorer that would consume one treats a missing `predictions`
block as "the cascade has not been run yet" (server/scripts/score_attribute_labels.py:97-98). What is
missing is a measurement layer, not a trained model.

Wired: GantMan NSFW MobileNetV2 (5 classes, drawing/hentai/neutral/porn/sexy; score = 1 - P(neutral));
NudeNet 320n (16 body-part EXPOSED/COVERED classes plus face gender, MID tier and above only — licence is
AGPL-3.0/MIT fork, a shipping risk); MediaPipe Pose lite (33 landmarks).
Wired but inert: Cloudflare CLIP ViT-B/32 zero-shot — labels are config, but account id and token are both
empty strings (config.py:539-541). Wired but off: the VLM arbiter, whose prompt asks explicit/violent/
gambling and never modesty. Designed only, absent from code: the selfie-multiclass and hair segmenters (the
geometric coverage layer) and the 12-label EfficientNet.

**THE DEFECT WORTH THE OWNER'S ATTENTION.** The cascade early-exits to SAFE and caches whenever GantMan's
nsfwScore < 0.15 (CascadeOrchestrator.kt:246-256), and the LOW tier never loads NudeNet or Pose at all
(TieredModelLoader.kt:36-45). So on ordinary screens the modesty-capable models never run. And Pose's
"exposure" is body-size-in-frame — shoulderWidth*2 and landmark-distance ratios (PoseLandmarkerWrapper.kt:
133-158) — so a fully clothed close-up scores higher than a distant bikini. There is no skin mask, no
garment edge and no segmentation anywhere in android/.

**The tzniut gap is assumed, not measured.** Its origin is directive 005:52-65, a tilde-prefixed estimate
table with no source; asserted at ROADMAP_AND_DECISIONS.md:540, cited as finding C7 in 015:90 via Q21
(QUESTIONS_AND_CLARIFICATIONS.md:192), then repeated at 016:73. No benchmark, test or dataset in the
estate runs any wired model on a tank top, tight clothing or a beach photo. The only nearby numbers
(24.7% NA-F1, 64.0% macro-F1, 015:189-193) measure cloud VLMs on catalogue imagery and settle neither side.

**Decision taken (mine, not his — the spend is withdrawn):** do not fund the 850-1,600 dollar labelling
pipeline. Run the free measurement first: 200 frames from a shop test phone, the three wired models as-is,
`applicable` and `compliant` labels per attribute, reported as two numbers per attribute. About one hour of
labelling, zero spend, and the pipeline already exists (docs/LABELLING_PILOT.md:17-19, 35-60). The
1,000-frame paid round is under 500 dollars and 3-10 days if the free round shows the gap is real.

**What training would actually buy, if anything:** tight-but-covered fit only — published supervised
baselines 0.45 Fit / 0.35 Skinny (015:441), frozen Fashion-CLIP 21% (015:192). The other four attributes
are better served by segmentation geometry: no training, about 22 MB, Apache-2.0, explainable, and unbuilt.
So roadmap item A-P0-03 is wrong-shaped as a 12-label model but not wrong in direction.

**Docs to correct:** A-P0-03.md (re-scope from money-blocked to measurement-first), ROADMAP_AND_DECISIONS.md:
540 and 016:73 (present an estimate as a finding), GantMan size claim "INT8 10 MB" (measured artefact is
17,355,548 bytes and is the upstream float model renamed), and the licence risk on NudeNet.
