**L3701 · How to present a customer case to the owner - the procedure is now a file, and it must be used**

## 2026-10-12. Written because he had to correct the same presentation six times in one session.

**The procedure is `harness-config/docs/how-to-present-a-customer-case.md`.** Read it before bringing
any customer situation to him. The short version, in the order he wants it:

1. **Who they are** - name, number, and whether the record knows them or they are new.
2. **What we are responding to** - their words verbatim, with timestamp and how long they have waited.
3. **What the record already says** - case file, work order, prior repairs, prior quotes, prior money.
4. **What we can and cannot confirm** - established / not in the record / my inference, kept apart.
5. **One question with a recommendation, or the exact draft body.**
6. **What I have already done or will do without him.**

He reads the first line and the last line: lead with the result or the question.

The five rules that produced that file, each with the incident:

- **`lptcase.py` FIRST** (`lpt-hub`, `python tools/case-index/lptcase.py find|show`). I brought him an
 alarm about an unpaid 660 order from the production database while the case file said closed, paid,
 703.79 collected, receipt already emailed. *"did you pull up her case file and info before bringing
 this to me or you are being lazy"*.
- **The case file beats the database beats the index.** Order `paymentStatus` is not the money truth.
- **Never trust `ps_comms_waiting` alone** - it reported an answered customer as waiting because the
 index predated the reply. Read the cache table in both directions.
- **Read the current state before presenting**, never after being asked - *"now what's the history and
 context of yosef hoff, you didn't give me enough info"*.
- **Every number computed in-session, and no two dollar signs in one paragraph** - his GUI turns a
 pair into inline maths and eats the spaces, which is why he has to say *"you keep making unreadable
 lines like in this pic"*.

Filed alongside the drafting rules (L3681, L3685, L3700). Those are how to WRITE; this is how to
PRESENT. Both are required before a customer message goes anywhere.
