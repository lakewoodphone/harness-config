**L11774 · Before researching alternatives to software the owner already pays for, check whether the vendor shipped the feature in the last year**

Owner asked for alternatives to Spotify because its AI DJ repeats itself and ignores mood instructions. Four research turns in, the answer turned out to be inside Spotify: 'Prompted Playlist' went into US/Canada Premium beta on 2026-01-22 (Spotify newsroom), takes a free-text prompt, uses full listening history, supports include/exclude genres, 'fresher tracks and emerging artists only', explicitly filters tracks he has overplayed recently, refreshes daily or weekly, and annotates every track with why it is there. That is precisely the constraint language he asked me to scope a build for two turns earlier, and it has been live in his market for nine months.
Transferable rules:
1. When asked 'is there something better than X', first ask 'did X ship this in the last 12 months'. Vendor changelogs and newsrooms are the highest-yield source and I reached them last instead of first.
2. He was judging the DJ (a live radio with a voice) while the prompt-to-playlist generator is a different, better feature. In a product with many surfaces, confirm which surface he is actually using before comparing it to competitors.
3. The category truth worth remembering: no streaming service sells a live, vibe-obedient selector that adapts mid-session. Apple Playlist Playground (iOS 26.4), Amazon Maestro (beta, US subset), YouTube Music's AI generator (Feb 2026), Deezer Flow Tuner (Feb 2026) are all generate-a-playlist, not a live DJ. Apple's Discovery Station is the only strict never-heard continuous radio.
4. Two-person US pricing verified 2026-10-20: Spotify Duo 19/mo, Apple Music Family 19.99/mo (July 2026 raise), Amazon Unlimited Family 219/yr, YouTube Premium Family 26.99/mo (June 2026 raise). All six major catalogues now exceed 100M tracks, so catalogue parity is no longer a differentiator.
