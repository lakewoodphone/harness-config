**L — The APN was the only cause. AT&T works, IMSI position is irrelevant, and the realm theory was noise**

**Confirmed on the owner's own device, 2026-10-15 00:04Z.** With `Name` (the APN) set to
`data00.telnyx`, DRN 2007 carries data — and the Telnyx connectivity log for exactly that moment
reads:

```
2026-10-15T00:04:11Z internet mnc=410 ch1-telnyx-internet-01 <- AT&T
2026-10-15T00:04:22Z internet mnc=410 ch1-telnyx-internet-01
2026-10-15T00:08:12Z internet mnc=410 ch1-telnyx-internet-01
```

**Three of my own conclusions died with it, and two of them cost the owner real time:**
1. **"AT&T carries no payload for this SIM" was false.** With a correct APN, AT&T carries data on the
 same SIM that "proved" it could not. **The wireless blocklist I put on the `lpt` group was
 wrong and the owner made me remove it. He was right.** The lane `ch1-telnyx-internet-01…` is not
 a broken path; it is the AT&T path and it was never asked for a usable APN.
2. **The IMSI/realm theory was noise.** The owner switched the SIM back to IMSI5 (`311588`) and it
 works. `mnc210` vs `mnc588` in the realm string correlates with the attach, not with the fault.
 **L4256's "realm = the fault" is retracted.**
3. The `mnc588` realm and the "new SIM batch" story were both artefacts of reading a correlation as
 a cause.

**What actually held up:** Apple's schema. `com.apple.cellular` has no `APN` key; the APN string is
`Name`. L4320 stands, and it took a photograph of the device's own Cellular Data Network page — which
I never asked for — to find it.

**The rule this cost the most to learn, restated because it applies to every "it doesn't work"
report in this estate:** the device was telling us its own configuration the whole time, in a screen
any iPhone shows. I ran a carrier theory, a blocklist, a filter strip and an IMSI hypothesis before
looking at what the phone said its APN was. **For any connectivity fault: read the device's own
settings screen FIRST.** A screenshot is cheaper than a blocklist.

**Deployed:** corrected `waze-telnyx-apn.mobileconfig` (`Name` = `data00.telnyx`,
`AllowedProtocolMaskInRoaming` = 3, bogus `APN` and `AllowedProtocolMaskInInternationalRoaming`
removed) pushed to all seven LPT devices; 2007's filter and kiosk profiles restored. Backups:
`*.bak-before-apn-key-fix-<ts>` and `*.bak-before-name-field-20261013T203913Z` on `waze-mdm-01`.
