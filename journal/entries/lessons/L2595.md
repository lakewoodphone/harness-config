**L2595 · No all-night cinemas in the NYC/NJ radius on a Sunday; drive-ins are the only past-midnight format**

Measured 2026-09-27 ~23:30 ET. Owner asked whether any theater further away stays open very late on a Sunday night. Answer: no.

Fandango <zip>_movietimes via firecrawl stealth, four zips read side by side:
 08701 Lakewood latest start anywhere in radius = 10:30p (AMC Freehold 14)
 08901 New Brunswick latest = 10:40p (AMC Aviation 12, Linden); Rutgers Cinema 10:30p; Regal Hadley 10:15p; Reading Manville 10:00p
 10036 Times Square latest = 11:20p (AMC Empire 25, Resident Evil); Primetime 11:00p; Avengers Endgame: Encore 11:00p; AMC Orpheum 7 11:00p
 10003 / 11211 manhattan+brooklyn latest = 11:20p (Regal Union Square, Heart of the Beast); AMC Kips Bay 15 Primetime 11:15p, Resident Evil 11:00p

Conclusion: on a Sunday night nothing in this radius starts after 11:20 PM. There is no all-night cinema in the NYC/NJ market - the latest starts are scheduled on Fri/Sat. Spending money on "further away" does not buy a later start.
Coverage gap checked separately: arthouses that sell their own tickets are not all on Fandango, but IFC Center's last show tonight was 9:45p, so no loss here.

DRIVE-INS are the only format that genuinely runs past midnight: Delsea Drive-In, Vineland NJ, New Jersey's last remaining drive-in, double features, first feature around 7:45 PM so the second screen runs to about 1 AM. ~1h20 from Lakewood. Its real site is delseadrive-in.com - delseadrivein.com (no hyphen) is a parked domain for sale; do not read that as the business being dead.
