**L2600 · Armpit browning is aluminum residue in the fiber, not sweat volume - cotton/modal release it, bamboo rayon does not; and modal/bamboo must not be tumble dried**

Cause of armpit browning in white undershirts, and the fix, stated so a future session does not have to re-derive it:

- Brown / yellow armpit staining on an undershirt is the aluminum salts in an antiperspirant accumulating in the fabric and oxidising over several wash cycles. It is not caused by sweat volume. Measured on the owner 2026-09-27: he reports undershirts end the day DRY, and the brown still appears "after some washes" - which is the signature of residue buildup, not of soaking. His antiperspirants as of that date: Right Guard Xtreme Defense gel and solid (ordered 2026-06-23) and Dove Men+Care dry spray, all aluminum-based. Arm & Hammer's own material confirms aluminum salts leave marks and that the reaction is between sweat, deodorant ingredients and fabric: https://www.armandhammer.com/en/articles/how-to-prevent-and-remove-deodorant-stains

- Fiber choice is the lever, not sweat volume. Bamboo rayon holds protein and aluminium residue and does not fully release it in the wash; cotton and modal release it. This is why the two bamboo-rayon shirts in his drawer browned and a cotton shirt in the same rotation would not. Consequence: recommending a "sweat blocking" garment for a person who reports dry shirts is solving a problem they do not have - I nearly sold him one at 4-8x the price.

- Wash routine, which matters as much as the fiber: wash warm with oxygen bleach (OxiClean), NEVER fabric softener (it glues the residue into the fiber, which is what makes the stain permanent), and never chlorine bleach on protein stains - it sets them. Reported and confirmed by an experienced owner-reviewer of white cotton undershirts who washes hot, keeps them white, and reports no deodorant marks: contained in the Amazon Essentials listing vendor reviews, read 2026-09-27.

- Modal, bamboo viscose and spandex knits must not be tumble dried. Heat kills the spandex recovery and the modal softness, which is the entire reason to buy them over cotton. Air dry. This is the likely reason his existing BAMBOO COOL pairs have degraded.

- Amazon under-sizes owners on apparel. On both 2026-09-27 listings the page showed "Small is recommended based on your profile" for a man with a 38" waist and a 42" jacket. The recommendation tracks the cart and the profile, not the body. Sizing by measured waist is correct; the profile nudge is not evidence.
