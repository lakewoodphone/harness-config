**L1096 · Feeding tool results back is not the same as making actions visible: prove a step-1 fact reaches step-2 context**

A tool-using loop must feed results back into what the next step is built from

Visibility is not feedback. I made a session's action NAMES appear in its own
output_log and mistook that for progress -- the log became honest about which tools
ran while the model still never saw what they returned. Making a symptom legible is
not the same as fixing it, and a legible symptom can look like a fixed one.

The general test for any agent loop: take one fact the model learned in step N and
prove it is present in the context of step N+1. If it is not, the loop cannot
accumulate knowledge, every instruction to "stop investigating and act" will be
ignored because acting blind is worse, and no increase in the step budget can help.
That also means a loop like this fails at a CONSTANT step count regardless of
budget, which is the fingerprint to look for.

Corollary for diagnosis: when an agent repeats reconnaissance, do not conclude the
model is lazy or the prompt is unclear until you have proven it received the answer
to its last question.
