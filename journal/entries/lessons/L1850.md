**L1850 · A 100%-full swap area can be 97% cold tmpfs and 1.7% process memory (secratary, measured with mincore)**

On secratary a 100%-full swap area was 97% cold tmpfs files and 1.7% process memory. It was not a
leak, not a company service, and not pressure — and the distinction is measurable in a minute.

MEASURED 2026-09-17 03:42Z, Ubuntu 26.04, 23 GB RAM, /swap.img 4 GiB:
 SwapTotal 4,194,300 kB, SwapFree 116 kB -> 100.0% used
 sum of VmSwap over every process -> 70,840 kB (69.2 MiB) across 34 processes;
 the largest single holder was 14,448 kB
 mincore(2) over 19,855 files under /tmp -> 5,940.5 MiB of files, 2,044.7 MiB resident,
 3,895.8 MiB NOT resident (= in swap)
 96.8% of used swap accounted for by tmpfs; si/so were 0 in 4 of 5 vmstat samples.

HOW TO ASK THE QUESTION. `free` cannot answer "whose memory is it". Two commands can:
 * per-process: `awk '/^VmSwap:/{print $2, FILENAME}' /proc/*/status` (or smaps_rollup `Swap:`)
 * per-file: mincore(2) over the swapped filesystem — for a tmpfs file, the non-resident pages
 ARE the swapped-out ones, so it is a measurement and not a subtraction.
`~/_scratch/o5-swap-probe.py` does both and prints the comparison.

THE FIX, AND WHY IT IS THE SAFE ONE. Reading the files back in faults a swapped-out tmpfs page into
RAM and frees its swap slot. It is incremental, interruptible, cannot OOM-kill a service, and it
deletes nothing:
 read 5,984 MiB from 36,615 files under /tmp in 57.8 s
 swap 4,194,152 kB -> 73,992 kB (4,023.6 MiB reclaimed; 100.0% -> 1.8%)
 Shmem 1,994 MiB -> 6,018 MiB (+4,024 MiB)
The Shmem delta equals the swap freed to within 0.4 MiB, which is the PROOF that the swap was tmpfs
and not something else. Rejected alternatives: deleting /tmp scratch (destroys other sessions' work,
needs a per-action yes) and swapoff/mkswap/swapon (must allocate all 4 GiB at once and removes the
outlet while it does; no upside).

THE SECOND HALF, WHICH IS WHAT MAKES IT STICK. `vm.swappiness` was 60 with 12.6 GB of clean page
cache sitting unused: the kernel evicted cold tmpfs pages instead of dropping reclaimable cache.
Set to 10, persisted in /etc/sysctl.d/99-mesh-authority-swap.conf. Without this the next pressure
event refills the area with the same cold files.

CONSEQUENCE INSIDE THE MESH: the broker reads `mem.swapUsedPct >= 90` and halves a node's slots.
secratary scored 1 slot instead of 3 — correctly, on a number that was a tuning artefact rather than
pressure. A `swapUsedPct` level is not a swap RATE; 76-broker.md §10.2 already warned about this and
this is the measurement that makes it concrete.

A FULL SWAP AREA IS NOT EVIDENCE OF PRESSURE. Check residency before you add RAM, add swap, or let a
threshold act on it.
