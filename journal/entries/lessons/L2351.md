**L2351 · Never name the upstream supplier on anything a customer sees - and check the billing system naming, not just your template**

**Never name the upstream supplier on anything a customer can see.** The shop resells service and
parts it buys wholesale; naming the source hands the customer a way around the shop, which is the
whole margin.

Measured 2026-09-22, Heichal Hatorah. The receipt I had built named **US Mobile** on two service
lines and in the "going forward" block. The owner caught it with six words: *"there's no need to
write usmobile."* Removing it from my receipt was the small half.

**The half I would have missed: the supplier name was in the billing system itself.**
`tools/receipts/receipt.py` was mine and easy to fix — but the Stripe **product** is named
`Us Mobile Service Monthly`, and **the product name is what prints on the customer's own invoice
line.** Measured on a real invoice: `MX71HGH5-0001` read
`6 × Us Mobile Service Monthly (at $11.00 / month)`. That product is attached to **all 8
subscriptions on the account**, so every monthly invoice from 2026-10-22 onward would have handed
every service customer the name of the carrier. My receipt would have been clean while Stripe
leaked it on the same day. Fixed by renaming the two leaking products; verified afterwards that
0 active products name the supplier and that the preview invoice now reads
`6 × Monthly Service (at $11.00 / month)`.

**The rule, and where to look.** A customer-facing document is not just the document you are
writing — it is every surface that reaches them: the receipt, the invoice PDF, the invoice email,
the card statement descriptor, the customer portal, the product or price name, and the line-item
description. **Check the billing system's own naming, not only your template.** The statement
descriptor was already safe (`LAKEWOOD PHONE & TECH`); the product name was not.

**How to search it correctly.** Match on the supplier names you actually buy from, with a precise
pattern, and expect the naive version to lie: a crude substring list fired on **"Battery"**
(contains `att`), **"Kyocera top"** and **"E4610 Sale"**. A device brand the customer already holds
in their hand is not a supplier, and "renaming" those would have been wrong. The fix used
`/us\s*mobile/i` and touched exactly 2 products of 48.

**Corollary for wholesale-adjacent names generally:** the same reasoning applies to lab partners
(300DDR, MDRepairs), to screens and parts sourced from a specific vendor, and to any margin-bearing
relationship. Before a document goes out, ask: does anything on this page tell the customer where
we bought it or who does the work for us?

**L2351 · L — The Family Chat speaks in exactly ONE sendable voice (Chip), through hub._send_to_member; and the owner cannot answer the ask_user_question widget from his phone**

**Evidence: 2026-09-23, SECRATARY, a requested Chip broadcast.**

1. THE SEND DOOR. The family chat forwards whatever a *member* texts to the hub, so an agent can never "text as ETZ" honestly. The only voice the system can add is Chip (the family-computer persona) - `personality._send_chip_reply(text)` for the automated path, or a loop over `db.list_members()` filtered to `active and not muted` calling `hub._send_to_member(member, sender_name="Chip", body=..., media_urls=[], status_callback=...)`. That second form is what I used because it returns a per-recipient result; it writes the ledger row itself, so no separate INSERT is needed (and per L1570 a hand-rolled send that skips this door is the bug).

2. BOOKKEEPING THAT MUST RIDE ALONG. After a send: `personality._record_reply(estimate_group_cost(text))` (increments chip_replies_<date> and chip_monthly_spend - measured 0.2739 for 3 SMS x 11 members, $0.0249 each) and `db.chip_add_memory(...)` for facts Chip should know later. Budget is $5/month, so a broadcast is ~5% of it.

3. GROUP SIZE IS NOT THE ROSTER SIZE. 13 members, 11 targets: Bracha (id 9) is active=0 and Yosef Dov (id 8) is muted=1. Always read the target list before quoting a cost.

4. THE OWNER CANNOT USE THE ASK-USER-QUESTION WIDGET FROM HIS PHONE. He answered a rendered question with "I can't see the question when you use the answer a question tour on the phone". Ask in plain chat text, one question, with the options spelled out in prose; the widget is for a desktop GUI session only. Rule 2 still holds - one question, one recommendation.

5. WHAT HE MEANT BY "WEIRD AND CUTE AND SHARP". First draft (headline + bullet-list tone, "MAZEL TOV x2") was rejected as not sharp/weird/creepy enough; the accepted register was the *surveillance log* voice - the baby is a file, the machine knows which closet shelf Zaidy's rok is on, illness is reported as "his phone went quiet and I noticed", and the closer is "Sleep well - I don't." Deliver the persona, not a greeting card.
