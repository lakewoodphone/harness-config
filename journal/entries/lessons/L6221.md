**L6221 · The session corpus's failure taxonomy is 7x inflated; filter on err:true, and the real top failure is the web toolchain**

The corpus's own failure taxonomy is 7x inflated, and the fleet's biggest real failure is the web toolchain — not the shell.

Measured 2026-10-14 by mesh child subagent-4 on zabz-tech-linux, over the 6,103 non-owner sessions in `/home/zabz/evolution-corpus-v2/` (script `machine_audit.py`, output kept beside the corpus; report `evolution-corpus-v3/03-machine-sessions.md`).

**The taxonomy is wrong, and it inflates every failure rate quoted from it.** `failures.jsonl` is pattern-derived, and the patterns match text, not outcomes. Measured: **47,239 of 54,730 rows are labelled `err:false` (86.3 %)**, and of the rows that are labelled as failures, **2,716 are labelled `exit_nonzero` while the hit text they matched says "exit code: 0"**, and **2,282 are labelled `rate_limit` while quoting a web page that was successfully fetched**. The honest count is `err:true` = **7,491 rows**. So any document that reports a failure share from `failures.jsonl` as written — including round 2's `tool-failure 67.3 %` upper bound — is measuring the corpus's labelling, not the fleet. **When you need failure counts, filter on `err:true`, never on the label.** Round 2 had already flagged this tag as loose (`01-corpus-v2.md` §4, `L3783`); this is the mechanism.

**What actually fails, by family, of the 7,491 real errors:** `web-family = 4,204` (**56.1 %**) — cross-origin-redirect-not-followed 1,101, fetch-failed 781, firecrawl 429 682, PDF-unsupported 461, Playwright-already-in-use 144. Then `edit-family = 816` (10.9 %): edit-without-read 328, old_string-mismatch 237, stale-read 192. The shell is not the top failure source; fetching is.

**The fleet is a fifth idle.** Of the 6,103, **1,382 sessions (22.6 %) made zero tool calls at all**, and **1,342 of those were still billed a title-generation LLM call**. A fifth of the machine side of this corpus does nothing and pays for the privilege.

**Children block on their own protocol, not on the task.** `edit-family = 816` real errors are all "the child did not read the file it is editing" or "the string it targeted had moved". And **165 of 690 mesh children produced `deliverables = 0` and no turn-end event**. A dispatch brief that does not name the exact file and the exact expected output shape is paying for a child that will discover neither.

**A definition correction that matters for every future census:** "dispatch" in this corpus is **preset-present vs preset-absent**, not human vs machine — 1,299 of 1,300 "dispatch" sessions are themselves subagents, and only 924 have a human-rooted parent. Round 2's `CENSUS-v2.md` line "machine sessions dropped: 132" is not a machine-session audit; the 6,103 were never read until now.

Also corrected: **the frozen office box was not written to.** Its 323 sessions all pre-date the 2026-10-11 freeze (newest 2026-10-09), which is worth having on the record because the freeze was violated once before by a session that read a masked unit as breakage.
