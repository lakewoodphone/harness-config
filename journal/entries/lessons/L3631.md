## 2026-10-12 17:25 UTC · ZABZ-YOGA · ssh multiplexing is live on the authority (10x on the Linux node) - and the "LAN is slow" theory was wrong: most of the latency is pwsh.exe starting

**Measured, from the authority, 5 sequential ssh round trips before and after:**

| target | before | after | gain |
|---|---|---|---|
| `linux-pc-ts` | 415 ms | **38-43 ms** | **~10x** |
| `desktop-ts` | 530 ms | 246-268 ms | 2.1x |
| `laptop-ts` | 1,840 ms | 1,025-1,268 ms | 1.4x |

Configured in `/root/.ssh/config` (backed up first): `ControlMaster auto`, `ControlPath /root/.ssh/cm/%C`,
`ControlPersist 10m`, `ServerAliveInterval 15`, `ServerAliveCountMax 4`, `ConnectTimeout 10` - applied to
**named fleet aliases only, not `Host *`**, and `ssh -G hetzner` proves an unrelated host stays
`controlmaster false`. 30 ssh calls now use **3 TCP connections**. Control dir is `700 root`. No sshd
touched, no service state changed.

**The finding that matters more than the speed-up: the premise was wrong.** Raw RTT is **13-27 ms**, all
paths direct and not relayed, and the tailnet path already rides the LAN. The Windows remainder is
**`pwsh.exe` starting** (measured 189-232 ms on `desktop-ts`, because `DefaultShell` is pwsh) - so most of
the 630/1746 ms the brief attributed to the network was never network. **"Make the LAN faster" was the
wrong diagnosis**: LAN 0.600 ms against tailnet 0.963 ms raw RTT is not where a second is going.
**Any future work on mesh latency should start at the remote shell's startup cost, not the transport.**

**Windows cannot multiplex at all, deliberately left alone.** Win32-OpenSSH 10.0p2 fails with
`getsockname failed: Not a socket` and creates no socket - reproducing `L3391` on a second machine. This
is a real asymmetry: the Windows nodes, which are the strongest in the fleet, cannot benefit from the
fix. Recorded as an open limitation rather than worked around.

**Failure behaviour was tested rather than assumed, which is the right way round.** A SIGKILLed master ->
stale socket auto-unlinked, recovered in 399 ms. A blackholed transport -> a new session hung 74,678 ms
before failing, bounded by `ServerAliveInterval 15 x ServerAliveCountMax 4`; the master self-reaped at
74 s and a fresh ssh then worked. sshd down -> refused in 108 ms. **And the trap: `ssh -O check` reported
"Master running" in 10 ms while the transport was dead - it is not a health check**, so anything that
gates on it would be measuring the wrong thing. That is the estate's recurring defect class, caught
before it was wired into anything.

(blob `b5ba0d7d`, sha256 `dbfb8406`), commits `808b8cc7` (zabz-tech) and `31c650a` (the authority).
