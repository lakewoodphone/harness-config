**L3127 · A provider burst limit expressed as a billing error consumed a permanent attempt per subject**

MEASURED 2026-09-30 on ZABZ-TECH, and it is the cause of every failure this system has been logging.

Five releases started inside twelve seconds (15:30:05Z to 15:30:12Z). Every one of them died in about
twenty-one seconds, and the runner's own output carries the provider's words:

 dsh: QUOTA: Insufficient Balance (request_id: 50aa8493-e9db-4755-9963-c0b7039c4909)
 === exit code: 1 === elapsed: 21s

IT WAS NOT A BALANCE PROBLEM, and checking that first is the whole lesson. Both credentials - the
authority's `~/.dsh/.credentials.yaml` and the desktop's - answered `is_available: true` with a balance of
49.10 inside the same hour, and the batch twenty minutes later at 15:45 ran clean. The account was funded
the entire time. Five simultaneous starts is what tripped it, and the provider expressed a burst limit in
the vocabulary of billing.

TWO DEFECTS FOLLOW, AND THE SECOND IS WORSE THAN THE FIRST.

1. The system cannot distinguish "the provider refused" from "the work failed". The release is recorded
 `failed`, exactly like a genuine bug, and the only trace is 21 seconds of reasoning in a 2-3 KB output
 file on a desktop. This is the same class as the 16 all-time rows whose recorded outcome is the ssh
 post-quantum banner.

2. A transient refusal permanently consumes one of the subject's two attempts. Subjects 133, 153 and 154
 are now `failed` at 1 of 2. One more burst and those subjects are capped out for ever, and the work
 behind them disappears without a single line saying it was lost to a flake. `wake.py`'s attempts cap
 exists to stop a runaway; here it silently destroys real work.

RULE: classify an outcome by what it says, not only by its exit code. A retryable signature (QUOTA,
Insufficient Balance, RATE_LIMIT, 429, TIMEOUT, TRANSPORT, connection reset, broken pipe, or an exit code
whose output contains none of the agent's own answer) should return the row to `new` with a short
`not_before` and leave the attempts counter alone. Filed as a priority-1 ledger item with that as its
definition of done.

ALSO WORTH KEEPING: the first thing to check on any burst of identical failures is the raw runner output,
not the store. The store said `failed` twelve times and named no cause; one read of the child's own output
file named it in a single line.
