**L11710 · Owner-text blackout, 2026-10-20: three silent failures that all reported green**

Three independent silent failures, all reporting green.

(1) THE QUEUE GATE. settings_overrides.owner_sms_min_urgency='urgent' since 2026-05-04 refuses every 'normal'/'high' owner message forever. 13 rows held, oldest 2026-10-18T01:40Z, each with 9-18 send attempts, refusal text "held: urgency <X> is below the send threshold urgent". The rows include a card payment due that same day (min 273.83, statement 4692.89), Stripe webhook delivery failures, amazon_sync/ebay_sync paused after 10 failed runs, and five owner-health reminders. All are past their own lifecycle horizon and none was expired. text-health.py reports 20/20 OK throughout, because every check measures plumbing (catalogue, ceilings single-sourced, flood brake, sent-has-row) and none measures arrival or the legitimacy of a refusal.

(2) THE CFO MOUTH IS THROTTLED. cfo-shift-cron.sh caps shifts at CFO_SHIFT_CRON_MAX_PER_DAY=14 on a */30 cron, so the day's 14 shifts are consumed by 06:30 UTC and the engine is silent for the remaining 17.5 hours. shifts.jsonl stops at 2026-10-20T06:30:03Z. The wrapper's own "daily ceiling reached" message goes to /var/log/cfo-shift.log, which is 0 bytes, so an operator sees silence rather than a gate.

(3) THE SWEEP CANNOT ASK. owner_attention_sweep writes owner_attention_findings (43,920 rows, 4,199 critical) and ranks 60 per run, but question_reason is "every question-worthy finding is already asked or already answered" on every run analysed, while the top-ranked finding is an UNFILED obligation: "ABLETEL SOLUTIONS LLC - New Jersey entity standing (revoked 2026-06-16)". Dedup discards everything; the run reports status "partial" indefinitely.

LESSON: a gate that refuses is invisible. A gate that refuses forever, with the refusal written only on the row, is a blackout that every monitor calls healthy. Measure arrival and the legitimacy of refusal, never only the wiring.
