**L3323 · Frum design schools: the certificate does not hire, the portfolio does - and the entry rung is the shrinking one**

Two frum design schools dominate this market (Design Alive, psdesignschool.com = Professional Standard Design School). Both teach real software and are not scams; the problem is the economics, and a future session should not re-research it.

PRICES (verified from the schools' own checkout APIs/pages, 2026-10-05): Design Alive Web Design = USD 4,250 + 150 non-refundable registration; Design Alive UX/UI = same; PS flagship Graphic Design = USD 2,450; PS UX/UI = USD 3,600 BUT it requires the graphic design course first, so realistically 4,500-5,000. Both schools are effectively non-refundable (DA: withdrawal only inside 2 weeks, discretionary; PS: "we do not offer refunds under any circumstances").

THE FIELD, 2026: BLS projects graphic design DOWN 2% to 2035 and names AI as the cause; interface designers +6%, web developers +4%. Entry-level tech hiring is down ~65% vs 2019 (SignalFire), design hiring -48%; web developer postings -60% (Indeed). Figma's own survey: 25% of hiring managers increased junior hiring vs 56% for senior. Commodity price of a beginner's product: median 5-10 page WordPress site USD 900, landing page 350, themes 13-89, AI builders 10-30/month.

THE DECISIVE FRUM-SPECIFIC FACT: every frum design posting that states a requirement demands a PORTFOLIO and none demands a certificate (YiddishJobs even tags roles "Graduate & Non-Graduate"). Frum pay evidence: Candibots Lakewood graphic designer 50-80k, junior 50-75k (Sep 2026); a frum designer with 10 years says employers cap at 40-50/hr. Non-design remote alternatives on the same frum boards pay 18-43/hr with no tuition (billing/bookkeeping), which makes the risk-adjusted comparison close.

CONSEQUENCE FOR ADVICE: never recommend the full web/UX track first. Cheapest structured entry that also unlocks the UX/UI ladder is PS's USD 2,450 graphic design course; the right pre-step is a free real project for a real client to test whether she finishes and enjoys it.
