**L535 · Delivery fees sit INSIDE the 1.45 multiplier, so they inflate the customer total**

LPT laptop resale: customer total = (source price + delivery) x approx 1.45-1.47, tax included. A $20 delivery fee is therefore $29 in the customer's total, not $20. Worked example 2026-09-15: the $249.00 Latitude 5400 FHD touchscreen that I had quoted at $365 'free shipping' is $249 + $20 = $269 cost -> $390 all-in (owner's own rounding precedent is 339.99 x 1.45 = 492.99 -> 500, i.e. ~1.47x). ALWAYS add the delivery fee to the cost basis before multiplying. I had been quoting the grid's $0 shipping field, which was not the checkout truth.
