**L1834 · DHL duty demands reach this owner as SMS forwarded from Google Voice - search the AWB, not the word 'duty'**

**Rule:** for this owner, an international parcel's customs demand shows up as an SMS to a Google Voice number, forwarded into email — never as an email from the carrier. When asked about duty, import fees or a held parcel, search Gmail for the waybill number and for `"Import Duty Payment Alert"` **before** concluding nothing was noticed.

**Mechanism.** The phone number on supplier invoices is 732-569-1594, the owner's Google Voice line. DHL texts a shortcode (72524 in the 2026-09-14 case); Google Voice forwards the text as mail from `voice-noreply@google.com` to `eliyahuzabrowsky@gmail.com`. Searching the waybill `1129965443` found the notice instantly; a search for the words "import fee" did not.

**Evidence.** 2026-09-14: DHL Express demanded duty on AWB 1129965443 via shortcode 72524, delivered as Gmail `1a09f93f727413d8` / `1a09f94a86842499`. The comparable 2026-06-13 demand on AWB 9787434274 came by email from `noreply@dhl.com` instead (`19ec1bc3c536ed8d`) — so both channels must be checked, and the waybill is the reliable key.

**Pattern to expect:** DHL pays the duty itself, then bills the consignee with a **5-day clock** and returns the parcel to the shipper if unpaid. It has already done this twice to this business (2026-06-13, 2026-09-14).
