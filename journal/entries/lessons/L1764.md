Correction to L1741. Owner's own live Amazon pages (Lakewood 08701, 2026-09-16) show the
S2725QC at $349.99, not the $302.99 my cached index returned. Two lessons:

1. Never quote a marketplace price from a search index or cached scrape to the owner.
 His own browser is the source of truth; ask him to paste the page when in doubt.
2. Revised pick for "two screens, all-day office, eye strain":

 Dell S2725QS (ASIN B0F1GF1KFC) - $268.99 each sold by Amazon.com, $255.99 third-party.
 Ports: 2x HDMI 2.1 + DisplayPort 1.4, HDMI cable INCLUDED. Same panel as the QC:
 27" 4K, 163 PPI, 120Hz, 1500:1, 350 nits, TUV Rheinland 4-star eye comfort,
 ComfortView Plus hardware low-blue-light, matte.
 The S2725QC (B0F1GFD44G, $349.99) differs only by USB-C 65W instead of DisplayPort-in,
 and ships without an HDMI cable. On a desktop PC the USB-C premium buys nothing;
 it only wins for a laptop the user docks. Verified on dell.com 210-brnd and 210-brnc.

Caveat to state whenever recommending a Dell S-series: warranty is 1 YEAR limited hardware
and advance exchange, vs 3 years on UltraSharp. A minority of reviews on both variants report
early failure and painful Dell support, so buy with a seller that has easy returns and test
both units inside the return window.
