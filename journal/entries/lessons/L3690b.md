THE CASE. chain from 180791 on 2026-07-01 [snapshot] plus 202121 of transactions gives 382912, but the

The balance moved UP by more than the transactions say, so the ledger is missing 3,053.08 of OUTGOING

gap was missing payments IN. Two accounts, two directions, same arithmetic.

WHAT I RULED OUT, and this is the part worth keeping. `personal-checking` is fed by TWO overlapping
sources - `Chase Credit Card CSV` and `plaid-api` - and the window is full of pairs: Cash Redemption
1,731.46 on 07-22; ATM CASH DEPOSIT 944.00 on 07-12/13; 919.00 on 07-17; REMOTE ONLINE DEPOSIT 367.00 on
07-18/20; Zelle 10.00 on 07-07. Every one is correctly marked:

 2026-07-13 944.00 DUP Chase Credit Card CSV ATM CASH DEPOSIT 07/12 ...
 2026-07-13 944.00 live active plaid-api ATM CASH DEPOSIT 07/12 ...
 and the same shape for 367.00, 919.00, 1731.46 and 10.00
So exactly one of each group is counted. **The flow of 2,021.21 is the correctly-deduplicated net**, and the
double-counting hypothesis - which would have inflated the flow rather than shrinking it - is dead before
it is worth testing further. A cross-source feed is not automatically a duplicate problem; this one is
doing the right thing and the code says so in the status column.

WHERE 3,053.08 CAN BE FOUND, and why I cannot do it from here. A missing OUTFLOW has no trace in the
ledger - by definition the row does not exist - so it cannot be located by reading what is present. The
statement detail is the only instrument, which is the same bound that stopped the other six sign-flips:
the alert emails carry a balance and no items. What I can say without it is the direction, the amount, and
that August 2026 is where it happened.

AND A NOTE ON THE PROVENANCE. The note names the opening reading `[chase-scraper]` while the month table
shows `snapshot`, and the two disagree about what kind of evidence founded this chain. A scraper reading
and a snapshot are not the same confidence, and a disagreement whose note and whose source column disagree
about that is worth a look the next time someone touches `mark_suspects`.
