**L3860 · A gauge that reports design capacity reads as a perfect battery; cycle_count is not health; no Kyocera dialer menu self-tests hardware**

Correction to H3655, same session, after the Kyocera source research landed (docs/condition-audit/kyocera-research.md).

**A fuel gauge that reports design capacity is indistinguishable from a perfect battery.** E4810's `qpnp-vm-bms` (Kyocera's published kernel, `qcom,batt-aging-comp`) publishes the pack's design FCC as `charge_full` - 1780000 uAh on the 1780 mAh cell - and never advertises `charge_full_design`. Any health calculation over those two numbers reports about 100% on a worn pack. My first version would have printed "Capacity health 100.0%" as a PASS on a phone with a dead battery, which is worse than printing nothing. The same failure existed one level up: `charge_full == charge_full_design` is not health, it is an echo.

The general rule this is an instance of: **ask what writes the field you are reading before you score it**. A counter named `cycle_count` looked like state of health; it is a charge-added total in NAND that survives a battery swap and resets to 0 on invalid NAND. Both were scored, both were wrong, both were corrected the same hour - the guards and the reasoning now live in `cli/lpt/condition/audit.py` next to the checks, and 5 tests pin the traps.

**And: no Kyocera dialer menu is a component self-test.** `*#*#4636#*#*` on Android 7.1.1 and 9 is AOSP's Testing menu (phone info, usage stats, Wi-Fi info) with no battery page and no hardware test; `##786#`/`##33284#`/`##3424#`/`##3282#` are CDMA reset/field-test/diagnostic/programming menus; `*#*#3424#*#*`, `*#0*#` and `*#*#83781#*#*` are HTC, Samsung and Spreadtrum codes. The internet's "secret code" lists mix vendor codes freely, and none of the Kyocera entries tests a transducer. A service menu found on a real unit is extra evidence, never a replacement for a measurement.

Source URLs for every claim above are in the research document; the load-bearing two are Kyocera's E4810 kernel zip and the AOSP `testing_settings.xml` for 7.1.1_r1 and 9.0.0_r1.
