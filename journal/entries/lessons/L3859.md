**L3859 · Real balances ARE readable (accounting.db balance_snapshots): 1,061 of cash against a 2,380 autopay**

**L · The estate can read real account balances, and there is far less money than the transaction feed implies — a balance column that does not exist is not a balance that cannot be read.**

Chasing a 2,380.06 overdraft, the honest first answer was "there is no live bank balance anywhere in this
estate." **That was wrong, and it was wrong because I looked in the wrong table.** `plaid_accounts` has no
balance column, so I said balances were unreadable. They are read every night:
`capture_plaid_balances()` at `personal-secretary-mvp/app/services/plaid_ledger_ingest.py:668` writes
**`balance_snapshots`** in **`/root/accounting-data/live/accounting.db`**, `source='plaid-api'`, refreshed
after each nightly Plaid sync (latest capture **2026-10-14 04:30:13 UTC**).

**Generalise: when a table lacks the column you want, look for the writer before concluding the data does
not exist.** `grep -rn "def .*balance" --include=*.py app/` found the capture function in one call. I had
already told the owner the number was unreadable and it never was.
