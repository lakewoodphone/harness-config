**L3861 · A customer's words come from the cache, never from the comms index**

A customer's own words cannot be taken from the comms index. On 2026-10-19 the
waiting tool returned, for 848-224-5096, "No I don't want it thank you for letting me know about
it just cancel it". The owner looked at Dialpad and said "I don't see that" - he was right. The
sentence lives in sms_log with direction=inbound for that number, and sms_log also carries our
own bench-test traffic ("LPT bench test - automated picture check" x11) and personal texts. The
number is a test phone, not a customer. Rule now enforced in _scratch/respond/build-response-queue.py:
a party's claimed message must appear in that party's own dialpad_sms_cache rows or the party is
dropped as DISPUTED. 51 waiting -> 22 live -> 8 genuinely owed.

Owner rules for customer comms, all stated 2026-10-19 and now in CUSTOMER-SMS-RUNBOOK.md rule 7:
- we answer texts by text; missed calls are returned by the SECRETARY during walk-in hours, never
 by a text, and never "call us back" when we owe them the call;
- a text older than 48 hours is not a debt - "it's too many days already if he still want that he
 would reach out again";
- the shop's own auto-reply is not an answer. 848-245-7112 asked a price question at 16:42:58 and
 the auto-reply landed a second later; a newest-row test called that thread answered.

Shop prices learned today: 60 = the drive into an enclosure (becomes a USB drive), 75 = copy the
data onto a USB stick. They are NOT interchangeable and I had them swapped once.
The desk is now automated: /root/bin/sms-desk-watch.py on the authority, cron */10 via
cron-wrap.sh, files one wake flag per unanswered party (48h, newest real row inbound, auto-reply
excluded); lpt-hub/_scratch/respond/desk.py renders the card and writes the draft. The gap left
is delivery: a woken shift's draft lands in wake-dispatch.log, not in front of the owner.
