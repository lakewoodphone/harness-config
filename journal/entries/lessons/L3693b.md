**L3693b · PayPal IS in the matcher's scope (607 rows); and 130 of the 484 matches are to a feed, not a card**

2026-10-13. Fourth hypothesis of the night that checking killed. The matcher is fine.

WHAT I EXPECTED. Last round showed a PayPal-paid order (newegg 362.51, "PayPal express checkout") with no
marketplace row of its own, and the sync reports 45 `no_candidate` orders. So I expected the matcher was
not scanning `personal-paypal` and that PayPal-settled purchases were systematically unmatched.

`no_candidate` orders are the RECENT ones - 2026-10-12, 10-11, 10-07, 10-06, 10-04, 09-25 - whose charges
have not posted or matched yet, which is what a lagging tail looks like rather than a gap.
