**L6150 · owner_attention shows only 12 of 153 queued questions, and a row with no blocks value is never one of them**

## A question with no `blocks` value never reaches the owner — the digest caps at 12 of 153

Measured 2026-10-13 on `secratary-cloud`, while trying to honour the owner's *"okay remind me in a
few days"*.

**How a deferred question actually comes back.** `owner-ask.py` is the tool built to text him one
question at a time, and **it is not scheduled anywhere** — no cron line, no wake source. The channel
that IS live is `lpt-owner-attention.timer` → `scripts/owner_attention.py send --urgency urgent`,
daily at 14:10 UTC. It reads `owner_decision_queue` itself and already honours `deferred_until`, so
`owner-queue.py defer <id> --until <date>` is the whole mechanism. I verified both directions rather
than assuming: a past deferral puts the row back in the list, a future one withholds it.

**The trap.** `from_owner_queue(limit=12)` sorts by
`(0 if blocking else 1, SEV[severity], -id)` and then takes **twelve**. There were **153 live
questions**. A `critical` row with **no `blocks` value** ranks behind *every* blocking row and is
then cut by the cap — it is filed, pending, correct, and never reaches him. I hit this on the
payroll row: `critical`, and absent from the digest's 12. Giving it a `blocks` value moved it to
position 1.

Two consequences worth carrying forward:

- **`blocks` is not decoration; it is the ranking that decides whether he ever sees the row.** A row
 created without it is filed into a queue whose visible window is 12 rows deep and 153 rows long.
 `owner-queue.py add` accepts `--blocks`; **`amend` does not** (context/recommendation/note only),
 so a row filed without one cannot be repaired in place — it has to be re-filed, and the duplicate
 guard refuses even against a *resolved* row unless `OWNER_QUEUE_ALLOW_DUPLICATE=1` is set. Set
 `--blocks` at creation time.
- **`blocking()` is an equality test, not a prefix test.** `blocks` is treated as blocking when it is
 not in `("", "nothing", "none", "n/a", "-")`. The ST-50 row's value reads *"nothing yet, but the
 deadline is 7 days out…"* and therefore counts as **blocking** — the word "nothing" in its own
 text does not exempt it. Accidentally right here; it will mis-rank the next row that phrases
 itself that way.

**And the honest limit of the channel:** 153 live questions against a 12-row window means most
questions are never surfaced by this digest at all. The reminder works; the queue behind it does
not.
