**L12391 · A worktree's size is mostly hardlinks - measure node_modules first**

## L— On Windows, a worktree's apparent size is mostly hardlinks; measure `node_modules` before promising GiB

Measured on ZABZ-TECH 2026-10-27 while auditing a 951.65 GiB C: with 11 GiB free.

141 abandoned agent working copies (`_w-*`, `_lpt-*`, `_wt*`, `_audit-*`, `_fleet`, `_worktrees`)
measure **309.5 GiB logical / 14.6 M files** by `robocopy /L`. That is not what deleting them returns.

Proof, not inference: `node_modules/react/package.json` is **one inode (844424932029948) with 132
links** in three different worktrees. pnpm hardlinks every package from its store into every
checkout. Measured end to end on `_w-auth`: **1.58 GiB total, node_modules 1.20 GiB, rest 0.39 GiB**
(`_w-tax` identical). So 141 trees x ~0.39 GiB = **~55 GiB real**, not 309.

The arithmetic that closes a whole volume: sum of all top-level logical sizes **1,024.2 GiB** minus
the hardlink overhang **~131 GiB** = 893.2 GiB, against **893.55 GiB used** (`Get-PSDrive C`). A
volume is never "missing" 130 GiB without a reason — on this estate the reason is hardlinks.

Rule: before quoting a reclaim figure, (a) measure one representative directory's `node_modules`
share, (b) state logical vs physical, (c) never let a fleet's checkout count become a space claim.
`measure-space.ps1` and robocopy report logical bytes and cannot see this. `du` sees it but on
Windows it follows junctions here and did not finish in 55 minutes — do not rely on it.
