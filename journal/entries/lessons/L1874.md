**L1874 · A DRN renumber does not re-render the wallpaper, so the phone keeps showing its old number**

**A WAZE MDM DRN renumber moves the row but never re-renders or re-pushes the wallpaper, so the phone keeps displaying its old DRN indefinitely.**

Evidence, 2026-09-17 (read-only reconciliation of the owner's hand-kept inventory sheet against the live registry; report at `C:\Users\ezabz\code\_scratch\sheet-vs-registry-20260917.md`):

- `_wallpaper_label(drn)` in the **deployed** `fleet_api.py` returns `f"DEVICE {drn}"` for `site='lakewood'`, read live from `fleet_devices.drn`. Rendered by `/deploy`, `/lockdown` and `/render-wallpapers`.
- `POST /fleet/devices/{drn}/renumber-v2` says it in its own docstring: *"Does NOT push profiles — use /lockdown after renumbering."* It updates `fleet_devices`, `fleet_deployments`, `fleet_commands`, `fleet_queue_snapshots` — and touches no wallpaper file and enqueues no command.
- Measured consequence: of 57 sheet rows, the **19 that disagree with the registry sit on devices whose current-DRN wallpaper PNG was never rendered on or after the 2026-07-19 renumber window. 19 of 19, zero exceptions.** The 36 that agree include every device whose current-DRN wallpaper *was* re-rendered.
- Corollary evidence: `drn-device-34-{home,lock}.png` does not exist at all even though registry DRN 34 is `deployed` and checking in through 2026-09-17; and stale renders for DRN 72 and DRNs 100–104 survive on disk with no device behind them.

**Rule:** after any renumber, the on-device `DEVICE N` label is stale until a `/lockdown` (or `/render-wallpapers` + `/push-wallpapers`) runs. Never treat a device's wallpaper label as its current DRN, and never treat the registry DRN as what the user sees without checking that a render exists.

**Related trap:** a renumber does re-render **nothing**, but it *does* silently merge or vacate DRNs. Registry DRNs 12 and 22 are now vacant while their render files linger, which is how a gapless hand-typed sheet (1–55) can look authoritative when it is not.
