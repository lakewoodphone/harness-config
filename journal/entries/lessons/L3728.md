**L3728 · The QL-820NWB said Media Status 'Not Empty' while the roll was empty - and READY before / PRINTING after is the proof a label printed**

**Measured 2026-10-13, Brother QL-820NWB `H3G354844` (firmware 1.25), ticket 2657 label.**

The printer sat in **Device Status ERROR** for over an hour with the job sent to it, and its own page said **`Media Status: Not Empty`, `Media Type: 29mm / 1.1"`** the whole time. The owner then reported the actual cause: *"ran out of paper, i put in new labels"*. So the roll was **empty while the device reported "Not Empty"** - that line is a roll-type/media-fit statement, not a tape-present sensor, and it must never be used as evidence that tape is loaded. (This corrects the assumption in P3157, filed the same day, which recorded the media line as proof the roll was present and left the ERROR unexplained.)

**The proof that a label printed**, and the strongest available from a remote session: re-sent with **state READY before** the send, the job reported `27.9mm of tape`, and the state read **PRINTING immediately after** it. `READY -> PRINTING -> (READY)` is what `lpt-label`'s own failure hint asks for, and it is the only confirmation the device offers - the status page's 60-second auto-refresh is far too slow to catch a 28 mm job, which is why a poll loop sees READY throughout. Record all three readings around a send (before, the send line, after), not just the send line.

**Practical consequences.**
- A job sent while the printer is in ERROR may be queued rather than dropped - a duplicate of the same text can appear later, e.g. on the next power cycle or the next send. Expect it; do not explain it as a second print.
- When `find` says ERROR and the roll *looks* present in the status output, ask the person at the shop whether the tape is actually out before concluding the device is faulty. "Not Empty" is not an answer to "is there tape".
- `lpt-label print` sends regardless of state (only `find` returns exit 2), so a non-READY state is a fact the session has to carry into its report rather than a refusal it can rely on.

**Evidence:** pre-send `state READY`, media `Not Empty 29mm`; `sent to 192.168.50.60 (Brother QL-820NWB, state READY, roll 29mm) - 27.9mm of tape, image /tmp/lpt-label-20261013T155433.png`; post-send `state PRINTING`; and the ERROR-state send an hour earlier at `/tmp/lpt-label-20261013T154832.png` with ERROR before and after.
