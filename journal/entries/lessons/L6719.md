**L6719 · A metric that under-reports is worse than a metric that is missing: the yardstick window was also the loop's reach**

Measured 2026-10-16 on the CFO scoreboard. Its default window was 24 months, and the shift draws its batch from that same windowed cluster list. So 13 unexplained rows between 2021-03 and 2024-09 were not merely unreported in the headline - they were UNREACHABLE by the loop that exists to clear them. A backlog the loop cannot see is a backlog that never closes. The arithmetic before deciding: --months 24 = 156 ms and unexplained 58; --months 72 = 159 ms and unexplained 71; --months 240 = 165 ms and unexplained 71. Three milliseconds against a 156 ms read, so window width was almost free and the only question was honesty. Default set to 72, covering the ledger's own first row of 2021-03-11. The headline moved the honest way: unexplained 58 to 71 and pct_clear 99.09 to 98.94, i.e. worse because it stopped excluding; dollars unexplained 25,394.15 to 35,888.76. Proven the loop can now reach them: the next bounded shift saw 21 unparked clusters instead of 8-15, chose 8 and resolved 5 with zero errors, including desc:system one at 4,999.80 - a statement line that exists only in the older data. The general rule: when a measurement has a window, ask what the window excludes AND who else reads the same window. Here the same parameter was both the report's scope and the worker's scope, so a reporting choice silently became a scope of work.
