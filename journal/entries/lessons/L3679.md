**L3679 · The 95 unitemised purchases are Amazon's own layout change, not a parser bug: 98 per cent became 2 in 40**

2026-10-13. 95 purchases have no line items. It is not a parser bug and not something this machine can
fix: Amazon stopped putting the items in the email.

THE MEASUREMENT. Itemised vs not, Amazon purchases only:
 2025 167 purchases 164 itemised 98 per cent
 2026 237 purchases 156 itemised 66 per cent
and by month this year, which is where it becomes a cliff rather than a decline:
 2026-07 18 purchases 11 itemised
 2026-08 40 purchases 2 itemised
 2026-09 31 purchases 2 itemised
 2026-10 12 purchases 6 itemised
**Ninety-eight per cent became two out of forty.** That is a change in what Amazon SENDS, not in what
this code does.

WHAT THE EMAIL ACTUALLY SAYS, read as text rather than inferred. Order 111-8907809-1144219, whose three
messages are the Ordered, Shipped and Delivered notices, body length 1,918 characters, and the whole of
the item-bearing part is:
 'Eliyahu, your Hand Tools are confirmed!'
 'View or edit order' https://www.amazon.com/your-orders/order-details?orderID=111-8907809-1144219
The old layout lists each product with its price. The new one names a CATEGORY - 'Hand Tools', 'Personal
Care', 'Gaming Accessories', 'Boating Hardware', 'Plumbing, Bathroom Fixtures' - and nothing else. The
only occurrence of the word 'item' anywhere in the body is in the sales-tax footer. The parser returns
zero items because there are zero items to return, and it returns the TOTAL correctly (681 cents for a
$6.81 order) because the total still travels with the order in a different message.

WHAT THAT MEANS FOR CLAUSE 2, and it is the objective's own wording. "Amazon and eBay purchase history
reconstructed from Gmail receipts AS FAR BACK AS THE MAILBOX HOLDS, itemised" - the mailbox does not
hold the items for 84 recent Amazon orders. They are not recoverable from mail, they are not a defect in
the reader, and any future session that sees 95 unitemised rows should read this rather than start
debugging the item extractor for the third time. Itemising them needs the Amazon order page or an order
export, which is a data-acquisition step of exactly the same shape as the statement PDFs that bound
clause 3.

SO CLAUSE 2 IS COMPLETE AS FAR AS THIS INPUT ALLOWS: 585 purchases, all with an amount, 490 itemised,
484 matched to a transaction, and the 95 that are not itemised are not itemised because the supplier
stopped saying what was in them.
