**L · Ask for the criterion, not the strategy — a queue row the owner never needed to see**

2026-09-20. I put owner-queue #20 to him — *"do you want me to stay domestic-only, or open the Hong Kong and
European trade channels in parallel?"* — with a long compliance analysis attached. He answered in one line:

> *"i just [want] the cheapest reliable ones with eSIMs, this question doesn't seem so important"*

**He was right, and the tell was in my own row.** The question I asked was about **strategy** — which lane to
build a supply channel in. The thing I actually needed was a **criterion** — what makes a phone acceptable.
A criterion is not a decision for him: it is a specification, and once he gives it the whole branch of
strategy collapses into arithmetic. *Cheapest reliable eSIM, US-spec* is a filter I can run. *Domestic vs
offshore* was me handing him a decision I had already done the work to make.

THE RULE. Before a row goes on the queue, ask: **can the owner answer this with a criterion rather than a
choice?** If a short criterion would make the whole strategic question moot, then the row is asking for
permission to think, and it is mine. He said as much months ago — *"these are dev questions, their not boss
qs"* (L117) — and this is the same error wearing a compliance hat: I dressed an engineering decision in
regulatory detail until it looked like his.

WHAT A GOOD ROW LOOKS LIKE, in contrast: **#111** (may the wake system spend $X/day) is a number only he can
set, and one word answers it. **#20** was a paragraph, and one word dissolved it.

AND THE COST OF GETTING THIS WRONG IS NOT THE MINUTE HE SPENT — it is that a queue of questions he did not
need teaches him to stop reading the queue, which is the exact failure this system was built to avoid (the
595-row `owner_message_queue` that went 54 days unread).

WHAT CHANGED: #20 answered and resolved with the criterion recorded; the sourcing work is now flag #21,
carrying the US-spec/FCC constraint as a hard filter and the shop's own repair history as the definition of
"reliable", so it proceeds without him and comes back with a shortlist and a landed cost.
