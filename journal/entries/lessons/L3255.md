**L3255 · Mesh subagent dispatch from zabz-tech failed deterministically on 2026-10-04 - fall back to subagent_local and mark the results LOCAL, then re-verify every load-bearing number yourself**

On 2026-10-04 all three mesh subagent dispatches from zabz-tech failed identically, with the provider's
own diagnostic: "the remote one-shot exited 1 and produced no final message". engine_health read zero
subagent loops on this machine before and after, and the mailbox directory
C:\Users\ezabz\.dsh-mesh-mailboxes was absent or empty, so nothing ran anywhere. The failure is not
partial and it is not a slow start - it is deterministic across three dispatches.

What I did, which is the pattern to repeat: quoted the diagnostic, stated the mesh was unreachable in
one line, re-ran the three briefs through subagent_local, and marked every result LOCAL in the
deliverable. One of the three LOCAL reports returned a figure that did not survive verification - a
salt quantity attributed to the Gaziantep specification that the Turkish original does not support -
which is the argument for re-reading every load-bearing number from a subagent against the primary
source rather than trusting the report's own confidence. The same report also claimed America's Test
Kitchen was inaccessible while I read it directly in the same hour.

Rule: a subagent report is a lead, not evidence. Re-read the primary source for anything the
conclusion rests on, and record in the deliverable what did not survive.
