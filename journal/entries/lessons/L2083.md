**L2083 · A due reminder reaches the owner as a HELD owner_message_queue row - read the deployed tree, not the laptop clone**

## What I believed, and why it was wrong

My local clone `C:\Users\ezabz\code\personal-secretary-mvp\app\services\personal_ops.py:732` reads
`if channel not in ("sms", "both"): continue` — so a `dashboard`-channel reminder is marked
delivered and reaches nobody. I almost reported that as a live failure.

The deployed tree on the authority says otherwise. `secratary:~/personal-secretary-mvp`, read
2026-09-20 ~14:52 UTC, HEAD `012f8a35`, routes every non-SMS reminder into
`enqueue_owner_message(..., status="held")` with `reason=reminder:<id>`:

> `held`, deliberately: a reminder must never become an outbound SMS on its own, whatever happens
> to the kill switch later.

Commit `cf800076` — *"fix(reminders): the in-app channel now reaches the owner, instead of marking
itself sent"* (P148) — is that fix.

The reader exists: `web/app/inbox/page.tsx` renders held messages and `web/lib/inboxAttention.ts`
counts them into the attention badge.

Live proof: `owner_message_queue` ids 1795-1803, created 2026-09-20T09:17Z-14:01Z, all `held`,
reasons `reminder:193`, `reminder:423-429`, `reminder:441-442`.

## The rule

**Before judging whether a reminder — or any delivery path — reaches the owner, read the DEPLOYED
tree on the authority.** A repo file on this laptop is not evidence about deployed behaviour; the
two revisions differ, and they differed today by exactly the block I was reasoning about.

## Corollary

**`held` is not failure.** It is the designed in-app surface: the owner chose the owner-message
queue as where he reads this, and the kill switch already falls back to it. Do not "fix" it by
flipping a reminder to `sms`/`both`.
