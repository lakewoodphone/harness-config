**L1828 · macOS 26 keeps a SECOND wallpaper for the idle state, and it can be a 4K video**

Measured 2026-09-16: the Mac Mini spent up to 5.2 core-hours/day (extension 7.7-9.8% +
VTDecoder 2.3% + most of WindowServer's 9.3-11.3%) decoding an aerial VIDEO wallpaper that no
human had chosen -- the store's birth time equals the account's creation, Provider 'default', and
the OS default URL on this build is a .mov.
The load-bearing detail: AppleScript's 'set picture of every desktop' sets only the DESKTOP slot
and the decode CONTINUED (7.18%). macOS 26 keeps a separate IDLE wallpaper in
~/Library/Application Support/com.apple.wallpaper/Store/Index.plist, and Idle is precisely the
state a worker node lives in. Rewriting the Idle entries plus 'killall WallpaperAgent' took it to
0.00%. Verify any wallpaper fix from the Idle state, not from a desk you are sitting at.
