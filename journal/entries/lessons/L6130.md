**L6130 · The first real sign-flip points at the statement readings: balances that oscillate and months with no payment**

2026-10-13. Worked the first of the seven real sign-flips and the evidence points at the STATEMENT
READINGS, not at missing transactions. That is a different recovery from the one the work list assumed.

THE CASE. `personal-chase-9650` 2025-05, delta 1,310.31, both ends statements. The window 2025-04-18 to
2025-05-17 holds 27 rows, all live, none marked duplicate:

The closing balances OSCILLATE by thousands of dollars month to month (548.64 -> 4,079.69 -> 714.13 ->
396.72 -> 1,232.45 -> 1,422.46 -> 4,996.74), the payment-to-prior-closing ratio ranges from 0.00 to 2.16

a month with no payment, and a balance does not plausibly swing 4,079 -> 714 -> 397 -> 1,232.

WHAT I THINK THAT MEANS, stated as a belief. `cfo-balances` reads Chase statement mail and its docstring
records that Chase's message carries `Statement balance $N` and a SECOND `Statement balance $N` with its
own date. If the reader sometimes takes the wrong one of those - or picks a different figure such as a
minimum payment or a total balance - the anchors would oscillate exactly like this while every transaction
row is perfectly fine. Under that reading the 24 disagreements on this account are READING errors, the
recovery is to re-read 25 statements, and hunting missing transactions there would be work in the wrong
place.

THE DECISIVE NEXT STEP, bounded and cheap: pull the Chase statement emails for these months through the
application's own reader and compare, line by line, the numbers the parser extracted against the numbers
in the message. That is one account and a handful of messages, and it settles whether the anchors or the
transactions are wrong - which is the fork every one of the seven sign-flips sits on.

AND IT REFRAMES THE WORK LIST A THIRD TIME, honestly: the seven sign-flips have both readings
authoritative, but authoritative in SOURCE is not the same as correct in VALUE. I have been treating
`statement` as a guarantee and it is only a provenance label.
