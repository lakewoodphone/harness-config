**L3874 · Reset wipes all software usage evidence; only battery, /persist calibration, IMEI and bootloader survive - usage band now says 'unknown since reset'**

Owner asked: "most of the phones were reset, does this just show you since the last reset? is there anything that survives a reset?"

Measured answer, 2026-10-19, five handsets.

The software traces are since the last reset, and the date range proves it per unit:
- 558800238329: oldest call 2026-10-06, so never reset since; 57 calls, 61m30s talk, 31 photos = real use -> heavy -> grade C.
- 615373543: oldest call 2026-05-20, not reset since May, but only 3 calls / 28 s since -> light -> B.
- 558800328253, 615963123, 616202539: oldest call is TODAY. Their use before that is unrecoverable.

WHAT SURVIVES A FACTORY RESET: IMEI and serial (modem NV), Wi-Fi and Bluetooth MACs, RF and sensor calibration on /persist, the bootloader lock state, and the battery charge counter where the gauge keeps it outside /data. NOTHING ELSE - call log, texts, contacts, photos, installed apps, usage stats and the dropbox crash history all live in /data and go with the wipe.

CONSEQUENCE, now in the code: the usage band reports "unknown since reset" when the oldest retained record is under 14 days old and nothing else shows use. It must not say "light", because a phone wiped last week can have been someone's daily driver for three years. The grade carries the reason and the date the data starts.

FOR THE SHOP: on a reset handset the only surviving evidence of use is the battery (a timed drain is the honest measure) and the physical condition. That is the one place the external check still carries information even though, by the owner's rule, it does not set the grade.

Also worth knowing while chasing this: ro.runtime.firstboot is unusable on these firmwares - empty on both E4810s, ms-epoch on one E4610, and garbage (62483) on another - so the call-log date range is the reliable reset marker, not the property.

Code: cli/lpt/condition/grade.py, docs/condition-audit/grading.md, commit 6411e0b8. 35 tests pass.
