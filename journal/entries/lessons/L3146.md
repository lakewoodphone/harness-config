**L3146 · A mesh child cannot push: git push is ssh, and ssh hangs from inside a child, so commit-and-push-from-the-node is not an executable instruction**

2026-09-30 round 34. I briefed three children with the instruction to commit and push FROM THE NODE, on the strength of the L3139 lesson that an unpushed artefact on a laptop is indistinguishable from work not done. That instruction is not executable by a mesh child on this profile, and the evidence is direct. At 17:2xZ, scripts/cap EXISTED on the laptop and scripts/make_zabz_preset.py was MODIFIED with make-preset-rows.mjs newly created and staged, so two children had done real work and neither had pushed it; nothing from them appeared on any remote branch. The laptop also carried hung ssh processes, two of them started two days earlier and two from 13:10 that day, which is the signature of this defect rather than a coincidence. The mechanism is already proven elsewhere in this session: an ssh call made from inside a mesh child prints the remote output and then NEVER EXITS, so a child that runs git push blocks on the transport with its work finished and unsent. WHAT I DID: pushed their work myself, because my ssh works, with explicit paths rather than -A so another session in-flight journal files were not swept into my commit. THE PATTERN TO USE FROM NOW ON, replacing the L3139 instruction: the child commits LOCALLY and reports the commit hash; the MANAGER pushes. A push instruction in a child brief is a trap that costs the whole run. L3139 was right about the goal and wrong about who can do it.
