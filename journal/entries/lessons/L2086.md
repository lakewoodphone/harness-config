**L2086 · A message that cannot be delivered is not attention debt: an alarm that is permanently true gets ignored**

**L · 2026-09-20 · ZABZ-YOGA — A message that cannot be delivered is not attention debt: an alarm that is permanently true gets ignored**

**Measured evidence.** The owner's SMS channel is off by his own decision (`data/OWNER_SMS_KILL_SWITCH`,
present since 2026-07-20) and `owner_sms_min_urgency` is `urgent`. So every `normal`-urgency row in
`owner_message_queue` is written `held` and stays held for its whole shelf life. On 2026-09-20 the
`attention_debt` check read "owner-attention debt: waiting: …" and reported **HIGH** on **9 held rows, all
urgency `normal`, zero critical, zero urgent** — and it would have reported HIGH every minute of every day
for as long as the switch stays on.

**Why it matters.** The same check's own history (see its docstring) records five weeks of CRITICAL driven
by 7 critical + 46 urgent rows that nobody could act on. `H341`'s conclusion — *an alarm that can never go
green is an alarm that gets ignored* — was applied to freshness (severity follows how recently an alert
was observed) but not to *deliverability*.

**The rule.** Severity must follow what the owner can act on now:
- fresh critical held → CRITICAL; fresh urgent held → HIGH; a question past the window → HIGH;
- `normal`-urgency held items → **counted and named in the summary, never a severity**. They are a
 standing queue behind a channel the owner switched off, not attention debt.

**Second half of the same defect.** The two headline slots the badge renders were occupied by
self-generated nag reminders — *"Retry closing duplicate task #26057"*, *"#26118"* — for tasks that were
**already `done`** at the time the reminder fired (25908, 25905, 25894, 25889, 26118, 26057 all closed by
2026-09-19; only #25776 remains `blocked`). A reminder scheduled against a task has to be re-checked when
it fires: a reminder that outlives its task is noise wearing the owner's own voice.

**How to apply.** Before promoting any queue depth into a badge severity, ask two questions: *can this be
acted on?* and *can it ever become false?* If either answer is no, it belongs in the summary and metrics.
