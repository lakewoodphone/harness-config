**L — Ask the archive before you ask the owner: two facts "only the owner had" were printed on a document this seat had archived itself.**

Measured 2026-09-30. Queue rows #183 and #196 had been open since 2026-09-28 and 2026-09-29 asking the
owner for two facts about NJ Form BC28: the last day Yisroel Weinberg actually worked, and the employer
legal name on the NJ DOL account. Both are printed on the face of the form.

The form was photographed by the owner on 2026-09-25 and archived by a previous session at
`/home/zabz/lpt-books/inbox/desk-2026-09-25/originals/9-nj-dol-separation-request-bc28.jpg`. The same
session's own transcription (`transcripts/legal-money-verbatim.md`) already recorded:

- box 12, **Last Day of Work 09/11/2026**
- box 10, **LAKEWOOD PHONE & TECH LLC** (and box 8, FEIN 039211923300000)

So the seat wrote the answer into its own archive, then asked the owner for it, twice. Two rows, two
days, plus the mirror, plus the "one fact still needed from the owner" line carried in three files.

**Why it happened.** The rows were written from the *payroll log*, which is where the last day worked
was genuinely missing, rather than from the *form*, which is where the question was being asked. The
gap was real in one artefact and absent in another, and nobody reconciled the two.

**The rule.** For any government form, notice or invoice, the document itself is the first source of
truth for its own fields — read the paper before declaring a field unknown. Concretely: when a row's
question names a document, open that document's own image or transcription before writing "only the
owner has it". The desk archive at `/home/zabz/lpt-books/inbox/desk-2026-09-25/` (and its `INDEX.tsv`,
`RECORD.md` and `transcripts/`) is a searchable first stop, not just a filing cabinet.

**Corollary, and the more expensive half.** A question routed to the owner that he cannot answer
better than a document we already hold is exactly pain P1 — his attention spent on questions that are
not his. The test is not "can he answer it?" but "can he answer it *better than the record can*?" If
the record can, it is not his question, and asking it spends the one resource this system has least of.

**Evidence.** `lpt-hub/docs/operations/payroll/Weinberg-2026-09-28/NJ-DOL-BC28-ANSWER.md` (rewritten
2026-09-30 with the direct re-read); queue rows #196 and #183 resolved with that reason; #205 filed
for the one item that genuinely is the owner's.
