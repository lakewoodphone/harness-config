**L3105 · I blamed a watchdog that was working - I waited 40 seconds on a five-minute timer and called it a failure**

CORRECTION, 2026-09-29. In round 1 of the owner-SMS work I wrote that "the watchdog did not bring it back" after I killed the API process and it stayed down for the ~40 seconds I waited. That was WRONG and I stated it as fact.

secratary's crontab contains:
 */5 * * * * pgrep -f "uvicorn app.main" > /dev/null || ~/personal-secretary-mvp/scripts/secretary-startup.sh > ~/secretary-startup-cron.log 2>&1

So there IS an API watchdog; it is a FIVE-MINUTE cron, not a 30-second poll. I waited 40 seconds and concluded the watchdog had failed. It had not - it simply had not ticked yet, and it would very likely have restarted the service within five minutes. My manual start was faster, not necessary.

THE GENERAL LESSON: a watchdog whose schedule you have not read is not a watchdog you can pronounce on. Before asserting that an automated control failed, find the control, read its interval, and wait at least one full interval. A 40-second observation cannot falsify a five-minute timer. Same class of error as the rest of this session - a confident claim from an unverified source - and it was recorded as a fault of the system when it was a fault of my measurement.
