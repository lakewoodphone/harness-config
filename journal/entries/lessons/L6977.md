**L6977 · 404 category rows pointed at transactions that no longer exist - and one of them held the answer to the largest open question in the book**

Measured 2026-10-16 on the CFO ledger, from a pragma foreign_key_check that I had glimpsed one round earlier and walked past: 751 violations, of which 404 are transaction_categories rows attached to transactions that no longer exist, plus 143 audit_log rows, 119 transaction_line_items, 18 transaction_evidence, 27 duplicate_reviews, 10 transaction_links, 29 transactions pointing at missing source_documents and 1 balance snapshot for a missing account. The vanished ids are still in transactions_fts_content with their text, and audit_log holds no delete action at all (categorize 1968, fix 418, link 53, recategorize 41, duplicate_resolve 6), so the signature is a re-key or a migration removal rather than a deletion. Recoverability was measured rather than assumed: matching each orphan's FTS description against a live transaction that has no category of its own recovers exactly THREE of the 404. The third is the 5,877.19 credit I had listed as question 2 for the owner - description CB SEL FD CL A CB SEL FD PPD ID: 1461050694 - and THREE sibling credits with that exact description are all categorised Investment Redemptions (6000.00, 2853.86, 4000.00), with four further orphaned rows saying in words that it is balance-sheet cash returning from invested funds rather than revenue. Applied through the sanctioned writer on the row's computed cluster key, with the neutrality proven not asserted: band_for says movement, and a statement run against the write's own pre-state backup reads identically to live (escape101 5,754.22, lpt -46,479.34, personal -74,143.36, all unchanged). Unexplained 49 -> 48, dollars 27,973.74 -> 22,167.18, pct_clear 99.28. Two lessons: an FK sweep is a cheap way to find rows that exist but belong to nothing, and orphaned rows are not always junk - they can be the only surviving record of a classification whose transaction was re-keyed, which makes re-matching them by their indexed text worth the minutes. Also corrected in the audit: the figure I had twice quoted as personal's net income, 5,754.22, is escape101's - personal's residual is -74,143.36.
