**L4512 · DeepSeek full outage 2026-10-15: DSH has no provider failover, and the retry policy retries the dead provider**

DeepSeek suffered a full API outage from 2026-10-15 00:11Z (still "Identified" at 2h37min when read at 02:54Z), taking down V4 Pro and V4.1 Flash inference. The owner's DSH had NO provider failover: 28 `llm/retry` events fired in the window and EVERY ONE names `provider: "deepseek-official"` — the retry policy retries the provider that is down. Five sessions across zabz-tech and zabz-yoga have a `turn/start` with no `turn/end` (hung turns); the worst, zabz-tech 9fda7c73-2e56-4a16-8715-44510656ea1c, burned 6 retries on one unclosed turn. Twenty sessions on three machines (zabz-tech, zabz-yoga, zabz-tech-linux) were affected, so the owner's "all the DSH across the entire mesh" is accurate for the machines he actually uses.

ROOT CAUSE, and it is a config shape not a bug: `settings/base.yaml:58-60` pins `agent-default-model: {provider: deepseek-official, model: deepseek-flash}`, and the owner's own three machines — ZABZ-TECH, ZABZ-YOGA, SECRATARY — have ZERO override rows, so all three resolve to that one literal. Only DESKTOP-FGV6KMH and LAKEWOOECHSMINI override it. The same literal is stated a SECOND time at `profiles/web/cordis.patch.yml:1739`. A second provider, `llm-pi-ai` (DeepInfra), is MOUNTED at cordis.patch.yml:1742 and is never used automatically — it is a catalog entry, not a fallback. `grep -i "fallback|failover"` over `@deepseek-ai/dsh-llm` and `dsh-llm-pi-ai` in the installed engine returns NO matches.

FOUR MEASUREMENT TRAPS that produced or nearly produced a wrong number, and each is reusable:
1. `/models` is the CATALOG, not the service. My first probe hit `api.deepseek.com/models`, got HTTP 200, and I reported "DeepSeek is up" WHILE INFERENCE WAS DOWN. The failing endpoint was `/chat/completions`. Probe the surface that fails, not the surface that is cheap.
2. These timestamps use a `T` separator. `created_at >= '2026-10-14 20:00'` matches EVERY `2026-10-14T...` string because `'T' (0x54) > ' ' (0x20)`. My first window queries returned ~24 hours while looking like they returned the window. Use the `T`.
3. `dsh_sessions.updated_at` is the AUTHORITY'S INGEST TIME, not session activity. "45 sessions written during the outage" measured the hourly ingest job. Per-event truth lives in `dsh_session_events`.
4. A window that is still open cannot be compared to a closed one. The 02:00Z event bucket reads 408 against 5,996 an hour earlier and looks like a collapse; it is mostly ingest lag.

`error_log` held TWO rows for this outage (both db_contention). It records unhandled exceptions only; the nine real model failures are in `model_usage`, including a **23-minute-3-second** timeout and four parallel `deepseek-v4-pro` calls each pinned at the 900s ceiling returning "no_choices".

THE COMPANY SURVIVED WITHOUT US. `app/services/ai_gateway.py` has a real escalation chain and `_should_try_fallback`; DeepSeek v4-flash volume fell to 14 calls in the 01:00Z hour against a same-day busy range of 55-143, while `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` — absent for the seventeen hours from 07:00Z to 23:00Z on 2026-10-14 — returned for exactly the three outage hours. So the estate already knows how to fail over on the company side; the gap is DSH's own model path.
