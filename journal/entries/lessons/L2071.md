**L2071 · A scheduler job gated on a disabled flag produces no log line, so a dead automation looks exactly like a quiet one**

**L· 2026-09-19 · A scheduler job gated on a disabled flag produces no log line, so a dead automation looks exactly like a quiet one.**

**What I did.** The owner asked whether the Shabbat/Yom Tov power automation worked for the past Shabbos. The first thing I looked at was the API log. There was nothing in it about Shabbat — which reads as "no news", i.e. nothing went wrong. It was not no news: `SHABBAT_AUTOMATION_ENABLED=false` makes both scheduler jobs (`shabbat_program`, `shabbat_execute`) `return` on their first line, so the absence of log lines was the *symptom of total silence*, and I nearly read it as health.

**Why it matters.** This is the general shape, not a Shabbat fact. Any job behind a feature flag is invisible when the flag is off: no error, no warning, no heartbeat, no row. Silence is indistinguishable from success by log-reading alone, and this system has burned itself on that before (P3, "reading the wrong data and believing it").

**The rule.** Never judge a scheduled automation by its log. Judge it by **an artefact the job is supposed to produce, on the device or in a datastore, carrying a timestamp**:
1. the *device's* own state (here: `Schedule.List` on the plug — empty is the finding, and it is only visible because I asked the device) and
2. the *effect's* continuity in a recorder (here: HA's history has exactly one gap >30 min in ten days — Rosh Hashana's 50.86 h — and none at all across the Shabbos in question, which is proof HA never went down, independent of any flag).

**Corollary.** `-i grep` the log *and* ask the device. A grep that returns nothing is not an answer unless you also know what the job would have written had it run.

**Evidence.** 2026-09-19/20: `journalctl -u secretary-api --since 2026-09-11 | grep -i shabbat` returned two `/shabbat/status` lines and nothing else for the whole period; `SHABBAT_AUTOMATION_ENABLED=false` in `/proc/3232884/environ`; `Schedule.List` -> `{"jobs":[]}`; power-sensor history 1 gap in 10 days. A turn earlier the same night I would have reported "no Shabbat activity in the log, looks like nothing fired" — which was true but for the wrong reason and with the wrong severity.
