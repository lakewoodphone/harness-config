**L3689 · The call recording is the only record of a free rework - read the calls before believing a case history is complete**

Measured 2026-10-12 on Mizrachi/Rahimi, customer 925, OnePlus Nord N30, order WO2608200003.

Production held two work orders and three notes for this customer and none of them held the visit
that matters. On 2026-09-07 the shop rang her to say the battery connector had been found
**disconnected**, reconnected it at no charge, and said on the recording *"it was my fault, I
apologize"* (Dialpad call 5425081734963200). No work order, no note, no charge — so nothing was
written down, and the next reader of the case saw a screen job and then, six weeks later, an
unexplained battery fault that looked like a new and unrelated problem.

What to do with it:

- **When a customer's history has a gap** — a fault that appears after a job with nothing recorded
 in between — read the calls before believing the record is complete:
 `ps_comms_thread <phone> --since <before the job>`. A free rework is the most likely invisible
 event precisely because nobody has to bill it, and billing is what drives the paperwork.
- **A zero-dollar rework still needs a note.** The note is the only thing that makes the warranty
 position findable later — in this case it is what shows the fault is ours and not a new one.
- The same defect, same order: the $95 of 2026-08-20 exists only as prose inside a status-change
 note. The order reads `total 0.00`, payment `PENDING`, and `payment_transactions` has no row for
 order 2726, so production reports that completed repair as free.
