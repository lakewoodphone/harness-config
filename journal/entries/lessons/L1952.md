**L1952 · A recorded 'missing part' is often arithmetic another document already closed - check the modulus before buying the fix (Shlock Gap B)**

**The case (2026-09-17, ZABZ-TECH).** Two of our own records disagree about the Shlock tarp, and the disagreement would have cost money and risked a non-returnable custom cover.

- `personal-secretary-mvp/docs/personal/shlock/2026-09-16-delivered-and-open-items.md` (secratary `master`, commits `7ca903a9`/`6c46b4ba`) states in §3.3 and §4.3: **"the 8 rail-alignment grommets (Gap B) are still not in the tarp. ... the tarp's grid is 21" OC and will not line up"**, plan of record **punch 8 aftermarket #4 grommets by hand at measured rail positions** (~$20, plus a hole punched into a custom order ALCO will not take back).
- `lpt-hub/research/miscellaneous/2026-09-17-shlock-master-index.md` §5 and `.../2026-09-17-shlock-sep25-build-plan-and-buy-list.md` §1 (same day, newer, derived from the final ALCO drawing and the Aug-27 locked spec) say the opposite: **the grid was designed aligned to the rails; "Gap B is wrong as of the final spec"; no punch kit.**

**The arithmetic that settles it.** Rails at x = 24, 66, 108, 150, 192, 234, 276, 318 (42" O.C.). Long-edge grommets: 17 per 342" edge at 21" O.C., starting 3" in = 3, 24, 45, ..., 339. 3 ≡ 24 (mod 21), and 3 + 21k hits every rail position exactly. So **every rail position is already a grommet position**, on both long edges (8 per edge, 16 usable attachment points, plus 9 midpoints per edge).

**What is still open is a tape measure, not a grommet:** whether the AS-BUILT rails sit on that same x datum. If the installed spacing differs, the band grommets miss the rails and *then* 8 grommets get punched.

**The rule.** When one document reports a feature missing and another reports it present, **do the geometry before buying the fix**. A "gap" in a plan is often a modulus the writer did not check, and the cost of not checking lands on the irreversible side (here: a hole in a custom tarp and $20).
