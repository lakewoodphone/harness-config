## L — the 82 "incompleteness markers" in kosher-filter-ai do not exist, and the counting method is the bug

Measured 2026-10-19 on `C:\Users\ezabz\code\kosher-filter-ai`. The discipline handoff
(`docs/HANDOFF-REPO-DISCIPLINE-2026-10-18.md` §5) reported **82** markers: 56 in `android/app/src`,
26 in `server/app`, 1 in `ios/`. Its Phase 2.2 was a whole phase of work: "classify all 82, none may
remain an untriaged TODO".

There are **zero**. With `\b(TODO|FIXME|HACK|XXX)\b` and case-sensitive matching across 923 code
files in seven directories, every division returns 0. The 82 were produced by a case-insensitive
**substring** match, and in Kotlin and Python that pattern matches ordinary code:

- `TODO` inside `toDouble` — 39 of the 56 Android hits. `chargeCounterUah.toDouble() / 1000.0`.
- `XXX` inside the blocklist keyword `xxx` — 15 of 56. `Regex("\\b(porn|nude|naked|nsfw|xxx)\\b")`.
- `ToDo` inside `…BackToDownloading…` — 2 of 56. `fun updatePushFallsBackToDownloadingText()`.

An independent read-only triage reached the same conclusion, and I verified it myself rather than
trusting the report.

**Why this matters beyond one number.** The handoff used the marker count as its evidence that the
estate is "severely lacking organisation" and as the raw material for its largest phase. The count
was an artifact of `Select-String -Pattern 'TODO|...'` without word boundaries. A phase planned on
it would have spent twelve to eighteen rounds classifying 56 things that are `toDouble`.

**What is actually unfinished** in that estate is four behaviours, none of them marked: the community
level does not gate loosening (`server/app/routers/owner_level.py:67`), the decision engine has no AI
path and grants no quota (`server/app/decision.py:68`), the iOS `BrokerClient` is a protocol with no
implementation (`KosherFilterCore.swift:11`), and Android's permitted-input-methods restriction
passes `null` where the comment above it claims IME + Gboard only
(`antievasion/AntiEvasionPolicyManager.kt:210`). All four are in `docs/COMPLETENESS.md` with a
definition of done each.

**The rule.** A marker count is a measurement, and a measurement needs a method you would defend.
Before planning work from a grep count: (1) use word boundaries and match case, (2) print the matches
themselves and read five of them, (3) ask what else the pattern matches in this language. `TODO` is a
substring of `toDouble`; `XXX` is a substring of a blocklist keyword; and a count that nobody looked
at is how a phase of work gets aimed at nothing.

Corollary worth keeping: **this repository does not use comment markers to record unfinished work.**
It records it in documents and in the work ledger. So the absence of markers proves nothing about
completeness, and the count should never be quoted as a progress metric again — a lint rule should
own it, not a reading habit.
