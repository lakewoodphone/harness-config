**L1833 · Gmail body reads 404 without the account param - search first, then read with account=<mailbox>**

**Rule:** reading a Gmail message body on this system needs the right account, and the account is only discoverable from search.

`GET /gmail/search?q=...&all_accounts=true` returns `{id, account, date, from, subject}` per hit. `GET /gmail/messages/<id>` **without** `?account=<that account>` returns HTTP 404 "Requested entity was not found" for anything not in the primary mailbox — which looks exactly like a deleted message. With the account it returns the full body under `message.body`.

**Evidence.** On 2026-09-14, `1a09f94a86842499` (DHL duty SMS notification, Google Voice mailbox) and `1a07d97c87cde328` (PayPal receipt, `ezabz68@gmail.com`) both 404'd bare and read fine with `?account=eliyahuzabrowsky@gmail.com` and `?account=ezabz68@gmail.com` respectively. Four mailboxes are in play: `lakewoodphoneandtech@gmail.com`, `ezabz68@gmail.com`, `eliyahuzabrowsky@gmail.com`, and whatever else the search returns.

**Also:** `/gmail/search?q=<two terms joined by OR>+something` can exceed 60s and time out — keep queries single-concept, and quote phrases (`"%22import+duty%22"`).
