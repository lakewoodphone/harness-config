**L1946 · CORRECTION to L1945: fulfilment is per-SKU - Best Buy's 870 EVO 2TB ships Sep 25 while its 1TB ships next-day; the 2TB fallback is 1-hour pickup, not next-day shipping**

CORRECTION - retracts a specific claim I made in L1945 and H523 the same hour, 2026-09-17.

I recorded the fallback for the Samsung 870 EVO 2TB as "Best Buy, sold by Best Buy, $499.99, Get it tomorrow". That is WRONG. Best Buy's 870 EVO 2TB product page quotes SHIPPING as "Get it by Fri, Sep 25" to 08701 - eight days out, not next-day. Only Best Buy's 870 EVO 1TB page says "Shipping. Get it. Tomorrow." I carried the 1TB listing's fulfilment across to the 2TB SKU because they sit in the same comparison strip.

The correct fallback for the 2TB is Best Buy IN-STORE PICKUP - the same page shows "Pickup: Ready within 1 hour" - which is faster than any shipping option and removes the delivery-date risk entirely.

Generalised rule, which is the durable part: never infer one SKU's delivery terms from another SKU's, even from the same brand at the same retailer. Fulfilment is per-SKU: stock location, whether it ships from a store or a warehouse, and whether it is a marketplace seller all vary by model and capacity. Read the delivery line on the exact product page you are about to buy from.

Where this matters: on a live job, "next-day" versus "eight days" is the difference between a shipment going out this week and not. The fallback exists to be used under time pressure, which is exactly when an unverified belief about it does the most damage.
