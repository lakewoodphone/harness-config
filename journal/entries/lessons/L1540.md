**L1540 · Handy's pill X discards a recording where Windows' X keeps it -- and the log distinguishes stop from cancel**

Handy's recording pill has an X that DISCARDS the recording. The Windows voice-typing bar's X KEEPS the
text. Anyone carrying the Windows habit therefore loses whole sentences, silently, and the app looks broken.

What it cost on 2026-09-15 (LAKEWOOECHSMINI, day one): the owner pressed Command+H, spoke for ~15 seconds,
and stopped it by clicking the little box on the screen. Twice. Handy's log shows the result exactly:
`Initiating operation cancellation...` at 18:46:59 and 18:47:19, with NO handy-keys event anywhere near
either one. Nothing was transcribed, nothing reached history.db, and he reported "no text automatically
appears in the text box" -- which is the worst kind of bug report, because every stage that a config check
can see is working.

TWO THINGS TO KEEP FROM THIS:
1. Fix: `overlay_style: "none"` -- no pill, so there is nothing to click that can destroy work. Start/stop
 beep plus the menu-bar icon are the indicators. Reverting is one line, and should only be done for a user
 who will never click the pill.
2. DIAGNOSTIC RULE for Handy's log, worth more than the fix: a keyboard-driven action is ALWAYS preceded by
 `handy-keys event: binding=... state=Pressed`; a stop shows `TranscribeAction::stop`; a discard shows
 `Initiating operation cancellation`. So "nothing appeared" splits cleanly into (a) the key never arrived,
 (b) it was stopped and the paste failed, or (c) it was cancelled from the UI -- and the log says which,
 every time, with no guessing.

EVIDENCE: ~/Library/Logs/com.pais.handy/handy.log on LAKEWOOECHSMINI, 2026-09-15 18:46:46-18:47:19 (two
cancellations) versus 18:49:01-18:49:06 (start -> stop -> 48,000 samples -> "Using paste method: CtrlV" ->
"Text pasted successfully in 562ms").
