**L8702 · A parse check and a deploy in the same command are not a gate**

Measured 2026-10-18, in my own hands, on the search service.

An edit removed a newline between a function's signature and its body, fusing them:

 def disk_health(path: str) -> dict: try:

I ran python -c 'ast.parse(...)' and the scp **in one command**. The parse failed, the scp ran
anyway, the service was restarted on a file that could not import, and \/health\ returned HTTP 500.

**The rule:** a check and the action it is supposed to guard must not share a command. Write it as
\check && deploy\ (short-circuits) or run them as two steps where the second is only issued after
reading the first. Anything else is a check that produces evidence nobody reads.

**The second half of the rule:** the failure was caught in under a minute — by the health read I ran
immediately after — not by the parse. Reading the result of the deploy is what found it; the parse
output was simply there in the same block, saying no.
