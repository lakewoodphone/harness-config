**L3677 · Third disproof: the two 2,481.72 charges are real, and personal-paypal nets to zero**

2026-10-13. Third hypothesis on the same case, third disproof. The by-product is worth more than the
hypothesis was.

THE HYPOTHESIS. Two charges of exactly 2,481.72 at `PAYPAL *REEBELOUSAI` in one month (2025-02-20 and
2025-03-17), 93 per cent of the month's unexplained balance movement, and two identical unusual amounts
at one merchant five weeks apart is the shape of one purchase recorded twice. I went looking for the
independent evidence a duplicate would leave: a reversal, or a single PayPal transaction behind two card
charges.

DISPROVEN by the PayPal feed, which the ledger already holds. Every row at that amount:
 2025-02-18 personal-paypal debit 2481.72 'HP Spectre x360 14 ... Laptop'
 2025-02-18 personal-paypal credit 2481.72 'HP Spectre x360 14 ... Laptop'
 2025-02-20 personal-chase-9650 debit 2481.72 'PAYPAL *REEBELOUSAI'
 2025-03-17 personal-chase-9650 debit 2481.72 'PAYPAL *REEBELOUSAI'
 2025-03-17 personal-paypal credit 2481.72 'HP Spectre x360 14 ... Laptop'
 2025-03-17 personal-paypal debit 2481.72 'HP Spectre x360 14 ... Laptop'
**Two separate PayPal transactions exist - one dated 2025-02-18 and one dated 2025-03-17 - and each is
independently mirrored on the card two days later and on the same day.** Two card charges, each with its
own PayPal transaction behind it, is two real purchases. Not a duplicate, and the 5,347.72 gap is not
explained by this pair after all. (The 206.81 at the same merchant repeats the same way - 2025-02-19 on
the card and 2025-03-20 - so the owner bought the same two items twice, a month apart.)

THE BY-PRODUCT, and it is a finding in its own right. `personal-paypal` records EVERY transaction as a
DEBIT AND A CREDIT on the same date, same amount, same description, from `paypal-api`:
 2025-02-18 debit 206.81 / credit 206.81
 2025-02-18 debit 2481.72 / credit 2481.72
 2025-02-27 debit 33.00 / credit 33.00
 2025-02-27 debit 35.19 / credit 35.19
So the account's signed flow NETS TO ZERO for every PayPal purchase it records. A payment made through
PayPal therefore does not move the PayPal balance in the ledger at all, in either direction. Whether that
is intended (an account used to itemise rather than to hold money) or an import artefact, I cannot say
from here - but it means `personal-paypal` is not usable as a balance-reconcilable account, and any flow
computed over it measures nothing.

HONEST STATE OF THIS CASE AFTER THREE ROUNDS. `personal-chase-9650` 2025-03: 37 real, unduplicated
transactions, statement anchors verified to the cent, no missing payment on the bank side, both 2,481.72
charges independently backed by PayPal transactions. The 5,347.72 gap is real and the cause is STILL NOT
FOUND. Three hypotheses, three disproofs, and I am recording that rather than reaching for a fourth -
the next step is the statement PDF, which carries an itemised list, and nothing on this machine can
substitute for it.
