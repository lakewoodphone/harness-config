**L3818 · The Dialpad text-answering system has two mouths: a canned hours reply for customers, and a personal responder that never touches them**

WHAT ANSWERS A CUSTOMER TODAY, AND WHAT DOES NOT

1. A first-time texter gets ONE canned message and nothing else. Observed live on 732-237-6600 at 2026-10-14 20:09:00.889Z, ref 6744484690567168, one second after the inbound: "Thanks for texting Lakewood Phone & Tech. Our walk-in hours are Sunday 12-4, and Tuesday and Thursday 10:30-5:30. You can drop your device off any time at our secure drop-off and we'll get started on it. 1001 W. Kennedy Blvd Basement left side of house. We'll text you back when we're in." It answers no question.
 NOTE: that body is NOT the `a2p_auto_reply_config.message_template` from migrations 20260925120000 / 20261005120000 (those say "Please reply with your device issue or question"). So the hours a customer receives are produced by a surface other than the config row those migrations maintain; the two happen to agree on hours because both feed from `business-hours.ts`. Do not claim a migration changed what texters receive without reading an actual outbound row.
 RATE, measured `dialpad_sms_cache` by day 2026-10-08..15 (inbound sms / canned replies): 87/16, 28/22, 2/2, 72/20, 50/24, 54/16, 28/17, 8/6. Roughly a fifth of inbounds are auto-answered; the rest get silence.

2. Everything else is a human. `ps_comms_waiting days=7`, 2026-10-15: **36 waiting**, 40 missed calls, index age 0.2 h. On 2026-10-14 the same reading was 45. This is journal P2942 and defect D2 in CUSTOMER-TEXT-RESPONSE-FLOW.md, and its §7 says the decision is the owner's.

3. `sms-responder.py` is NOT the customer system, and its own help says so: "The line is PERSONAL - owner, family, close friends. Customers are Dialpad, and this system never touches them." (Owner's rule, 2026-09-20.) It runs from cron every 5 minutes as `run --send --limit 8 --max-sends 3`. Last real work 2026-10-15 01:35:22Z (it learned from one of his answers, then logged "sent this run: 0"); after that 162 consecutive "0 to decide" to 15:1xZ — which is a correct reading of an empty personal inbox, not a fault (H3000 made the same point).

4. THE LIVE RESPONDER IS NOT ANY REPO COPY. On secratary-cloud 2026-10-15: `/root/bin/sms-responder.py` 134,567 B, mtime Oct 14 01:42; `/root/personal-secretary-mvp/scripts/sms-responder.py` 71,720 B; `/root/code/harness-config/scripts/sms-responder.py` 56,311 B. md5 of the first two differ (829ee92325c0ed554b2d471c355e65ef vs e1ce8ec22af705c63074b97dcfc8aabc). The 2026-10-14 run was refused by the owner surface (`reason=rate_floor`) and the row sits as 'held' in `owner_message_queue`. So "36 items surfaced" and "what he was sent" are different quantities by design — 237 chars is a pointer, not the list.
