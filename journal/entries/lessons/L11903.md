**L11903 · Spotify prompt bar obeys what it can count and silently drops what it must exclude**

NOTE ON THE RECORD: the previous write (L11902) landed with a heading and no body - a PowerShell parse error aborted the pipeline that was meant to carry the text. L11902 is an empty artifact; this entry carries the content.

Owner tested the Spotify prompt bar ("Tell Spotify what to play" / Talk to Spotify) himself on 2026-10-20 night: it "just built it's regular stupid playlist and ignored my request". Researched the published record the same night.

1. IT IS SUPPOSED TO DO THIS. Spotify's own model prompts (newsroom.spotify.com/2026-02-23/prompted-playlist-prompts-to-try): "Create a list of Australian artists that I haven't heard before", "Find one artist I haven't listened to yet". The same page states the pool is "your full listening history ... combined with what's happening across the world of music and culture right now" - a taste-anchored GENERATOR, not a filter. Support page: beta, English only, "Usage limits are in place".
2. THE PUBLISHED FAILURE SHAPES, all recorded:
 - Explicit exclusions dropped: "don't add any Elliott Smith songs" -> playlist still served Smith, including tracks where he played guitar (What Hi-Fi hands-on, 2026-03-06).
 - Novelty overshoots into strangers: Luke Combs and Morgan Wallen recommended to a listener who had never streamed them or anything similar (TechRadar, 2026-01-22).
 - Prompt has no visible effect; scheduled refresh does not fire (r/truespotify "Prompted Playlist problems", 2026-03-21; "prompted playlists keep repeating the same songs", 2026-05-21).
 - Output capped at 60-90 minutes, far under a real session (TechRadar).
3. WHAT IT DOES OBEY: constraints it can COUNT from history. What Hi-Fi's data-driven prompts worked well - "the first 10 songs I ever played", "top tracks from every year I started using Spotify, in ascending track length order". Years, counts, ordering, era = obeyed. Prohibitions and behavioural exclusions = dropped.
 The rule this yields: the bar obeys what it can count and silently drops what it must exclude. Ask it for years and counts; do not ask it for "not played in the last 30 days", "no artist twice", "nothing from my liked songs".
4. CONSEQUENCE: the owner's requirement (never-heard, no artist twice, nothing recent) is exclusion-shaped, which is the category no service implements - Apple, Amazon, YouTube and Deezer all generate a playlist and hand it over. The one-evening filter remains the only surface that can enforce it: ListenBrainz keyless similarity seed -> exclude /me/player/recently-played -> write back via POST /me/playlists + POST /playlists/{id}/items (Feb 2026 Spotify API paths, per L11710's sibling session).

Correction owed and paid: session 52b49949 (2026-10-21 00:25Z) and my repeat at 01:20Z recommended the prompt bar as "the answer, already in your app" on the strength of Spotify's support-page examples alone. Those examples are the aspiration, not the shipped behaviour.
