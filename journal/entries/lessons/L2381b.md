**L2381b · For a business gap, search lpt-hub/docs/customer-operations FIRST - the code repos do not hold operational state**

**Rule:** before declaring any *business* gap unstarted (sourcing, packaging, policies, pricing, operations), search `lpt-hub/docs/customer-operations/` and `lpt-hub/artifacts/`. The code repos do not hold operational state, and the operations repo does.

**What happened (2026-09-22):** asked to "seriously source the iphones, the labels, the boxes, the wires and the cases", I read `personal-secretary-mvp/deploy/waze-mdm/`, the site repos, and the journal. I found no physical BOM anywhere, concluded the sourcing was unstarted, wrote a launch plan whose critical path was "custom boxes carry a 10-15 business-day lead time", and dispatched two agents to research packaging and device supply from scratch.

**What was actually true:** the sourcing had been swept exhaustively on 2026-09-14/15 and SETTLED in `lpt-hub/docs/customer-operations/sourcing/kosher-waze-device-supply-pipeline.md` (410 lines). It already held a measured bill of materials (Uline S-15028 box $1.01, Sticker Mule stickers $0.73, EVA insert $0.85, SUPCASE case $21.95), a $135.80 device lot, a $162 ceiling, a **$32.73 all-in non-device cost**, a $299 list, a 5-unit shopping list totalling ~$1,132, and an explicit decision that the custom rigid box and pad-printed case were DELIBERATELY DROPPED. A box spec and dieline already existed at `lpt-hub/artifacts/kosher-waze-box/`.

**The cost:** a wrong critical path in a plan the owner reads, a fabricated risk (box lead time) that did not exist, two agents briefed to re-research a settled question, and owner-queue #138 carried as a live decision for six days when D99 had already answered it.

**The generalisation:** absence of evidence in the places I looked is not evidence of absence. My default sweep is code-hunting; for a *business* question the evidence lives in the operations repo. When a question is about the business rather than the system, look there FIRST.

**Secondary catch, same session:** the record said the product is "sold in the shop, in person - the portal takes no money". The owner now wants it sold on the website with Stripe per-usage billing. That is a genuine change to a settled decision, and it reopens the packaging reasoning (a stock retail carton is not a shipping carton, the insert card becomes the only instruction, returns become shipped). Flag a changed premise; do not silently re-plan around it.
