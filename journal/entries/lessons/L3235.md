## ROUND 11, second half — the duplicate detector now sees both askers

question for every asker — *has this merchant already been put to the owner, by anyone?* —
by unioning `~/.cfo-asks/asks.db` (sent merchants) with `~/.owner-ask/asks.db`
(`source='purchase'`, `source_id='m:<merchant>'`). No new store, so it cannot drift.

Normalisation is `cfo-ask.py::merchant_key` byte-for-byte, because a dedupe key that

## ROUND 13, correction — the `sent_today` counter was RIGHT and my note was wrong

I recorded twice that `cfo-ask.py`'s gate line showed `sent_today=2/3` while the store held
1. **The store holds 2**, and both are real sends with a provider sid:

 #13 12:30:23 amazon order total sid=SMe73f56d228beb6be912d5965102e07c4
 #16 17:28:50 data reclos sid=SMaa32860dc549f1f0fb828f2099f4bd70

So the counter, the store and the send all agree, and there is nothing to fix. Two things
produced the false alarm, and both are worth keeping:

 * **I read the store at 17:12Z, mid-sequence, and then reasoned from that snapshot as if
 it were current.** The second row existed by the time I compared again at 17:37Z.
 * **I did the age arithmetic in my head and got 14 minutes when the timestamps say 8.6.**
 The tool was right both times it said 8; I was comparing against a timestamp I had
 misread.

The earlier note also called `~/.cfo-asks/cron.log` unreliable — that part stands, and it is
*why* the snapshot was confusing: the log interleaves runs, so the same gate line appears
repeatedly and out of order. **The lesson from this one is the standing rule, not a new
one: read the store, state the timestamp with the number, and do the subtraction with a
tool.** This is the third false alarm of the engagement produced by my own arithmetic
(`P2828`, `P2829`'s counter claim, and this), against zero produced by the send path.

Row **#9** (2026-10-01 15:17:42, `the home depot`) carries `sent=1` with **no sid** — the
same shape as the dry-run record corrected in `P2829`. It predates the fix and is left as
found: the fix stops new ones, and rewriting history here would hide that the class existed.
