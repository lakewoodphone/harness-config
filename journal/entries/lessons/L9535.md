**L9535 · Owner text channel: 52% dead texts, preask never deployed, topic recorded and never read (2026-10-19)**

The owner text channel, measured and partly repaired — 2026-10-19, ZABZ-YOGA / secratary-cloud.

## 1. `sms_log.agent_id` does NOT separate the two halves of the channel. `status` does.
Filtering `agent_id <> 'sms_service.audit'` to get "the conversation" deletes the CFO's
entire voice: the relay tags its **delivered** texts `sms_service.audit` too. The split is
`sms_service.audit` + `suppressed`/`failed` = a REFUSAL (2,033 + 930 rows, all time) versus
`sms_service.audit` + `delivered`/`sent` = the CFO and reminder engine on his phone (105).
I made this mistake at the start of the audit and concluded he had received 81 texts in 30
days when the answer was ~186. **Ask what WRITES the field you are filtering on.**

## 2. The channel recorded topic identity since 2026-10-14 and read it back nowhere.
`attempt.topic` and `ask.topic` are columns; producers pass `topic=`. Dedup was
`policy_hash(body)`. Four reminders for one $597.29 payment, four wordings, four hashes, four
texts in 61 hours — all four cleared every gate. Fixed by `bin/owner_topic.py`: a key derived
from what the message is ABOUT (`money:597.29`, `case:WO2602180002`, `task:26089`), scoped by
FAMILY so a correction and the alarm it corrects never collide (measured: Telnyx -0.01 alarm
vs "103.50 available" correction). Wired into `owner_text_relay.submit()` and into
`app/owner_text.gate()` as M1c, exempting `interrupt`.

## 3. `preask.py` — the owner's own rule — was never deployed.
His words 2026-10-16: *"before you give me a charge, you have to exhaust all of the resources
for exploring it. And then, if you still don't know, give me all the details you found."*
708 lines implement that as five rungs (owner_answer, ledger, comms/email, docs, web).
Measured: **absent from `/root/bin`; the literal string "preask" appeared 0 times** in
cfo-ask.py, owner-ask.py, card-due-watch.py, owner_text_relay.py, charge_msg.py, sms-responder.py.
Its docstring claims "the relay refuses an item that opens an ask without a receipt" — the
relay contains 0 matches for `receipt`. **A gate that is written and not wired is not a gate.**
Deployed and wired into cfo-ask's resolve loop this session.

## 4. The baseline number, and it is the one to move.
New instrument `scripts/text-channel/owner-text-scorecard.py` joins each delivered text to
his next inbound within 6 h: ANSWERED / COMPLAINED / IGNORED / UNMEASURABLE. First reading,
30 days: **47 answered, 7 complained, 44 ignored → 52% dead-text rate** (98 judged, 99 distinct
sids from 149 logged rows). Worst families: evening briefing 4/4, the retired digest `'10 UTC
36 items` 3/3, morning briefing 2/2, Weinberg reminder 2/2. The briefings he banned in March
were still being sent in September — volume fell, quality did not.

## 5. Two defects in the deployment path, both now fixed.
`deploy-owner-text.sh` used `install`, which truncates the destination IN PLACE and keeps the
inode — AGENTS.md §8's hazard, on files that cron and the wake loop execute. It now stages to
a sibling, verifies the hash there, and `mv`s (new inode), printing the inode.
`MANIFEST.sha256` is compared with **md5** by `verify-owner-text.sh`; writing sha256 into it
reports every file OUT OF SYNC. It is also CRLF-sensitive: a CRLF manifest reads every name
with a trailing `\r` and says all 17 files are MISSING.

## 6. My own new code produced a false identity within one minute of deployment.
The first `case:` regex required only `WO` + 4 characters, so an ordinary body containing
"WORKING" recorded the topic `case:WORKING` in the live ledger at 15:12:40Z. A topic keyed on
an English word is the same disease as the old `owner_text_suppression` row keyed on a
complaint sentence. Now: case ids must carry digits, and the noun is stripped so `Case #4485`
and `case 4485` are one key. **Derivation rules must be tested against live traffic, not
fixtures** — this was found by reading the ledger, not by the 32 unit checks.

## 7. Restarting the authority's app is not a routine act.
`systemctl restart company-api` took **6.5 minutes to stop** (state `stop-sigterm` timed out,
SIGKILL, 9.9 GB peak) and the new process then could not finish startup: another agent's FTS
migration (`/root/migrate-fts-final.sh`, dropping a 2.7 M-document FTS5 index) held the write
lock on the 34 GB `/root/authority.db`, whose WAL had grown to 17.5 GB. The owner's instruction
was to leave that migration alone — it was progressing (~71% of a core, WAL not growing), not
hung. **Before restarting anything on the authority, check who holds the database**, and
distinguish "slow" from "wedged" with a measurement (CPU ticks, WAL size) rather than patience.

## 8. Transport notes that cost rounds.
`scp`/`ssh` to `/root/code/...` returned **255 intermittently** while `/tmp` worked; piping a
base64 blob into `ssh "base64 -d > path"` succeeded every time. `$Host` is a **reserved**
PowerShell variable — assigning it silently breaks every later expansion. Nested quotes in an
inline `ssh` command get mangled by PowerShell; push a `.sh` and run it (the repo's own rule).
