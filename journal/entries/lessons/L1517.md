**L1517 · A price is not verified until its currency is verified (printerready.com defaults to CAD, not USD)**

On 2026-09-15 I put "a complete renewed ThinkPad X201 retails at $197.99" into a customer case file as
a repair-vs-replace comparison. That number was wrong in currency: printerready.com renders the figure
with an unlabelled "$" while its own control reads "Pricelist: CAD Pricelist". Requesting the USD
pricelist (/shop/change_pricelist/60) just redirects to /shop with CAD active and no changed figure.
The real figure is C$197.99, about US$145 — a ~27% overstatement of the replacement cost, which makes
the repair look *better* than it is.

Rule: a price is not verified until its CURRENCY is verified, not just its digits. On shop sites that
have a currency switcher, read the switcher, and try switching; if switching does not change the
rendered number, treat the price as being in the site's default currency (often CAD/AUD for
"US-looking" refurbishers) and say so. Never let a bare "$" into a customer-facing comparison.

Same page also carried a bogus spec (1366x768 for an X201, which is 1280x800) — a reminder that a
retail listing's spec block is marketing text, not evidence; the manufacturer's HMM/spec sheet is.
