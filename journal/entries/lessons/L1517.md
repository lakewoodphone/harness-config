**L1517 · iPad 6 (A1893) charging port has no solder-free part**

Asked 2026: 'A1893 bent charging port - can I order the part without soldering?' Answer: no. On iPad 6 (2018, A1893/A1954) there is no drop-in/plug-in charging port. The Lightning connector sits on a flex that is soldered to the logic board; the aftermarket part is one shared SKU across iPad Air 1 / iPad 5 (2017) / iPad 6 (2018) and every distributor labels it '(Soldering Required)'. Evidence: MobileSentrix product 'Charging Port Flex Cable For iPad Air 1 / iPad 5 (2017) / iPad 6 (2018) (Soldering Required)' (\.73); LML Repair iPad 6 charging-port guide: 22 steps, ~81 min, tools include soldering iron + desoldering braid, parts include solder and flux. Same distributioner listing also at eSourceParts/BrosPhoneParts. Practical consequence for the shop: part cost is trivial (~\), the whole cost is bench labour, so it is a board-job decision, not a parts-order decision. First field check before any board work: if only the outer shell is deformed and the pins/pad are intact, straighten the shell and re-test - that can avoid the job entirely. Before quoting, confirm which of the three failures it is: (a) shell bent only, (b) connector pads lifted/ripped off board, (c) flex torn.
