**L3923 · The company's completion rate jumped 15->66.7% and the jump is the cap I lifted, not the fixes (n=3); and the collision re-count has an idle-authority confound**

Two readings taken 13:55Z, one confirming a deploy and one that must NOT be read as good news.

**1. The company's completion rate jumped, and the jump is the budget cap, not the fixes.**

 pre (12:00-13:37, the cap in force) 20 sessions 3 completed 0 failed 17 PAUSED 15.0 %
 post (13:37 onward, cap lifted) 3 sessions 2 completed 0 failed 0 paused 66.7 %
 post-raise sessions: 78722 completed/4 steps, 78723 completed/3 steps, 78724 active/1 step

The rate went from 15 % to 66.7 % and **the honest reading is that this measures the row I changed, not the work.** 17 of the 20 pre-raise sessions were `paused` — they did not fail, they did not run — so the pre-window is a cap measurement and comparing it against a running window compares a stopped company to a running one. **This is exactly the claim I committed in writing not to make**: *"a completion rate that rises merely because more sessions run is not evidence of anything, and I will not report it as if it were."*

And the sample is nothing: **n = 3.** Against the day's 36 % baseline, three completions out of three occurs about **4.7 % of the time by chance**. Two completions of three is unremarkable.

**The column that does matter, and it is looking right so far.** The mix among sessions that actually ran:

 post-raise: stalled_repeating 0 STEP_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED 0 TOOL_LOOP_DEADLOCK 1
 pre-raise : stalled_repeating 0 STEP_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED 1 TOOL_LOOP_DEADLOCK 0

`stalled_repeating` was the 38 % class the redirect was built to convert, and it is 0 in both windows — but the pre-window had only three sessions that ran at all, so it is 0 for a trivial reason. **The measure needs 20-30 sessions that ran**, which at ~6/hour is a few hours away. Until then the eight fixes rest on their own tests, not on outcomes.

**2. The allocator: no growth and no post-fix collision — and the confound is still there.**

 collisions 63 at 12:20Z -> 70 at 13:20Z -> 70 at 13:55Z (no growth in 35 minutes)
 newest W2807, W2806, W2805, P3188, P3187, P3186 — the SAME ids, unchanged
 post-fix entries (lessons L3859, wins W2869): 0 collide of 2

**The alternative explanation stands and I will not hide it:** the authority's journal is at **4040 entries, unchanged across every reading this hour** — it has minted nothing, so "no new collisions" is equally explained by the other side being idle. The decisive evidence for this fix remains the *mechanism* reading, which is a direct measurement rather than an inference: `next-id pain` moved **P3200 → P3263** with `highest seen P3199 → P3262`, and the live append minted **W2869**, exactly the number `next-id wins` had predicted. **A prediction that lands is stronger than an absence that does not.**

**What would make the rate reading decisive:** a window in which the authority writes too. The company writing during it does not help — company work sessions do not append journal entries; only sessions on the authority do. So the collision-rate check needs the authority to be writing, and the way to know is its entry count moving off 4040.
