**L1983 · Walk-in hours are not office hours - Dialpad's long phone windows are deliberate, and I nearly 'fixed' them**

OWNER'S WORDS, 2026-09-18, when I proposed changing the phone system's configured hours: "Well, you have to understand the difference between walking hours and office hours. Walk in hours means we're actually their office hours just means when the phone rings. So even if I'm not answering the phone, many times Moshe backrack will answer the phone"

THE RULE: walk-in hours and office/phone hours are DIFFERENT THINGS and must never be reconciled with each other.
 - WALK-IN HOURS = when the shop is physically open to customers: Monday, Tuesday and Thursday 10:30-17:30; Friday 10:30-13:00; CLOSED Wednesday, Saturday and Sunday. Sunday and evening visits are by appointment only. Source: the live website's own published hours, read 2026-09-18.
 - OFFICE / PHONE HOURS = when the phone rings, i.e. when a human may pick up. Moshe Bachrach answers the shop line outside walk-in hours, so this window is deliberately far longer than the walk-in window.
 - LIVE DIALPAD CONFIG, read 2026-09-18 from GET /api/v2/offices (office 5695945642065920, US/Eastern): mon/tue/wed/thu ["00:00","01:00","09:30","23:55"], fri ["09:30","18:00"], sat ["21:00","23:55"], sun ["00:00","01:00","09:30","23:55"]. availability_status was "open" at 23:05 ET on a Thursday, which is CORRECT for phone hours and would have looked like a bug to me.

WHAT I ALMOST DID: I read those long windows as a misconfiguration and asked the owner for permission to rewrite them to the walk-in schedule. He corrected me before I wrote. Had this gone through, after-hours callers who Moshe would have answered would have hit a closed greeting instead. Recorded because the same reading will look like a defect to the next session too.

WHICH SURFACE CARRIES WHICH:
 - CARRIES WALK-IN HOURS (and must say Wednesday closed): Google Business Profile location hours; the website; the phone IVR recordings (scripts/dialpad-ivr-audio.py - closed-hours and dropoff-info both say "Monday through Thursday", which is wrong on Wednesday); the SMS after-hours auto-reply ("Our walk-in hours are Mon-Thu 10:30-5:30, Fri 10:30-1:00" - also wrong on Wednesday).
 - CARRIES PHONE HOURS (leave alone): Dialpad office business hours and the routing options built on them.

DEFECT TO FIX, and it is ours: three of the four walk-in surfaces still say Monday-Thursday. Only the website was corrected.

TRAP FOR THE NEXT READER: Dialpad's own API key in ~/personal-secretary-mvp/.env works (GET /api/v2/offices -> 200), but scripts/dialpad_ivr_admin.py returns HTTP 401 against the group endpoint with that key, so the admin script is not usable as-is for reads. Read the office object directly instead.
