## LESSON 4 — I recommended cancelling Wilner's chip-off on a margin assumption the lab's own email refutes; and a subagent's "settled" fact does not survive contact with the authority (2026-09-22)

**WHAT I GOT WRONG, PLAINLY.** Queue row #127 asked whether the $250 deposit on Wilner's SD card (300DDR ticket 73890) is credited toward the $500 chip-off or charged in addition, and my recommendation was **"Cancel for now"** — on the reasoning that if the deposit is additional then the lab costs $750 against a $660 quote, a $90 loss chosen in advance.

**THE EMAIL SETTLES IT THE OTHER WAY.** 300DDR's Chances-Form policy email, 2026-09-07 17:47 PDT, `chances@300dollardatarecovery.com`: *"we charge $500 per data chip… A non-refundable $250 deposit is due before we can begin a chip-off recovery, and it is applied toward your total."* Their open-cover fee uses the same construction (*"applied toward a successful recovery (it's a deposit, not an addition)"*), and the T&C says open cover *"will be subtracted from"* the fee. So lab = $500 against the $660 quote ⇒ **+$160 margin, not −$90**, and the reason to cancel evaporates. **The correct recommendation is to authorise the chip-off.**

The general rule this is an instance of: **when a queue row turns on a term of a third party's contract, the recommendation must wait for the contract's own words, not a plausible reading of them.** My row was written as a judgement between two readings and then presented as a recommendation — which is exactly how a plausible inference gets acted on. It was one email away, and the email had been on file since 2026-09-07, ten days before the row was written.

**THE SECOND FAILURE, AND IT IS THE MORE DANGEROUS ONE.** A subagent reported the 2025 personal return as **"SETTLED: IT WAS FILED"**, citing `email_triage_log` ids 2461/2468/2482 for FreeTaxUSA acceptance, receipt and account emails. I checked on the authority before it reached the owner and:

- the authority's `email_triage_log` holds no FreeTaxUSA rows at all — searching `subject LIKE '%tax%'` AND `sender LIKE '%freetaxusa%'` across July–August 2026 returns **0 rows**;
- the cited ids 2450/2461/2468/2482 exist but are **different emails entirely** (a newsletter, a Waze policy notice, a Netlify invoice, an FNIRSI promotion), all dated 2026-08-31 — so the ids were not merely stale, they pointed at unrelated mail;
- `memories` holds no FreeTaxUSA row either.

The subagent had said its `ssh secratary-ts` hung and that "its data came from local replicas" — so it was reading a **different machine's database and reporting it as the authority's**. That is `P3 — Reading the wrong data and believing it` in a new costume, and it is the exact failure this system lost customer data to before.

**THE RULE.** A subagent's "settled" is a claim; the authority is the fact. Any fact that will change a decision must be re-queried on the authoritative store — by row id — and the row id must be shown to contain what was claimed, not merely to exist. Where a brief tells a child to verify from records and the child's route to the authority is broken, the child must report **"unverified, no route to the authority"**, never substitute a replica silently. The verification I performed cost two queries and changed the answer: the contradiction between `2025-tax-filing-guide.md` (filed, $0 owed) and `2025-tax-filing-requirement.md` (extension only) **stands**, and both files now carry a dated banner saying so rather than either being quietly trusted.
