**L3647 · A duplicate rule needs a signal OUTSIDE the pair, and the order store is one**

2026-10-13. This is the follow-through on `W2743`, which resolved every collision that could be
judged by SHAPE and left 611 same-source groups alone with a note saying they needed a non-shape
signal. They did, and one was already on the box.

THE SHAPE RULE, and where it stops. Same account + same date + same |amount| is enough when the two
rows come from DIFFERENT sources (one is an import of the other) and share a direction. It is NOT
enough when both rows come from the SAME source: measured, 598 same-source groups, and the biggest
classes are exactly the ones where repeated identical amounts are normal -
 paypal-api 355
 twilio-billing 89 (two SMS at the same per-message price on one day)
 Generic Three Column 48
 Heuristic CSV Profile 43
So a broader shape rule would have marked hundreds of GENUINE transactions as duplicates.

THE NON-SHAPE SIGNAL, already present: `marketplace_orders`. It holds 671 orders with dates and
totals, built this session from Gmail receipts. For a collision group it answers a question shape
cannot: how many purchases actually happened that day at that amount.
 * exactly ONE matching order -> two identical charges cannot both be it. 70 groups resolved.
 * two or more matching orders -> both charges are real. Left alone.
 * no matching order -> no evidence either way. Left alone.

CUMULATIVE EFFECT of the whole duplicate pass, measured:
 collision groups 1,982 (unchanged - the same account/date/amount census)
 none marked duplicate 693 -> 541
 exactly one marked 1,080 -> 1,322
 ALL rows marked (invisible) -> 0 (was 209 groups with two or more marked; 97 with all)
 scoreboard non_duplicate 6,263 -> 6,209
 unexplained 556 -> 597
 pct_clear 91.12 -> 90.38

THE RULE. When a duplicate test is symmetric in the two rows, it has no information beyond their
similarity, and similarity is exactly what two genuine identical purchases also have. The
discriminator has to come from OUTSIDE the pair - an order, a receipt, the other leg of a transfer.
Before writing "these look alike, so one is fake", ask what in the world would have had to happen for
both to be real, and go and look for it.
