**L3049 · Directory hours are routed through the wrong branch and will silently kill a time-critical plan**

Measured 2026-09-28, confirming an 8:00 PM booking at Activate Woodbridge.

**The venue's own location record says Mon-Thu 12:00 PM - 10:00 PM.** I read it at `playactivate.com/woodbridge`.

**A directory says Mon-Fri 9:00 a.m. - 6:00 p.m.** (Birdeye's profile for the same 893 St Georges Ave address).

Had I trusted the directory, I would have told the owner that an 8 PM slot was impossible and to reschedule a family outing that was already arranged. Both listings name the same address and the same business. **One of them is a stale branch record and it is not obvious which, because a directory listing looks exactly as authoritative as a venue page.**

**The tell, when there is one:** an entertainment venue open 9-6 Monday to Friday and *closed at weekends* is backwards. Family entertainment peaks on evenings and weekends; a Mon-Fri 9-6 / Sat-Sun closed pattern is an **office's** hours. **The shape of the business should match the shape of the hours** - a mismatch is a signal that the hours belong to something else, often a head office or a shared listing.

Related and already in the record (L3048): Baskin-Robbins Route 37 West publishing a **05:00** opening - implausible for an ice cream shop, almost certainly inherited Dunkin' hours behind a shared listing. Same failure, different costume.

**RULE for anything time-critical: read the hours off the venue's own booking/location page, and treat every aggregator's hours as a lead to verify, never as the fact.** Birdeye, Loc8NearMe, Trip.com, MapQuest and Yelp are useful for address and phone and for *finding* a venue; they are not the record for whether the door is open. When a directory and the venue disagree, **the venue wins and the disagreement gets written down** - not silently resolved.
