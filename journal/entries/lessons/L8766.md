**L8766 · A check that fires on empty files teaches you to ignore the check**

Measured 2026-10-18, on the search index's own integrity check.

The rule was 'documents with no chunks are a defect'. It went red on **4,071 of 676,075** documents. Every single one was a **0-byte file** — an empty \__init__.py\, an empty XML stub — where having no chunks is correct.

The failure that check exists for is the opposite shape: a document that HAD content and produced no chunks. It would answer nothing and look exactly like 'we never had it'. By conflating the two, the check produced a number that was true and useless, and the lesson from this estate is what happens next: an alarm that fires on a non-defect gets muted, and the real one fires into silence.
