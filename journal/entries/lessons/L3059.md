**L3059 · Put the MESH-HOST first-line instruction in every mesh subagent brief, or the lane output is thrown away**

Evidence: 2026-09-29, five mesh subagent lanes dispatched from ZABZ-YOGA to ZABZ-TECH for time-critical trip research. Three children opened their report with the line "MESH-HOST: ZABZ-TECH" and came back complete. Two did not, and both settled as failed: "the child did not begin its report with MESH-HOST: <hostname> - its location is unproven, so the run is not reported as complete" - and job_output returned "(no new output)", so the entire answer was discarded, not merely flagged. Cost: 2 of 5 lanes dead after roughly 2.5 minutes each, on a task with a hard clock.

The one-line fix that works: open every mesh brief with - Begin your report with the line MESH-HOST: <hostname> as the very first line. That marker is required by the dispatcher; without it the run is rejected. Every lane dispatched with that wording afterwards came back complete (3 of 3, same fleet, same node).

Related pain: P2667, which additionally asks the harness to preserve the child's text on a location-unproven failure, since only the provenance is in doubt and the content is usually still usable.
