**L3059 · A mode that claims to change nothing must leave the RECORD unchanged too: three defects on the owner text path**

MEASURED 2026-09-29, on the path that texts the owner. Three defects, one shape: a mode or a
mechanism that claims one thing and does another. This is the same family as the silent
deterministic fallback that hid a week of model outages (L1606) and the truncated-column
measurement that misread a row (H2987).

### 1. `run --dry-run` COULD SEND A REAL TEXT TO THE OWNER'S PHONE
`sms-responder.py` documents `run --dry-run` as "decide, send nothing". Two owner-reply call
sites hardcoded `textsend.send(..., dry_run=False)` and nothing in `cmd_run` gated them:
the brain-reply branch (`_action == "reply"`) and `_reply_with_top_question`. Any tick with a
claimable row in dry-run mode would have texted +18483897895 for real. Fixed by threading
`dry_run=args.dry_run` from `cmd_run` through `_maybe_ask_one` and `_reply_with_top_question`.
The other four `dry_run=False` sites were checked and left alone: `_send_holding` (guarded),
`_do_work` (guarded), the non-owner reply (guarded), and `_ack_owner_request`, which has no
caller at all - dead code, and its unguarded send is a landmine for whoever wires it up.

### 2. `--dry-run` CONSUMED THE QUESTION IT DID NOT SEND
`_reply_with_top_question` sends, then stamps `owner-queue.py defer --until +7d` and calls
`_record_offered`. Both ran regardless of mode. So a dry run marked a question as asked and
made it re-askable only in seven days, without putting it to him - silently spending the one
thing that path exists to deliver. A dry run must leave the record exactly as it found it.
Verified: `offered.db` before `[162,164,166,167,183]`, after `[162,164,166,167,183]`.

### 3. THE RECOMMENDATION WAS THE PART BEING DESTROYED
`_reply_with_top_question` composed `question + " I recommend: " + recommendation` and then cut
with `body[:297].rstrip() + "..."`. Measured on the four texts this path actually sent on
2026-09-29: all four were exactly 300 characters and three ended MID-WORD, every one of them
inside the recommendation - `I recommend: Pay the $597.29 in ca...`, `It is four mont...`,
`the form a...`. The recommendation is the part he acts on, so the old code reliably deleted
the actionable half of every message he received. He had already complained: on 2026-09-25 he
texted "The text you are sending me have way too much information at once. I don't read them."
- and that message was itself filed `ignored` with reason `allow=never`, because his own number
was stored as a stranger. See P-owner-as-stranger below.

FIX AND PROOF. The question now absorbs the trim, nothing is cut except at a sentence boundary,
and the recommendation is never dropped; `textsend`'s own SOFT_LIMIT is 930 so a 600-char
budget is safe. Proven by extracting the patched block from the installed file and running it on
the real queue rows behind those four texts: #162 300c-cut -> 584c ending `Do not use the
superseded $2,318.73 run sheet.`; #167 300c-cut at `four mont` -> 303c `It is four months on.`;
#183 300c-cut at `the form a` -> 597c ending at a sentence boundary. `PASS on all 4 rows`.

THE RULE. When a mode or a helper claims to change nothing, it must leave the RECORD unchanged
too, not just the world. And when a message has a part the reader has to act on, that part is
the one that must never be the one that gets truncated.
