**L6169 · The waiting tool was right: an outbound call closes a wait, and dialpad_call_full shows calls the SMS thread hides**

Pulling up Yosef Hoff (8456712943) on 2026-10-13: the comms thread (ps_comms_thread) and the indexed comms search both ended with his inbound "Are you available to look at it today?", so I concluded he had been waiting ~25 hours - and ps_comms_waiting did not list him at either 3 days or 7 days. I was one step from filing that as a tool defect.

It is not a defect. A direct read of dialpad_call_full showed two OUTBOUND calls to his number at 2026-10-13 19:03:12Z and 19:03:13Z, ended 19:03:41Z, ~4.5 s of talk each - minutes old and already in the index the waiting tool reads. The wait had been closed by a call, and an SMS-first thread view does not show that at the end of the thread.

Rules that follow:
- ps_comms_waiting counts calls. When it omits someone the SMS thread says is waiting, check the calls before believing either: select direction, state, duration_seconds, datetime(date_started_ms/1000,'unixepoch') as started_utc from dialpad_call_full where contact_phone like '%<last10>%' order by date_started_ms.
- Every call appears TWICE in dialpad_call_full, once with target_name 'Eliyahu' and once with target_name 'Lakewood Phone & Tech'. Two rows are ONE call; never report it as two calls to two people.
- state='hangup' with 4.5 s of talk is not a conversation. Talk duration, not state, decides whether anyone spoke.
- "An empty result is a refusal, not health" applies to my own inferences too: a party missing from a waiting list is a measurement to explain, not evidence about the tool.
