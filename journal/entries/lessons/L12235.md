**L12235 · Who opened the thread is the consent: outbound policy has two axes, not one switch (owner, D45)**

Outbound policy is not one switch. Owner, 2026-10-21, answering the website lead path (register D45): inside a conversation the customer started — including the single acknowledgement that opens it — the shop line answers LIVE. He rejected "approve every message" in as many words: it would make any promise of a reply impossible.

He chose option 1 of three: one immediate acknowledgement text, then live replies from the shop line.

THE RULE, GENERALISED (and it is the part worth carrying): the customer opening the conversation IS the consent. Two axes decide a message, not one — WHO OPENED THE THREAD, and WHETHER IT ASKS FOR MONEY. Cold message, or a request for money = draft and approve. Reply inside a thread they started = live. A single "live or drafts" bit either floods the owner or freezes the business, which is why the old framing could not be answered.

WHERE THIS IS RECORDED: docs/decisions/web-lead-path-2026-10-21.md §7, and docs/website/OWNER-DECISIONS.md D45. Owner-queue #906 asked the general form of this question; its COLD half — agent-initiated outreach that starts a conversation — is NOT answered by this and must not be read as if it were.

WHAT IT CHANGES NOW: the website's Text-us acknowledgement may ship live; the SMS desk may reply live inside a customer-started thread.
