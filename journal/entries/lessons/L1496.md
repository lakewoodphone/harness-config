**L1496 · Her machines were crippled by a policy override that the owner had already reversed**

MEASURED 2026-09-15, by diffing her merged settings against mine.

Both of Yocheved's machines (DESKTOP-FGV6KMH laptop, LakewooechsMini) carried permission.defaultPreset: workspace-write in settings/machines/*.yaml, overriding ase.yaml's danger-full-access. The comment above the override still justified it with 'unable to make breaking changes to his systems'. The owner has since stated the opposite twice, explicitly: err toward MORE power, she is full LPT manager, don't cripple her agent, and the boundary is that edits from her machine are LABELLED -- not restricted.

A policy that contradicts the owner's stated intent is worse than no policy, because the comment makes it look deliberate. Both overrides are now removed, with the owner's own words quoted in the file so the next reader cannot re-narrow it by accident.

LESSON: when the owner states a boundary, grep the fleet for code that enforces the OLD boundary. Prose in a config comment is not a record of the current decision.
