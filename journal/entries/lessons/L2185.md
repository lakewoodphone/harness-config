**L2185 · Store an owner's dictated health episode verbatim; never resolve a truncated sentence by inference**

RULE: When the owner dictates a health episode by voice, store the dictation **verbatim**, put every reconstruction of date/time/sequence behind an explicit `INFERRED` or `REPORTED` marker, and never silently resolve an ambiguity - not even an obvious one.

WHY: The 2026-09-20 report was garbled and cut off mid-sentence ("the big problem is that tonight is ___"). The obvious reading was a minor detail; the truth was **Yom Kippur starting that evening**, which turned a self-limited viral illness into a time-boxed dehydration risk and added ~25.5 hours nil-by-mouth on top of vomiting and drenching sweats less than 24 hours earlier.

HOW TO APPLY: Quote the owner's words unedited in the record, mark the reconstruction separately, and treat an unfinished sentence as an open question rather than a rounding error - then ask for just that missing clause. The clause you assume away is the one that changes the plan.
