**L1882 · A slow capacity reading removes a node from the broker's fits pool entirely**

A node whose capacity read comes back `slow` is removed from the broker's `fits` pool ENTIRELY, not ranked last: packages/mesh-broker/lib/broker.js:608-614, `usable = preferred.length > 0 ? preferred : dispatchable`, where `preferred` drops every slow node. `76-broker.md` §10.8's intent ("a busy node is not a dead one") holds for the highest-slots/transport tiers but not for fits. MEASURED 2026-09-17: `zabz-yoga-1` (the owner's laptop, which is also running his engine) alternated ok/slow every few seconds (three consecutive fresh reads: ok, slow, ok), and in TWO separate runs of 2 and 8 concurrent children the laptop read `slow` in the reading the placements used - so it received nothing while the desktop took everything, even when it had more free slots than the desktop had left. Consequence for the owner: the machine he works on is the one whose gate is slowest, so "the laptop idles while the desktop is full" may be a reading problem, not a capacity problem. Fix belongs in packages/mesh-broker (not this package): rank a slow node last inside `fits` rather than dropping it. Evidence: docs/mesh/92-provider-placement.md §5.2, §5.3, §6 item 2.
