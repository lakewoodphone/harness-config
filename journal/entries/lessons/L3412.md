**L3412 · To record an owner-paid business expense, append to the A8 owner-ruled schedule — a hand-inserted owner_loan_movements row double-counts the loan**

**L3399 · To record an owner-paid business expense in the books store, append to the A8 schedule — never insert an `owner_loan_movements` row by hand.**

Measured 2026-10-09 while recording the Weinberg settlement (1,000.00 paid from the owner's personal Chase College *9730 for an LPT wage obligation).

- `books/personal/owner_loan.py` **generates** the register: `write_movements()` upserts by `_stable_id` (a hash of date|amount|direction|component|method|source_ref|memo) and **prunes only rows whose provenance names its own generator** (`personal.owner_loan`). A hand-inserted row therefore survives the next run *beside* the generated one and **double-counts the loan** — 1,000.00 of phantom debt to the owner, with no error anywhere.
- The supported insertion point for a payment the owner made personally (a Zelle, a cash handout, a personal card) is the **A8** row list in `books/personal/owner_ruled_schedules.json`: `{"date", "amount", "description"}`. The module verifies every scheduled row against the ledger mirror at run time and counts only what it finds.
- **A scheduled row that is not yet in the ledger is a WARN, not a zero and not silence**: `owner-ruled-row-not-found`, naming the nearest same-date ledger rows. The match key is **date + amount in cents**, so a Plaid row that lands on a different date will fail to match and must have the schedule row re-dated.
- The same reasoning applies to the journal: `books/accounting/journal.py` derives entries from `transactions`, so a manual entry against a row the pipeline also posts would post the cash account twice. **Do not hand-write either table.**

Related: the A8 component's own ruling is the owner's ("if it came out of personal funds for the business, it is a loan"), and the 2026-06-15 Abdan payment (1,453.03, `Zelle to Yaakov Abdan (business car)`) is the precedent row.
