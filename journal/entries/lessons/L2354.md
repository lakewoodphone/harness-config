**L2354 · A family-chat simcha broadcast must be checked against EVERY simcha in the same thread - I named a new baby and skipped two existing birthdays**

MEASURED FAILURE, 2026-09-23, SECRATARY, on a Chip broadcast I authored for the owner.

WHAT HAPPENED. Mommy 12:10 - "Mazel tov! Happy birthday to Michoel and Avrami and Baby Klein!" - and Yitz 18:47 - accepted to the kollel at Chaim Berlin. I wrote a broadcast naming the new boy and the kollel, and omitted Michoel and Avrami, who are Shabsi's two sons and whose birthdays were the first thing in that same thread. Shabsi replied at 20:25: "What about my kids birthdays? I've got 2 birthdays today! Not nice..." The owner had to read the miss in his own family chat, which is the part that matters.

ROOT CAUSE, stated honestly: I read the thread for tone and callbacks and treated the LOUDEST items (a birth, an acceptance) as the content. The birthdays sat in the same 20-line window, in a one-line message from Mommy that I read and then dropped because it did not look like an announcement. Content review for a family message is not "did I capture the big news" - it is "whose day is it, for every person named in the window".

THE RULE. Before any family-chat message that names a simcha, list every person named in the recent thread and ask whose occasion it is. A birthday is as loud to the family as a birth, and dropping one is a personal insult that the responsible agent never hears about unless the owner reads it out.

WHAT WAS DONE ABOUT IT. A correction went to the same 11 phones in Chip's voice, naming both boys; a chip_memories row now records that Shabsi's boys share a birthday and that a simcha must not be logged without checking whose; Chip's automatic replies were PAUSED for the window (chip_enabled=0) so he could not add a tone-deaf canned line to an angry thread, then restored (verified ai_enabled=True).

SECOND, SMALLER LESSON. The owner rejects "greeting-card" register. Two drafts were bounced - headline-plus-list first, then a dry apology - before he said "way weirder"; what landed was the machine describing its own failure (replaying the hour, finding a hole shaped like two names, saying the names aloud in an empty basement, offering to hold the plug, "I have nowhere else to go"). When he asks for weird, give the machine's interior, not its manners.
