- **#676** - informational: the 1,000 Zelle is his own money moving between his own accounts (row 725 has the matching in/out).

## Two failure shapes worth naming
1. **"Left active as a watch flag" is not a watch flag.** A `status` field with two meanings - "obligation open" and "remind me to check the balance" - guarantees the sweeper raises a settled bill with the severity of an unpaid one. A watch flag needs its own field (`recheck_after` / `watching`), or the note that says PAID is invisible to the query.
2. **A queue row whose recommendation is "Investigate it and bring me a recommendation" is a non-recommendation.** #1027 was the top of the queue with that text, `options_json` offering "decide: pay it or dispute it". That sentence costs the owner a decision and gives him nothing; the row should not have been created in that state - it should have been investigated first, which cost about six minutes.
