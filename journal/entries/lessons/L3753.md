**L3753 · company-api does not leak - it plateaus at about 7.2 GB, and that working set is the problem**

## Corrects the 'slow growth' reading recorded in L3752 and P3140, 2026-10-12

MEASURED, secratary-cloud, same boot:
- 13:19-13:33Z (cold, five measured samples): 2826 -> 3298 MB, i.e. the start-up ramp.
 NOT the steady state, and I first reported it as though it were the whole curve.
- 17:46-17:50Z (six samples, 45 s apart): 7171, 7191, 7249, 7182, 7191, 7199 MB.
 That is a PLATEAU, flat to within ~80 MB over four minutes.

CONCLUSION: company-api is not leaking. It settles at about 7.2 GB resident and stays there.
Its peak before an oom-kill was 7.4 GB - the same number - so an oom-kill takes it out at
essentially its own working set, which is why the kills look so regular and why raising the
ceiling of the OTHER consumer is the right stopgap.

WHY THIS MATTERS MORE THAN THE CORRECTION: on a 15 GiB box, company-api needs ~7.2 GB and one
heavy transcript-archive pass needs ~7.2 GB. Those are the two big residents and they cannot
both peak. That is arithmetic, not tuning. Either the box grows or one of the two must be made
to need less: the pull's decompress-then-scan is the cheaper one to fix
(transcript-archive-server.py:decompress_zstd holds both the compressed and decompressed bytes).

HOW I GOT IT WRONG: I measured a window that happened to be the start-up ramp and generalised
from it. A growth claim needs samples that span a plateau, not samples from the ramp - and
'systemctl show -p MemoryCurrent' is cheap enough that there was no excuse.
