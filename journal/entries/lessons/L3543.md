**L3543 · Galaxy S26 rear camera lens glass is a separate 1.79 part; MobileSentrix does not stock it**

## What was learned

A Galaxy S26 (base, SM-S942) **rear camera lens glass is a separate, cheap, buyable part** — it is not a back-cover job.

- **Injured Gadgets** (US): `Premium - Rear Camera Lens for Samsung Galaxy S26 5G (S942) (Glass Only)`, SKU `SS-26-SP-05`, **$1.79, 16 in stock** — page read 2026-10-08 via r.jina.ai (the direct fetch 403s; the reader proxy works where the fetch tool does not).
- **Samsung OEM service pack:** lens part `GH64-10107A` (wide), `GH64-10108A` for S26/S26+ wide — listed by EU shops, no US lens-only OEM SKU found.
- **MobileSentrix stocks NO lens-only part for the S26** — only *Back Cover Glass With Camera Lens* ($11.38–11.97 aftermarket). Confirmed twice on 2026-10-08: the owner's own page dump and an independent refetch. So the correct sourcing for this repair is **not** our usual house.

**Repairability, correctly attributed (this is where a careless read goes wrong):**
- **Base S26: PBKreviews scored it 9/10** and named camera lens covers/rings as quickly replaceable.
- **S26 Ultra: iFixit scored it 5/10** — and that score is about the *screen* and the epoxied front camera, **not** about the rear lens glass. Do not merge the two scores into one claim about "the S26".

**Procedure/time:** 30–60 min for an experienced tech; heat 60–80 °C, mask the optics, score and lift with a scalpel, clean, bond, press, inspect under magnification. Risks that belong in the quote: shards scratching the module window, trapped dust, module damage from the original impact (S26 wide module only $19.18 at MobileSentrix), IP68 not restored.
