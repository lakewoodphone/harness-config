**L1661 · When a mitigation works the leak underneath stops being visible: measure the resource, not the suite**

**When a mitigation works, the leak underneath it stops being visible -- so measure the resource, not
the suite.**

Measured 2026-09-15. Raising `RLIMIT_NOFILE` in conftest turned the suite green in round 46, and the
temptation was to call the descriptor problem handled. It was not: the suite was green *because* the
limit was raised, and nothing in the suite said how much was leaking. Two probes made it visible:

- per-file descriptor counts: **+8,160 across 199 of 205 files**, peaking at 4,250 open;
- descriptor **types** at session end: 285 sockets + 139 eventpoll, i.e. leaked event loops, after the
 SQLite handles were closed by the first fix.

The ordering mattered: the first probe said *how much*, the second said *what*, and only then was the
fix obvious (two fixtures, one calling a helper the app already had).

Rules:
- **A raised limit is a measurement waiting to happen.** If you mitigate a resource limit, measure the
 resource afterwards and write the number down -- otherwise the mitigation becomes the reason nobody
 looks again.
- **Classify, don't just count.** "+437 descriptors" is a worry; "285 sockets and 139 eventpoll" is a
 diagnosis that names the fix.
- **Check the app before writing new machinery.** `close_db_connections()` existed and was documented for
 test teardown; the tests simply never called it.
