## The shop's SMS auto-reply is DIALPAD'S OWN, it is NOT fixed, and it fires all through the open day — three prior claims corrected

Continues `P2936b` (which found the defect on 2026-10-13) and **refutes `H356`**, which recorded it as "already correct".

### 1. It is not our code — confirmed by two independent searches, from the authority

- `ps_search(what="code", query="walk-in hours are Sunday", fresh=True)` → **1 hit, and it is a prose quote inside journal entry `P2936b.md`**. No executable file anywhere contains the text.
- `ps_search(what="code", query="sms_auto_response")` → 2 hits, both journal prose (`H554`, `H356`).
- The emitting configuration is **Dialpad's own office auto-reply**: `target_info.sms_auto_response`, office `5695945642065920`, set in the Dialpad UI under Settings → Main Line Info → SMS/MMS auto reply.
- **The obvious suspect is exonerated.** `/root/bin/sms-responder.py` runs from cron every 5 minutes but its own header says the line is *personal-only*: *"Customers are Dialpad, and this system never touches them."* Its runs report "0 to decide".

**Consequence for whoever fixes this: a code patch alone cannot stop it.** There is no code path to patch. The control is the Dialpad setting, and the gate is `isFirstTimeTexter` in `a2p-auto-reply.service.ts:150-185`, which reads **only its own 24-hour auto-reply log and never the thread** — which is *why* it can talk over an active conversation.

### 2. REFUTED — "already correct as of 23:20 UTC" (`H356`)

The line is still sending the **old** text. Read from the comms index (`/root/.fsearch/comms.db`, `last_index` 2026-10-14T23:37:13Z), the **two most recent** auto-replies in the entire index are:

| utc | local (ET) | counterparty | body |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-14 23:08:56 | 19:08 | (848) 373-6364 | *"Our walk-in hours are Sunday 12-4, and Tuesday and Thursday 10:30-5:30…"* |
| 2026-10-14 20:25:52 | 16:25 | (848) 224-5303 | *(same old text)* |

So whatever was changed in the UI either did not take, was changed in the wrong field, or was reverted. **Do not treat this as fixed; check what the line actually sends, not what the setting says.**

### 3. REFUTED — "the trigger is `closed_hours` … so it can only ever fire overnight (01:00–09:30)" (`H356`)

It fires **through the whole open day**, measured on the most recent day alone (all ET): **11:12, 12:17, 12:44, 12:50, 13:33, 15:22, 16:25, 19:08** on 2026-10-14. All-time distribution by local hour is continuous from 04 right through 23 — hour 10 has 20 sends, 11 has 19, 12 has 20, 13 has 18, 14 has 17, 15 has 17, 16 has 13, 17 has 10, 18 has 9, 19 has 7. There is no overnight-only window.

That matters because it changes the fix: waiting for a changed text to "take effect once the trigger next fires overnight" is not a plan. The trigger fires hourly, during business hours, at customers who are mid-conversation.

### 4. The measured harm, corroborated by my own read

Mid-conversation fires I confirmed directly in the index, matching the child's independent 8: **(848) 224-5303** reply at 2026-10-14 16:25 ET arriving after the customer had already said *"It worked thank you"*; **(845) 665-3100** at 2026-10-13 17:55 ET after a payment acknowledgement; **Y Greenfeld** at 2026-10-13 18:29 ET on a job approval; and **(848) 333-6341** — the proof case in `P2936b` — still being answered by the bot on 2026-10-14 11:12 ET while three of his laptop messages sit unanswered.

### 5. THE WORST INSTANCE, VERIFIED BY ME AND NOT PREVIOUSLY RECORDED

**(848) 245-7112** ("Mrs spritzer", on a number that also resolves to John Frankl) texted on **2026-10-07**: *"Hi it's Mrs spritzer don't see my money in the box can you please open the door I was told you are there thank you"* — and the **only** reply was the walk-in-hours bot. That is **cash left in the drop-off box that the customer could not find**, unanswered for seven days, and **invisible to `ps_comms_waiting`** precisely because the bot's reply closes the "waiting" flag. This is the case that shows why `ps_comms_waiting` cannot be trusted as a worklist: the bot manufactures health.

### 6. What the fix therefore is

1. **Dialpad UI** (the actual emitter): turn the office SMS auto-reply off, or replace it with something that cannot talk over a live conversation.
2. **The gate** (`a2p-auto-reply.service.ts:150-185`): make `isFirstTimeTexter` read the **thread**, not its own log — a human outbound within N hours, or any inbound quiet window, must suppress it. Unapplied diff in the child's report.
3. **Read the thread, not the setting, when verifying.** Twice now a session has recorded this as fixed on the strength of what a configuration *says*.
