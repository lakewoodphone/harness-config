**L3533 · A case file can be stale relative to production, and the sync record's nextAction is where a dead price hides**

A hub case file and production drift apart in one direction that matters, and it is not
the direction the standard assumes. `lpt-hub/AGENTS.md` says "when they disagree, the case
file wins and the mirror is regenerated" — that rule is about the sync record being a
mirror of the case file. It says nothing about production moving on its own, which it does:
the secretary reconciliation job walks the open work-order board and closes rows.

Measured 2026-10-08 on Chaviva Eisermann / WO26100612723 (order 2971). Production had
carried `READY_FOR_PICKUP` since 2026-10-08 03:22 with a RESOLUTION note, while the hub case
file still read `status: in_progress` and `updated: 2026-10-06` — and the case file's own
last activity entry already recorded the retrieval as complete. Two records of one case
disagreed, and the stale one was the file everyone treats as the record.

The reconcile move is to read production back through `lpt ticket` and fix the case file to
match, not the other way round. What made it findable was checking `updatedAt` on the order
against `updated` in the front-matter: a mismatch of more than a day on an open case is the
signal.

The second, sharper defect was in the sync record and is worth hunting by name:
`operational.nextAction` is free prose that no guard validates, and it kept
"75 if the contacts come off, 50 labour if they do not" for two days after the owner
superseded that quote with a 65 total in the same hour he gave it. `pricing.quoteTotal` was
already correct at 65 — only the prose was stale, and the prose is the field a later session
quotes a customer from. **When a case's price is superseded, the sync record's
`operational.nextAction` is the field most likely to still carry the dead number.** Check it
by name whenever you touch a case file, not just `pricing`.

Related: a case status of `ready` in the hub vocabulary requires the customer *has been
told* (CASE-FILE-STANDARD.md §3), where production's `READY_FOR_PICKUP` is set by
reconciliation on the bench outcome alone. The two look like the same fact and are not.
