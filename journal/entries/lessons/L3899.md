**L3899 · Galaxy A42 5G (SM-A426) screen swap: no calibration exists for this model; carry the original fingerprint sensor**

Question that produced this: does a Galaxy A42 5G (SM-A426U) screen replacement need recalibration, or is it a straight swap?

VERIFIED 2026 (sources read in-session):
1. Samsung's own repair guide (SM-X716B_RepairGuide, FR, p.31) says: optical fingerprint reader calibration is required after a SCREEN replacement to guarantee optimal performance, and it is performed through the Self Repair Assistant app -- "availability of this function depends on your model".
2. Samsung support (self-repair-assistant-app) lists what that assistant calibrates: optical fingerprint sensor, proximity sensor, battery.
3. The A42 5G is NOT in Samsung's self-repair programme (samsung.com/us/support/self-repair lists S25 series / Z Fold6 era). So on SM-A426 there is no calibration routine to run.
4. The fingerprint module on the A42 is a SEPARATE service part: GH96-14029A, sold as "Fingerprint sensor For Samsung Galaxy A42 5G Service Pack" (foneday.shop). Panel and sensor are two parts.
5. The 26-step A426 panel replacement guide (lmlrepair.com) never disconnects or transfers the fingerprint sensor -- it stays on the frame. Same in a with-frame assembly: carry the old sensor over.
6. Aftermarket incell/TFT assemblies for A426 are explicitly sold as NOT supporting the fingerprint (kiftech: "Not Supporting Fingerprint Identification"; MobileSentrix: "Without Finger Print Sensor"). That -- not calibration -- is what kills the reader.

OPERATING RULE: carry the ORIGINAL fingerprint sensor; never fit a new one unless the old is dead, because a brand-new optical sensor is the only case where Samsung's calibration requirement bites and this model cannot be calibrated. Buy the Service Pack OLED (GH82-24375A), not the incell, whenever the customer uses biometrics.

BENCH TEST before closing the phone: enroll a fingerprint (Settings > Biometrics), and check the proximity sensor on a call (screen must blank). Do NOT hand the phone back untested -- an uncalibrated/dead reader is a comeback, and it is not detectable from the screen working.

Sources: downloadcenter.samsung.com SM-X716B RepairGuide; samsung.com/de/support/mobile-devices/self-repair-assistant-app; samsung.com/us/support/self-repair; foneday.shop GH96-14029A; lmlrepair.com A426 screen guide; kiftech.co.za + mobilesentrix.co.uk A426 incell listings; gsmarena A42 5G spec (under display, optical).
