**L6189 · Google Messages has no cloud archive - a phone off for months can never be backfilled**

Answer to the recurring shop question: "customer has two phones with Google Messages, one was off for
months — how do we get all his messages in."

## The answer is mostly no, and the reason matters

Google Messages is **not a cloud archive**. Message history is a local database on each phone.
Signing into the Google account restores nothing; RCS and device pairing restore nothing. Google's
only Google-side copy is the Android device backup, which restores **only during new-device setup**
and **only if it was switched on beforehand** — it will not help a phone already set up and in use.

Undelivered messages expire in **days, not months**:
- SMS/MMS wait in the carrier's store-and-forward queue (SMSC) for roughly a day to a few days,
 carrier-dependent, then are dropped as expired.
- RCS holds an undelivered message on a TTL, then expires it.

**Consequence:** a phone off for months has permanently missed everything sent to it in that window.
No tool, no carrier ticket, no Google support path recovers it. Never sell "message recovery" for
this, and never quote a data-recovery vendor for it.

## The branch IS the answer — check it first

Same number on both phones? Messages → tap the profile avatar shows the number; or Settings → About
phone → SIM status.
1. **Same number** → nothing was lost to the network. Everything went to the phone that was on, so
 the full history exists on that phone and can be copied across.
2. **Two different numbers** → each phone only ever held its own number's messages. The off phone's
 missed traffic is gone. There is nothing to consolidate.

## The tool, for case 1

**SMS Backup & Restore** (SyncTech, free, Play Store). Back up/export on the source phone, restore on
the target. Restore **merges** — it does not wipe what is already on the target. With both phones on
the same Wi-Fi it transfers direct, no cloud. This is the shop path; Google's own backup cannot do it
on a phone already in use.

**Trap, and it is the expensive one:** never factory-reset or "clear data" on Google Messages on the
phone holding the history. That phone holds the only copy. The shop has lost customer data twice.

## Then fix the phone that was off

After months powered down, RCS usually comes up stuck on "connecting/verifying", which the customer
experiences as *no messages coming in at all*. Google's article:
https://support.google.com/messages/answer/9077245

Fix: update Google Messages; Settings → RCS chats → off, wait, on. If still stuck, clear cache on
Google Messages **and** Carrier Services, toggle airplane mode, reboot. SMS/MMS flow regardless once
the SIM is active — RCS is the part that breaks.

## Going forward

Google Messages can link several Android devices to one number, but that is **live delivery**: both
phones must be powered on and connected, and it does not backfill. A phone that is off still misses
everything. If one history is wanted, one phone is primary and history is copied; that is the only
real consolidation.
