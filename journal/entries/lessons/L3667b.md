**L3667b · Rank the reconciliation by evidence, not magnitude: eight sign-flips come first**

2026-10-13. Ranking the 69 disagreements by MAGNITUDE was the wrong order. Ranking them by the
EVIDENCE each carries points at eight cases first, and they are the ones most likely to give up their
cause on the first look.

THE CLASSIFICATION, from the chain numbers themselves. For each disagreement the note gives the opening
value, the flow, and the closing value, so the class is computable rather than assumed:
 large, same sign 32 40,653.95
 SIGN-FLIP (flow and movement opposite) 8 13,471.09
 small (<= 100) 29 1,194.17
 unparsed 0
A sign-flip means the account's balance moved one way while the transactions recorded against it moved
the other - the balance went UP while the ledger says money went OUT. That is not a fee and not
rounding; it is the signature of a row that is MISSING from the ledger, large enough to reverse the
month. Nothing else in the set carries that quality of evidence.

I had set `personal-amex-94045 2026-08` aside earlier because its opening anchor is a `manual` reading
the tool warns about, reasoning that its 6,427.66 was as likely a bad anchor as a missing row. That
reasoning was fine as far as it went - but the SIGN is independent of which anchor is at fault. A
balance that rises while the ledger says the account went down is wrong under either explanation, and
it is the single strongest case in the set rather than the most doubtful one.

A STRUCTURAL FACT THAT MAKES THE WHOLE SET SMALLER. `personal-chase-9650` holds ALL 25 month-anchors -
the best-covered account in the ledger - and 24 of them "disagree". But their ratios tell a different
story: median flow/movement 1.01, and 15 of the 24 agree within about 40 dollars. **A disagreement is
not the same as a problem.** The tool reports every non-zero difference, which is right for an audit and
misleading as a work list: most of those 24 are fees and rounding, and the real work in that account is
the handful of months whose ratio flips sign or runs to 2.51 and 6.39.

THE WORK LIST, REORDERED. The eight sign-flips first - each should reveal the mechanism on its own
because the cause is large and directional. Then the 32 large same-sign. **The 29 small ones, 1,194.17
in total and 21.6 per case, are fee-shaped and should be closed as a class rather than as cases** - a
note that they are unexplained fees, not twenty-nine investigations.
