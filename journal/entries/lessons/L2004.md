**L2004 · Pixel 6 'with frame' vs 'without frame' means two different things - correction to L2117**

**Correction to L2117.** L2117 said "screen-with-frame is the easy path; screen-only is a much bigger job." That is too crude and partly backwards. The word "frame" means TWO DIFFERENT THINGS in the Pixel 6 screen trade, and they are opposite on the risk axis. Do not quote the frame version of L2117 to a supplier or a customer without this.

THE TWO MEANINGS (this is the whole confusion):
1. **The phone's midframe/chassis** - the body the internals mount to. This is what aftermarket listings mean by "with frame" / "without frame", and what the iFixit Answers thread means.
2. **The panel's own thin plastic carrier** (iFixit calls it the "safety plastic frame") - bonded to the rear of the OLED at the factory. This is what iFixit's guide note means.

iFixit guide 148750, verbatim: "This guide instructs you to replace only the screen, including its safety plastic frame. However, some replacement screens for this phone come without the pre-installed frame (a.k.a. chassis), which requires a different removal procedure. Make sure you have the correct part before starting this guide." Note it uses "frame" and "chassis" as synonyms in one sentence while "safety plastic frame" means the carrier - that ambiguity is iFixit's, not a reading error.

WHAT EACH VARIANT ACTUALLY COSTS IN LABOUR:
- **Screen WITH the phone's chassis (aftermarket "with frame")**: the phone's frame comes out and is replaced, so more of the phone comes apart, but you get a fresh, flat frame and you skip the glass-bonding step. iFixit community verdict, verbatim: "although the cost is a little higher, your framed screen is a less risky repair to do."
- **Bare screen, no chassis** (iFixit's own part, and its guide): cut the old panel off the phone's existing frame, clean the old adhesive, bond the new panel on. Fewer internals touched, but delicate glass work and it depends on the old frame being flat. Community, verbatim: "Be sure to clean off the old adhesive from the phone and be careful installing the precut adhesive; it's not very forgiving so you'll need to get it placed correctly the first time (I usually buy a spare just in case)."

THE RISK THAT COSTS MONEY (strongest argument for the framed version):
- iFixit, verbatim: "If the frame is deformed, it's important to replace it to make sure the new screen will mount correctly and won't suffer damage from uneven pressure."
- So with a no-frame screen you are stuck reusing the customer's frame. On a phone that just cracked its screen from a drop, a bent frame is likely - and a bent frame means the new panel can crack again from uneven pressure. With a framed screen you are immune to that. This is a re-crack AND a warranty exposure, not just a labour difference.

VERIFIED PART FACTS (iFixit product page, checked 2026-09-18):
- Google Pixel 6 Screen, $169.99, used A-stock, "Difficulty: Moderate", "Time Required: 20-30 minutes". Still "the integrated fingerprint reader is included in the assembly."
- "This replacement screen does not include the front camera plastic bracket. This will need to be transferred from your original screen panel."
- The front camera bracket and the protective liner over the front camera hole are both real gotchas. First-hand 2025 blog account by Thomas Claudius Huber: "I forgot it the first time and wondered afterwards why the front camera was always just black." That same account independently confirms the fingerprint claim from L2117 - the only thing broken after his replacement was the fingerprint sensor, fixed by Google's calibration tool.

DECIDING RULE AT THE COUNTER:
Do not accept the word "frame" from the customer or from a listing. Physically look at the back of the panel: is there a full chassis body with screw bosses for the internals (that is the "with frame" part), or a naked panel / thin carrier (that is the no-frame part)? If it is a naked panel, check the customer's own frame for bends before quoting - if the frame is bent, the no-frame part is the wrong part and the job should not proceed on it.

UNCHANGED BY THIS CHOICE: the fingerprint sensor rides on the panel either way and calibration via https://pixelrepair.withgoogle.com/udfps is still mandatory. Serial-number/sensor transfer risks from L2117 are unaffected.
