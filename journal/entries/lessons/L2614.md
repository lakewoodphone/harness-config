**L2614 · Apple Digital ID: do NOT assert the Wallet credential carries the State Department's signature — that is not established in the public record**

Follow-up to L2613, from a direct question about TSA's trust model.

What IS established:
- The ePassport chip holds the holder's data (incl. the face image) under a security object signed by the issuing authority's Document Signer certificate, chaining to the US Country Signing CA — the same Passive Authentication check border officers and doc-examiner software run. That signature cannot be minted by Apple or by the holder, and the chip read is unlocked by the MRZ (why setup scans the photo page first), so it also proves physical possession.
- Apple's WWDC25 wording is generic: 'the information in an mdoc is cryptographically signed by its issuer.' Its Verify with Wallet page claims the issuing authority confirms authenticity and ownership during proofing. Its developer docs publish IACA certificates for state mDLs and Japan My Number Cards only — no US passport/State Department certificate is listed.
- TSA CAT-2 units carry a library of 2,500+ ID types, read the credential, validate it themselves, and capture a live photo to compare against the credential photo (TSA press release 2025-04-28). TSA's ID FAQ (pub 2026-09-04) still requires a physical ID at the checkpoint.

What is NOT established: whether the credential presented carries State's signature over the passport data groups or Apple's own signature over data Apple verified at enrollment. No Apple whitepaper, TSA document or independent teardown found in this research settles it. Also unpublished: the match/liveness thresholds for enrollment, and any live revocation link to the State Department if a passport is cancelled or renewed.

Rule for future sessions: present the chip signature (State's keys) and TSA's own face match as the two hard checks, describe the enrollment face match as Apple's algorithm and the real seam in the model, and say plainly that the verifier's trust anchor for the passport credential is not publicly documented. Do not upgrade a marketing sentence into a technical fact.
