**L2575 · Home Depot's website cannot be read from the mesh — store-level aisle/bay data lives only in their app, so source shelf guidance by SPEC, never by SKU**

**Measured 2026-09-25 on secratary.** Trying to answer "what rope and where is it in the Home Depot at Lakewood" turned into
four dead routes, all 403 or JS-empty:
- `curl` with a current Chrome UA and Accept-Language → **403, 2,259 bytes** on both `/l/Lakewood/NJ/08701/6272` and
 `/s/braided%20polyester%20rope`.
- `web_fetch` on the same store URL → HTTP 403, HD's own error page.
- `r.jina.ai` text proxy round the same 403 → it returns the 403 too ("Target URL returned error 403: Forbidden").
- The harness's own `web_search` does not index HD US product pages usefully: `site:homedepot.com rope cleat` returned
 only homedepot.**ca** and appliance-parts subdomains. Third-party aggregators (manta, chamberofcommerce, nextdoor)
 are behind Cloudflare "Just a moment..." and return 403 as well.

**What DOES work from a shell, and what to use instead.** Store facts come from `storeopeninghours.com` and
`loc8nearme.com`, both fetchable and both agreeing: **The Home Depot #0902, 1900 Shorrock Street, Lakewood NJ 08701,
(732) 920-4200, Mon-Sat 6:00 am - 10:00 pm, Sun 7:00 am - 8:00 pm**; it sits at NJ-70 & Shorrock, 0.29 mi from
**Lowe's Brick (520 Route 70)**, which is the natural fallback. HD's *product* aisle/bay ("Aisle X, Bay Y") is served
only to the app / a store-selected session, so it is genuinely not reachable from here.

**The rule that follows:** when the owner is walking into a store, answer with the **store, the department signage
words, the size/material spec to match on the shelf label, what to reject, and the quantity** — not a SKU and not an
invented aisle number. A spec is also strictly more robust than a SKU, because stock varies store to store and week to
week. Say plainly that the bay is unsourced and give the app as the 20-second precision tool. Never fill the gap with
a plausible aisle number: that is exactly the confident-wrong-number failure this journal exists to prevent.

**Corollary on tool choice, measured the same day.** `ps_action` on the secretary MCP (`browser_use_run`,
`browser_session_list`, even `web_search`) returned `MCP error -32001: Request timed out` on every call for several
minutes, and three `subagent_local` research children were still running with no output after ~5 minutes. For a
question with a live human waiting at the door, the reliable path is the harness's own `web_fetch`/`web_search` in
parallel plus the engineering arithmetic — local fan-out is not the fast path, and it must be killed rather than left
running when the errand window closes.
