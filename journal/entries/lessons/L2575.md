**L2575 · A brief must require an EARLY push, not a push at the end - two mesh children died with nothing on the remote and the whole slice had to be redone**

Recorded 2026-09-23, from the first fleet this seat dispatched.

Three of the four children had not pushed when they died. One (`subagent-2`, shell) had pushed nothing
either as of the failure; the two survivors were still working. The briefs all said "push your branch" in
the git-rules section - and every brief implicitly meant *when you are done*.

THE RULE: **a brief must require an early push, named as a step.** "Add and push a placeholder commit for
your branch as soon as the worktree exists" costs nothing and converts a transport failure from "the slice
is lost" into "the slice resumes from what landed". The measured evidence is above: two children killed
mid-run with a correct, half-finished slice and no remote branch to recover it from. Recovery then means
redoing discovery, not just the edits.

This belongs in every future brief, and it belongs in the brief template so no session has to rediscover it.
