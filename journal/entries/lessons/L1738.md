**L1738 · A site unreachable from one line is not "your internet" - prove it with a same-hoster control, a multi-node check and a transit comparison**

**Symptom.** rattlebyte.com would not load on the home line; the owner's read was "is something wrong with my internet?"

**Measured 2026-09-16 on ZABZ-YOGA** (home, public IP 172.59.215.73; traceroute 192.168.12.1 -> 192.0.0.1 -> 10.177.x -> 4.14.216.97 (AWS) -> 64.125.x (Zayo) -> drops = T-Mobile Home Internet CGNAT):

- google 6/6 HTTP 200, cloudflare 6/6 200, **1984.is 8/8 200 - and 1984.is is the SAME HOSTER as the site that "fails"**
- rattlebyte.com (185.112.145.212): **0/10**, and *every* port (22, 25, 80, 443, 8080) times out; the whole 185.112.144.0/22 and 1984hosting.com (185.112.145.81) are dead too
- check-host.net **8/8 nodes** (CA, CH, IL, IR x2, RU, SE, SG) -> HTTP 302
- secratary (office) 302 in 0.47s; hetzner 302 in 0.45s; mac-mini 302 in 0.36s
- the two working paths transit the **same Zayo routers** (64.125.22.0 -> 64.125.31.111 -> 64.125.28.15) that the home trace dies on

**Conclusion.** The site is up worldwide. One prefix (185.112.144.0/22, 1984 Hosting shared range) is silently dropped for this line only. Not the ISP being down, not the router, not DNS, not the site.

**Rule.** Three cheap controls turn "your internet is broken" into a pinpointed fact in minutes: (1) a **same-hoster control** (1984.is vs rattlebyte.com - same company, different prefix), (2) a **multi-node third-party check** (check-host.net API), (3) **transit comparison** between a working vantage and the failing one. An empty result is not evidence of health - the direct path returning code=000 is the evidence, and 8/8 nodes succeeding is what proves it is not the site.
