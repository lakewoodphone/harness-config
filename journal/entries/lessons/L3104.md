**L3104 · PDFs and Israeli gov stats: web_fetch fails, curl+pypdf works; idf.il/media FOI responses are the primary source**

Text
Reusable finding from a research task (owner asked for real Israeli draft/enlistment
statistics). Two things a future session should not have to rediscover:

1. `web_fetch` cannot read PDFs — it returns "unsupported content type application/pdf".
 Working path on this box: `curl -sL -A "Mozilla/5.0" -o f.pdf <url>` then
 `python3 -c "from pypdf import PdfReader; ..."` (pypdf is installed for /usr/bin/python3).
 The `Mozilla/5.0` user agent matters; bare curl gets 403 from gov.il and idf.il.

2. For Israeli government statistics, the primary source is the FOI ("חוק חופש המידע")
 response PDFs, hosted under `idf.il/media/<id>/<name>.pdf`. They contain the raw tables
 that news articles only quote in part:
 - psychiatric "profile 21" exemption percentages by year and gender (2015-2024),
 plus exemption composition by cause (medical / Torato Umanuto / abroad / criminal);
 - haredi call-up numbers: orders sent, how many reported, arrest warrants issued,
 how many actually enlisted (Jul 2024-Aug 2025: 10,015 / 7,803 / 427);
 - draft-evader ("משתמט") counts by draft cycle and gender, indictments, convictions,
 prison sentences, arrests; desertion reports.
 They are found via web_search on the Hebrew terms, not by browsing idf.il. Note the IDF
 also *refuses* absolute manpower numbers under sec. 9(a2) (state security) and answers in
 percentages instead — so treat any precise-looking aggregate from a secondary source with
 suspicion.

Evidence: /tmp/idf/{idf-nefesh,idf-haredi,idf-haredi2,idf-mishtamtim,idf-bedouin}.pdf
(downloaded 2026-09-30; idf-nefesh dated 25 Aug 2026, idf-haredi 01 Sep 2026,
idf-mishtamtim 05 Jul 2026, idf-haredi2 12 Oct 2025).
