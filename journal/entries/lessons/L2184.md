**L2184 · On an owner health report, the missing measurement is the finding - write the UNKNOWN into the file**

RULE: On any owner health report, the finding to look for first is not the symptom list but **which measurement nobody has**. Record the absence explicitly, as `UNKNOWN`, in the file itself.

WHY: The owner describes how he felt. Nobody ever asks for the reading. Measured 2026-09-20: two febrile episodes 19 days apart, and the health record contains **not one number** - no temperature on either episode, no COVID test, no flu test, no strep culture, no bloodwork, ever. The 2026-09-03 workup named all four tests and there is no record that any was done. A "fever" that was never measured cannot be handed to a rav, a doctor, or a future session; it can only be re-argued.

HOW TO APPLY: When documenting an episode, ask for the number in the same breath as the narrative (temperature, and whether a test was taken), and write `UNKNOWN` next to what is missing rather than letting prose imply completeness. Prove it with the gap list, not with the prose: `docs/personal/health/README.md` "Known gaps" and §1.5.
