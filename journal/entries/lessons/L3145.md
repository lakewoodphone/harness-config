**L3145 · CORRECTION of W2622: Qwen is NOT orphaned, and three greps gave three different answers - stop grepping and probe at runtime**

2026-09-30 round 28. W2622 named QWEN_API_KEY as the first defensible retirement candidate on the strength of a declared-field test that found no field for it and one mention of qwen anywhere. THAT CLAIM IS WRONG and is corrected here. Re-measured with a proper recursive search over the whole application, excluding the narrower and broken forms used before: qwen is named in 7 files. The full picture, corrected: credentials with no exact declared field are HA_PTT_API_KEY (field exists but under an alias, named in 2 files), HA_SECURITY_API_KEY (alias, 11 files), OPENROUTER_API_KEY (16 files), QWEN_API_KEY (7 files), plus OPENAI_ORG_API_KEY and SPIDER_API_KEY with 0 files, SPIDER being expected because I added it today. So the only untouched credential is OPENAI_ORG_API_KEY, and even that cannot be called a candidate, because the alias mechanism shows that a missing exact field name proves nothing: HA_SECURITY_API_KEY has no field of that name either and is used in 11 files. THE REAL LESSON, and it took three failures to see it: I have now produced three different answers to the same money question from three greps - the uppercase env var name gave a false negative on a live paid provider (Tavily), grep -ric with -r printed per-file counts and produced a garbage table, and an exact-name field regex missed every alias-based declaration. Each looked authoritative and each was wrong. A question about MONEY cannot be answered by text search at all; the only trustworthy instrument is a runtime probe that asks the provider whether the credential works and asks the system whether anything can reach it. That is exactly what the capability registry is being built to do, and until it can probe, the correct answer to the owner is NOT a list of candidates but the statement that the question is not yet answerable. I nearly handed him a cancellation list built on a false negative twice in two rounds.
