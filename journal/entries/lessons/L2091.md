**L2091 · L2085 - broker-unreachable from the mesh provider is a transport symptom, not a broker fact - verify the broker on its own host before believing it**

The `subagent`/`subagent_remote` provider can fail with:

 remote-ssh: broker-unreachable: the placement broker could not be reached at
 secratary-ts:http://localhost:3091/place - no answer within 30000 ms

On 2026-09-20 I read that as "the mesh is down" and nearly fell back to local children. It was false.

Measured on the authority itself, minutes later:

 GET http://127.0.0.1:3091/ -> 200, service mesh-broker, schema 1
 POST http://127.0.0.1:3091/place -> 200 in 0.236 s, node=zabz-tech, eligible=5
 pid 3077721, mesh-broker.service, up 1 day 18 h, actively logging placements

The broker was healthy throughout. What actually failed was the SSH hop from the laptop to
secratary, because secratary was at `io full avg10 = 95.6%` and sshd was accepting nothing for ~30 s
at a time. The provider's error text named the component it could not reach (the broker) rather than
the thing that broke (the transport to the broker's host).

**Rule: when the mesh errors, test the broker from the broker's own host before concluding anything.**
 ssh secratary-ts "curl -s 127.0.0.1:3091/ && journalctl -u mesh-broker -n 15"
If the broker answers, the mesh is up and the failure is the SSH hop. Retrying is then correct;
falling back to `subagent_local` is not, and would have introduced a silent locality change.

Cost of not knowing: two dispatches lost, and I told the owner the mesh was down. The mesh was up.
