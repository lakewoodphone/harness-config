**L7551 · Read the record under the owner's own word; and never prescribe a bare blade**

**The task:** owner asked for beard shape advice from 10 photos; first shaving session in months. A prior plan was drafted, then corrected twice after the record was found.

**Correction 1 — the record already existed. Rule 7 was not applied to the right word.** I searched the journal, code and comms for `beard` and got clean negatives, and started designing. The word in his record is **shaving**. Searching `shav` on the authority found three durable docs at `/root/_hk168_wt/docs/personal/`: `shaving-research-2026-08-12.md` (22,501 B), `wedding-day-shaving-rescue-2026-08-16.md` (6,368 B), `walmart-wedding-day-shopping-guide-2026-08-16.md` (6,046 B), plus VS Code slots `d0916e11` (2026-06-18) and `2f179bef` (2026-08-12) via `authority.db`. A negative for the word I chose is not a negative for the area.

**Correction 2 — the drafted plan prescribed a `bare blade` on the neckline and cheek line. That is the one thing forbidden.** The owner is an Orthodox Jew; Vayikra 19:27 forbids cutting the beard with a blade at skin level. `shaving-research-2026-08-12.md` had already settled this, and the upper cheek is one of the five corners (Makkos 20a). The correct instrument is a **1-2 mm guard**, which leaves hair and never lets the blade reach the skin — DinOnline, *Can I trim my beard?*: "If the blade doesn't go up against the skin, but leaves some hair, such as with a trimmer, it would be permitted." Every number in a plan for this owner must carry a guard.

**Correction 3 — the maintenance arithmetic was backwards.** He asked for *lower* maintenance than shaving. A 5 mm beard reaches ~13 mm in two weeks and looks fuzzy, so it is trimmed often. A graded beard at 6 mm sides / 14 mm chin is cut every two weeks, 5-8 minutes a pass, no cleanup, nothing on the skin between.

**The plan, delivered:** graded short beard, sides and cheek mass 6 mm, chin and under-jaw 14 mm, mustache scissors-only (his joins at both corners — worth more than any line), cheek line natural with a 1-2 mm guard over the strays, neckline two fingers above the Adam's apple edged with the same guard. Sources cited on the page. Written to `/root/_hk168_wt/docs/personal/beard-plan-2026-10-16.html` (17,714 B).

**Transferable:** two rules fired at once and both were avoidable. (1) Before searching for the owner's history in an area, search the word *he* would use, not the word the task arrived in. (2) When the owner has a standing religious or medical constraint on file, read that file before designing anything that touches it — a guard-free recommendation is not a style error here, it is a forbidden instruction.
