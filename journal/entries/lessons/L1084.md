**L1084 · Chromium headless shell segfaults on eBay's anti-bot challenge page (not GPU, not memory)**

EVIDENCE (secratary, 2026-09-14 22:05, DEBUG=pw:browser* on
scripts/ebay/download-activity.ts):
 CONSOLE source: https://www.ebay.com/splashui/challenge?ap=1&appName=orch&ru=
 https%3A%2F%2Fwww.ebay.com%2Fmye%2Fmyebay%2Fpurchase
 Received signal 11 SEGV_ACCERR
Playwright surfaces it as "page.waitForTimeout: Page crashed" at whatever await
happens to be pending — the reported line is where Playwright noticed, not where
the browser died. Do not chase the line number; attach page.on('crash') and read
the browser stderr with DEBUG=pw:browser.

IT IS NOT THE GPU AND NOT RESOURCES. Two-arm control on a quiet machine
(22 GB RAM, 16 free, 12 G /dev/shm, zero other chromium processes):
 arm A plain headless -> page crash
 arm B --disable-gpu
 --disable-software-rasterizer -> page crash
Identical. So neither of the two stock answers applies.

CONSEQUENCE: chrome-headless-shell cannot be trusted against eBay. The working
configuration on this host is the full chromium build under Xvfb — that is what
carried the visible login through hCaptcha and OTP without a crash.

GENERAL RULE: when a headless scraper dies on an anti-bot interstitial while the
same page loads fine from an ad-hoc probe, suspect the interstitial, not the
page. Compare arms with page.on('crash') before changing timeouts or selectors.
