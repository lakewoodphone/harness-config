**L1963 · WhatsApp Status cannot be linked or subscribed to - only contacts see it**

Researched 2026-09-17 for the Weinberg-wife case.

FACT (WhatsApp's own privacy page, faq.whatsapp.com/1170535238421230): Status is shared with MUTUAL CONTACTS. Both sides must have each other saved. The status-privacy setting offers only My contacts / My contacts except / Only share with. A Status has no address, so no link, QR, deep link or API can add a viewer.

CONSEQUENCE FOR ANY FUTURE 'make a link so people see my status' ASK: the link half is real, the status half is not. What people actually mean is the click-to-chat link wa.me/<number>?text=<prefilled> - it opens WhatsApp with the message typed but unsent; the visitor types their name and sends; the business receives their NAME AND NUMBER as a normal message. That is the 'template with a few messages, they enter their name' flow exactly. It does not subscribe anyone to anything.

THE 2026 RELAXATION IS NOT THE RULE: WhatsApp is rolling out sharing with numbers you have saved and recently messaged/called, even if they have not saved you (WABetaInfo-derived reporting only - NOT on a WhatsApp help page, and WhatsApp has publicly denied any privacy change). Regional, gradual, on-device. Build for the mutual-contact rule; treat the relaxation as a bonus. Never promise a feature that is 'in rollout'.

WHEN THIS COMES UP, SAY: to guarantee a Status is seen, the customer must SAVE THE NUMBER - one tap via a contact.vcf link. If they want reach without saving anything, that is a WhatsApp CHANNEL (whatsapp.com/channel/<id>, unlimited followers, one tap, anonymous - admin cannot see or message followers). Do not reach for the WhatsApp Business API: Facebook business verification, a dedicated number, monthly cost, and it still does not touch Status.
