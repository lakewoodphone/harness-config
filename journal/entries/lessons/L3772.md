**L3772 · ps_action send_sms caps a body at 400 chars and silently appends a '- Daniel' signature; sms_log double-writes so a row count over-counts sends**

Measured 2026-10-13 sending one SMS to the owner via `ps_action("send_sms")`.

## The action rewrites the body you give it

- **It caps the body at 400 characters.** A refused attempt reports *"is N chars of gsm7 and the transport carries 400 for a body like this"*. The count it uses runs **about 10 characters higher** than a plain `String.Length` of the same text (382 locally measured, 408 reported), so leave real margin — aim for roughly 385 submitted characters. Note the encoding changes the message but **not** the cap: converting em dashes to hyphens moved it from UCS-2 to GSM-7 and the limit stayed 400.
- **It appends `"\n\n- Daniel"` to every body.** "Daniel" is the CEO agent's persona name, so this is the business line's standing sign-off rather than a fault — but it means **the delivered text is never byte-identical to the approved text**, and a body has to be budgeted for those 10 extra characters. Anyone who approves an exact body for a customer must know it will arrive signed.

## Counting what actually went out over-counts

`sms_log` writes **two rows per delivered message**, sharing one `twilio_sid`: `status='sent'`, `agent_id='orchestrator'`, `to_number` as dialled; plus `status='queued'`, `agent_id='sms_service.audit'`, `to_number` normalised to `+1…`. A refused attempt **also** writes a row, with `status='failed'` or `'suppressed'` and `twilio_sid IS NULL`, and one more again for the audit side. So a naive `count(*)` over recent rows reports duplicate sends that never happened.

**The only honest read of what was delivered is `SELECT twilio_sid, to_number, status FROM sms_log WHERE twilio_sid IS NOT NULL AND status='sent'`** — and reading `to_number` back is the recipient check, not the tool's `"Sent to …"` label.
