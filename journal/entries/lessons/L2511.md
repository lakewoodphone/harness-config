**L2511 · A message from our own address in a thread is not a reply until you read its labels: an unsent DRAFT read as 'we replied', and an unfit model draft parked on a live vendor thread**

**A message in a thread from our own address is not a reply until you read its labels — and a model's meta-commentary is now sitting in a live vendor thread where a human could send it.**

**MEASURED, 2026-09-24, Wilner SD card / 300DDR ticket #73890.** Searching the live mailbox for `73890` returned four hits. One of them was dated **six minutes after the lab's diagnosis** and carried our own address as the sender:

```
1a0b713340ec700a | Fri, 18 Sep 2026 17:31:24 -0700 | lakewoodphoneandtech@gmail.com | Re: Update from 300DDR: Ticket #73890
```

Read as a search result, that is "we replied to the lab". Read as a message, it is `LABELS: ['DRAFT']` — **never sent** — and its entire body is:

> "I don't have the actual content of the update message from Brian, so I can't draft an informed reply. Could you share the body of the email so I can respond appropriately?"

I was one step from reporting our own unsent draft as outbound activity on a case where "did we answer the lab" was the whole question. It was the **labels**, not the date or the sender, that told the truth.

**TWO RULES.**
- **Before asserting that we replied, opened, or acted, read the message's own labels/thread flags — not the search hit.** `DRAFT` means it never left. The corollary: "our address appears in the thread" is evidence of a draft, not of a send. The same discipline the SMS runbook needed (H2658: a `STORED` log line is not a stored row) applies to Gmail: the artifact is the witness, the summary line is not.
- **A draft is a loaded gun if it is unfit to send.** This one sits inside a customer-facing vendor thread with a one-click Send next to it, on a case whose owner-queue row is deferred to 2026-10-02. Any draft written by a model that is *about* the model's inability to write ("I don't have the content…", "Let me check the owner's email…") must be **deleted, not parked**: it cannot help, and it can be sent by mistake. This is the second instance of the family recorded in pain `P2252` (the pipeline writing raw model JSON into draft bodies); this one at least fails in English, which is worse, because it looks sendable.

**WHAT I DID.** No draft was sent, nothing was deleted from the mailbox (that is the owner's mail), and the case file now carries the verbatim thread, an explicit "do not send this draft" warning in its READ FIRST block, and the record that no reply has reached the lab since the 2026-09-19 diagnosis. Fix-forward belongs in `P2252`: the draft pipeline should refuse to store a body that is meta-commentary rather than a letter.
