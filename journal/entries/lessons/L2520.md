**L2520 · USB-A Lightning (C89) cannot do USB-PD, and the customer-SMS script blocks links until --force**

From the Goldberg A2602 case, 2026-09-24. Both facts are corrections, not discoveries.

## 1. I gave the owner wrong charging advice, and the correction is mechanical

I told him a **USB-A** to Lightning cable and a **USB-C** to Lightning cable had the same ceiling on
an iPad 9. That is wrong. Apple's **C89** chip is the USB-A-to-Lightning part and **cannot do
USB-PD**; **C94** (and **C91M** in Apple's own cable) are the USB-C-to-Lightning parts and do. So on
this iPad, USB-A is the *slower* path, and the cable that actually resolved the case was USB-C to
Lightning. The owner bought nothing on my word — but he was about to standardise on the wrong SKU.
**When a charger/cable question has a connector dimension, get the chip generation right before
answering.**

Second correction in the same exchange, mine: a listing that says "MFi Certified" is seller-typed
text and there is **no public MFi brand lookup** — Apple's authorized-manufacturer page is behind an
MFi login and an NDA. The only real checks a buyer has are the printed text and 12-digit serial on a
genuine Apple cable, a named brand sold by a real retailer, and a bench spot-test. Treating a
marketplace "MFi" badge as evidence is how a shop hands a customer the cable that fails.

## 2. The customer-SMS script has teeth, and `--force` is how you go past them

`scripts/send-lpt-sms.py` refuses to send a link: it prints
`Message contains a URL/link. Customer SMS must not include supplier or listing links.` plus
`Message includes restricted sourcing detail: 'amazon'`, and **exits 1 with nothing sent**. It
requires `--force` to send anyway. Worth knowing before drafting: a draft containing a supplier link
never reaches a preview-only stage as a sendable message, and `--force` is a deliberate, visible act.

The owner overrode the rule and sent the link, and that is his call — it is his margin. What is not
optional is the record: the case file says the link went out **on his explicit instruction after he
was shown that warning**, so a later session reading it cannot mistake it for an agent slipping past
a guardrail.
