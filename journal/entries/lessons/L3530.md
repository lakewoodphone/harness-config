**L3530 · brother_ql's did_print is false by construction on the QL-820NWB - the printer's own status page is the evidence**

`brother_ql` reports `did_print: false` on this printer even when the label physically comes out,
and that is by construction, not a fault. Measured 2026-10-08 on the Brother QL-820NWB
(4c:d5:77:5e:d8:35, 192.168.50.60): `send(..., blocking=True)` fills `did_print` from a status
read-back over port 9100, and this printer NEVER answers reads on 9100 - a raw `ESC i S` status
request times out, and so does an IPP Get-Printer-Attributes on 631, while its HTTP interface
answers instantly.

So `did_print: false` is information-free here. What is actually decisive, and what to use
instead: poll the printer's own web status page (`/general/status.html`, "Device Status") while
the job goes in. A real print shows READY -> PRINTING -> READY. Measured: 4 consecutive PRINTING
samples across 14:44:57.9-14:45:00.2, exactly when the job was sent.

Related trap in the same device: during printing the status page sometimes returns something the
parser cannot read, which shows up as a blank sample. A blank is NOT evidence of a fault and must
not be read as "not printing".
