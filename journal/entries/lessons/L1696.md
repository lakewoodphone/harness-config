**L1696 · Dialpad Personal Working Hours are readable by API — internal /api/user/{key}?extended=1, not the UI**

Dialpad's per-user **Personal Working Hours** ARE readable by API. The prior audit (`personal-secretary-mvp/docs/operations/walkin-hours-2026-09-15-change-plan.md` row A6) recorded them as UNREAD/UI-only. Wrong.

Where they live: internal `GET /api/user/{user_key}?extended=1` on `desktop-ts`, reusing `scripts/dialpad_ivr_admin.py::_load_captured_headers()` + `_request()`. The response carries `{monday..sunday}_hours` (each an array of HH:MM pairs — `["09:30","19:50"]`, or split shift `["00:00","01:00","17:45","23:55"]`), plus `hours_on`, `auto_dnd_working_hours_enabled`, `auto_dnd_calendar_enabled`, `ring_seconds`, `ring_settings`, `no_answer_action`.

The distinction that hid it: the **base** `GET /api/user/{numeric_id}` returns `hours_on` and `presence` but NOT the ranges. Only `?extended=1` **on the obfuscated key** (`agxzfnViZXItdm9pY2VyGAsSC1VzZXJQcm9maWxl...`) carries them. `/api/user/me` also carries the caller's own settings.

Semantics, from Dialpad's own help page `set-your-personal-working-hours`: PWH "impact routing rules for your individual line, assigned Departments and Mainline... your devices will not ring" outside them, and the call routes to "other available Operators". `auto_dnd_working_hours_enabled` makes Dialpad flip presence to `dnd_pwh` automatically — so a user showing `do_not_disturb: true` outside their hours is NOT a manual toggle and will clear on its own.

Operator list for a main line: public `GET /api/v2/offices/{id}/operators` → `{"users":[...]}` **in routing order**. With `operator_routing: "fixedorder"`, rank = list order.

Also: `help.dialpad.com` returns 403 to plain fetches but reads fine through the Jina reader; `mcp__firecrawl__*` was not connected in this session.
