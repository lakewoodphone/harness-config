**L2352 · An unrecorded recommendation is not a recommendation; and never scrape Amazon search pages**

Kind: a recommendation the owner acts on but that never reaches the journal does not exist. On 2026-09-22 he said "i want to buy a thermometer like you recommended" - and there was no record of it anywhere: journal search "thermometer" = no match; ps_memory_search = 0 rows; grep of ~/.dsh/sessions = none; VS Code workspaceStorage = none. Either a past session recommended it and died without writing, or he is remembering a conversation in another harness. Either way the cost is his: he was about to spend money on my advice and I could not reproduce the advice.

Rule: when I recommend a product, a purchase, a dosage, a vendor, a route - anything he might act on later - it goes into journal/decisions in the same turn, with price, source URL and the reason. A conversational recommendation is not a record. This is the same failure class as P4 (the improvement loop does not close) and the same cost as reporting a stale database as truth: the next session cannot verify what the last one said, so it either re-researches from zero or, worse, answers from vibes.

Also learned (cheap): do NOT scrape Amazon search-result pages - one firecrawl_scrape of an amazon.com/s?k= page returned 224 KB of junk (sprite URLs, ad redirect links, base64 extension errors) with zero usable prices, and spilled to a temp file. Amazon product detail pages by ASIN, or a search engine snippet carrying the price, are the efficient path.
