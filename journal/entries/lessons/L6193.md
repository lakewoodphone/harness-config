**L6193 · A managed web clip never reads the page's icon - the Icon key in the payload IS the icon**

**L — A MANAGED WEB CLIP NEVER READS THE PAGE'S ICON. The `Icon` key in the payload IS the icon.**

Apple's WebClip payload reference, `Icon` (data): *"The PNG icon to show on the Home Screen. If not set,
the system displays a white square. For best results, provide a square image that's no larger than 400 x
400 pixels and less than 1 MB when uncompressed."*

**What that cost.** The three LPT shop counter devices wore two `com.apple.webClip.managed` payloads with
no `Icon` for months, and the owner saw grey squares under "DSH" and "Lakewood Phone & Tech". Every
instinct pointed at the websites — missing `apple-touch-icon`, stale cache, dead host — and **all three
were wrong for a managed clip**:

* `lakewoodphoneandtech.com` has served a correct 180x180 `apple-touch-icon.png` and a
 `<link rel="apple-touch-icon">` the whole time, and its managed clip was grey anyway. That is the
 measurement that settles it, and it was available before any change was made.
* The DSH clip's host *was* genuinely dead (the frozen office box), and that is a **separate** defect with
 the same symptom. Two causes, one square.

**The rule.** For any home-screen icon complaint, ask first **who created the clip**: a hand-added Safari
clip reads `<link rel="apple-touch-icon">` from the page; an MDM-managed clip reads the profile payload's
`Icon` and nothing else. Fix the surface that owns it — and if the answer is "the profile", an
`apple-touch-icon` on the site will not move the icon by one pixel.

**The two fixes are not alternatives.** Both were needed here: the profile for the three staff devices, the
gate's PNG for the clips the owner (and anyone else) adds by hand.

Related: `P` (the shop profile named a machine that was frozen), `D` (the MDM repoint), `D` (the gate
icon), runbook §12, `docs/mesh/67` §9.1.
