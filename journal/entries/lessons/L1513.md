**L1513 · eBay blocks its SEARCH endpoint fleet-wide, but item pages still work — and the harvester fails silently**

CORRECTION AND EXTENSION of L1512, same day, measured properly.

WHAT IS ACTUALLY BLOCKED: eBay's search endpoint, https://www.ebay.com/sch/... . It returns HTTP 403 with a body of 'Error Page | eBay ... Something went wrong on our end'. This is NOT machine-specific and NOT a headless-browser problem:
- Mac mini (LAKEWOOECHSMINI), web_fetch -> 403.
- Mac mini, headless Chromium 139 with a desktop UA -> 403.
- Owner's desktop (ZABZ-TECH, a different network), PowerShell Invoke-WebRequest with a desktop UA -> 403.
So the whole fleet is blocked on /sch/. Do not spend time re-testing other hosts for it.

WHAT IS NOT BLOCKED — the useful part: individual item pages, https://www.ebay.com/itm/<id>, return HTTP 200 with full price, condition, seller, shipping and location. On the same session: /itm/298443946391 = 200 US 74.99 Swing Computers; /itm/236330723702 = 200 US 89.99 Synergy Industrial Corporation, free USPS. Some items 403 intermittently (rate limiting) — retry, and note that a retry can flip 403 -> 200.

HOW TO USE EBAY ANYWAY, in order:
1. Find items with a search API, not with /sch/. The company's ps_action web_search (Tavily) answered 'site:ebay.com Dell Latitude 5420 16GB 256GB i5-1145G7 refurbished' with five real /itm/ URLs and their condition lines. That is the working front door to eBay from this fleet.
2. Verify each candidate by loading its /itm/ page directly and reading price/condition/seller/shipping from the page — do not trust the search snippet.
3. WARNING: on an eBay item page the FIRST dollar figure in the body text is NOT reliably the item price, because a 'related items' carousel renders inside the same text. Two of my first-pass numbers were wrong (49.99 and 59.00 turned out to be other listings; the real prices were 89.99 and 74.99). Read [data-testid='x-price-primary'] on the page, not a regex over body text.

WHY THIS MATTERS MORE THAN THE BLOCK: ~/personal-secretary-mvp/scripts/ebay_laptop_search.py wraps /sch/ and, when eBay serves the 403 error page, parses zero rows and exits 0 with result_count 0 / candidate_count 0 and no warning. Run from the Mac mini AND from the owner's desktop, it reported a clean empty market on 2026-09-15 for both 'Dell Latitude 5420' under 15 and 'intel N100 laptop' under 15. There ARE such listings. A zero-result eBay harvest from this fleet is a ban, not a market fact, and the tool cannot tell you which.

EVIDENCE: 403/'Error Page | eBay' from three hosts on /sch/; 200 on /itm/ with US $189.99 (236330723702) and US $374.99 (298443946391); Tavily site:ebay.com search returning five item URLs, all 2026-09-15.
