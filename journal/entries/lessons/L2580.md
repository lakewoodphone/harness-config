**L2580 · Combined oral contraceptive mechanics: daily indefinitely with the break inside the pack — never 'a few weeks then stop', which is what a period-postponement course looks like**

Asked by the owner 2026-09-24 about his wife's new drospirenone/ethinyl estradiol 3 mg/0.02 mg (Glenmark generic of Yaz, 24/4): 'does she take it indefinitely or a few weeks at a time and then stop, are there different pills?'

The confusion is structural and will recur: Aygestin (norethindrone 10 mg/day) is a SHORT COURSE taken to postpone a period and then stopped; a combined oral contraceptive is taken EVERY DAY indefinitely with the hormone-free break built into the pack. Same drawer, opposite logic.

Mechanics, from the product label (Glenmark, quoted in docs/wedding/aygestin-after-effects.md): days 1-24 brown active tablets (drospirenone 3 mg + EE 0.02 mg), days 25-28 white INERT tablets - one tablet daily at the same time, next pack starts the day after the previous ends, no gap ever, tablet taken through the withdrawal bleed. Withdrawal bleed typically starts 2-3 days into the inert tablets and is lighter than a period. Protection continues through the inert tablets if the next pack starts on time. Qty 84 = 3 months.

No maximum duration - it is taken for as long as contraception is wanted. Designs: 21/7, 24/4 (hers), extended/continuous (84/7 or skipping the placebos; ACOG 2022 consensus: omission of the placebo week 'can be done over an indefinite number of consecutive cycles'; 79-88% amenorrhoea at 12 months), and separately progestin-only pills (continuous, no break).

Label warnings worth quoting: 'if withdrawal bleeding does not occur, consider the possibility of pregnancy' AND 'women may experience absence of withdrawal bleeding, even if they are not pregnant' - so a skipped bleed means test, not panic.

For a niddah-observant couple the key consequence: each withdrawal bleed is a niddah onset, and it is now PREDICTABLE (a large gain over the Aygestin era). Skipping the inert tablets to have fewer bleeds is medically recognised but is a doctor + rav decision, never improvised mid-pack.
