**L3353 · A lab's ship-back/destroy/donate question is often about OUR hardware; and the SMS cache cannot prove a negative**

## Two lessons from the 300DDR sweep, 2026-10-07/08

**1. A lab disposition question is often about OUR hardware, not the customer's device.**
300DDR offered *ship back / destroy for 25 / donate* on Fishman ticket 74114, whose drive was unrecoverable. Read
alone, that is a question about a dead Seagate worth nothing. **But the same box held our own Samsung 870 EVO
2TB** (order `1132651452`, 533.11) sent as the transfer drive, per 300DDR's own rule that the transfer drive ships
*"in the SAME box as its own bad drive"*. Choosing **donate** would have given them a 533-dollar SSD for free, and
**destroy** would have destroyed it. The owner caught this in one line: *"we need it shipped back because it
contains our expensive backup drive."*
**Rule: before answering any lab's ship-back/destroy/donate question, read the packing record for what else is in
that box.** The packing record is the authority, not the lab's framing of the question. Same class as "an
uncollected deposit is not a deposit" — the question is never only what it says it is.

**2. `dialpad_sms_cache` cannot prove a negative; two independent call sources can.**
I first concluded "nothing has reached the customer since 2026-09-17" from the SMS cache alone. That is not a valid
inference — the cache has whole days with zero rows (09-05, 09-12/13, 09-19, 09-26/27, 10-02/03/04) and is known to
be missing ~296 production messages historically. **What made it provable was:**
- `dialpad_call_full` (13,688 rows, `max(fetched_at)` minutes old) **plus** the `dialpad_call_link`
 `source='phone-and-tech-full'` backfill — two independent lists, both ending at the same last contact; **and**
- **demonstrating the harvest captures outbound calls** — a recent outbound with a 162 s call and a 2,723-char
 transcript. Without that demonstration the absence proves nothing.
**The generalisable form: to prove "we never contacted X", you must show (a) the channel is live, (b) it captures
messages in that direction, and (c) two independent sources agree. And the 2026-10-05 sweep's flat claim
"outbound call rows: zero" is FALSE — do not inherit it.**
