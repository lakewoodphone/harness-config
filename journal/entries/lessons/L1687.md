**L1687 · Do not run fan-out agent fleets on ZABZ-YOGA - it saturates the laptop he is sitting in front of**

## Rule
Do not run a fan-out agent fleet on ZABZ-YOGA. It is a 22-thread / 31.6 GB
laptop and it is the machine Eliyahu is *sitting in front of*. Fan out on
ZABZ-TECH (i9 / 64 GB) or `secratary`, and keep the laptop for the session he
is driving.

## Measured 2026-09-15 21:07–21:29 -0400 on ZABZ-YOGA
- Total CPU pinned at **74–77 %** of 22 threads over 5 samples / 10 s.
- Process count **508 → 572** in ~20 minutes, growing ~3/min. 70 `node`,
 106 `msedge`, 25 `chrome`, 29 `pwsh`, 103 `svchost`.
- A live DSH fleet under the `dsh web --port 3099` server (PID 21124, 2.2 GB,
 27 h old): **36 live children**, ~10 new node+PowerShell workers spawned
 per minute, running eslint (1 GB), vitest, pytest, unittest, `git grep`,
 `ssh` probes to ZABZ-TECH, `journal.py check` — across `phone-and-tech-full`,
 `personal-secretary-mvp`, `unified-search`, `_lpt-audit`.
- Memory: **committed 33.5–36.7 GB against 31.6 GB physical**; pagefile 2.3 GB
 in use; hard page faults 600–2400/s (normal < 100); `\PhysicalDisk(_Total)\%
 Disk Time` 126 % with queue 2.5 at the worst sample.
- Sustained per-process CPU (share of ONE core): MsMpEng (Defender) 29.5,
 `services` 22.6, **dwm 19.9**, `system` 18.8, WmiPrvSE 7.6, SearchIndexer 6.6,
 msedge 5.2.

## Why it *feels* slow even though the CPU graph is only ~60–70 %
`dwm` at ~20 % of a core is the desktop compositor; Defender rescans every file
the fleet touches; and 5 GB of commit is living in the pagefile. The user feels
window switching and typing lag, not a pegged CPU. Concurrency + paging, not
thermals.

## Corollary
Also seen: `Get-MpComputerStatus` **timed out after 120 s** and
`Get-NetTCPConnection` timed out, while the fleet ran — under this load the WMI
surface is unresponsive. A hung diagnostic is itself a load signal.
