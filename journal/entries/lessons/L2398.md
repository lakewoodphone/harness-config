**L2398 · US duty on a China-origin plastic phone case is 25.3% (FLIP 301 replaced Section 122 the same minute)**

**US duty on a China-origin plastic phone case is 25.3%, not 5.3% and not 12.8% (measured 2026-09-23).**

Classification is settled by a real CBP ruling: **N360978 (2026-05-12)** — "the tariff classification of a
smartphone case from China" → **HTS 3926.90.9989**, Col. 1 General **5.3%**, and it states smartphone cases
"lacking storage for articles beyond the device are excluded from heading 4202". Read in full via the CROSS
JSON API (`rulings.cbp.gov/api/ruling/N360978`) — the HTML page is JS-only and a scrape returns the title alone.

The stack:
- 5.3% MFN
- **+7.5%** Section 301 List 4A (9903.88.15)
- **+12.5%** Section 301 **"FLIP"** forced-labor action (9903.05.31), USTR **91 FR 47317** (2026-07-28),
 CBP **CSMS #69326983**, effective **12:01 a.m. ET 2026-07-24** = **25.3%**
- Band 17.8%–25.3%; the gap is whether 3926.90.9989 is literally enumerated in U.S. note 20(r)/20(s).

Two layers that are **dead and must not appear in a landed cost**:
- **IEEPA tariffs** — struck down by SCOTUS in *Learning Resources, Inc. v. Trump* **2026-02-20**; CIT ordered
 refunds 2026-03-04.
- **Section 122 10% global surcharge** — expired by its own 150-day cap on **2026-07-24**, not extended.

**The trap:** the FLIP 301 12.5% took effect the *same minute* the Section 122 surcharge lapsed. So anyone who
"knows" the IEEPA tariffs were struck down and drops the extra layers lands on 12.8% and understates duty by
**half**. Conversely the commercial tariff sites (wove, tariffsapi) were RIGHT at 25.3% — they were not stale.
Confidence: very high (USITC + CSMS + Federal Register), from mesh node ZABZ-TECH.

**Corollary for D163 arithmetic:** at small order sizes the fixed per-entry fees dominate the duty. MPF
minimum **$33.58** (FY2026, eff. 2025-10-01) + broker entry ~$150 + ISF $50 + single-entry bond $75 ≈ **$306
of fixed cost per formal entry** — ~31% of the landed cost of a 250-unit case order and $3.06/unit at 100.
The carrier-cleared informal route avoids most of it. Always price BOTH the goods and the entry path.
