**L2002 · A thing the owner cares about, with no ledger and no milestone task, will be forgotten — the car proves it**

**L · A thing the owner cares about, with no ledger and no milestone task, WILL be forgotten — and he will
find out months later and be angry, twice: once at the thing and once at me.**

Measured on the 2022 Hyundai Elantra, 2026-09-18. The owner asked for car-health tracking on 2026-06-14.
Four things were true at once, and none of them was a mistake anyone made on purpose:

1. The company's own initiative engine **commissioned the work** — task **#4251**, 2026-06-15, "Research
 Hyundai Elantra Limited (VIN 5NPLP4AGXNH073821) common issues & maintenance schedule."
2. A stale-task sweep **auto-closed it 13 days later, marked `done`, never executed**.
3. The chat that asked for it ended with the owner saying *"for now update the docs … and I'll pick it up
 another time."* Nothing was built that could notice the picking-up never happened.
4. The car therefore had **no ledger, no odometer record between purchase (2026-05-04, ~55,593 mi) and
 2026-09-18 (60,326 mi), no service history, no recall check, and no monitoring.** And an overdue
 transmission-fluid service nobody had ever mentioned to him.

**The same shape produced the insurance failure** three weeks earlier: binding a policy had no compliance
checklist, so the mandatory NJ pre-insurance inspection was never scheduled, collision and comprehensive
were suspended mid-term, and it went unnoticed for two months.

**The rule.** When the owner asks for something to be *watched over time*, the deliverable is not an
answer and not a document. It is: (a) a **ledger file in a git repo that gets pushed**, (b) **milestone
rows in the task table keyed to a number or a date**, and (c) a **stated source of truth for the primary
measurement**. A chat reply is not any of those. And a stale-sweep that closes unexecuted work as "done"
is worse than no task at all, because it makes the gap invisible — the fix is that a task closed as stale
must say *unexecuted*, not *done*.

**Evidence:** company task #4251; chat session `f32e90b3-7485-4a8a-8500-7fcfb6ab081f` ("Car data and
tracking device", 2026-06-14, where the agent answered about comma.ai/OpenPilot three times instead);
`docs/personal-vehicle/CASE-car-2022-elantra.md`; journal P215 for the insurance twin.
