**L3493 · lpt-label clips long lines silently - render the PNG and look at it before printing**

The Brother QL-820NWB renderer behind `lpt-label` (lpt-ops, runs on secratary) CLIPS a line wider than the 29 mm tape instead of wrapping it, and says nothing about it. Measured 2026-10-11 on the Weinreb bench label: lines of about 42 characters came back as "Opening it may worsen or fully break the sc" and "DATA JOB ONLY - his files and pictures onto", while a 31-character line came out whole. Keep label lines under roughly 40 characters.

Procedure that prevents it: `lpt-label print - --dry-run --out /tmp/x.png`, copy the PNG back with scp, and READ THE IMAGE before printing. A dry run whose JSON looks perfect can still be a truncated label. Two facts about this printer that look like faults and are not: `did_print` is always false (it never answers reads on port 9100), and a successful print leaves the state at READY, so the tape in your hand is the only witness. `lpt-label find` before and after is the cheap check that the roll is loaded and the device was reachable.
