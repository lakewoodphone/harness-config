**L1619 · Production SMS runs on Dialpad and works - but the source tree would break it. Grep cannot tell you what prod runs**

Claim checked: "outbound SMS on the operational path always throws, so customers are not being texted."

CODE says yes-ish: backend/src/lib/twilio.ts is a deliberate NoOp stub ("Twilio has been completely removed
as part of Dialpad Phase 1", :2-12) whose messages.create throws (:42-56). smsService.sendSMS reaches it at
sms.service.ts:364 -- but the catch at :433-473 converts the failure into {success:false} and writes a FAILED
row. It fails SOFTLY; it never throws. Every ~15 call site does.

PRODUCTION says no. lpt_prod.communications, 112,351 rows, newest 2026-09-15 21:29Z (live). Last 14 days:
218 outbound DELIVERED + 6 SENT + 16 FAILED = 240 attempts, 224 successful (93.3%). All 503 SIDs begin "di"
-- Dialpad's prefix, not Twilio's "SM". Sender +17326552355. SMS runs on Dialpad and works.

WHAT IT ACTUALLY MEANS, and this is the valuable part: the source tree is ahead of production, and the
migration is HALF DONE. Twilio was removed from source; ~15 call sites still route through smsService. If
the owed production rebuild happens from current source, every one of those call sites starts failing
silently -- repair-status texts, payment-link texts, MFA codes, AI-workspace customer SMS -- with nothing
but a FAILED row to show for it. THE REBUILD IS BLOCKED ON THIS, not merely deferred.

RULE: a grep of call sites cannot tell you what production is running. Read the artefact.
RULE: "the code path throws" and "the business is broken" are different claims needing different evidence.
Second time this series a broken-business reading needed prod before belief; the first was a stale DB read
that invented a crisis. Both directions of error are real.
