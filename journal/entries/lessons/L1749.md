**L1749 · LPT customer texts: no opening name, no signature, short lines - the voice lives in dialpad_ui_message_body_row, not in my imagination**

Owner correction 2026-09-16, after I drafted a customer reply he called too long and AI-like and reminded me we do not open with the customer's first name.

THE RULE for any LPT customer-facing text:
- No opening name. Not 'Hi Yocheved'. 'Hi,' at most, or nothing.
- No signature. No '-- Lakewood Phone & Tech'.
- No numbered ask-lists, no em dashes, no corporate softeners ('I'd rather be honest with you than hold out hope I can't deliver' is exactly what he means by AI-like).
- Short lines. Bad news and money stated directly, then one concrete next step.
- 'we' not 'I' for the shop. Occasional yeshivish shorthand is natural ('bezh').

WHERE TO GET THE VOICE instead of inventing it: the secretary DB on the authority, table dialpad_ui_message_body_row (direction='outbound', filter by length) - real shop replies. Also sms_log. Two real examples to imitate:
- 'Hi, it would probably be between \-\. You can drop it off and we can give you a quote before doing the work'
- 'It definitely does not look so recoverable. If you want, you can bring it in and we can double doublecheck but it will be a minimum of \ if we succeed'
- Standing address line, verbatim: 'Our address is 1001 West Kennedy Boulevard, Lakewood, NJ 08701. Please come down the left side of the house.'

WHY IT MATTERS: he reads these before they go out. A draft in the wrong voice costs him a correction round every time, which is exactly the attention tax the audit says to remove.
