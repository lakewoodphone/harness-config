**L3048 · Three times in one evening I presented an unverified absences as a finding**

Measured 2026-09-28, Toms River kite run, Chol Hamoed. Three separate claims I made that were wrong in the same direction - **I asserted an absence without doing the search that would have falsified it.**

1. **'There is no hashgacha-verified ice cream in Toms River proper.'** I ran three searches, found one unverified gelato reference in a newspaper story, and reported an absence. A dedicated pass found **two KCL-certified places**, one of them 11 minutes from the store - **closer than the Lakewood fallback I had led with.** The searches I ran were for the wrong thing: I searched 'ice cream Toms River' and read a feature article, rather than querying the **kashrus authorities by category** (Mekomos, gokosher, Kosher Connect, KCL).

2. **'No kite shop in the corridor'** - this one turned out to be TRUE, but I had not established it; I had inferred it from a stale dealer locator. The owner had already suspected it. Cobra Kites is dead and its own manufacturer's locator still lists it.

3. **'The shore will be windier'** (implicit in the drive-east instinct) was **backwards** - the offshore buoy read 3 mph against 7-8 mph at the mainland airports, because a NW wind suppresses the sea breeze. Geography plus wind direction, not intuition.

**THE RULE: an absence is a finding that requires the same evidence as a presence.** Before writing 'there is no X', ask what the authoritative list *for X* is and query it by category. For kashrus the authorities are Mekomos, gokosher, Kosher Connect, KCL, OU, Star-K - and the tell was already in the record: an earlier lesson says a venue's own page is not evidence of hashgacha, and this is its mirror image - **proximity to kosher patrons is not evidence of hashgacha either.** 'Sprinkles of Lakewood' appears on Uber Eats inside the kosher corridor and is on no authority's list. That is the trap.

Also recorded, because it is the same failure wearing a different coat: **Baskin-Robbins' Route 37 listing publishes a 05:00 opening.** I nearly carried it. An implausible value is a data error, not a fact - check it against the shape of the real world before repeating it.
