**L12242 · A roadmap box's text is its supply key: rewriting a box refiles it as new work unless the ledger title moves in the same commit**

A ROADMAP BOX'S TEXT IS ITS SUPPLY KEY. EDIT THE TEXT AND THE LOOP REFILES THE WORK.

`roadmap-supply.py` matches each unchecked box to a ledger item by the leading text of the box line (the generator's `norm_key`, first 60 characters). A commit that rewrites a box's wording therefore looks, to the supply, exactly like a brand-new box: `new=1`, `filed_this_run=1`, and a duplicate item for work that already has one. The old row is not deleted - it simply stops matching and becomes orphaned.

MEASURED 2026-10-21, TWICE IN ONE SESSION
1. Seven boxes rewritten against the owner decisions (G1-1, G1-2, G1-4, F1-19, F1-3, F1-16, U5-8) -> seven ledger titles had to be renamed in the same change (920, 921, 923, 909, 918, 901, 985).
2. `G1-3` rewritten from "five towns" to "Lakewood only" (D28) -> item 947 stopped matching and the dry-run went to `new=1` after the merge. Fixed by renaming 947.

THE RULE, for every future roadmap edit
- Any commit that changes a box's TEXT or TITLE renames the matching ledger item in the SAME commit, and says so in the message.
- Prove it after every roadmap merge: `roadmap-supply.py --project <p> --dry-run` must report `new=0 unmapped_boxes=0 stale_mapping_rows=0`. `new>0` means the roadmap and the ledger have diverged, not that there is new work.
- Retiring a box entirely is different and legitimate: the item is dropped with the reason (835, 913 tonight).

WHY IT MATTERS BEYOND TIDINESS: the loop's whole supply chain is "the document is the work". A box that silently re-files itself every time someone improves its wording turns a rewrite into duplicate shifts, and duplicates are the exact waste the owner named: "doing the same work over and over".
