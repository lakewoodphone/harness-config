**L3894 · Two children orphaned on a node the broker placed at 85% commit pressure - harvest, do not re-dispatch**

2026-10-20, LPT money fleet. Six children dispatched; two (counter-payment fix, money-watch) were placed on ZABZ-YOGA by the broker with the node's own pressure reading HIGH: 55,522 MiB of 65,173 committed (85.2%), commit free 13,747 MiB. The broker excluded the local node and chose the laptop anyway. Both children were killed at the 1,800 s bound: 'the child started and was NOT re-dispatched; its work may exist on the node and only its report was lost'.

The work WAS there. The counter-payment child had left 12 changed files and a 292-line test file uncommitted in C:\Users\ezabz\Code\pt-payments-wt on ZABZ-YOGA. Harvesting cost three ssh calls: stage explicit paths, commit (d620f5ee0), then run the new test file - 6/6 passed, reproduced by me on that node. The money-watch child had left nothing (it spent its 30 minutes re-deriving matcher semantics after finding its worktree absent on that host).

Rules learned:
1. Before dispatching a child that edits code, check the node's commit headroom against its own share; the broker's placement does not do this for you. A laptop at 85% will take the child and kill it at the ceiling.
2. A child that edits a worktree on a remote node leaves uncommitted work there. On time-out the diagnostic says ORPHANED and NOT re-dispatched - re-dispatching duplicates side effects; harvest instead: inspect the worktree on the named node, stage explicit paths, commit, then run the tests yourself.
3. The brief should name the node and the worktree path it expects to exist. The money-watch child's first 20 minutes died on 'C:\Users\ezabz\Code\lpt-ops-money does not exist on zabz-yoga' - the worktree existed only on ZABZ-TECH.
Useful salvage from the killed child's reasoning, independently true: duplicates reproduce exactly (53 pairs / 6,520.15) when a charge is matched by charge id OR by its payment intent, and no_db_row's 177 succeeded charges exclude the 3 ambiguous ones.
