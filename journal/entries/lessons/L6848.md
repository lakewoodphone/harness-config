**L6848 · I published a question document that put five answers in his mouth he never gave - written from titles, not from the rows**

Measured 2026-10-16 against the owner_decision_queue itself. The question document I published and told him was ready to answer attributed answers to him that do not exist: id 169 Felician presented as 'I pay it myself' when the row's own record says that attribution was WRONG and was corrected and reopened on 2026-09-30 with the question still standing; id 110 Elantra presented as a decision when his words were a deferral ('remind me in 2 weeks') that came due on 9 October; id 463 Audible and id 519 Telnyx presented with quotes he never gave, both rows carrying NO answer at all; id 531 DST/800 asked back to him when the row is resolved because our own records answered it (the Zelle note names customer Tzipporah Tchiprout); and id 261 Deep Infra listed as open when he decided it the same day. The cause is specific and repeatable: I wrote the section from the queue rows' TITLES plus my own recollection of the session, and never read each row's answer, status, resolved_at and defer_note fields - so I could not tell an answer from a deferral from a correction from nothing. The document has been corrected in place, section 5 now quotes the queue's own current words with each row's real status and the date any deferral came due, the two resolved rows are removed, and the header now says plainly that every 'Say yes to' line is MY proposed answer and never his. The rule this teaches: when a document makes a claim ABOUT a person, read that person's own record field by field - a title is a summary written by someone else, and 'the row exists' is not 'the row says what I remember'.
