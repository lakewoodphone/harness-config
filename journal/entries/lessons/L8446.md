**L8446 · A bill that arrives as a family forward exists in no archive — archive the image, then register it**

Measured 2026-10-18 on the household's utility bills. The owner's wife forwards the landlord's
"Fwd: Electric for this month is $X" texts to his phone; he is the payer and had forgotten two
months of them. Before writing anything I looked for the bills where the estate normally keeps
things, and every single place was empty:

- business comms index (`ps_comms_search`) — covers the shop's Dialpad line, not his personal
 thread; the landlord never texted the business number;
- Yocheved's Gmail, connected since 2026-09 (`/gmail/search?account=chevedy@gmail.com`):
 `"Electric for this month"` 0 hits, `Eversource` 0 hits — the landlord texts, he does not email;
- both books: the four amounts and the total looked up in cents across every entity returned only
 a Topgolf charge that happened to share digits with one bill.

So the screenshot in the chat was the **only** copy in existence, and a phone thread is not
searchable by anything I run. The transferable facts:

1. **The image is the evidence, and it is perishable.** Copy it out of the attachment store into a
 durable folder the same turn, hash it, and cite the sha256 in the record. A record that says
 "he texted me 138.42" with the image gone is a claim, not evidence.
2. **A family forward is a first-class source.** It has no sender header, no invoice PDF and no
 email to reply to, so the *message timestamp printed by the phone* is the only date anchor —
 and the weekday it prints is a free cross-check that the year is right (2026-09-22 is a
 Tuesday; had it not been, the year would have been wrong).
3. **File-plus-registry, or it is not documented.** A markdown ledger alone is invisible to the
 attention sweep. The rows went into `doc_registry` (one per bill, with `value_usd` and
 `key_date`) and one `obligation_registry` row so it raises like every other debt. The amount
 lives in the ledger only — the obligation points at it rather than restating a number that a
 part-payment would make stale.
4. **`harness-config` is published, and that includes the journal.** Measured: an unauthenticated
 `api.github.com/repos/lakewoodphone/harness-config` returns `private: false`, and
 `contents/journal/entries/handoff?ref=master` lists 288 files — the entries themselves, not just
 the tooling, are on a public remote, and nothing in the gitignore stops it. So the ledger lives
 only on the authority (`/root/accounting-data/household/`) and the repo gets a pointer with no
 figures (`docs/household/README.md`). It also means a handoff will be published: name the path,
 keep the street address out. The larger pre-existing exposure (address and rent in H3372) is an
 owner decision, not something a later session should quietly rewrite.
