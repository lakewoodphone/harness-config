2026-09-17, the Bachrach Family Link case. I had already committed and pushed the case file and the
procedure when Brief 3 came back. It contradicted TWO things I had committed:

1. I had called **Livigent** an alternative kosher Android filter vendor. Wrong. GenTech's own FAQ,
 which I then verified at source: *"What is Livigent? Livigent is the name of the filtering software
 used by GenTech."* The flip-phone fleet was already running GenTech's engine; there is no separate
 Livigent consumer product. My table had named a non-option as the tamper-resistant option.
2. I had written **"Family Link cannot block the Google app, full stop."** Too strong. Google documents
 the system-app APP-LIMIT exclusion; a parent documents an in-app path (Google -> App details ->
 Search -> OFF) that may sit outside it. That one is a community report, so it went in as a LEAD and
 as the first step to try - not as a fact.

What I did NOT do is take the agent's word. I re-fetched GenTech's Apps-Only wiki and Help Center myself
(maxAge 0) and confirmed verbatim: *"Verify that Google search is on block under the Filtering tab"*,
browsers into Hide/Uninstall, *"For Samsung devices the filter can be installed remotely by our
technicians over the phone"*, *"the filter cannot be uninstalled by the user"*. THEN I checked the
agent's most consequential internal claim in our own repo - and it was right, and it mattered more than
the agent said: `Kyocera E4610 Device Lockdown Research.txt:301-312` names
`com.google.android.googlequicksearchbox` as a hide target and :390 establishes that
`DevicePolicyManager.setApplicationHidden` persists through Safe Mode where `pm disable` does not.
That made my own recommended procedure the WEAKEST of three options, and the real answer one we had
already written down a year earlier for a different phone.

THREE RULES THIS PRODUCES
- A late research return is not a footnote; it is a **correction to a committed record**, and the wrong
 version must be visibly superseded in the same repo, not quietly overwritten. Keep the corrected
 claim visible and say what it supersedes.
- Delegated findings are hypotheses. **Verify at source the one claim that would change your
 recommendation**, and verify the internal one by reading the file at the cited line.
- "We have never solved this" is worth checking against your OWN old work in a DIFFERENT domain. The
 Kyocera lockdown answered a Samsung Galaxy question because the platform mechanism (Device Owner,
 Safe-Mode-persistent app hiding) is the same and only the package list differs.
