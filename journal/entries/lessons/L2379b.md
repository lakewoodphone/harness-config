**L2379b · Mesh children must open their report with MESH-HOST or the entire run is discarded (H7)**

**The rule:** before dispatching ANY child over the mesh (`subagent` / `subagent_remote`), read `harness-config/docs/agent-brief-template-mesh.md` and make the child's FINAL message open with the literal first line `MESH-HOST: <hostname>`. Nothing may precede it.

**Evidence (measured 2026-09-22, ZABZ-YOGA):** I dispatched two research children over the mesh with briefs copied from the LOCAL worktree template. One completed its work and was discarded: status failed, "the child did not begin its report with MESH-HOST: <hostname> - its location is unproven, so the run is not reported as complete", and `job_output` returned `(no new output)` - the whole run unrecoverable. `send_message` could not correct the sibling mid-flight ("subagent is unavailable").

This is already documented as **H7** in `docs/agent-brief-template-mesh.md` (measured 2026-09-20, when a workstream was rejected the same way and its output was DISCARDED, the same brief having gone to four more children). The fix existed; I did not read it.

**Two further mesh facts measured in the same session, both of which change how a build fleet is briefed:**
1. Children land on **ZABZ-TECH**, not the dispatching machine. Worktrees created on ZABZ-YOGA are invisible to them.
2. From ZABZ-TECH, `ssh` to `yoga`, `zabz-yoga`, `laptop`, `desktop`, `secratary` ALL hung past 25s with zero output (an 8s ConnectTimeout never returned). A ZABZ-TECH child cannot reach back to the dispatcher or the other nodes; it must work locally and be told not to attempt ssh.
