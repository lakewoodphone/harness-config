**L3710 · A case file can stop at the price text while production carries a second price - check comms newer than the file's updated date**

MEASURED 2026-10-12 on the Greenfeld pair (lpt-hub docs/customer-operations/cases/dell-latitude-3301-hgbs463-greenfeld-2026-09-08.md and surface-laptop-4-1950-greenfeld-2026-09-08.md). Both case files ended at the moment the two prices were texted (2026-10-07 21:49Z) and read "awaiting acceptance". Production held three later facts neither file carried: the customer's three inbound questions within a minute (notes 2802/2804/2805/2806 on order 2883), the owner's 341 s call the next day, and a SECOND price given on that call - data-only off the Dell at 75 with his own drive / 85 with ours, needing no board repair. So a case file can be internally consistent, freshly committed, and still be missing a quoted price.

RULE: before reading a case's state, compare the case file's front-matter `updated:` against the newest comms/notes on the linked production order, and read anything later. `lpt ticket <key>` returns notes and deduped comms in one call, which is the cheap way to see the gap. The front-matter date is a claim about the FILE, not about the case.
