**L · The owner's dictation decides ONE way: Superwhisper free with local Whisper Large v3 Turbo — and the September "no independent test exists" claim is now wrong**

HE CONFIRMED THE DIAGNOSIS, 2026-10-13, verbatim: **"Apple dictation is just plain bad."** Nothing is
broken — no stuck mic, no switched-off setting. The engine *is* the problem, and on an iPhone 15 Pro it will
never be fixed by Apple. **Do not send him back to settings and do not mention an iOS update again.**

RE-VERIFIED 2026-10-13 against the vendor's own pages, because September's instructions could have gone stale:

- Superwhisper is at **2.25.2** (2026-10-09), 4.4 stars / 842 ratings, needs **iOS 26.0+**. Four releases
 since September; the mechanics did not change.
- **The best local model is now free.** The vendor made **all Whisper models free for everyone on
 2026-08-27**, including **Whisper Large v3 Turbo (1.6 GB)**. Free tier = *unlimited* dictation with local
 Whisper, custom vocabulary, text replacements, two modes, and the vendor's own words: **"Nothing you
 dictate leaves your device."** Only Cohere Transcribe / Parakeet and the AI cleanup modes are Pro.
- **Allow Full Access is still mandatory**, the app still hands off to itself to open the microphone (Apple
 forbids keyboard extensions the mic; the vendor names Apple's bug FB22247647), and **you still swipe back
 yourself, every time**. Unchanged on iOS 27.0.1.
- Prices unchanged: 8.49 a month, 84.99 a year, 249.99 lifetime.
- **No iOS 27 or 27.0.1 note exists** in Superwhisper's docs or changelog — no known breakage, no claimed fix.

**THE DECISION (so nobody re-derives it): free tier, mode "Voice to Text", model "Whisper Large v3 Turbo"
local, keyboard with Allow Full Access, plus the Action Button bound to "Toggle Superwhisper Dictation" for
hold-talk-release-paste. Pay 84.99 a year ONLY if the free local model disappoints after real use — and even
then pin the mode to a local Whisper model so customer speech stays on the phone. Do not buy Pro for cloud
models: his calls are already transcribed in the cloud by Dialpad, so it is not a new exposure, but there is
nothing to gain that justifies moving customer speech off-device for free.**

CORRECTION TO MY OWN SEPTEMBER CLAIM: I told him "no independent head-to-head accuracy test of these apps
exists at all". **That is now false.** Wirecutter's "The Best Dictation Software" (updated 2026-06-22; 50
hours, 19 tools, Apple's included) rates **Wispr Flow 98 %, Apple's dictation 94 % on both iOS and macOS**,
and calls **Willow the most accurate app tested**. Note the useful signal inside that number: a 94 % Apple
score does **not** reproduce his complaint, because his complaint is the disappearing-words defect rather
than raw word error — **so do not use accuracy percentages to argue with his lived experience.**

ESCALATION, recorded as a decision not a question: the only channel where friction can be removed entirely is
his chat with me — a record-and-transcribe button in the phone GUI using the company's existing Whisper
service (`personal-secretary-mvp/app/services/transcription.py`). **Not built**: it covers one channel,
Superwhisper covers every app. Build it only if Superwhisper is rejected.
