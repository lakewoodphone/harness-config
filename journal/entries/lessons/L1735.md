**L1735 · A flat window shorter than the failure interval is not evidence of a fix - state the bar, mark the claim provisional, and make the next session read the watcher**

**L · 2026-09-16 · A flat window shorter than the failure interval is not evidence of a fix. State the interval distribution, not the last run, and mark the claim provisional with its falsification test and an armed watcher that the next session actually reads.**

On 2026-09-15 I deployed two fixes for the `secretary-api` OOM and wrote, at 15 minutes of flat memory, that the second one
"does hold, so far". By the next afternoon there had been **23 more OOM kills**, plus a SIGBUS. The subagent and I had both
noted the correct bar in the same breath — pre-fix survival times were 62, 54, 15 and 6.5 minutes, so ≥60 minutes was the
real test — and the kills resumed inside my own soak window anyway (kernel log, 2026-09-16, hours 00–02).

The transferable rules:

- **Compute the bar from the failure distribution before you judge a fix.** Here the observed intervals spanned 6.5 → 62
 minutes; a 15-minute flat run is *less* than the median pre-fix survival, so it discriminates nothing. Write the bar down
 with the number (`≥60 min`), not as "so far so good".
- **A provisional claim must carry its own falsification test and a watcher that outlives the session.** I armed
 `~/phone/oom-evidence/soak.tsv` (60 s rows, 3 h) and a burst catcher, which was right; what I did not do is make the *next*
 session read them. 23 kills then happened with nobody tracking them. If a claim is provisional, the handoff entry must say
 in `NEXT` what to read and what result would overturn it.
- **"Not yet disproven" is not "fixed".** Words matter in this record: write *provisional — holding for 15 min, bar is 60*,
 never *holds*.
- **A fix that changes the shape of a failure can be real without being sufficient.** Both 09-15 fixes did something
 measurable (page cache 64 MB → 8 MB per connection; DB handles 60 → 13 at the same age), and the failure interval moved
 from 6.5 minutes to 30–60 minutes. That is progress worth recording as progress — the mistake was calling it a fix, which
 is what stopped the hunt. Kill a good lead's false closure, keep its real measurement.
