**L3514 · A re-exported iPhone photo has a different sha256 and identical pixels - compare pixels before calling it new evidence**

MEASURED 2026-10-11, LPT Pearl case (HP EliteBook 840 G5, 848-226-5112).

Three .HEIC photographs the owner sent as `Photos-1-001 (1).zip` (IMG_1116-1118)
had sha256 values different from the three label shots already filed on the case
(positions 2-4). That is NOT evidence they are new captures: an iPhone Photos
re-export re-encodes the same frame, so the bytes differ while the picture does not.

The check that settles it is 4 lines of Pillow - convert both to greyscale, resize
both to 378x504, take ImageChops.difference, and report the mean and the fraction of
pixels above a threshold. Result here: mean absolute difference 0.39-0.42 of 255 and
0.00% of pixels differing by more than 16 => the same three photographs.

So: before filing an incoming photograph as "new evidence", or telling the owner it
adds something, compare pixels, not hashes. sha256 inequality is the DEFAULT for a
re-export and proves nothing about content.
