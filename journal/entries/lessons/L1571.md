**L1571 · Verify the premise before it reaches the owner - an agent's report is a claim**

**Rule.** Before you put a decision in front of the owner, verify its premise yourself. An agent's report is a
claim, and a claim that reaches the owner is a claim that costs him attention and damages his trust when it
is wrong.

**Evidence, 2026-09-15.** A workstream reported: *"16 CUSTOMERS WAITING on a reply with
`dialpad_customer_sms_live_sends_enabled=false`."* That reads as an emergency and is exactly the kind of
thing to bring to the owner. Two commands killed it:

- `.env:463` → `DIALPAD_CUSTOMER_SMS_LIVE_SENDS_ENABLED=true`, while `app/config.py:887` only holds the
 **default** `False`. Environment wins over a pydantic-settings default, so the flag is **on**, not off.
 The report had read the default and called it the effective value.
- My own re-measure of "waiting customers" with a time bound returned a very different picture from the
 reported 16, and the unbounded version returned 2,115 — the store cannot answer the question without a
 dedup key (see L1563).

So a question that would have gone to the owner was wrong in both its mechanism and its number. It was not
asked.

**Why this is the expensive direction of error.** Rules 1 and 2 exist to stop the agent bothering the owner
with things it should decide. The mirror-image failure is worse and less guarded: **bothering him with
something that is not true.** He cannot audit it, he acts on it, and the system's recorded history says the
most expensive failures here were confident wrongness, not silence.

**How to apply.**
1. Any claim destined for the owner gets re-derived first — especially numbers and especially anything with
 the word "customers", "money" or "urgent" in it.
2. When a report names a config flag, **read the effective resolved value**, not the file default. Grep all
 of `.env`, `app/config.py` and the consumer, and say which one wins.
3. When two of your own measurements disagree (16 vs 2,115), that disagreement is the finding. Do not pick
 the more alarming one, and do not present either until they reconcile.
4. Write down what you did **not** ask and why. A question withheld on evidence is a result.
