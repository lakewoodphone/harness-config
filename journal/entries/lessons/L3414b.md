**L3414b · GE JGB630 range: the Shabbos beep cannot be disabled from the keypad**

Appliance: GE JGB630DEF3BB gas range, control board WB27X44729, owner's manual doc 49-85214-5.

Rule: on this control generation Cook Time (auto-off) and silence are mutually exclusive. Cook Time ends
in a beep that repeats until a pad is pressed -- the owner measured it looping through a full Shabbos
(~24 hours) -- and there is no setting to stop it. Pressing BAKE+BROIL together for 3 seconds opens the
SF menu, which in this generation holds ONLY oven temperature calibration and the 12-hour/Sabbath option
(on / oFF / SAb). The word "beep" appears exactly once in the whole manual, inside the Sabbath paragraph.
The older GE generation had a "CON BEEP -> BEEP" cancel in that menu; GE removed it for this one.

Consequence: Sabbath mode is the only silent option and it cannot use Cook Time, so the oven never shuts
itself off. Permanent silence is hardware: the buzzer is on the ERC board, not a separate replaceable part.
