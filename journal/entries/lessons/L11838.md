**L11838 · One decision queue, three text senders: the queue's own texter has never sent a question**

Measured 2026-10-21 00:56-01:05Z on secratary-cloud, after the owner asked why the
questions he gets by text are "a different queue than the regular queue". He was right.

## There is one queue and three text senders, and they were never joined

ONE LIST. `owner_decision_queue` on `/root/authority.db`: 865 rows all time, 96 pending,
oldest 32.3 days (`owner-queue.py health`, 00:56Z). That is the only list of what needs him.

THREE PROGRAMS WRITE TEXTS AT HIM, each with its own ledger:

 ~/.owner-ask/asks.db owner-ask.py, owner-ask.timer every 30 min
 reads owner_decision_queue directly (line ~180).
 `select count(*) from ask where sent=1` = 0. It has never
 sent one question.

 ~/.cfo-asks/asks.db cfo-ask.py, cron every 30 min. sent=1 = 15.
 Accounting questions ("what was 25.00 at api on 16 Jun").

 ~/.decision-deliver/state.db decision-deliver.py, cron every 30 min, built 2026-10-21.
 send rows = 0. Reads the same queue via decision-triage.py
 shortlist (SEND_NOW 80, COMPOSE 16).

Everything that reaches his phone is mirrored into `owner_text_turn` (155 turns) regardless
of which producer wrote it, so the table looks like one stream while the producers are
three. A fourth producer, lpt-owner-attention.timer (daily 14:21), sends the numbered digest.

## Why owner-ask sends nothing: it refuses its own top item, every run

`journalctl -u owner-ask.service`, 2026-10-21 00:43:21:
 gate: sent_today=0/2 hours=20:00 local candidates=97 kept=94
 chosen: decision:511 The camera product's entire difference from what already exists is tha
 --- body (152 chars, ceiling 160) ---
 [q1792543401] The camera product's entire difference from what already exists. Ask one rav
 you trust before I spend a dollar on this - one conversation,
 GATE REFUSED this body - nothing was sent, nothing recorded:
 - it neither asks him anything nor tells him anything he can act on (152 characters)
 - carries a reference ([q1792543401]) but asks him nothing he can answer

The row's stored `question` is a truncated statement. The asker is not broken; the stored
wording is. The gate is doing its job — it is the only thing standing between him and a text
that cannot be answered.

## The defect this exposes

Two deliverers now point at the same queue: owner-ask.timer (:13/:43) and decision-deliver
(:00/:30). Each dedupes against its OWN ledger — owner-ask on `ask(source,source_id)`,
decision-deliver on `send(row_id)`. Neither can see the other's sends, so the day either one
starts sending, the same row can be texted twice. Fix belongs on the deliverer side: one
sender, or one shared seen-ledger. Not yet fixed.

## Transferable

 * "The queue looks fine" and "the queue reaches him" are different measurements. The first
 is `owner-queue.py health`; the second is `select count(*) from ask where sent=1` in
 whichever ledger the sender uses.
 * When he says two things look like different queues, believe him and read each producer's
 ledger before describing the architecture. The docstring of owner-queue.py says
 owner_message_queue "became noise and then invisible" (2,664 rows) — that is a third,
 retired queue, and quoting it as if it were live is how I gave him a wrong answer first.
 * An asker that refuses every run is silent in the most expensive way: the service exits 0,
 the timer is green, and 96 questions sit.
