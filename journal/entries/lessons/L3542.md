**L3542 · A phone contact is invisible to the shop's record until someone writes it back - read dialpad_call_full for contacts NEWER than the newest case-file entry before trusting either**

Measured 2026-10-08 on the Kogel/73459 case (`WO2608060003`). A production note written **2026-10-08 01:05:25** ends with "the customer called on 2026-10-06 ... he should hear from a person before then, not after". By **12:24 PM ET the same day** the owner had taken the call and told the customer the drive was on its way back. **Neither the case file nor production carried it eleven hours later**, and the case file's own newest section still read "has heard nothing from us since the 2026-09-15 text".

**Why it happens:** a Dialpad call is captured automatically into `dialpad_call_full` with a transcript. Nothing writes it to the case file or to production. So the two records the shop actually reads are, for phone contact, **behind by an unbounded amount** - and they drift *within a day*, not within a month.

**The rule.** Before reporting or acting on "the customer's last contact was X" or "nobody has spoken to them since Y" - and before writing a note that repeats it - run `ps_comms_thread <phone>` or read `dialpad_call_full` for that number and compare the newest row against the newest case-file entry. If the call log is newer, **the call log is the truth and the record needs a write-back** (case-file section + production note, per the lpt-production-case-work skill, section 4).

**Second form of the same bug, same session:** the case file carried "**gender not established ... stop assigning a gender**" while production had held the customer as **"rabbi kogel"** since intake and the shop's own side of an earlier call said "a gentleman". A caution written to stop an *unfounded* assumption had become an *unchecked* one. **A "not established" flag is a claim with an expiry: when two independent records agree, retire it rather than repeat it.**

Both were caught only by reading the comms thread and production side by side. Neither is visible from the case file alone.
