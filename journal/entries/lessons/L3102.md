**L3102 · Picking Broadway seats: find where the overhang starts, and never read the row letter as the price**

MEASURED 2026-09-29, buying two seats at the New Amsterdam Theatre for Aladdin.

1. THE OVERHANG LINE IS THE DECISION. Ticketmaster's checkout for the New Amsterdam states it outright: "Mezzanine Overhangs Orchestra Row M." That one line explains the whole price ladder. The $96.50 orchestra seats were rows N-P-Q-R-S - i.e. UNDERNEATH the mezzanine - which is why they were the cheapest orchestra seats on the board; for a show with flying sequences you lose the top of the stage. The $81.50 balcony was too high and far for a spectacle. Front orchestra rows B-E at $138.50-159.50 crane the neck and cut the top of the stage. FRONT MEZZANINE Row BB at $112.50 sees the whole stage including the floor, with nothing overhead. General rule for a large Broadway house: find where the overhang starts, then sit just in front of it in the tier above rather than under it in the orchestra.

2. THE ROW LETTER IS NOT THE PRICE. Sec ORCH Row N appeared at $96.50, $112.50 AND $159.50 in the same list. Ticketmaster prices seats within a row by position - centre versus side. So: choose the row first, then take the pair closest to the centre line the budget allows. Reading the list as "row = price" makes it look arbitrary and it is not.

3. THE VIEW PREVIEW IS FREE EVIDENCE. The booking flow offers a 360-degree photograph from the exact row band ("VIEW FROM ROWS AA-CC"). Look at it before paying - it settles overhang and rake questions that no seating chart shows.

4. DO NOT LET THE CLOCK PICK FOR YOU. This checkout had a 3-minute countdown with two required selections still unanswered (payment method, ticket insurance) and a final terms checkbox. Decide the extras BEFORE opening the payment page: merchandise bundles were venue pick-up (friction before a 7pm curtain), insurance was $4.50/ticket for a 50-mile drive, and the marketing-SMS box is opt-in and was left unchecked.
