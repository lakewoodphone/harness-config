**L· US Mobile mail is a per-line ledger — but two of the numbers in it are US Mobile's own.**

Every line-level event on a US Mobile account arrives by mail and **names the line**: `Your line is
activated!` ("activation has been completed for line (732) 606-3808"), `One of your US Mobile lines is
about to expire` ("Your 30 day plan for 9089109268 expires…"), `Your US Mobile plan expired` ("Your
plan for *9089109268* has expired"), `Your autopay attempt failed` ("couldn't process the payment for
your line: (908) 770-1707"), `was successfully moved to new SIM`. Read together they reconstruct a
roster — activations, ports, expiries and renewals — without the portal.

**The trap:** the extraction regex finds 10-digit numbers everywhere, and `(878) 205-0088`
(support) plus `(888) 878-1488` (text TOPUP) appear in nearly every message. Counting them as lines
would have overstated the roster by two. Exclude both, and never report a number as a line without
the sentence that makes it one.

**Also:** support/US Mobile mail is served through `support.usmobile.com/ls/click?...` tracking URLs
that dominate the body; strip `https?://\S+` **before** scanning, or the evidence you extract is
link soup (measured 2026-10-05: the first 700 characters of the "account information updated" mail
were one URL).

Evidence: 208 US Mobile messages across 4 mailboxes, mined 2026-10-05; per-number proofs in
`lpt-hub/docs/operations/us-mobile-lines-review-2026-10-06/usm_line_context.json`.
