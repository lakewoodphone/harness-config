**L3351 · Task 'COMMITMENT SIGNAL due' dates are invented by initiative ticks - verify against the source document before repeating them to the owner**

MEASURED 2026-10-07 on the authority (secretary.db, read live). Task 27718 read: 'COMMITMENT SIGNAL - due 2026-10-10 (T+3). Export 256 iCloud Photos before deletion window closes.'
