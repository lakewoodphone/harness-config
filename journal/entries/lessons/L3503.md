**L3503 · Never wait for a customer reply to wake you: look in the comms index**

A reply from a customer on the LPT **Dialpad business line** will not wake a session. `wake.py awaits` is Twilio-fed (`sms-inbox.py` -> `~/.sms-inbox/inbox.db`), so an `await` registered against a shop customer silently never fires - measured 2026-10-11: the reply arrived 2m33s after the ask and the await was still active 27 minutes later.

RULE: after sending a customer anything that expects an answer, the reply must be looked for in the comms index, not waited for. `ps_comms_thread <phone>` or `ps_comms_search "<phone>"` is the check, and it is fast (index age 0.01h). An `await` on a customer number is still worth registering - it costs nothing and it will work the day the bridge exists - but it is NOT a guarantee, so never treat "no wake" as "no reply".

The general form of this mistake is worth keeping: a mechanism that reports success while watching the wrong store looks exactly like a quiet one. The responder printed "0 to decide" for months for the same reason (it read a store whose newest inbound row was 2026-04-29).
