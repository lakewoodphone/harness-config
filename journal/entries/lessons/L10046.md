**L10046 · An execution is not a closure: row 721 was done at 02:07 and asked again at 21:40**

Queue row #721 asked the owner to authorise deleting ten pre-deploy Home Assistant backups. He was
asked twice and was right to be annoyed: the work had already been done at 2026-10-19T02:07:42Z by a
mesh child - one slug at a time through `ha backups remove`, after archiving all ten to R2 (8,167,546,880
B of tar data plus manifests) - and the row was never closed. Free space went from 7,651,446,784 to
15,415,775,232 B that night. A second child today, told to own the decision, re-measured and found six
backups, 929.7 MiB, and decided delete none.

TWO TRANSFERABLE FACTS.
1. An execution is not a closure. Any agent that discharges a queue row must close it with the evidence
 in the same breath, or the next session re-asks. That is now a hard rule in the owner-queue skill.
2. An audit's numbers are a snapshot, not a state. The 2026-10-18 audit measured 16 tars / 9.8 GB and
 by the 19th there were 6 / 929.7 MiB; two children in a row were working from the audit instead of
 from the box. Re-measure before speaking, always.

A detail worth keeping: Home Assistant backups are UNCOMPRESSED tar, so `tar -tzf` fails on every one
of them and looks like corruption. The correct open test is `tar -tf`.
