**L3336 · L: A zero-second inbound call is not a missed customer - score a ring against evidence before counting it**

MEASURED 2026-10-05, round 2 of the contact-queue goal. The merged queue showed 128 open items
whose only signal was a ring. That number cannot be reported as customers, and here is the
evidence for why.

WHAT THE RING-ONLY SET ACTUALLY CONTAINS
Of 83 ring-only items, scoring each against facts we already hold:
 credible 75 (of the full 128 with a ring) - a case file, a past conversation with talk
 time either way, or an outbound call WE placed to them
 unverified 53 - nothing but an identity-link row or a repeat ring
Three obvious non-customers sit in the set: 8337022125 labelled "800 Service", 4383998468
labelled "MONTREAL QC", and 8482243248 which rang 22 times.

WHY `identity-link` AND `repeat-ring` ARE NOT ENOUGH
comms_identity_link maps phones across seven sources, so a number can land there from a single
inbound call - the "800 Service" and Montreal numbers carry that flag. And robocallers ring
repeatedly, so count alone proves nothing. The signals that DO prove a customer are facts
about that specific number: a case file, a conversation that actually happened, or an outbound
call we initiated.

THE RULE
A zero-second inbound call has at least three possible meanings - a customer who hung up, a
robocaller, or the second leg of a ring group. Never report the raw count as customers. Score
each ring against evidence, publish the credible and unverified counts separately, and keep
the labels the phone system gave ("800 Service") visible so nobody mistakes them for people.

WHERE IT LIVES
/home/zabz/_scratch/dialpad/build_contact_queue.py records per-item `evidence` and
`ring_is_credible`, and its totals publish `open_ring_credible` and `open_ring_unverified`
side by side. Verified figures at 2026-10-05T19:57:53Z: 281 numbers with any contact, 183 still
open, 100 of those where a person actually spoke or wrote, 17 open voicemails, 88 open SMS.
