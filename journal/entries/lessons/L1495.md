**L1495 · A detector that covers two of three places reports health it does not have**

A detector that covers two of three places reports health it does not have

The ghost-agent monitor was running, reporting, and correct about what it looked at -- and the
one real instance lived in the table it did not look at. `tasks.assigned_to` is where live work
is assigned; `goal_plans` and `goal_steps` are where its plan is. Checking the plan tables and
reporting "0 ghost agents" is a true statement about the plans and a false one about the
company.

THE HABIT: when a detector reports clean, ask which places it did NOT look, and whether the
thing it detects could live there instead. Enumerate the tables a value appears in before
trusting a count from one of them. The same shape appeared in this session twice more: 39 rows
whose status matched no filter, and 1,780 run records inside a health payload.

And note what made it invisible rather than merely wrong: the monitor's own resolution hint
named the exact fix ("add the alias to _LEGACY_ASSIGNMENT_AGENT_ALIASES"), so the machinery for
reporting this was complete -- it was simply never pointed at the row.
