**L3863 · Charge rate is meaningless without the starting level; same model negotiated 450 vs 900 mA on different ports; E4810 exposes no electrical sysfs**

Charging measured on all five handsets 2026-10-19 with `lpt charge-test --minutes 10` (artifacts `lpt-flip-phone/cli/logs/charge/<serial>-20261019-173643/`).

Measured: 558800238329 (E4810) 27->38% = 66.0 %/h; 558800328253 (E4810) 85->91% = 36.0 %/h; 615963123 (E4610) 34->40% = 36.0 %/h at 595 mA; 616202539 (E4610) 73->80% = 42.0 %/h at 541 mA; 615373543 (E4610) full, 42.6 mA trickle, 0.0 %/h.

Three facts that a "charging: yes" flag hides:

1. **A charge rate without the starting level is meaningless.** The two E4810s differ by 30 points per hour and both are healthy: the one at 27% is in constant-current and moves fast, the one at 85% is in constant-voltage and moves slowly. Compare units at a similar state of charge, or the same unit over time.
2. **The same model negotiated 450 mA on one cable and 900 mA on another** (615963123 vs 616202539). A slow charge is as likely to be the port or hub as the phone, so the artifact records `usb/current_max` and a rate from a different port is not comparable.
3. **The E4810 exposes nothing electrical over sysfs** - no `current_now`, no `usb/current_max`, no `charge_full` - so its rate is level-slope only. The E4610 reports both current and port limit, and its numbers cross-check: 595 mA into an E4610 pack lands where the level slope says 36-40 %/h.

Also: `Charge counter` in `dumpsys battery` is 0 or absent on both models, so a mAh figure only exists when the node does. And `current_now` is negative while charging on E4610 (-595,000 uA at 450 mA limit), so direction must come from the status flag, never from the sign.
