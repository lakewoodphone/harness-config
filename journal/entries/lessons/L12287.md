**L12287 · Violife Just Like Smoked Provolone Slices (Walmart, 7.05 oz): hechsher is Balkan Kosher (BK), pareve, but certificate expired 2023**

Violife "Just Like Smoked Provolone Slices" (7.05 oz / 200 g, 10 slices, UPC 0 810934 030123, made by Arivia S.A., Thessaloniki, at Prosotsani/Drama, Greece for Upfield / Flora Food Group; US packs are labelled "Product of Greece", e.g. https://stopandshop.com/groceries/product/violife-dairy-free-vegan-just-like-smoked-provolone-cheese-slices-10-ct-7-05-oz-pkg/273218) carries the crowned "BK" mark, which is Balkan Kosher (logo match: https://www.balkankosher.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Asset-7CLEAN-300x287.png), NOT OU, OK, Star-K, cRc or Kof-K. Violife's own US page lists "Kosher" but names no certifying agency (https://www.violife.com/en-us/products/dairy-free-cheese-slices/just-like-smoked-provolone-slices); the US retail back-panel artwork published in the Walmart listing shows the BK KOSHER badge (https://www.walmart.com/ip/Violife-Just-Like-Smoked-Provolone-Slices-Dairy-Free-Vegan-7-05-oz-Pack-Refrigerated/785356000, image https://i5.walmartimages.com/asr/ad817458-8192-4ace-abf7-647b02170ac1.72d90fc8f173ec1878ab7b689f7362ca.png). The manufacturer-published Balkan Kosher certificate (Arivia S.A., dated 14 July 2022) lists "Violife Just Like Smoked Provolone" and "Violife Smoked flavoured Provolone Style Alternative to Cheese" among products that are "Kosher Parve all year round excluding Passover", but states "This certificate is in effect until July 15th, 2023" - so it is EXPIRED, and no current public certificate naming this product was found; today the pareve status rests on the pack symbol, not on a live document (https://www.floraprofessional.com/pl-pl/-/media/Project/Upfield/Brands/VPro/Flora-Professional-PL/Assets/Nasze-certyfikaty/Violife_Arivia_and_Rama_Flora_Kleve_Kosher_Certifications.pdf). Label: ingredients are water, modified starch, coconut oil, sea salt, natural flavors, rowanberry extract, smoke flavor, citric acid, olive extract, beta carotene, B12; no dairy ingredient and no "may contain"/shared-equipment statement on the artwork. Agency lookups: OU product search returns no Violife listing (the only OU documents found are letters for Violife plant-butter bricks, status Pareve, valid through 31 Dec 2026, e.g. https://argo.webstaurantstore.com/documents/pdf/violife_salted_kosher.pdf); OK, cRc, Star-K and Kof-K show no Violife/Upfield/Flora entry and their search forms are not URL-queryable (cRc and Kof-K return 403/404 to automated queries); Balkan Kosher publishes no consumer product lookup. Bottom line for the owner: the pack is certified by Balkan Kosher as pareve (not dairy equipment) per the 2022 certificate, that certificate is expired in public, and whether he accepts Balkan Kosher is his decision alone.
