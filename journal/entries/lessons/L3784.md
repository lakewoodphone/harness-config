**L3784 · Shop-purchase questions: do not open with the comms archive or a repo-wide grep**

**The rule:** when the owner asks where to buy something for the shop, start from `lpt-hub`
sourcing docs, the parts houses he already uses, and live product pages. Do NOT open with
`ps_comms_search` and do NOT run a repo-wide grep in `lpt-hub`.

**Measured 2026-10-13**, answering "where do I buy more universal battery chargers":

- `ps_comms_search "battery charger"` returned 15 hits, every one a customer call transcript where
 "charger" meant a laptop power brick. It cost roughly 28 KB of context and answered nothing. The
 index itself is healthy (`index_age_hours` 0.24, 180,071 comms indexed) - this was aimed wrong, not
 broken.
- One `grep` over `lpt-hub/**/*.md` for `supplier|vendor` returned **250 of 3,170 matches**, most of
 them the Dialpad/Twilio vendor analysis, and it also answered nothing.
- What actually answered it: `parts_inventory` + `purchases` (3 rows, 30 rows) and then four live
 research agents.

**Why:** the shop's purchasing truth lives in `purchases`, `parts_inventory`, `docs/.../sourcing/`
and on live product pages. Customer comms are a different subject with an overlapping word.

**Corollary:** for a *research* question, fan out - do not read the archive.
