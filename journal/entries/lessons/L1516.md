**L1516 · ThinkPad FRU numbers: take them from the HMM, not from a seller's compatibility list (44C9909 vs 44C9991)**

On the Shlesinger X201 screen job (2026-09-15) I first recorded the display cable as FRU 44C9909
because multiple parts shops list "IBM/Lenovo ThinkPad X201 44C9909" in their titles. That FRU is the
**CCFL X200** cable. The correct LED cable for a clamshell X201/X201i is **44C9991**
("LED-backlight LCD cable assembly with Bluetooth connector, with camera connector"), taken from
Lenovo's own X201 hardware maintenance manual 43Y6632_05, 12.1" WXGA parts list, machine types
3249/3323/3357/3626/3680/3712/4492.

Other wrong parts in the same family, all sold as "X201" somewhere: 44C9919 = X201s (WXGA+),
45M2944 = X201 Tablet cable. Vendors mislabel them - Impact Computers tags 44C9909 X201-compatible,
Waresphere tags 45M2944 "X201/X201T".

Rule: for a ThinkPad FRU, take the number from the machine's own HMM parts list, not from a seller's
title or a compatibility list. The seller's page is for price and stock; the vendor's manual is for
identity. Where the cable is variant-sensitive (camera / Bluetooth / ThinkLight), the FRU sticker on
the old cable is the final word.

Verified in stock same day: impactcomputers.com 44C9991 refurbished $21.46, 1 unit, Hollywood FL,
30-day warranty; Newegg 44C9991/44C9990 $27.25 from China, seller 3.8 stars.
