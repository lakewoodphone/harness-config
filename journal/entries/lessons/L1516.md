**L1516 · Flashing U1 with the carrier SIM in gives the carrier's CSC, not XAA — judge the flash by the carrier config**

Flashing U1 firmware with the carrier's SIM inserted yields the *carrier's* CSC, not the XAA code the plan named — and that is the better outcome. Judge the flash by the applied carrier config, not by the letter of the plan.

Evidence (2026-09-15, Eli Green S22 Ultra): the case file's plan was "flash SM-S908U1 (XAA)". After the CSC-wipe flash the device reports `ro.csc.sales_code=FKR`, `ro.omc.multi_csc=OYM`, `persist.ril.sales_network_code=FKRFKRFKR`, active OMC `/prism/etc/carriers/FKR`, and FKR's `customer.xml` carries Visible's own APNs (VSBLINTERNET, VSBLIMS, VSBLADMIN, VSBLAPP). FKR is Visible's CSC — a third-party source puts it plainly: "VZW,VZW/VZW,VZW means your phone doesn't have Visible's CSC, FKR. With the Visible SIM in it, it does show the FKR CSC." The original defect cleared with it: `isWifiCallingEnabled=1` (was 0 on the ATT CSC) and an IWLAN IMS connection came up.

Also worth knowing when reading Samsung state: `ro.boot.carrierid` is a factory *param* value (here still `ATT`) and does not mean the AT&T OMC is active — the OMC that matters is `mdc.sys.omc_etcpath` / `persist.sys.omc_path`. Read those before declaring a flash failed to change the carrier.
