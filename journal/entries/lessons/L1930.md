**L1930 · A voice-dictated serial, EMC or part number is not evidence - re-read it off the device before ordering**

The owner dictated "model 2338, EMC 3578, serial FVFG6T6VQ05D" for this MacBook. A 12-character serial and a
4-digit EMC are exactly the fields speech-to-text corrupts by ONE character, and a one-character serial error
becomes a wrong part on a ~$290 order, or a warranty lookup that reports "not found" on a machine that is
in fact covered.
RULE: a dictated identifier is a LEAD, not evidence. Read the model/EMC off the underside printing, and the
serial plus coverage status out of System Settings > General > About, then write the VERIFIED value into the
case front-matter with a dated note. Where an identifier decides a purchase or a warranty claim, the device is
the source and the transcript is not.
Applied here: the case file and the diagnostic log both instruct the bench to re-read serial/model/EMC and to
record the machine colour, because the colour alone moves the part price $5 and a wrong year moves the part.
