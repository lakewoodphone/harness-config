**L7042 · 704 rows carried no category of their own - and 628 of them had it one hop away, on the row they duplicate**

Measured 2026-10-16 across the whole CFO ledger rather than the statement's population, because the objective says EVERY transaction. 704 rows had no transaction_categories row of their own: 42 active, 638 duplicate, 23 archived, 1 migrated - the duplicates carrying 216,320.57 by absolute amount. Measured precisely, the classification for 628 of the 638 duplicates was recorded ONE HOP AWAY on the row they duplicate: 632 had a parent, 628 parents carried the category, 0 parents were missing, and only 4 had a parent that is itself uncategorised. So the gap was a bookkeeping convention rather than a hole, and the statement's own predicate (ingestion_status='active' AND duplicate_of_transaction_id IS NULL) is why no total was ever wrong. Closed it anyway - 651 rows filled from their parent, category rows 8,666 to 9,317 - so every row in the ledger is self-describing, and proved it three ways rather than asserting it: pairs added 651 with pairs REMOVED 0 and every added pair on a row that had none; the statements byte-equal in every entity (escape101 5,754.22, lpt -45,621.04, personal -70,766.32), which is a REQUIREMENT because the predicate excludes every filled row; and quick_check ok after a cp -a backup. The residual is now named instead of folded into a percentage: 53 rows with no classification anywhere, 42 of them the active working set and 11 non-active (6 with no parent, 5 whose parent is itself uncategorised). And the first run failed safely on my own bug - TypeError: tuple indices must be integers, because fillable holds tuples and I indexed them like dicts - failing BEFORE the backup and before any write, which I verified three independent ways rather than assuming: unchanged counts, an absent backup file, zero rows filled. Two lessons: a completeness figure measured over a narrower population than the words of the requirement will understate the gap, so read the population the objective names; and when a row's classification is one hop away on its parent, the right frame is inheritance rather than absence - which also means the detector for a real gap is parent-aware.
