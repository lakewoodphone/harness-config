**L3663 · Clause 3's audit is clocked and thorough: 69 disagreements over only 119 anchored months**

2026-10-13. Clause 3's audit half is done, well-built and on a clock. I called it unclocked for about
ninety seconds and was wrong.

WHAT THE RECONCILIATION IS, in the tool's own words. `/usr/local/bin/finance-reconcile.sh`:
 "the CFO program is defined as done when every account-month is (1) explained, (2) RECONCILED
 against the account's own balance movement, and (3) carries no unnamed exception. Only (1) was
 ever measured."
That is the cleanest statement of the completion bar I have found anywhere in this estate, and it is
the one the objective's clause 3 is asking about. The worklist it writes is the measurement of (2):
 window 2024-10-01 .. 2026-10-13
 account-months 925 anchored 119 derived 164 unknown 642
 DISAGREEMENTS 69, of which 2 sit on an anchor the tool itself warns about
and its docstring says what a disagreement means: "a disagreement is either a missing transaction or a
bad balance, which is the thing the owner asked to have hunted down."

AN HONEST HEADLINE, from that tool rather than from me: 642 of 925 account-months are UNKNOWN and only
119 are ANCHORED. So 69 disagreements is the visible part of a much smaller verifiable base - roughly
13 per cent of account-months can be judged at all. The tool is explicit that this is the reason it
reads mail at all:
 --no-mail : anchored 59, disagreements 21
 with mail : anchored 119, disagreements 69
 "Reading statement balances out of Gmail ... DOUBLES the months that can be judged at all ...
 A reconciliation that cannot see half the months is not a cheaper reconciliation, it is a smaller
 one."

MY ERROR, THE SEVENTH OF ITS KIND TODAY. I grepped the crontab for `cfo-balances|balances`, found only
a COMMENT, checked `runs.tsv`, found nothing, and concluded the financial reconciliation had no clock -
the same defect I had found twice already today, which is exactly why I believed it so readily. It is
scheduled: `23 6 * * 0 cron-wrap.sh finance-reconcile ...`, weekly, Sundays at 06:23, and the job is
named for what it DOES (`finance-reconcile`) rather than for the tool it calls (`cfo-balances.py`). The
box came up on 2026-10-11, after that Sunday's slot, so its first run is 2026-10-18 - the identical
situation to `db-integrity-check` earlier today, where a weekly job was also correctly reporting that
it had not yet had a turn. **A grep for a tool's name is not a check of the crontab; grep for the
OUTPUT or read the annotated block, which in this crontab is delimited by `# >>>` and `# <<<`.**

WHAT THIS LEAVES FOR CLAUSE 3. Condition (1), explained, is measured by `cfo-scoreboard.py` and stands at
526 unexplained of 6,217 non-duplicate. Condition (2), reconciled, is measured weekly and stands at 69
disagreements over 119 anchored months. Condition (3), no unnamed exception, is not something I have
seen measured anywhere and is worth a look before anyone calls the program complete. The RECOVERY half -
turning each of the 69 into either a found transaction or a corrected anchor - needs the chain rule
inside `cfo-balances.py`, and I deliberately did not analyse individual disagreements with ad-hoc SQL:
my first attempt returned 9,976.05 where the tool's chain implies 8,878.77, which is the difference
between a finding and a number that does not reconcile.
