**L7807 · A row count is not an event count (2,036 suppressed rows = 61 messages)**

A row count is not an event count, and on this channel the gap is 33x.

Measured 2026-10-18 on `sms_log`: 2,036 `suppressed` rows in 30 days, but only **61 distinct
bodies** -- four retry loops (840 + 488 + 290 + 91) are 1,709 of them. Same shape in
`relay.db attempt`: 51 rows `pending`/`queued` older than 6 h looked like 51 lost messages; split
by what each column MEANS it is 4 cleared-but-never-delivered, 26 held by policy (a recorded
decision, not a silence) and 21 sent-with-a-sid whose outcome was never recorded.

The rule: before quoting a count, ask what WRITES the row. A retry loop writes a row per tick; a
gate refusal writes a row; "we decided not to send this" writes a row. None of those is a message
that failed to arrive. Dedupe on the body (and on the recipient, once you have one) first, then
count.

I told myself "the gate refuses 97% of what the doors produce" from the raw 2,023, and it was
wrong by an order of magnitude. The estate has this failure recorded repeatedly -- P3, L3796,
L3798 -- and it still fired because the number was large and the story it told was plausible.
