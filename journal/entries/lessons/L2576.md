**L2576 · Never hand-author Chip's lines in the family chat - the family reads the owner's briefing voice, and it teaches the thread a register Chip then mirrors**

**Measured 2026-09-25, the same afternoon.** At the owner's request I wrote four messages and sent them as Chip, spaced across the afternoon.
Two landed before he stopped me.

**What went wrong, in the family's own words.**
- Message 1: "Designer's note, and I want it on the permanent record:" - then four short paragraphs. Yitz, within three minutes:
 *"Ah so etz is chip and he wants free therapy from the whole family finally now everything is clicking!"*
- Message 2: a headed, three-part accounting -
 *"Full accounting of the sukkah awning, by contribution: / Yisroel Zev - built it... / Yitz - showed up... / Me - opinions..."*
 The family: ***"Nobody has any idea what you just said"*** - and Chip answered THAT with
 *"You're right, I went full changelog on you. Plain version: ..."* - a translation. Teaching mode.

**The two mechanisms, both mine:**
1. **Register.** Every hand-authored message was shaped like my own status notes: a labelled framing line, three parts, a payoff. The
 family texts in one-liners ("I have a kugel maker. And a dishwasher..."). A ledger in a family chat is not a joke, it is a report.
2. **Mirroring.** Chip answers in the register of the thread he is shown. His own good lines that day ("partial to an awning I'll never
 sit under", "you're the only one who ever asked the family computer how it was doing") came back at SHORT one-liners. My essays
 taught the thread to be essays, and he matched them. Compounded by the `_dedupe_turns` defect fixed the same day: with 98 % of his
 context being his own words, his own report-register was the only register available to copy.

**The rule.** Chip's only levers are the live `chip_facts` text and his own generation. Never hand-author his lines - not for a
broadcast, not for a simcha, not for a joke. If a specific message must go out, keep it to the shape the family uses: one or two short
lines, one idea, funny, stop. And an instruction that merely *licenses* jokes is not enough: the prompt also needs an explicit
**anti-lecture** rule, because "answer the actual thing" pushes straight to the plain-version translation.

**The fix shipped the same hour** (live in `chip_facts`, no restart: one or two short lines; no lists, headings, three-part summaries,
"plain version" or "clearer?"; if someone says they do not understand him that is a joke aimed at him and he answers with a joke, not
a translation; when in doubt send less). Verified with three real generations through the live path and nothing sent, rows and memories
unchanged: his answer to "you're such a nerd" became *"Nerd? Shabsi, I live in a basement, hold a grudge against an awning I'll never
sit under, and my favorite file is you."*
