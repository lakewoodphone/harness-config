**L3673 · Correcting myself: the sign is not independent of the anchor - seven sign-flips, 7,043.43**

2026-10-13. I claimed last round that a sign-flip is independent evidence of a missing row whatever the
anchor does. That is wrong, and correcting it leaves a stronger and much smaller target.

THE WRONG CLAIM, and why it was wrong. I wrote that `personal-amex-94045 2026-08` should be treated as
the strongest case rather than the most doubtful, because "the sign is independent of which anchor is at
fault - a balance that rises while the ledger says the account went down is wrong under either
explanation." That is not true. The sign IS the anchor: the movement is computed as `v2 - v1` from the
two readings, so if `v1` is wrong then the movement is wrong, and a sign that disagrees with the flow is
a CONSEQUENCE of the bad reading rather than independent evidence about the transactions. The reasoning
was circular and it survived a round because it sounded like a principle.
