**L3942 · Enumerating a remote Windows C: with robocopy/Get-ChildItem is a 15-minute job that outlives the ssh session**

WHAT I DID: to explain 2 GB free on the owner's laptop I ran robocopy /L /E /XJ /BYTES per top-level directory over ssh. C:\Windows and C:\Users each ran past 15 minutes and produced nothing; a .NET recursive walk was worse and can follow junctions (Application Data / Local Settings in a user profile) into a loop.

WHAT IT COST: killed local ssh jobs left the REMOTE robocopy processes walking the disk (two stray pids found and killed afterwards). The owner was sitting at that laptop. A near-full NTFS volume makes enumeration slower, not faster.

RULE: on a remote Windows box, never enumerate C:\ or C:\Windows or C:\Users wholesale. Measure the named suspects only (Program Files, ProgramData, the specific profile subfolders, SoftwareDistribution, pagefile), pass /XJ, and after killing the job verify no robocopy/powershell of mine is still running remotely. When a stray is found, kill only processes whose CommandLine matches my own marker (C:\NULL), never by image name.
