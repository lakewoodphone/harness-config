**L2984 · The money ceiling was enforced against a measurement 18 hours stale: a mechanism that is not on a schedule is a mechanism that does not exist (third instance today)**

MEASURED 2026-09-28 19:35Z on the authority. The defect: THE MONEY CEILING WAS ENFORCED AGAINST A
STALE NUMBER.

 ~/.sms-inbox/wake-cost.jsonl last written 01:03:22Z (18 hours earlier), holding 3 priced rows
 wake-cost.py --days 1 priced 10 releases, real spend 0.468477 USD
 wake.py stats spend_today_usd = 0.135076 <- the OLD figure, 3.5x too low
 and nothing in cron ran wake-cost.py at all.

WHY THIS MATTERS MORE THAN AN ARITHMETIC ERROR. The owner corrected the whole design on 2026-09-28:
volume is not the limit, SPEND is (D2715, 70 USD/day). I then built `spend_today()` to read the real
per-release measurement, and it worked in the test I ran - but nothing kept the measurement CURRENT,
so the guard was reading a figure frozen since the small hours. A ceiling enforced against a stale
measurement cannot bind, which quietly returns the system to the state the correction was meant to
end: unbounded by anything real.

THE PATTERN, and it is the third time today in a different costume: a mechanism is built, proven once
by hand, and then nothing SCHEDULES it. The first two were wake.py's doubled cap default (nothing
ran while every reading looked healthy) and the 27 wake rows nobody consumed. The lesson is not "be
careful" - it is that a mechanism which is not on a schedule is a mechanism that does not exist.

THE FIX: `*/15 * * * * cd /home/zabz && python3 ~/bin/wake-cost.py --days 1`. Cheap (it reads local
files and the shipped session archive), and it keeps the guard within a quarter hour of the truth.
Verified: the file's mtime moved from 01:03Z to 19:35Z in the same run, and the store now reports
0.46902 USD of a 70.00 ceiling instead of the frozen 0.135076.

USEFUL SIDE FINDING: the real cost per release works out at about 0.04-0.07 USD for 3-5M tokens,
because the great majority of the volume is cache-hit input. At 0.47 USD for 11 releases the owner's
70 USD/day ceiling is roughly 1,500 releases' worth of headroom - so it will bind only on something
pathological, exactly as he wanted.
