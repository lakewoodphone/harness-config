**L12255 · A price filed as owner-only was one product-page fetch away (iPhone 12 Pro camera)**

## A "price only the owner can set" turned out to be one product-page fetch away

**Measured 2026-10-22, on the Rothstein thread ((972) 523-2905).**

On 2026-10-13 the owner promised this customer, out loud on a recorded call, a price for an
**iPhone 12 Pro rear camera**. Nothing happened for nine days; the customer called twice on 2026-10-21
and got nothing. A previous session's brief (`lpt-hub/_scratch/respond/briefs/batchB.md`) filed the
price as **"Owner-only: yes — the camera price"** with the reason: *"No part cost, no margin and no
quote for an iPhone 12 Pro rear camera exists anywhere in what I could read."*

That sentence was true and the conclusion drawn from it was wrong. **The part cost was on two
public product pages.** Read today:

- Injured Gadgets `IP12P-SP-22`, Rear Camera for iPhone 12 Pro — **71.00**, 84 in stock, and its own
 listing title carries the caveat: *"Warning: Replacing this Camera May Result in Restricted
 Functionality / Non-Genuine Part Notification."*
- MobileSentrix, Back Camera for iPhone 12 Pro (Genuine OEM) — **173.22** with core returned,
 **220.98** without. That is precisely what *"genuine is coming up very expensive"* meant on the call.
- MobileSentrix used OEM pull, grade A — 54.18; individual modules main wide 15.78 / tele 30.51 /
 ultra wide 7.40.

Run through the house rule (`docs/customer-operations/PRICING-RULE.md`,
`(cost + shipping) x 1.4 + 65`): **180 aftermarket, 320 genuine with core, 385 genuine without.**

**The lesson is the distinction, not the number.** "No part cost exists *in what I could read*" is not
"no part cost exists". A repair price has exactly two inputs — a landed part cost and the shop's own
published formula — and **both are obtainable without the owner**. What is genuinely his is whether to
*quote* the figure and which tier to offer, which is one question, not a blocking research gap.

So: before writing "owner-only" against a price, name the supplier page you tried. On this estate the
suppliers are Injured Gadgets and MobileSentrix, both readable unauthenticated, and this repo already
holds dozens of recorded reads from both. **A price filed as owner-only is usually a fetch nobody did.**

**Second finding, same thread:** the 0-second inbound calls on 2026-10-21 are *missed calls*, and the
standing rule (`CUSTOMER-SMS-RUNBOOK.md` §0 rule 7, owner 2026-10-19: *"we are not texting people who
just called and need a call back, that's her job"*) makes the correct action a **phone callback**, not
a text. A session that reads the thread and drafts a text for the two hangups is breaking a rule the
owner stated twice. The callback was blocked on the camera price and on nothing else.
