**L1715 · DSH cost is requests x mean context; the cache is already healthy, so only volume is left**

Measured 2026-09-16 from the DeepSeek console export (authoritative). The 2026-09-15 spike of 54.738153411 USD of deepseek-flash was NOT a pricing event: dollar-per-request was 0.0015 on 09-15 and 0.0020 on 09-14 - flat. Request count went 8,863 (09-11) -> 20,501 (09-14) -> 34,787 (09-15). Mean context 189,030-262,949 tokens per request. Cache hit rate is 99.1% locally and the ledger's own miss rate is 0.67-3.19% per day, so prefix caching is working and is NOT the problem. Composition of the DSH pool over 30 days: cache-hit input 42.1%, output 33.5%, cache-miss input 24.4%. 13.73 billion cache-hit tokens moved in 30 days. CONSEQUENCE: do not propose prefix-stabilisation work or a model-tier change (deepseek-flash is already 99.1% of requests; deepseek-v4-pro is 5.12 USD of 195.74). The reducible term is context x steps: tool output is 82.5-95.8% of prompt tokens and the pruner suppresses only 23% of it.
