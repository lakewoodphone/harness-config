**L2118 · Writing up work on a device you can no longer reach: record it as a DATED OBSERVATION, not as current state - and say which parts were yours**

> RENUMBERED 2026-09-20 (ZABZ-TECH -> trunk reconciliation). This entry was minted as
> `L2003` on ZABZ-TECH while the trunk minted its own `L2003`; both were live, so that id
> meant two different entries on two machines. The trunk keeps `L2003`; this entry is now
> `L2118`. Body is unchanged apart from this note and pointers to its renumbered siblings.
> Alias recorded in `journal/index/aliases.tsv`.

2026-09-17, the Bachrach SM-S721U case. The owner asked for the case files to be updated
with "exactly what you did". Two traps, both worth keeping.

TRAP 1 - THE FILE HAD DRIFTED FROM THE DEVICE. The front-matter still said "NOT tamper-proof",
the title still said "(phone not captured)" while the serial was recorded lower down, and
Device / Issue still said the model was "not yet recorded" - all false by then. Anyone opening
that file would have concluded nothing had been done to the phone. Long append-only logs drift
this way silently: only the log gets updated, and the header that people actually read rots.
FIX: a "Current state - read this, not the log" section at the TOP carrying the exact device,
the exact commands, what was verified, what the device already had, what is left undone.

TRAP 2 - I TRIED TO RE-MEASURE AND COULD NOT. Immediately before committing I re-queried the
phone to timestamp the state as current. `adb devices` returned ZERO devices - he had
disconnected it. So the honest options were to write a stale reading as if it were live, or to
say I could not. I said I could not:
- a provenance notice at the top of the state section: every measurement is from 2026-09-17,
 nothing re-verified since, treat as a dated observation;
- and the reason it probably still holds spelled out AS A REASON, not as evidence:
 "pm disable-user persists across reboots by design" is grounds to EXPECT the block survived,
 NOT a measurement that it did.
To make it current: reconnect and re-run the three checks. An unverified "still blocked" is the
guess-dressed-as-fact this corpus keeps getting burned by (cf. the stale-database incident).

TRAP 3 - CREDIT. The four restrictions doing most of the real work in that state
(no_safe_boot, no_factory_reset, no_install_apps, no_install_unknown_sources) were ALREADY
enforced by Family Link before the session touched anything. My actual change was the enabled
state of two packages. Writing it up as if the strength were our work would be self-flattering
and would mislead whoever reads it next about what is proven. The file now says explicitly:
"This session changed exactly two things - the enabled state of two packages."

RULES
1. Re-read the front-matter before finishing. If it contradicts the log, the front-matter is
 what gets believed and it is the part nobody updated.
2. If you cannot re-verify, say WHEN you measured and that you have not since. Never present a
 stale reading as current, and never let "it should still work" stand in for "I checked".
3. Distinguish what you changed from what was already true. On these devices most of the
 protection is usually the owner's existing configuration.
