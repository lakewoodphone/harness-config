**L218 · Bulk iPhone liquidation sourcing: fees, gates, dead channels, and live lot benchmarks**

Bulk-iPhone liquidation channel facts, verified 2026-09-14. These were expensive to establish (10 channels, firecrawl rate limits, several dead ends) and are all cheap to reuse.

1. BULQ IS DEAD. Optoro shut the bulq.com marketplace 2025-07-28 (final inventory 2025-07-25, ordering stopped 5PM ET 2025-07-28). The domain no longer resolves at all. Do not treat it as a live channel; do not re-research it. Source: fulfill.com/partners/bulq.

2. SVD = Silicon Valley Disposition, svdisposition.com. Founded 2010 by John Carroll; runs a "SAM" (Surplus Asset Management) platform. It is an industrial/lab/tech-EQUIPMENT auctioneer (biopharma, R&D, EV/AV, energy, manufacturing). It has NO phone or iPhone lots. If someone says "SVD" in a phone-sourcing context, the premise is wrong.

3. treasurehunt.com is a PARKED GoDaddy domain for sale, not a liquidation company. "Treasure Hunt Liquidators" is a physical bin store. No "Trevco" liquidation entity exists. Unverified/no such thing.

4. BUYER FEE IS THE DECIDING VARIABLE, and it varies 6x across these channels:
 B-Stock fee = exactly 2% of winning bid (verified 2 ways: 100.50/5025, 25.00/1250).
 AllSurplus + GovDeals = seller-set, capped 12.5%, typically 7.5-12.5% (confirmed 12.5% printed on real lot pages).
 DirectLiquidation = per-auction, auctioneer-set, NOT published platform-wide (their own FAQ says so).
 Quicklotz/Amazon Bulk = no premium (fixed price), but wrong inventory shape.
 => On a $5,000 lot that is $100 vs $625. Always compute per-unit INCLUDING the fee, never the bid alone.

5. Real per-unit benchmarks, 2026-09-14: clean iPhone 11/12 UNLOCKED B/C lands ~$64/unit on B-Stock GameStop. iPhone 12 64GB on AllSurplus lands ~$124/unit (12.5% premium). ~2x gap from fee+grade alone.

6. CARRIERS DO NOT RUN PUBLIC BRAND-NAMED LIQUIDATION STOREFRONTS. bstock.com/auctions/t-mobile-auctions and /att-mobile exist as brand FILTER pages and return 0 auctions; /verizon, /tmobile, /superiorwireless, /ecomat under /cellphones all 404. Carrier trade-in volume flows through the anonymous "Mobile Carrier" storefront (bstock.com/mobilecarrier): ~500 auctions/wk, TX, Tue+Thu 12:30 ET, US only. Its gate is the expensive one: reseller cert PLUS a $500 screening fee paid to the seller, plus R2 certification for non-functional inventory. B-Stock's own whitepaper confirms AT&T/T-Mobile/Samsung moved trade-ins to B2B marketplaces.

7. Liquidity Services (AllSurplus + GovDeals + liquidation.com) is ONE family, NASDAQ LQDT, sharing lot IDs and seller accounts (30009=Michigan E-Recycle, 8445=WA State Surplus). Free registration, NO reseller cert needed. New-bidder probation: 3 active auctions + $1,000 card cap, cleared instantly by a $1,000 REFUNDABLE bid deposit. Payment due 5 business days; invoices >= $5,000 require wire. Mostly pickup-only; Michigan E-Recycle charges $5/box or $60/Gaylord for freight. NO buyer protection whatsoever (Trustpilot ~1.6/5).

8. The dominant iPhone 11-15 inventory on GovDeals/AllSurplus is iCloud/MDM/PWL-LOCKED and sold "for parts". It fails an "Activation Lock OFF" requirement. Filter on lock status FIRST, price second - the cheapest per-unit number found ($27.33) was also the least useful (pre-iPhone-11 models).

9. A "Financially Obligated" lot = devices still on a carrier installment plan (observed: AT&T iPhone 17 Pro Max lot, 40 units, $20,550 = $513.75/unit). Blacklist risk. Avoid at any price.

10. Lot velocity is BRUTAL: of the iPhone lots surfaced by search on 2026-09-14, four were already closed (GovDeals 287 closed Feb 2026, GovDeals 577 closed Sep 11 2026, AllSurplus 525 closed Aug 28 2026, AllSurplus 82 "item not available"). Never trust a listing found via search without re-fetching the lot page and reading its close date - the search index lags days.

TOOLING: firecrawl rate-limits at 15 req/min and is easy to exhaust; the Jina reader (mcp__jina__read_url) has NO such limit and renders bstock.com/allsurplus/govdeals fine - use it as the workhorse and spend firecrawl only on JS-heavy pages. Also: firecrawl_scrape's nested jsonOptions intermittently fails param validation ("url: expected string, received undefined"); plain formats:["markdown"] always works, so fall back to that instead of retrying the schema.
