**L1685 · MagicDNS on ZABZ-YOGA blocks one call for ~60s and then heals; retry before blaming the mesh**

MEASURED 2026-09-16 01:09-01:20Z on ZABZ-YOGA.
`ssh secratary-ts "python3 ~/bin/owner-queue.py next"` failed with `Connection timed out` after the client sat on name resolution. A `Resolve-DnsName secratary.tail93e6e6.ts.net` timed out at 60s, and so did `Get-DnsClientServerAddress`. Eleven minutes later the SAME name resolved instantly from both the system resolver and 100.100.100.100, and `ssh secratary-ts` returned in 5.7s. Tailscale status was healthy throughout (secratary `active; direct`), iphlpsvc and Tailscale both `Running`.

DO NOT diagnose a dead mesh from one timed-out call. Order: (1) `tailscale status`; (2) retry the call; (3) retry by Tailscale IP 100.84.72.88, which is what worked while DNS was wedged. Only then consider restarting iphlpsvc/Tailscale (the D172 pattern) - a restart drops the mesh for ~30s and will break any session mid-ssh, which is why it must not be the first move.
