**L2318 · A time-boxed risk needs a scheduled check, not advice - 'what will notice if this goes wrong tonight?'**

RULE: When I identify a risk with a **known deadline in time** — a fast beginning at 18:39, a 25-hour window with no food or water, a medication course ending on a date — giving the owner good advice is NOT the deliverable. Something must be scheduled that will notice the outcome at or before the deadline: a dated task with the deadline in its text, or the wake machinery. Otherwise the only thing watching is the owner, and he is by definition the person who cannot watch.

WHY: Advice is not monitoring, and nothing in this system notices while no session is open. On 2026-09-20 I documented his febrile illness, named the fast as the real risk, wrote a hydration plan, and let him go. On 2026-09-21 he spent Yom Kippur deteriorating on a couch — worsening thirst, a massive headache, **laboured breathing**, dizziness, feverish and freezing under a blanket — and the fast was broken for him by his wife with 1 oz of water at a time, about 75 minutes before I heard anything. The file I had written the day before already carried the red-flag list. The flag was hit and nothing was watching.

HOW TO APPLY: At the same moment the advice is given, create the check with an explicit date and deadline (task 26341 is the pattern), and where the window is short and hard, use `wake.py` so a session is actually started. Ask the one question that closes it: **"what will notice if this goes wrong tonight?"** If the answer is "nothing", the work is not finished.
