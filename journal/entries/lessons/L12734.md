**L12734 · Measuring a YouTube channel's Shorts/long-form split without yt-dlp**

No yt-dlp on this host. Working method, no API key: curl 'https://www.youtube.com/@handle/videos?hl=en' and '/shorts?hl=en' with a browser UA and -b 'CONSENT=YES+1', then parse the ytInitialData JSON out of the HTML. Videos tab: duration lives in thumbnailBadgeViewModel.text; the view count exists ONLY in accessibilityLabel as '71 thousand views' (the text beside it is just '71K'), titles in content. Shorts tab uses shortsLockupViewModel with views in content as '763K views'. Parse K/M/B or you silently read 71 instead of 71000 - my first two parse attempts returned a mean of 245 views. Measured @aljokes 2026-10-21: 48 recent Shorts mean 1,472,000 views (median 1,000,000; top 10 = 49% of all Shorts views); 30 recent regular videos mean 300,000; 9 of those 30 are 8-20 minute compilations carrying 18% of the video-tab views. Lesson: a channel's '25% long-form' by upload count was ~6% by views, and every monetisation number moves with that. Measure the split in VIEWS, never in uploads.
