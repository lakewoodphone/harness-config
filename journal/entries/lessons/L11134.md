# A note we wrote to ourselves reset the customer-activity clock

Measured 2026-10-20 on the operational store (`d5oq83veii4rlb`), secratary-cloud.

The owner gave the 50-day pickup rule at 14:19Z. The rule's script ran at 14:31 and closed
**105** orders, then every later run said `auto-close: no order qualifies` — while **314** orders
still sat at `READY_FOR_PICKUP`, the oldest entered on 2025-12-24 (300 days).

Why: the selector's second condition was "no activity for 50 days", and activity included **any**
row in `customer_notes`. On 2026-10-14 our own jobs wrote notes on **243** of those orders —
254 `PAYMENT RECONCILIATION` reviews, 74 corrections, 16 `CLOSED OUT` decisions — plus 63 payment
rows imported that day. Each note restarted the clock, so the orders that most needed closing were
the ones our bookkeeping kept open.

The predicate was reading a field **we** write and treating it as a field the **customer** writes.
That is the general shape worth remembering: when a staleness gate is built on "last activity",
enumerate who can write that column, and if it is us, the gate measures our own noise.

`customer_notes."authorId"` does not rescue it: the reconciliation job files under the owner's own
account (user 100, `eliyahu@lakewoodphoneandtech.com`), and the assistant files under
`admin@test.com` (user 1). Discrimination had to move to the note's opening marker, which is a
maintenance duty — `BOOKKEEPING_NOTE_PREFIXES` in
`backend/src/services/work-orders/auto-close.service.ts`. **Any new automated job that writes a
note on a work order must add its prefix there**, or it silently keeps every order it touches open
for another 50 days.

After the fix: 147 candidates, 147 closed, 0 skipped, `READY_FOR_PICKUP` 314 -> 167, and the next
dry run reports none. Zero of the 147 had any SUCCEEDED payment, so no customer was told they owe
anything by this. Notes are private, name the assistant, and say the newest record on the order was
our own bookkeeping rather than contact from the customer.

Second lesson, cheap but repeated: `lpt doctor` reported the operational store **DOWN** on the
authority because the system `python3` has no `psycopg2`, though
`/root/personal-secretary-mvp/.venv/bin/python3` does. Running `PYTHONPATH=/root/code/lpt-ops
/root/personal-secretary-mvp/.venv/bin/python3 bin/lpt ...` needs no install and no system change.
