**L1530 · A fresh id is not a new thing: dedup keyed on identity cannot see a re-offer**

**A fresh id is not a new thing: dedup keyed on identity cannot see a re-offer.**

Measured 2026-09-15. `evolution_log` holds 38 unapplied rows for `app/services/activity_sync.py` with
**one distinct description** and **38 distinct `proposal_id`s**. Any dedup keyed on the proposal id --
the obvious key, and the one the schema invites -- would have concluded these are 38 different
proposals. They are one offer, made 38 times, each recording a fresh id.

That is why the kernel's `check_evolution` could read HIGH for weeks without anyone being able to act on
it: the *record* was faithful and the *identity* was meaningless.

Rules:
- **Dedup on the content, not on the id the producer assigns.** An id proves the producer created a
 thing, never that the thing is new. This is the same reason the owner-message queue dedups on
 `(reason, body)` and the journal on `entry_hash`.
- **A filter that was never written is not a filter.** 19 rows targeted files inside `node_modules`, and
 nothing anywhere said those were out of bounds -- the boundary existed in everyone's head. Write it
 down where the write happens, so the refusal is a fact rather than a hope.
- **Prefer to leave the history alone and stop the bleeding.** Marking the old rows "applied" would have
 turned the badge green in one statement and put a false claim in the record. The bounded window exists
 so history clears itself; the fix is that nothing new can join it.
