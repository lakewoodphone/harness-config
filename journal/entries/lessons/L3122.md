**The owner-SMS rate limiter counted its own suppressed and failed rows as delivered texts - two bad attempts locked a recipient out for the day**

Measured 2026-09-30 on SECRATARY while trying to send the owner's message to his wife.

`app/chat_action_runtime.py` (the "Owner SMS rate limit: max 2 per day (UTC)" block, ~line 1913) counts:

```
SELECT COUNT(*) FROM sms_log WHERE direction='outbound'
AND to_number=? AND date(created_at)=?
```

No status filter. `sms_log` carries statuses `delivered, failed, pending, queued, sent, suppressed`. So a row that was **suppressed** (by the dedup or length gate) or **failed** is counted as a send that happened.

Consequence, measured: my own two attempts to that number earlier in the same conversation (row 1054 `suppressed`, row 1055 `failed`) took the counter to 2, so the third attempt was refused with "Owner SMS rate limit reached (2/day). The owner has complained about too many texts." **Nothing had reached her at all.** The refusal's own explanation was therefore false, and the guard was blocking on its own failures.

THE FIX, applied: `AND status NOT IN ('suppressed','failed')`. The cap now counts only real sends. One-line change, backed up to `chat_action_runtime.py.bak-suppressed-count-20260930T153520`, py_compile clean, `sudo systemctl restart secretary-api`, /health 200 (it needs ~30s to bind port 8002 - a check at 12s reads 000 and looks like a broken restart), and the send then went through on the first attempt.

TWO GENERAL RULES.
1. **A limiter that counts its own failures is not a limiter, it is a lockout.** Any counter guarding a scarce or sensitive action must filter on "did this actually happen", not "was a row written". The same bug was already affecting the owner's own alternate number: rows 1052 and 1053 were two suppressed Elantra reminders to +18483897895, which put that number at 2/2 for the day as well.
2. **Read the guard's own numbers before believing its story.** The message said "the owner has complained about too many texts". What it meant was "two rows exist with this recipient". Those are different claims and only one of them was checked.

Also still true from L3117: the same path caps ONE text at 320 characters and suppresses a longer body rather than splitting it.
