**L1896 · The recovery-chances answer is the 300DDR chances-form email, not a repo doc**

When a customer asks "what are the chances of recovery" at LPT, the answer already exists and is not in any repo: it is the 300DDR Chances Form reply from `chances@300dollardatarecovery.com` in the `lakewoodphoneandtech@gmail.com` mailbox, and nothing else.

- The reply gives, per drive: chance of success (a per-model figure off their own history), the flat lab rate, whether donor parts are likely, the average donor cost, how many units of that exact model they have handled, the average % of data recovered on a success, and the % of that model that arrive with bad heads. That is the whole answer.
- Find them with `gmail_search` query `from:300dollardatarecovery.com newer_than:45d` and `account=lakewoodphoneandtech@gmail.com`, then read by id with the `gmail_read` action (`gmail_read_message` does not exist). The shop's own DB and lpt-hub hold none of this — `email_catalog` was empty for these.
- Measured 2026-09-17: the 300DDR reply arrives roughly 40 minutes after form submission, and the shop's customer quote (lab flat rate + donor pass-through + roughly $200-250) is derived from it within the hour. So a customer quote must always be traceable to one of these emails; if it is not, the quote is unverified.
- The $160 per drive is LPT's non-refundable deposit, and it is defensible precisely because 300DDR charges nothing when a drive yields no files — the $160 covers the bench attempt and shipping. Say that to the customer instead of only "you don't get it back".
