**L2084 · The 07:30 morning briefing is the channel that provably reaches the owner - to use it, make it an open task with a due date**

## The fact

The 07:30 owner-timezone morning briefing is the routine channel that provably reaches the owner.
Evidence: `owner_message_queue` id 1794, reason `owner_briefing_morning`, urgency `urgent`, status
**`sent`**, created 2026-09-20T07:31:11Z — while ordinary `normal`-urgency reminders in the same
hour were `held`. Setting is `owner_briefing_morning_time` (default `07:30`), `app/config.py:876`.

## How to put something in front of him through it

`app/personal_assistant_context.py:273-335` builds the briefing's TASKS section from
`list_tasks(status="open", agent_id=settings.primary_agent_id)`; a task is a candidate when it is
**overdue OR due today OR priority urgent/critical**, and the top 6 are taken by
(overdue, due_today, urgent, priority rank).

So an owner-facing item is guaranteed to surface when it is:

- `status="open"` — **`in_progress` tasks do NOT appear** (line 276); leave owner reminders open;
- `agent_id="orchestrator"` (`primary_agent_id` default, `config.py:839`) — or the primary agent
 filter hides it;
- `priority="high"` with a due date: `chat_action_tasks.py:270-294` auto-downgrades `high` to
 `medium` when the due date is more than 48 h out, so a `high` without a near deadline is really a
 `medium`. Any due-date = today qualifies regardless, so the due date is the load-bearing field.

## Proven

2026-09-20: task **#26305** (open, high, due 2026-09-21, agent_id orchestrator) created for the
T-Mobile cancellation and read back from the DB. It will appear in Monday's briefing TASKS section.
