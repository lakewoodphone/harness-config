**L12277 · Money owed vs money collected: search orders by amount-times-tax, not by name - a duplicate customer row hid a paid 60**

A case file can own TWO production orders, and the money for one of them can be sitting on a
duplicate customer row under a misspelled name. Read both before saying a balance is uncollected.

What happened, measured 2026-10-22 on the Schreiber case (lpt-hub case inspiron-15-3520-schreiber-
7325972935-2026-10-08). The repair order WO26100812737 carries a 60.00 data-recovery total and read
`collected USD 0.00 in SUCCEEDED transactions`. The obvious sentences were "she has not paid" and
"if she paid cash at the counter it was never entered". Both were wrong to stop at, because a
SEARCH OF ORDERS BY AMOUNT-TIMES-TAX, not by name, found this:

 orders 3149 / WO2610200004, ticket 2783, created 2026-10-20 17:35:13,
 total 63.98, payment CAPTURED, 63.98 in one SUCCEEDED payment_transactions row (id 3411),
 customer 2982 firstName "Shriber" with NO phone and NO email, auto-created from a MANUAL
 intake case with no photographs, no intakePayload and no metadata, 0 notes, 0 comms.
 63.98 = 60.00 x 1.06625 exactly.

Same evening the owner had phoned her to say the drive was ready at 60 and she said she would come
with cash. That is very likely her money, booked against a customer row that will never be found by
her phone number. It may also be an unrelated Shriber - three spellings of the same letters already
exist in production (Schreiber / Shrieber / Shriber) - so nothing was merged, renamed or repointed.

THE RULE. Before you write "uncollected", compute the money owed plus the shop's own 6.625% tax and
search `payment_transactions` and `orders` for that exact amount in a window around the quote. An
amount search finds a duplicate customer row that a phone search by definition cannot. Two records
for one household is the normal failure, not the exotic one: the form typed Shrieber, production
says Schreiber, the counter typed Shriber, and only the arithmetic joins them.

Corollary, and it is the same shape as the Pollok case (2026-10-20): a payment row can also exist
with `stripeChargeId NULL` and `customerId NULL`, which means it was written by a backfill or a
terminal, not by a recorded charge - so "no Stripe charge" does not mean "no payment".

Second, smaller fact worth keeping: the website's `computer_requests` row IS the intake of record for
a computer sale, and `handoffStatus SKIPPED` with `handoffAttempts 0` is the honest reading that NO
text has been sent to the customer about it. A row recovered by hand after a lost submission carries
exactly that shape, so never read SKIPPED as failure.

Third: `lpt-hub` is cloned on the OFFICE box (`secratary-ts` -> host `secratary`), NOT on
secratary-cloud. `sync-health-audit.py` on the authority therefore reports "lpt-hub local git root
not found" and `lpt-sync-to-production.py --dry-run-all` exits with "Cannot find lpt-hub repo"; the
four-surface `audit.py` must run on the office box, which host-posture.tsv marks frozen but READ-ONLY,
and a read-only verifier is within that. Also: `cmd | tail -30; echo "EXIT=$?"` prints tail's status,
so a failed script reads as exit 0 - capture the exit code on its own line before the pipe.
