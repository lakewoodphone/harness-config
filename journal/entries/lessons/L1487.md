CORRECTION to H217: the supplier-delivery watchdog DOES exist, is scheduled, and works.

H217 (written 2026-09-15 01:15, the previous round) said "nothing in the system watches a paid
supplier order that never got tracking ... That watchdog does not exist." That is FALSE, and I
took it from a procurement document's own claim ("There is no watchdog anywhere ...") instead
of checking the system. Before finding this I had designed a replacement and was about to
build it -- a duplicate of a working, scheduled job.

WHAT IS ACTUALLY THERE:

It searches shipping evidence in three places -- Gmail by order number, Gmail by amount
(PayPal's "on its way" carries the amount and transaction id), and contact_interactions
(tracking for order 62415 arrived on WhatsApp, which no mailbox saw) -- and classifies each
order SHIPPED / WARN / CRITICAL / UNKNOWN, where UNKNOWN means no payment receipt could be
found and the tool refuses to claim a missing shipment it cannot evidence.

VERIFIED WORKING, and against known ground truth (the procurement doc states which orders
shipped): the 2026-09-15 run printed

four orders with "evidence search failed: Connection refused" -- because the API was down
during a restart of mine -- and said so rather than reporting health. That is the behaviour

THE RULE: an existence claim in a note or a document is not a measurement. `ls`, `crontab -l`

authoritative about what I did, not about what is currently true.
