**L10175 · The two 11-dollar customers were not filter customers at all - corrects H10026**

Correction to H10026, written the same evening, because the handoff states something that is false.

H10026 says: "Two paying customers are recorded nowhere: avrohom newman and mrs greenwood, 11.00 each on
16 October." That was my reading of a webhook report, and it was wrong. Measured on the live broker at
2026-10-19T22:44Z:

- Both 11.00 subscriptions sit on price_1TcU7bLnlgYj1gmTA8tokOa9, product "Monthly Service" ("Phone
 service for us mobile monthly"). The FILTER price is price_1UFj8ZLnlgYj1gmThNxiIvFK at 7.00 per
 device, and **zero** subscriptions are on it.
- Both invoices are verbatim "1 x Monthly Service (at 11.00 / month)". They bought phone service, which
 the shop sold them and which they have received: four paid invoices each, current period
 2026-10-16 to 2026-11-16. Nothing is owed and nothing failed.
- The broker has 0 tenants, 0 households, 0 phones, 0 tenant_signups, 0 billing_provider_events.
 The filter product has never had a customer.

So the money is not unrecorded; it belongs to a different product. Stamping metadata.tenant_id on those
two subscriptions — which is what I was about to authorise — would have written a phone-service charge
into the filter's billing ledger and, worse, would have made the filter count and eventually suspend
customers who never bought it.

THE REAL DEFECT: the filter's webhook handler keys on metadata.tenant_id for ANY charge on the account.
A non-filter invoice can therefore never be ingested correctly, and any attempt to make it work by hand
mis-attributes it. The fix is to route on price/product id (or give the filter its own endpoint), not to
stamp tenants onto unrelated subscriptions.

Lesson worth keeping: a payment is not a customer of your product just because it arrived at your
endpoint. Check WHICH PRICE it paid before deciding it is unrecorded revenue.
