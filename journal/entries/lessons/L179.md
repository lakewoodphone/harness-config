**L179 · A dedupe key over the body alone dropped seven entries whose bodies are empty**

*Learned:* migrating the six flat files into shards, the first run reported complete success and had silently
dropped **7 entries**. The dedupe key was the sha1 of the body alone, and eight lessons in the old file
(**L23, L162-L166, L170, L173**) put the entire lesson on the heading line and have an *empty* body — so all
eight hashed identically and seven were discarded as "already present".
*Rule:* an identity key must cover every part of the thing it identifies. Hashing the body alone is safe only
if the body is guaranteed non-empty, and nothing checked that. The key is now heading **and** body, and the
same class of bug is what `journal.py audit` exists to catch: it re-reads the sources and compares content,
rather than trusting the migration's own report.
