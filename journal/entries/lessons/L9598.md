**L9598 · Unpaid is not a fact until Stripe says so: our payment_transactions is written by a nightly job, not by the payment**

Proving "the customer has not paid" takes four sources, and our own database is the weakest of them.

Order 3018 / WO26100812739 (Elimelech Weinreb, USB data copy, 79.97) looked unpaid in the operational store: two payment_transactions rows, one CANCELLED and one PENDING, zero SUCCEEDED. The owner asked "are you sure? did you check stripe?" - and the honest answer was no, I had read one store and called it a fact. Checked properly, the conclusion held, but only because four independent sources agreed:

1. STRIPE IS THE SOURCE OF TRUTH FOR CARD MONEY. The payment intent behind the pay link (pi_3UQYFyLnlgYj1gmT0KYUy56P) is `requires_payment_method` with amount_received 0 - the card form was never submitted. `charges/search` on metadata orderId returns nothing. Listing every charge since the order was created (44 of them) and reading amount + cardholder name is cheap and catches a mis-recorded counter sale.
2. OUR OWN payment_transactions IS NOT PROOF ON ITS OWN. `webhook_events` in the operational store holds TWO rows in total, newest 2026-10-12, while the Stripe endpoint (we_1UNzjALnlgYj1gmTorg8lhoB -> api.lakewoodphoneandtech.com/webhooks/stripe) is enabled. So a PENDING row means "nobody has reconciled it", not "no money arrived". Payment state moves only when the daily stripe-sync / lpt-record-payment runs. Any "he hasn't paid" claim that stops at this table can be wrong in the direction that loses the shop money.
3. NON-CARD MONEY LEAVES NO STRIPE TRACE, so it needs its own read: the business mailbox's bank notices (BofA/Chase "sent you") for Zelle, and accounting.db for anything reconciled. An absence in either is only meaningful if you also show that table is current - accounting.db's newest transaction date is the check.
4. The app store (lpt_prod on lpt-apps-01) is corroboration only; its orders froze 2026-09-14, so its silence proves nothing by itself and must be labelled as such.

Method worth keeping: before saying a customer has not paid, name which store answered, then ask what WRITES the field you are trusting. Here the writer is a nightly job, not the payment itself.

Second finding, same session: the pay text may never have been read, and the record can tell you that. The payment page shows viewCount 4 with lastViewedAt an hour BEFORE the message was sent and nothing since, and the SMS carries no delivery confirmation. "Customer ignored us" and "customer never saw it" are different sentences; the page-view timestamp is what separates them, and it changes the next action from a second text to a phone call.

Third: `lpt-write`, run on the authority itself, tried to ssh to host `secratary` - the frozen rollback anchor - because AUTHORITY_NAMES did not contain the authority's real hostname `secratary-cloud`. It only failed because no key is carried for that box. Fixed in commit de45f34 (lpt-ops), with the reason in the code.
