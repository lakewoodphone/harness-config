**L1672 · Read a Google Business Profile's full weekly hours without owner login -- render it, do not fetch it**

Google Maps place pages are JS-only: a plain HTTP GET of https://www.google.com/maps/place/?q=place_id:<id> contains ZERO hour tokens (verified 2026-09-15: 206 KB desktop-UA response, 0 hits for 10:30/17:30/openingHours; googlebot UA returned a 19 KB shell). Signed-out Chromium shows only the header ('Closed - Opens 10:30 AM Wed') plus TODAY's row -- the week table and even the 'Suggest new hours' editor are gated behind 'Sign in to get started'. Google Search SERPs are 429-blocked from this network entirely. WHAT WORKS: a JS-rendering scraper with a fresh fetch. firecrawl_scrape on the Maps place URL with maxAge:0 returned all seven days in one call (scrapeId 01a0a769-4644-776e-91d2-4d10a506fdca, 2026-09-15 ~23:32Z), plus the same 'Opens 10:30 AM Wed' header. Corroborate against the signed-out panel and against Bing Maps 'More hours' (also full week, read 23:30:08Z) -- two independent reads agreed exactly. Also: Yelp is unreadable from this fleet by every path (scripted 403, Chromium 403 on www and m., Firecrawl refuses the domain, Jina returns empty) -- do not burn time on it, ask the owner to read it. Same for Yellow Pages/Superpages/Hotfrog/Manta (403).
