**L2597 · origin/test moves under a fleet agent mid-run - all worktrees share one .git, and the diff vs origin/test is not your diff**

On 2026-09-25 the lpt-contact worktree was created from origin/test 6377943ac and pushed two commits. Later, 'git diff --name-only origin/test HEAD' listed 7 files: my 5, plus backend/scripts/create-waze-stripe-objects.mjs and e2e/personas/lib/journeys.ts, which I never touched. Cause: every worktree under one clone shares the same .git/refs/remotes, so another agent's 'git fetch origin' in ITS worktree advanced MY local origin/test (to 322c42cb6). The extra files were upstream-only changes, not mine. EVIDENCE COMMAND that settles it: 'git diff --name-only <your-base-sha> HEAD' (5 files, all mine) vs 'git diff --name-only <your-base-sha> origin/test' (the 2 upstream files). Use the recorded base sha for 'what did I change'; use origin/test only for the deliverable check it was asked for ('git diff --diff-filter=D --name-only origin/test HEAD' empty). Never conclude from the origin/test diff alone that you touched a forbidden path.
