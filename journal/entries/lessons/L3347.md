**L3347 · Shop tooling: MECHANIC CP14/CP14+ separator surface is silicone, not metal - clean with heat + plastic scraper, never abrade**

FACT (verified 2026-10-06 from vendor listings): the MECHANIC CP14 / CP14+ is a 14-inch (350 x 225 mm) LCD separator HEATING MAT, 150 W, 50-120 degC, **double-layer silicone heating plate**, green LED dust-detection backlight, USB 5 V output, 770 g, 38 mm thick. Sources: diyfixtool CPB/CP14+ listing (manual text), gsmserver.com.ua spec page, bemax.com.my spec page, all-spares.com listing.

CONSEQUENCE - the surface is NOT metal. Never sand it, never wire-brush it, never dig with a razor at an angle. The silicone is both the contact face and the light-transmitting face of the backlight; abrasion destroys backlight function, not just cosmetics.

Correct cleaning for burnt/melted polymer on it:
1. Power to max (120 degC), stabilise 10 min - the residue softens.
2. Scrape with a PLASTIC razor blade / wooden scraper held nearly flat. Not a metal blade.
3. Carbonised brown film: melamine sponge (Magic Eraser) damp with IPA, light pressure. Do not use it dry or hard.
4. Never: razor at an angle, sandpaper, wire brush, acetone soak (swells cured silicone), heat gun above ~200 degC, oven cleaner (does nothing to LDPE anyway).

THE FIX THAT PREVENTS A REPEAT for single-digit dollars: a PTFE (Teflon) cover sheet cut to size over the mat. The device touches the cover, never the mat; replace the cover when dirty. Thermal cost is negligible - 150 W over 0.07875 m2 = 1905 W/m2; 0.1 mm PTFE at k = 0.25 W/m.K gives dT = 1905 x 0.0001 / 0.25 = 0.76 degC.

Transfer test before believing residue is a problem: heat to ~80 degC, press a clean glass/screen protector on the stain, lift, look for a smudge.

THE "WARN ME WHEN IT IS DONE HEATING" FEATURE ALREADY EXISTS ON THE CP14+: its manual documents a settable countdown (double-click M) and audible "di" beeps on mode activation, with a phone mode (10-120 degC) and a tablet mode (10-90 degC). Do not buy a new unit for this. No unit in this class documents a "reached temperature" beeper; the MECHANIC 361 Mini+ documents an OVER-temperature alarm only.

OWNER-FACING VERDICT: do not throw it out. A stain on a silicone mat is cosmetic plus a possible transfer risk; a cover sheet removes both.
