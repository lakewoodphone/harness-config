**The two phone numbers he sees, and how to tell which one is broken (2026-10-19)**

He has exactly two numbers that text him, and conflating them has now cost two sessions:

- **AI line `+17324447361`** — shop Twilio account (`TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID`, ends 7e71b6), webhook
 `https://api.abletelsolutions.com/webhook/twilio/sms`. This is "you text me directly": CFO
 questions, owner_text gate, 20-minute rate floor, `owner_text_turn` ledger.
- **Family hub `+17328009174`** — AbleTel Twilio account (`FAMILY_CHAT_TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID`, ends
 c9f292), webhook `https://ai.abletelsolutions.com/webhook/family-chat/sms`. Every family message
 arrives from this number and is fanned out 1:1 by `app/family_chat/hub.py`. Its own DB is
 `/opt/company/data/family_chat.db`; its own cron watchdog is `/usr/local/bin/family-chat-watchdog.py`
 (`/var/log/family-chat-watchdog.log`).

**The entanglement, measured.** On 2026-10-18 01:20–01:37 `sms_log` 4280-4283 show the family leg
being judged by the owner-text gate: two questions to his wife suppressed by `rate_floor` ("we texted
him 9 minute(s) ago"), a third by `similar_hold`, and a family *delivery-failure notice to him*
suppressed by the same gate as class `family_chat_owner_leg`. Fixed the same morning: relay
`SEPARATE_CHANNELS` (`owner_text_relay.py`, L7870), `10ddcc1e4` (family_chat submits to no owner-text
relay, gate or turn), `d37bc845a` (a gate-held outbox row is retried, not destroyed), `ask-person.py`
(a directed question goes out the AI line, never the family hub — `sms_log` 4284 was the incident).

**How to answer "I am not getting the family chat" without guessing.** The carrier's own record is the
only source that can distinguish "we lost it" from "it was never sent":

 # every message the family account has, both directions, since a date
 /tmp/tw_acct.py # Messages.json?DateSent>=YYYY-MM-DD, PageSize=200
 # proof the inbound leg is alive right now, machine-to-machine, no human sees it:
 POST Messages.json To=+17328009174 From=+18482836621 Body="diagnostic"
 # then read data/family_chat.db messages: inbound row + "not a member" reply within seconds

Measured 2026-10-19 14:44 UTC: the probe came back in under a second (messages 175351/175352), so the
number, the tunnel, the signature check, the DB and the fan-out are all live. Twilio, however, held
**zero inbound** on that number after 2026-10-18 01:12:23 UTC (last family message 00:53:44
"MOOSE MASTER!" from Shabsi; daily usage 2026-10-19 = 0). So a missing family message is, this time,
not ours: nothing was held, queued or suppressed — there were no rows at all.

**Two traps.** (1) The AbleTel account is separate from the shop account; `reconcile-sms-log.py` had to
ask both, and asking the wrong one answers 404. (2) The family leg still writes its suppress/quiet-hours
rows into the shared `sms_log` with `agent_id='family_chat.owner_leg'`, and the direct-request burst
guard counts *all* `sms_log` outbound rows to the owner in the last hour — so family traffic still
consumes the AI line's burst ceiling even though it no longer asks its gate.
