**L7040 · An invariant sweep found 3,395.02 counted on TWO entities - the fourth P&L defect in a row that pct_clear could not see**

Measured 2026-10-16 on the CFO ledger by an invariant sweep rather than a coverage metric. Thirty charges on personal-chase-9680 are the same description, the same amount and the same DAY as thirty charges on lpt-chase-3929, and BOTH sides are counted in the statements - the shop as Cost of Goods Sold, the person as Business, Industrial, & Scientific Supplies Basic, which is the largest single block of that category on the personal books (76 rows, 7,052.33). Two charges of the same thing cannot both be a cost. The ledger duplicate machinery marks a copy when two imports carry the same charge, and here NEITHER side is marked - which is why no existing check noticed: the statements exclude marked copies, so the marked groups are harmless and the unmarked ones are invisible. Found by three mechanical invariants applied as queries, none of which asks whether a row HAS a category: refund-language rows in a revenue band (0), transfer-language rows in a P&L band (mostly Stripe payout credits, which are revenue by design - a false positive of my own test), and rows sharing description+amount+date across two entities landing in different bands (31 groups, 30 unmarked, 3,395.02). All thirty live on the unverified-entity card 9680, which turns the owner question Q1 from a curiosity into a priced item: its 120 rows total 26,646.21, of which 12,575.70 has no category, 9,541.49 is charged to his personal expenses on a guess, and 3,395.02 is counted on both entities - with 0 of the 120 rows marked as a duplicate of anything. A detector (cfo-crossentity-twins.py) now reports the class with exit codes so it can be run on a clock. The lesson, and it is the fourth instance in this session: coverage answers 'does this row have a category', never 'is the category right', so the checks worth running are the ones that test an INVARIANT - same charge counted twice, a credit in a spending bucket, a refund in revenue, a liability in expenses - because each of those finds a class of error that no percentage can.
