**L2357 · How to text the owner: send_sms (Twilio, owner-only, 2/day) and it auto-signs '- Daniel'**

Owner-SMS path, measured 2026-09-22: to text the owner, call the MCP action send_sms with to=+17325691594 (that is OWNER_PHONE_NUMBER in personal-secretary-mvp/.env) and body=<text>. Facts that matter before using it:
- It is Twilio and it is OWNER-ONLY. app/chat_action_runtime.py:_exec_send_sms refuses any recipient that is not the owner when settings.twilio_sms_owner_only is true, and tells you to use dialpad_send_sms for customers instead. So a wrong number fails safe rather than texting a stranger.
- Hard cap: 2 outbound owner SMS per UTC day (app/chat_action_runtime.py, counted from sms_log), plus near-duplicate suppression inside 10 minutes. Compose ONE message, do not send the same content twice.
- format_agent_sms_body() appends a signature, and on 2026-09-22 the delivered text ended with "\n\n- Daniel". That is the secretary persona's name, not mine and not the owner's. If the owner is reading the text with someone else, the signature is confusing - worth removing at the source (format_agent_sms_body) rather than explaining it each time.
- Verified delivery: action result {"action":"send_sms","success":true,"detail":"Sent to +17325691594"} plus a Twilio SID. Claiming a send without the SID is not evidence.
