**L12274 · Two fixes that passed their own gate and damaged the product: a word cap bought with unfinished sentences, and a health probe that 503'd real writes under load**

TWO FINDINGS from the Pass Six measurement (89c52f3), both created by fixes I shipped today. Recorded together because both are
"the fix passed its own gate and damaged the product".

## 1. The word-cap fix bought the cap with unfinished sentences
All five cards now measure exactly 105 words and ALL SEVEN QUALITY GATES PASS for the first time (transport.reportQuality =
"all 7 gates passed (0 violations)", violations []). The old 108/112/110/108 is gone. But the trimmer reaches 105 by cutting
words off the END of the longest touchable sentence, so 5 of 5 cards ship a field ending mid-clause:
 "...about how their days actually" (EA nextSteps)
 "...the halachic base you already hold is" (Kashrus nextSteps)
 "...a single week's task list" (Operations nextSteps)
 "...feels energizing or draining for" (Procurement nextSteps)
 "A community-facing kashrus role built on standards, detail" (Mashgiach description)
Four cards dangle on NEXT STEPS - the field a reader is meant to act on. `length` cannot see it: it counts words, not sentences.
This is intended stage-two behaviour per the docstring, i.e. the implementation did what it was told and the instruction was wrong.
FIX NEEDED: a cut must land on a sentence boundary, or the sentence must be dropped whole, or the field must be rewritten shorter -
never truncated mid-clause. Add a gate that fails on a card field with no terminal punctuation.

## 2. My Caddy health probe 503'd real user writes during synthesis (SECOND self-inflicted incident, same config)
Caddy log, 20:24:49-52: health_checker.active "HTTP request failed ... Get http://personality-test:3000/api/health?scope=liveness:
Client.Timeout exceeded" -> "no upstreams available" status 503 on PUT /api/persistence (Content-Length 255889) -> "host is up"
at 20:24:52.818. The app container's entire log has 30 lines and no 503; its last line is "report review: all 7 gates passed".
So: a single upstream + an active probe with health_timeout 2s. Idle probes measure 349-428 ms. During report synthesis the probe
exceeded 2 s (the synthesis occupies the event loop for ~16 s), Caddy marked the ONLY upstream down, and a real person's
persistence write got a bodiless 503. No data lost only because the client retried after recovery.
FIX APPLIED: removed the active probe from both personality vhosts entirely (19b053d). A liveness check that fails under
legitimate load is worse than no check: the trade it offers is "detect a hung app, at the cost of dropping live requests whenever
the app is busy". For a single upstream, Caddy's passive failure handling is the correct behaviour.

## Why both happened the same way
Each fix was verified against the gate it was written for, and both passed. Neither was verified against the thing a person
sees. The measurement run is what caught them, again - it is the only step in this whole loop that has ever caught a
user-visible regression that the test suite called green.
