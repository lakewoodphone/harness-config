**L1750 · Owner-facing research that lives only in a repo doc gets re-commissioned the next day: the always-read tier needs the answer, not just the path**

**Evidence (2026-09-16):** the owner asked on the web GUI, *"pull up the docs and info on getting a new
homeline for my mom powered by internet if it's cheaper"* - and then, one message later, *"we had a
whole session discussing this on my iphone dsh already, what happened to all that info, did it get
lost?"*

Nothing was lost. The 2026-09-15 iPhone session produced four files at
`personal-secretary-mvp/docs/network/home-phone-2026-09-15/` (README.md, options-research.md,
MAGICJACK.md, PORT.md), the lesson `L1466`, and owner-queue row `#36`. He asked a second time anyway,
because the research was filed in a repository doc that he never opens, and the *answer* was never left
anywhere a new session reads at startup.

**The rule this produces.** Filing a decision in a repo doc plus a journal entry is necessary and not
sufficient for owner-facing research, because he does not read documents and does not browse repos. A
fresh session must be able to answer his question from the always-read page alone. So: when research
answers a question the owner will ask again, the *finding* (the pick, the price, the one open decision)
also goes into a place `journal.py status` / `journal.py newest handoff` surfaces - not only into the
evidence folder. The evidence folder keeps the proof; the always-read tier keeps the answer.

**Corollary, measured:** searching the journal for the topic *he would name* ("homeline", "mom",
"home phone") found nothing, because the entry said "home phone" only in prose and "mom" only as
"mother". The lesson was reachable only by searching "landline" or "Ooma". Journal titles and bodies
should carry the owner's own vocabulary alongside the correct term.
