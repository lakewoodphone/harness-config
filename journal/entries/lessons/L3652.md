**L3652 · 49 was 20: the count that became a claim, and the payment link that does not exist**

2026-10-13. Follow-through on W2751, which reported "49 orders flagged CAPTURED with no payment row,
3,921.63". The amount survived; the order count did not, and the reason is worth keeping.

THE CORRECTION, measured against the live production store (store `operational`, the RDS
`d5oq83veii4rlb`) read-only:
 49 orders -> flagged CAPTURED, no SUCCEEDED payment row
 29 of 49 -> carry a ZERO total. Flagged "captured" with nothing to collect. Flag noise.
 **20 orders -> real amount, no payment row, totalling 3,921.63**
So the row I raised said 49 where the honest number is 20. Same money, wrong count, and a count is
what the reader uses to judge whether to care. #467 was resolved with the correction in writing and
replaced by #468.

THE 20, largest first: WO2605190007 1020.00, WO2602010001 650.00, WO2604210012 450.00,
WO2604260009 235.00, WO2606180004 200.00, WO2604210006 195.00, WO2607200010 150.00,
WO2604210112 125.00, WO2602020002 106.63, WO2607200011 100.00, WO2608100006 90.00,
WO2608310003 85.00, WO2603240005 80.00, WO2605250006 75.00, then four at 65.00 and three below.
Eighteen of the twenty sit in READY_FOR_PICKUP, which for this shop means done and paid.

THEN I TRIED TO SETTLE THEM AND COULD NOT, which turned out to be the finding.
 * `customers.stripeCustomerId`: **0 of 588 customers have one.** Every customer is anonymous to
 Stripe, so no charge can be attributed to a customer.
 * of 452 paid Stripe charges, only 34 carry a customer at all.
 * matching the 20 by customer NAME plus amount within 30 days matched **exactly one**
 (WO2605190007, 1020.00, "ezra faham").
So the honest state is not "these twenty did not pay" - it is **"the record cannot say"**, and those
are very different sentences. The absence of a link is the defect; the twenty orders are its symptom.
It also explains why the ~30 work-order questions in the owner's queue exist one order at a time:
nobody can look one up, so every one becomes a question.

THE GENERAL LESSON, and it is the same shape as the last three rounds. I reported a number I had
computed correctly for the question I asked ("how many have no payment row") and then used it to
answer a different one ("how much money is unaccounted for"). The zero-total rows were counted as
money because the query never looked at whether there was anything to collect. **When a count is
about to become a claim, re-read the predicate against the claim**: `no payment row` is not
`unaccounted money`, and 49 is not 20.

NOT DONE, and here it becomes an obligation rather than a note: the write-back duty in the
case-work rules says a case I have learned something about should carry a production note. The
rails for that (`lpt-write`, dry-run by default, audited, idempotency guard) are NOT installed on
the authority; only raw psql is. Editing customer records through raw SQL to satisfy a rule that
exists precisely to make writes audited would defeat it. The twenty notes belong in production once
the write path is reachable from this box, and this entry is the record that the obligation is open.
