## Apple's own numbers, and the fact that decides the job

- Apple publishes **no** out-of-warranty display price for the 14-inch M4. Its US estimator for
 "MacBook Pro (14-inch, M4, 2024)" returns only `Battery service $249` and `Other damage —` (inspection
 required). Anything quoting a firm Apple price for this part is inventing it.
- **AppleCare+ makes screen or enclosure damage a 99 service fee**, 299 for other accidental damage —
 `apple.com/legal/applecare/fees-deductibles/`.
- Therefore: when a machine this new shows a live warranty date, **whether that date is AppleCare+ is the
 first question, not the part price.** A 99 fee through Apple cannot be beaten by any quote we can
 build, and quoting first burns the customer's time and the shop's credibility.

## Egress traps — two of them, and they produce a false negative

From this estate's IP `71.104.140.242`, measured 2026-10-13:

- **laptopscreen.com returns HTTP 200 with an EMPTY body** on every path, to plain HTTP *and* to a real
 browser. It is not a 404 and not an interstitial you can wait out. **An empty 200 is not "no stock"** —
 read it through `https://r.jina.ai/<url>`, which renders the model page and its item list.
- **dvwarehouse.com and mobilesentrix.com return Cloudflare 403** to this IP even in a real browser;
 also read them through the jina proxy.
- **eBay challenges the browser after the first search**, so a used-condition-filtered pass does not run.
- The jina proxy **truncates laptopscreen at roughly 2.5 KB** — enough for the item list and its grades,
 not enough for a long page. Use it for the negative and the control, not as the only price read.

## The lesson, stated for the next session

A supplier that answers with an empty body is a **refusal**, not a negative — and the way to tell the two
apart is a **control page on the same site that does render**. Had the A3401 page been read alone, "no
items listed" would have looked like an out-of-stock part; the A2442 page rendering 2 items through the
identical path is what makes it a fact about the catalogue.
