**L4576 · The journal stamps entry dates from two different clocks: date-only kinds use LOCAL date, handoff uses UTC - they disagree across local midnight**

MEASURED 2026-10-15: the journal stamps its entry dates from TWO DIFFERENT CLOCKS depending on the entry kind, and the two disagree across the local-midnight boundary.

Evidence, from five entries appended in two batches on ZABZ-YOGA (local = America/New_York, UTC-4 on this date):
- L4512 (lessons) -> `2026-10-14`
- P4121 (pain) -> `2026-10-14`
- H4552 (handoff) -> `2026-10-15 02:58 UTC`
- D3722 (decisions) -> `2026-10-15`
- H4616 (handoff) -> `2026-10-15 14:07 UTC`

L4512, P4121 and H4552 were appended seconds apart at ~02:55-02:58 UTC. At that instant the LOCAL date was still 2026-10-14 (22:55 EDT) and the UTC date was already 2026-10-15. The handoff recorded the UTC date; the lessons and pain entries recorded the LOCAL date. So the date-only kinds resolve from local time and the handoff kind resolves from UTC.

WHY IT MATTERS, and it is not cosmetic: `--since` / `--until` filtering, `newest`, and any chronological reading of the journal compare these strings. An entry written at 02:58 UTC on the 15th is filed under the 14th, so it sorts BEFORE entries written hours earlier that same local evening, and a `--since 2026-10-15` query misses it entirely even though it is newer than everything it returns. On a fleet whose machines sit in more than one timezone this gets worse, because "the local date" is not the same question asked on two hosts.

WHAT TO DO: when an entry's date is load-bearing - an incident timeline, a before/after comparison, anything you are about to reason about chronologically - read the handoff form (which carries UTC) or check the file's mtime, and do NOT trust a date-only stamp to place an entry on the right day. When appending across local midnight, pass `--date` explicitly rather than letting the default pick a clock. I hit this while writing an incident document dated 2026-10-15 that references lessons and a pain dated 2026-10-14, written from the same session minutes apart.

NOT VERIFIED: whether this is intended behaviour for human-readable date-only entries or a defect. I measured the stamps and the times; I did not read the journal tool's date logic.
