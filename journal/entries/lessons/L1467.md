**L1467 · Resolve a phone number's real carrier with Twilio Lookup instead of trusting the MVNO paperwork**

**Evidence:** 2026-09-15, moving the owner's mother's house number. The shop's mailbox said a US Mobile
line was transferred to the **Warp** network (Verizon wholesale) — but that email was about a different
line. A read-only query against the owner's own Twilio account settled it in one call:

```
GET https://lookups.twilio.com/v2/PhoneNumbers/+1XXXXXXXXXX?Fields=line_type_intelligence
→ {"line_type_intelligence":{"carrier_name":"T-Mobile USA, Inc.","type":"mobile"}}
GET https://lookups.twilio.com/v1/PhoneNumbers/+1XXXXXXXXXX?Type=carrier # cross-check, agreed
```

Two endpoints, same answer: **T-Mobile**, type **mobile**. That turned a guess into a fact and reframed
the whole problem — the weak signal was T-Mobile coverage at that address, not US Mobile, and the
customer's "which network is it on?" question had a one-call answer all along. It also classifies the
port as the easy kind (mobile → VoIP) rather than a copper landline port.

**Rules this produces:**
- A number's current carrier is **measurable** — never infer it from which MVNO bills the customer, and
 never from an email that may be about a different line.
- `Fields=portability` on the v2 API returned **HTTP 400** on this account ("Portability" is a Twilio
 product integration, not a free field). So portability cannot be read for free — treat "portable" as
 **inferred from number type, not proven**, and say so.
- The lookup is read-only, costs a fraction of a cent, and touches no customer data. Use it before any
 port conversation.

months** (renewal $43/yr or $119/3yr), the port is $19.95, and the box is standalone now — router port
in, phone out, no computer (only the 2007 USB original needed a PC). Its terms allow termination with
**no refund** for "excessive use" (their example: calling more than 50 different numbers in a day) and
say porting *"may not be available for all numbers"*. Full write-up:
`personal-secretary-mvp/docs/network/home-phone-2026-09-15/MAGICJACK.md`.
