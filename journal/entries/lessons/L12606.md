**L12606 · Two false negatives in one session: UNRECORDED from a stale checkout, and 0-form-fields from pypdf on a filed 941**

## Two false negatives from two tools, in one session, both reported to the owner as fact

Measured 2026-10-21. Both were tools saying "nothing here" and me believing the tool about a document instead of reading the document.

### 1. `filing_register.py --status` said UNRECORDED for a return that was filed

Cause: it read the working-tree copy of the register (the authority checkout sits on `keepalive/323-pickup-live-restore-20261021`, whose copy has no NJ-927 entry and also claims `updated: 2026-10-21`), and `lookup()` required an EXACT form string so `NJ-927` could never match `NJ-927 + WR-30`. Fixed in commit `43a94ab02` (and cherry-picked to master lineage as `8792c509d`): token-wise form matching, provenance printed on every verdict, and master answers when the checkout is behind.

### 2. pypdf said "0 form fields", so I told the owner the downloaded 941 was a blank template

It was a **completed, signed, electronically-filed Q3 2026 Form 941**. The owner rendered the page and the difference was immediate. The filled values are **page content, not AcroForm fields** — `get_fields()` correctly returned zero and `extract_text()` correctly returned only the printed labels, and I read both correct answers as "the form is empty".

Read off the rendered pages: quarter box 3 checked; line 1 = 1; line 2 = 13,182.69; 5a 13,182.69 × 0.124 = 1,634.65; 5c 13,182.69 × 0.029 = 382.30; 5e = 2,016.95; line 7 = 0.01; lines 10/12/13 = 2,016.96; line 14 blank; Part 5 signed **Chintan Patel, Reporting Agent, 10/13/2026**; watermark `PAID ELECTRONICALLY / COPY`.

### The rule this proves

**A tool reporting "no fields", "no record" or "not found" is describing what the tool read, not what the document says.** Before telling the owner a form is blank or a filing is missing, render the artifact and look at it: for a PDF, get the page images, not the text layer. `pypdf` lives at `/tmp/pdfvenv/bin/python` on the authority (system python3 has no PDF library, and `pdftotext` is not installed) — but its text layer is not the document.

Same class, twice, same day: the second one is exactly the first one wearing a different tool.

### Gusto's screens answer different questions — read the right one

- **Taxes & compliance → Filings → Recent** = what Gusto **filed**. This was correct all along: `941 2026 Q3 Federal`, `NJ COMBINED SUI SIT 2026 Q2/Q3`, `NJ UNEMPLOYMENT 2026 Q2/Q3`.
- **Tax documents → Federal tab** = what Gusto will **supply**. "Nothing to show here — when you canceled your account, you told us not to handle 2026 tax filings" means *no blank forms for the owner to file*, **not** that the period went unfiled.
- **Tax documents → State tab** showed "Filing in progress" for Q3 while the underlying representations print `Date Filed 10/19/2026`. The UI status lags; the representation is the artifact.
