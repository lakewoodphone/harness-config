**L9984 · Investigation is delegated, the conversation never pauses**

The owner's rule, stated 2026-10-19 after the third time I stopped to investigate instead of moving:

"if there is work to do to figure this out you pass it off to a subagent to do teh heavy lifting
and move on to the next thing with me in teh meantime, you keep forgettting"

What that means in practice, for every queue session: the moment an item turns out to need
investigation rather than a yes or no -- which account, which commit, is it already built, why did
this fail -- the investigation leaves my hands. One `subagent` call (the MESH, never a local fork
while the mesh is reachable) with the full brief, in the background, and the very next thing I do is
`owner-queue.py card` for the next item. The owner is sitting there waiting; a session that goes
quiet for twenty minutes to read commits is a session that has stopped being useful to him.

Two failures of this shape in one evening. First the Hetzner account: I read the card, then started
checking for a key. Second the computer-finder: the owner said "there is already an agent working on
this", and I went off to inspect a worktree and an ssh route while he was still talking.

The rule also has a corollary he has said before: a subagent's result is a REPORT, not a decision.
When it lands, the finding goes on the row as evidence; the decision stays his.
