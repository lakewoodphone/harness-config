**L181 · Prose is not status - 56 of 57 pain entries prove nothing either way**

*Learned:* `journal.py state` lists **56 of 57** pain entries as open. That is not 56 live problems — it is
the measure of how little the record said. Searching the prose for a completion word found it in only 19 of
57 entries, and a line-initial `DONE`/`FIXED` in **1**. Status was never a field; it was a hope that a future
reader would infer it from sentences like "fixed at the API boundary".
*Rule:* status is an **event, not an adjective**. `state/status.tsv` is append-only — `journal.py resolve
<id> --status done --why "..."` writes a row — so the log stays immutable and the status is still a fact with
a date and a reason. Anything that must be *counted* has to be a field, never prose.
