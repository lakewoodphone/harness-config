**L7044 · The thrash cap is installed, unit-verified with a control, and has NEVER fired - and the whole-day repeat rate cannot tell me that**

Measured 2026-10-16 at 04:35Z on the CFO loop while verifying every mechanism end to end. The thrash was measured in round 24: 413 attempts over 237 distinct clusters in a day, 176 repeats, one cluster attempted seven times. The cap was installed in round 24 and unit-verified with a control in round 25 (a moved fingerprint at cap=1 is held back; the same row with the cap at 99 comes back due). What the verification round adds is the part I could easily have claimed falsely: park rows written since the cap went in 0; clusters carrying an attempts_by_day counter 0; shifts after the cap 2, both stopping with 'nothing to do: every cluster is covered by a rule', both parking nothing. So the counter has had nothing to record and the cap has had nothing to hold back. The whole-day repeat rate - 78 clusters attempted 2+ times, 15 at 5+, 2 at 7 - is NOT evidence about the cap, because 25 of that day's 27 shifts ran before the patch existed. A before/after split on the same day is also useless: BEFORE shows 0.74 repeats per distinct cluster over 25 shifts and AFTER shows zero attempts over 2, which is not a comparison, it is a period with no work in it. The number that would settle it is the repeat rate over a full day of post-patch shifts, and only tomorrow can give it. Two rules: 'installed and unit-verified' is not 'exercised', and an instrument that has never fired has no effect to measure - so state the trigger it needs and the period it needs, and read it then rather than implying the result from the installation. And when splitting a day into before/after, check that both halves contain work; a zero-turnover half is a gap in the data, not a null result.
