## Two plausible mechanisms for one customer symptom died on measurement in one evening - both were the one that "explained the words"

The Kramer WhatsApp case, 2026-10-13. The customer reports that the app *"randomly sends a peace symbol or
something as a reply to messages"*. Two candidates, both killed by a query:

1. **A corrupted reply pointer.** Plausible, explains the words exactly: `ZPARENTMESSAGE` is a plain integer FK
 and a wrong value would render a fragment of an old message as the quoted snippet. Measured across her
 source store, the store we injected and the store off her phone: **nine replies in her entire history, all
 intact, dangling 0, cross-chat 0, blank parent 0, parent-newer 0.**
2. **The app replaying one of her own old rows** — the allocator points inside the imported range, so it could
 pick up one of her messages and transmit it, carrying her original text *and* her original timestamp, which
 would explain the symbol AND the "sent a month ago" in one fault. Measured on her 17,662 messages: **exactly
 ONE message containing a peace sign (U+270C), and it is incoming.** Her outgoing symbol-only messages are
 😍 👍🏻 🎉 💕 🤍 🫶🏻 🥺 🥰 🙏🏻. And her whole store holds **4** outgoing rows in status 0, so there is no
 hidden outbox to fire from.

**The rule, and it is the whole point:** a mechanism that explains the customer's *words* is not a mechanism
that explains the *data*. Both of these read beautifully and both were false. One query each killed them, and
the second one cost nothing because it was a measurement instead of an argument.

**And the honest residue:** one symptom (messages drawn at the wrong position) is explained end to end; two
(symbol transmitted, notifications appearing and vanishing) are not, and no third guess gets written down. The
binding limit is that every artifact on hand is a snapshot from 20 minutes after the app was installed — the
symptom is the phone five days later, so the next artifact has to be a fresh backup of the phone.

Stores on `mac-mini-ts`; scripts `_scratch/kramer-reply-audit.py`, `kramer-symbol-audit.py`,
`kramer-peacecheck.py`, `kramer-info-audit.py`. Case file 2026-10-13 (night) section.
