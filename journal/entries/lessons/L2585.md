**L2585 · How the owner actually writes to a vendor - and the retraction was the spec, not an incident**

## L — How the owner actually writes to a vendor, and what he called "a fat stupid AI"

**His words, 2026-09-25, on a draft reply to 300DDR:** *"that was too long and complicated, that's not
how i respond to them that sounds like a fat stupid ai"*.

**The tell.** I wrote a three-paragraph email with a subject-line restatement, a money clause, a
shipping clause, and an apology paragraph that included the phrase *"for the time it has cost your
team."* He does not write like that, and — the part I missed — **the email he himself retracted as a
mistake the day before was written in exactly that register.** On 2026-09-24 a six-item numbered
questionnaire went to the lab and he apologised for it: *"Sorry about that - one of my employees sent
that email by mistake. Please ignore it."* I read that case file, called the questionnaire "not the
problem", and then wrote the reply in the same style. The correction was sitting in the evidence.

**What his real vendor email looks like, measured.** All 36 outbound messages between the shop and the
300DDR mailboxes, all time, read in full:
- Typical length: **one or two sentences.**
- Opens `Hi <name>,` or `Yes, <name>.`; closes `Thanks,` / `Best,` / `Sincerely,` then `Eliyahu`
 (or earlier, `Yisroel Weinberg / General Manager`).
- **No** numbered lists, **no** bullet points, **no** headings, **no** restating the subject line,
 **no** explaining why the request is reasonable, **no** naming of consequences, **no** apology
 padding.

Verbatim, his own:
- *"Yes, that is correct. Please remove the Signature Confirmation requirement. Thank you"*
- *"I will reach out in a few days. Sorry for the delay."*
- *"Hi Nathan, Thanks for the estimate. I'll fill out the mail-in form now. Does the fact that it has
 bitlocker change anything?"*
- *"Thanks for the great news on the SanDisk controller!"*
- *"hi Brian, Thanks for the update on ticket #73459. Please let me know the current status of the
 data recovery. sincerely"*

**The rule.** For anything going out under his name to a vendor: **ask for the thing in one sentence,
state the fact in one sentence, stop.** If a draft needs a list to be clear, the draft is too long —
cut it until it does not. Do not explain the reasoning, do not pre-empt objections, do not soften. The
only thing that may justify a second paragraph is a factual correction the other side needs.

**Corollary, and this is the transferable part.** When the owner has already retracted or apologised
for something, that thing is a **style instruction, not just an incident.** Read the retraction as the
spec. I had it in front of me and used it as background.
