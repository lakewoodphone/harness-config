**L3506 · On the authority config_sync is measured, not ported from autosync.sh**

On the authority the kernel's config_sync check must be MEASURED, not ported from autosync.sh.
autosync.sh/converge-authority.sh are office/consumer jobs and docs/mesh/140-PORT-CORE-AND-GAPS.md
A2 already verdicts converge-authority DEAD and "not ported". Three measured reasons not to port:
(1) autosync's default repo $HOME/harness-config does not exist on the authority;
(2) the authority's origin is /root/hc-main.bundle, a same-disk frozen bundle, and its configured
 @{u}=origin/main is stale (ee3a7e2f) - a fast-forward would move the source of truth backwards;
(3) no DSH engine on the authority reads ~/.dsh, so applying presets there is a no-op.
What the sentinel needs is a record, not an apply. /usr/local/bin/harness-config-sync-status.sh
writes /root/.harness-config-autosync/status.json by reading local HEAD and vault HEAD; it never
fetches, commits, merges, resets, applies or pushes. Both branches proven by hand:
HARNESS_STATE=/tmp/cs-probe HARNESS_REMOTE=origin -> result=attention converged=false;
HARNESS_REMOTE=vault -> result=clean converged=true, kernel check INFO "config converged 5s ago".
