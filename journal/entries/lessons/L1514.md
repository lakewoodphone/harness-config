**L1514 · Lenovo model ID from a bottom-cover label when the MTM label is not in the photo**

A Lenovo laptop's model can be identified from the bottom-cover label photo alone, even when the
machine-type (MTM) label is out of frame. Two independent identifiers did it for a customer's laptop
on 2026-09-15:

1. `Compliance ID: TP00007A` — the TP prefix means ThinkPad, and that ID is used on ThinkPad X201
 listings (found via web search for the exact string).
2. The cover-label part numbers `label P/N: 75Y6065` and `75Y4640` — parts resellers list those as the
 ThinkPad X201/X201i bottom case, FRU `60.CV417.001`.
3. Corroboration: `INPUT 20V 3.25A/4.5A` (65 W adapter / 90 W dock) matches that class, and
 `©COPYRIGHT 2005, 2010 LENOVO` dates the platform to 2010.

Note the trap: those two `label P/N` values are the number of the *printed sticker*, not of the
machine, so they are easy to dismiss — but resellers reuse them as the identifier for the whole bottom
cover, which makes them a usable proxy for the chassis. The `Made in China` / `Factory ID` / `CM-2`
marks carry no model information at all.

Method that worked: crop the photo into regions and upscale 2x before reading (the raw 1773x2364 photo
was legible but the fine print was not reliably readable). Then search the exact strings, including
the ones that look like noise.
