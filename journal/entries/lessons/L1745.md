**L1745 · NJ pre-insurance vehicle inspection: the failure mode is a suspension of collision+comprehensive, never a cancellation**


Owner's Plymouth Rock/Rider policy HPA70002908356 lost physical-damage coverage in mid-2026 and he read the refund cheque as a cancellation.

THE RULE (verified from primary sources, not summaries): N.J.A.C. 11:3-36.7(a) — if the pre-insurance inspection is not done by the end of the deferral period the insurer 'shall suspend automobile physical damage coverage'. (b) — it must mail a Notice of Suspension within 30 days and make a PRO-RATA premium refund or credit, sent within 45 days of the suspension effective date. 11:3-36.5(b)1 — 7 calendar days deferral on new business, calculated from the day after coverage starts. 11:3-36.7(d) — if the insurer never gave the required notice or never obtained the Appendix A acknowledgment, physical damage 'shall not be suspended' (failure to mail the Notice of Suspension does not restore coverage but exposes the insurer to a N.J.S.A. 17:33B-39 penalty). Physical damage = collision, comprehensive, fire and theft ONLY; liability, PIP and UM/UIM stay in force. Suspension continues until the car is inspected; reinstatement is effective AT the inspection, never backdated.

SO: a refund cheque plus 'you missed the inspection' means SUSPENSION, not cancellation. Tell the two apart before panicking: a suspension refund is ~the physical-damage premium (here $55 comp + $304 coll = $359 per 6-month term); a cancellation refund is the whole unearned premium. Also 11:3-36.4(b): the inspection must be WAIVED if the car is over 7 model years old or the insured has been with the same insurer/affiliate 4+ years — check that before assuming an inspection was owed.

Why it cost us: the May 2026 binding research package (53 KB Plymouth deep-dive + 62 KB decision doc + a 'Binding Script / Checklist') contains ZERO occurrences of the word 'inspection'. The gap was in how we bind a policy, not in the regulation.
