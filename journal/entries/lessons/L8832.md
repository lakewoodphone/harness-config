**L8832 · The 526 missing Copilot sessions are mostly oversized chats the extractor refuses by design**

Continues **L8831** (backfill, not a broken clock; 335 zabz-tech / 184 zabz-yoga / 7 mini; 374 of them Nov 2025 - Jan 2026).

Measured 2026-10-18 with the estate's own reader (\ ranscript-archive-server.py --read\), after wasting time reimplementing the object-store reader first:

- The archive holds **43,497 copilot-chat files, 26.9 GB**, and **107 exceed the extractor's 50 MB ceiling** — the largest are a **2.39 GB** \local-index.1.db\ (Copilot's own index, not a conversation), a **479 MB** chat \.json\, and two **~400 MB** chat \.jsonl\ files. \scode_chat_extractor.py\ skips these deliberately (\_MAX_JSONL_BYTES\); that is the documented behaviour, not a defect.
- The objects are **JSONL**, not single JSON documents. The reader works (rc=0), and the small ones it returns have \.requests: []\ — an empty session is a file, not a conversation.
- A full sweep of the 526 stalled on a **220 MB** session: at ~minutes per large file it is an hours-long job, not a measurement.

**So closing this needs a chunked parser for very large chats, and the value is low**: the DSH corpus (the thing the owner actually holds conversations in now) is complete at 8,623 of 8,637 ids, and Copilot use stopped on 18 September. Recorded as a known, bounded, deliberately-not-done item rather than an open wound.

**The mistake worth keeping:** I started by writing my own object-store reader (path by sha, zstd via CLI, JSON parse) and hit 513 'unreadable' files — when \ ranscript-archive-server.py --read\ already existed, was documented in the estate's own AGENTS.md, and handled it in one call. **Search for the existing reader before writing one.**
