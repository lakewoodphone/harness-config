**L9982 · One person, one thread: the queue clustered on words and lost two texts behind a ring**

A person who texts, calls and leaves a voicemail about one thing is ONE item, and the old
rule could not see it.

Measured 2026-10-19, the owner's own words: **"You shouldn't have given me the info about the
missed calls without the text."** Genack produced four rows inside three and a half hours —
#817 text "Does the Rosh hamoisad have a spare minute for me?", #818 text "I need info on Chaim
segall for my sister-in-law", #819 missed call at 14:04Z, #828 voicemail at 17:13Z. All four
were ingested correctly. The card put #828 at the top (critical=1000) and offered only #819 as
"probably the same matter", because `_siblings` required two rows to share **words**: `shared_e`
(the party) *and* at least two shared significant tokens of the title. A voicemail and a text
from one person share no words. So the two rows that carried the actual request were invisible
behind the one row that carried only a ring.

An earlier session that same day (zabz-yoga session b3ae51ec, 15:21Z) had already read a card
showing "personal message, no ask and no date — I need info on Chaim segall" and did not close
it. That is the same defect twice, which is why this is written down rather than remembered.

Two fixes, both verified on the live row set:

1. `owner-queue.py` `_siblings()` — **same party within 24 h is one matter, whatever the
 channel.** `_party_key()` normalises `Genack.` == `Genack` == `genack` and matches two phone
 numbers on their last ten digits; `_hours_apart()` reads the event stamp. Advisory only — it
 proposes `--with 817,818,819`, never merges, so a wrong proposal cannot hide an item. The
 card also prints a *text* sibling whole instead of truncating its title at 88 characters.
2. `owner-queue-ingest-gv.py` `RE_ASK` — "I need info on X" is a request. The pattern knew only
 `need you` / `need your`, so #818 was scored `low, personal message, no ask and no date` and
 sat below the fold. Added `\bi need\b`, `need (info|information|details|help|advice)`,
 `do you know`, `tell me`, `send me`, `any chance`, `i'd like`. Re-scored #818 to medium.

Verification, not assertion: `owner-queue.py card` now prints, under #828,
`add --with 817,818,819` with both texts in full; the regex is True on Genack's two texts and
False on "Thanks", "Ok", "Do not be a stranger".

**The transferable rule: cluster on the identity of the correspondent and the clock, not on the
wording.** Words are how two notices about one case are recognised; a *name plus a day* is how
one person's conversation is recognised. And: when a card shows a call or a voicemail, the words
that came with it belong on the same card — a ring is not a message.

One slip worth recording: I patched both files with `open(path,"w")` — truncate-and-rewrite —
while `lpt-owner-queue.timer` runs `owner-queue.py` on a schedule. That is AGENTS.md §8 exactly.
Nothing was mid-execution (`pgrep -af` empty), so no mixture ran, and I republished both by
`cp -p` + `mv` immediately afterwards (inodes 2081181→2081809 and 2165816→2081181). The patch
script should have done the rename itself; it now would.
