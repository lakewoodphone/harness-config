**L3720 · A long label job's own device report CAN catch state PRINTING - and it still is not proof of tape**

MEASURED 2026-10-12 on the QL-820NWB while printing the two Greenfeld labels from the office anchor.

The label skill says the READY -> PRINTING -> READY transition is unobservable and not to spend calls on it. That is true of the STATUS PAGE (60 s auto-refresh vs a ~1 s job) and it is NOT true of the tool's own device identification: print job 1 (Dell, 90.0 mm), then 4 seconds later job 2 identified the printer fresh and its report read `state PRINTING` - the state the device holds while feeding. So a LONG job, or simply the next job's own report, can catch it. Cheap, and worth knowing: it is real evidence the printer was physically working, which `instructions_sent: true` is not.

IT IS STILL NOT PROOF OF TAPE. PrintING means feeding, not that a label was cut and delivered. The honest proof remains the roll or a person. Do not upgrade this to "proved". Corollary worth keeping: it is another reason to print long labels rather than batching tiny ones if you want any signal at all.
