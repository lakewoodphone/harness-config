**L3666 · Read the recorded call before writing intake facts into production - the capacity was the owner's guess written as a measurement and the deposit was double-counted**

**Measured 2026-10-11 (Fisher SD card, WO26101112743).** The owner gave me four intake facts
verbally: name/phone, "SD card was formatted", "paid a $50 non-refundable deposit for diagnosis",
"if we succeed it's $100". I wrote them into production as device "64 GB SanDisk Ultra Plus" and
total **150**. **Both were wrong, and the shop's own telephone system held the correction the whole
time.**

The number had been called by the owner for 8 minutes that afternoon, and Dialpad held the
**recording and a full transcript** (call `6264034010374144`, 484.8 s, 4,472 chars, plus a
`dialpad_gpt` recap). In it:

- the owner asks *"and was the 64 gig?"* and the customer answers **"I don't remember"** — so 64 GB
 was never a fact, and writing it into `deviceInfo` made it read like one;
- the owner says *"it's $50 for an attempt that's gonna go towards $100"* — so **100 is the all-in
 success price and the deposit is credited, not added.** I had written 150 by adding them, and
 `orders."total"` is what a quote is read from;
- the customer says *"I didn't pay nothing"* — the deposit was taken **after** the call, in person,
 in cash;
- and the customer's own description of the failure — *"I put it in my new phone. So the phone start
 to delete the card. So I take it out right away"* — is a far better diagnosis input than
 "it was formatted", and an **interrupted** format is a good recovery candidate.

**The rule: when the customer's number is in the shop's phone system, the recorded call is the
record of truth, and it outranks anybody's later recollection. Fetch and read it BEFORE writing a
device description or a price into production.** It is one API call: `settings.dialpad_api_key` ->
`dialpad_service.get_transcript(token=..., call_id=...)` -> `_flatten_transcript_text()`, plus
`get_ai_recap()` for the GPT summary. Call ids are in `communications.contactPhone` /
`dialpad_call_full`. A call whose transcript cache row says `has_transcript=0` has no transcript —
that is not evidence the conversation did not happen, so fall back to the recording.

**Second-order rule this exposed:** a fact the owner states from memory must be written into
production as **unconfirmed**, never as a measurement. Note 3121 now supersedes the device line in
note 3119, and 3119 itself is untouched.
