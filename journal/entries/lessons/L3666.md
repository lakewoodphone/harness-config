**L3666 · Nearly reported 25,037.65 of duplicates; the real figure is 891.39 and duplicates are ruled out**

2026-10-13. I nearly reported 25,037.65 of duplicates. The real number is 891.39, and getting there
required reproducing the tool's flow exactly first.

THE METHOD FIX THAT MADE EVERYTHING ELSE POSSIBLE. `account_flow_cents` signs a movement by account
type - a debit is NEGATIVE on a bank account and POSITIVE on a credit card, because a card charge
increases what is owed - and the interval is half-open `(d1, d2]`. Applying that rule, my computed flow
for `personal-chase-9650` 2025-02-18..2025-03-18 came to **8,878.77, exactly the tool's chain
(887,877 cents)**. Three attempts before this one had produced numbers that did not reconcile, because
they summed raw `amount_cents` over an inclusive window. **Reproducing the tool's own arithmetic is the
gate that has to be passed before any case analysis means anything.**

THE FALSE ALARM, AND WHY IT WAS TEMPTING. Grouping by same account + same amount + same merchant and
requiring more than one live row produced this at the top:
 personal-checking 525.00 x9 chase credit crd autopay
 personal-checking 750.00 x8 chase credit crd autopay
 lpt-boa-checking 1035.42 x4 gusto
 escape101-amex 770.00 x3 autopay payment thank you
 lpt-chase-3929 101.67 x3 dialpad inc
and a total of **25,037.65 if every repeat were a duplicate**. Almost all of it is MONTHLY RECURRING
PAYMENTS - autopay, Gusto payroll, a Dialpad subscription - for which a repeat is the expected state and
not evidence of anything. Acting on that number would have deleted twenty-five thousand dollars of real
charges from the books. The pattern is only evidence when the interval is too short for a recurrence,
so the group has to include the DAY.

THE HONEST NUMBER. Same account + same amount + same merchant + SAME DAY, both live, over 100.00:
 lpt-boa-checking 455.00 x2 zelle payment to adeena 2025-09-16 | 2025-09-16
 personal-checking 233.81 x2 american express ach pmt 2024-03-22 | 2024-03-22
 personal-paypal 202.58 x2 walmart.com 2024-12-27 | 2024-12-27
**Three groups, 891.39 of excess - and the duplicate detector misses them even though the day is
identical.** That is a small, real defect in the detector and worth fixing on its own terms, but it is
0.0016 of the unreconciled total, so it is NOT the explanation for the 55,319.21.

WHAT THAT LEAVES. Duplicates are ruled out as the general cause, which sharpens the earlier
`both ends authoritative` split: when two statements of one account disagree with the transactions
between them and the transactions are not double-counted, the rows are MISSING. The recovery job is the
top ten month-pairs by statement diff, now with one alternative explanation struck off rather than
merely disfavoured.

AND THE CASE ITSELF, for honesty. `personal-chase-9650` 2025-03 has two charges of 2,481.72 at
`paypal reebelousai`, 25 days apart. That interval is normal, both rows are live, and the balance says
5,347.72 of the recorded flow is not there. So the pair is probably two genuine purchases and this case
is NOT explained by duplication either - it is one of the ten that needs its statement pulled.
