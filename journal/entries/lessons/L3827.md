**L3676 · A label's reader is the SECRETARY - it must let her find the job, know who and what, and know what to collect. The house format was already on disk and I designed my own instead**

**Owner, 2026-10-09:** *"i want the label to include the info, it's for my secretary to read the labels also so she needs this info."*

## What he was correcting

I printed a customer label containing **a name, a phone number and two prices** — and **no ticket number, no device, and nothing that identified the job.** A label whose reader is the person at the counter who has to *find* the job and *hand over* the right thing.

**The house format was already on disk**, as a label printed for another customer (`_labelprinter/out/ym_reitman_2652.png`):

```
LAKEWOOD PHONE & TECH
#2652 <- ticket number, BIG
YM Reitman 848-373-8293 <- customer and phone on ONE line
Kyocera Cadence <- the device
charging port fixed - NO CHARGE, job redo <- the instruction / what to collect
```

**When the shop has already printed the thing you are about to design, print it again — do not design it.** I invented a layout instead of looking at one that existed, and the shop's own file was three directories away in the same repo.

## The rules

1. **The label's reader is the secretary.** It must let her **find the job** (ticket number, big), **know who and what** (customer + phone on one line, the device), and **know what to do** (paid, nothing owed, ready for pickup, what to charge).
2. **The ticket number goes on it, big.** `lpt ticket 2269` resolves to the order; that number is how a job is found.
3. **Do not put money arithmetic on a label without the state it belongs to** — "PAID" or "NOTHING PAID YET", not a bare pair of figures.
4. **Layout facts that are easy to get wrong** (from `lpt_ops/label_printer.py`): with `--ticket` the positional `info` lines are **IGNORED** (it switches to `work_order_label()`); lines are **TRUNCATED, never wrapped**; the plain form takes **`--heading` AND `--barcode`**; long lines shrink rather than cut, so keep the load-bearing lines short.
5. **No barcodes** (owner, 2026-10-09) — the ticket number is the big heading and nothing is encoded.

Operator note for whoever prints next: `~/code/_labelprinter/journal/l5.md`, which carries all of the above plus the printer's own traps.

## The generalisable part

**A document generated for a human to act on has to contain what that human needs to act.** I optimised the label for "the customer's information is present" when the reader was the secretary and the requirement was "she can run the pickup from this tape alone". **Ask who reads the artefact and what they have to do with it, before deciding what goes on it.**
