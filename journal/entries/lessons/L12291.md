**L12291 · Card-field shape predicate is an ending check; verbless fields 9+ words or containing a comma are invisible to it**

MEASURED on production c3e22ce (2026-10-22), one complete assessment run, recordId session-1792711691033.

The card-field shape predicate tests whether a field ENDS like a sentence, not whether it HAS a predicate.
`cardFieldShapeProblem` -> `cardFieldStatesNothing` (report-quality.ts:2239) is the only arm that looks for a verb,
and it returns false when the field (a) is more than one sentence, (b) contains any of , ; : em-dash en-dash,
or (c) is outside 3-8 words (lines 2246-2258). Every other arm tests the field's ending.

Consequence: 3 of 4 card descriptions and 1 whyItFits were verbless and all four shipped unfiltered:
- "A community-facing role built on planning programs and logistics." (9 words, one over the ceiling)
- "A seasonal leadership role that leans on reputation and cultural fit inside a community." (14)
- "A community-facing role built on reputation and strong cultural fit." (10)
- "A leadership role combining outreach, logistics, and warmth with families." (10, has commas)
- "Your Artistic pull toward directed creative output, rather than open-ended improvisation." (11, comma, no verb at all)
`length` passed (length=true), refusedCardFields was empty/absent, nothing was cleared.

Second finding: `refusedCardFields` is built at report-shipping.ts:534 but carried by no payload. The shipped POST
schema (app/api/assess/shipped/route.ts:107) accepts recordId, participantId, verdict, qualityLine, report only, and
the name appears nowhere else in the repo. A refusal is therefore only ever visible as the qualityLine clause
("N card field(s) refused as not a sentence: ...") — never as structured data an operator can read.

Rule of thumb for the next pass: treat the predicate as an ENDING check. A sentence-shaped word at the end satisfies
it regardless of a missing verb, so never take `refusedCardFields: []` as evidence that the prose parses.
