**L3462 · An office tool pointed at the cloud's stale secretary.db stub prints 'nothing to ask' while 160 owner questions sit unreachable**

WHAT: Two office tools that talk to the owner's decision queue (bin/owner-queue.py, bin/owner-ask.py) default to /home/zabz/personal-secretary-mvp/data/secretary.db. On secratary-cloud that path resolves to /opt/company/data/secretary.db, which is a 1.8 MB stub of 145 tables with NO owner_decision_queue table. The failure is silent in the worst way: `owner-queue.py next` raises `no such table: owner_decision_queue`, but `owner-ask.py` prints `nothing to ask`.

EVIDENCE (2026-10-11): /root/authority.db has owner_decision_queue with 444 rows / 160 pending (oldest 2026-09-16). After `sed`-ing both defaults to /root/authority.db, `owner-queue.py stats` reports those 160 and `next` returns a full question with recommendation and source.

RULE: on the cloud authority the app database is /root/authority.db (DATABASE_URL=sqlite:////root/authority.db). Any office tool whose default DB path is the app data dir must be checked for that table before believing its "nothing to report".
