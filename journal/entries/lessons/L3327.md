**L3327 · AI in design deletes the commodity middle, not the trade - and AI-fluency is table stakes, not positioning**

QUESTION ANSWERED: "can AI be the way into design work so the job survives?" Answer: the tool deletes the commodity MIDDLE of the trade, not the trade. AI reliably helps novices most (NBER WP 31161: +13.8% overall, +35% for the least experienced; Noy & Zhang, Science 2023: 40% faster, 18% better, weakest gain most), and simultaneously the bottom is being deleted (Upwork open design posts: median budget USD 90, 50% under 100, only 12% entry-level; Fiverr active buyers -21.9% in a year and its own filing blames AI; graphic design/3D freelance demand -17% post-image-generators; AI-assisted contracts +90% volume at -13% per contract; entry-level tech hiring -65%/-76% vs 2019).

WHAT ACTUALLY WORKS, ranked by evidence: (1) accessibility/WCAG remediation - WebAIM Million 2026 (verified myself): 95.9% of the top million home pages fail WCAG, errors 56.1/page up 10.1%, complexity +14.3%, first reversal in six years, WebAIM blames AI-assisted "vibe coding"; DOJ moved ADA Title II to 2027/2028 and said automated remediation is not reliable. (2) AI-site repair - Freelancer.com 10,760 "AI fix/error/hallucination" posts Aug 2025-Jun 2026, +87%; a live vendor sells rescue from USD 499; caveat, the winners today are experienced designers because spotting errors is the skill. (3) e-commerce/Shopify conversion - storefronts average 75.1 accessibility errors (+33.9% vs average); Ecommerce Management the fastest-growing freelance skill at +130%. (4) AEO/GEO. NOT: "web design" as a title (UK median GBP 33,000, -34% YoY, on 12 adverts).

TWO FACTS THAT SHOULD STOP A FUTURE SESSION FROM REPEATING THE OBVIOUS MISTAKES. (a) AI-fluency is TABLE STAKES, not positioning: about half of surveyed Upwork talent say their last project was AI-related while only 16% of job posts mention AI, so clients assume it and pay no premium. Selling "I use AI" sells the one thing every entrant has. (b) AI makes novices CONFIDENT, not better, absent a human critic: METR's RCT had 16 experienced devs take 19% LONGER with AI while believing they were 20% faster. A beginner's self-assessment is worse. So critique is not a nice-to-have; it is the mechanism of improvement. Canonical case for shipping unaudited AI code: CVE-2025-48757 (verified on NVD, published 29 May 2025, MITRE CVSS 9.3 critical, 170+ Lovable projects exposed by a missing Row-Level-Security default; record marked disputed by the supplier).
