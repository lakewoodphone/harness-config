**L3327 · A render that reports success is not a render that is correct**

# Sign layout: a render that reports success is not a render that is correct

**Date:** 2026-10-05
**Project:** lpt-website / LPT door signage
**Pain:** P-sign-geometry

## What happened

I produced the Lakewood Phone & Tech door sign across ~13 revisions in one evening and handed the owner
a render **four times** with the same class of defect in it, which he had to find by eye each time:

1. Phone number running through the bottom of its band (band heights were shrunk but the contents kept
 their fixed offsets from the band top).
2. A "NOT FRIDAY" line pushed out of its band by the same cause.
3. The last line of body text sitting 27px above the footer, reading as a collision.
4. **Day separators drawn straight through "SUNDAY", "TUESDAY", "THURSDAY".**

His words, which are the point of this entry: *"You don't seem to be iterating and looking at your own
signs ... Why do you take a picture of your sign? Look at it and find the problems yourself."*

He is right. I was rendering, glancing at the thumbnail, and shipping.

## The measurement

On the shipped render, the separators were at rows 1115-1118 and 1267-1270, **inside** text bands
1050-1124 and 1202-1276. A rule and glyphs on the same scanlines. Not taste - geometry.

## The fix that matters: put the check in the generator

Nudging the separator 10px would have fixed *that* separator. The reason it happened is that the layout
had no way to know it was wrong. So the generator now records:

- `text_boxes` - every string, with its exact pixel box
- `graph_boxes` - every graphic element, with its box
- `check_collisions()` - runs **before** the file is written, and **raises** on any overlap

It is not advisory. The script cannot produce a sign that fails it. It earned its keep immediately: the
first corrected attempt derived the separator offset from row arithmetic and the check refused to write
the file, reporting a **1px** overlap with the SUNDAY glyph box. The same class of defect he had caught
by eye, caught by the machine, before anyone looked at it.

The separators now sit midway between the *measured* glyph boxes of adjacent rows - equal clearances by
construction, verified 38px above and below by the internal check **and** by an independent pixel scan
of the finished PNG.

## The durable lessons

1. **A successful render proves nothing about the render.** `img.save()` returning is not evidence. Four
 separate defects shipped behind a green exit code. When the artefact is visual, the check must be
 geometric, or a human must look - and if a human must look, not shipping is the default.
2. **Assert the geometry, do not eyeball it.** Any generated artefact with overlapping elements (signs,
 invoices, labels, PDFs, dashboards) should record its element boxes and refuse to emit on collision.
 This is cheap and it generalises.
3. **Do not hand the owner a render to critique.** He said it directly. If I am about to ask "does this
 look right", the correct move is to look harder first, or to measure.
4. **Fixed offsets from a container's top edge are a bug generator.** When a box is resized, contents
 pinned to its top fall out of the bottom. Derive padding from content and centre blocks in their
 container. This single mistake caused defects 1, 2 and 3 above.
