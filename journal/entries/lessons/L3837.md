**L3837 · Voice input on the Waze units: keyboard dictation WORKS on DRN 2005 (on-device, radios off); Waze's own mic fails and holds no Speech Recognition grant; the blocked CNAME never mattered**

**Voice input on the Waze units, 2026-10-15 — the state that should not have to be rediscovered.**

On **DRN 2005** (`F4GD2ZAAN72K`, sold unit, iOS 26.5.2), established by the owner's own hands:

- **WORKS: the Apple keyboard microphone.** Enable it from **Settings** with Waze force-quit (the "Enable Dictation" prompt sticks there and is torn down inside Waze — H1, now a field fact, not a hypothesis). Then, **with airplane mode on and no network**, dictation types text into Waze's own search field. On-device, nothing sent, nothing for the filter to refuse.
- **FAILS: Waze's own microphone button.** Not the microphone grant (Waze is listed under Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and ON). Not the network (fails in airplane mode; and across the tap window the DoH filter logged **zero** lookups of `guzzoni`/`smoot`/any speech host — an app refused a host asks for it, and Waze asked for nothing).
- **Open candidate:** `SFSpeechRecognizer` authorization. The **Speech Recognition pane is empty — no apps listed at all** on that device, so Waze holds no speech-recognition grant. Next experiment: reboot → open Waze → tap its own mic immediately → did any prompt appear, and did Waze then appear in that pane?
- **Blocker that turned out not to matter:** the blocked `updates.g.aaplimg.com` CNAME. `updates.cdn-apple.com` is allowed and returns A records; dictation provisioned with **no allowlist, PAC or profile change**, at a steady ~3 lookups/min with no burst when it was enabled. Do not buy the allowlist widening on that theory.
- **Fleet recipe:** force-quit Waze → Settings → tap the mic in the search field → accept → keyboard dictation works, in Waze too. Two minutes per unit. DRN 2005 is one of eight.
