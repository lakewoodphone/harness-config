**L1955 · For a job that turns on one property, the aggregate star rating is not evidence - read the aspect breakdown**

**The rule.** When a purchase or a part choice depends on **one specific physical property**, the **aggregate star rating is not evidence about that property** — read Amazon's own **aspect breakdown** (*Connectivity*, *Stability*, *Durability*, *Overheating*) and its quoted review lines. A product can be 4.4★ across 1,371 ratings and still be the wrong part.

**Measured 2026-09-17, and it inverted a recommendation.**
Job: an Acer Aspire (N20C5) whose complaint was **a charging plug that would not grip**. The cord needed exactly one property: a tip that seats and holds.

| Listing | Aggregate | The aspect that mattered |
|---|---|---|
| **ROLADA 65W, 3.0x1.1mm, $17.99** | **4.4★ / 1,371 ratings** — ten times the review count of the alternative, and marketing that literally says *"snug fit clip prevents falling out"* | **Connectivity 5 positive / 23 NEGATIVE. Stability 2 positive / 10 negative.** *"The adapter doesn't stay in the charge port… comes out very easily."* *"…loose and only works in certain positions, so I often have to prop it up."* *"You have to constantly wiggle it. It loses connection easily."* |
| **Smsutu 65W, 3.0x1.1mm, $19.99** | 4.5★ / 151 ratings | Printed **all four** facts on the page — 19 V, 65 W / 3.42 A, tip **3.0×1.1 mm**, and "**Not fit 5.5×1.7mm**". Amazon-fulfilled, free returns. |

**So the highest-rated, cheapest, most-reviewed option was the wrong one, and its own marketing claimed the exact property its own reviews refute.** Marketing is a claim; the aspect breakdown is evidence. **They disagreed, and the evidence won.**

**How to apply it.** Before recommending a part for a job, name the **one property the job turns on** (grip, capacity, tip diameter, sealing, bootloader unlockability) and then look for the review aspect that measures *that*. A 4.4★ product with 23 negative connectivity reviews is a 1.5★ product for a connectivity job. Ask the reverse too: is the property **even measured** in the breakdown? If not, the rating says nothing about it.

**And the second-order lesson, which is the one that actually changed the shop's plan.** A **generic replacement cannot serve as the control** for a fault in the same category as the generics' known weakness. Fitting a cheap cord into a damaged port and finding it still loose proves **nothing** — the new cord may simply be as loose as the old one. That is the same rule as `L1954`/the SD-reader gate (*no device condemned without a control watched working on the same path*), applied to parts instead of readers: **the reference item must be of a class whose quality is not itself in question — for this job, a genuine Acer brick, not a $18 generic.**
