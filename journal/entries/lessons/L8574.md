**L8574 · A scheduled tool pointed at a missing path reports health: '0 items filed' is not 'nothing to file'**

Measured 2026-10-18 on secratary-cloud. work-integrator.py ran every 30 minutes for 27 runs and printed "0 integration item(s) filed within 21 days" every time, while kosher-filter-ai held 72 unmerged branches and origin/main had not moved since 2026-10-15. Cause: its registry repo path /home/zabz/repos/kosher-filter-ai did not exist on the authority, and the guard was a bare `continue` - no report row, no stderr. A second defect stacked on it: its branch filter still matched agent|kf*|integrate|rescue while the fleet had moved to item-<n>-<slug>, so 21 of the 72 refs were invisible even with the repo present. Third: by design it only files items and "the merge is a shift's job", so with nothing filed no shift was ever handed a merge.

THE RULE: any source, guard or scheduled tool that can skip a subject must SAY it skipped and why, and its output must separate "nothing to do" from "I could not look" (a REFUSED line, exit 4). Silence must mean healthy, never mean absent input. Applied: cloned the repo at the registered path, widened the filter, made no-repo loud, and added bin/sources/wake-progress.py so a fleet that stops producing for 6 hours files exactly one ledger item instead of looking healthy.

Cost of the gap, measured: 10 ledger titles were completed TWICE in three days (22 done rows) because the first completion never landed, so roadmap-supply refiled the unchecked box and a second shift redid the item.

Side finding, same session: `journal.py` WITHOUT --no-fetch hangs here (46 s with it, >10 min without) because the two-URL diverged origin stalls; use --no-fetch on this checkout.
