# The owner's own instructions were in the transcript and were not being read

**Rule.** Before designing a fix for "the system annoys me", grep his **own past messages** for the
instruction he already gave. Two were sitting in the store, in plain text, and both were being ignored:

- 2026-09-25T15:26:40Z — *"The text you are sending me have way too much information at once. I don't
 read them. You only should text me when you have like a question one at a time for me to answer and
 I'll answer it."* — stored **`state='ignored'`**. After it: 231 more outbound, 63 over 320 chars.
- 2026-05-05T15:42:21Z — *"I thought we're stopping with the morning and evening briefings by text"* —
 and 288 briefing-shaped attempts followed in the last 30 days.

The briefing one had a mechanical cause worth remembering: the flag was read from a **single source**
(`getattr(self._settings, "owner_briefings_enabled", False)`), and a stale `settings_overrides` row could
hold it True while `.env` said False. When an off-switch "does not work", count the **readers** of the
switch before doubting the switch.
