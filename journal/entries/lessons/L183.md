**L183 · A missing trailing newline glues a marker onto the line before it and merges two entries**

*Learned:* a shard whose last line had no trailing newline produced `---<!-- e:pain|P56|... -->`. The marker no
longer started its line, so the parser stopped seeing that entry and folded its text into the **previous**
entry's body — P55 silently grew from 4,161 to 7,313 characters and the duplicate disappeared from every
count. Nothing complained: the index was still generated, no error was raised, and only a "source grew"
comparison revealed it.
*Rule:* a writer must guarantee its file ends with a newline before appending (`append_block` now does), and
the reader must be able to fail: `journal.py check` flags any `<!-- e:` that does not start its line. A format
whose corruption is invisible to every counter is not a format, it is a trap.
