**L3605 · A 302 proves nothing: audit the FINAL hop and read the destination's own log**

**The rule.** When you audit a public door, a `302 Found` from hop 1 proves **nothing** about whether the
surface works. Only the FINAL hop's body, and the serving machine's own log line for your request, count.

**How it was found, 2026-10-11.** `https://ai.abletelsolutions.com/phone` (the link on the owner's iPhone
home screen) returns `302 Found` → `https://zabz-yoga-1.tail93e6e6.ts.net/`. That is the owner's **laptop**,
not the authority. The authority's own tailnet name (`secratary-cloud.tail93e6e6.ts.net`) has *no listener
at all* (`tailscale serve status` = `No serve config`). So the phone app works only while the laptop is
powered, awake and on the tapnet — and if it is off, hop 1 still answers `302 Found`, cheerfully pointing
at a machine that is not there. Every status-only check passes while the surface is dead.

**The three tests that make it visible:**
1. `curl -sL -o body.html -w '%{url_effective} %{http_code}' <url>` and **read the final body**, not the
 first status line. The final URL tells you *which machine* the user actually lands on.
2. Ask the serving machine to identify itself: `systemctl show <unit> -p ExecMainPID`, `ss -ltnp`,
 `tailscale serve status`. The port a redirect names is not the port that is listening.
3. Read **the destination's own request log** for your request (the gate's log named my client IP and the
 byte count it returned). A log line naming your request is the difference between "I got a 200" and
 "the machine I think served it did".

**Corollary — an allowlist is only proven by a refusal.** The phone gate refused
`secratary-cloud (100.73.176.126)` with "not in phone-gate-allow.txt" while admitting the owner's iPhone.
The 403 is the evidence that the list is enforced rather than decorative; a 200 alone would not have been.

**Also measured:** real phones *do* reach it — the gate log shows `iphone-15-pro` (100.85.105.93) signed in
at 2026-10-05T22:39:21Z, and `iphone-11-1` and the shop iPad have never made a single request in the log's
entire history. Usage is a separate question from reachability.
