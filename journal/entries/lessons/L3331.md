**L3331 · Nine sellable services ranked against design: accessibility wins, plain web design is near the bottom**

Published vendor benchmark (Accessible.org, updated Jul 2026): 100-250/primary page, "most audits cost between 1,250 and 2,750", entry-level auditor 75-110/hr. Litigation-enforced: 3,117 federal website-accessibility suits in 2025, +27%, e-commerce ~70% of targets. 2. AEO/GEO 20 (but 52% of ChatGPT #1 rankings rotate - the measurement science is not stable). 3. EMAIL MARKETING/KLAVIYO 19 - the better pick for a firmly non-technical person: free HubSpot and Klaviyo credentials, free sandbox, no code, and Klaviyo's own benchmark that flows are 5.3% of sends but ~41% of email revenue. 4. E-COMMERCE MGMT 14. 8. GRAPHIC DESIGN 12 (the incumbent). 9. WEB DESIGN 11. 10. PRINT/SIGN PRODUCTION 8.

THREE CORRECTIONS A FUTURE SESSION SHOULD NOT RE-MAKE: (a) Upwork's "+130% e-commerce management" is real but filed under CUSTOMER SERVICE & ADMIN, and it is #1 by growth while only #8 by volume in its category; (b) "presentation design ranks high by freelancer earnings" is a WITHIN-CATEGORY rank (#3 of 10), not a cross-category earnings table, and the viral $145/hour story is about graphic design; (c) the big US accessibility deadlines were EXTENDED in April/May 2026 (ADA Title II to 2027/2028, HHS 504 to May 2027), so "the deadline has passed" is wrong - the live driver is litigation. Also: no dated sourced estimate of time-to-first-paying-client exists for ANY of the nine services.
