**L3377 · table-fixed ignores the share of a display:none column - the denominator must be per-breakpoint**

A `table-fixed` table does NOT redistribute the declared share of a `display: none` column.

MEASURED 2026-10-08, LPT work-orders table at 1024x768: the three `xl`-only columns held
222/1003 of the declared shares and were `display: none` at `lg`. The table's box was 720px,
its cells ended at x=841, the table's right edge was x=1000 - 159px of the table rendered as
nothing, while `Total` had 60px and clipped even an ordinary 340-dollar price.

Chrome scales columns UP to fill the table only when the declared shares sum to LESS than
100%. A share belonging to a hidden column is simply lost. So the denominator must be the sum
of the slots the CURRENT breakpoint renders, not all of them - and because a breakpoint cannot
be read from an inline style, it has to live in CSS (`--wo-den`) with the width written as
`calc(<slot> / var(--wo-den) * 100%)`.

Two numbers in two languages drift, so pin them: a test reads the CSS and asserts it equals
the sums the component computes.

AUTHORITY: this corrects a claim that was in the repo as a comment WITH a side-by-side probe
behind it. The probe was right; it just had no `display: none` column in it. A probe that does
not contain the case under test proves nothing about that case.
