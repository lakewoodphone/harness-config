**L3616 · Finding a session by name fails when the record calls the person 'Uncle' - search content, not the name**

**L (2026-10-12, secratary-cloud)** - The owner asked for "the session about Uncle Nosson and the audio clips he sent". `Nosson` appears in **no** session text anywhere: grep across all 529 local sessions, all 6,433 archived session objects (30 GB decompressed) and the comms index returned nothing for nosson/nussen/nosan. The session exists and I found it in minutes once I searched the right things.

What the record actually contains, and what finds it:
- the screenshot in the session shows Messages contact **"Uncle"** (not a name) - so `uncle` + `audio clip` in the same session is the durable handle;
- the audio arrived as **MMS to the secretary line (732) 444-7361** from the owner's cell 8483897895, so `sms_media` / `audio/amr` / the MessageSids find it;
- `ps_comms_search "uncle"` matched two unrelated calls; `ps_comms_person 8483897895` is what identified the number as the owner's.

The names live only in the *directory* the later work was filed into (`noson-yanofsky-profile`), not in the conversation. **When a search for a person's name comes back empty, do not conclude the session is absent** - search the artefact (media type, phone number, MessageSid, the label the human used) instead, and search the session store, not just the journal and comms.

Evidence: session `4ae943f5-3163-4654-9e29-5e0bd78e6fb3` (+ its continuation `0f3ae2f2-2267-4911-8d80-27edf9088483`), secratary, 2026-10-10 23:52 -> 2026-10-11 00:37 UTC; `~/evidence/owner-mms-2026-10-11T001236Z/TRANSCRIPTS.md`; archive objects 207bc6da (session) and 20-rel_path `sessions/--home-zabz-phone--/session-0f3ae2f2-...`.

Search tool written for it: `/root/_scratch/tssearch.py` (decompresses .zst/.zstd one child per file, so a hit names the file). Plain `zstdgrep -- */*.zst` fails with exit 126 (arg list too long) and the python `zstandard` module cannot be installed here (PEP 668).
