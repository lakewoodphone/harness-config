**L · The owner's iPhone dictation cannot be fixed by an iOS update — the good engine is gated at 12 GB of RAM**

STATE, verified 2026-10-13 against Apple's own pages: his phone is an **iPhone 15 Pro (A17 Pro, 8 GB)**.
Apple's iOS 27 dictation engine ("Advanced Dictation", a ~20-billion-parameter on-device model, preferred
44.7% to 17.6% over the old system in Apple's own human evaluation) ships only on **iPhone 17 Pro / 17 Pro
Max / iPhone Air** — the gate is **12 GB of RAM**, and Apple's iOS 27 feature page still reads "iPhone Air
and iPhone 17 Pro models and later". **No change is coming**: no Apple statement, no credible report of a
staged rollout for the 15 Pro, 16 Pro or standard 17. It is also **off by default** even where it exists
(Settings ▸ General ▸ Keyboard ▸ Dictation ▸ Advanced Dictation).

**iOS 27.0.1 does NOT fix bad dictation.** Its release notes contain exactly three fixes — iPhone 18 Pro
Face ID restarts, a 2× photo colour artifact, and an unresponsive touchscreen — and its security page says
"no published CVE entries". There is no 27.0.2. Users were still reporting the disappearing-words bug on
29-30 September, the day after it shipped. Fixes discussed in the same MacRumors thread actually landed in
the **27.2 public beta**, and 27.2's developer notes contain no dictation entries at all.

THE DURABLE FACT, so nobody researches this again: **do not answer "update your phone" to any owner
dictation complaint.** The answer is the engine, and the engine is hardware.

WHY THE SYMPTOM LOOKS LIKE HIS FAULT AND IS NOT: dictation runs on the model that powered Siri — Apple's
own WWDC session describes it as working "well for short-form dictation". The live pass types words; a
second pass rewrites them; that second pass is where words vanish. It is the single most-reported
dictation defect of 2026 and Apple has said nothing since February 2025.

AND WHY NO APP CAN BE FRICTIONLESS EITHER: iOS forbids a keyboard extension from opening the microphone,
so every voice keyboard hops out to its own app and back; iOS 26.4 removed the automatic return and
26.5's toolbar covers custom keyboards. Every vendor pays the same tax. **No independent head-to-head
accuracy test of these apps exists** — every number in that market is a vendor's own.
