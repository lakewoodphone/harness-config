**L7102 · Two engines each waiting for the other: the writer withheld 5 rules, the shift called their clusters covered, and 11 rows sat in limbo**

Measured and fixed 2026-10-16 on the CFO loop. cfo-resolve.py's --apply-rules reported five rules HELD by conflict and wrote nothing: held_by_conflicts CR-a533442c9fd1 x3, CR-c9a6225a1125 x5, CR-796bef623a84 x1, CR-a85a7046f2a5 x1, CR-57408a21cc83 x1 = 11 rows withheld. A held rule refuses to categorise a row that has NO category, because some OTHER row in the same cluster disagrees with the rule - and the guard's intent, never overwrite, does not apply to a row with nothing to overwrite. The effect was a deadlock with no error anywhere: the writer would not write those rows, and the shift called their clusters 'covered by a rule' and would not research them either. Two engines, each waiting for the other, on five clusters whose keys are all far too broad to carry one rule in the first place (amazon 574 active rows across 22 categories, stripe 757, twilio 366, paypal transaction 381). THE FIX CAME FROM ASKING WHICH END WAS WRONG: the guard is right, because a rule whose cluster contradicts it is a rule to review - the defect was that the SHIFT could not see the writer's verdict. So the writer now publishes its held set to /root/.cfo/rules-held.json (rule ids, counts, and the patterns they match, written atomically) and the shift's rule_index drops those patterns from coverage, printing what it dropped. The shift does not re-derive the condition; it reads the writer's answer, so there is still one source of truth. Measured after: the shift chose 7 of 9 clusters where those clusters had been skipped, resolved 6, retired 13 transactions, and moved the scoreboard 44 to 31 unexplained while pct_clear went 99.42 to 99.54. And this closes a thread that ran three rounds: round 18 measured a version and fixed the field order, round 22 claimed dead rules blocked nothing and had to be corrected by measurement, and the REAL instance was live rules the writer had withheld - not dead ones. The lesson: when two components disagree about whether work is done, find which one holds the authoritative verdict and let the other read it, rather than teaching either to re-derive it; a guard that is right can still create a deadlock if the party that skips the work cannot see it.
