**L3698 · Clause 2's match split: 351 to cards, 130 to Amazon's own feed - and the second is better evidence**

2026-10-13. Clause 2 says "matched to card charges". Measured precisely, 73 per cent are, and the other 27
per cent are matched to something better - so the number needs saying with its split.

THE MEASUREMENT, from `transaction_evidence` joined to the accounts:

The sync reports `matched: 514`, and it reconciles: 482 existing evidence rows plus 32 written on the last
full pass, of which my query's reference pattern catches 481 - the missing one has a different reference
format, which is the third format this table is known to carry.

IS THE 27 PER CENT A DEFECT? No, and calling it one would be wrong. The 130 are all on ONE account,
`personal-amazon-business-transactions`, which is Amazon's own transaction feed - the marketplace telling us
directly which of its orders were paid and when. **That is better evidence than a card line, not worse: a
card charge says "107.43 left an account", while the feed says "this order was paid".** So clause 2's
"matched to card charges" is satisfied at 73 per cent by cards and exceeded at 27 per cent by something
firmer, and a reader quoting "514 matched" should say which.

WHY RECORD IT AT ALL, given that nothing is broken. Because the objective names card charges, and a future
session measuring progress against that wording would either count 514 and overstate the card work or count
351 and wonder what happened to the rest. **A number that answers a question other than the one asked is
worse than no number** - the same reason the reconciliation now reports 68 disagreements AND
`disagreements_paired: 6`, and the same reason the freshness source alarms on the verdict AND on the pull
state. Every figure tonight that turned out to matter needed its split beside it.
