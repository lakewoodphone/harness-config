## L· Cheapest self-service US prepaid lines for disposable phone-test SIMs (researched 2026-10-19)

**The problem as he stated it.** He uses prepaid SIMs to test repaired/flipped phones, loses them constantly, and every replacement means US Mobile support, security questions and re-verification. On 2026-10-19 he narrowed the requirement to: cheapest possible *activation*, self-service, and lose it without losing much money.

**The structural answer, which is provider-independent.** The security questions are the SIM-swap anti-fraud check. Any carrier that moves an existing number onto a new SIM must verify the person asking. So the fix is to stop carrying a number on test SIMs at all: keep unactivated kits, activate a fresh line per phone, let the old line expire. Nothing to cancel, nobody to convince.

**Read directly on SpeedTalk's own site, 2026-10-19** (the only provider whose pages I could actually load — Tello, US Mobile, Ultra Mobile, H2O, Red Pocket and Tracfone all answered HTTP 403 Cloudflare to every fetch, including through Playwright and r.jina.ai):

- `speedtalkmobile.com/plans/`: 5.00/month for 100 minutes, 100 texts, 100MB; 7.50 for 250/250/250; 9.00 for 500/500/1GB. Page carries "No contracts! No Credit Checks!"
- `speedtalkmobile.com/sim-cards/activate-mobile-sim-cards/`: activation is a web form at activate.speedtalkmobile.com — 19-digit SIM number, shipping ZIP, then email and contact phone. No ID, no SSN, no agent.
- `speedtalkmobile.com/faq/`: *"I lost my SIM card. How can I replace it? We will send you a new SIM card free of charge, You will only have to pay for shipping. Go HERE and enter the phone number that is associated with the lost SIM card"* — self-service, no verification, days of shipping.
- `speedtalkmobile.com/faq/`: *"you can use any unlocked GSM phone"*; unsupported devices still activate, just with less support.
- `speedtalkmobile.com/terms-and-conditions/`: "TRUE PREPAID Plans that will auto renew with the same plan when any of the allotted service is consumed"; all sales final after 14 days or 30 min/30 text/30MB; all devices must support VoLTE.

**What I could NOT verify today** (do not repeat these as fact without reading them): Ultra Mobile PayGo's own page (its title, surfaced through search results, reads "Talk, text and data for $3/mo"), its kit price or where the kit is sold; Tello's minimum custom plan and replacement-SIM policy; H2O's pay-as-you-go terms; Red Pocket's eBay-annual loss exposure.

**The trap in the cheap end.** Annual/multi-month prepay (Red Pocket at 2.50 via eBay) is the cheapest headline and the worst shape for a man who loses SIMs: losing the card forfeits the rest of the year. Verizon-network MVNOs (mobileX 1.99, Page Plus 2.54) run an IMEI compatibility check, which rejects random unlocked test handsets. T-Mobile and AT&T MVNOs take anything.

**Where the money actually goes.** The plan is noise next to the kit: 3-5 dollars a month is 36-60 a year per line. The recurring cost is the SIM kit itself plus postage, so the correct move is one bulk order of kits, kept inert in a drawer, not one kit at a time.
