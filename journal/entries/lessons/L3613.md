**L3613 · An unknown that never clears because of a porting error teaches the reader to ignore unknown**

At the 2026-10-11 authority cutover three CEO-kernel checks ended up permanently unknown: app_voice asked systemd for the office unit name secretary-api on a host where the API is company-api; model_failover read a status file whose producer had not been ported; config_sync read an autosync record that only consumer hosts write. Two were port artifacts, not blindness. A monitoring surface that reports unknown forever for a reason the reader cannot act on is worse than a red check: it trains the reader to skip the unknown tier, and the next real unknown is skipped with it. The fix is to resolve what the check depends on from a host-independent fact (the app ExecStart module, the canonical remote), or to say not-applicable in the report with the reason in the code, and never to let the reason stay invisible.
