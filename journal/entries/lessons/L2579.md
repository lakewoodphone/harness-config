### The owner throwing paper out is an obligation to convert, not to file

He said it plainly (2026-09-25): *"I am going to just throw out all the papers and
rely you to remember them so you better do a good job."* An archive that only holds
images has not remembered anything — the images cannot be acted on.

What made this intake complete was that each document was converted into exactly
one of three things, and nothing was allowed to stay "filed":

- **A task and a reminder in the authority database** when the paper carried a
 clock or a cost (8 tasks, 6 reminders), with the amount and the consequence in
 the reminder text itself so it is payable/actionable from the reminder alone.
- **A row in the owner decision queue** when the next move was genuinely his, with
 a recommendation attached and no menu (7 rows, #163–#169).
- **A sentence in the record** when there was nothing to do — and for the two
 documents that were already satisfied, an explicit line saying "this is not an
 open item", so a future session does not re-open them.

A paper is only safe to throw out when the thing it demanded now lives in a system
that will *raise it* without anyone remembering to look.
