**L12383 · A literal READY check marked a working SIM unprovable - the E4810 says LOADED**

2026-10-26, ZABZ-TECH bench. E4810 558800567352 went through the full resale flow and the telephony
leg refused: "SIM state is LOADED on 558800567352: a call or text cannot be proven without a live SIM.
Fit one and re-run." The SIM was working. Measured at that moment on the handset:

 gsm.sim.state LOADED
 gsm.operator.alpha US Mobile
 gsm.network.type LTE
 airplane_mode_on 0

The gate in cli.py was `if sim.upper() != "READY": exit 2` - a literal string comparison against a
value that varies by firmware. The E4610s report READY; this E4810 reports LOADED while registered on
LTE with an operator. So a working, call-capable phone was recorded as "not provable", the wrapper
returned NOT AUTHORISED / exit 1, and the whole authorisation would have failed on a string.

That is the same failure class as the vibrator PASS: a verdict taken from the weakest proxy available
instead of from the thing that actually proves it. Here the proof lives two lines further down the
same function - the leg dials out, answers a call, and sends a text; the SIM check exists only to
fail early with a clear message.

Fixed in cli.py: a SIM counts as usable when the state is READY **or** LOADED **and** an operator is
present; ABSENT or an operator-less state still exits 2 with the reason spelled out. When LOADED is
accepted the leg prints the operator and network it is relying on, so the reader can see what the
gate believed.

Verified on the handset (not in a unit test alone): condition-telephony --serial 558800567352 --sms
printed "SIM reports LOADED but is registered on US Mobile (LTE) - proceeding, the call and the text
are the proof" and then passed all three: outbound call 4 s, inbound call 5 s answered with a
recording, inbound SMS delivered and seen on the handset. resale-record then wrote AUTHORISED
(cli/logs/resale/558800567352-20261026-174706.json), and the filter-only deploy finalised with
sellable_release: true, recovery_verified: true.

Two reporting traps met on the same phone, neither fixed yet:
- the register's "Talk time" column is the DEVICE call log at audit time (bench.payload.usage.calls),
 so this handset reads "0 calls" while its telephony leg proved both calls and the text. The proof
 is in cli/logs/telephony/telephony-<serial>.json, not in that column.
- camera luma came back 4.8 against a floor of 4 (MIN_CAMERA_LUMA). It passed, and that is a bar
 with almost no margin - re-shoot in dayight before trusting it as evidence of a working camera.
