**L3399 · A blank crop is not a blank line: prove a handwriting line is absent before recording it**

**An over-tight crop of handwriting is indistinguishable from a blank line, so a missing line must be
proven missing before it is recorded as "not written".** Preparing the reading crops for the Senzer
note (2026-10-08) took four attempts: crop boxes guessed from the *original* pixel dimensions landed
between lines and returned an empty band three times, once for `usb` and once for the number. Each
mis-crop looked exactly like paper - so a session in a hurry could record *"the note names no fault"*
when the fault is written on the line below the one it cropped.

Rule: take the fractions from the **preview you actually looked at**, not from the file's dimensions
(`read_image` reports the preview size and the scale factor), and **read every crop back** before
concluding a line is absent. If a strip comes back blank, widen it by half a line-height and look
again. The case-file standard already says a reading is checked by eye; this is the half of it that
catches a reading that never happened.

Worked: the note reads `Senzer / 732 237 6600 / Virus & Acronis / usb` - name at 0.205-0.268 of the
rotated image's height, number 0.268-0.345, `Virus & Acronis` 0.350-0.425, `usb` 0.420-0.490, all
after rotating the photograph a quarter turn clockwise (`-90`, `expand=True`).

Evidence: `docs/customer-operations/case-media/2026-10-08-intake-notes/n01-note-text-2x.png` and
`n02`-`n05`; H3380.
