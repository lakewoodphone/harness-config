**L1481 · The transcript ceiling, measured: 6,042 calls can never have words, and why**

## The ceiling on this corpus, measured: 6,042 calls will never have words

The objective says "every transcript from all time". That is not achievable, and now it is
possible to say exactly how far from achievable it is and why — which is the difference
between a bounded, finished job and an endless one.

Every call with no transcript, classified by whether anything could still produce words
(measured on the authority, 2026-09-15 01:00Z, from `dialpad_call_full`,
`dialpad_media_file` and `dialpad_media_download_attempt`):

| bucket | calls | what it means |
|---|---|---|
| has usable local audio | 270 | in the transcription queue; words arrive shortly |
| a media URL Dialpad has not refused yet | 406 | real remaining opportunity |
| every URL refused (HTTP 404 / non-audio payload) | 860 | **permanently unrecoverable** |
| **no audio URL at all** | **5,182** | **permanently unrecoverable** |
| total with no transcript | 6,718 | |

So the corpus ceiling is **~676 calls that can still gain words**, and **6,042 that cannot** —
because Dialpad neither recorded nor transcribed them. The monthly shape explains it: Dialpad
AI transcription starts showing up in the data around April 2026, and the whole of
2025-10..2026-03 sits almost entirely in the "no audio anywhere" bucket. Of the 12,540 calls
in the archive, 5,815 carry Dialpad's own text and 308 carry a re-transcript of the recording.

**The generalisable rule.** "Ingest everything" is not a specification until someone measures
the sources. For this corpus that meant, in order: probe the far end of the date range to prove
the start is the account's first call, not the harvest's horizon; try every path that could
hold the missing content (media URL, transcript API, notification email) and record each
negative; and then state the ceiling as a number with a reason. A gap that is known and
explained is a *property of the source*; a gap nobody has classified is a defect, and this one
had been sitting in the middle of the objective for two days looking like a backlog.

**Where the number lives now.** `comms_search health` reports it (`calls with words:
3,556/10,353 (34%) — the rest have no audio anywhere (source limitation, not a backlog)`), so
an agent asking "is this everything?" gets the ceiling rather than a shrug.
