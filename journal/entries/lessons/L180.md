**L180 · A single-line heading regex dropped thirty-seven entries and reported success**

*Learned:* the second identical failure in the same migration. Bold headings in the old journal **wrap onto a
second line** (`**L159 · 2026-09-11 · A 500 from your own API can be a write lock, and the client's retry
budget must outlast` / `the request within the write lock.`). A regex that required the closing `**` on the
same line matched 136 of 153 lessons and 26 of 41 wins and reported them all cleanly — **37 entries silently
absent**, including every lesson from the recent sessions.
*Rule:* a parser for hand-written text must be verified by **counting its matches against an independent,
permissive count**, not by looking at the entries it did find. `anchored == parsed` for every kind is the
test; anything less is data loss with a green tick.
