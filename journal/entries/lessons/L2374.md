**L2374 · Norethisterone/Aygestin duration: no source defines a maximum at 10 mg/day — the supported band is up to ~6 months, and the one measured signal at that dose is reversible transaminitis**

Asked 2026-09-24 (wife on norethindrone acetate 5 mg BID = 10 mg/day continuous since 2026-07-30, restarted 2026-08-27, still on it). Six-axis review + hand verification. Do NOT redo this work: the full review is docs/wedding/aygestin-duration-safety.md in personal-secretary-mvp on branch docs/owner-health-2026-09-20, with four raw axis reports in docs/wedding/aygestin-duration-research/.

THE FINDINGS THAT MATTER:
- No label, guideline, Cochrane review or cohort states a ceiling for continuous oral norethindrone at 5-10 mg/day in a healthy young woman. The literature is silent, not reassuring.
- Only real ceiling for HER indication: Primolut N SmPC 'postponement of menstruation ... continuing for not longer than 10 to 14 days' (verbatim, verified by me). Endometriosis at her exact dose (5 mg BID): 'continued for at least 4 to 6 months'. US label: 6-9 months at 15 mg/day. NHS SPS says 'up to 3 to 4 weeks' with no citation; UK commercial services say 20/27/30 days = pack constructs.
- Long exposure exists only at LOWER doses: 24 months at 5 mg/day (76% amenorrhoea, pmid 3953694), 5 years at 2.5-5 mg/day (n=103, pmid 28384540). POPs (0.35 mg) = FSRH 'no maximum length of time'.
- The one measured harm signal at her dose: reversible transaminitis (case series n=10, mean 8.6 months, 8/10 on 5 mg/day) recommending LFT monitoring — PMC11402480. No label/guideline gives a monitoring interval.
- Duration-dependent harms that matter operate in YEARS: hepatic adenoma 3.3/100,000 rising after 4+ years is a COMBINED-OC label figure (Rooks 1979), not progestin-only; HCC >8 years same. VTE and mood are the EARLY risks, and VTE excess is highest in the first year or on RESTARTING after a >=4-week break (she restarted 2026-08-27).
- Norethisterone converts partly to ethinylestradiol (UK SmPC 4-6 ug per 1 mg = ~40-60 ug/day at 10 mg/day), so COC-class warnings apply — but the equivalence figures disagree 2-3x (ACOG ~4 ug per 5 mg; Huvinen 20-30 ug per 10-20 mg) and therapeutic-dose VTE has never been measured.
- Bone: no bone warning on either the US or UK label for this molecule; bone damage belongs to DMPA (boxed warning, 2-year limit). ACOG: the EE conversion 'may have some protective effect on bone mineral density'.
- ACOG CC3 (2022) verbatim: 'Norethindrone acetate is not approved as a contraceptive' — cycle suppression is off-label and is NOT contraception, while pregnancy is a labelled contraindication.
- Closest study to her actual purpose: norethindrone 5 mg TID for religious menses delay (PMC6537409) — spotting 2/27 (8%) vs 10/23 (43%), durations capped 28-45 days; menses ~day 3 after stopping; nothing clinical exists for ritual suppression beyond ~45 days ('years in Jewish circles' is anecdote).
