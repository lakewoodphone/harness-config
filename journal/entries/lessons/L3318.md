**L3318 · L: The owner's dictation is the final text; capture it verbatim and store the polished version as the draft**

ESTABLISHED 2026-10-05, first live walk-through of the Dialpad reply queue.

WHAT HAPPENED
For customer 8483336341 (Mrs. Whiteman, "Is the laptop in?") I showed a polished draft.
The owner did not edit it word for word. He dictated the substance and the framing:
 "draft a text to send that it''s in and high priority, we are setting it up and it
 should be fully ready in a few hours"
He named three facts and their order: it is IN / it is HIGH PRIORITY / it will be
fully ready in A FEW HOURS. He also corrected my premise silently by answering a
question I had asked with instruction instead of a yes/no.

THE LESSON
When he dictates content, the correct move is not to argue the wording or to present
options. It is to:
 1. write his framing into the final text and send THAT,
 2. store his dictation as final_text and my polished version as draft_text, so the
 ledger measures my gap against him, not his gap against me,
 3. keep the message short and put his three beats in his order.

WHAT MY DRAFT HAD MISSED
I had written "It''s set and ready - you can come by any time during our hours". That
claims it is ALREADY READY, which was not established, and it omits the priority and
the time estimate he wanted to give her. Two beats, both his, both absent from mine.

RULE FOR THE LEDGER: owner dictation counts as the final text even though no message
was sent yet. Record it at once - a dictation that is not written down is lost the
moment the session ends.
