**L1755 · The product photo was a USB stick but the device is standalone: read the vendor's SETUP page, not its requirements page**

**Evidence (2026-09-16, before ordering the magicJack for the owner's mother's line +1 732-363-1752):**

The owner sent a screenshot of the magicJackHOME order page. The product photo is a **USB-A stick**,
which is the 2007 computer-tethered form factor - so it looked like the box might require a PC at her
house. Two facts resolved it, and both are worth keeping:

1. **The device is standalone; the USB end is POWER.** magicJack's own Set up Your Device page
 (help.magicjack.com/faq/support/get-started/set-up-your-device, read 2026-09-16) gives labelled
 ports: Ethernet cord into `INTERNET`, phone into `PHONE`, and USB-A into a small power adapter.
 Plugging into a computer's USB port is explicitly marked **[OPTIONAL]** - that is the alternative
 mode, not the required one.

2. **magicJack's own site contradicts itself on exactly that question.** Its Requirements page still
 says *"To use a magicJack, you MUST have: 1. A computer with Windows 8, 8.1, 10 or MAC operating
 system"* - stale legacy copy from the USB era, contradicted by their own setup page. **When a vendor's
 requirements page and setup page disagree, read the setup page, and treat the discrepancy itself as
 the finding.** The stale page would have produced a wrong "this won't work at her house" answer.

Also captured, both order-time traps rather than research findings:

- **The $44.99 = 12 months ONLY with two-way service.** The order page footnotes say 12 months when
 *both* magicIN (receive) and magicOUT (dial out) are selected, and **15 months** for a one-way
 subscription. The longer term is the worse product: one-way means the phone cannot place a call.
 Generalise: when a cheap plan advertises a longer term for a subset of features, check which feature
 was removed before reading the term as generosity.
- **Activation needs a second device on the same LAN** (mjreg.com; magicJack: "Your computer, tablet, or
 smartphone MUST be connected to the same network as the magicJack"), and the documented first fix for
 failed calls on a healthy router is opening **SIP ports 5060 and 5070** on it.

**Cost of the miss if it had gone unchecked:** an order for a device that could not have been installed
at a house the fleet cannot reach.
