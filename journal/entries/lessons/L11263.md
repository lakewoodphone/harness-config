**L11263 · The comms index lags the production table by minutes - read the live table before telling the owner a customer is not waiting**

Measured 2026-10-20, 17:05-17:12Z, on the Pollok ThinkPad intake.

`ps_comms_thread 8452636081` (index age 0.5 h) returned 17 messages ending with an outbound "You can also drop it off 24/7". Two minutes earlier the customer had texted: "Hi my laptop won't turn on now at all. Did he pay around with somethibf?" - a message that changes the whole case. It was already in `communications` (production, operational store, ids 142206/142227 at 17:05:56Z and 17:09:06Z) and already visible to `ps_comms_waiting` (index age 0.07 h).

So: "is the customer waiting" was answered correctly by the waiting tool and incorrectly by the thread tool, in the same minute. `ps_comms_waiting` refreshes far more often than the thread/search index.

RULE: for the question "what is the newest thing this person said", read the live table (or `ps_comms_waiting`), never the full-text index, and never quote a thread as complete without saying how old the index was. A 0.5 h stale index is enough to hide the sentence that decides the job.

Also measured: production stores every inbound SMS TWICE - once with phoneNumberFrom/phoneNumberTo, once with contactPhone only - so 18 distinct messages read as 36 rows. Count distinct bodies before publishing a row count.
