**L7039 · A band function that ends in 'everything else is an expense' will put the balance sheet through the P&L**

Measured 2026-10-16 on the CFO statement module. _band_inner() walks numbered rules - money movement, payout clearing, gross receipts, non-trading income, cost of goods - and then falls through to 'everything else is the ledger own expense line, signed by direction'. Any BALANCE-SHEET category name lands in that fallback. Other Current Liabilities did, so three Zelle RECEIPTS (notes 'due to phone and tech', 'Payment for rent and ...') were banded as a reduction of expenses: the statement printed '| other current liabilities | 1,750.00 | 3 |' INSIDE lpt expense band as a POSITIVE, which reduced expenses and inflated profit by 1,750.00. Found by applying the direction guard invariant retroactively - for each (entity, category) in a spending band, does it carry credits with no debit at all - which flagged exactly three categories, of which this was the material one. Fixed with a step before the expense fallback matching a few explicit words (liabilit, current asset, equity, deferred, prepaid, payable, receivable) and banding them movement/balance_sheet; measured against the whole ledger exactly two names match and both were wrongly expense, so the change is bounded. Verified: the line leaves the expense band, appears as '| balance_sheet | 1,750.00 | 3 |' in the movement section which the statement excludes by construction, lpt net income -43,871.04 -> -45,621.04 exactly -1,750.00, escape101 and personal unchanged. Together with the two previous rounds that is 4,358.30 of P&L error found in three rounds while pct_clear stayed at 99.28 throughout. The general rule: a classifier whose last clause is 'everything else is X' silently converts every category it was never taught about into X - so a fallback band must be the band you are least willing to be wrong about, and balance-sheet shapes must be named explicitly. The check that finds them costs one query and looks for the invariant being violated rather than for a missing value.
