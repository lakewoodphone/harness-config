**L1702 · Journal append: legacy absorption no longer runs on every write**

See the constant ABSORB_INTERVAL_SEC in journal.py. Measured before: cProfile of append --dry-run --no-fetch was 14.9 s total, of which absorb_all was 12.6 s (legacy_candidates 12.2 s, seven max_number calls at 11.7 s, 15,303 pathlib relative_to calls, 8.2 s in tree_signature); the append itself is ~0.3 s. After: absorption runs only when the stamp is absent/older than 6 h, when a legacy source changed, or with --absorb / JOURNAL_ABSORB=always. This entry is the proof run.
