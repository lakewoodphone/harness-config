**L7041 · The band is mine and the label is his - and the arithmetic that explains a 17.98 gap instead of hand-waving it**

Measured 2026-10-16 on the CFO ledger. Thirty personal-side rows that were the same purchase as shop rows (same description, amount and DAY, with eBay item numbers in the description) sat in personal's Business, Industrial and Scientific Supplies Basic expense. They were moved to personal Internal Transfers - money movement, excluded from the statement by construction - with the uncertainty written into each row own justification. The split this makes is the useful part: THE BAND IS MINE, because under every reading of the open owner question the personal side is not a personal expense (if the card is the same card as the shop one the copy is a duplicate, and if it is his card the purchase is the shop and this is money owed to him), and independently a business-supplies category has no place in personal expenses. THE LABEL IS HIS, because which of the two it is IS the question, and a label asserted on an inference would have to be un-asserted if he answers the other way. THE ARITHMETIC: personal net income moved -74,143.36 to -70,766.32, delta +3,377.04. The rows total 3,395.02 by absolute value, but 29 are debits (3,386.03) and 1 is a credit (8.99), and a credit inside an expense band is a POSITIVE expense - so the net-income effect is 3,386.03 minus 8.99 = 3,377.04, and the 17.98 difference from my earlier absolute total is exactly 2 x 8.99. Worth stating rather than rounding away, because the absolute total and the P&L effect are different numbers and only one of them belongs in a profit statement. AND the detector still reports 27 groups, 3,237.00, all lpt cogs versus personal movement - by design: the pair is still one purchase on two books and only his answer decides whether the personal leg is a duplicate or a receivable. A detector that went quiet when a band changed but the question was still open would be a detector that lies.
