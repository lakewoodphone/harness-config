**L4577 · A repair step said 'nothing to repair' while the frozen box was still the default - a false negative reads like a clean bill of health**

The first version of step 4 in `scripts/evolution/deliver-persona.py` used a regex that required the hostname to be the first thing after a quote. The real line is:

 brokerSsh: !!js "process.env.MESH_BROKER_SSH ?? 'secratary-ts'",

The quote before the value blocked the match, so the step printed "no `brokerSsh: secratary-ts` line -- nothing to repair" while the FROZEN office box was STILL the composed default on the authority (proved afterwards: `--dump-config` carried `brokerSsh: !!js process.env.MESH_BROKER_SSH ?? 'secratary-ts'`). The tool's own step had reported clean.

RULE: a repair step's negative result is a CLAIM like any other. Print the evidence for the negative (what was scanned, how many lines, the count of hits) or do not report clean. Replaced with a line-based substring transform that cannot miss it this way, which now also prints every remaining stale-hostname line for judgement instead of silently passing.

This is the same shape as the failure the step exists to fix: a green signal sitting on top of the thing it was meant to catch.
