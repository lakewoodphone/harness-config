**L1510 · A case can be unfindable by the phone number that actually calls**

Evidence (2026-09-15, lakewooechsmini): 732-678-6417 (customer 'Savitz') has 108 SMS rows and 9 Dialpad calls, but grep of lpt-hub for that number returns ZERO hits. The SD-card case is filed as 'abramczyk-sd-card-8482857640-2026-08-25.md' because Abramczyk (848-285-7640) was the friend who dropped the card off; Savitz owns the card and is the only one who ever calls or texts about it. A whole owner session on zabz-tech (2026-09-14) burned time concluding 'the number does not exist in the corpus'.

Rule: when a device is brought in by someone other than the owner, record the OWNER as the customer and the bringer as an alias, and put BOTH phone numbers in front matter + the sync record. Otherwise the customer's own calls and texts cannot find their own case.
