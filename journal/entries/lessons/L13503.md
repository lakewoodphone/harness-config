**Correction to `L13440`: the eBay opt-out was NOT the honest choice — we do persist eBay data, so we subscribe instead.**

**2026-10-25, correcting an entry written the same day.** `L13440` ends by recommending the
Marketplace Account Deletion *exemption* ("Exempted from Marketplace Account Deletion") on the
grounds that "we only read public item data". That reasoning was wrong, and it was wrong in a way
that could have cost the account.

**The exemption's own wording is the test:** *"I do not persist eBay data."* We do persist it. A
single listing record — `lpt-hub/docs/customer-operations/sourcing/listings/ebay-147473202913.json`
— carries `title`, `source_price`, `condition`, `image_urls` and, decisively,
`seller_ledger.seller_name: "FunTechToy"` plus `seller.feedback_count`. A seller display name is
tied to an eBay user account, which is exactly the subject of a deletion request. The portal says
plainly: *"Failure to provide correct information may result in penalties or having your account
disabled."* A convenient declaration is not a compliant one.

**So the company subscribes.** `personal-secretary-mvp/app/services/ebay_account_deletion.py` plus
two routes under `/webhook/` — the prefix `_api_access_guard` already leaves public, where every
handler verifies its own input. The challenge is SHA-256 of `challengeCode + verificationToken +
endpoint`, concatenated with no separators, lowercase hex.

**Verified, not configured:** over the real public hostname
`https://api.abletelsolutions.com/webhook/ebay-marketplace-account-deletion`,
`GET ?challenge_code=…` returned `200` with a `challengeResponse` equal to a SHA-256 computed
independently on the calling machine — both before and after a clean `company-api` restart — and
`POST` of a synthetic notification returned `{"ok":true,"recorded":true}`. Selftest 13/13.

**Deletion is answered by anonymisation, not deletion.** `seller_name` becomes `[deleted-user]` with
`seller_name_redacted_at`; price, title, condition and outcome survive. A closed account does not
retract the fact that we inspected a listing, and the ledger is sourcing evidence that cost money to
build (D2907: exposure shrinks, capability does not).

**The one thing this does not do, stated rather than hidden:** the authority records a deletion
request but **cannot redact it**, because lpt-hub is not checked out there. The handler reports
`"note": "no ledger directory"` instead of claiming success. Redaction runs where the ledger lives:
`python -m app.services.ebay_account_deletion --redact <username>`.

**The transferable rule, and it generalises past eBay:** before accepting a compliance exemption,
read the sentence you are certifying and check it against what your system actually stores — the
`listings/` directory, not the architecture diagram. "We only read public data" is a statement about
ingress; the exemption is about what you keep. And when a recommendation turns out to be wrong the
same day you wrote it, correct every surface it reached — here the client's `doctor` text, the
sourcing README and `docs/mesh/150` all said "opt out" — rather than only the newest one.
