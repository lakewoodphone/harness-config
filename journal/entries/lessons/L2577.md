**L2577 · Mesh fleet, measured: a 30-minute hard cap kills children silently, a missing MESH-HOST marker discards the report even when the work is pushed, and the deliverable is the BRANCH not the narrative**

Recorded 2026-09-25, from the staff-console fleet (7 children: 2 completed, 5 ended without a report while
their work survived on the remote because of the early-push rule).

MEASURED FACTS
1. **The mesh child has a hard turn cap of 1,800,000 ms (30 min) and it kills silently.** Two children
 (`subagent-10` work orders, `subagent-17` settings) ended with exactly:
 `the remote turn did not finish within 1800000 ms and the ssh client was killed`. Both were mid-work and
 both had already pushed useful commits. **Scope a child to finish inside ~25 minutes**, or split the
 slice - a bigger brief does not produce more work, it produces a truncated one.
2. **A child's report can be discarded wholesale.** `subagent-15` (customers) failed with: *"the child did
 not begin its report with `MESH-HOST: <hostname>` - its location is unproven, so the run is not reported
 as complete"*, and `job_output` returned nothing. Its two commits were on the remote and complete.
 **When a child dies, evaluate the BRANCH, never wait for the report.** The report is a convenience; the
 push is the deliverable.
3. **This repo's Vitest UI suites are heavy enough to stall a sweep.** `pnpm test:frontend:one -- <14 files>`
 ran 20 minutes without finishing and had to be killed; the decisive subset (the NEW behaviour tests:
 `essentialsFirst`, `TransactionType`, `ChargeLineItemsSection`, `WorkOrderDetailPageTakePayment`) ran
 44 tests in under two minutes. **Gate the changed behaviour first; run the legacy suites separately.**
4. The three children that pushed early lost nothing when they died; the one slice round that did not push
 early (2026-09-23, four children) lost everything. The rule is now load-bearing, not a nicety.

WHAT TO DO DIFFERENTLY IN THE NEXT BRIEF: one deliverable per child, ~20 minutes of work, push after the
first coherent commit AND after each increment, and a report that starts with the required marker.
