**L2399 · Alibaba.com is CAPTCHA-walled from this fleet; Made-in-China is the readable B2B channel - and no Chinese listing publishes tier or logo-print pricing**

**Measured 2026-09-23 (mesh node ZABZ-TECH + local ZABZ-YOGA) while sourcing a rugged iPhone 11 case.**

**Channel reachability — this is the reusable part:**
- **Alibaba.com: BLOCKED.** Anti-bot "punish" page on every host (incl. all 9 language subdomains), and
 Playwright/Chromium — headless **and** headed, real Edge — landed on a **CAPTCHA**. **So Alibaba's
 US-warehouse filter, the single most valuable field for any import decision, could not be read at all.**
 Do not promise an Alibaba price until a different route past this exists.
- **DHgate: 403 Akamai.** Global Sources: renders but returns **no product cards**, plain fetch 429.
- **eBay: HTTP 403 on every attempt** (search + item, browser UA) from both nodes. eBay *should* work via
 Firecrawl `proxy: stealth` (measured 2026-09-22) — plain fetch does not.
- **Made-in-China.com: reachable and the richest source** — price, MOQ, material attributes all readable.
- **AliExpress: reachable**, search + item pages; the `shipFrom: US` flag is embedded in
 `trace.pdpParams.pdp_cdi` in the page JSON, **not** rendered as a badge.
- **Amazon `/dp/` pages: render via Firecrawl `proxy: stealth` with `maxAge: 0`** (a huge page — extract with
 `formats:["json"]` + a schema, not markdown, or it costs ~190 KB of context for one price).

**The B2B pricing reality for this class of part, which will hold for other accessories too:**
**Not one of 22 listings publishes a 100 / 250 / 500-piece tier, and not one publishes a pad-print or deboss
cost or minimum.** Made-in-China shows a price *range* (e.g. US$1.60–2.40) whose ends track MOQ and spec, and
every page prints *"Shipping Cost: Contact the supplier"*. "Customized logo are welcome" is a statement of
**capability, worth nothing as a quote**. Only real observed quantity break anywhere: AliExpress "buy 2, get
2% off". So tier prices and logo costs must be reported as **quoted-only**, never inferred.

**Fit trap:** in a 22-listing scan, **17 named a model *range*** ("iPhone 11 12 13 … PRO Max") and where
spelled out, **"11 PRO Max" (6.5") appeared far more often than plain "11" (6.1")**. Only two listings named
the 6.1" device unambiguously. Always buy a sample before a batch — the wrong size is the likeliest loss.

**Material strings are not specs:** listings that look identical at thumbnail size variously said
"PC+TPU", "Acrylic+TPU", "PC, Silicone, EVA", "PC+ABS", "Plastic" and plain "TPU" — different products.
