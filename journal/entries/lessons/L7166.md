# L: `unit_amount` is not the subscription amount — I told the owner two wrong numbers from the same bug

**2026-10-16, on the Stripe/US-Mobile reconciliation.** The owner said *"doesn't heichal pay 66 a month, get this straight"* and he was right; I had reported 11.00.

**The bug, stated plainly: I printed `price.unit_amount` and never multiplied by `items[].quantity`.**

Heichal Hatorah's subscription is `sub_1UIWLJLnlgYj1gmTk4w0saSx`, `qty=6` at 11.00 = **66.00/month**, with a **66.00 invoice paid 2026-09-22**. My script read `unit_amount / 100` and printed `11.00`.

**It produced TWO wrong numbers to the owner in one turn:**

| what I said | what was true |
|---|---|
| "Heichal pays 11.00/month and has no matching line" | they pay **66.00** for **6 lines** — the six unlabelled `Line 15`–`Line 20` |
| "89.00/month recurring" | **144.00/month** — I had understated by exactly Heichal's missing 55.00 |
| "Heichal is the one subscription with no line" | their subscription covers **six** lines, on `quantity` |

**And it corrupted the reconciliation's shape.** I concluded "7 of 8 subscriptions map to a line, 1 unmatched" — the truth is **8 of 8 map, covering 13 lines**, because one subscription covers six. Worse, I nearly reported the missing 55.00 as *revenue leakage* and asked him to authorise a billing correction on a customer who was being billed correctly.

**The generalisation, which is the third time this session the same shape has bitten:** a field that *looks* like the answer is not the answer until you check its multiplier or its scope. Siblings in the same session — `internal_ts` is epoch milliseconds, not a date string (my "zero NJ DOL mail"); `status` is read-only output while `disabled` is the writable field (the webhook "can only be fixed in the Dashboard"); a GET 404 on a POST-only route means nothing (I nearly called a working endpoint broken); and counting a *surname* as a claim match finds a Chase Zelle notice.

**The rule worth keeping: for any money figure, print `unit x quantity = total` with all three visible, and never quote the unit alone.** `144.00 = (11.00 x 6) + (12.00) + (11.00 x 6)` is self-checking; `11.00` and `89.00` were both confidently wrong and both came from the same missing term.

**The reconciliation, corrected and verified:** 20 US Mobile lines — 13 billed (6 Heichal + 7 individual customers), 7 unbilled (the owner's own two, four test sims, one pending). Nothing is leaking. Total recurring **144.00/month**.
