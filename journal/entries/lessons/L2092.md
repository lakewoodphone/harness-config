**L2092 · L2086 - the company is 18 departments wide and ONE agent deep - read work_sessions, never the roster, to find out who is working**

The AI company on secratary holds 124 agent identities (~34 `active`, ~40 `deactivated`) with souls,
departments, budgets, routing rules and standing work orders. Measured 2026-09-20:

 select agent_id, count(*) from work_sessions where started_at > datetime('now','-14 days')
 group by 1;
 -> orchestrator | 2470 (and NOTHING else)

Every work session in 14 days, and 100% of the 1,237 in 7 days, ran under `orchestrator`. Meanwhile
`agent_identities.json` lists `dept_business_ops`, `dept_engineering`, `dept_finance`,
`dept_security`, `evolution_architect`, `cross_domain_synthesizer` and ~28 more as `active`.

So the org carries the full overhead of 18 departments and runs one worker. The identity roster
tells you what the company INTENDS; `work_sessions` tells you what it DOES. They disagree, and only
one of them is measured.

The visible cost: 71 assignments in 7 days target agents that do not exist in the roster
(`dept_management`, `dept_admin`, `dept_home`, `dept_ceo`, `dept_executive_assistant`, ...), and
23,046 open `goal_steps` sit assigned to departments that never claim them - `dept_business_ops`
6,016 of them, `dept_engineering` 2,815.

**Rule: never report company capability from the blueprint. Count executors in `work_sessions`.**
