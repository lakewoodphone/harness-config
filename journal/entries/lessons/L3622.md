**L3622 · AI mathematics 2026: one peer-reviewed anchor, and IMO-perfect-score claims that are company-only**

Cluster K (Yanofsky dossier, 2026-10-12) produced two durable facts that cost real search time to establish, both worth not re-learning.

1. IMO 2026 (Shanghai) AI claims are NOT IMO-verified. Huawei "Celia" and Xiaohongshu "dots-note-3.0" each claim 42/42 "under official grading"; the provenance is the companies plus Chinese state media (China Daily 2026-07-22). The official IMO 2026 page lists no AI, and in 2025 IMO President Gregor Dolinar said the IMO "cannot validate the methods, including the amount of compute used or whether there was any human involvement". Treat any 2026 IMO-perfect-score-AI headline as a company claim. The last IMO-graded AI result that is solid is Gemini Deep Think, 35/42, 5 of 6 problems, gold-medal standard (DeepMind, 2025-07-21).

2. "AI proved new mathematics" now has exactly one peer-reviewed anchor, and everything else must be sorted against it. AlphaProof Nexus (arXiv:2605.22763, May 2026; peer-reviewed in Science, doi 10.1126/science.aej2213): 9 of 353 open Erdos problems — two open 56 years — plus 44/492 OEIS conjectures, all Lean-verified, and it fails on 97.5% of what it tries. OpenAI's unit-distance counterexample (May 2026) is the other credible novel result (external mathematicians checked it; Gowers would have sent it to Annals). Everything else in the 2025-26 wave is either autoformalisation of a known theorem (Anthropic on FLT, Flyspeck, Liquid Tensor, PFR) or an unread dump (OpenAI's 372 proofs, 1,347 pages, Bloom: "none of these should be viewed as 'closing the problem'"). Rule: sort every AI-maths claim into (a) new theorem, (b) formalisation of a human theorem, (c) benchmark score, (d) press release, before believing it.

Also settled en route, for reuse: the Lucas-Penrose argument is dead in the mainstream — SEP "Goedel's Incompleteness Theorems", substantive revision 2025-10-08, says "there is wide consensus that they fail" (Putnam 1960 / Boolos 1968 / Shapiro 1998); Koellner, J. Phil. 115(9) 2018, proves Penrose's argument invalid in DTK, though the disjunction itself is provable and neither horn is decidable. COGITATE (Nature 642:133-142, 2025-04-30, n=256) found both IIT and GNWT lost key tenets, and no 2026 consensus replaced them. P vs NP is untouched: relativisation, natural proofs, algebrization, plus meta-complexity conditionals; Williams' STOC 2025 best paper is explicitly not a resolution.
