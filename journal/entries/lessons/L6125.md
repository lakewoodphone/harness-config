**L6125 · The 196 opposite-sign duplicates: 159 are correctly marked, 12 are demonstrably wrong**

2026-10-13. The 196 opposite-sign duplicates are mostly correct, twelve are demonstrably wrong, and the
question does not explain the reconciliation gaps. That is three answers from one measurement.

THE SPLIT, BY ACCOUNT FIRST, because that is the structural fact:
 same account, opposite signs 179 33,705.70 <- one account: a reversal or a sign-mirror
 different accounts, opposite signs 17 2,686.43 <- two accounts: a transfer, BOTH sides real

WITHIN THE 179, THE EVIDENCE IS OVERWHELMING THAT THEY ARE ARTIFACTS, NOT EVENTS:
 159 pairs are dated the SAME DAY, 98 have byte-identical descriptions, and 13 more are one day
 apart. Verbatim:
 2026-06-28 debit 3974.90 AUTOPAY PAYMENT - THANK YOU
 2026-06-28 credit 3974.90 AUTOPAY PAYMENT - THANK YOU
 2025-05-02 debit 2000.00 Zelle payment to Moishe Bachrach Conf# bv6pesvcn
 2025-05-02 credit 2000.00 Zelle payment to Moishe Bachrach Conf# bv6pesvcn
 2025-10-07 credit 902.86 RETURN OF POSTED CHECK / ITEM (RECEIVED ON 10-06) ELEC
 2025-10-07 debit 902.86 RETURN OF POSTED CHECK / ITEM (RECEIVED ON 10-06) ELEC
One bank line, on one account, on one day, written twice with the sign flipped. That is exactly the
`R1 sign mirror` defect this tool already documents for snapshots, appearing in transactions - and
marking one of the pair a duplicate is CORRECT and protective. The 27,023.35 of same-day pairs should
stay as they are.

WITHIN THE 17 THAT STRADDLE ACCOUNTS, twelve are wrong and I can say so from the descriptions alone:
 'Payment to mercisouthernlife_24 - 180000...' vs 'Refund to mercisouthernlife_24 - ...'
 'Payment to pmg_distributors - 2200010642...' vs 'Refund to pmg_distributors - ...'
 'Payment to 1shopdirect305 - ...', 'discountutopia1', 'allstateus', 'melharr_4517',
 'neo_futuredoit', 'moleno2010', 'ele-y021' - all on `None` vs `lpt-ebay-transactions`
A PAYMENT and its REFUND are two movements, not one event recorded twice. Marking the refund as a
duplicate of the payment deletes a real credit from the flow - inventing money in the opposite direction
from the one `load_transactions`' own comment guards against. **12 rows, 2,363.97.** The other five
cross-account pairs are genuine duplicates: `PAYPAL *BETCKEYYJFJ` and `PAYPAL *WALMART COM WALMA` appear
with the same description under two account ids, which is a row imported twice under two names.

WHAT THIS SETTLES. The excluded-duplicate question is NOT the cause of the reconciliation gaps: 2,363.97
of demonstrable error against 55,319.21 of unexplained movement, and the largest single class is
correctly handled. I opened this two rounds ago as a live hypothesis for the sign-flips; it is now
struck off with evidence rather than left open, which is worth as much as finding a cause would have
been - the next person does not have to wonder.

ONE THING THAT DOES NOT FIT, recorded rather than smoothed over: `personal-amex-22005` holds a 5,499.67
pair dated a day apart - a CREDIT at ALIBABA.COM on 2026-05-07 and a debit 'Alibaba' on 2026-05-08. A
same-day mirror with a one-day posting lag is the likely reading, but 5,499.67 is large enough to be a
genuine refund and one account asking that question is cheap to check.

THE TWELVE ARE A FIX, AND SMALL. `Payment to X` paired with `Refund to X` on different accounts should
not be treated as duplicates by the detector; that is a narrow rule with a clear signature and 2,363.97
of real movement behind it.
