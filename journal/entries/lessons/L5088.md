# Correction: Google Voice was RETIRED BY DECISION, and I reported it as a failure for many rounds

**2026-10-15. This corrects my own reporting, not someone else's work.**

## What I said, and why it was wrong

For a dozen rounds I told the owner, in the same shape each time, that *"your personal line is dark
and needs one action from you"* and that the Google Voice reader *"has been read by nothing since
4 July"*. The measurements behind that were real — `google_voice_secretary_health` reading `failed`,
the newest `google_voice_secretary_log` item at 2026-07-04, 7,977 and 7,315 consecutive failures. **The
measurements were accurate and the story I told with them was not.**

Two rows already in the queue, both **resolved**, say what actually happened:

**#260 — "Retire it, or keep it?" → RETIRED IT, option A.** `GOOGLE_VOICE_SECRETARY_ENABLED=false` in
`/opt/company/.env`, restarted and health-gated. Its own measured before/after: 126 GV journal lines in
the preceding 3 hours, each a headless Chrome launch failing and leaving a `.broken-*` profile behind —
553 MB of them; and after the restart, **0 GV lines**, no chrome process, `/health` 200 in 0.0057 s. And
the sentence that matters most:

> *"Dialpad carries the live line and **Gmail forwards the old account texts**, so no operational
> capability was lost."*

**#15 — "What should happen to Google Voice?" → split and closed.** The owner-facing half did not need
him (keeping the numbers is the status quo). The delivery half was **already decided**: *"he keeps SMS
off and surfaces urgent items in the app (his 2026-09-15 decision), so GV stops being a delivery path."*

## So the truthful position

- The scraper's death is **a decision, not a fault.** My gate (`shadow_learning`) stops it being driven,
 which is consistent with that decision rather than a repair of an outage.
- The replacement path was **Gmail forwarding the old account's texts** — and *that* is the thing I
 actually measured failing: `google_voice_secretary_log` `source='gmail_forward'` holds 39 rows on
 2026-07-02/03 and **nothing after**. So the gap is the forward, not the line.
- And the owner's own 2026-09-15 decision is that **SMS is off and urgent items surface in the app** —
 which is a different delivery model from the one my "one action from you" framing assumed.

## The lesson, which is the fifth instance of the same one today

**I read a status field and inferred a story, without reading the decisions that produced it.** Every
number I quoted was correct; the conclusion was not. This is the same failure as the "2,780 gate errors"
I first reported as a live defect (it was one bug over three days), the "digest fired twice in 30 days"
(its timer was two days old), the "201 profile copies" (11 live ones), and the "citation cannot be
verified" (wrong repository).

**A row that is resolved is evidence.** Before reporting anything as broken, read the resolutions of the
rows that named it — the queue contains the answer more often than the queue contains the problem. In
this session I checked the pending rows thoroughly and the **resolved** rows barely at all, and that
asymmetry is exactly where this went wrong.
