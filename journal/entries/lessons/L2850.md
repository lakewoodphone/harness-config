**A scraper's summarized answer is a claim, not a measurement — it invented a price and moved a film to the wrong cinema**

Measured 2026-09-28, planning the owner's Chol Hamoed night out.

Two errors in one session, both from the same source: the `answer` field Firecrawl returns when a scrape is
given a `formats: ["query"]` prompt. It reads as a measurement. It is a summary written by a model over the
page, and it will state a number the page does not contain.

1. **The invented price.** Scraped `topgolf.com/us/edison` asking for Monday bay pricing; the answer came back
 "*A hitting bay costs $34 per hour on a Monday evening.*" I repeated that to the owner as "the site quotes
 34 per hour for a Monday evening bay". A second, independent pass over the same site found the opposite:
 the pricing widget renders client-side, `topgolf.com/us/edison/pricing/` is a **404**, and the price appears
 in no retrievable HTML. The published money facts are a 6 one-time new-account activation fee and a warning
 that walk-in rates can exceed online rates. **The 34 was fabricated.** Cost of believing it: a quoted price
 to the owner that no source supports.

2. **The moved film.** `fandango.com/08701_movietimes?date=2026-09-28` — a *zip-level* page — was asked which
 theater had Princess Mononoke. It answered **AMC Brick Plaza 10**, and a second pass over the same page
 answered **AMC Freehold 14**. Reading the two **theater-level** pages settled it: Brick Plaza 10's schedule
 contains no Mononoke at all, Freehold 14 lists it at 7:00 PM. That error had already survived into a written
 report overnight.

RULE: when the fact being decided is a *number* or an *attribute of a named entity*, the summarised answer is
not evidence. Go to the page that owns the fact — the theater's own page, the venue's own pricing page — and
read it. Use the summary to find the page, never to replace it. And when two passes of the same summary
disagree, that disagreement is the signal that the source is a paraphrase.

Corollary already recorded as L2594 and reconfirmed: prefer structured sources with no summariser in the path
(the NWS gridpoint API returned the hourly weather exactly, first try, three times).
