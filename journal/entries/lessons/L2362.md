**L2362 · A true fact about one channel is not a fact about the owner**

## The rule

**A true fact about one channel is not a fact about the owner.** Before a check
may say "nobody can reach him", it must name every channel it knows about and
show that each one is dead. Otherwise it will report the channel it happens to
measure, and the day that channel is quiet by design the alarm will be false and
loud.

## Measured evidence

2026-09-23 03:15Z: `check_delivery` reported CRITICAL -- "nothing is reaching the
owner for 2d; 36 message(s) waiting undelivered". At that moment the app surface
payload had been written 3-5 minutes earlier, and the badge built from it was
carrying that finding to the owner. Two days earlier the owner's own instruction
("only text me for truly important things and don't overdo it or spam me") had
retired the SMS kill-switch marker and set the send gate to `urgent`; nothing
since then had legitimately qualified for a text. The check read an intentional
quiet as an outage.

## The shape of the fix

State the claim condition, then require it: unreachable = (surface stale or
absent) AND (no send inside the window). Anything weaker becomes a lower severity
with the real reason named. Corollary: when a class of message can never be
carried, that is a **routing** finding, not a delivery one, and it needs its own
measurement -- here `reason='ask_owner'` (newest sent 2026-04-16T12:00Z, 169
expired, 10 held) hidden behind `MAX(sent_at)` across all reasons.
