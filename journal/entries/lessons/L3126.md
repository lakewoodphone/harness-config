**L3126 · A member mute is self-reversible: our own #unmute undid an admin mute in two minutes, and re-read state before telling a person what it is**

**L — A member-level mute is self-reversible, and our own `#unmute` command undid an admin mute within two minutes. If one person removes another, say what the removed person can do about it, and re-read the state right before you tell them it happened.**

MEASURED 2026-09-30, the family chat, all times UTC

- 15:41 the manager muted Shabsi (`members.muted=1`, verified).
- 15:42:28 he texted `#Unmute` and the hub unmuted him - correctly, by design: `#` commands are handled *before* the mute check precisely so a muted member is never trapped, and the help text had advertised `#unmute` since the feature shipped.
- 15:42:44, now unmuted, one of his texts was relayed to **10 family phones** (message ids 128489-128498, all delivered). Nothing was broken; the removal simply was not in force any more.
- 15:44-15:46 his `#Help`, `#Status`, `#Help`, `#Add` texts went nowhere near the group - the `#` privacy guarantee held exactly as documented.
- 15:46:12 the manager sent him the unsubscribe notice. The recipient check in the same script printed `muted=0` - the mute had already been undone - and the notice still went out, so for a moment the message told him he had been unsubscribed while he was in fact still subscribed. Re-muted at 15:46:32.

RULES

1. **`muted` is a member's own setting that an admin can set, not a lock an admin holds.** Any workflow that says "X is out" must say what X can do to get back in, because X can - and the more discoverable the command (`#help` lists it), the faster they will. If a removal is meant to stick until someone with authority reverses it, that is a different mechanism (an admin-only flag with no member-facing counterpart), not a bigger mute.
2. **Re-read the state immediately before you tell a person what their state is.** The notice was written from a check made minutes earlier; the check printed the truth at the moment of sending and the send ignored it. A stale assumption in a message to a human is indistinguishable from a lie.
3. Related: the only family-visible junk in this window arrived because the mute had been *reversed by the member*, not because a relay was broken. Do not "fix" the relay for this - it did what it was told.
