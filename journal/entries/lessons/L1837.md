**L1837 · The Dahua NVR numbers channels from 1 while the HA integration stores them 0-based**

The Dahua NVR at 192.168.50.170 numbers channels from **1**; Home Assistant's `dahua` integration stores a **0-based** `channel` in the config entry. The two differ by one, and nothing in either place says so.

Measured 2026-09-15 by pulling one frame per channel and reading each stream's burned-in OSD title (no guessing, no config archaeology):

| NVR/RTSP/snapshot ch | OSD title | HA entry (0-based) | the view |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Deserted House Exit | Cam Deserted Exit (ch 0, disabled) | control room, from above the exit door |
| 5 | IPC | Cam Waiting Room (ch 4, disabled) | front area / waiting room |
| 6 | Main Entrance | Cam Front Hallway (ch 5) | front hallway |
| 7 | Deserted House inwards | Cam Deserted Interior (ch 6) | control room, looking back at the exit door |
| 8 | Reolink Video Doorbell PoE | (none) | doorbell, the only camera with a real mic |
| 2, 3, 4 | — | — | no camera attached; RTSP refuses |

`ChannelTitle` is 0-based over the same list, so `ChannelTitle[N-1]` is the name of snapshot channel N. `configManager.cgi?action=getConfig&name=ChannelTitle` prints them in one GET.

What the off-by-one actually cost, verified in the archive rather than reasoned about: every one of the 170 clips named `*_front_hallway.mp4` was recorded from RTSP channel 5 — the IPC waiting-room camera — and the `*_waiting_room` passes asked for channel 4, which has no camera, so they failed on every single event (after a slow RTSP timeout, inside a sequential automation, delaying the clips queued behind them).

Rule that came out of this: **never address a camera by a bare number**. Address it by name and keep the number in exactly one table beside the OSD title that was read off the live stream, so the mapping is auditable instead of inherited.

Related, and the cheapest general method for any camera work: a frame plus the OSD clock identifies both *which camera* and *whether the clip is real-time*. Byte sizes and durations cannot: `ch1` and `ch7` stills were 330,917 and 327,602 bytes — near-identical, different rooms.
