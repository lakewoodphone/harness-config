**L12608 · A watermark stroke reads as a checkbox mark at low dpi - render at 400+ dpi**

LESSON ? a watermark stroke reads as a checkbox mark at low dpi; render at 400+ before believing a box

On the Gusto Q3 2026 Form 941 the page-scale view (140 dpi) showed what looked like a
pencilled X in the line-17 "business closed / stopped paying wages" box. It was the
grey "Paper Copy - Filed Electronically" watermark: its diagonal strokes cross that
checkbox. At 400 dpi the box is EMPTY. Line 17 is not checked.

The same page has genuine marks - 15b "Send a refund" and the first line-16 box - which
at 700-900 dpi are solid black X's clearly distinct from the watermark's grey. So the
rule is not "distrust every box", it is: render the specific box at 400+ dpi and compare
stroke colour and weight against a box you know is empty.

Cost of getting this wrong: the pack asserted a defect on a filed federal return that
does not exist, and proposed a 941-X to "uncheck" it. Caught only by cross-checking the
estate filing register, which said "line 17 NOT checked". A disagreement between your
reading and a prior record is a signal to re-render at higher resolution, not to prefer
your own eyes.

Also settled here: the watermark text is exactly "Paper Copy - Filed Electronically"
(three 50pt spans: 'Paper Copy -', 'Filed', 'Electronically'), not "PAID ELECTRONICALLY
/ COPY" as an earlier record had it. Read watermark text from the span list, not from a
low-res glance.
