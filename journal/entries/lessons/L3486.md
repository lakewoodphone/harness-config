## L — A printed repair-outcome label is a PHYSICAL claim: when the outcome changes you must recover the old one, not just supersede it on screen

**Measured 2026-10-11, ticket 2652 (Reifman Cadence, Lakewood Phone & Tech).**

The owner reported the charging-port redo had fixed the phone. The case was written up, production
moved to READY_FOR_PICKUP, and a label was printed reading
**"charging port fixed - NO CHARGE, job redo"**. Within the hour the phone itself said otherwise — it
now reports a **"not properly charging"** warning — and the case was reopened.

Every digital store corrected cleanly and cheaply: production got a **new note that cites the old one
as a correction** (there is no edit-note and no delete-note), the status was set back, and the case
file plus its sync record were rewritten and re-validated.

**The label could not be corrected that way.** Two or three copies existed as *tape*, and one was
probably stuck to the device. Nothing in any database can recall them, and a superseding label in a
directory does not reach a piece of paper on a shelf. Any staff member reading the old one would hand
the phone back as finished.

RULES
1. A repair-outcome label is a claim with a **physical lifetime**. When the outcome changes, print the
 replacement **in the same sitting**, and say out loud that the old copies are now false and must be
 recovered from the device and the shelf — not merely that a newer label exists.
2. Prefer wording that **does not assert an outcome the device has not confirmed**. "update customer:
 still not charging" ages far better than "charging port fixed", and it is the safer default for any
 repair whose success has not been demonstrated by the device itself.
3. **An owner's "it's fixed" is a hypothesis until the device agrees.** The device's own warning is the
 evidence; write the case and print the label from the evidence, and expect to be reopened.

TAPE-LAYOUT GOTCHAS FOUND WHILE DOING THIS (Brother QL-820NWB, 29 mm roll, via `lpt-label`):
- `--issue` is a **single line and is silently TRUNCATED at the font floor** — the first attempt
 rendered `update customer: still not charging - workin` with no error and no warning.
- Positional `info` lines are **IGNORED entirely** whenever `--ticket`, `--customer` or `--device` is
 present (`label_printer.py` L689: the work-order branch wins). So you cannot mix them.
- `--issue` **does** reach the tape as several lines if you put a real newline in it —
 `render()` splits each line on `\n` (L491). That is how the correct label was produced.
- Always `--dry-run --out <png>`, copy the PNG back and **LOOK at it** before spending tape.
