**L3718 · Label printing: compare the printed PNG's sha256 to the dry run's, and hand-break body lines to ~24 chars**

MEASURED 2026-10-12, printing the Greenfeld bin label from ZABZ-YOGA through the office anchor.

A PRINT IS PROVABLE IN ONE RESPECT AND NOT ANOTHER, and the two get confused.
- NOT provable: that tape came out. Confirmed again - the QL-820NWB's only counter is `Total Print Length(m)`, an integer metre, and a 64.4 mm label cannot move it. Status-page transitions are unobservable (60 ms/page refresh vs a ~1 s job). `instructions_sent: true` means the transport took it.
- PROVABLE, and cheap enough to always do: that the bytes printed are the bytes reviewed. `lpt-label print - --dry-run --out /tmp/x.png`, copy the PNG back, LOOK at it, then print WITHOUT --out; the tool writes its own image to /tmp and the job line names it (`image /tmp/lpt-label-<stamp>.png`). Copy that back and compare sha256 with the dry run. Both were de4e7338... - identical. So "I reviewed the label" stops being a claim about my intent and becomes a claim about the tape's content.

SECOND, a layout fact worth not rediscovering: the new renderer (f40f0e6) picks ONE body size for the whole label, the largest (cap 22 px) at which no WRAPPED line overflows - so on the 29 mm roll it sits at 22 px and you get roughly 24 characters per line, whatever your text. It does NOT shrink to fit more. Leading spaces are stripped (`para.strip()`), so indentation cannot be used to group; empty-string lines render as an 8 px gap and are the only grouping device. Hand-breaking lines to about 24 chars, mixed case for long phrases (all-caps is wider and wrapped "WAITING ON HIS DECISION"), took the same content from 90.0 mm ragged to 64.4 mm where every line held.
