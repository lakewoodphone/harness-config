**L2854 · The owner's own phone number was in the permissions table as a friend set to NEVER, so the system refused to answer him; Zabrowsky is the correct surname and Chip Zabrowsky is the AI**

OWNER'S OWN CORRECTION, 2026-09-28, after an earlier session (2026-09-25) got this backwards.

 * He said: "my last name is ZABROWSKY"; the misspelling "Zebrowski" is WRONG (2 uses in the
 journal: H545, L2099; 18 correct uses of Zabrowsky). A person's name is not a style choice.
 * `+18483897895` = 848-389-7895 = HIS OWN PERSONAL iPHONE. It is the ONLY number he sees, and
 therefore the only place an autonomous decision or question may be sent.
 * `+17325691594` = 732-569-1594 = his GOOGLE VOICE. Texts and voicemails there arrive by EMAIL;
 he rarely looks at it. Others reach him there and he reaches out from there, so inbound from it
 is still him - but NOTHING may be sent TO it (see the new `owner-google-voice` permission row).
 * "Chip Zabrowsky" is NOT a third party and NOT the owner: it is THE AI'S OWN NAME on the family
 chat. "Zabz" is the owner's nickname for the AI, not "Chips".

WHAT THE SYSTEM HAD DONE. The permissions table carried `+18483897895` as
"Chips Zebrowski / friend / never" - so when the OWNER texted the family AI from his own phone
(measured: 239 inbound, 232 outbound before 2026-06-29, and again on 2026-09-25 asking "What's up
I'm sitting waiting for my X-ray"), the system classified the owner as a friend and REFUSED TO
ANSWER HIM. That single wrong row is why "you have to be able to text me normally" was not true.
Corrected in place on the authority; backup at
`~/.sms-inbox/inbox.db.bak-pre-owner-identity-20260928`.

THE LESSON, AND IT IS NOT ABOUT A NAME. Two failures compounded:
 1. An inference ("the heaviest user of the family line must be a friend of the owner") was written
 into a permissions row as though it were a fact, with `allow=never` - and `never` is the one
 value that makes the system silent rather than wrong. A wrong `queue` gets corrected the first
 time a human notices; a wrong `never` fails invisibly forever.
 2. Identity was inferred from VOLUME instead of from the owner, when the owner is the one person
 the system is never allowed to guess about. The owner's own numbers must be set by the owner
 and never inferred - and evidence for an identity claim must name its source (here: his own
 words, 2026-09-28), not a message count.

HOW TO APPLY IT. When a permissions/identity row decides whether a HUMAN GETS AN ANSWER, an unknown
must resolve to the safe-but-visible side (queue/ask), never to the silent side (never). And a name
written into the record must be traceable to the person it names.
