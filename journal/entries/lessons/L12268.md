**L12268 · MatVT activation key 17 is scriptable - dump, tap by resource-id, then verify with a second dump**

Measured 2026-10-22 on both E4610s (616202539, 616574160), scripted end to end, no human press.

Recipe that works:
 am start -n com.android.cts.io.github.virresh.matvt/.gui.GuiActivity
 uiautomator dump -> cb_override bounds [15,248][225,272]
 input tap 120 260 (expand; et_override and bt_override appear)
 input tap 99 289 (focus the field)
 input text 17
 input keyevent 4 (BACK closes the IME, keeps the edit)
 input tap 204 282 (SAVE at its NEW bounds [183,264][225,300])
 fresh uiautomator dump -> cb_override checked="true", et_override text="17"

Two traps, both of which gave a wrong read first:
- After typing, the soft keyboard covers SAVE. A tap at y~290 hits the keypad and the
 value is never saved - the row stays expanded and looks done.
- The 240x320 screenshot lies twice: an unticked checkbox reads as ticked, and while the
 IME is up et_override reports text="" although the field visibly shows 17. A fresh
 uiautomator dump is the authority; a screenshot is evidence, not a measurement.

General rule: on this hardware, drive the UI by resource-id from a dump and verify by a
SECOND dump after the action. Never accept the screen image as proof of a setting.
