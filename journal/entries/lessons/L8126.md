emergency contact for me. If you need to get through to me, you can [reach me through
her]. But it's her number, not mine."

He is right and the estate had it wrong since 2026-09-18. `.env:383` read
OWNER_ALT_PHONE_NUMBERS=+18483897895,+18482245096. Her number was in the list that
defines "this number IS the owner".

WHAT THAT COST, measured, not inferred
 * `owner_recognized_numbers()` -> `is_owner_number()` returned True for her, so
 sms_service.py:1975 opened an OWNER conversation turn when a text went to her, and
 text-health's own history shows the result: sms_log 4280-4282 refused with "we
 texted HIM 9 minute(s) ago and his own limit is one text every 20 minutes" -- about
 a message to his wife. Three attempts, one message, never delivered until 4284.

 party needs. That is the part that could have hurt someone other than him.
