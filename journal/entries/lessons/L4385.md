# The AI balance is ONE account shared by the company, the wake system AND the agent fleet — and the fleet is the dominant consumer

**Measured 2026-10-15 00:21–00:27Z on the authority, and it is the missing half of the owner's directive.** He said: *"i won't keep rasing the money, it should learn to work within it's budget."* The company cannot be made to work within its budget without controlling the fleet, because the fleet is what spends the budget.

## The proof that it is ONE account (three keys, one balance)

Three different credentials exist, with three different sha256 fingerprints, so the keys are genuinely distinct:

| holder | where | key fingerprint (sha256, first 16 hex) |
|---|---|---|
| the company app | `DEEPSEEK_API_KEY` in `/opt/company/.env` on the authority | `f09706ced3e0870e` |
| the wake guard / authority DSH | first `sk-` in `~/.dsh/.credentials.yaml` on the authority | `af95c38b16bdea7b` |
| this laptop and every mesh worker | `~/.dsh/.credentials.yaml` on ZABZ-YOGA **and** `DEEPSEEK_API_KEY` in `/etc/dsh-worker.env` on zabz-tech-linux | `1b6ef0aea83e072a` |

Distinct keys are not distinct accounts, so fingerprints cannot settle it. **The provider settles it.** Querying `https://api.deepseek.com/user/balance` with each key in turn:

- my fleet's key (`1b6ef0ae…`, from this laptop) -> `total_balance: "4.48"`
- the company's key (`f09706ce…`) -> `total_balance: "4.48"`
- the guard's own reading at the same minute -> `balance 4.48`

**One account, one balance, shared by all three.** No key was ever printed; only hashes and the response.

## Why that matters: the ceiling cannot bind the thing that spends

- **The company's own recorded LLM spend: `0.6539` in the last 60 minutes** (`model_usage`, summed on `substr(created_at,1,19)` — note the trap that cost me a wrong number earlier: `created_at` is an ISO string with a `T`, so comparing it to `datetime('now')` compares `'T'` against `' '` and matches every row. `SELECT` on the un-corrected form returned the identical total for 1h, 6h and 24h, which is how the error announces itself.)
- **The balance actually fell `7.84` -> `4.48`, about `3.36` in roughly 50 minutes = roughly `4.0` per hour** (readings: 23:36:39Z `7.84`; 00:21:53Z `4.53`; 00:26/27Z `4.48`). The hourly cron readings in `wake-balance.log` corroborate a sustained fall (`7.84` -> `5.70` across two of them, ~`2.1`/hour).
- **So roughly 80-85% of the drain is NOT the company's own calls.** It is agent sessions: this very session, the mesh children I dispatched, and whatever the wake system releases.

**The structural defect, in one line:** `WAKE_MAX_USD_PER_DAY` (now a runway-scaled `1.49`/day) gates only *how many releases start*. It does not bound the token cost of the sessions that run, and it does not bound sessions started outside the wake system at all — which is how this session's own fleet behaved. A ceiling of `1.49`/day watching a drain of `4.0`/hour is not a ceiling; the account empties in about an hour regardless of it.

## What a correct control would have to do

1. **Treat the fleet as a budget consumer, not just the ticks.** Any ceiling that does not account for agent-session token cost is decorative.
2. **Measure spend per agent session and attribute it**, the way `model_usage` attributes the company's calls — today the harness's own calls are invisible to every instrument on this estate, which is precisely why the drain looked unexplained.
3. **Gate on the runway, and let the fleet scale down too** — fewer concurrent children, cheaper lanes, shorter sessions — rather than throttling only the wake releases.
4. **The fleet is mine to throttle immediately**, and that is the honest interim control: do not fan out a large fleet on a balance of `4.48`. One child at a time is the correct posture until a spend-aware gate exists.

## The one-line warning for the next session

A balance of single-digit dollars with a measured drain of `2-4`/hour is **about an hour of runway**, and neither the wake ceiling nor the budget tier reflects that, because both were built for the company's ticks and the fleet lives outside them. Before dispatching any fleet: read the balance, and count the sessions you are about to start as spend.
