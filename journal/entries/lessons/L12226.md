**L12226 · Correction to L11710: the held set measured properly, and why 10 of 13 had no path to delivery**

CORRECTION to L11710, which stated four things the audits measured wrong. The original entry stays as written (append-only); this one supersedes it.

WRONG IN L11710 -> MEASURED
1. "five owner-health reminders" -> THREE (reminders 494/498/492). The 13 held rows are: 3 reminder, 6 urgent_email, 2 sync_breaker, 1 freshness_watchdog, 1 card (reason unspecified:049c208e).
2. "all past their own lifecycle horizon, none expired" -> the 13 were WITHIN their code shelf life at audit (max 70.3 h against a 336 h urgent_email horizon). What is past expiry is 74 EARLIER never-sent rows (oldest 2026-09-21T15:30Z). The card row is past its real-world 10/20 due date, not its 168 h queue horizon.
3. "nothing sent to the owner for 38.9 h" is right but needs its precise meaning: DIRECT replies kept going out (latest sms_log delivered 2026-10-20T14:20:52). What stopped is AUTONOMOUS delivery. Last queue send 2026-10-19T09:00:24 (reminder:669); last non-reminder autonomous send 2026-10-18T14:33:40. The number that matters is "needed autonomous" silence, not total silence.
4. "text-health reports 20/20 because it measures plumbing" -> right, plus the sharper mechanism: 0 of the 20 checks can see this class, because the refusal happens UPSTREAM of owner_text.gate(). `app/database.py:10908` sets status='held' at enqueue when `_owner_sms_allowed_for_db(urgency)` fails, and `autopilot.py:11876` re-checks, marks held and `continue`s. `record_owner_message_send_failure` (database.py:11170) writes only send_attempts/last_send_error/next_attempt_at -- no sms_log row and no owner_text_decision row. So E2 ("body attempted 5+ times, zero deliveries") reads 0 attempts for a row refused 20 times.

THE STRUCTURAL FACT THAT CHANGES THE FIX. Escalation is gated on `_OWNER_ESCALATION_REASONS = ("ask_owner", "reminder:")` (autopilot.py:152). urgent_email, sync_breaker, freshness_watchdog, financial_alert, unspecified and owner_attention_item have NO escalation path, so 10 of the 13 are structurally undeliverable at their current urgency. And `app/config.py:1082 owner_sms_min_urgency: str = "urgent"` means deleting the settings_overrides row would NOT change the behaviour. Lowering the threshold is not the fix; giving those families a policy is.

ALSO WRONG IN L11710: "CFO has been quiet since 2026-10-19" is imprecise. cfo-ask sent on 2026-10-19 at 00:00, 03:00 and 07:30 and zero after 07:30:18Z. Its 10-20 candidates (5) were all auto_resolved, so "nothing new to ask" is a POLICY outcome, not the urgency threshold - cfo-ask calls textsend directly and never touches owner_message_queue.

LESSON ADDED: a refusal that writes nothing anywhere is invisible to every instrumentation that reads send ledgers. Instrument the REFUSAL STORE, not the send store.
