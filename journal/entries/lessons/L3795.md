**L3795 · Two always-alive systems on the authority; the cheap ledger-driven one has the better discipline**

The authority runs TWO always-alive agent systems, and the cheap one has the better engineering discipline.

Measured 2026-10-14 ~11:50-12:05Z, both on `secratary-cloud`, read-only:

WAKE LOOP (cron, ledger-driven). Cron: `wake-fanout` every minute (`WAKE_FANOUT_TARGET=3 WAKE_FANOUT_MAX=8 WAKE_MAX_USD_PER_DAY=25 WAKE_MAX_PER_DAY=150`), `run-wake-sources` :07/:37, `wake-balance.py --guard --warn 75` hourly, `wake-cost.py --days 1` every 15 min, `cfo-shift` every 30 min. Its cost log `/root/.sms-inbox/wake-cost-cron.log` ends: "spend today (2026-10-14): 132 release(s), 419788866 token(s) MEASURED across 132 release(s); 0 release(s) UNMAPPED; 0 release(s) with no usage recorded; $9.076644". Per-shift costs $0.03-$0.20. Its contract, in the prompt each woken session receives: claim from the ledger (`work.py claim-next`), and if it answers `nothing-todo`, SAY SO AND STOP; every shift must close its item with `--did/--proof/--result/--left`, and "a shift that ends without this close is recorded as having produced nothing, however well it reads".

THE COMPANY (`/opt/company`, bespoke agent loop). Measured from `/root/authority.db`: last 24 h **45.45 USD** and 174 failed / 93 "completed" work sessions; last 7 days **140.42 USD** and 624 failed / 266 "completed"; failure markers 7d STEP_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED 415, TOOL_LOOP_DEADLOCK 176, STALLED_REPEATING 56. Tick self-grading in 24 h: 192 `complete`/`productive` against 8 `error`. `tick_telemetry.total_cost` is ~1.02 USD/week against 140.42 USD actually spent, so the company cannot report its own spend.

So the 9-USD loop does ~132 units of work a day with a proof required per unit and a hard daily ceiling; the 45-USD loop fails about two thirds of what it attempts and its completion signal is a token. The lesson is not "the company is bad" — it is that **the discipline is the cheap part and the bespoke loop is the expensive part**, and the two systems have overlapping purpose with no single owner.

This reframes the owner's headline ask ("audit and evolve the AI company… an order of magnitude better"): the better architecture may already exist on the same box, doing engineering projects, for a fifth of the money. Workstream A9 tests whether the wake loop's RESULTS justify that reading before any architecture decision is taken from the cost table alone.

Not to be confused with each other: the wake loop advances engineering projects (lpt-sync, lpt-website, cfo, kosher-ai-filter, housekeeping, prod-db-sync, personality-system, chumash); the company runs business operations (reconcile, inventory, comms status, research scan). The overlap is cfo and comms.
