**L2062 · Stale first impression: read every row of a generated plan, not its headline**

## What happened
I ran my own caller program's `plan` command, saw the headline "Plan: 30 calls over 45
minutes", skimmed the table, and called it working. The actual last line read T+25:57 and
only 15 of the 30 messages were distinct. Both defects were visible in output I had already
generated - I just read the summary, not the substance.

## The rule
When a demo/generation command prints a table, read EVERY row and the extremes, not the
header. Specifically check: min, max and last value; the count of distinct values where
distinctness is the requirement; and whether the numbers add up to the claim.

Two concrete defects that only appeared at the extremes:
 - message reuse: the index formula `(i*7 + floor(i/nVoices)) % nMsgs` cycles the same
 4 messages per voice cycle -> 15 distinct out of 30. Fix: shuffle the index pool and
 consume it without repetition.
 - window not filled: 29 gaps drawn from [30,90]s average 60s = 26 minutes, not 45.
 Fix: derive intervals from the requested window, and REPORT windowFillPct when the
 interval limits make the request impossible.

## Corollary worth keeping
A request can be self-contradictory, and the tool should say so rather than silently
picking one horn. 30 calls over 45 min with max 90s gaps is arithmetically impossible
(29 x 90s = 43.5 min > 45 min is fine, but with min 30s and random draws it is not) - the
honest output states the shortfall instead of pretending.
