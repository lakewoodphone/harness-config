**L3712 · BK Kosher = Balkan Kosher (Thessaloniki) — procedure for identifying an unknown hechsher**

Asked by the owner 2026-10-12: "is this kosher, i never saw this hechsher before" over a Walmart page for Violife Just Like Smoked Provolone Slices.

RESOLVED — the mark is BK Kosher, i.e. BALKAN KOSHER, Thessaloniki, Greece.
- Crown over "BK" + the word KOSHER underneath; logo asset matches https://www.balkankosher.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Asset-7CLEAN.png exactly.
- Rabbinical Director Rabbi Yosef Kaplan (Chabad shliach to Thessaloniki); org is a branch of Balkan Jewish Communities (501c3), offices Thessaloniki/Tirana/Bet Shemesh/Miami; backed by Rohr Foundation + Merkos Chabad, encouraged by R' Shlomo Amar. Site balkankosher.org.
- Balkan Kosher publishes ARIVIA S.A. as a client (balkankosher.org/arivia/). ARIVIA is the Greek manufacturer of Violife.

HOW TO IDENTIFY AN UNKNOWN HECHSHER (reusable procedure, ~5 minutes):
1. Crop and upscale the symbol off the photo before guessing (PIL crop + LANCZOS x6-7 on this box; the symbol was at ~(1020,1130)-(1260,1300) of a 2815x1488 screenshot).
2. Fetch the certifier's own logo/asset and compare byte-for-byte with the printed mark — a name guess is worthless until the logo matches.
3. The two symbol directories that actually resolve small agencies:
 - IKG / Einheitsgemeinden Koscherliste (icz.org, e.g. Koscherliste.Einheitsgemeinden.02.26.pdf) — has a symbol table AND per-product rows with conditions ("NUR mit Symbol BK").
 - cRc list of kosher agencies (consumer.crckosher.org/kosher-agencies-pdf/) — 403 to plain curl; needs a browser.
4. For US-market items, the OU product database is searched through the browser (playwright) at oukosher.org/product-search/ — the hash URL does not re-run the query, you must type into the box. Distributors also publish OU letters of certification as PDFs (e.g. argo.webstaurantstore.com/documents/pdf/<brand>_kosher.pdf).
5. Greek/Balkan certifier PDFs are excellent evidence because they name the producer and the exact product list, e.g. dnomikos.gr and papadimitriou.com.gr publish Balkan Kosher letters; balkankosher.org publishes per-client posts.

FINDINGS ON VIOLIFE (2026-10-12, all citing source):
- IKG Koscherliste Feb 2026: symbol table lists "BK = Balkan Kosher (Thessaloniki)"; a product row reads "Violife — Mozzarella Geschmack Vegan Gerieben 200g — NUR mit Symbol BK" (only with the BK symbol). So a mainstream kehilla list already relies on this exact certification for Violife.
- OU product database (oukosher.org, read live 2026-10-12): Violife appears only for SHREDS — 10 rows, all Pareve/OU ("Symbol required. Not Kosher for Passover."). No slices, no smoked provolone, no cream cheese. A "provolone" search returns Good Planet Foods Plant-based Smoked Provolone Slices and Daiya Dairy-Free Provolone Slices as OU pareve, but no Violife.
- An OU letter dated 2026-02-16 for Flora Food Group (433 Hackensack Ave, NJ) covers product "91304064 PB BRICK SALTED 36PK 1LB USA", Violife brand, Pareve — so Flora holds OU for part of the line, but the OU list is NOT exhaustive of Flora items (that brick is absent from the searchable DB). Absence from the OU DB is therefore suggestive, not conclusive.
- The Walmart pack art (US label: net wt 7.05 oz, US Nutrition Facts) carries the BK mark and the Vegan Society sunflower; no OU visible on the face.

CONCLUSION GIVEN TO OWNER: parve plant-only product (water, modified starch, coconut oil, sea salt, natural flavors, rowanberry extract, smoke flavor, citric acid, olive extract, beta carotene, B12), certified by a real but small regional agency; recognizable and recognized, not an OU/Star-K-class hechsher. Recommendation: acceptable; if the house standard wants a mainstream symbol, Violife shreds are OU and Good Planet / Daiya smoked provolone slices are OU pareve.

METHOD NOTE: whole investigation done with 1 image crop + web fetches + one playwright session; no shell loops needed beyond PDF text extraction (fitz) and downloads.
