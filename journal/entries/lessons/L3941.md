**L3941 · Superwhisper: the cloud was a 3000-word free trial, and the 1.6 GB local model is the slowest free choice on an 8 GB phone**

**L · He never "ran out of a subscription" - Superwhisper meters free Pro as 3,000 words, and the 1.6 GB local model is the slowest free choice on an 8 GB phone**

HIS WORDS, 2026-10-13: *"Originally I tried the Cloud one until I ran out of the subscription and it was
pretty good. And now I moved to the 1.6 gigabyte V3 Whisper model and it takes way too long... The Cloud
model took about two or three seconds at most. This takes like 15 seconds."*

**THE TRIAL, not a subscription -- this is the fact that explains the whole complaint.** Superwhisper meters
free Pro access by WORDS, not time, and **the cloud models are inside that allowance and nowhere else**:
macOS 2.18.2 (2026-08-26) -- *"Free Pro trial changed from 15 minutes to 3,000 words, and reset for existing
users"*; iOS 2.21.1 (2026-08-27) -- *"Whisper Models are now free for everyone!"*. Before that the trial was
15 minutes of Pro. **Cloud models are Pro-only and are NOT in the free tier**, so the good 2-3 second
experience was a metered allowance being spent from the first dictation. It was always going to end.
Consequence: "go back to the cloud" is not a free option in this app, and BYOK is Pro-gated too, so the free
tier cannot pay-per-use around it either.

**WHY 15 SECONDS IS EXPECTED, not a bug (two vendor-documented facts):**
1. The vendor's own first cause of slow dictation is **model LOAD time**: *"Model choice and model load time
 usually matter most"* and *"Local models need free memory... pick a smaller model such as Whisper Base,
 or switch that mode to a cloud model"* (superwhisper.com/docs/common-issues/performance-tips). A 1.6 GB
 model on an 8 GB phone is a model that is evicted and re-loaded. **iOS has no "voice model active
 duration" control** -- that setting is documented for DESKTOP only -- so it cannot be tuned around.
2. **Whisper Large v3 Turbo is rated 8/10 for speed by its own maker -- the same as Whisper Small -- at
 more than three times the size.**

**THE MODEL TABLE (superwhisper.com/docs/models/voice, fetched 2026-10-13) -- keep it, it is re-asked constantly:**
Tiny (English) 75 MB, old name *Fast*, speed 10, accuracy 1 | Base (English) 150 MB, *Nano*, 9, 3 |
**Small (English) 500 MB, *Standard*, 8, 5** | Large v3 Turbo 1.6 GB, *Ultra V3 Turbo*, 8, 8 (his) |
Medium 1.5 GB, 6, 8 | Large v3 3 GB, *Ultra*, 5, 10. Pro-only and genuinely fast: **Parakeet 476 MB (10/10
speed)**, Cohere Transcribe 1.3 GB (9/10). **Names changed -- he will say "V3" and "the 1.6 gigabyte one";
that is Large v3 Turbo / Ultra V3 Turbo.**

**THE DECISION, recorded so it is not re-litigated:** (1) free, one tap -- switch the model to **Whisper
Small (English)** and dictate in a **Voice to Text** mode (transcription only, no AI pass, "results arrive
quickly"); (2) if that still feels slow, **stop tuning and pay: 84.99 a year, select S1-Voice** -- which is
what his 2-3 seconds actually was. 7.08 a month buys the end of a two-week argument.

**DO NOT PROMISE A NUMBER.** No iPhone latency measurement for ANY dictation model exists, from any party --
the vendor's benchmark page is M4 Mac only and says *"a slower machine will be slower"*. And **no free local
Whisper model on an 8 GB iPhone should be assumed to match cloud latency.** Free in this app means Whisper
only; every fast engine is paywalled. Stating that plainly is the difference between advice and a promise.
