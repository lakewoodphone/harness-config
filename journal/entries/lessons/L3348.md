**L3348 · NY's pre-insurance photo inspection still exists; the 2024 relief act made it waivable per-insurer, not repealed**

Fetched and read 2026-10-06 while building the Yitzchak insurance case file.

WHAT IS TRUE
- 11 NYCRR 67.4 still requires an insurer to notify the named insured of a **physical-damage**
 inspection and permits a 14 calendar-day deferral, with coverage "suspended at 12:01 a.m. on the
 day following the fourteenth day" if not completed. Amended effective 2024-06-18.
 https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/new-york/11-NYCRR-67.4
- NY DFS still publishes it today: failure to comply "will result in a suspension of physical damage
 coverage"; "There is no cost to you for this inspection." https://www.dfs.ny.gov/consumers/auto_insurance
- What the 2024 Auto Insurance Consumer Relief Act did was let an insurer WAIVE the inspection, by
 FILING WITH THE SUPERINTENDENT, per insurer (DFS Final Adoption, 7th Amendment to Reg 79,
 2024-06-04). Effective 2024-05-15 and SUNSETS 2027-10-01. It is not a repeal.
- So it is carrier-by-carrier: GEICO filed a blanket waiver ("Effective June 1, 2024 New York vehicle
 photo inspections are no longer required", geico.com/auto-insurance/states/ny/vehicle-inspection-faqs)
 while other carriers may not have filed at all.

WHY THIS IS A LESSON AND NOT A FACT
Popular reporting says New York "ended" the photo inspection. Believing that headline is exactly how
a car ends up with NO collision and NO comprehensive, silently, because the failure mode is a
suspension of physical-damage coverage rather than a cancellation and nothing announces it. The owner
lost the physical-damage coverage on his own 2022 Elantra to the New Jersey version of this same rule
(N.J.A.C. 11:3-36.5/36.7; 7 days there, stricter) and misread the resulting refund cheque as a
cancellation for months. Reinstatement is effective AT THE TIME OF INSPECTION and is never backdated,
so every day of the gap is uninsured time on the car.

WHAT TO DO INSTEAD
Ask the carrier, in writing, before binding: "Does this policy require a pre-insurance photo
inspection?" If yes, diary the 14-day deadline the day the policy starts. Do not infer the answer
from the state's name or from a competitor's practice.

REGULATORY-NUANCE WARNING (the naive version of the garaging rule is wrong in BOTH directions)
- NY Ins Law 3105: no voiding for a NON-material misrepresentation; if the insurer would merely have
 charged more, it "may not void the policy retroactively" (DFS OGC Op. 06-12-11).
- Where LIABILITY is concerned, V&T Law 313 protects the innocent third party and permits PROSPECTIVE
 CANCELLATION ONLY, even against a policy procured by fraud (Teeter v. Allstate; INA v. Kaplun).
- But 3425 makes "discovery of fraud or material misrepresentation in obtaining the policy" express
 grounds for MID-TERM CANCELLATION on 20 days notice, and physical-damage coverage has NO
 innocent-third-party shield, because that shield exists to satisfy the financial-responsibility law
 for liability. So: do not tell anyone "they can just void you" (false) and do not tell them "it is
 fine" (also false). State what each coverage actually risks.
