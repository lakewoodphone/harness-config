**L6978 · A percentage of CLEAR tells you nothing about whether the clear ones are right - one of them moved a P&L figure by 2,108.30**

Measured 2026-10-16 on the CFO ledger. The scoreboard read pct_clear 99.28 both before and after a correction that moved LPT's net income by 2,108.30, because the row in question was always categorised - it was categorised WRONGLY. The mechanism: a rule (CR-493b007f0f69, method duplicate-copy) was learned from three duplicate copies whose categories DISAGREED - two said Credit Card Payment(s), money movement, and one said Phone/SMS Services, an expense - and the minority was written. On the same account, lpt-chase-3929, a 5,460.41 Returned Payment was correctly reasoned as a card-payment reversal while a 2,108.30 row with the SAME description sat in an expense, overstating LPT expenses and understating its profit by 2,108.30. Fixed by retracting the self-contradicting rule to a rejected file and correcting the two rows it wrote through an explicit UPDATE with a recategorize audit_log entry and a cp -a backup: lpt net income -46,479.34 -> -44,371.04, escape101 and personal unchanged. Then the finding was generalised rather than left as a one-off: a detector for rules citing duplicate copies that disagree reports 7 of 371, and honesty about it matters because the detector compares strings and meaning is not a string - one is a spelling variant, three are my own deliberate ATM-deposit rule, one writes the book's dominant treatment, one disagrees only about which expense, and ONE is material and named for next: desc:chase crd epay xxxxx50491 writes Rent where a copy says Credit Card Payment. Two lessons: a completeness metric is not a correctness metric, so a book that is 99 percent clear can still carry a wrong figure in a P&L line - the check that finds it is internal consistency (does this rule's own evidence agree with itself), not coverage; and when a rule's evidence contradicts itself, the rule must be retracted rather than voted on, because a rule is a claim about a mechanism and a self-contradicting one has no claim to make.
