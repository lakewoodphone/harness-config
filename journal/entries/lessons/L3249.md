**L3249 · Read the source not the task summary; read the monitor before fanning out; a category holding everything is not a category**

L3241 - Read the source, not the task's summary of the source.

Measured 2026-10-05. The board carried eight tasks about 300DDR data-recovery lab mail
(#27259, #27385, #27306, #27402, #26891, #26816, #26786, #26619), every one marked
`done` while its own body asserted the email could not be read: "gmail_read returns 404",
"circuit breaker HALF_OPEN, 8 failures", "thread not found in any index", "the referenced
email no longer exists in Gmail". A reminder about the same tickets had been "blocked 25
times by the send guard and has never reached you" (owner_decision_queue 211).

I nearly reported an unpaid lab bill and a lost customer-data crisis. Instead I called
gmail_service directly and pulled 28 lab messages: all five tickets were fine. Two had
been paid on 2026-10-02 (520.01 AMEX, 500.00 Visa, confirmed against the Square receipt
pages), one had shipped and was arriving that day, one was closed, one was declared
unrecoverable. Nothing was ever unreadable. The mail sat in the inbox the whole time.

A `done` task is a claim, not a fact. When a task says a source could not be read, the
first move is to read the source. If you cannot state where a fact came from and when it
was written, you do not have the fact.

L3242 - Read the monitor before fanning out a fleet.

Measured 2026-10-05, same session. I dispatched four remote subagents to the mesh. All four
failed identically: "the remote one-shot exited 1 and produced no final message". The
company's own monitor had already written rows 2376-2386 to `owner_message_queue` between
2026-10-05T00:05Z and 03:00Z saying `lakewooechsmini` timed out and `zabz-yoga-1` was slow,
with the exact remedy. I had the queue open before dispatching and did not read it.

Before a fleet: read the mesh/capacity monitor, and run one trivial dispatch as a probe
rather than committing the whole fan-out. A failed fan-out costs the full brief-writing
time and produces nothing.

L3243 - A category that holds everything is not a category.

Measured 2026-10-05. The live email triage (`email_triage_log`, 3550 rows) assigns
`category='personal'` to 3481 of them - 98%. The bucket carries no signal, so nothing is
prioritised, and every "how many need attention" question answered from it is meaningless.
When a classifier output is one value almost always, the classifier is the finding.
