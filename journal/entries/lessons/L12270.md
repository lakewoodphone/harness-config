**L12270 · Bar 1's API-30 failure is a TEST RACE, not platform non-uniformity: the seal applies asynchronously and the suite asserts synchronously**

MEASURED 2026-10-22 on Medium_Phone_API_30 (Android 11), wiped, app installed, then dpm set-device-owner. Result: 'Success: Device owner set'. Twelve seconds later logcat shows 'LPTSealPolicy: Seal policy at admin_enabled applied=true' with SEAL_RESTRICTION no_install_unknown_sources=true, no_usb_file_transfer=true, no_create_windows=true, and dumpsys device_policy reports permittedAccessibilityServices=[com.lakewoodphoneandtech.filterai]. So on Android 11 the seal DOES apply and the accessibility allowlist IS installed. The instrumented suite had reported the exact opposite - 'dpm.getPermittedAccessibilityServices(admin) returned null, which means NO allowlist is installed' - plus four sibling failures, all of the same not-applied shape. The difference is TIME, not platform: dpm set-device-owner delivers ACTION_DEVICE_OWNER_CHANGED / onEnabled, the apply happens in a BroadcastReceiver, and the suite ran connectedDebugAndroidTest immediately after dpm returned and asserted the policy before the receiver had finished. THE TRANSFERABLE RULE, and it is the measurement rule again: before asserting a field, ask what WRITES it and whether that write is synchronous. A policy written by a broadcast handler must be awaited, not assumed. The five tests should poll for the applied state with a bounded timeout and a named failure ('the seal never applied within Ns') rather than assert once, immediately. This also explains why API 34 passed all fourteen: that AVD had the app already installed and opened from an earlier session, so policy was long since applied. NOT YET FIXED - the fix belongs in android/app/src/androidTest (a bounded await helper), and until it lands bar 1's 'not uniform' verdict is WRONG and must not be reported as a product defect.
