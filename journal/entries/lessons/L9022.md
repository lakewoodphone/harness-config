**L9022 · One owner sentence about money killed eight tasks: close the cluster, not the row**

The owner explained one money event - "i am waiting for it to bounce back, i didn't want to pay that much of the 9650 credit card this month it was a mistake" - and closing that single answer meant killing EIGHT open tasks and one held alert that had all been generated around the same false premise.

Measured on the authority, 2026-10-18:
- owner_message_queue id 2631 (rendered as queue #m2631, surface=held): "returned_payment 2380.06 on Personal Chase Card *9650". repeat_count 21, send_attempts 15, last_send_error "held: urgency 'high' is below the send threshold 'urgent'". It had been trying to reach him for two days. Now status=dismissed with status_note, next_attempt_at NULL.
- tasks cancelled: 29232 (FINANCE ALERT), 29240 and 29229 and 29248 (three separate "reconciliation packet" builders), 29250 ("[ACTION] Re-pay 2380.06 to Chase *9650"), 29089 ("OWNER ACTION: address Chase Card *9650 returned payment" - a task that asks HIM to do the thing he had just explained), 29193 and 29213 ("reconcile ... against the originating charge and confirm whether it was a customer refund, a failed ACH, or a chargeback"). Query after: 0 open tasks matching 9650/returned_payment.

The false premise, stated so the next session does not rebuild it: a returned payment on a personal credit card is the owner paying a card too heavily, not a merchant refund, not a failed ACH and not a chargeback. There is no originating charge to reconcile. Re-payment is not "the fix" - he chose the smaller payment.

Transferable rules:
1. One owner sentence about money closes the whole cluster, not the row he said it on. After answering a money item, query tasks and owner_message_queue for the same amount and the same last-4 BEFORE moving on, or the same event comes back wearing three different hats.
2. A task titled "OWNER ACTION: <explain your own transaction back to us>" is a defect by construction. If the answer is already in the owner's head, the task is not his - it is missing information on our side.
3. The withheld-alert path degrades silently: 15 send attempts, one line of last_send_error, a repeat_count of 21, and no one reads it. A held row that has exhausted its retries should surface once as an engineering fault, not forever as quiet.
