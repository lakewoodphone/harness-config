**L7935 · NJ ST-50: repair labour is taxable, basis is accrual, and the receipt tool says the opposite**

Measured 2026-10-18 while building the ST-50 basis for 2026 Q3.

1. WHERE EXEMPTNESS COMES FROM. NJ S&U-4 (Rev 07/22, fetched live) taxes the sale of TPP AND the sale of services to TPP: most services on tangible personal property are taxable; its services table marks Appliance Repairs T and Automobile Repairs Labor T; and its Urban Enterprise Zone carve-out excludes sales of services (e.g., maintenance and/or repairs). So a phone repair is taxable labour+parts, and the usual labour/parts/device distinction creates NO exempt bucket. The only exemptions available to this shop are resale (ST-3), exempt organisation (ST-5), exempt items (none), and delivery outside NJ. Exempt = 0.00 in every period, and it is unsupported rather than proven zero: no destination field exists on any sale in the ledger.

2. THE ESTATE BELIEVED THE OPPOSITE AND IT IS IN CODE. opt/company/scripts/send_lpt_receipt.py line 42 and its --no-tax flag help say NJ labor is not taxable. Measured cost in 2026 Q3: 53 of 191 Stripe charges totalled 4762.00 at bare round prices with no tax in them (descriptions dominated by repair/rep), 315.48 of tax never collected — and due anyway, because the ST-50 worksheet takes the GREATER of tax collected and balance x rate.

3. BASIS IS ACCRUAL, and that is not a preference. S&U-4: The accrual method of accounting must be used for Sales Tax records. So the charge date decides the quarter, not the Stripe payout. lpt-monthly-pnl.py counts Stripe NET PAYOUTS (its own comment, lines 406-448), i.e. net of the processor fee — that is not gross receipts, and it also shifts revenue across quarter ends. Never use canonical PNL revenue as a sales-tax basis.

4. MOST PRICES ARE TAX-INCLUSIVE. 138 of 191 Q3 charges equal round(P x 1.06625) exactly (53.31=50, 133.28=125, 213.25=200). Applying 6.625 percent to a tax-inclusive total charges tax on tax: 25629.19 x .06625 = 1697.93 where the correct figure is 24674.97 x .06625 = 1634.72.

5. THE LEDGER FLAGS ITS OWN DUPLICATES — USE THE FLAG. The inherited Q3 card figure 20161.17 included a 42.65 charge on 2026-08-04 that carries duplicate_of_transaction_id with a recorded reason. Correct sum 20118.52. Always filter duplicate_of_transaction_id IS NULL and ingestion_status=active, and never sum a monthly rollup query without them.

6. THE BOOKS CANNOT AUDIT THE PAST: for 2026 Q1 the books compute HIGHER than the owner filed (+64.69), which proves the books understate revenue somewhere. A books-vs-return delta is two records that do not reconcile, not a finding of underpayment.

7. MONTHLY VOUCHERS: su_12.shtml makes the test conjunctive — tax over 500 AND more than 30000 collected in the prior calendar year. LPT collected about 563 in 2025, so quarterly ST-50 is correct and the monthly Gov2Go reminders are generic prompts to every registrant (the owner brother wrote this down on 2025-04-10).
