**L2583 · Push after your first coherent commit is not enough: two children lost 20 minutes each because a checkpoint was never taken - say WHEN, WHAT and that a wip commit is fine**

Measured 2026-09-25, on this fleet. "Push after your first coherent commit" is NOT enough: the two
children given the biggest UI jobs (`subagent-44` work-orders hub, `subagent-45` customers list) died on
the ssh transport at ~20 minutes with **NOTHING pushed**, because neither had reached a state it considered
"coherent" - both were still editing. Two other children in the same batch that had committed early
(`subagent-29` A2P, `subagent-34` pricing map) survived their own drops with their work intact.

THE RULE, now in the briefs: **a child must push a checkpoint within the first 10 minutes, explicitly
labelled `wip`, even with failing tests, and then push after every increment.** A checkpoint that is ugly
costs nothing; a lost run costs the whole slice. Two corollaries:
- **Keep the first increment small** - change one thing, commit it, continue. A child that tries to finish
 the redesign before its first commit has nothing when the channel drops.
- **A brief that only says "push early" is not enough.** It must say WHEN (10 minutes), WHAT (a wip commit,
 tests may be red), and that ugliness is acceptable. Otherwise the instruction is read as "push when it is
 good", which is exactly the state that never arrives.

CONTEXT: this node dropped 9 of ~14 children today across several batches, always with the same stderr
(`Read from remote host ... Connection timed out / client_loop: send disconnect: Connection reset`) at
15-25 minutes. The drops are survivable only because of checkpointing; the transport itself is not
something this seat can fix, so the method has to absorb it.
