**Reading method, because two sessions called this unreadable:** a YouTube watch or playlist page serves no text to a fetch, but
- `https://www.youtube.com/feeds/videos.xml?playlist_id=<ID>` returns titles, video ids, order and publisher as XML (capped at 15 entries), and
- `https://www.youtube.com/oembed?url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=<ID>&format=json` returns title + channel for one video.
