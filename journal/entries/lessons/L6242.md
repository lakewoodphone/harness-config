**L · Trace an unexplained bank debit to the other side of the transfer, and read the alert's body, not its subject.**

Owner asked, 2026-10-14: *"where was that 2300 dollar to my chase account from, which card was it paying"*
— about the 2,380.06 that overdrew Chase checking `...9730`. Answer, fully resolved, in
`lpt-hub/docs/operations/attention-sweep-2026-10-14.md` §1.

**The method that worked: match the amount on the opposite side.** A bank debit descriptor alone cannot
identify a card — `ORIG CO NAME:CHASE CREDIT CRD CO ENTRY DESCR:AUTOPAYBUS … ORIG ID:4760039224` names
the *originator*, never the account. Querying the same absolute amount across **all** accounts returned
the pair instantly: **+2,380.06 out of checking `9730`** (2026-10-13, still `pending`) and **−2,380.06
into credit card `9650`** (2026-10-11, `AUTOMATIC PAYMENT - THANK`). Same originator id. One internal
Chase-to-Chase payment, to the cent. Do this before theorising: an internal transfer is invisible from
one side only.

**The defect was the funding account, not the card.** Two Chase card payments, same originator, two
different source accounts:

| card | amount | drawn from |
|---|---|---|
| `3929` | 1,524.18 | **`7930` Business Adv Fundamentals** (business) |
| `9650` | 2,380.06 | **`9730` CHASE COLLEGE** (personal, held ~72.47) |

The business card autopay is wired to the business account; this one is wired to a personal account
holding 72 dollars. That — not the card choice — is what produced the overdraft.

**Two errors caught mid-investigation, both worth remembering:**

1. **I nearly attributed the wrong card from a subject line.** *"Your Ink Business Unlimited Visa
 automatic payment is scheduled"* (Chase, 2026-10-10) names card **`...5578`**, due 2026-10-20,
 auto-pay **939.18**, statement balance **4,692.89** — a different card and a different amount. The
 subject matched the pattern I was hunting and I had already written 5578 into the record before
 reading the body. **The body is the only place the account number, due date and amount live; a
 subject line is a label, not data.** Generalise: for any vendor alert, the numbers are in the body.

2. **Two subsystems held contradictory beliefs about the same card, and neither noticed.** Our own
 system texted the owner on 2026-10-10 — *"Chase CC (...9650) payment is due 10/11/2026 — schedule the
 payment in the Chase portal before the due date"* — i.e. it believed nothing was scheduled, while a
 2,380 automatic pull was already pointed at an account holding 72. A reminder that says "schedule
 the payment" is only correct if nothing is scheduled; whoever emits that text has no view of the
 autopay state. Unfixed.

**Also not established, and stated as such in the record:** whether `9650`'s pull was true autopay or a
one-off scheduled payment. The card descriptor says `AUTOMATIC PAYMENT` and the bank side says
`AUTOPAYBUS`, but **no "set up automatic payments" email exists for `9650`** in the ingested window
(`3929` has one dated 2026-09-20, `4258` dated 2026-10-08 — both bodies read). Say what the evidence
supports and name what it does not.

**Still unfixed and now the live risk:** whatever schedules that 2,380 pull out of `9730` will attempt it
again next cycle against an account whose observed balance was 72.47.
