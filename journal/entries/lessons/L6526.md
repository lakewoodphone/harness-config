**L6526 · Ask what DELIVERS the question, not only what answers it: 21,861 USD was waiting on questions no channel had ever put to him**

Measured 2026-10-16 on the CFO system. The park store held 21 clusters worth 22,081.21 and the owner mouth had asked three of them; 18 had never been asked by anything, only 2 appeared anywhere in the owner decision queue, and together they carried 21,861.15. The cause was structural: cfo-ask.py candidates() groups on normalized_merchant and requires it to be non-empty, and those clusters exist precisely BECAUSE there is no merchant string - Returned Payment, 'CB SEL FD CL A CB SEL FD PPD ID: 1461050694', PAYMENT - THANK YOU. So every report of 'this money is waiting on the owner' was wrong: it was waiting on nobody, because nobody had asked. This is the same shape as the earlier finding that the weekly reconciliation wrote a verdict nothing read, one layer further out. The discipline: when a residual is attributed to a human, check the CHANNEL, not just the question - name which process would put it to him, how it names the cluster, and what filter could exclude it. The fix (parked_candidates() in cfo-ask.py, additive, behind CFO_ASK_INCLUDE_PARKED=1 and OFF by default because enabling it changes what he receives by text) makes 16 of the 18 offerable, worth 20,765.82, and the two biggest are among them. Also worth keeping: an unsent ask row does not consume a question - asked_keys is built from WHERE sent=1 - so a dry run is safe to run, which I verified rather than assumed.
