**L6132 · The window's payment is exactly the opening balance, and 93 per cent of the gap is one merchant charged twice**

2026-10-13. The window is now enumerated row by row, the payment is exactly the opening balance, and the
gap narrows to one merchant.

THE 37 ROWS, in full: 36 debits and exactly ONE credit.
 2025-03-11 credit 548.64 'AUTOMATIC PAYMENT - THANK'
 signed flow total 8,878.77
and 548.64 is EXACTLY the 2025-02-18 anchor. So the story the ledger tells is: the card owed 548.64 on
18 February, it was paid in full on 11 March, and it ended owing 4,079.69 on 18 March. The movement is
therefore +3,531.05 and the flow says +8,878.77, a gap of 5,347.72 - and the arithmetic closes exactly:
charges 9,427.41 - payment 548.64 = flow 8,878.77, and 9,427.41 - 3,531.05 - 548.64 = 5,347.72. So
5,347.72 of recorded charges are not reflected in the statement's balance movement at all.

THE SINGLE LARGEST CANDIDATE, and it is the pair I flagged in a different context two rounds ago:
 2025-02-19 debit 206.81 PAYPAL *REEBELOUSAI
 2025-02-20 debit 2,481.72 PAYPAL *REEBELOUSAI
 2025-03-17 debit 2,481.72 PAYPAL *REEBELOUSAI <- same merchant, same amount, 25 days later
Both 2,481.72 charges fall inside this one window. Two charges of an identical, unusual amount at one
merchant five weeks apart, in a month that cannot account for its own balance movement, is the shape of
one purchase recorded twice - and at 4,963.44 it is 93 per cent of the 5,347.72 gap.

WHY MY EARLIER CHECK MISSED IT, which is the methodological point. I tested for unmarked duplicates by
requiring the SAME DATE, and found zero groups; 25 days apart fails that key by construction. A
same-date test can only ever find the import-twice defect, never the charged-twice defect, and the two
need different evidence: the first is a database question, the second is a statement question.

WHAT WOULD SETTLE IT, and what I cannot reach from here: the statement DETAIL, not the alert. The alert
email carries only `Statement balance`, a due date and a minimum payment - I read five of them and they
contain no transaction list. The detail sits behind the 'View statement' link, so this one needs the PDF,
not the mailbox. That is a bounded next step for whoever has the statement access, and it is the same
step that would close the other six sign-flips: the alerts give authoritative BALANCES and no ITEMS.
