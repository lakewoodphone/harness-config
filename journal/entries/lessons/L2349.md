**L2349 · secratary has no photo or face identification; family_photo_context_resolver identifies nobody**

Searched 2026-09-22 while answering an owner image question.

FINDING: nothing in the company identifies a person from a photograph.
- 203-table DB: no photo/image/face/embedding table. Identity is phone-keyed only (person_canonical, person_phone, comms_identity_link, contacts). contacts holds name/relationship/phone - no image, no biometric.
- Host filesystem: only photos on secratary are network-install photos and plumbing photos.
- ~/personal-secretary-mvp/data/tools/family_photo_context_resolver.py (75 lines) is named as if it resolves who is in a family photo. It does not. It takes family_member as an INPUT param and echoes it back into notes/summary; zero lookup, zero matching, no ML, no image read. If called with no params it returns ok:false. Anything reading its name and believing it is a face-identification capability is being misled by the filename.
- app/services/board_photo_analyzer.py is circuit-board repair analysis, not people.
- No local vision/face-embedding deps in .venv (no face_recognition/dlib/insightface path found).

CONSEQUENCE: an owner request to identify a person from a photo cannot be served by lookup, and must not be answered by guessing. Correct response is to say so plainly and ask for the name.

LESSON: filenames and table names that assert an AI capability are not evidence of it. Verify by reading the body and running it.
