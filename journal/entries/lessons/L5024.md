**L5024 · REFUTED by its own control: a low-output guard would have closed 80% of the sessions that COMPLETE - completed and failed traces are the same size**

CORRECTION to the pain filed on this tree as P4314 (the STEP_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED low-output guard). The remedy in that entry was wrong and the control that refutes it is two queries.

I proposed: after N steps, if the accumulated trace is under ~3 KB AND fewer than ~3 distinct tools have been used, close the session BLOCKED. I had measured only the FAILING population (286 of 335 step-budget sessions are under 3 KB, average 2,275 bytes) and never asked what a SUCCESSFUL session's trace looks like.

 status sessions avg_bytes under_3kb under_1.5kb under_800
 failed 478 2095.0 85% 72% 10%
 completed 288 2055.0 80% 64% 33%
 paused 61 1348.0 91% 73% 47%

The two populations are INDISTINGUISHABLE on trace size -- 2,095 vs 2,055 bytes, 85% vs 80% under 3 KB. 231 of the 288 completed sessions are under 3 KB. The guard as I wrote it would have closed 80% of the sessions that succeed. Distinct-tool count does not separate them either: sampled completions and failures both show ~2 tool signatures.

So the step-budget class has no cheap structural signal, and the honest statement is that my proposed fix was harmful rather than merely unproven. What DOES separate them is WHICH tools ran -- filed separately with the table.
