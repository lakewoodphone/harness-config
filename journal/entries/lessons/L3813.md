**L3813 · Hand-fixes to the loaded instruction surface are reverted, and the deployer reports clean while it happens**

Hand-fixes to the loaded instruction surface do not survive, and the deployer reports `clean` while they are undone.

MEASURED 2026-10-14, same day, same machines. At **12:25Z** I did the same on ZABZ-YOGA (`yocheved/agent.cordis.yml`, `parallel-agent-orchestration`, `secretary-wake`) and its detector reported **`match=15 drift=0`**.

**Every one of those loaded hashes is byte-for-byte the value the detector reported BEFORE the fix.** `03ab4611f36a`, `c40a5a5a1112`, `f5125f8badb5`, `207922ab745a`, `e186674a7158` are the exact pre-fix hashes. **`0 file(s) written`, and `result: "clean"`, at 12:47 — between my verified fix and its disappearance.** I cannot from this evidence alone name the step that rewrote the six files, and I am recording that rather than guessing: what is *established* is that the loaded surface was byte-identical to the repo at 12:20, and is not at 12:57, and that the only writer configured to touch `~/.dsh/skills` on that machine reported writing nothing.

**What follows, and it is the durable lesson.** `autosync.ps1` exports **`git archive HEAD`** into a temp snapshot and runs `sync.py` from there (`L3807`), so only committed content is deployed. A hand-fix to a loaded file is therefore not a fix — it is a **temporary reprieve with a typo's durability**, and any subsequent sync pass can restore the committed bytes at any moment, including one that reports success. **The only durable way to change what an agent is told is to change the commit the machine's sync exports.**

**And that has a direct consequence for today's work, stated plainly.** The persona improvement deployed at 12:57Z — the four new rules, verified present on both machines — **is not durable on ZABZ-TECH** for exactly this reason, because I wrote its working tree and not a commit on its sync lineage. On ZABZ-YOGA it becomes durable once the change is committed on this checkout, because that is the lineage this machine's sync exports. **Two machines, one fix, two different durabilities — which is the four-lineage problem (`P3178`) restated in the only terms that matter operationally.**
