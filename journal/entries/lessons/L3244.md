**L3244 · dsh-update: preflight and promote each had their own reading of verify.json, and the disagreement deadlocked the whole write path**

MEASURED 2026-10-05 on ZABZ-TECH. verify.mjs computes pass = 'every gate that ran is ok AND G4/G5 ran' (lib/verify.mjs:1829). G8 -- the session-format one-way door -- is deliberately not ok for any candidate that starts writing session format v4, and such candidates are exactly 0.1.7-rc.1/rc.2, 0.2.0-rc.1/rc.2.

preflight learned on 2026-09-28 that G8 is a DOOR rather than a defect, and can reach GO with --accept-session-format-upgrade (lib/cli.mjs, verified:acceptance). promote condition 1 still demanded verify.pass === true (lib/cli.mjs promoteGate, condition 1). So: preflight GO -> promote REFUSED on condition 1 -> switch-engine.ps1 rolled the entire switch back. --accept-session-format-upgrade is passed TO promote by switch-engine.ps1:1334 and could never satisfy condition 1. A flag that is advertised, threaded and structurally impossible to honour.

switch-engine.ps1's own warning at lines 1242-1248 describes the deadlock exactly: 'unless G8 passes, promote WILL refuse at step 2 and this switch will roll the pre-state back.' It was written as a warning; it was the actual permanent state.

CONSEQUENCE: every 2026-09-28 history row for 0.1.7-rc.2 (analyze BREAKS, patch-effect BREAKS, preset-gate RISKY, preflight NO-GO) is a row from a pipeline that never reached its own write path, and no session-format candidate could have been promoted by any operator, however careful.

FIX: lib/promote-reading.mjs now holds THE ONE reading, verifyPromotable(), imported by both verbs. ok:true for exactly two shapes -- (a) pass===true AND no non-opt-in gate unrun AND no gate failing (the extra failing check catches a self-inconsistent document carrying pass:true next to a failed gate); (b) exactly the DECISION gates failed, every required gate ran, and each failing door was accepted. Strictness otherwise unchanged: a second failing gate is never swallowed. promote records verifyReading ('pass' | 'door-accepted') and verifyFailingGates in pin.json and in the history row, so the audit trail never has to re-derive which reading let an irreversible step through.

HELD BY: dsh-update/tests/guards/promote-door.mjs, 16/16 assertions, including 'G8 + G2 fail, door accepted -> refused' and 'G8 is the ONLY decision gate'.
