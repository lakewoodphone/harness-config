**L1984 · Owner's household is kosher - every food decision is a kashrus decision**

Eliyahu corrected me flatly on 2026-09-17: 'obviously it's kosher. You should know that about me.' I had built a cooking recommendation without treating kashrus as a first-order constraint, and even speculated about cut identification without anchoring to the kosher supply chain. Evidence: kosher butchers use 'minute steak' for BLADE STEAK (chuck), not the top-round cube steak that the same term means in general US retail - per https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cube_steak . So for this owner the default cut behind a generic butchery term is the kosher-butcher cut, not the mainstream-US one. Rule for future sessions: any food, catering, gift-basket, office-meal, event or customer-gift task defaults to kosher, and any cut/ingredient ambiguity resolves to the kosher-market meaning first. Sources: cookbook/reference claims about cuts must be checked against kosher usage, not assumed from mainstream US sources.
