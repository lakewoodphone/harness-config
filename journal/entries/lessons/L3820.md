**L3820 · A temporary posture with a 'defer until we retest' note becomes permanent when nobody owns the retest**

**L · A temporary security posture with a "defer until we retest" note becomes permanent the moment nobody owns the retest**

WHAT HAPPENED, measured 2026-10-14. `audit-logs/2026-06-14_171829.md` records a "DRN-50 Microphone Rollback":
*"User chose the safer temporary posture for DRN Device 50 … and defer Waze/keyboard voice input until a controlled
physical retest can prove it does not reopen broader Siri/bypass behavior."* Four months later:

- The retest was never run. No document between 2026-06-14 and 2026-10-14 records a decision about voice input;
 `journal.py search "waze microphone"` returns nothing.
- The posture is now the **default argument** in `app/services/nanomdm_service.py` (`allow_dictation: bool = False`,
 `allow_microphone: bool = False`), so every profile and every customer unit inherits it.
- It is in the `InstallProfile` commands still going to the fleet on 2026-10-14 14:40Z (read from NanoMDM's own
 `commands` table, not from the repo).
- The same 2026-06-12 audit log's "Remaining Physical Checks" were never recorded as completed, so **the rollback
 was probably never observed working on a device at all**.
- The owner's report of the symptom four months later is the first field evidence that anything was ever verified —
 and it was reported as a bug, not recognised as our own switch.

RULES:
1. **A "temporary" posture needs a named trigger, not a named intention.** "Defer until a controlled retest" is a
 promise made to nobody. Write the retest as a dated artefact with the commands in it at the same time as the
 rollback, or the rollback is the decision.
2. **When you flip a switch "to be safe", the safety claim needs a measurement before you stop looking.** Here the
 believed mechanism (`allowMicrophone`) turned out not to be an Apple key at all — see the next lesson.
3. **A default argument is a policy.** What started as one device's temporary state became the product's shipped
 posture through a function default. Changing a default changes every future device, and nothing in the review
 path says "this is a policy change".
4. The owner will experience the consequence as a defect in a product he sells. The cost of the missing retest is
 not four months of a setting; it is a conversation where he reports our own rollback as a bug.
