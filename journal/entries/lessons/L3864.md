**L3864 · His explanation is not copy, and every critique is a row in the runbook**

One session, 2026-10-19, eight customer texts and eleven corrections from the owner. What the
corrections had in common is worth more than any single one: every rejected draft was rejected for
a rule that ALREADY EXISTED in CUSTOMER-SMS-RUNBOOK.md or in the harness prompt, and I broke it
while reciting the others.

The rules he stated today, all now in CUSTOMER-SMS-RUNBOOK.md (0a + critique log, 0b, rule 7):
- an EXPLANATION is not copy. "Sometimes I'm just explaining things to you. I wasn't telling you to
 include it in the text." When he explains why a thing is true - a device's build, a price's logic,
 how the shop works - that is context for me. The customer gets the decision.
- cut to one sentence plus the number: "customers don't want to hear so many words."
- lead with the answer to the question asked; a question back is not an answer ("thats ignoring his q").
- when he supplies wording, reproduce it verbatim (the Exalt text is his sentence, not my paraphrase).
- 50 is the diagnosis fee; a vague "not working" on a cheaply-built device is a NO, not a "bring it in".
 "cheaply built", never "cheap" - these are often expensive niche items.
- 60 = drive into an enclosure (becomes a USB drive); 75 = copy the data onto a USB stick. Swapping
 them cost a draft.
- texts are answered by text; missed calls are returned by the SECRETARY during walk-in hours; never
 text someone who called, not even to say a callback is coming.
- older than 48 hours is not a debt ("if he still want that he would reach out again").
- the shop's auto-reply is not an answer: 848-245-7112 asked a price at 16:42:58 and the bot replied a
 second later; a newest-row test called that thread answered.

MECHANISM NOTE, and it is the one that generalises: he asked for the learning to be structural. So
each critique is now a row in the runbook's critique log, in his words, written BEFORE the next draft
exists. A critique that lives only in the conversation is gone - and a draft that repeats a logged
mistake is the failure he has no patience left for.
