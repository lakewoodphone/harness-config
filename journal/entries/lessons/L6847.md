**L6847 · The owner answered four times and the system recorded, in writing, why each answer was unusable**

Measured 2026-10-16 on the CFO ledger, in the mouth's own alias store. Four rows, source owner-answer, each carrying the reason no rule was written: 'his answer names no category the ledger holds for entity personal, so no rule was written'. The answers were plain English about the substance - 'my wife buying a dress', 'likely a laptop we bought', 'a holiday trip where we went biking', 'my wife's professional liability insurance' - and the writer was matching them against an EXACT category name, so none of the four produced a rule. Two of the four answers also carry his irritation at being asked at all ('are you sure I didn't tell this to you already?', 'you should be able to see research your questions before asking them to me for no reason'), which is the exact failure the owner-question channel exists to prevent. And two of the four alias rows were malformed on top of that: entity recorded as the merchant name, category recorded as the entity value or the word 'unknown'. Fixed by a tool that maps the substance he named onto the ledger's own vocabulary, following the ledger's existing precedent where it exists (newegg -> the category its sibling neweggcom row already carries), writing a rule whose evidence is HIS WORDS, and applying it through the sanctioned writer: 4 rules, 13 rows written, unexplained 62 -> 49, dollars 33,207.95 -> 27,973.74. Verified that all four alias rows now carry a rule, so no recorded owner answer remains inert. The general rule: when a human's answer is stored as free text, something must own the mapping from his words to the system's vocabulary - otherwise the asking works, the recording works, and the answer changes nothing, while the record says the question was answered.
