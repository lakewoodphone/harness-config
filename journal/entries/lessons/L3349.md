**L3349 · A LOCAL research child cannot fan out further, and a background child's job id is not a messageable agent id**

MEASURED 2026-10-06, ZABZ-TECH, during the Yitzchak insurance case.

1. DEPTH LIMIT. Three children were started with the LOCAL fallback after every mesh dispatch failed
 (broker-unreachable, secratary offline). All three completed. But each of those children was itself
 unable to fan out: one child's own report says "the assigned subagent fan-out failed - the mesh was
 unreachable ... and LOCAL child agents were rejected by the session depth limit, so every source
 below was retrieved and read directly in this session". So a local fallback fleet is SINGLE-LEVEL:
 the orchestration depth that the mesh provides does not survive the fallback. A brief that says
 "partition this further across children" is unexecutable on a local child and comes back as one
 agent doing the whole job serially.

2. NO STEERING. After dispatching with run_in_background, the returned id is a JOB id. `send_message`
 to it fails with `subagent "<id>" is unavailable`. There is no way to add a requirement to a child
 already in flight. Two consequences:
 - Write the brief COMPLETE on the first pass; assume you cannot correct it.
 - If, while a child runs, you discover the brief was aimed at the wrong question, the fix is to do
 that part yourself, not to steer. In this session the decisive reframing (NY DMV refuses
 out-of-state insurance, so the real question was never "which policy" but "where is the car
 registered") was found mid-flight and could not be pushed to the children; the session carried it
 itself and one child's memo is partly aimed at a closed question as a result.

3. WHAT WORKED, AND IS THE PATTERN TO REUSE. Fanning out was still correct even degraded: three LOCAL
 children returned ~190 KB of sourced research in parallel while the parent session did the
 high-value primary-source verification by hand. The split that produced the best result was
 PARENT = the two or three load-bearing facts fetched and read directly, CHILD = breadth (published
 rates, carrier tables, statute surveys). The children produced the breadth; the parent produced the
 finding that decided the case. Mark the children LOCAL explicitly wherever cited - a fallback nobody
 can see is a claim about where work ran that is not true.

4. ONE MORE TRAP WORTH KNOWING. Only artifacts the tool spills to disk are recoverable. Two of three
 children spilled their full output to
 C:\Users\ezabz\.dsh\tmp\dsh-spill-<...>/<session>/<hash>-job_output.txt; the third did not, and its
 full text was then unrecoverable from disk even though it was shown in-session. COPY THE SPILL FILE
 TO A DURABLE PATH IMMEDIATELY, and for any child whose output you cannot confirm on disk, write the
 substance up while it is still in context.
