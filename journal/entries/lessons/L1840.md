**L1840 · Three Zigbee2MQTT facts: renaming keeps the entity_id, Last seen N/A is not absence, and availability is per-device**

Three facts about Zigbee2MQTT that cost real time on 2026-09-15, all measured rather than read.

**1. Renaming a device in Zigbee2MQTT does NOT move the Home Assistant entity_id.**
I expected a rename to regenerate the HA entity and break every reference, so I renamed the least-referenced device first to test. Result: `switch.smart_plug_waiting_room` kept its entity_id and simply began displaying **"Desk Light"**; the same held for its `select.` and `update.` siblings. This makes renaming safe and cheap — names can be made truthful without touching a single automation. Renaming is done at runtime, no restart, via Home Assistant's own MQTT publish:

 POST /api/services/mqtt/publish
 {"topic": "zigbee2mqtt/bridge/request/device/rename",
 "payload": "{\"from\":\"0xffffb40e06003a17\",\"to\":\"Desk Light\"}"}

Zigbee2MQTT writes the new name back into its own `configuration.yaml`.

**2. `Last seen: N/A` and `Availability: Disabled` do not mean a device is absent.**
I concluded two plugs "are not talking to the network" from `Last seen: N/A`. That was wrong. `lastSeen` is only populated when a device **reports**; with availability disabled for those plugs they never report, so the field stays empty forever. The plugs answered ZCL On/Off commands immediately — proven by `sendZclFrameToEndpointInternal 0xffffb40e0600382b:17512/1` followed by `dataConfirm status:0` in the log. Never infer absence from a bookkeeping field whose population depends on a setting you have not checked. The owner's "they blinked" settled it.

**3. Availability is per-device, costs nothing on mains, and is the only way to get a truthful light state.**
`availability.active.timeout` in the config does nothing on its own; each device needs `availability: true` under `devices:`. Only the three door sensors had it. With it off, an unreachable plug still shows `on`, so "turn the lights off" appears to succeed while nothing happens. Enabled for the seven mains devices only (never the Kwikset lock — pinging a battery device drains it) and restarted the add-on. Two devices immediately and correctly flipped to `unavailable`: the "Control room smart light" and a Third Reality plug silent for 361 days, both of which Home Assistant had been cheerfully reporting as `on`.

Also worth knowing: the availability failure for these shows in the log as
`Failed to ping 'X' (attempt 1/1, ZCL command .../1 genBasic.read(["zclVersion"]))`, and a genuinely absent device produces `Data confirm error (<ieee>:<nwk>,205,N)` — status 205 is a route/MAC failure, i.e. the device is not there.
