**L328 · How we open a customer text - and it is never 'Hi Mrs. X, Daniel from...'**

The owner corrected me on 2026-09-15: 'you keep forgetting, we don't start off like that.' My draft opened 'Hi Mrs. Weitman, Daniel from Lakewood Phone & Tech. Sorry for the slow reply, that's on me.' Two things are wrong with that and the record shows both. (1) The appositive introduction is not our form. Every real outbound opener on file uses 'Hi, this is Daniel from Lakewood Phone & Tech.' or 'Hi, this is Lakewood Phone & Tech.' or, for a customer we already know, just 'Hi,' straight into the substance: lpt-hub/docs/customer-operations/cases/laptop-perel-kuritsky-2026-06-16.md line 118 'Hi, this is Lakewood Phone & Tech. We looked into your Lenovo laptop screen - we can replace it for approximately \ (parts + labor).' and line 138 'Hi, this is Daniel from Lakewood Phone & Tech. Thanks for filling out our computer form!'; laptop-tzipporah-tchiprout-2026-06-16.md line 96 'Hi Tzipporah, this is Daniel from Lakewood Phone & Tech.'; sync-records/hp-laptop-15-dw3xxx-hinge-8482261895-2026-06-22.json 'Hi, this is Daniel from Lakewood Phone & Tech. We can fix the hinge on your HP laptop - ... Total is \ out the door.' (2) We do not open with an apology about our own slowness. The only apology on file comes after the substance ('Sorry about the miscommunication.'). Also note the register: 'we'/'us' not 'I', 'out the door' for the total, close with a direct question ('Want us to go ahead?'). And when the customer is already in a live thread, re-introducing the business is noise - the Weitman thread itself opens 'Hi, I tried a bunch of things for you, and ended up doing a temporary fix.'
