**L3798 · Read terminal; a delay is not a death sentence; a wrong label becomes a wrong record**

**L · 2026-10-14 · A refusal the gate itself calls retryable must never be raised as a permanent send error.**

Evidence, same night: `app.owner_text.Verdict` documents `terminal=False` for
`rate_floor` and hands back `retry_after`; `_guard_owner_leg` ignored both and
raised `OwnerLegRefused(PermanentSendError)` for every refusal. The hub's
`except PermanentSendError` then did the thing permanent errors are for: it
deactivated the recipient. The recipient was the owner's own phone, and it was
removed from the family chat at 2026-10-14T04:00:27Z, so every later family
message skipped him — the failure silenced the channel it was protecting.

Three transferable rules, each of which alone would have prevented it:

1. **Read the vocabulary of the thing you are classifying.** `Verdict.terminal`
 existed, was documented, and was never consulted. A caller that collapses a
 type system the callee publishes is guessing.
2. **A wrong label becomes a wrong record.** The notice hard-coded "unsubscribed
 at Twilio" for any permanent failure, so the ledger, the admin text and
 `messages` all blamed the carrier for a decision the app had made. Twilio had
 no opt-out for that number and no STOP was ever received from it. The audit
 trail then contradicted the evidence, which is worse than having none.
3. **Never let a notice consume the resource it is reporting on.** The failure
 notice to the same number was itself gated, so one refusal produced three
 more failed sends and a `Failed to deliver to:` chain.

Also measured, and the reason the guard could starve its own owner: the gate's
rate floor is global to the owner (`owner_text_turn.sent_at`), while the roster
carried TWO owner-classified numbers (`OWNER_ALT_PHONE_NUMBERS` includes his
wife's line). The first leg of a fan-out wins the 20-minute budget and the
second loses it — so the family chat was structurally unable to reach his own
phone whenever his wife's line was ahead of him in the roster.
