**L1881 · The placement broker fills the roomiest node first, so two children never spread**

The broker's `fits` tier ranks by `score = freeSlots` and fills the ROOMIEST node first, so two remote children requested at once do NOT go to two nodes. MEASURED 2026-09-17 13:17Z, live broker on the authority, through packages/plugin-remote-fanout: two real children, both placed on `zabz-tech` (scores 18 then 17 - the second one's rationale literally says "minus 1 broker lease(s) running here", i.e. it saw its sibling's lease), both MESH-HOST: zabz-tech, both leases released. Eight children at once: still all eight on zabz-tech, scores 18..11. The mesh spreads PROPORTIONALLY and only under load: the desktop takes children until it is down to the laptop's free count, and the next one moves. That is the right policy for the owner's 55 agents (18-slot desktop first, then the 12-slot laptop) and it is NOT a per-child round robin. Do not "fix" it in the provider: `POST /place` names exactly one winner per call and has no verb for a distribution.
