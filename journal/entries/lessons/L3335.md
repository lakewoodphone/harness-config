**L3335 · L: Two of the shop's five phone lines are inbound-only, and 38 voicemails sat unanswered because nothing read that channel**

MEASURED 2026-10-05 on the authority (dialpad_call_full). The owner asked why Dialpad showed
far more unread than my queue: "why does dialpad show way more new and unread stuff".

MY QUEUE COUNTED ONE CHANNEL OF FOUR. It read unanswered SMS only. Dialpad''s badge counts
missed calls, voicemail and SMS together. Last 7 days: 159 inbound calls, 137 of them
zero-second, none of which were in my queue.

THE FIVE LINES, and only one is two-way
 +17326552355 main business line 9433 calls 2848 in / 2104 out 85 vms
 +17323052065 "Eliyahu" 3513 calls 1804 in / 0 out 2 vms
 +18452955554 165 calls 138 in / 0 out 14 vms
 +12029225287 158 calls 133 in / 0 out 0 vms
 +17322925356 111 calls 73 in / 38 out 1 vm (2026-09-08..09-25 only)
Only +17326552355 has ever placed an outbound call. Every other line is INBOUND-ONLY, so
anything that rings there cannot be returned from it.

FINDING 1 - +17323052065 has 1804 inbound and 0 outbound all-time. It is a ring destination,
not a working line, logged under a person''s name. That is the mechanical cause of the
missed-call volume.

FINDING 2 - +18452955554 is inbound-only with 14 voicemails and appears ZERO times in
dialpad_sms_cache. No text flow, no document, no queue. Voicemails landing there are
invisible to every system the shop has.

THE BACKLOG, last 60 days: 46 distinct callers left a voicemail, 54 rows, 8 answered,
38 STILL UNANSWERED. Three are money rather than courtesy: 848-238-0924 called 09-08 to
hand over a CREDIT CARD for a lab repair and was never called back; 845-540-3452 left three
voicemails about a camera left in a white bag with a green key and asked only "did you hear
this"; 848-224-0262 was chasing a laptop collection on 09-03.

THE RULE THIS TEACHES: count every channel before calling a number the answer. An SMS-only
queue is not "who needs a reply" - it is "who needs a reply, if they happened to text".
Voicemail is a customer who called, got nothing, and spoke into the void; it is a HARDER
failure than an unanswered text and it was the one channel nothing read.
