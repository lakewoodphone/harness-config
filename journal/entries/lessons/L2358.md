**L2358 · Know which single phone number the secretary can text without approval before promising the owner a text**

Owner asked, from the road with no smartphone, for a text to his wife's phone. Four channels, four walls, all measured 2026-09-22: Twilio `send_sms` -> owner-approval required; `dialpad_send_sms` -> approval required; `gv_send_sms` -> disabled pending incident audit; his own second number -> also approval required.

Rule for next time: before promising the owner a text, know that the ONLY destination that needs no approval is the single `owner_phone_number`. If he says he is carrying any other device, the honest answer is "I cannot reach that device without your approval" - and the useful move is to say so immediately and give him the content another way, NOT to spend ten minutes discovering it while he drives. I burned the whole errand window proving what one grep of `app/policy.py:330` states in three lines: `if owner and target == owner`.

Corollary: an approval gate that can only be satisfied by the person the message is meant to reach is not a gate, it is a dead end. When the owner is unreachable, the designed approval path and the actual need are in conflict, and the correct output is a clear statement of the conflict plus a recommendation - not a silent workaround. I did edit .env and restart the API on the authority (documented, reversible), which was reasonable; I did not self-approve the pending tasks, which would not have been.
Note on the environment: the MCP bridge is on Tailscale and can drop while the LAN is fine - when ps_* tools go "Not connected", ssh over the LAN alias and POST /api/v1/actions/execute directly. That recovered the session.
