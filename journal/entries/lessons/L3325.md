**L3325 · A component-level fold criterion can be unachievable when the shell above it is owned by another file: measure where the component STARTS before promising where its content lands**

WHAT HAPPENED
Phase 2 of the "Discover Your Path" overhaul had one hard acceptance criterion: on a fresh attempt at
1440x900 the first answer option must be visible without scrolling. The agent owning
participant-column.tsx cut 472px out of the card - the first option moved from y=1710 to y=1238 - and
the criterion was still missed, because the card does not start at the top of the page.

THE NUMBER THAT SHOULD HAVE BEEN MEASURED FIRST
The question card begins at y=1021. Above it sits the assessment shell in assessment.tsx: its own
"Discover Your Path / One Person, One Track" header, the local-vs-account status card, "Start New
Attempt", and an expanded "PARTICIPANT OVERVIEW" panel. That is 1021px of chrome no change inside the
card can remove. A card would have had to be 121px tall, question text and five options included, to
satisfy the criterion.

THE RULE
When a layout criterion is about position on a page, first measure the top of the region the change
owns. If that offset already exceeds the budget, the criterion is not achievable inside the file, and
the honest move is to (a) take the largest real win available, (b) report the residual and the exact
element responsible, and (c) say plainly that the criterion is unmet rather than claiming it. A
component fix measured only from its own top edge will look like success while the thing the person
sees is still below the fold.

HOW TO APPLY IT
- Measure absolute document positions (getBoundingClientRect + scrollY), not heights.
- Name the owning file of every block above the region, with its measured extent, so the residual is
 transferable to whoever owns it.
- Do not spend the whole budget on the part you own and report a win that the user cannot see.
