**L3893 · I inverted Plaid's sign convention and mis-stated three payroll facts; gross = net + tax only holds when the tax line is employee-only**

I told the owner three things about his Q3 payroll that were wrong. All three came from one root cause, and the root cause is the reusable part.

## 1. I inverted Plaid's sign convention

In `secretary.db` → `plaid_transactions`, **`amount` is positive for money OUT and negative for money IN.** Proven from the same feed rather than from the payroll rows alone: B&H Photo `+533.11`, Dialpad `+146.09`, Verizon `+129.00` are all debits; Stripe transfers `-487.10` and a customer Zelle `-159.00` are inflows.

Applied to the payroll rows:

| Date | Row | Amount | What it actually was |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 2026-09-24 | `GUSTO DES:NET 607334` | **+240.01** | the payment |
| 2026-09-24 | `GUSTO DES:TAX 611249` | **+54.43** | the payment's tax |
| 2026-09-29 | `GUSTO DES:NET 784649` | **-240.01** | the reversal |
| 2026-09-29 | `GUSTO DES:TAX 784780` | **-54.43** | the reversal |

I read it backwards and told him the 29 September run was a real Q3 payment and that the 24 September one had been reversed. It is the exact opposite. Corroborated three ways: the 29 September rows carry `personal_finance_category INCOME_SALARY` and `transaction_type "special"`; Gusto's own email of 2026-09-24 says *"Payroll's been reversed… Sep 14 – Sep 20"*; and the 2026-09-28 separation file records the 294.44 reversal as *"has not posted"* — i.e. still expected as a credit, which arrived on 9/29.

**Last Q3 wages were therefore paid 2026-09-17, not 29 September.** Last day worked 2026-09-11 (NJ DOL Form BC28 box 12, printed by the state); separation 2026-09-14.

## 2. `gross = net + tax` is only valid when the tax line is employee-only

I asserted the identity held because employer taxes inside the tax line would leave negative income tax. That reasoning silently assumed a different payroll shape from the real one. **15 Gusto paystubs exist** (`lpt-hub/docs/operations/payroll/yisroel-weinberg-paystubs-2026-05-11-to-2026-08-28.pdf`), and they show the employee had **zero federal income tax withheld** — so the blended `DES:TAX` debit contains employee *and* employer taxes, and `net + tax` **overstates gross** by the employer share.

Corrected Q3: gross **13,182.69** (not 14,630.13), NJ GIT **240.53**, employer **435.04**, employee **111.34**, total **786.91**, less 108.34 already paid = **678.57** due. Closure proof: the 11 Q3 TAX debits total 2,807.79 = employee 1,360.35 + employer 1,447.44, to the cent.

**So my "the employer taxes were never debited" alarm was wrong.** They were debited, inside the blended line. What is still unknown is whether Gusto **remitted** them — a debit proves the owner paid Gusto, not the state.

## 3. Q4 is not a zero quarter

I said Q4 would be a zero return. There is a **1,000.00 Zelle to Yisroel Weinberg on 2026-10-09** from the owner's personal Chase *9730, memo `final lpt`, split 400 bonus + 600 settlement. Both are 2026 wages, so a **Q4 NJ-927 + WR-30 is due 2027-01-30**.

Sequence that follows: file Q3 → resolve Q2 (delinquent, ~122.71 bill) → file Q4 → *then* end the account's tax eligibilities via online REG-C-L plus notification to the Division of Employer Accounts.

## The rules

- **Establish a feed's sign convention from rows whose direction you already know, before reading any row you care about.** Never infer it from the rows under investigation — that is circular. One debit you can name (a merchant you paid) settles it.
- **A blended "tax" debit is not employee withholding.** If employer contributions are folded into it, `gross = net + tax` overstates gross by the employer share. Check a paystub before using the identity.
- **"No payroll now" is not "no payroll in the quarter being filed."** Filing obligations attach to the quarter the wages were paid in, and a single ad-hoc payment (a settlement, a bonus) can create a return obligation in a quarter with no payroll runs at all.
