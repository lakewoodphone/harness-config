**L1993 · I reported the SMS auto-reply as wrong from the same restamped cache I had just filed a pain entry about - read the live value, not the cache**

1. THE SMS AUTO-REPLY IS ALREADY CORRECT. Live value read tonight from Dialpad's own office object (GET https://dialpad.com/api/office/agxzfnViZXItdm9pY2VyEwsSBk9mZmljZRiAgJ-F642PCgw through a replayed session): "Thanks for texting Lakewood Phone & Tech. WHY I GOT IT WRONG, EXACTLY: I read a "Mon-Thu 10:30-5:30" auto-reply out of dialpad_sms_cache and concluded the template was wrong. That row was an SMS sent on 2026-09-09/09-10 whose created_at had been REWRITTEN to 2026-09-17T11:46Z by the re-import. I had written P263 about precisely this trap four hours earlier in the same session, and then walked into it. Knowing a defect class is not the same as applying it: before calling a live value wrong, read the live value from its own system of record, not from a cache that is known to restamp timestamps.

2. THE IVR RECORDING'S CONTENT IS NOT VERIFIED. The live ivr_menu_script references "lakewood-phone-tech-option-2-dropoff-hours-v2.mp3" and "main menu.mp3" - names the repo's generator does NOT produce (it produces lpt-ivr-*.mp3). So the deployed audio was produced from some other source, and the repo's "Monday through Thursday" text may or may not match what callers actually hear. Fixing the repo source was still correct and worth doing, but it is NOT evidence that the live recording is wrong. It has not been established that the phone says anything incorrect.
 ALSO UNCHECKED: the office carries play_ivr_inbound=false. If the IVR does not play on inbound, the recordings may be inert on incoming calls regardless.

3. WHAT IS ACTUALLY VERIFIED: the website is correct (read live: "Monday, Tuesday and Thursday 10:30 AM-5:30 PM; Friday 10:30 AM-1:00 PM. Closed Wednesday and Saturday."). Google was wrong on 2026-09-16 (Wednesday 10:30-5:30) and the owner is correcting it himself. Nothing else is confirmed wrong.

RULE: "this other surface is probably wrong too" is a hypothesis, not a finding. Each surface is read from its own system of record before it is reported to him. Reporting three defects when one is fixed and one is unproven spends his attention on work that does not exist - which is P1 itself.
