**L3841 · Per-mL price comparisons must include the delivery fee — a 10 dollar fee on a 13 dollar item reversed the recommendation**

**A per-unit price comparison from a product listing is a shelf-price comparison. Add the delivery fee before you recommend.**

2026-10-18, contact-lens drops: I compared five Walmart options per mL and recommended the cheapest bottled one; the owner bought it. It arrived in 27 minutes and the confirmation showed **22.97 total for a 12.97 item** — roughly a 10.00 express-delivery fee. Delivered, that bottle was **2.30/mL**, worse per mL than the 1.72/mL bottle it was meant to replace. The recommendation survived on formulation grounds (preservative-free, contact-labeled) but the value argument I led with was false the moment delivery was priced in.

Rule: when a recommendation turns on price, price the thing **as it will be bought** — delivery fee, membership-gated free-delivery thresholds, tax, multipack minimum — and if the cheap option only beats the alternative on pickup, say so in the same sentence as the number. A fee of a few dollars is not noise against a 12.97 item; it is 77% of it.
