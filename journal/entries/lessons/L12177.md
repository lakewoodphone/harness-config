**L12177 · Niddah questions: the wedding corpus is Tier-3, two of its numbers are wrong against the SA, and this field is never ruled from here**

Live niddah question arrived 2026-10-21 (dam besulim at ha'arah: her blood on the tip of his penis after relations that were not a complete biyah). What a future session must not re-derive:

WHERE THE CORPUS IS. The marriage/niddah material is 12 docs in personal-secretary-mvp/docs/wedding/ (index: 00-MARRIAGE-INDEX-AND-STORY.md). It is COMPILED FROM TIER-3 SUMMARIES (Halachipedia, Yoatzot, blog posts), not from fetched texts. The halacha repo has no niddah sources at all. So: before answering anything in this field, fetch SA YD 193 + 196, Bavli Niddah 64b-66, Rambam Isurei Biyah 11:8 — all reachable on Sefaria — and treat the guide as a *map of the questions*, never as the source.

TWO NUMBERS IN OUR OWN GUIDE ARE WRONG AGAINST THE FETCHED TEXT, and both were being relied on:
- wedding-night-complete-guide.md §5 says he may stay in "as long as you want". SA YD 193:1: "ופורש מיד" — he withdraws at once.
- §13 and physical-side §8 say the count is FOUR days. The Rema on SA YD 196:11 gives the four-day clause and then writes that the practice in all these lands is to add a day — "וכן נוהגין בכל מדינות אלו ואין לשנות". Across the three live readings the mikveh night moves between 1 and 3 November for a 21 October tishmish. In a karet-adjacent field a one-or-two-day error is not a detail.

THE ACTUAL HALACHIC SHAPE, so it need not be re-assembled: gezeirah attaches to the FIRST COMPLETE BIYAH, not to the first blood — so if there was no complete biyah, the first one still costs a full count even with no blood seen (SA 193:1 "אפילו בדקה ולא מצאה דם טמאה"). The Rema's leniency for ha'arah is written twice conditioned on "ולא ראתה דם", so blood seen at ha'arah is the genuinely open branch: no biyah means no gezeirah and dam besulim is dam makkah (tahor min HaTorah), but blood at ha'arah may be dam niddah from chimud. R' Meir's colour test is rejected by the Rabbis (Niddah 65b) — red proves nothing. Rambam 11:8: ongoing wound-blood keeps her tameia and the count starts when it stops.
