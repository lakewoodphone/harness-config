**L3683 · The 29 small disagreements are not fees: a savings account with 22 negative months**

2026-10-13. The 29 small disagreements are NOT fee-shaped. I claimed they were two rounds ago; here is
the case that disproves it and the defect class it exposes.

THE CLAIM I MADE AND AM WITHDRAWING. "The 29 small ones (1,194.17, 21.6 each) are fee-shaped: CLOSE THEM
AS A CLASS, not as 29 investigations." Wrong on the arithmetic: the deltas are 98.27, 97.64, 97.60,
91.04, 82.24, 78.00, 71.19, 68.15, 53.98, 44.59 ... - fees are round and small; these are neither.

THE CASE THAT SHOWS WHAT THEY ACTUALLY ARE. `personal-amex-hysa-3181` is a SAVINGS account. Its monthly
readings run -8506, -8502, -8498, -8494 ... -8474, then -8429 on 2026-07-01, and then +1337 on
2026-08-31 and +1340 on 2026-09-30. **A savings account cannot hold a negative balance**, and it cannot
move from -84.29 to +13.37 on six cents of transactions. Its real transactions are `Interest Payment`
credits of 0.04 to 0.34 - pennies - so the account is a small dormant savings account and the balance
chain is simply WRONG. 21 of its 25 months are `derived`, i.e. computed from an anchor rather than read,
which is how a single bad anchor becomes 22 wrong months, and the tool's own note already says so: "the
opening reading is one the tool already warns about, so this delta is as likely to be a bad anchor as a
missing row".

THE CLASS, measured across all 925 account-months:
 personal-amex-hysa-3181 savings 22 of 25 months negative lowest -85.02 <- IMPOSSIBLE
 personal-amex-94045 credit_card 4 of 25 lowest -3326.19
 lpt-amex-business-gold-1005 credit_card 7 of 25 lowest -113.37
 lpt-boa-checking checking 1 of 25 lowest -1360.52
 lpt-amex-blue-business-cash-61000 credit_card 1 of 25 lowest -746.98
A negative balance is legitimate for a credit card (a credit balance) and possible for checking (an
overdraft); for a SAVINGS account it is a sign or magnitude error, full stop. So there is a cheap
invariant available that nothing currently checks: **a deposit account's closing balance must be >= 0,
and any reading that breaks it is wrong before any transaction is examined.**

AND THE BROADER POINT, which is why this is worth recording rather than just fixing. For 642 of the 925
account-months there is no reading at all, and many accounts (lpt-stripe, lpt-twilio-spend,
personal-amazon-*, the unmapped ones) are SPEND LEDGERS rather than bank accounts, so having no statement
is correct for them. The problem is not the absence - it is that a DERIVED reading carries the same
authority as a read one in every count this program produces. `derived 164` and `anchored 119` are
printed side by side, and this account shows that 21 derived months can all be wrong together.
