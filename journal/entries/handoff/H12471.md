### Defects found, and fixed today
1. **FATAL - `shell_command` has no shell.** HA runs it as `create_subprocess_exec(*shlex.split(cmd))`, so
 the first word must be an executable. `phoenix_verify_evidence_files` began `for f in ...` -> exec of a
 binary named `for`. `continue_on_error: true` does not save it: that covers service errors, not a
 `FileNotFoundError` raised inside the service. Fixed with `bash -c '<loop>'`; proven live -
 `POST /api/services/shell_command/phoenix_verify_evidence_files?return_response` now returns
 `PHOENIX_EVIDENCE_OK <path>`, rc 0.
2. **FATAL - the same class in `phoenix_prune_intrusion_evidence`.** `|` and `;` were passed to `find` as
 arguments, so retention never ran: 48 `intrusion_*.jpg`, oldest **Sep 16, 40 days old**, against a 14-day
 rule. Rewritten with `bash -c` and without `xargs -r`/`ls` (the HA container is BusyBox 1.37; `find` there
 has no `-printf`, measured). Proven: after one drill run, 42 files, **0 older than 14 days**. The 9 doomed
 files were tarred off the box first - `ha-config/_scratch/phoenix-evidence-archive/intrusion-over-14d-20261026.tar.gz`.
3. **SEVERE - the siren step did not re-check the alarm.** Added `- condition: state
 input_boolean.phoenix_intrusion_active == on` as a `condition` step in `phoenix_intrusion_initial_response`,
 placed after all evidence capture and before siren/strobe/loop/page. A `condition` step aborts the
 sequence. A real intrusion never reaches it with the flag off, so nothing is weakened.
4. **SEVERE - the loop ran 85 minutes.** `WARNING ... looped 5000 times` at 00:49, `CRITICAL ... terminated
 because it looped 10000 times` at 02:14:22. That is HA's cap, not a control: D1 meant nobody was ever
 told, and D3 meant nothing turned the siren off.
5. **MODERATE - the false-alarm engine is untouched.** 6 triggers in 43 h, all false. The grace window is
 6 s; arrival was credited after 14 s. This needs an owner decision (raised, not fixed).
6. **MINOR** - Larry's interior camera 400s on every snapshot; the roster tolerates it.
