## 2026-10-19 17:14 UTC · ZABZ-YOGA · Owner text round 3: the CFO question volume measured - 48% of its questions were already answerable from our own records

## Addendum 2 to H9513 (same session, round 2) — the CFO's question volume, measured against the only denominator that matters

### The corrected reading of the ask store (66 rows, the whole history)

My first pass at this was wrong in two ways and both are recorded rather than tidied:

- I reported "0 questions in the last 21 days" using the marker `CFO [ask-`. The live bodies do
 carry it, but my date filter and my `to_number` filter together selected nothing; the marker
 that actually matches recent rows is the same, so the fault was mine, not the channel's.
- I then wrote `unanswered = total - answered` and printed "57 never answered (380% of what he
 was sent)". 57 rows include the ones never sent at all. The percentage was nonsense.

The honest numbers from `/root/.cfo-asks/asks.db`:

| | count |
|---|---|
| asks in the store | **66** |
| sent to him (`sent=1`) | **15** |
| **resolved by our own records, never needing him** (`auto_resolved=1`) | **32 (48%)** |
| he replied | 9 |
| … of which the reply was a refusal, not an answer | **~4** |
| provider refused the send | 16 |
| rows left unresolved | 57 |

**Half the CFO's questions were answered by our own records and never needed to be asked at all.**
Of the 15 that did reach him, he replied to 9 and roughly 4 of those replies were refusals
("I don't know. I want you to figure this out", "Didn't we file a few days ago? What are you
talking about?", "Are you serious?", "That doesn't seem like a smart question isn't that clearly
gas?"). So about a third of the questions he actually received got a real answer.

For scale: the ledger holds **25 uncategorised transactions of 9,069 (0.28%)** — 0.6 asks per
uncategorised charge in the store, and rising to 2.7% uncategorised in October alone.

### What follows from it

The ladder already auto-resolves 48% of candidates, so the volume lever is not "ask fewer, ask
worse" — it is **raise the auto-resolved share**, which is exactly what the new descriptor rung
does (Fuel, EZPass, the AI provider and the other descriptor-determined charges now resolve with
no question at all). The number to watch after the deploy is therefore not the dead-text rate but
`auto_resolved / total` in this store, and the conformance census in the scorecard.

**The unanswered tail is also the specification for what still needs work.** By merchant, the
rows he was asked about and never answered: visiontek systems, verizon, telnyx-llc, sticky brand,
pocono whitewater, paypal bilgala, `pass`, nj division of taxation, american professional — nine
of the top twelve are recognisable kinds of charge. `pass` is the E-ZPass toll the descriptor rung
now answers. `american professional` is his wife's professional liability insurance, which he told
us in a text on 2026-10-08 and which therefore should already have been learned as an alias.

### Do not repeat

A question store's `state`/`status` column is not portable between these stores: `/root/.cfo-asks`
has no `state` column (it has `sent`, `provider_ok`, `answered_at`, `resolved_at`,
`auto_resolved`), while the relay's `ask` table does. Read `pragma table_info` before writing a
`group by state` against an ask store — I cost two round-trips on that.
