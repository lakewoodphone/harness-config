# Post-Shabbos verification and fix. 2026-10-10/11.

Commit **b8a270c4**. Deployed on secratary: `home_assistant.py` `397ff7d2bb81`,
`shabbat_orchestrator.py` `a031400cd0c2`, `agent_bus.py` `c952019189c1`.

## WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED OVER SHABBOS

**Observation 1 — the Sat 10:00 reprogram: PASSED, and changed nothing.**

The new window opened mid-run and ran happily for its 3 hours. Every run
reported the ON jobs as `already_present`:

```
existing_timespecs : ['4 20 10 10 *', '44 19 24 10 *', '53 19 17 10 *']
wanted_timespecs : ['4 20 10 10 *', '44 19 24 10 *', '53 19 17 10 *']
deleted: 0
```

That is the create-then-prune rewrite working exactly as designed: zero drift,
nothing created, nothing deleted, no window in which the device held no schedule.

**Observation 2 — the motzei power-up: the plug came back, the secretary's steps
FAILED, and the end state was nevertheless correct.**

The plug turned itself ON from its own device schedule at 20:04:27. The
secretary's power-up then ran and **every Home Assistant step failed**:

```
relock_and_restore ok: False door_relocked: False
 lock -> 500 Internal Server Error (mqtt/publish)
 restore -> 400 Bad Request (automation/turn_on)
 maglock -> 404 Not Found (switch.smart_switch_l4)
ceiling_light_restore ok: True verified: False (404 Not Found)
```

**Root cause:** `check_connection` reported CONNECTED, but HA *core* serves HTTP
long before its integrations finish loading. Zigbee2MQTT and MQTT were still
coming up, so the entities did not exist yet. "HA answered" is not "the entities
I need exist".

**Why the outcome was still fine, and I verified this rather than assuming it:**

| entity | state now | last changed |
|---|---|---|
| `switch.smart_switch_l4` (interior maglock relay) | `on` = **LOCKED** | 00:06:46 — **62 s** after the failed attempt |
| `automation.phoenix_interior_maglock_tamper_restore` | `on` | 00:05:53 |
| `switch.deserted_house_lights` (ceiling light) | `on` | 02:59:17 |

HA's **own assert-on-start automation** relocked the relay 62 seconds later, and
the light came back. The secretary did not do it and said so loudly.

**Do not confuse two different doors.** `lock.0x002446fffd0a4705`
("Apartment_Door_Smart_Lock") reads `unlocked`, and that is *correct* — it is a
different door and not the one the Shabbos automation touches. The interior
maglock is the relay `switch.smart_switch_l4`, and it is locked.

**The recovery never fired, correctly.** By the time HA's entities were readable
the relay already read locked, so `power_up_is_complete` returned
`{'complete': True, 'reason': 'door_locked'}` and `recover_incomplete_power_up`
returned `already_complete`. That is a no-op by design, not a failure.

## THE FIX (deployed)

`home_assistant.wait_for_entities_ready()` — poll until the entities we are about
to act on are readable — called from `_power_up_critical` AFTER HA answers and
BEFORE it touches the door or the light. It gates on exactly what it uses: the
maglock relay and, when configured, the ceiling light.

**Proven against real HA on the host:** `ok=True attempts=1 elapsed=0.01s`. One
probe, no delay, entities present — so it costs nothing in the normal case and
only does work on the run it exists for. It never aborts on expiry; it proceeds,
names the missing entities, and lets the every-minute recovery retry.

5 new tests, 94 passed on the server.
