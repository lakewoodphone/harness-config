## 2026-10-19 18:47 UTC · ZABZ-TECH · Five SIMs in; both E4610s are refused registration, not failing - two handsets identical, so the line or the IMEI binding is the cause

Owner put five SIMs in at ~14:40 on 2026-10-19; every handset rebooted (the battery comes out on a flip to fit a SIM), which kills the Tech Mode window because it stores its start time in elapsed realtime. Two E4810/E4610 units came back `unauthorized` and one (558800238329) is not on ADB at all; the owner has to tap Allow again and re-open the window where ADB is off.

He reported that the Verizon E4610s "had service for a minute and then it went away". Measured on the two E4610s that answered (615373543, 615963123, both identical):

- `gsm.sim.state=READY`, `gsm.sim.operator.numeric=311480`, ICCID prefix `8914800` - a genuine Verizon-IIN SIM, correctly read.
- `gsm.network.type=Unknown`, `mServiceState=1 1 ... Searching for Svc. ... RoamInd=1 ... mRilImsRadioTechnology=0` - voice AND data out of service.
- `mSignalStrength` carries LTE rsrp -94 dBm, so the radio hears a Verizon cell; the antenna path is intact.
- `mDataConnectionPossible=false`, `mDataConnectionState=0`; radio log repeats `isDataAllowed: No - Not attached`.
- Verizon carrier config is loaded (`eri_311480.xml`), IMS daemons running, `net.lte.ims.volte.provisioned=true`, `wfc.provisioned=true`.

Conclusion: registration is REFUSED, not failing - and two independent handsets do it identically, so neither phone is at fault. APN/provisioning-in-the-phone is ruled out because the phone never attaches to try an APN. What remains is line-side: the SIM is not active for that IMEI (Verizon and its MVNOs bind a line to a device IMEI; an unlisted handset attaches briefly then is knocked off - exactly the "minute then gone" symptom), or the card is data-only/unprovisioned.

Decisive test, blocked on two taps: move one of these SIMs into an E4810 and read the same properties. E4810 registers => line fine, E4610s rejected as devices. E4810 also "Searching" => line is the problem.
