 projection per edge. No re-clock, knot or drive fixes it.

 precondition; the rope must stay slack when parked because the re-clock makes it the highest line on the edge).

 yard edge — which is the owner's stated priority. Skirt money and roll money buy the same edge, so do not buy the
 skirt until the tape measure and the roll-up decision are settled.
