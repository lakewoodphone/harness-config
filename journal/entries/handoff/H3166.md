CHANGED (all committed, all verified)
- `d5e96b02` — two real UI defects on /listing/[id], found by measuring the DOM with
 `getBoundingClientRect()`, NOT by reading a screenshot:
 1. The detail map was INVISIBLE and reserved 420px of dead space. `.map-box` is display:flex with a
 fixed 420px height and the detail page nested it inside `.map-box-sm`; the inner box's content is
 width:100%, which resolves against an auto width in a flex row, so it measured 0x420 while
 Leaflet had loaded 3 tiles into it. Fixed with a `fill` prop on RentalsMap; after: 832x240, 8
 tiles.
 2. Every photo thumbnail was piled in one invisible spot. `.photo-thumb button` also matched the
 thumbnail button `.thumb-btn` and absolutely positioned it, taking it out of flow, so
 `.photo-thumb` collapsed to 0x0 and four 72x72 images stacked at the same coordinates under the
 map's background. Fixed by scoping the rule to `.del-btn`; after: x=224/308/392/476 in an 832x82
 row.
 Gate after both: 164/164 tests, tsc 0 errors, next build OK. Browse page re-checked, no regression
 (map 526x420, 9 tiles).
