## 2026-10-18 03:04 UTC · ZABZ-YOGA · NJ ST-50 filing method and every trap: the Q3 2026 figures, the portal path, and the five ways it goes wrong

# NJ ST-50: the complete method, the figures, and every trap — Q3 2026

**Written 2026-10-18 during the actual filing.** A future session should be able to file the next quarter
from this page without re-deriving anything. Everything here was measured, and where it was not, it says so.

## 1. The filing, end to end

**Portal:** `https://taxportal.nj.gov` — the New Jersey Tax Portal (the exact host the treasury's own
`Return Filed` notices link to). Path: **Log in → Online Transactions → File a Return → Form Type
"ST-50 Sales and Use" → Return Type "Original Return" → Filing Method "FILE ONLINE" → Period End Date**.

**Identity as the portal renders it:** LAKEWOOD PHONE & TECH LLC · TAXPAYER ID `392119233-000` ·
account type "SALES AND USE/REGULAR RATE" · 1001 W KENNEDY BLVD, LAKEWOOD NJ 08701-1264 ·
Tax Portal ID `TU01100696163` · login email `lakewoodphoneandtech@gmail.com`.

**Filing frequency is QUARTERLY — proven, not assumed.** Five returns were accepted by the treasury on
2026-07-01 with `periodEndDate` values of 2025-06-30, 2025-09-30, 2025-12-31, 2026-03-31 and 2026-06-30 —
every one a quarter end. A monthly filer cannot file five quarter-end returns and have all five posted.
The portal's own "File a Tax Return" page says quarterly filers have **no change** to the ST-50 process.
The four Gov2Go "Monthly Payment Notification" emails (5/20, 6/20, 8/20, 9/20 2026) impose nothing: no
monthly payment was ever made and no receipt exists. **They are prompts, not an obligation.**

## 3. THE TRAPS — each one changes the answer

**3.1 The ledger double-imports. Never compute tax from it unreconciled.** The same transaction arrives
from `plaid-api` AND from a `Chase Credit Card CSV` / `bank-page-manual`, same date same amount, with
`duplicate_of_transaction_id` **unset** and `duplicate_confidence` 0 — the duplicate machinery never
fired. Measured cases: ATM cash 1,045.00 (2026-07-01), 944.00 (07-13), 919.00 (07-17); Stripe transfers
335.54 and 311.56; a Stripe sale of 261.23 twice. **Filed as ledger item 550.** De-duplicate by real
transaction identity — Zelle `Conf#`, Stripe `ch_`/`ST-` id — not by date+amount, because one deposit is
described `07/12` but posted `07-13` and then looks like two.

**3.2 Card prices are mostly TAX-INCLUSIVE, and 63 are not.** 129 of 192 Q3 Stripe charges are exactly a
whole-dollar price × 1.06625 (53.31 = 50.00; 106.63 = 100; 159.94 = 150; 261.23 = 245; 895.65 = 840), so
**932.14 of tax sits inside them** and must be backed out of gross receipts. The other 63 are flat round
dollars ($11, 50, 150, 250 …) with no tax added — **tax on 5,159.00 of takings was never collected and is
still due.** That is a quoting habit to fix, not a filing error.

**3.3 There are NO cash sales. Do not reconstruct cash from bank deposits.** The owner, 2026-10-18:
*"No, I get cash from all sorts of places. Nothing to do with the business."* 6,608.00 of Q3 ATM cash went
into personal/college checking and is **not** shop revenue. An earlier working paper invented an
"in-store cash" figure from those deposits; that was wrong by construction. **The business account contains
no currency deposit in the entire ledger.** If a future period ever reports cash sales, it needs a
Z-report — and **no Z-report or POS export exists anywhere in this estate** (`source_documents` holds only
statements and email-derived order feeds).

**3.4 eBay and PayPal carry no sales.** All 32 Q3 eBay rows are `debit` purchases and the order
confirmations ship to the owner; all 96 PayPal credits are funding transfers or vendor refunds.

**3.5 Transport of persons is not taxable.** 300.00 of Zelle memo'd AIRPORT DELIVERY / DRIVER was excluded.

**3.6 The payment portal charges a convenience fee.** Payment runs through `njportal.com` (New Jersey
Government Services) at roughly **2.3–2.5%** — measured 6.67 / 7.29 / 9.34 / 26.21 on the four July
payments. **It is not tax and not deductible.** This also resolves the earlier "49.51 unreconciled card
service fees": they are those four fees.

**3.7 Treasury emails prove periods, not money.** `noreply@ses.treas.nj.gov` "Return Filed"/"Return Posted"
bodies are four-line stubs containing no amount — only `confirmationNumber` and `periodEndDate` in the
link. **The amounts live in a different sender: `no-reply@njportal.com`, subject "New Jersey Government
Services Payment Receipt".** A prior session never read that sender and therefore could not prove what was
paid.

**3.8 Not every state payment is tax.** A 78.00 receipt on 2026-10-04 is the **Annual Report agency fee**.
A 128.50 on 2025-05-13 is the LLC formation fee. Only "NJ TAX Payment" lines are tax.

## 4. The prior published figure, fully explained

An earlier pack said 25,671.84 / **1,700.76**. It is wrong, and it is explained with **no residual**:

 1,700.76 = 1,619.55
 + 61.75 (932.14 of embedded tax NOT backed out)
 - 2.92 (44.06 of tax wrongly backed out of refunds)
 + 2.50 (37.73 PayPal payout booked as an in-store sale)
 + 19.88 (300.00 of transport booked as sales)

Four differences, nothing unexplained. When an old figure differs, make it reconcile to the penny or do not
believe either number.

## 5. What is still open

- **Use tax: 0 to 458.06.** Parts from Alibaba, MobileSentrix, Injured Gadgets, Mouser, Digi-Key, Bilgala
 and BH Photography show no evidence of NJ tax being charged; used in taxable repairs, that carries use
 tax. The books cannot tell a resale purchase from a consumed one. **Settled by the supplier invoices and
 any ST-3 resale certificates** — not by the ledger. It is 0.00 on the filed return until that is known.
- **Two 20.00 Pirate Ship postage buys in September** with no shipped sale to attach them to.
- **The 42.65 charge of 2026-08-04** carries a duplicate flag that cannot be proven either way; keeping it
 is the defensible choice and costs 2.65 of tax.
- **Whether the two 300.00 transport payments were really transport** rests on the Zelle memo text alone.

## 6. How to do the next quarter faster

1. Pull Stripe **directly from the API**, not the ledger — the charge stage, gross, and it cannot be
 double-imported.
2. Pull the bank from **one** feed (the Chase CSV is more complete than plaid-api), and classify every
 credit into sales / Stripe payout / owner funding / card payment / refund. They must sum to the account.
3. Back out embedded tax by testing each charge against `amount / 1.06625 == round dollar`.
4. Never count a payout and a charge; prove they match to the cent instead.
5. Reconcile two independent derivations before filing. On Q3 that caught a 5.9% overstatement.
