## 2026-10-13 15:07 UTC · ZABZ-YOGA · The journal is consolidated but NOT self-maintaining: 3839 vs 3826, and why I was overclaiming

CONSOLIDATED, NOT SELF-MAINTAINING -- and the difference matters enough to write down.

I have been reporting "the journal is one record". Measured again at the end of this pass, 2026-10-13:

 this checkout 3839 entries OPEN PAIN 403 check 0 errors
 the authority 3826 entries OPEN PAIN 402 check 0 errors

**Thirteen entries apart, and one open pain.** The union I did earlier was real -- it made both trees
identical at 3,819 entries and 401 open pain, and I verified that on both sides. What it did not do is
STAY identical, because **both trees write and nothing moves entries between them.** Every entry this
session writes re-opens the gap; every entry a session on the authority writes re-opens it too.

So the precise claim is: **the journal was consolidated, and nothing keeps it consolidated.** Saying
"one record" without that qualification is the kind of half-true number this whole program exists to
stop -- and I said it, in three handoffs, because I verified it once and then went on quoting it.

WHY NOTHING IS LOST, which is the part that matters for the owner: both trees hold every entry either
tree ever had, the union is mechanical and repeatable (149 + 341 entries and 6 status rows the first
time, measured before it was done), and no entry has ever been overwritten -- `status.tsv` is
append-only and the merge is a union of rows. The failure mode here is sessions quoting different
numbers, not data disappearing.

THE FIX, for a session that can do it properly: a sync between the two journal trees on a schedule --
the same shape as `deployed-drift` and `preset-drift`, which are checks nobody has scheduled either.
Three mechanisms now exist that are correct, verified, and run only when a session remembers them,
which is the exact defect this program identified in other people's work.

RECORDED, NOT FIXED: this is a handoff, not a claim.
