## 2026-10-19 01:46 UTC · ZABZ-YOGA · Anti-reset build round 3: the classifier is wired into the live broker, and the next unlock is storing the evidence

Goal round 3 of the anti-reset objective. The classifier is no longer a module sitting beside the broker -
it IS the broker behaviour now, and it is verified.

WHAT CHANGED:
_run_phone_offline_monitor in server/app/main.py used two magic thresholds and recorded no reason. It now
builds a Checkin from the phone record (wipe_detection.checkin_from_phone_row), asks classify(), and maps
the verdict onto the same three words the partner-alert path already speaks (online/soft/hard) via
notify_level(). WHO gets told does not change; when and why do, and every non-live verdict logs its class,
level, silent seconds and reason.

THE ONE FAILURE THIS GUARDS, and it is the kind that hides forever: a missing or unparsable timestamp must
become UNKNOWN, never "seen just now". Read as "now", the whole fleet looks alive and the alarm is
silenced permanently. Pinned by a test.
