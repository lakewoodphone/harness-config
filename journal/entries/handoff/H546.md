The agent measured this itself (19 of its 20 cases failed at boot) and worked around it with a module-scoped opt-out that mirrors `tests/conftest.py::legacy_main_app_auth_token_audit_opt_out`. So the general rule is sharper than "register your route": **an unregistered route is a fail-closed startup failure, which is the design working — but it means a fleet branch that is forbidden from touching the registry cannot be green on its own, and its tests will lie about why.**
