## THE RISK IN THAT DECISION, STATED PLAINLY

Removing them from the text path is the right call, but it is not free: **sms-desk-watch reads
the Dialpad SHOP line, not the Google Voice personal line**, so a question like Alon's "Can I get
6 of them?" is now surfaced by nothing. He received it directly and can reply directly, so no
work is lost -- but if he wants an assistant drafting replies to his PERSONAL texts, that is a
feature he has not asked for and I am not inventing it. Recorded here so it is a decision rather
than an accident. The alternative -- continuing to text him his own messages -- is clearly worse.
