### WHAT I LEARNED

- The system is **not** broken and it is not a rewrite. It is 96.35 % categorised with a working loop,
 a working scoreboard, 166 learned rules and a disciplined owner mouth. What it lacks is
 **measurement of its own depth** (39 % evidenced), **a consumer for its verdicts** (the weekly
 reconciliation is read by nobody), and **an end to the thrash** (83 % of shift slots are repeats).
- `clear` was doing the work of two different words: *categorised* and *known*. Everything downstream
 inherited that conflation.
- Estate patterns exist and are cheaper than invention: `ceo-kernel`'s bundle remote gave D11 a
 10-minute fix, and `finance-fleet-20261013/history-coverage/` had a finished, `git apply --check`-clean
 statement importer sitting unapplied with 512 + 648 recoverable rows named.
- Two correction cycles inside one session: the audit's "two account rows" was really one orphaned
 snapshot with foreign keys off, and its "the daily ceiling did not hold" was really 14 cron shifts
 plus 6 manual. **Write the command next to the number or the number will be wrong in a way nobody
 can see.**
