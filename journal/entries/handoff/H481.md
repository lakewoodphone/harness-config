## TWO CALIBRATION MISTAKES, MADE AND CORRECTED

A verifier that cries wolf gets ignored, so this is the whole risk of the tool. It was wrong twice:

1. **Ranked statuses by net transition direction**, on the theory that the common direction is
 forward. That flagged `AWAITING_PARTS → READY_FOR_PICKUP` as *backward* — plainly forward work.
 Cause: the most numerous transitions in this corpus are **resets** (`CANCELLED`, moves to
 `CHECKED_IN`), so a net tally measures "what happens most", not "what is later". **14 false
 errors.**
2. **Then flagged all 11 remaining backward moves**, but most are ordinary shop work —
 `IN_REPAIR → AWAITING_PARTS` means a part is needed. **6 more false errors.**

Now severity follows **customer-visible harm**: away from `READY_FOR_PICKUP`, or from a terminal
state back into active work, is an ERROR; any other pipeline retreat is a WARN; moves **into**
`CANCELLED`/`UNFIXABLE` are excluded entirely, because giving up is not a regression. 15 ERRORs
stand, 7 WARNs are reported without failing the run.

**Deliberately NOT checked**, each with the measurement that justifies it: `device` differs in
23 of 23 comparable cases and `last-updated` in 25 of 25 (no controlled device field exists, so it
is structural); `prod.paymentStatus` is **not** a money signal (514 of 591 orders are PENDING
including 295 COMPLETED, so a broad rule would fire on half the business — only `paid→PENDING` and
`uncollected→CAPTURED` alert); a missing given name, two "unknown" sentinels, a one-store
next-action.
