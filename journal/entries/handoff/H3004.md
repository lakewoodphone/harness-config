ITEM #8'S SECOND HALF - "no path from a finished shift back to his phone" - and with it the reason the owner
is still the only one who can start work. `~/bin/shift-report.py`.

THE RULE IT IMPLEMENTS, in his words: *"why would it text me this, whats the point, is there a question or
clarification you need from me or a warning you need to give me?"* and *"daily briefings are completely
useless"*. So: **a finished job is recorded in the ledger; a text happens only when it carries a question, a
warning, or a decision with a deadline.**

TWO PATHS, DELIBERATELY DIFFERENT

 `record` - the common case. It prints that the outcome is already in the ledger's attempt row and that
 NOTHING WAS SENT, so a shift calling it cannot believe it notified anyone. Writing a second copy of the
 outcome anywhere would rebuild the message queue this replaced.

 `decision` - the rare case. Files ONE owner-queue row WITH A RECOMMENDATION, prints the exact text that
 would go to his phone in a fenced block, and DOES NOT SEND IT. `--send` requires `--approved-by-owner`,
 deliberately awkward to pass, because approval is per message and a shift is not a person who can approve
 one - the rule broken on 2026-09-24 by a six-item questionnaire he had never seen.

AND IT REFUSES NON-QUESTIONS. A decision queue that accepts status reports becomes the message queue it
replaced: 595 rows, nothing delivered for 54 days. If a text carries no question mark and none of the
markers, the tool says so and declines, pointing at `record`.

VERIFIED, all four paths live:
 * record on #37 -> "RECORDED IN THE LEDGER, NOTHING SENT", naming the item's state and newest attempt;
 * decision with 'I finished the integration work and pushed a branch' -> REFUSED; a status report is not a
 decision;
 * decision with a real question -> queued **#184** [medium] with the recommendation, and the exact text
 printed and left unsent;
 * `--send` without approval -> REFUSED, with the reason.
