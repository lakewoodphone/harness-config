## 2026-10-19 23:49 UTC · ZABZ-YOGA · LPT website + wake loop audited: the loop works, the last mile was broken, production promoted and verified

# 2026-10-19 · ZABZ-YOGA · LPT website + wake loop: full audit, the loop was working and the customer could not see it

## The question and the answer

The owner asked two things: **is the website's autonomous nightly loop doing its work, and is it
heading in a good direction.** Both answers are measured, and they are different answers.

**Is it doing its work — YES.** `project:lpt-website` releases per day from
`~/.sms-inbox/wake-dispatch.log`: 17, 17, 20, 16, 3, 16, 17 across 2026-10-13..19. Releases run all
night (22 at 00:00Z, then 6-10 every hour). Ledger closures went 44 → 84/45 in three days. The deploy
gate is OPEN (6 of 6 secrets), and during this session the loop merged PR #486 and #487 on its own.

**Is it heading in a good direction — PARTLY, and the gap was named.** It has a genuinely good
strategic spine (`docs/website/ROADMAP.md`, 5 themes, evidence discipline, supply via
`roadmap-lpt-website.json`). But it was converging on staff-counter defects while the last mile to
the customer was broken, and two themes that answer his actual ask (T2 customer journey, T3 one
look/one vocabulary) had never started.

## The finding that mattered more than everything else

**`origin/main` was 74 commits and three days behind `origin/test`.** Production served the
2026-10-16 bundle. Measured side by side, same minute:

| | test `/screen-repair` | production `/screen-repair` |
|---|---|---|
| `innerText.match(/\$\s?\d[\d,]*/g)` | `["$154","$173"]` | `[]` |
| `<table>` | 1 ("Screen Repair Prices") | 0 |

`/find-a-computer` rendered on test and answered the 404 page on production. The shop could not
quote a price for a cracked screen — the one question its site exists to answer — while the fix sat
finished on test. **MERGE HYGIENE rule 2 in the roadmap already forbade this; nothing measured it.**

Why it accumulated: PR #485 sat `BLOCKED` on four `Test Frontend (Shard n/4)` jobs, and the required
check could not tell green from dead —
* `pr-precheck.yml:967` gated test enforcement on `is_copilot == "true"`; every PR is human-authored,
 so the suite ran and its verdict was discarded, while the else-branch printed *"the test suites do
 NOT run anywhere in CI for a human PR"* (false since 2026-10-14).
* the shard's kill branch did `exit 0` on a timeout.
* root cause of the timeout, measured locally: `pool: 'forks'` cannot start a worker
 (`Timeout waiting for worker to respond`, 60.25 s, **0 tests run**); `pool: 'threads'` runs it
 (**53 passed**, exit 0). Also `poolOptions` was removed in Vitest 4, so `maxForks` had been
 silently ignored.

## What still hurts

- **The promotion is still a manual act.** Nothing fails or warns when main drifts. That is the
 highest-value remaining item and it is filed.
- **34 % of all releases today ran under 300 s** (`dur<300s` 50/146), concentrated where the ledger
 has nothing claimable — lpt-sync 9 of 14, housekeeping 7 of 16. A pre-release guard should ask the
 ledger whether the project has a claimable item before spending a release. Not done.
- **The count ceiling that binds is a stale fallback.** `wake-budget.json` carries `count_max: 150`
 while `wake-budget-env.sh` calls it `FALLBACK_MAX_PER_DAY`, "the pre-2026-10-14 count". Today's
 spend was **4.178258 of 47.64 USD = 8.8 %**. The count stops the system eleven times before the
 money does, against the owner's stated policy. Fix the no-op waste first, then this.
- **The mesh was unreachable** (`MISSING_CREDENTIAL: llm-deepseek … deepseek-official` on ZABZ-TECH)
 so all five audit children ran **locally** and are marked LOCAL.
