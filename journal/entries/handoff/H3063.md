 is not required to play. Cheapest thing to remove from the equation.
3. A game "crash" can be a hang. The EA App reported clean short sessions; the
 process was actually still alive hours later. READ THE PROCESS TABLE, not just
 the app's session log.

 and then "the 23 Hz mode was the fault" - which was the OWNER'S OWN SETTING. I
 read a value he had just typed and reported it as a system default. I never
 asked. That is the confident-wrong-number failure this system exists to prevent.
