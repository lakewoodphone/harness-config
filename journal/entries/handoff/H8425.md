## What was found by asking what a slow source actually contains

\db:localdb\ took **704 s of every hourly refresh** on ZABZ-YOGA and was still running after seven
minutes on ZABZ-TECH (167,614 documents before it was stopped). Asking what it held instead of
tolerating the cost: **215,427 documents, the first of them \web:chrome:*\ — a personal browsing
record** — plus per-host SQLite dumps whose rows the centre already holds. Decision D-C had already
kept its content out of the central index for exactly that reason; it was still being built locally,
on every machine, every hour.

Retired: disabled in the registry with the measurement written next to it, and its documents dropped
from the laptop index — **215,427 → 0**.
