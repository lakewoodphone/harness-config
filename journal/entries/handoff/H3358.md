### IN FLIGHT
- NEXT CHANGE, designed and not started: a RESIDENT LAUNCHER. A click costs four cold starts
 (pwsh, a 204 KB script parse, WMI, HttpClient); a warm process that is already parsed and already
 knows the slot origins takes a click to ~1-3 s regardless of load. Sketch: a small process (node or
 pwsh) started by the watchdog beside the origins proxy, listening on a loopback port, that picks a
 free origin and spawns the browser with the same arguments dshw.ps1 uses. Must keep the
 `--app=` chrome-free window (the owner's layout depends on it) and must not duplicate the argument
 list silently - either share it or assert the two agree.
- The single-implementation rule in packages/plugin-windows says `dshw new` is the only opener; any
 resident path has to be reconciled with that rule rather than ignored.
