## STILL WIRED-AFTER-THE-FACT, checked rather than assumed
- The gate **is** called from three live paths: `app/sms_service.py:1130`,
 `app/tool_factory.py:2015`, `app/autopilot.py:10075`.
- A refusal is **held, not dropped**: `tool_factory` enqueues with `status="held"`,
 `autopilot` records the send failure. So a `rate_floor` refusal is retried by the existing
 flush (`AutopilotController._maybe_flush_owner_message_queue`) and `app/services/lifecycle_sweep.py`
 — nothing needs to consume `retry_after` for the message to survive.
- **`retry_after` is recorded but not yet consumed** by any caller (`grep` is empty in
 sms_service/tool_factory/autopilot). They rely on the existing periodic retry. That is
 correct-but-looser: a producer retries on its own clock rather than at the moment the floor
 lifts. Wiring the horizon into the retry is the next improvement, not a defect.
