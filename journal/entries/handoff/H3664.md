## The one shop rule worth writing down

Row 340 turned on a rule the shop already states in writing: **a finished order sitting in
`READY_FOR_PICKUP` with no payment row is to be treated as collected**, and note 2885 puts it in
production's own words ("which in this shop means paid and on the shelf"). That rule is why 255 orders
carry a closing note and no payment row, and it is also why marking them verified would be wrong: the
convention says *the shop believes it was paid*, not *a payment was recorded*. The two are different
sentences and the notes now say which one is true.

## Lesson

**A queue row that asks the owner a physical question is a misrouted row.** Five of these ten said "is
this device still in the shop?" — that is a fact for the counter, and it had been sitting in the owner's
attention list for five days. When a row cannot be answered from any system, ask whether a person could
answer it in ten seconds by looking, before asking the owner.
