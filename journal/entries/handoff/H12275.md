### What exists now

### The decisions the research produced

1. **The model learns eight facts, not ten levels** — the meter already maps facts to levels.
2. **Trigger women's rules on the garment, not the person** — removes a sex classifier from the pipeline.
3. **Ship PP-HumanSegV2-Lite re-headed 2→3 classes** (Apache-2.0, 5.4 MB, 15.9 ms on a Snapdragon 855).
 Sapiens, FASHN and SegFormer are licence-poisoned for a paid app at any speed.
4. **Labels come from Fashionpedia's sleeve/hem annotations** (CC BY 4.0), applied to images we may ship —
 its own images are 51.2% NC.
5. **The patch-at-joint design was abandoned on evidence**: 60 px landmark tolerance against a 16 px patch
 half-width, 22% of landmarks outside it.
6. **Escalation is conformal risk control, not model confidence** — the repo already has
 `server/scripts/conformal_calibration.py`; measured calibration error is up to 0.496.
7. **Ceiling is about 70% on fine-grained attributes**, set by the 64% teacher. Promise is "never let a
 violation through silently", not "detect everything".

### Next, in order

Stage 1: build the 3-class head, validate against the 165 ratings, beat the 0.35 figure.
Stage 2 (needs the owner): 300–500 fresh ratings to turn the risk dial into a bound.

### Traps paid for today

- `read_image` reports the hash of its **normalised copy**, not the file — labels keyed on it match nothing.
- MediaPipe keeps a grayscale JPEG at one channel and then fails every later frame with a tensor-size error;
 build the Image from an explicit RGB array.
- A PowerShell `-replace` with `\n` writes a literal backslash-n; `$pid` is read-only; `git` in a worktree
 keeps its lock in the main repo's `.git/worktrees/<name>/`.
- `scp` to a non-existent remote directory hung for 25 minutes rather than failing; `mkdir -p` first, then
 `scp -o BatchMode=yes`.
- A metadata block-list cannot judge pixels: a fetish image passed the filters because "hogtie" was not a
 blocked word. It was deleted and the block-list extended.
