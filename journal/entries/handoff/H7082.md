### The defect that the recipient column explained

`OWNER_ALT_PHONE_NUMBERS=+18483897895,+18482245096` makes a **family member** an "owner" for the
owner-text gate, so a message to him is held by the owner's 20-minute attention floor. Proven in
`sms_log` 4280: *"owner_text gate refused (rate_floor): we texted **him** 9 minute(s) ago"* — about
a message to someone else. Three attempts (74/75/76, 377 chars each) are one message the owner
asked us to send, cleared by the relay as `SEND`, refused downstream, and left `pending` with no
sid because the door raised `OwnerLegDeferred` without recording the outcome. It has never been
delivered.

### Two corrections to my own work, made in the session

- `merchant_resolve.resolve()` resolves by merchant **name** and ignores the amount, so handing it
 an amount "solved" a 418.77 charge with a text about a 25.00 co-pay, and a web rung took 12.7 s
 on the ask path. Preask now does its own amount-anchored `plaid_transactions` lookup and only
 calls the resolver for a merchant-only question, with research off.
- The audit reported `double_sends = 7`. All seven were the correct two-recipient family fan-out.
 It also counted `queued`-with-a-sid rows as "silent". Both rules were tightened: a pair counts
 only with the same recipient; silence means `verdict=SEND` with no sid. True value: **4**.
