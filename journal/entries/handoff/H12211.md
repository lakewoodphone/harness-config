## 2026-10-21 03:18 UTC · ZABZ-YOGA · Round 33: the triage finished the rows it judged -- the queue was lying to him by a factor of three

Round 33. Window per the clock: 2026-10-21T03:18:00Z.

## THE TRIAGE CLASSIFIED AND NEVER FINISHED, SO THE LIST LIED

Measured before this round: the database held 62 rows at status 'pending' while only 20 were
decisions. The other 42 had been judged -- 13 echoes of his own phone, 7 digests, 4 passive notes,
1 duplicate, plus noise and stale -- and EVERY ONE of those verdicts lived only in the triage's
stdout. owner-queue.py reads the database, so rief and
ext reported 62 things waiting on him
when 21 were real.

That is this programme's whole purpose arriving through the back door. The queue is supposed to be
"the single list of everything that needs him"; a list padded with things that do not need him is a
list he stops trusting, and a list he stops trusting is a list he stops opening.

decision-apply-triage.py writes the verdict back:
 ECHO dismissed his own message, already on his phone
 INFORM resolved the notice arrived in his email first; this was a second copy
 SYSTEM resolved passive record-keeping, filed as ledger work instead
 DUPLICATE dismissed another row carries it
 NOISE/STALE dismissed with the triage's own reason
and LEAVES ALONE SEND_NOW, CONTROL, ENGINEERING and JUDGE_GAP -- the first is the work, and the last
two are genuinely undecided.

SAFETY: dry run by default; only rows still 'pending' are touched, so nothing another session
answered or resolved is overwritten; every close carries a written reason; nothing is deleted.

THE JUDGEMENT CALL: resolving INFORM hides those rows from rief, and they were meant to stay
visible as information. I resolved them because each is a notice that arrived in his EMAIL first --
the row was a second copy, the same defect as the echoes -- and seven non-decisions in a decision
list is worse than losing a duplicate. Recorded so the choice can be reversed with one line.
