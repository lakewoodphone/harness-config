## 4. The credentials, and the state they are in

Stored at `~/.secrets/ebay-personal.env` (0600; ACL-restricted on Windows) on ZABZ-YOGA,
ZABZ-TECH and `zabz-tech-linux`, and `/root/.secrets/ebay-personal.env` on `secratary-cloud`.
Resolution order: env → `$EBAY_SECRETS_FILE` → that path. Pair fingerprint **`a47e8d3196e8`**
(sha256[:12]) is identical on all four nodes.

**eBay answers `401 invalid_client`.** The measurement that matters: a malformed grant answers
`invalid_request` / `unsupported_grant_type`, so the *request shape* is accepted and eBay is
rejecting the **pair**. eBay does not say which half is wrong. Both shapes are correct (App ID
40 chars `Name-App-PRD-<9>-<8>`; Cert ID 36 chars `PRD-` + 12-4-4-4-4). So the open item is a
re-copy of the two values, or acceptance of the API License Agreement / activation delay.

**I nearly reported this wrongly, and that is the lesson worth keeping:** my first validator
demanded `12-4-4-4-12` for the Cert ID and duly told the owner his — perfectly canonical —
`12-4-4-4-4` secret was "truncated, 8 characters missing". One search of eBay's docs and two
third-party guides showed the 4-character final group is the normal layout (`PRD-83fd-ffcc-400e-8676-a441`,
`PRD-1234abcd-5678-90ab-cdef-1234`). The validator was the broken thing. Fixed, and it now reports
only what it can prove plus a named list of what it cannot.
