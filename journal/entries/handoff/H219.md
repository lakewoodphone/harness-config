### What made the difference

Ten defects, all of the same family — **a job that runs, exits 0, and produces nothing**:
a flag that never reached the layer doing the work; selectors that joined the media *row* and
never tested the *file*; a multi-URL call judged from one joined row; a scan window too small to
see the work; three writers where `database is locked` discarded completed work (in one case work
already paid for — **L1484**); a selector that kept re-offering URLs Dialpad had already refused;
and a selector that kept re-offering files that are not audio, which is why the queue could never
reach zero until the test matched the transcriber's own. Every one was found by measuring the
subject of the job instead of watching its exit code.

### Standing arrangement

Four cron entries on the authority own it: harvest `*/30`, media fetch `*/5`, transcript gap
`*/10`, index refresh `7,37`. Nothing depends on a session. The fetch and transcript tools carry
their own idempotence, disjoint sharding (`rowid % N`) and cumulative spend cap, so a human and
cron are bounded identically.
