**MEASURED (the owner, 2026-09-17) - these supersede every plan figure**
- Rail span (beam to beam, centre to centre): **11 ft 10 in**. Post spacing: **~14 ft** both rows.
- Tarp: 342 x 144 in, 4 in sleeves BOTH 342 in edges, both ends OPEN, #4 grommets at 21 in o.c. from 3 in in.
- Frame: 6 posts 2.375 in vertical; beams the same section, two pieces each meeting in a three-socket tee at the middle post; 8 rails 1.660 in running house-to-yard on the fall.
- Connections: rails U-bolt clamped to the beams **at both ends**; beams sit in Kee Klamp set-screw sockets (L at the ends, T at the middle); **no through-bolt anywhere in the beam-to-post path**.
- Footings: backyard 12 in dia x ~40 in as dug, 4 bags of Sakrete per hole (**~36.7 in of concrete**); house side 3 bags of Lowe's bagged mix per hole (**~27.5 in**). Net pull-out **84 mph** backyard, **77 mph** house side. **The base is NOT the weak link.**
- Mechanism: **UHMW sleeves on the rails with the AKIHISA hangers already on them**; the tarp is folded on the deck, not installed; the operating station is **on the deck at the house wall, both directions from there**.
- **No post is tied to its footing and nothing ties the frame to the house.** The "steel cable" is a hand-lashed wire rope with no turnbuckle, thimble, clip, eye bolt or shackle - it carries nothing.

**ENGINEERING, CONVERGED**
- Deployed limits: **rails ~72 mph, beams ~71 mph, unbraced posts ~71 mph** - three members within 1 mph, the signature of a schedule sized off one assumption. Then pull-out 77-84, post yield 87.7, footing rotation 91.
- **Serviceability governs first: the rails sag past L/180 at ~48 mph.** The 40 mph deploy rule is sound, twice over.
- **The fix is two things: deck-height braces on all six posts, and a through-bolt at each beam socket.** The rod-into-pier work dropped off the critical path.

**IN FLIGHT / BROKEN**
- **Four design packages were running when the owner paused and are to be committed when they land**: the two leading poles (with the support question - the 1-3/8 in top rail sags 7.98 in over 28.5 ft and needs two intermediate supports or a ~2-1/2 in pipe), the house-side straps, the rope/pulley system, and the fabric-to-rail hardware.
- **Open physical checks, first one first:** the hanger boss thread (M10 vs 3/8-16 vs M8) - the owner deferred it; whether the May brace hardware is on site; how many cam straps are on site.
- Still unmeasured: deck depth, beam heights above deck, joist depth, and how the deck attaches to the house.
