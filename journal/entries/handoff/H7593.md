## What he asked for, and the correction that reshaped it

He asked for one queue holding both (a) anything from his email/texts/calls/voicemails he would want to
know about, and (b) every decision still waiting on him — delivered one at a time when he says "let's
knock out the queue". I started on the shop telephone lineage; he stopped it:

> *"dial pad calls sms and voicemail do not count. I only want personal ones ... Google voice numbers
> and you do have access to my Google voice numbers because they get forwarded to my email ... The dial
> pad is not relevant to my queue And you can access my goal of voice through my emails."*

**Binding from that message on:** Dialpad never feeds the owner queue. Google Voice via the forwarding
mail is the personal channel, and email is how it is reached.

## Rules that were wrong first and are now measured

The first classifier queued **256 of 624**. Corrected against the corpus until 14 days yielded 34.
What each fix was for:

- `contact_name` vs `name` key mismatch dropped every item as nameless (caught by `--explain`).
- `not name` dropped real people who are simply not in his address book.
- The GV footer *"To respond to this text message… "* is on **every** body; it defeated exact
 comparisons ("MMS Received") and turned one-word texts into 200-character titles. Now cut.
- One family group relay produced 179 messages: it folds to **one row per sender per ISO week**.
- `dedupe_key` on the **thread** made a second text in a thread overwrite the first (4 silent
 refreshes). Now keyed on the message id.
- Voicemails were all `critical` because they say "call me back" — so do robocalls. Now critical only
 with a real name + callback; robocall phrases drop.
- A 4-6 digit *name* is a shortcode; a bare *number* is a person. Conflated at first.

## Traps paid for this session

- **`gmail_service._tokens_dir` is a RELATIVE path** (`data/gmail_tokens`). Running the ingest from
 `/root` reported every account "not authenticated" while tokens sat in the checkout — it looks
 exactly like a credential outage. The script now `chdir`s to the app root.
- **`app.config` exports `settings`, NOT `get_settings`.** Use the fallback ladder in
 `owner_attention_sweep._load_settings`.
- **The app venv is `/root/personal-secretary-mvp/.venv/bin/python`**; system `python3` has no
 `pydantic_settings`.
- **PowerShell here-strings carry CRLF**, so a piped remote script passes `\r` into paths (`sed` said
 "can't read …/email_actions.py" while `ls` showed it). Use **single-quoted** PowerShell strings for
 remote one-liners; `ssh host "cmd with | or \"` is re-parsed by PowerShell and fails.
- **`systemctl list-timers` can show `NEXT -`** immediately after enabling; re-read it a moment later
 rather than concluding the timer is dead.
