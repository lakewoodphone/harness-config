 * The channel and party are derived EXACTLY as `owner-queue.py:_dimensions_from_row` derives
 them (same `_SOURCE_CHANNEL` prefixes, same `_RE_PARTY` regexes), because the profile's

Measured on the live queue: 14 of 70 rows carry an adjustment (two Alon rows at -160). The
adjustment is now PRINTED beside each shortlist row so its effect can be audited rather than
taken on trust.
