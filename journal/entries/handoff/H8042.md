### Three failures during the load, each a defect worth keeping

1. **The service was killed by its own memory cap.** `MemoryPeak 629,260,288` against
 `MemoryMax 629,145,600` — 600 MB was too small for a 4000-document chunk, and systemd killed it
 mid-push; the client saw HTTP 502 after 22 chunks. Fixed twice: the body is now streamed
 (`TextIOWrapper` over `BytesIO`) instead of decoded and split into a list, and the unit's ceiling
 is 1.5 GB with a 1 GB soft limit. `MemoryCurrent` held at **1.07 GB** for the rest of the load.
2. **`--flush-spool` did not exit.** It flushed the kept spool and then carried on into a full run of
 every source, colliding with the hourly task — two writers on one index. It now flushes and stops.
3. **A 888 MB un-checkpointed WAL** left the retry wedged. `usearch.py` has no `checkpoint`
 subcommand (the function exists, the CLI does not expose it), so the WAL can only be truncated by
 a raw `PRAGMA wal_checkpoint`.
