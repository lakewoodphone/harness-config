### WHAT HAPPENED, because it shapes everything below
At 15:32:08 the live config was rewritten to 0.1.7 names while the 0.1.5 engine ran (P2733). Cause: the
version guard resolved its engine root from `dshInstall`, which a switch run sets transiently during
`promote`; a sync tick 40 s later believed the candidate was running, passed the check, and applied.
Second cause: the switch's rollback restored the engine and NOT the config it had already written. Both
are being fixed. The remediation was complete: restore by recorded sha256, quarantine (never delete).
