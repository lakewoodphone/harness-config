THE CORRECTION, AND IT IS THE IMPORTANT PART
- My first assessment reasoned from the paper label and treated the handset as a cheap feature phone.
 **It is not.** FCC ID `2A28USL006D` is **Coosea's SL006 platform** (grantee Coosea Group HK, granted
 2023-07-06), sold as Consumer Cellular's IRIS Easy Flip and DISH/Boost's Summit FL!P: **Android 12 on
 MediaTek MT6739WW**, ~1 GB RAM, **8 GB eMMC**, microSD to 128 GB, MP4 video.
- Three consequences: **a Bluetooth-received file on Android lands in the internal `Bluetooth` folder**,
 so the video most likely went to PHONE storage and the empty card slot is consistent with that; its
 `userdata` is **F2FS on eMMC and Android 12 must use file-based encryption**, so a chip-off dump is
 **ciphertext** - the internal branch is an encryption problem, not an undelete problem; and USB gives
 **MTP/PTP, not mass storage**, so any card must be pulled and read in a reader.
- Probability, honestly: microSD not rewritten since **~50-70%**; microSD used since **~10-25%**;
 internal storage on an FBE device **~0-5%** - which is the branch the owner's "less than 5%" correctly
 describes. Both the case file (appended, citing the sources) and the day README carry the correction;
 a second production note carries it to the counter.
