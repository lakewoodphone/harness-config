### Two CI lessons worth keeping

1. **A change to a gate's contract breaks its sibling assertion, and only full CI sees it.** #572
 made `/water-damage`, `/back-glass`, `/charging-port-repair` redirects; `routeDecision.test.ts`
 still asserted they must 404 (correct when written). The local guard passed; CI went red. The
 test was **split**, not deleted — one case for real unknowns, one for the redirects.
2. **Removing a loading guard moves when `isError` arrives.** #575's own suite failed with
 `Unable to find … /the live list couldn't be loaded just now/`, because the fallback now renders
 on the first tick while react-query is still retrying. The assertion needed `waitFor`; asserting
 synchronously was measuring the renderer.
