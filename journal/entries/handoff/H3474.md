## FOUND AND FIXED THIS ROUND

`_program_coming_weekend` called `clear_all_schedules` **FIRST** and created the
new jobs afterwards. Between those two calls the plug held **no schedule at
all** — and if the create then failed, the device had nothing left to switch it
back on. Power would never return, with HA and every camera dark and nobody on
site.

It runs mid-Shabbat: the daily window at 10:00 is about 10 hours into a run, with
the plug **off by design**. That is exactly the wrong moment to have an empty
schedule.

Fix: **write first, prune afterwards.** Read what the device holds, create only
the missing ON jobs, then delete only the jobs we own and no longer want. The
device always holds a usable ON schedule, and a failure during the write leaves
the previous schedule intact. New additive helpers in `shelly_plug.py`:
`_is_our_switch_job`, `clear_schedules_except`, `schedule_timespecs`. A job that
does not switch our output is never touched.

This also makes the run **idempotent**, which round 8's retry window made
necessary: recreating unconditionally would have added a duplicate schedule every
15 minutes for 3 hours, every day.
