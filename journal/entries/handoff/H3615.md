**The answer, and it is short.** The hard drive needs **one** thing removed: the **back cover — 2 screws**.
- `Before You Begin`: power off, unplug everything, hold power 5 s. Lay the machine **face down on a soft
 cloth**, ≥30 cm (1 ft) of desk free — it rests on its stand arm.
- Back cover (manual p2): **2 screws** at the bottom edge, then **slide** the cover off its tabs into the
 middle frame and lift.
- Drive (manual p25): **loosen the 2 captive screws** (they stay in the chassis), **slide out and lift the
 assembly**, unplug SATA data + SATA power, then **4 screws** release the cage from the drive.
- **8 screws total.** Front stand (p23), rear stand (p56) and rear stand cover (p58) each list back-cover
 removal as *their* prerequisite, so all three are downstream — **the stand does not have to come off.**

**Data side.** 3.5" SATA = **12 V, powered dock only** (bus-powered 2.5" adapters cannot spin it). Bench
tool named in the shop's own playbook is the Sabrent ASM1153E dual dock on `zabz-tech-linux`.
`lsblk` → `smartctl -H -i` → then **decided now, not left open**: SMART clean and no read errors =
**read-only copy** (`lpt-copy-userdata`); any pending/reallocated sector or read error = **ddrescue image
first** (`-n -a 7864320 --skip-size=16M --check-on-error --reopen-on-error`, mapfile, ≥525 GB free), work
from the image, never the original. No BitLocker on Win7 Home Premium, so no decryption step expected.
