PART-128 — MFA IS WELL BUILT AND NEVER REQUIRED
584 lines of `mfa.service.ts` (TOTP, SMS, backup codes), **ten procedures all `protectedProcedure`**, backup
codes hashed where `PasswordResetToken.code` is plaintext. And **zero rows** in `user_mfa_settings`,
`mfa_backup_codes` and `user_devices`, with `sessions.isMfaVerified` false on all 287 — because nothing in the
sign-in path ever asks.
- **F-1 High:** `SessionSecurityService` defaults `requireMfaForAdmin: true` and implements the check, but the
 class is **exported and never instantiated** (only other reference: a re-export). `createLoginSession` has
 no MFA branch, and `mfaService` is imported only by its own router. **Declared policy, no enforcement
 point.** So none of the **6 ADMIN** rows (3 ACTIVE, 3 SUSPENDED) has a second factor.
- **F-4 Medium:** `disable` and `generateBackupCodes` need only a valid session, no step-up — for a surface
 whose purpose is to survive a stolen session, the two operations a thief most wants are the two a session
 suffices for.
- **F-5 Low:** device trust is recorded and unusable — `mfa.devices.trust` writes `user_devices` while
 `sessions.deviceHash` is NULL on 287/287 (PART-122 F-4).
- All ten procedures reject CUSTOMER, and the customer allowlist has no `mfa.` entry, so the surface is
 staff-only by two mechanisms — that part is right.
