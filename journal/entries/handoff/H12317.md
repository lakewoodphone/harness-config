## The 502, and an experiment that stopped a wrong fix
The report synthesis was failing ~half the time. Measured from lpt-apps with the app's key: the SAME report-sized
request returned 500 at exactly 30s, then 200 at 12s; 200 at 10s, then 500 at 31s. The 500 body is 21 bytes of
plain text - the Python app's own unhandled error. Ruled out: ai_gateway_timeout_sec (600, unset), gunicorn (plain
uvicorn), the proxy (no 502 in Caddy), gateway health (26 models, 0.41s).
Then the decisive experiment (docs/verification/gateway-ceiling-2026-10-22.md): 3 runs at max_tokens 4096 and 3 at
2560. Times 14/10/24 and 20/24/31s with completion tokens 931/901/1125/857/1375. THE CAP DOES NOT DRIVE THE TIME.
So cutting the report's word budget - proposed twice - would have cost depth and bought nothing. The structural
fix is smaller units (split the synthesis), because one 10-31s generation is a coin flip against a fixed 30s
ceiling while several few-second calls are not.
Also fixed: the tier resolver let every tier pick the same replacement when the catalog dropped the measured
defaults, and dedupe then left ONE rung with nothing to fall through to. Latent, not live (deepseek/deepseek-v4-pro
IS in the catalog), but it would bite the first model retirement.
