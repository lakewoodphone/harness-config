## 2026-09-28 19:52 UTC - ZABZ-TECH - Round 18: the ledger had NO lease reaper; wired and proven

THE GAP. The wake store reaps its own leases - the dispatcher calls `wake.py reap` on every tick. The
LEDGER is a SEPARATE STORE with its own lease mechanism, and nothing whatsoever reaped it: no cron, no
source, no script. Measured 19:50Z: five items sat `running` with leases to 21:07-21:17Z.

WHY THAT IS NOT A THEORETICAL WORRY. A shift is bounded at ~36 minutes, so a 90-minute lease is fine
while work is happening - but if a session dies mid-shift (the desktop reboots, ssh drops), its item
stays `running` FOR EVER: neither done nor available, and no other shift may take it. The only symptom
is a backlog that quietly stops moving, which is exactly the failure this system exists to prevent, and
it is invisible to every reading because the item still exists and still looks claimed.

THE FIX: `run-wake-sources.sh` now runs `work.py reap` on the same 15-minute cadence as everything else.
Idempotent and cheap - it only touches leases that have already expired.

PROVEN, because a reaper that has never freed anything has been RUN, not tested. `prove-reaper.py`
creates a scratch item, claims it with a lease forced into the PAST, and checks two things:
 reap says: reaped {"count": 1, "items": [44]}
 after : state=todo claimed_by=None lease=None
 recorded : "lease expired (held by proof-dead-session until 2000-01-01T00:00:00Z); returned to todo"
The second check matters as much as the first: a reaped item must not look untouched, or the record
loses the fact that a session died on it. The scratch project was then disabled and its item dropped,
so no test artifact is left in the backlog. Committed 4c52cf6 and pushed.

THIS IS THE FIFTH INSTANCE OF ONE PATTERN, now well recorded: a mechanism exists, works when called by
hand, and NOTHING SCHEDULES IT. Earlier tonight: wake.py's doubled cap default; 27 wake rows filed and
never consumed; the money ceiling reading a measurement 18 hours stale; the discovery deadlock (no item
-> nothing-todo -> no discovery). The rule (L2984): a mechanism that is not on a schedule does not
exist. The corollary found tonight: a mechanism on a schedule that is never EXERCISED has been run, not
tested - hence the planted expired lease rather than a `reap` that returned 0.
