## 2026-10-20 21:16 UTC · SECRATARY-CLOUD · DOOR_STUCK_OPEN on the control-room door was the watchdog rule, not the door: occupancy gate and a restart-proof recorder clock

## 2026-10-20 21:20 UTC · SECRATARY-CLOUD · DOOR_STUCK_OPEN on the control-room door was the watchdog's rule, not the door

At 20:07Z the watchdog tripped on `binary_sensor.external_control_room_door_sensor_contact` ("has read OPEN for 4 h"), and on the other two doors as well. I checked the live states, the recorder and the control-room camera before changing anything.

**The door is really open, and that is normal for this shop.** `everyone_away=off` (someone is present), and the channel-7 camera at 16:18 NVR time shows a person working inside with the exterior doorway standing open. Seven days of recorder history show the same trading pattern: the exterior contact ran 14:30→21:51 on Oct 13, 15:59→21:42 on Oct 18 and 14:35→22:41 on Oct 15; the interior contact ran 14:29→21:59, 15:59→21:59, and 14:35→Oct 17 17:14. A door propped for five to eight hours on a business day is not a stuck reed, and the alarm is disarmed while someone is there.

**Two software faults in `ha-watch.py`, both measured.**

1. **The 4-hour rule ignored occupancy.** It now alarms at 4 h only while `everyone_away=on`, and at 24 h even when the shop is occupied — a door propped for a whole day is still worth a look. That backstop keeps the dead front-door reed visible during the day: it has no recorded close in 30 days, so its recorder age is 692 h.
2. **`last_changed` is not a clock.** All six door entities reset to 16:01:04Z — the HA restart — while the two control-room doors had actually opened at 15:15:40 and 15:00:52. The 2026-10-19 note that the raw device's `last_changed` survives a restart is wrong; it does not. The probe now walks the recorder back through the current open run (treating the restart's `unavailable`/`unknown` gap as transparent) and emits `DOOROPEN:<entity>=<seconds since the run's first on>`. The watchdog prefers that clock and falls back to `last_changed` only when the recorder has no `off` to anchor the run.

**What changed** — `/root/bin/ha-watch-probe.sh` and `/root/bin/ha-watch.py` only. Backups: `ha-watch-probe.sh.bak-20261020T2115Z`, `ha-watch.py.bak-20261020T2115Z`, `.bak2-…`, `.bak3-…`; both files mode 0755, `py_compile` and `bash -n` clean. The canonical audit doc (§4.5) and the script docstring now describe the occupancy rule and the recorder clock. No HA restart, no HA config, Zigbee2MQTT or alarm change; the doors were left as they are.
