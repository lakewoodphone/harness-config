## 2026-10-18 · SECRATARY-CLOUD · owner asked: audit why last Shabbat failed, make it work when the router is on, look for collateral damage

Commit `87075705c` in `/opt/company` (personal-secretary-mvp). Doc: `docs/operations/shabbat-lan-path-audit-2026-10-18.md`.

### The answer to his question
The router was NOT the cause. The authority has no route to the office LAN 192.168.50.0/24 and has had none since the 2026-10-11 cutover — 1,835 `Shelly unreachable` warnings and zero successes in that unit's whole journal. The plug (192.168.50.103) is LAN-only; `HA_URL` was `homeassistant.local` (mDNS, unresolvable from a datacenter, `Errno -3`). The 2026-10-16 13:12 EDT power-down and the 2026-10-17 19:52 EDT power-up both failed. The interlock then correctly REFUSED to cut power because it could not prove the door released — which is exactly why the cameras stayed on.

### The fix (deployed, verified)
`tailscale serve` on `zabz-tech` (office LAN, 192.168.50.138, never sleeps on AC) forwards 8123→HA and 8080→plug. `.env`: `HA_URL=http://zabz-tech.tail93e6e6.ts.net:8123`, `SHELLY_PLUG_HOST=zabz-tech.tail93e6e6.ts.net`, `SHELLY_PLUG_PORT=8080`. Added the missing `shelly_plug_port` field to `Settings`.

Readings: `/shabbat/status` = `plug_connected:true, switch_on:true` (first time ever on this host); device `output:true apower:27.2`; `Switch.Set{on:true}` → `{"was_on":true}` (write path proven); HA `/api/config` 200 in 74 ms; `/shabbat/safety` = `power_down_allowed:true`; `program_week()` ok, deleted 1 stale job, holds `44 19 24 10 *` and `35 19 31 10 *`.

Also added `agent_bus._alert_shabbat_failure` — owner SMS on a real failure only, silent on success. Untestable-in-production so it was tested with a stubbed sender: 2 failure shapes fire, healthy run silent.

### NOT verified — do not report as done

### Residual risk

Durable fix recommended in the doc: give the HA box its own tailnet identity and move the Friday power-down into HA as a local automation, leaving the cloud as programmer/verifier.
