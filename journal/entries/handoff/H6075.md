## 2026-10-15 16:41 UTC · ZABZ-TECH · Ullman WO26101312747: the jack is EXONERATED (charges at every angle) - the fault is board-level in the charger IC enable network, and because the machine works now no repair can be validated today

Owner's bench test, 2026-10-15: **the Ullman laptop charges at every angle the plug is put in.** The jack, its harness and its solder pads are exonerated, and the fractured-jack / cracked-input-solder-joint line the case has carried as the leading candidate since 2026-10-13 is **withdrawn**.

**What is left: the board, in the charger IC's enable network.** `PQ5400` (AONS32314) / `PQ5401` (AONR32340C), the charger IC `PU5400` (TI `BQ24780S`), the ACDET divider `PR5436` / `PR5437`, the pin-25 BTST resistor, the `PR5425` shunt. Published same-board cases replace the `BQ24780S` and change nothing because a passive around it was the real fault — so the divider and the BTST resistor get checked before any IC is ordered.

**Why the jack looked guilty and was not.** Working at it is what restored the machine on 2026-10-14 — real evidence that disturbing that area changed the state. But a connector that conducts at every angle cannot be an open circuit; the likelier mechanism is a power cycle releasing a latched state. That was the owner's option (b) on 2026-10-14, and always the less physically satisfying of the two.

**The hard part, and it decides the money.** The machine **works right now** — it charges and it boots.

1. **A board-level repair done today cannot be validated.** It would only be tested the next time the machine drops out, after the customer has paid. On a working machine that is a promise, not a repair.
2. **The 0 A condition cannot be reproduced.** Nothing can be measured in the failing state, so the divider, the BTST resistor and the ACDRV-collapse signature cannot be taken to a conclusion now.

**Consequence for the price.** The **150 all-in with the 50 credited** recommended on 2026-10-13 was sized for a jack-level fault. With the jack exonerated and the fault board-level and unverifiable, that figure needs re-deciding before anything reaches the customer.

The two available jobs are now clearly different things, and they carry different risk:

- **a board-level repair** priced against an unverifiable outcome, or
- **a data-only job** off a machine that currently boots — the one outcome that can be guaranteed, and the customer's own words on 2026-10-13 were *"if the cost of it is a lot I might as well just buy a new one… and then I want to just save what's on it."*
