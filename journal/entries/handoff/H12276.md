### What was done

The owner asked for the whole kosher-filter AI system and a real research pass on the on-device model: ten
rungs, an always-on NSFW floor, the rest by filter settings, "extremely fast and reliable". A nine-agent
workflow ran: seven disjoint evidence streams, then a bounded-risk architect and a red team that attacked
the seven. Raw output archived verbatim at `docs/corpus/research/07-fanout-raw-20261021.txt` (50 KB).
Synthesis is `docs/corpus/CASCADE-DESIGN.md`; a second garment pass is `docs/corpus/research/08-garment-recheck.md`;

**Read `CASCADE-DESIGN.md` first.** It does not replace the six streams that were already there — it tests
them, and contradicts three of their conclusions where the evidence says so.

### The three findings that change the build

1. **Decode owns the budget, not inference.** 45–90 ms to decode a 1080p JPEG against 2.4–15 ms to
 classify it. Every latency argument in this repo so far has been about 10% of the cost. No stage may
 allocate a bitmap wider than 256 px in the decision path.
2. **Rung 1 cannot carry the promise.** UnsafeBench Sexual recall: Falconsai 40.7%, AdamCodd 67.8%,
 NudeNet 320n 78.2% (`arXiv:2603.16181`, Mar 2026). So blur at t=0 and classify to *reveal* — but see
 the trap below, because that has a cost.
3. **Eight of ten rungs are sex-conditioned** and sex estimation is 94.0% on adults but **81.7% on ages
 0–9** (FairFace, WACV 2021 Table 6), 20.8–34.7% error on darker-skinned women (Gender Shades 2018).
 Rungs 4 (cling) and 6 (short sleeves/trousers) are where sex is load-bearing — men are the common case
 — so those two become **household settings, not detections**. Four rungs are buildable now: 1, 5,
 7-hem, 8.

### The trap that nearly inverted the design

Blur-first converts an invisible miss into a **visible false blur** — and a parent who sees their own
daughter blurred uninstalls the product, which is worse than an imperfect filter. Resolution is in §4.1:
reveal only at the certified `lambda_hat`, keep the operational dial separate from the safety threshold.

### What already existed and must not be rebuilt

`server/app/conformal_calibration.py:71` already does per-attribute conformal risk control with `coverage`
and an `alpha_floor` refusal. What is missing is *simultaneous* control across rungs (Learn-then-Test,
`arXiv:2110.01052`), the ladder as the attribute axis instead of `category`, and the sample-size arithmetic
surfaced: α=1%, δ=0.05 needs **298 violating frames**; ten rungs at 0.1% is **≈3,000 each**, ≈30,000
labelled frames. Blur-first is what makes that affordable, because abstention becomes free.

### The legal wall, recorded not resolved

US20230162337A1 (NetSpark, pending) recites local prefilter → on-device AI → escalate-on-ambiguity.
**US12524994B1 (Safetonet, granted 2026-01-13, to 2045)** claims on-device inference + frame-by-frame pixel
differentiation + "blocking portions without blocking all". US11856260B2 (Covenant Eyes) claims the fused
score. GB2602159B constrains the broker. The free mitigation doubles as the FTO mitigation: emit a
structured attribute vector, never a score, and evaluate the ladder as policy on the handset. Full-width
blur (not per-region) likely steps outside the "portions" claim. **Whether to buy a patent opinion is the
owner's money and is still open.**
