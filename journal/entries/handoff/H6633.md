Owner, 2026-10-16: **"sounds good wire it all in fully now"** — approving the ladder and the two fixes I
proposed. The product, as decided:

| plan | price | data | driving time | margin at 50 devices |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | 7.00 | 150 MB | ~25 h/month | +3.12 (45%) |
| **Standard** (recommended) | 11.00 | 250 MB | ~42 h/month | +5.88 (53%) |
| Plus | 16.00 | 500 MB | ~83 h/month | +7.75 (48%) |

**The billing model:** the subscription is always **Starter**; extra data is a **step-up bought for the rest
of the cycle** at the price difference between tiers (Starter→Standard 4.00 for +100 MB, Starter→Plus 9.00
for +350 MB). Past 500 MB: **+250 MB for 5.00**, repeatable, up to a **1 GB ceiling**. Automatic step-up
**only on opt-in, only to Plus, one per cycle, after the 80% alert**. Never print a per-GB figure; sell
hours of driving.

**Why this beats what I proposed:** the margins are 45–53% against my 35–43%, and on the same 50-device fleet
and usage the owner's ladder earns **243.60 a month (46.8%)** against my four-rung 6/9/13/17 at **154.85
(32.9%)** and my two-rung version at **202.35 (38.2%)**. Dropping a 17.00 rung removed the only
loss-making rung. **And billing Starter plus step-ups removes the proration problem entirely** — a step is a
one-time charge, not a plan change, so no subscription-schedule machinery is needed and "just for one month"
answers itself.
