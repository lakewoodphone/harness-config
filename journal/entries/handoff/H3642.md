### The answer, in three lines
1. **The wave is a US Mobile (Warp/Verizon) MMS fault affecting flip phones, dated Fri 2026-10-09**, with US Mobile's
 own phone menu reported as saying *"known MMS issue on Warp, escalated to the parent carrier, fix expected by end
 of day Oct 14"*. Not our filter, not our builds, not the handsets.
2. **Our filter is exonerated on data:** a **factory** E4610 (1 disabled package, zero LPT apps) and a **filtered**
 one (27 disabled) both send picture messages reliably — 5/5 (median 7.90 s) and 10/10 (median 10.05 s).
3. **The customer's handset is the outlier:** 0 deliveries across three SIMs, while **its own SIM `+18483267693` in
 a factory handset delivered 5/5**. Same SIM, same line, different device.

### The tooling that now exists (all under `_scratch/mms-ab/`)
`Invoke-MmsAutoTest.ps1` — the unattended runner (device → state → attach → **send** → radio timeline → Twilio
verdict). Plus `Get-MmsPhoneState.ps1`, `Send-MmsTestPicture.ps1`, `Measure-MmsLatency.ps1`; all four with
`-SelfTest` (green). 33 run records in `auto-runs/`.

**The three things that took the longest and are now written down:**
- **The send is `input keyevent 23`.** Message+'s `Abc | Send | Options` bar is a *label for the physical keys*;
 tapping it does nothing and uiautomator exposes no send node. Detect a send via `requestNetwork.*legacyType=2`.
- **A factory handset stops at Message+'s Terms & Conditions gate** and never composes until Accept is pressed.
- **`am force-stop` before a SEND intent**, else Android raises the existing task and **silently saves a Draft**.

**Keep ADB alive:** `enable-tech-window --duration-minutes 1440 --test-mode` (`--test-mode` is load-bearing; the
normal cap is 60 min).

### Open items (the next session should not have to rediscover these)
1. **The customer's handset was never retested** — the single most informative measurement left. If it now sends,
 the fault was time-bounded; if not, it is a device fault and the **10 orphaned VZWAPP MMS connections** (vs 1–2 on
 healthy units) are the thread.
2. **`com.qualcomm.qti.CdmaCallOptions` is still ENABLED on that handset** (my test; it left the bench that way).
 Restore with `pm disable-user --user 0 com.qualcomm.qti.CdmaCallOptions`.
3. On 616574160 I **accepted the Message+ T&C gate** and left **drafts** from the pre-fix attempts; and set
 `stay_on_while_plugged_in 7`. All flagged in the case files.
4. **The A/B was not simultaneous** — one handset on the cable at a time. `-All` runs both when two are attached.
5. Two hours separate the failing and passing measurements, so a carrier change across that window is not excluded.

### Two lessons worth more than the rest
- **A carrier's own words beat any log I can read.** Two hours of forensics on one handset did not find what a forum
 post of US Mobile's support script found in minutes. When a fault arrives across a *population* on a *date*, ask
 the carrier and read the forums **first**, and do the bench work last.
- **Hold the line constant and vary the device.** The same SIM moving from a failing handset to a working one is
 worth more than any ten log readings, and it was nearly free once the harness existed.
