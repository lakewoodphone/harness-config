IN FLIGHT / BROKEN

- **The live store `operational` holds ZERO `payment_transactions` rows dated in September 2026.**
 `app` (frozen Hetzner archive) holds 35; the missing set is exactly ids 3243-3267, 2026-09-04 to
 2026-09-24, totalling 2,652.32. Not a quiet month — `operational` has 41 September orders, 12,708
 September communications and 49 status rows, and the accounting ledger records 18 Stripe payouts
 to the shop's bank totalling 7,256.22 in September. Recorded as journal pain; full evidence in
 `~/phone/lpt-money-integrity-2026-10-08.md`.

 `operational`; 12 payment ids exist only in `app`; and 257 of 337 shared payment ids carry a

 single-substring patterns (`SALE`, `PHONE`, `LINK`, `ZIP`, `FIG`, `APPLE`, `CABLE`, `A14`), the
 `unclassified` bucket is silent, and `classify()` accepts `source_system` and ignores it.

- Partner commissions of 70.00 are owed (`fig` 10, `gentech/weichbrod` 40, `matis` 20), plus two
 orphan referral attributions (`moshe bachrach`, `rami`) with no `referral_sources` row.
