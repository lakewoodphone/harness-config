## 2026-09-15 21:40 UTC · ZABZ-TECH · Chumash Timeline audit 2026-09-15: located, measured, 9 criticals found, 10 workstreams launched

## The Chumash Timeline audit, 2026-09-15 — where the app is and what the fleet found

**The asset.** `github.com/lakewoodphone/chumash-timeline` — maps the Torah's 5,846 pesukim onto
~6,000 Hebrew years under four traditions (A one-pasuk-per-year, B scaled to 6000, C sefer-to-
millennia, D Devarim-to-centuries), with a gematria calculator, four search modes, and signed-in
community feedback. Live at `https://chumash.lakewoodphoneandtech.com`; container `chumash-timeline`
on `lpt-apps-01` (2.28.33.58), `127.0.0.1:3008`, database `chumash_timeline` on the shared
`lpt-postgres`. **Located only after a journal search for "chumash" — it was not in any repo list on
this machine, and its source path was recorded as "not found" by a previous session.** The lesson:
the journal's `search` is what found a live production app I could not otherwise see.

**The method that worked.** Six read-only parallel audits partitioned by file scope (search/API,
data/DB, gematria engine, live UX, auth/security, build-deploy-docs), plus a seventh whose only job
was to **disprove** the others' claims. Every finding was then re-verified by me directly. Result:
~90 findings, 9 critical, and a corrigenda list of nine of my own claims that were wrong — including
"the deployment runs as root" (it does not), "there are no backups" (there are), and "gematria=26
wrongly returns 0" (0 is correct; the minimum pasuk gematria is 229).

**The state, measured.** 5,846 pesukim all with Hebrew; **0 translations, 0 transliteration,
0 parsha**; `era_events` 21 rows all `method_id='d'`; `year_events` 0; `feedback`/`submissions` 0.
Gates on `main` are green: typecheck 0, eslint 0 warnings across 42 files, 36 tests, build 0. The
live container runs exactly `main` @ `35a25c6` — no code drift.

**The nine criticals, in one breath.** Hebrew text contaminated by Sefaria markup (1,082 rows)
making gematria wrong on 716; production secrets baked into the Docker image; `?gematria=N` fetching
the whole table per request; gematria `count` reporting the truncated count as if it were the total;
Method D mis-mapping today's year to Ha'azinu while labelling it "Nitzavim-Vayelech"; **sign-in dead
for every user** from two independent misconfigurations; `user_ref` being the user's plaintext email
returned by an unauthenticated GET; an empty session secret yielding forgeable sessions; and the
deployment not being reproducible from the repo at all.

**What was clean and must not be re-litigated.** The pasuk→reference resolver (0 mismatches of
5,846); the Hebrew letters themselves (0 byte diffs against a full Sefaria re-fetch of all 174
chapters); the session-cookie crypto; no SQL injection; no secret in git history; the gallery's
"computed, never hard-coded" claim; and the nightly restic→R2 backups.

**How to find it again.** `journal.py search chumash`; the ranked ledger with evidence is at
`~/Code/_chumash-audit/LEDGER.md` and the six full audit reports sit beside it. Ten workstreams were
run as branches `agent/chumash-{data,gematria,search,timeline,ui,deploy,auth}` in
`_worktrees/chumash-timeline/`, integrated serially by the manager.

**Honest gap.** The audits were all read-only, so nothing here is fixed yet at the time of writing —
these are measured defects and a plan, not results. Anything in this entry that reads as *done* would
be wrong.
