## A FULL RE-AUDIT OF EVERY BODY: zero mechanical problems, three REAL ones

63 composed bodies passed every mechanical check (length, trailing "?", engineering words,
doubled words, punctuation artefacts, queue references). Reading them found what the checks
could not:

1. THE SAME QUESTION TWICE. #889 "Techloq called about your cancellation request and got no
 answer" and #895 "Techloq wants a call to process cancellation; you asked to stop billing
 without calling" are one cancellation, filed 3.5 hours apart. The identifier dedupe could
 not see it because neither carries an order number.

2. "Yes thanks" SURVIVED AS A QUESTION. The acknowledgement rule required a SINGLE token, so
 "Text from Alon: Yes thanks" and "Ok thanks!" -- the same non-decision as "Thanks" -- got
 through to the shortlist.

3. SEVEN ROWS WHOSE ONLY ASK WAS "WANT ME TO SKIM?". Six notice bundles and one terms notice.
 That asks him to authorise READING, which is our work, and its only answer produces no
 decision. It is the worst kind of message: it costs his attention and settles nothing.

## WHAT I CHANGED, AND THE THREE TIMES I GOT IT WRONG FIRST

SEMANTIC DEDUPE -- measured, not guessed. The Techloq pair shares only 17% of its words, so
Jaccard is the wrong measure for prose: the identity is a shared NAME plus a shared TOPIC.
Three corrections were needed before it was right:
 * `_entities()` returned NOTHING for "#889 Techloq called..." because it excluded
 sentence-initial capitals -- the one row whose name was the entire point. Now it subtracts
 a short list of sentence starters instead.
 * "same name + same topic" then over-merged: #889 was marked a duplicate of a Techloq NOTICE
 BUNDLE, and the money bundles chained into each other (#626 -> #672 -> #675). A bundle is
 multi-subject by definition, so a bundle can never be "the same question" as anything.
 * Two missed-call notices from DIFFERENT phone numbers then merged at 100% shared words.
 Now: if each row carries an identity token and those tokens are disjoint, they are
 different matters. Exactly ONE true duplicate remains.
ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS: one to three tokens, so "Yes thanks" is caught.

INFORM -- a new disposition. A row whose only ask is permission to read is information, not a
decision. It stays in the queue and appears in `owner-queue.py brief` (verified: all 65 pending
listed), it simply stops being texted as a question. Six bundles plus the Langfuse terms notice.

THE EMPTY-ASK RULE WAS WRONG TWICE. First version was end-anchored and missed "want me to skim
terms before Nov 16 if using ...". Second version (skim|review|read|check|go through) swallowed
TWO REAL QUESTIONS: "#690 Yocheved asks if the Violife smoked provolone hechsher is OK"
(composed as "Want me to check the hechsher?") and "#666 Google Cloud: mark AI agent OAuth
clients as agentic by Nov 11". "Want me to CHECK X" is a genuine decision when X is a fact to
establish; "want me to SKIM this" is not. Narrowed to read-verbs whose object is a document we
were sent.

in his own words, and "one question at a time" is his instruction; a digest of six money notices
