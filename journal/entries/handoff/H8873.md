## 2026-10-18 23:27 UTC · ZABZ-YOGA · No-reset Android sealing: the gate is the account, not the data - six-stream research, one conclusion that matters more than the wipe

Owner asked for a full fan-out on one question: can a customer phone be sealed without a factory reset and
still be foolproof? Six research streams ran over the mesh (AOSP/Android Enterprise rules, Family Link,
commercial MDM on personal phones, industry practice, adb-only hardening, OEM/root/legal). Documented in
kosher-filter-ai docs/research/ANDROID-ENROLMENT-NO-WIPE-2026-10-18.md, pushed on
agent/kf-counter-install-nonwipe (550aa83 + test fix).

CONCLUSION 1 - YES, the reset is avoidable, and the industry already does it. On the adb path the gate is
ACCOUNTS and extra users, NOT setup-completion: AOSP DevicePolicyManagerService skips the setup check on
the isAdb branch and never consults device_provisioned (verified in source for 7.1.1 through main). So:
customer signs out of every account (and removes extra users/work profiles), we claim device owner, they
sign back in. Signing out does not delete photos, messages, apps or app data. Techloq - the closest
competitor - clears accounts over adb "without resetting" and gates removal behind a reseller ticket.
Every mainstream vendor in the survey installs on a phone in use; only the pre-configured-phone sellers
(eGate on a fresh reset, KosherCell, KosherOS) start from a wipe.

CONCLUSION 2 - NO foolproof path without device owner, and each alternative was checked to its source:
Family Link cannot block Settings or account removal ("parents can't ... block your child from stopping
supervision"), a work-profile MDM controls only the profile (six vendors' docs agree), and adb-only
hardening - always-on VPN lockdown, pm disable-user on Settings/Play/installer, forced home - survives
reboot but is undone by safe mode, a recovery reset, or an uninstall. The twelve controls that make our
filter unremovable (PolicyApplier.kt) are DevicePolicyManager calls that throw without device owner.

CONCLUSION 3 - THE SEAL IS NECESSARY BUT NOT SUFFICIENT, and this is the finding that matters most.
DISALLOW_FACTORY_RESET blocks only the Settings path; a recovery/bootloader reset still wipes the phone,
and FRP then asks for the last Google account - which the child has if the account of record is theirs.
So the real anti-reset control is whose account is enrolled, or Samsung Knox Guard (blocks reset, USB,
flashing, FOTA without a reset step). Whoever holds the account holds the phone.

NOT MEASURED: no device was touched; every claim above is documentation. scripts/seal_probe.ps1 answers
