## 2026-10-19 ~17:05 UTC · ZABZ-YOGA (deploys to secratary-cloud) · owner text round 3: the ledger closed asks on non-answers, and the resolver claimed solutions it did not have

The owner asked for the texting system to be made "way better and way smarter": too much of it
is stupid, too much of it he does not need, and the CFO asks questions it could have answered
itself. Round 1 (H9385) and round 2 (`7de8340a9`) built the topic ledger and preask. This round
found the mechanism underneath most of his complaints, fixed it, and **measured every guard
against his entire history before deploying it**.

### THE ONE BUG UNDER FOUR OF HIS COMPLAINTS

`textdecide.best_ask()` falls back to "the newest ask that names a merchant" at strength 0, and
that strength-0 attribution was treated as an ANSWER — it closed the ask AND wrote a learned
alias. Proof from the ledger, not from the code's description: his reply *"I don't know. I want
you to figure this out and give me more details"* is attached to TEN consecutive asks
(`owner_text_turn` ids 117–127), and *"That was just groceries"* to six (48–53).

Chain: reply mis-attributed → ask closed with a non-answer → alias learned from it →
`merchant_resolve` reads that alias back as `solved=True confidence=0.92` → **solved means DO NOT
ASK** → the charge is never categorised → the question returns weeks later. Which is exactly why
he says *"Didn't we file a few days ago?"*, *"are you sure I didn't tell this to you already?"*
and *"which one are you calling out?"*

Fixed: `is_non_answer()`, `attribute_reply()` carrying `n_open`/`learnable`/`closes`, and
`build_alias()` refusing a non-answer, an inferred match with 2+ asks open, the ask's own guess
and the literal `unknown`. `sms-responder._learn_from_his_answer` now calls it.

**The licence that took two attempts.** Refusing ALL strength-0 learning cured the wrong alias and
lost *"That was just groceries"* — so the variable is not match strength, it is **ambiguity**:
`learnable = not non_answer and category_in_his_words and (strength >= 2 or n_open == 1)`.
Justified by measurement: **193 of 240 replies followed more than one text (median 4, max 32)**.

### THE RESOLVER LIED, AND ONE LIE COST MONEY

Run by hand on the authority, `merchant_resolve.py --no-research` reported `solved=True` at 0.92
for *"I don't know"*, for *"Didn't we file a few days ago"*, and for his Dave & Buster's reply
filed against a merchant called `photo`. Also: **four category names the rung emitted do not
exist** in the ledger's 137 categories (`Tolls & Travel`, `Software & AI`, `Parking`,
`Adjustment`) — `category_id_for()` returns "" for an unknown name, so the rung would have
resolved and filed nothing. And the DeepSeek vendor entry was booking our own AI bill as
*Business, Industrial & Scientific Supplies* at 0.93, **overwriting the ledger's own
`auto_rule:DeepSeek API (conf=0.99)`** which says Software & Online Services.

### THE INSTRUMENT THAT CHANGED THE OUTCOME

`scripts/text-channel/replay-owner-text.py` replays every text that ever reached him through the
live guards and reports **WOULD-LOSE (texts he engaged with that a guard would refuse)** separately
from WOULD-SAVE. Its first version scored the new guards at 14 lost / 61 saved. Reading all 14
showed **TEN were false positives**: `addressed` treated "Done —", "No —" and "Understood —" as
names, and `instruction` silenced two genuine **security questions he had answered**
("Legitimate", "Yes, it was authorized by me"). Narrowed — an id is not an instruction, a word and
a dash is not a name — it scores **5 lost / 28 saved**, and four of the five are texts that should
never have been sent in that form.

**Without this replay I would have deployed a rule that silences security questions**, and its own
115-check suite was green.
