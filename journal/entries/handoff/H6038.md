## 2026-10-14 15:26 UTC · ZABZ-TECH · Greenfeld pickup label v7 (customer-facing, 51.4mm) - and the lesson that v6 was the internal secretary shape printed for a box going out

## 2026-10-14 · ZABZ-TECH · Greenfeld pickup label: v7 printed (customer-facing, 51.4 mm). v6 was the internal shape and was wrong for a box going out.

**WHAT HAPPENED.** The owner asked for a pickup-box label and I printed **v6** — built on the shape of the earlier bin label, which is **addressed to the secretary** ("hand over the stick, collect 15, then tell Eliyahu", "WHAT TO HAND OVER"). His correction, verbatim: *"this is a label for the pickup box which will have his laptop and usb, so there's no secretary instructions on it just his info how much he paid and how muc is due"*. So v6 is the wrong document for the purpose and its **88.4 mm of tape is spent**. If v6 is stuck to anything, take it off.

**LESSON, ONE LINE: before designing a label, know WHO READS IT.** The labels already on this job were for the bin and read by staff; this one is read by the customer. Train-of-thought reuse of the previous JSON produced a perfectly-rendered, correctly-printed, wrong-target label — the failure was in the audience, not in the layout, and a dry run of it looks correct.

**v7 IS THE LABEL: 51.4 mm**, printed 2026-10-14 15:26 UTC, Brother QL-820NWB at 192.168.50.60 (identified by model, `state READY`, roll 29 mm), from `_labelprinter/greenfeld_dell_v7.json`. His information only:
FOR Yehoshua Greenfeld / 845-659-0807 / Order WO26091712718; IN THIS BOX the 256 GB USB stick with his files (label GREENFELD) and his Dell Latitude 3301 laptop; PAID 95.96 on 2026-10-13; STILL DUE 15.

**PROOF:** image sent == image reviewed, `sha256 f619fe74818ee25ff3cb0bffd4e476c886b85facf9bb77f5a7f9c972e646f56e`. **Tape is not provable by the tool** (`did_print` false by construction on this model, only counter is an integer metre) — the roll or a person at the box is the proof.

**THE 15 / 16.00 POINT STANDS AND MUST NOT BE "FIXED":** the label carries the **basis** figure the shop quotes (the terminal adds 6.625 pc, which is exactly why 90 became 95.96 on this order); the account's tax-inclusive balance is **16.00**. Both are true statements of the same debt.

**STILL OUTSTANDING:** collect the 15 basis (16.00 charged) at pickup.
