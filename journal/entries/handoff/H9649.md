### Three things follow, all of them worse and more useful

1. **Nearly a quarter of everything that reaches him is a truncated half-message.** 315 texts were
 cut to fit the ceiling. The half-sentence I fixed as one case in the audit (`"…the contact block
 you supplied is"`) is not one case — it is **the routine shape of a fifth of the channel**. That is
 the next real piece of work, and it is a **composer** problem across many producers, not a gate
 problem.

2. **When he answers, he is answering into a pile of nine** (median 9, maximum 76). The "unified
 brain is not working" complaint is exactly this, and at true scale it is far past what a 4-text
 median suggested. Any inference about which message he answered is not a coin toss at nine — it is
 noise. Which is why the attribution fix (round 1) mattered more than it looked.

3. **The guards are safer than the narrower sample said.** 11 lost against 123 saved, versus 14/61.
 `credential` now shows 17 refused and **zero** losses. And a class that was invisible before —
 `cfo_guess`, 6 refused / 2 lost — only appears because the AI line's ledger holds the CFO
 questions at all.

### The denominator, stated plainly

The reply rate fell from 49.8% to 21.8% purely because the population grew: the 3,306 added rows are
mostly machine sends he was never going to answer. **That is not the channel getting worse; it is the
first time the denominator has been honest.**

`replay-owner-text.py` now reports which sources answered, so a run that silently covers one ledger
again cannot look like a full one.
