## The location complaint is ours, and it is measured

`docker logs --since 6h waze-mdm-dns-filter-1`: **6,249 queries `default-blocked`**, top two are
`gs-loc-new.ls-apple.com.akadns.net` (2,515) and `gspe19-geo-selector.ls2-apple.com.akadns.net`
(1,285), plus `gs-loc.ls-apple.com.akadns.net` (417) — and the hourly rate is climbing
(10h 6 → 19h 1,319 → 20h 1,127). Probed directly: all three return **rcode 3**, while
`gsp-ssl.ls.apple.com` and `gspe19-ssl.ls.apple.com` return rcode 0.

Cause: the allowlist carries `ls.apple.com`; iOS 26 resolves through `ls-apple.com.akadns.net` /
`ls2-apple.com.akadns.net`. The 2026-08-14 location fix (`/opt/waze-mdm/scripts/apply-location-fix.py`)
added the `*.ls.apple.com` family and stopped there. The proxy PAC has the same gap for those names
and blackholes anything unmatched (`PROXY 127.0.0.1:9`).

**Not the cause:** the kiosk profile does not restrict location (`allowLocationServices = True`);
and AT&T is not implicated by anything we hold — `fleet_telnyx_sims` has 0 rows for 23/25/36.

**Not applied, because `waze-mdm-01` is `read-only` in `settings/machines/host-posture.tsv`.**
