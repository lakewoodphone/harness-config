**First blind result (geometry engine, desktop CPU, all 282 frames):**

- median **363 ms**, p95 **440 ms** per frame — a desktop, not a phone, and reported as such.
- found a person in **235 of 282** frames.
- mean forearm-skin fraction by owner level: 1 → 0.65, 2 → 0.75, 5 → 0.25, 9 → 0.08, **10 → 0.35**.
 Spearman **−0.36**. The direction is right and the strength is weak: level 10 should be near zero and
 is not, because the segmenter calls light clothing skin.
- **false-subject rate 31%**: it found a person in 4 of the 13 frames the owner rated as containing none
 (mannequins, wax figures, a printed plate).

**The other four engines are unavailable, with exact reasons, not zero scores:** mediapipe's old
`solutions` API is gone in 1.1 so the Tasks models had to be downloaded; GantMan and NudeNet weights are
not on this machine; CLIP needs torch and 350 MB of weights. Two of those four cannot reach a ladder level
even when they run — NSFW models answer rung 1 and nothing else.

**Traps paid for in this session, worth keeping:** `read_image` reports the hash of its normalised copy,
not the file, so labels keyed on it match nothing; MediaPipe keeps a grayscale JPEG at one channel and
then fails every later frame with a tensor-size error; a PowerShell `-replace` with `\n` writes a literal
backslash-n; and `$pid` is read-only, so a liveness loop silently never runs.
