## The four defects, with the number that proves each

1. **4258 had 931.00 archived with nothing behind it.** Estis 632.00 (09-25) and Polka Dot 299.00
 (09-27), `ingestion_status='archived'`, `duplicate_of_transaction_id` NULL, **no audit_log row
 at all**, and an exact amount+date twin search across every account returned **zero rows**. The
 plaid balance 4,557.92 equals the sum of all 68 rows **including** them, so the balance carried
 them and no expense line did. The statement closure check cannot see this because it sums active
 rows only. Fixed: reactivated, `performed_by='cfo-cards-20261020'`.

2. **Both cards booked business spend to the personal entity.** 14 rows / 1,572.26 on 4258 and 20
 rows / 10,311.11 on 2005 carried business categories assigned by cfo-resolve at >=0.95 (7 of the
 4258 rows with a matched supplier marketplace order) but `entity_id='personal'`. Fixed: moved to
 `lpt` with an lpt-scoped category row, audit_log and transaction_evidence each.

3. **On 2005 it only LOOKED attributed, and this is the trap worth remembering.** Three rows on
 22005 said `entity='lpt'` (Alibaba 5,499.67, 3,110.30, plan fee 31.37) and I first read that as
 "the business side is handled". They were exactly the rows a later de-duplication had
 **deactivated**, and their live twins were `personal`. Running `owner-loan-balance.py` after the
 fix returned `A3_amex_biz $0.00`, which is what exposed it. Any "is this card attributed?"
 query MUST exclude duplicates, or a dead twin will answer yes.

4. **The owner loan counted the whole card.** `A3` summed every debit on 22005 minus every payment
 and labelled it ALIBABA-NET. It now also carries groceries, medical, a phone bill and ice cream,
 so personal spending was booked as money he lent the business. Card 4258 had no component at
 all. Now A3 is entity=lpt gross (the convention A2/A7 already use) and A9 adds 4258. Register
 48,347.76 -> 57,532.81. Also: `cfo-entity-statements.py:1091` printed the owner loan as a
 hardcoded `53,777.10`; it now points at the register rather than quoting a number that cannot
 move.
