 owned by no repository; a byte-identical copy is `deploy/tools/search.py` (md5
 `e15aa2d9777544f23570dfe7fe611918`). It carries three fixes made this round:
 * a symbol fallback ranks definition-shaped hits (`def name`, `function name`, `class name`,
 `const name =`, `name(`) ahead of documents that merely mention the name — `read_sessions` went
 from `measure-tool-call.py` to `read-session.js`;
 * `what=files`/`what=symbol` fall back to the estate index when the live scan finds nothing (this
 is what made the definition reachable at all — it lives on a workstation);
 * `limit` is coerced to `int`: `ps_search` sends every argument as a string, so `limit * 3` was
 `"333"` and `max()` raised `'>' not supported between instances of 'int' and 'str'` — a crash in
 the one mode that must never crash.
