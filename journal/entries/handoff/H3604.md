# Round 12: the 8x "no Charge control on the hub" major is a harness artefact - and there is a real question underneath it

2026-10-13, ZABZ-YOGA. Goal `goal-a8614123`, round 12.

## THE CLAIM, AND WHAT IT ACTUALLY IS

`[major] No "Charge" control exists on the Work Orders hub - the route is reachable only by typing the
URL` - **8 occurrences, the loudest finding the walk produces.**

Traced to the end this round:

- **The walk types the URL.** `journeys.ts` `chargeOnTheSpot()` opens with `await walk.go('/charge')`.
 So the finding "the route is reachable only by typing the URL" describes **the walk's own behaviour**,
 not the product's.
- **`/charge` is a CUSTOMER page.** `git grep -n "'/charge'\|/charge/" -- frontend/src` returns
 **nothing** - because the route is a Cloudflare Function, `functions/charge/[token].js`, the
 tokenised pay page sent to a customer. It is not, and was never meant to be, a staff action.
- **The staff charge surface is `StaffSaleChargePanel`**, rendered in `StaffNewIntakeView.tsx:539` and
 acting through `payments.chargeOnTheSpot`. Staff take a walk-in payment by starting a **New** intake,
 which is the `New` control that IS on the hub.

**So the hub having no charge control is by design**, and the major is the harness pointing at the wrong
page and then reporting that nothing links to it.

## THE REAL QUESTION UNDERNEATH, WHICH IS THE OWNER'S TASTE AND NOT A DEFECT

A staff member looking at the hub sees `50 non-refundable labor & diagnostic fee` on the row (measured
last round) and has no way to act on it without leaving for the intake screen. **Whether that is right
is a design question**, and there is a defensible answer either way:

- **For the current design:** a sale is a distinct act with its own screen, tax handling, deposit logic
 and offline segments; putting payment into a list row invites charging the wrong record.
- **Against it:** the balance is shown where the decision is made, and the action is one screen away.

**That goes to the owner as a taste question if anywhere** - it is exactly the "not easy to use"
territory, and it is not a bug to fix from a harness report.

## THE PATTERN, NOW THREE FOR THREE

Every loud finding I have traced this session has turned out to be the harness:

| finding | what it actually was |
|---|---|
| 8x no Charge control on the hub | the walk types a **customer** URL and reports nothing links to it |
| 8x no work order could be opened | `table tbody tr` locator against a `ul` of buttons |
| the counter wastes half a 1920px window | a deliberate `<aside>` side panel + a deliberate `80rem` cap |

**The three loudest classes of finding in the daily report are all instrument error.** That is the
single most important fact about this loop right now, and it is why the last three rounds went into the
instrument rather than into "optimisations".

## WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE LOOP'S VALUE

The pipeline is sound and proven end to end - roadmap -> supply -> ledger -> wake -> a woken shift - and
the walk runs 107 journeys in under five minutes against the live site. **What it does not yet do is
produce trustworthy findings**, because the specs encode the app's shape in several hand-copied places
and the app has moved. **Until that is fixed, the loop's product is noise**, and a shift acting on it
would change working screens.

The fix already started (PR 393: read the screen's own `data-row-count` before filing a blocker). The
next step is the same idea applied to *these* claims: **a "no X control" finding should be preceded by
an enumeration of what controls DO exist**, the way it was verified here. Then a stale expectation
fails as "the screen changed" rather than as "the product is broken".
