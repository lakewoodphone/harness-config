small adaptation and not a copy:

1. two module constants: `SENT_TTL_HOURS = 168` and the state path `/root/.owner-text/sent-items.json`;
2. `item_fingerprint(text)` (sha1 prefix) plus `_load_sent()` / `_mark_sent(text)`, best-effort, erring

3. in their `sms_body`: pick the first item whose fingerprint is not in the state, or return `""` when

4. in their `send()`: call `_mark_sent(body)` **only** on `result.get("ok") and not held and not
