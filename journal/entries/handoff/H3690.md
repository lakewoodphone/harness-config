## 2026-10-20 16:13 UTC · ZABZ-TECH · PR #554: the counter can take the payment it asks for; charge descriptions name the job

Follows H3688 (the WO26101812752 money audit). This session ended with **PR #554** open against
`test`: `lpt/counter-money-20261020` → https://github.com/lakewoodphone/phone-and-tech-full/pull/554

**What is in it, and the one judgement call worth remembering.**

1. The counter's charge panel read every typed figure as pre-tax and added 6.625%. On a job whose
 total already carries the tax, that made the amount the screen itself asks for (106.63 on a 159.94
 job with 53.31 paid) into 113.69 — over the balance — and the over-collection gate refused the
 payment. The fix keeps tax-added as the default (the owner's own "i entered 15, it should add tax
 to it automatically") and re-reads a figure as tax-INCLUDED **only when adding the tax would push
 it past the balance while the figure itself still fits**. It can only move a figure downward, the
 gate still re-checks whichever reading wins, and the checkbox still pins either reading.
 That asymmetry is the whole safety argument: no path can take more than it did before.
2. Charge descriptions named the job by database id (`work order 3138`). `describeChargeJob` now
 writes `WO26101812752 (#12752)`, ticket derived from the order number's trailing digits so no
 extra query; a caller with no number still writes the id rather than inventing one.
