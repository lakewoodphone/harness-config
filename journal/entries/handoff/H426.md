## 2026-09-14 23:20 UTC · SECRATARY · The question card's question is on screen: anchored to the visual viewport, proved in the live app

## 2026-09-14 23:25 UTC · SECRATARY · The phone question card's QUESTION is now on screen, proved in pixels

**CHANGED**
- The owner's second complaint about `ask_user_question` on the phone — *"I can't scroll up enough to see the actual question. I just see the options for responses"* — is fixed and verified. Root cause: `assets/question-card.css` anchored the sheet with `inset: 0` + `height: 100dvh`, i.e. the **layout** viewport. The visible area is the **visual** viewport, and with the keyboard up it is both shorter and **offset**, so the sheet began above the first visible pixel and the question — the first thing in the card — was the part that vanished. Nothing could scroll it back: the page behind the sheet has nothing to scroll. CSS cannot read `visualViewport`.
- Two halves. `assets/question-card.css` now top-anchors the frame as the no-JS layout. `packages/plugin-mobile/lib/client.js` gained a third behaviour: on card mount, and on every `visualViewport` resize/scroll, it measures the sheet and its title against the band the reader can actually see and pins the frame to that band with inline `!important` styles, removing the repair once the card fits. It leaves its measurement at `window.__dshPhoneCard`.
- New tool `scripts/question-card-live-probe.py`: mounts the card's real markup into the **real app's** composer seat and reads the result back — the replica in `question-card-check.py` cannot see the real ancestor chain or the visual viewport.
- Evidence: replica check + composer check pass (`scripts/check-phone-ui.sh`); the plugin smoke test gained 7 assertions on the repair (all pass); live probe at 393x852 shows the title at y=39 with no repair, and with a forced band of 240x420 (the keyboard case) `fits:false, repaired:true` before and frame y=240 h=420, title y=279, Submit y=608 after — `QUESTION VISIBLE IN BAND: True`.
