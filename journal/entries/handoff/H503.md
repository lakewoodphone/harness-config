## 2026-09-17 17:08 UTC · zabz-tech · Round 19: the fifth surface - C13 asks whether a conversation exists behind the declared phone, and found 3 wrong-phone candidates

# H503 — round 19: the FIFTH surface — is there a conversation behind the declared phone?

## WHAT C13 IS, AND WHY IT IS INFO

The other four checks answer *"what does the record say"*. **C13 asks whether the shop and the customer
ever actually spoke** — the only evidence a case is real rather than a transcription. It is the comms
surface built in `dialpad.py`, now wired into the verifier.

**The calibration is the whole problem, and the numbers say so.** MEASURED: **31 of 351 hub records with
a phone (8.8%) have ZERO comms behind that number** across all four Dialpad tables, against **2,494
distinct phones indexed**. That is **not** automatically a defect — a walk-in whose entire contact
happened in person is a normal pattern in this business, and so is a job phoned in months before
Dialpad retained it. Reporting those as errors would be the *third* time this file cried wolf (C07's
net-transition ranking; C11's 40-error proxy).

## WHAT IT IS GENUINELY GOOD FOR — and it found something on its first run

**The case where the declared phone is WRONG.** Those show as zero comms on the declared number while a
sibling record for the same customer name has plenty on a different one, so the finding **names the
candidate numbers** rather than just reporting silence.

**3 of the 31 carry that shape:**

| record | what C13 found |
|---|---|
| `apple-iphone-14-test-customer-2026-07-16` | 'Test Customer' with three numbers that do have comms — correctly flagged, it is a test record |
| `galaxy-a16-ms-shochet-2026-06-24` | **'Ms. Shochet' has comms on 8482453600 and 8484592338**, while this record declares a third number |
| `manual-shapiro-tobie-green-7739430859-2026-04-23` | **'Tobie Green' has comms on 8482102138** |

**Those two are worth a human look, which is exactly what an INFO is for.** The other 28 have no
candidate at all — the shape of a walk-in or a stale number. **The finding says which shape it is**,
because a bare "no comms" invites the wrong conclusion in both directions.

## ENGINEERING NOTES

- **One indexed pass** over four tables, collecting distinct normalised phones into a set, then set
 membership per record — not a query per case. **Total runtime 12.8 s** for the whole verifier.
- **Read-only**, and an unreadable table reports **UNMEASURED** rather than "no comms". An empty result
 is not evidence.
- **Verified in both directions:** ERROR unchanged at **6**, WARN unchanged at **67** — C13 introduces
 no false positive, which is the test a check has to pass before it is worth having.
