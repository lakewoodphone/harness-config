# Round 11: "no Charge control on the hub" CONFIRMED by enumerating every control and clicking a row

2026-10-13, ZABZ-YOGA. Goal `goal-a8614123`, round 11. This applies the lesson from L3766 - describe the

## THE CLAIM

`[major] No "Charge" control exists on the Work Orders hub - the route is reachable only by typing the
URL` — **8 occurrences, more than any other finding.**

## THE VERIFICATION, BY LOOKING FOR THE POSITIVE RATHER THAN THE NEGATIVE

**Path 1 — enumerate every clickable thing on the hub and search money words.** 43 controls. The ONLY
charge-like one is `staff-open-chip-AWAITING_PAYMENT`, labelled **"Awaiting payment"** — and it is a
**filter chip**, not an action. Money words present on screen: `collected`, `owed`, `payment`, `price` —
all labels and text, none of them actions. Every row button is labelled `Open <device> for <name> —
Ticket <n>`.

**Path 2 — click a row and see what appears after.** A control that only exists post-click would not be
in that list, so this was tested separately:

```
before: /work-orders
after click: /work-orders <- did NOT navigate
headings: ONEGAPP-desktop-1791344182621
 50 non-refundable labor & diagnostic fee
moneyControls: [] <- no charge/pay/collect/tender/checkout control
controlCount: 11
hasCopyLink: false
```

**So clicking a row opens an IN-PLACE PANEL** showing the record and its price, and that panel offers no
way to take a payment.

## WHAT IS ESTABLISHED, AND WHAT IS NOT

**Established:** from the Work Orders hub, by both paths tested — scanning every control, and opening a
record — a staff member cannot reach a payment. **And the price is right there**, which is what makes
the absence pointed: the screen tells you what is owed and gives you no way to collect it.

**NOT established, and not claimed:** whether a charge action exists somewhere I did not look — a
work-order *detail page* rather than the in-place panel, a row menu opened another way, or a control on
the side panel. **The most likely explanation of the walk's three loudest majors is that they are all
this one thing:** the specs expect a work order detail PAGE with pricing and payment controls, and the
app now opens an in-place panel instead, so J3/J4 "cannot open a work order" and J5 "cannot take a
payment" — while a charge screen DOES exist (the walk's own J5 reaches one, with 7-8 controls).

## WHY THIS MATTERS MORE THAN THE NUMBER

Taking money is the shop's whole day. A screen that shows a balance and offers no way to collect it is
the kind of thing the owner means by "not easy to use". **It is now a verified, well-evidenced item
rather than an 8-times-repeated lookup**, and the next step is small and specific: find how a staff
member is *supposed* to charge from this screen, and either point them at it or add it.
