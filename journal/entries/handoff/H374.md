## 2026-09-16 02:43 UTC · ZABZ-TECH · Her launcher opens: one default resolved to the wrong person's paths, and the delivery that keeps it fixed now proves itself

## CHANGED

**Yocheved's laptop launcher works, and the reason it did not is one default.**

Her desktop shortcut opened a console for a few seconds and no assistant appeared. The cause was not the
window code: `multi-window/windows.json` is the OWNER's fleet config (stateDir `C:\Users\ezabz\.dsh\...`)
and `dshw.ps1` defaulted `-ConfigPath` to it, so two entry points on her box -- the task's
`dshw.ps1 new` and the `open.cmd` chain -- ran against a stranger's directories. Her box has a stale
`C:\Users\ezabz` profile, so the first diagnostics write got **access denied**, and under
`$ErrorActionPreference='Stop'` that aborted the launch before `Open-SlotWindow`. Her engine answered on
3099 all day; `dshw status` said 0 windows. Details and evidence: **L1688**, **D202**.

Fixed, in `harness-config` (all on origin/master, all delivered to her box):
- `dshw.ps1` picks `machines/<COMPUTERNAME>.windows.json` when it exists; `doctor` prints the config and
 state dir it resolved. Best-effort log writes now go through a shadowing `Add-Content` that warns and
 never throws -- failing to *narrate* a run can no longer be what ends it.
- `launch.cmd` pointed at `machines\dshw.ps1`, which does not exist; now `%~dp0..\dshw.ps1`, with
 `-ConfigPath` on BOTH invocations. `open.ps1` is a delegator, not a second copy. `open.cmd` counts the
 windows that actually exist and will not add a second one.
- **Delivery.** Applying was working; delivering was not. Her harness-config has no `.git` and cannot
 pull, so it sat at 2026-09-14 while the repo moved. `scripts/push-harness-config-to-laptop.ps1` builds
 a journal-free archive of origin/master, pushes it over the exec API, applies it, runs her own
 `harness-sync.cmd`, and reads `HARNESS-CONFIG-APPLIED.json` back before recording success. Scheduled as
 **`PersonalSecretary-PushHarnessConfig`** every 15 minutes on ZABZ-TECH. **L1689**.

**Journal integrity: fourteen ids meant two entries, and the allocator's cause is fixed.**
`git_max(fetch=True)` fetched and then grepped only the working tree and HEAD, never a remote ref, so two
machines both chose the same next number. Proof at HEAD `d905c44`: old allocator `next-id lessons` ->
**L1500**, fixed -> **L1686**. Renumbered the 14 (origin keeps the id; originals MOVED to
`journal/archive/collided-ids-2026-09-15/`), fixed 6 stale hashes, and added the missing
`journal/tools/rehash.py`. `check` is now **0 errors** and the checkout fast-forwards again. **L1690**,
commits `720bcb0`, `1421dab`.
