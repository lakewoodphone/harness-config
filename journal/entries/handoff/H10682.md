### Two things were wrong with the ruler itself

1. **Two questions could never pass.** "the label printer media size" expected `DK-2210` — text inside
 the runbook — in the LOCATOR, which is a path. Expectations may now be a list (`expect_any`) and
 those two name the document that holds the content, or an alternative that is equally the answer.
 **A benchmark that cannot pass is worse than no benchmark: it reports a failure that is an artefact
 of the test.** Check that what you measure can produce the value you test for.
2. **The candidate pool was narrower than the answer.** A name match is the best NAMED match, not the
 best BM25 match, so it only competes if the pool reaches far enough: `label_printer.py` sits at
 rank 2 in a pool of 120 and is absent from the pool of 40 a caller asking for three hits used to
 get. Pool is now `max(limit * 6, 80)`, which took hit@3 from 7/12 to 9/12 at no latency cost.
