## What I was asked, and what was actually missing

The owner asked me to pull up the whole Waze device system and to fix the gap he named himself:
*"we didn't like get accessories… a USB-A car adapter [and] a magnet mount for the car… mostly air
conditioning vent mounts… very strong and not take up a lot of space and reliable but not too expensive."*

He was right, and the repo already said so — `15-business/product.md` §2 carried the line **"No charger,
no mount"**. Every device sold so far went out with a phone, a case and a cable and **nothing to plug it
into and nothing to hold it.** The whole-system half was already documented (the 54-file tree behind
`00-START-HERE.md`, read this session), so **nothing was rebuilt** — the accessory area did not exist and
was created new.

## The three questions

**1. What I learned that I did not know.** Amazon cannot be read from this box by any automated route except
one — `web_fetch` on the **mobile** endpoint `amazon.com/gp/aw/d/<ASIN>`; plain `curl` and headless Chrome
are both served a 3.5 KB bot wall that returns **HTTP 200** with nothing in it, and `browser_use_run` is in
L0_SHADOW and refuses. **And a price anywhere on a page is not an offer:** the Anker runner-up one agent
recommended turned out to be *"Currently unavailable"*, its 14.99 living only in a related-items carousel.
The rest is `L7358`.

**2. What is different, and what is next.** The device now has two accessory part numbers, prices with
arithmetic, and a verification pass — where it had a known hole. **Nothing is ordered and no price is set:
both price decisions are owner-queue rows #542 and #543**, the second being the wall charger the box still
lacks entirely (a customer cannot charge at home on day one). **The next physical step is a five-minute
hand check, not a purchase:** open one Lifedance case and confirm the ESR ring sits flush — whether it fits
*inside* the case is **not verified**, and LISEN's own listing warns the in-case install weakens the pull.
Then buy one charger and one mount and try them.

**3. What still hurts.** The mount pick is the **bulkiest** candidate (3.56 × 3.39 × 2.72 in) against the
owner's "not take up a lot of space" — strength and size genuinely trade here and I ranked strength first
because he did. The ESR seller is a third-party marketplace seller (`Minghutech-US`), so a counterfeit is
possible and should be checked at order time. No bulk price exists anywhere — every figure is a retail read,
and any 25- or 50-unit landed cost would be invented. **The fix for all three is the same and it is his
hand, not mine: buy one of each and put them in a car.**
