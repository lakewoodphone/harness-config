## 2026-10-14 14:40 UTC · secratary-cloud · Waze kiosk: Wi-Fi and Bluetooth reachable, and the customer decides whether Wi-Fi is on

## CHANGED

Waze kiosk can now reach and switch Wi-Fi and Bluetooth; Wi-Fi is not pinned on anymore.

1. **The controls exist again.** `com.apple.wifi`, `com.apple.wifiSettings` and
 `com.apple.BluetoothSettings` removed from `blockedAppBundleIDs` in the DRN kiosk profiles
 (136 -> 133). Until this, the Settings panes themselves were blocked and `allowListedAppBundleIDs`
 is `["com.waze.iphone"]`, so the 2026-10-13 `allowBluetoothModification=true` decision had no
 reachable surface and was inert. Every OTHER Settings pane stays blocked.
2. **The customer decides whether Wi-Fi is on.** `forceWiFiPowerOn` true -> false. Owner overruled my
 initial pin-it-on after I asked: *"No. Let the customer decide. People don't like when things are
 forced on them for no reason."* Pinning it on only had force while the pane was blocked.
3. **Scoped to the kiosk, not the shared default.** The shared `make_restrictions_payload()` keeps its
 `True` default, so `mdm-quarantine`, the soft kiosk and the owner's phone are **unchanged** - proven
 by building each profile and reading the key back, not by reading the source.

Files: served on `waze-mdm-01` (`waze-layered-kiosk`, `-phase1`, `-drn13`); repo
`app/services/nanomdm_service.py`, `deploy/waze-mdm/scripts/generate-profiles.py`, the tracked
`waze-layered-kiosk.mobileconfig` (== served file byte for byte, md5 `f4a71cbfbeec938c4f44ce8ad7f79c00`),
`tests/test_nanomdm_service.py` (47 pass), and four docs.

Pushed to **the six only** (DRN 2002, 2004-2008) via `POST /fleet/devices/{drn}/lockdown`; all six
`all_succeeded`. Backups: `/opt/waze-mdm/backups/wifi-bt-allow-20261014T143031Z/` (pre-everything) and
`/opt/waze-mdm/backups/forcewifi-off-20261014T143742Z/` (pre-decision-2). **Two backups now exist and
copying the wrong one silently reverts the other decision** - the rollback section names which is which.

Commits on `fix/waze-wifi-bluetooth-switching-20261014` (pushed): `ee3c5e979`, `e03037362`, plus the
three 2026-10-13 Bluetooth commits cherry-picked in.
