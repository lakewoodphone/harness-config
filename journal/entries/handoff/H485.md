fields in its own record — `workflow.orderState: "declined"`, `customerState: "declined-at-intake"`,
`internalSummary: "CLOSED - DECLINED AT INTAKE, owner decision 2026-09-17"`, `pricing.structure:

**Measured effect:** `--dry-run-all` before → `383 record(s) — CREATE 8, SKIP 375`; after →
`383 record(s) — CREATE 7, SKIP 376`.

**Why I did NOT flip the other seven.** Marking them `shouldSyncToWebsite: false` would assert the shop
is not doing work it IS doing — a live deposit, ordered parts, a held device. The honest finding is
that the reporter's headline conflates **"would create"** with **"should create"**, and the records
*literally cannot carry a reason*: `operational` is `additionalProperties: false`, so there is nowhere
on a record to say "held, here is why". That is a design gap in the record schema, and it is the real
next fix.
