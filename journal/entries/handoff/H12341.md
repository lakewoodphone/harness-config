### The lesson that cost the most and is worth keeping

**Ask what WRITES a field before asserting it, and whether that write is synchronous.** The API-30
"non-uniformity" was invented by a test asserting policy in the ten seconds before a BroadcastReceiver
delivered it. Two rounds were spent treating a harness race as a product defect. `L12270`.

And the second: **a flag with no consumer is worse than no flag** (the `monitorImages` case), and **a
test whose name contradicts its assertion is the same defect in the suite**.
