# 2026-10-14 · ZABZ-YOGA · Round 7: 360 and 361 disposed of as unverifiable, and 377 turned out to be a real receivable — so it was NOT closed

Goal round 7. State: **`journal/state/work-order-remediation-program-2026-10-14.md`**.

## WHY THESE THREE CAME UP

The objective's item (1) itself directs a disposition for cash and Zelle rows: *"cash/Zelle recorded
honestly as unverifiable rather than chased."* That is an instruction to me, not to the owner, so the
rows whose crux is genuinely unestablishable money — which I had been holding back as "his decisions" —
are mine to close under it. Three of the fourteen were that shape. **Examining them changed the answer
for one of them.**

## 360 — CLOSED. A missing figure, not a debt

`WO2605130005`, Cohen on a Shapiro referral, a shell swap. The customer wrote *"Please send invoice via
email"* on 2026-05-13; on 2026-05-14 the order moved to `READY_FOR_PICKUP` with the reason **"paid and
sent invoice"** and has rested there since. Total 0.00, chargeAmount null, no payment row.

**The backlog question was "what was Cohen charged", not "what does he still owe".** The order was
settled; only the figure was never entered. That is a **missing figure on a settled order**, and nothing
on the record says the customer owes anything. Closed as such: nothing owed, nothing chased, and the
note says the invoice is an email body that the indexed surface does not reach — so if it is ever
produced, it belongs on the order as a **factual correction, not a new charge**.

## 361 — CLOSED. Genuinely unverifiable

`WO2605140004`, Mattis Klein, the Qin water-damage job. The customer asked *"What's the name for the
zelle?"* on 2026-05-19 and a message reading *"K just sent 60"* follows. The order was set to
`READY_FOR_PICKUP` by the secretary's own reconciliation at confidence 0.98.

**The message proves a 60 Zelle was sent. It does not say what it paid for** — and whether the Qin was
agreed at 75 or 60 is equally unavailable. Nothing in either store ties the 60 to this order, and a Zelle
leaves no processor record to check. UNVERIFIED, neither owed nor settled, **not chased** — and
deliberately **not** attributed as a payment row, because that is exactly what the record cannot support.

## 377 — NOT CLOSED, because it is the opposite case

`WO26072412517`, a B2B customer (contact Moshe Bachrach), three drives recovered. Its own description
itemises them to a stated total of **1210** (160 + 450 + 600, 300DDR tickets 72523/72524/72525). The
closing note of 2026-08-18 records **1000 paid in cash**. The status row written **the same minute** says
*"still owes 250"*.

**1210 − 1000 = 210, not 250.** Two figures written by one hand inside one minute.

So this is **not** an unverifiable row — the arithmetic from the order's own figures gives **210
outstanding**. It is a **real receivable on a real B2B customer**, and whether to pursue it is the
owner's, not a note's. The arithmetic and the contradiction are now written onto the order so the next
reader does not have to work them out; the queue row stays **open on purpose**.

**The general lesson, and it is why I read these three before closing them:** *"cash rows are
unverifiable"* is a category, not a finding. One of the three had the figures on the order all along.
