## 2026-10-11 21:15 UTC · ZABZ-TECH · Coordinator round 13: the mesh broker move is fully staged and needs one owner decision - stop it on the frozen box or leave the single point of failure

Coordinator round 13, 2026-10-11 ~21:36Z. Objective 4's structural piece, converted into a decision.

THE FINDING, MEASURED
`000-SYSTEMS-REGISTER.md` says the placement broker runs on the authority. It does not:
- office (FROZEN) box: `mesh-broker.service` **active/enabled**, `node` pid 105841 on `127.0.0.1:3091`
- cloud authority: `mesh-broker.service` **inactive/disabled**, **nothing** on `:3091`

So the thing that makes `subagent` fan-out work runs on the box the migration froze. That is why fan-out
kept working today and why the freeze is not total.

THE MOVE IS ALREADY STAGED - nothing needs building
Everything the cloud broker needs was placed at 13:27 today:
`/opt/mesh-broker/bin/mesh-broker.mjs` (5670 B, same size as the office copy),
`/opt/mesh-broker/nodes.json` (14335 B vs the office's 15217 - **the node list has been adapted**),
`PROVENANCE.md`, `bin/`, `deploy/`, `lib/`, and node v20.20.2. Its unit has already been rewritten for
that box (`WorkingDirectory=/opt/mesh-broker`, `Environment=HOME=/root`, with a comment recording the
office original). **`:3091` on the cloud is free.** So starting it is one command:
`systemctl enable --now mesh-broker` on the cloud, then verify with `ss -ltnp | grep 3091` and a real
test dispatch.

THE ONE DECISION, AND IT IS THE OWNER'S
The office broker must then be stopped, or two brokers serve one mesh (a double-dispatch hazard).
Stopping it is **a state change on the frozen host**, which Rule 1 forbids and which
`scripts/mesh/guard-service-state.sh` now refuses by name. So it is not mine to do. Written up as
`/root/out/migration-review/154-MESH-BROKER-MOVE.md` with the two options and a recommendation
(**stop and disable it** - the freeze is meant to make that box inert, and a live broker contradicts it;
leaving it means the estate's fan-out has a single point of failure on the one machine the migration
deliberately stopped maintaining).

WHAT I CHANGED: NOTHING. All of the above is `systemctl cat`, `ss`, `ls` and `curl` on both boxes. No
unit started, stopped, enabled, disabled, unmasked or restarted.

ALSO THIS ROUND: `subagent-18` dispatched on the five red jobs, with the write-as-you-go rule as its first
hard requirement (a previous child did the work, planned to report at the end, and the transport wedged).
