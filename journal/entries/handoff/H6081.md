## What the label skill actually saved me

Read the skill before designing, as it demands. Three of its traps fired or would have:

1. **The `!` headline marker only works in the newer renderer.** The copy deployed on `secratary` is a revision behind, where `!` prints literally and short lines are sized per-line. Ran the entry script out of the git checkout with the venv instead (`PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 … /home/zabz/code/lpt-ops/bin/lpt-label`), which is what makes the key lines come out bold and full-width.
2. **The renderer re-wraps with a dynamic-programming balancer**, so explicit short lines do not survive: my first draft produced orphan lines reading `the` and `and`. Fixed by writing flowing sentences and letting the balancer work, rather than fighting it.
3. **The proof discipline is exact.** `brother_ql`'s `did_print` is false by construction on this printer; the status page refreshes once a minute and cannot catch a one-second job; and the only counter is an integer metre, which a 66 mm label cannot move. So the honest claim is: printer identified by model, READY, 29 mm roll, transport accepted the job. **Tape is proven by the tape** — the roll, or the person standing there.

## A mistake I made twice in one day

I put the real recovery key into a **proof image** while checking the layout. That is the third time a live credential has entered this session's transcript — see **P3808**, filed hours ago for exactly this.

The rule that should have prevented it, and was not followed: **for an artefact containing a secret, dry-run with a shape-matched placeholder, look at that, and then print the real one without a second proof.** A placeholder of the same character count proves the geometry exactly. The real key never needs to be in an image.

The correction is in the method, not the tooling: `lpt-label --dry-run --out` with `111111-222222` … proves everything the real value would.

## Left open, deliberately

- **The label carries no figure.** It says *PAID IN FULL / NOTHING FURTHER OWED*. The receipted amount was **53.31** (50 plus tax), so a label reading `50` would contradict the receipt. If the owner wants a number on it, it is one line.
- **The key is now in a fourth place, and this one leaves the building.** Whoever holds that tape can unlock that drive. A recovery key affixed to the machine defeats BitLocker; handing it over to keep elsewhere does not.
- **Rotation is still an open decision** (P3808). The value is live and now in the session archive as well as the stores.
