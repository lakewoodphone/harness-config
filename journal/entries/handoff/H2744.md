## 2026-09-25 15:52 UTC · ZABZ-YOGA · USABILITY AUDIT: a money bug (customer A's card can be committed against customer B), 1,060 words of prose of which ~700 prevent nothing, and the customer-facing script is the part that is hidden

**A USABILITY AUDIT FOUND A REAL MONEY BUG, and it is the kind that would have shipped invisibly.**

**`onCustomerSearchSubmit` (`frontend/src/features/admin/pages/WazeCounterSalePage.tsx:375-389`) clears the chosen customer but leaves `paymentMethodId` / `setupIntentId` untouched.** So the sequence below is possible today, and nothing stops it:
1. Choose customer A.
2. Take A's card (a real `pm_...` is now on file).
3. Search for customer B - **the customer selection is cleared, the card is not.**
4. Choose B.
5. Commit.

**Result: customer A's card is attached to customer B's sale.** `canCommit` never tests that the customer and the card belong to the same attempt (`steps.ts:402-419`), and the screen shows `Card on file: pm_...` with no warning. **A wrong person is billed for another person's device, which is the single worst outcome this screen exists to prevent** - and it is reachable by a completely ordinary mistake: searching for the customer again after taking the card. This must be fixed regardless of any redesign, and it is the first thing to fix.

**THE HEADLINE, AND IT IS A MEASUREMENT:** the counter-sale screen renders **~1,060 words of visible prose**, and roughly **700 of them re-describe a bad outcome the CODE ALREADY PREVENTS**. The sentence *"starting a card capture is not a card on file"* is written **five separate times** (`WazeCounterSaleCaptureForm.tsx:237`, `:373`, `steps.ts:286`, `:158`, `WazeCounterSaleCardForm.tsx:352`). The unverified-link warning appears **five times** (`saleStatus.ts:236`, `:239`, `format.ts:228`, `WazeCounterSaleResultPanel.tsx:292`, `steps.ts:245`). **And the ten sentences staff must SAY ALOUD TO THE CUSTOMER are folded behind a disclosure.** The prose budget is spent explaining ourselves and the customer-facing script is hidden. Exactly inverted.

**Two more findings:**
- **The card is identified to the operator by a Stripe object id**: `steps.ts:158` renders *"`pm_1Nx...` - this card is on file; the server returns no brand or last 4 for it"*. The comment at `steps.ts:136-149` shows the team hit "card ending unknown" and wrote a sentence about the API contract instead of collecting the brand/last4 **that already exist in the browser**. A staff member cannot verify a `pm_...` against a customer's card.
- **"Plan" is not a step a person does**: `planDone = state.plan !== null` (`steps.ts:185`) where `plan` is a compile-time constant (`WazeCounterSalePage.tsx:292`). So the rail shows **Plan green on load** and `completedStepCount` reports **`2 of 5 done` before anyone has done anything.** Four steps, plan folded into Commit.

**THE BEST THING ON ANY OF THE FOUR SCREENS, and the pattern the counter should copy:** the provisioning order card leads with **`Next up: step 2 - Assign the fleet number (DRN)`** (`WazeProvisioningOrderCard.tsx:114-129`). One line, no jargon, tells the person what to do next. That is the whole design language we want, and it already exists in our own codebase.

**The audit listed 13 load-bearing strings by file:line (§9) so the honesty is not deleted along with the noise** - which is the risk of a simplification pass, and the reason that section exists. It also marked every layout/contrast claim `needs verification` rather than guessing, having deliberately worked without `node_modules`.

**Still to come:** `subagent-36` (external options research) and `subagent-37` (information architecture). **No code has been touched - the owner asked for options before a rewrite, and that is being honoured.**
