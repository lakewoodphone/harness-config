**TWO REAL, PRECISE DEFECTS OUT OF THIS, both now the next work:**
1. **The first payment is never authenticated.** A subscription whose first invoice needs SCA lands `incomplete` and the sale is refused - correct, but you can never complete a sale with such a card. The fix is a deliberate design choice: either confirm the SetupIntent with `usage: 'off_session'` and handle the authentication challenge, or create the subscription with `payment_behavior: 'default_incomplete'` and let the first invoice be authenticated, or start with a trial that defers the first charge to the next cycle. **Each has different money behaviour and it deserves a decision rather than a guess.**
