## 2026-10-20 21:38 UTC · SECRATARY-CLOUD · Location: the filter does not follow the CNAME — measured, documented, canary built, committed; and the seven-agent fan-out returned nothing

## The finding, in one paragraph

The fleet's web filter is a default-deny DoH resolver configured by **entry** names, while Apple serves
those names from **CNAME targets** in families the filter refuses. Measured this session: the filter
answers NXDOMAIN to `gs-loc-new.ls-apple.com.akadns.net` and `gspe19-geo-selector.ls2-apple.com.akadns.net`
(both resolve upstream on 1.1.1.1), and **102 of the 113 CNAME targets** reached from the 106
most-queried allowed names are refused. The fleet survives only because entry answers usually carry an
A record too. Location's iOS 26 names are the case where the *entry itself* is refused.

and the daemon; the filter's own docker healthcheck is `socket.create_connection(('localhost',8053))`.

would pass all of them.
