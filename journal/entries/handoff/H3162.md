## 2026-10-05 04:33 UTC · ZABZ-TECH · 2026-10-05 disk emergency on secratary: 6.4GB free at 99 percent before the 06:00 backup - found, fixed, verified; offsite gap remains

THE DISK WAS ABOUT TO TAKE THE COMPANY DOWN. Found 2026-10-05 04:35Z, fixed same session.

WHAT WAS ACTUALLY WRONG
 secratary's / was at 6.4 GB free of 478 GB (99%), and the next six-hourly backup
 (06:00Z) needed 20.9 GB. It would have failed. prune-backups.sh's own header records
 what follows a full disk here: "with the disk full, the backup step failed ... retention
 ran -> disk stayed 100% full -> SQLite SIGBUS core-dumps". On 2026-09-30 the same
 condition was measured at 7.6 GB free with 298 GB of snapshots. Tonight was worse.

WHY
 prune-backups.sh keeps a GFS TIER LIST (all under 1 day; 1/day for 1-7 days; 1/week for
 7-30 days). That is a POLICY, NOT A BUDGET. All 15 snapshots were inside the window, so
 prune removed NOTHING across five runs while free space fell
 71 -> 34 -> 23 -> 15 -> 6.4 GB. 15 snapshots at ~21 GB = 253 GB on a disk that also
 holds the 17.2 GB DB and ~180 GB of everything else.

I WAS WRONG FIRST, AND CHECKED BEFORE SAYING IT
 My first read was "there are no backups". FALSE. The newest snapshot really does contain
 sqlite/data/secretary.db at 16,708,575,232 bytes, written 00:08 today, six-hourly
 cadence. What was missing was SPACE, not a backup. This is the second time this session
 that the alarming hypothesis was refuted by the measurement (the first was the phone
 line). Measure first; the dramatic conclusion is usually the wrong one.

WHAT I DID (owner approved the deletion in-session)
 Pre-flight resolved EVERY path against an exact allow-list and aborted on any mismatch,
 symlink or unexpected name. Deleted exactly four things:
 quarantine-debris/secretary.db.pre-queue-fix-20260928 12.4 GB (pre-change copy, done)
 secretary-backups/20260906T180001Z 6.4 GB
 secretary-backups/20260913T180001Z 6.8 GB
 secretary-backups/20260920T180001Z 13.8 GB
 Then fixed the policy: PRUNE_MAX_TOTAL_KB, default 205 GiB, a SIZE budget not a count
 (each snapshot grows with the DB), newest snapshot never dropped. Committed d5397630 in
 personal-secretary-mvp. Dry-run verified, then applied.

RESULT, MEASURED AFTER
 free space 6.4 GB -> 76.7 GB
 disk used 99% -> 84%
 snapshots kept 15 -> 10
 backup store 253 GB -> 196 GB
 The 05:20Z cron runs the same script, so the budget is self-applying.

THE BIGGER RISK THIS DID NOT FIX
 Every backup is on the SAME DISK as the database. The offsite monitor already running
 covers waze-mdm-01 and lpt-apps-01 - two OTHER boxes - and does not cover the authority
 database at all. Losing the machine loses the DB and all ten copies together.
 Raised as owner_decision_queue 272 (offsite copy), recommending Cloudflare R2 because
 the business already has the account, a bucket in use and an approved token, so no new
 vendor and no new card. Row 242 was resolved as SUPERSEDED - its premise was wrong and
 the immediate emergency is fixed.
