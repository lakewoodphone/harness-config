## 2026-09-14 22:50 UTC · ZABZ-YOGA · Only 8 of 65 enrolled devices have EVER replied: the DRN fleet is largely not attached to the MDM

The MDM fleet is mostly not attached to anything. Measured 2026-09-14 on the authority
(waze-mdm Postgres, `enrollments` / `commands` / `command_results` / `fleet_devices`).

THE NUMBERS
- 65 enrollment records exist. All 65 are `enabled`, all have a push token, all share
 one topic.
- **Only 8 devices have EVER replied to a command.** 57 never have.
- Of the 57 that never replied, **48 are marked `deployed`** (47 lakewood + 1 monsey); the
 other 9 are `retired`.
- The whole real fleet, by replies: DRN 26 (3,380), DRN 13 (2,701), LPT 2001 (114),
 LPT 2002 (91), DRN 19 (7), DRN 32 (6), DRN 3 (6), DRN 45 (2).
- Replies by day stop dead after **2026-09-11 16:00** (the day before: 132). Nothing since.
- The "24 commands/day" that looks like fleet activity is ONE hourly `Settings` sweep to
 ONE device (DRN 26, `Settings-PersonalHotspot-off-sweep`), confirmed in NanoMDM's own
 log (`id_count=1 id_first=00008150-000D04DA0133401C`).
- NanoMDM shows no push errors; it enqueues fine. The devices simply never answer.

WHAT IT MEANS
- `last_seen_at` for the 57 is essentially their enrolment moment: they were enrolled and
 never heard from again. So the dashboard's "53 devices silent" was TRUE but read as
 "phones we manage went quiet", when the truth is that most were never reachable at all.
- Consequence for the product: a profile push, a lockdown or a Lost Mode command to any of
 those 48 does NOTHING. Any belief that the DRN fleet is managed, locked or profiled is
 unfounded for 48 of 63 devices.
- The genuinely actionable signal is much smaller and sharper: five DRN devices that WERE
 reporting and stopped, and one of them (DRN 26) stopped at 2026-09-11 16:00 — a recent,
 real outage on the only actively-managed phone.
- Two different questions: NEVER REPORTED is a setup question (powered? on Wi-Fi? did
 enrolment complete?), WENT QUIET is an outage. They need different people.

WHAT WAS BUILT FROM IT
The fleet-api now reports `ever_replied` per device (an EXISTS over command_results),
threaded to the tRPC layer and the UI. The DRN page shows two separate banners — a red
outage count and an amber setup count — and rows read "Never reported" instead of an age,
because an age implies the phone reported once.

OPEN, FOR THE OWNER: whether those 48 are supposed to be in service. If DRN believes those
phones are managed, they are not, and nobody has been told. That is a customer conversation,
not an engineering one, so it is his.
