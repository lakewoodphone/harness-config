## 2026-09-23 03:29 UTC · ZABZ-YOGA · Re-scoped to the counter-sale model: two workstreams building the sale that starts a metered Stripe subscription, plus arrears tracking

**CHANGED - re-scoped after the owner's correction (D2330) and fanned out two workstreams on the CORRECT model.**

**In flight, both based on `origin/test` @ `d575c6425`:**
- `subagent-90` -> branch `waze/counter-sale`, worktree `_fleet/waze-billing`. **The core:** a Stripe Billing Meter + two Prices (base $9.00/mo, metered $18.00/GB) created idempotently by a script, plus the **counter sale** service and staff router: record the sale, capture a card for OFF-SESSION charging (Stripe Terminal exists as `terminal.service.ts`, SetupIntent as the fallback), create the **real Stripe Billing subscription**, and **link the device serial to it** - that last step is the one that matters, because the metering engine matches on `Device.serialNumber` and a sale without the link bills nobody.
- `subagent-91` -> branch `waze/counter-sale-ui`, worktree `_fleet/waze-store`. The staff screen that drives that sale with the state of every step, plus a **billing/arrears view**: who owes what, the current period, and **failed charges distinguished from unpaid-because-no-usage**.

**Both briefs carry the correction explicitly** so neither rebuilds the storefront: the device is sold at the counter, there is no online purchase, the page at `/kosher-waze` is informational only and its checkout is permanently disabled.

**The honest state of the "fully built" requirement:** the billing half is largely done and verified (metered engine, usage ledger, scheduler - 50 + 29 tests reproduced). What was missing is the **sale that creates the subscription and attaches the card**, plus the **view of arrears and failed charges**. Those are what the two agents are building.

**THE BLOCKER ON REAL BILLING IS NOT CODE.** A Stripe **Billing Meter** and two **Prices** must exist in the account, and `STRIPE_WAZE_METER_EVENT_NAME` + `WAZE_PLAN_METERED_PRICE_ID` must be set. Those are account objects and creating them needs Stripe access. **Nothing bills until they exist** - which is exactly why the owner said not to sell anything yet. The setup script is being written to be idempotent and safe to run twice, and to describe rather than create if no test key is present.
