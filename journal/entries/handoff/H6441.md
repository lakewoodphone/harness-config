## 2026-10-16 03:58 UTC · ZABZ-YOGA · Plans + add-on data design: Basic 9/250MB, Plus 17/1GB gated at 5GB account volume, add-ons +250MB/6.00 and +1GB/17.00 - an add-on is a carrier limit raise (auto-transition, never a re-enable), the market band is 2-10/GB vs our 12.80-19.50 cost, and the no-top-up policy in four code comments is superseded - committed waze-fleet 5829388

## The decision this records

Owner, 2026-10-16: **sell fixed plans plus add-on data bought mid-month; drop metered overage.** His words:
*"it could be you are right that people don't like metered usage, we'll just sell plans and addon data and
thats it i think … analyze this well and research it and come to the best options and ways to addon in
middle of a month or change for just one month."*

## The design (full detail: `15-business/plans-and-addons-design-2026-10-16.md`, commit `5829388`)

- **Plans:** Basic 9.00/250 MB (cost 6.88 in the account's ≤2 GB tier → +2.12; +3.87 above 5 GB). Plus
 17.00/1 GB (loses up to 4.97 until the account passes **5 GB/month**, so it is gated on that).
- **Add-ons, one-time, this cycle only:** +250 MB for 6.00, and **+1 GB for 17.00 — priced identically to
 the Plus plan**, so "for just one month" needs no subscription schedule and nothing for the customer to
 remember.
- **The mechanic that makes it safe:** a SIM stopped at its data limit is recovered by **raising the
 limit** — Telnyx's `sim-lifecycle` says `data_limit_exceeded` auto-transitions back, and only
 `unauthorized_imei` needs an `enable` call. `enable`/`disable` is exactly what hangs on this account
 (DRN 2001, stuck since 13 Oct). So an add-on is a settings write, and the existing failure mode is
 designed out.
- **Do not use the group data_limit** — it is a *shared pool*; one heavy user would eat every customer's
 headroom. Per-SIM `data_limit` is an override (set, not add).
- **Two clocks:** the carrier resets the cap on the calendar month, Stripe renews on the sale anniversary.
 Recommendation: anchor the subscription to the 1st, prorate the first partial month.
- **Stripe:** one licensed price per plan, swapped **on the existing subscription item** — omitting
 `items[0][id]` *adds a second item and bills the customer for both plans* (their documented trap). An
 add-on is a subscription-pinned `invoiceitem` (not prorated, lands on the next invoice). Upgrade =
 immediate + prorated; downgrade = next cycle. Stripe now routes usage billing to Metronome, so the
 fixed-plan model is the direction of travel.

## The correction to my own earlier advice (keep this)

I proposed add-ons at 24–30/GB. **The market sells mid-month add-ons at 2.00–10.00/GB** (1 GB clusters at
5.00–10.00), and one carrier page names a 10.00/GB 1 GB add-on as reading like a penalty. **Our marginal
cost is 12.80/GB at best and 19.50/GB in the tier we will actually be in** — so a market-priced add-on
loses money on every purchase. Consequences: the add-on must be **small and framed as a rescue**, the
**plan must be the instrument for more data**, and **only the Telnyx volume quote unlocks a cheaper
add-on**. I also corrected the reverse of an earlier call: plan upgrades should be **immediate and
prorated**, not cycle-boundary.

## What this reverses (recorded, not overwritten)

The 2026-09-11 policy *"no prepaid top-up … do not build a buy more data flow"* lives in **four code
comments** (`waze-device.service.ts:26`, `WazeDevice.tsx:19`, `WazeUsageHistory.tsx:33`,
`waze-billing.service.ts:16`) and in `PRICING-AUTHORITY` row 5 (*"no top-up — APPROVED"*). **No test
asserts it** — which is why it survived as a habit rather than a decision. All five are superseded.

## Build gap (read-only audit of `phone-and-tech-full`)

**The add-on purchase, the plan change, the proration path and any data bucket do not exist.** The cap
authority is the SIM's `data_limit_gb` (`customerPortal.ts:1053` → `waze-device.service.ts:1230`) and **our
code never stops data — the carrier does**. The metering engine is load-bearing for the portal's usage
history, the admin Money page and arrears, so it should **stop reporting and keep its history**, not be
deleted. The readiness gate *requires* the metered price and seven test files pin the old model.

## Worth, and what is next

At 50 devices priced this way: 40 on Basic (150 MB) + 10 on Plus (700 MB) = 12.70 GB of account volume,
cost 297.65, charged 530.00, **profit 232.35/month (43.8%)**; if all 50 took Plus and maxed it, **+74.85**
— still positive. Nothing is live; the counter-sale flag and the deploy runner are still the gates.

Next, in order: fix the two clocks; rewrite the five places carrying the no-top-up policy; launch Basic +
the 250 MB add-on; build the add-on path (read the account's Stripe **billing mode** first — it decides the
invoice route); turn on Plus at the 5 GB gate; wire the 80% notification into the counter script and the
storefront copy. **Test a limit raise on one stock SIM before anything is built on the auto-transition** —
the docs assert it, the docs are silent on hangs, and no test exists.
