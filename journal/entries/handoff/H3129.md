**The audit immediately caught a bug in itself, which is the point of running it.** Its
first version matched the owner's number with `LIKE '%84897895%'` and returned **zero**
rows on this host even though `from_number = '+18483897895'` returns 252 — LIKE with a
leading wildcard is not dependable here. A check that silently matches nothing reports
"no data", which reads exactly like "no problem". It now matches by exact value against
every form the stores use. **Do not build a check on LIKE in this SQLite build.**

**What D1 measures, precisely (this is the actionable finding).** The app holds 252
owner inbound rows; **225 of them are older than the 7-day window**
`~/bin/mirror-owner-sms.py --days` will carry, and **0 of them lack a `twilio_sid`** —
