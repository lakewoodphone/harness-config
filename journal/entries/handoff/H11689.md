## The SIM finding, at its real strength

Bachrach: unlocking 36 made its service come back with no carrier action; 25, never unlocked, still
fails. Recorded as an anecdote with the confounders spelled out — the big one being that removing an MDM
profile forces iOS to rebuild its network stack, which on an eSIM re-registers with the carrier and can
clear a stuck "SIM failure" by itself. The reversal test he is now running is the only thing that can
separate the two.

Both phones are **iPhone 14 US models — eSIM only, no tray**. A SIM failure there is an activation
exchange, not a dirty contact, so it needs working data and DNS. That makes the DNS profile a plausible
accomplice: `AllowFailover=false` over a resolver that refuses a third of all queries is a single point
of failure for any network operation. But the filter's 24 h log contains **no carrier or activation
hostname at all**, so the mechanism is unproven and is recorded that way.
