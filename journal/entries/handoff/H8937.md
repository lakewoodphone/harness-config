**WHY IT IS PHYSICAL, NOT CONFIG**

- The device's own Zigbee2MQTT cached payload (`/config/zigbee2mqtt/state.json`) says `"contact": false` — open. That file is fresh: temperature 15.74 °C matches HA's 60.3 °F, the axes match HA's, battery 66 % and linkquality 112. The vendor definition is unambiguous: contact `true` = closed, `false` = open (zigbee2mqtt.io/devices/IM6001-MPP01.html). HA's discovery mapping `payload_on: false` / `payload_off: true` is therefore correct.
- Two identical SmartThings sensors on the two control-room doors use the same mapping and are healthy: 99 on / 101 off transitions (external) and 72 on / 70 off (interior) in the retained 30 days. The outside sensor has 6 `on`, 10 `unavailable`, 6 `unknown` and **zero `off`** — every row is a restart sequence.
- The radio and the rest of the device are alive: 1,028 x-axis samples in 7 days with a range of -2047..+841 mg, 250 `moving` events, battery 66 %, linkquality 112. The door is being used; only the reed circuit never changes.
- The HA template `phoenix_outside_door_contact` (`configuration.yaml:358-378`) is a plain `is_state(...,'on')` with an availability guard. Nothing in HA or Zigbee2MQTT is inverting or dropping this contact.
