**F3 — 74 live orders carried a false payment verdict.** An automated pass at 12:14:14-12:14:22 on
2026-10-14 wrote 254 payment notes in eight seconds and reported 78 card claims as
`COULD NOT BE RECONCILED`. It searched Stripe for the **untaxed** figure; this shop charges card
payments **with 6.625 % NJ tax**, so a job quoted at 290 is charged 309.21 and a search for 290.00
cannot find it. **74 of the 78 were false negatives.** A false negative is worse than no answer,
because "no charge found" reads as "the customer did not pay".

shop charged 10663; Python's `round()` gives 10662), and a match on amount and timestamp is a
**candidate, not a receipt** — every correction note says so in those words and creates no
`payment_transactions` row.
