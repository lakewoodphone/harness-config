He asked whether the night loop is doing its work, whether it is heading somewhere, and for a full
audit that becomes **the roadmap the autonomous system always follows** — front end first, then
logic, then UI/UX — then PRs that start implementing it.

Delivered: four audit streams (2,000+ measured lines, 96 committed screenshots), the roadmap rebuilt

### The five findings that matter
1. **The loop is working.** 109 lpt-website releases in 7.04 days (15.5/day, mean 663 s, 102 exit 0),
 85 ledger items closed. Its defect is delivery, not effort: 138 unmerged remote branches (71
 older than a week), 6 PRs older than a day, 2 ledger items still open after their PRs merged, and
 7 roadmap boxes whose ledger item was `done`.
2. **The public site has one indexable URL.** Every route serves the same 8,422-byte document with
 one title and `rel=canonical` `/`. The browser fixes it at runtime for 9 pages
 (`utils/publicPageSeo.ts`); `/kosher-waze` — the only page with a real price list — never does and
 canonicals to the homepage. 4 of 4 unknown paths answer **200**. Sitemap lists 4 where 13
 indexable routes exist.
3. **The gate that certifies everything could exit 0 on a red suite.**
 `frontend/src/global-setup.ci.ts:47` called `process.exit(0)` unconditionally after teardown and
 could overwrite the reporter's exit 1. Fixed in #512, proved red then green.
4. **The staff audit refuted five claims the old roadmap carried**, all 24 h stale (search ranking,
 merged slices, fake empty states, pickup tile, `0.00` money panel). The two real HIGH defects it
 replaced them with: the Advanced search tab returned 0 rows for every query, and a ticket-number
 search listed the row while all four counter tiles counted 0. Both fixed in #510.
5. **THE PIPELINE IS DOWN FOR BILLING, AND THAT IS THE BIGGEST FACT OF THE NIGHT.** From 03:31Z
 GitHub Actions started **no job at all**: every annotation reads *"The job was not started because
 recent account payments have failed or your spending limit needs to be increased"*; the `Deploy
 environment` workflow on `test` fails identically, so a merge would not deploy either. Reproduced
 on a re-run at 03:41Z while the same workflows had succeeded at 03:26Z. Owner queue **#861
 (critical)**. Nothing on this repo can merge until it is settled.

### Lessons worth keeping
- **L (new): an integration branch is the only instrument that tests the combination.** Seven
 branches were green individually; together they failed a page test and would have failed three CI
 shards. One merge, one run, one place to resolve.
- **L (new): a billing outage and a quiet queue look identical if you only read `conclusion`.** The
 difference was `steps: []` plus the job annotation. Read the annotation, not the colour.
- **L (new): a roadmap whose supply mapping is typed by hand is a roadmap that can be rewritten into
 silence.** The mapping is now generated from the document, with a `--check`.
