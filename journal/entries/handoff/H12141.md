goes to the tab and stops being texted" -- and dropped the other half. An asked-but-unanswered row

remove, one layer up from the message queue where it was first found.

attribution. A row that is in decision_sent, still pending, and was asked more than 48 hours
ago is dismissed with a written reason. Nothing is deleted; the row can be reopened by naming it.
