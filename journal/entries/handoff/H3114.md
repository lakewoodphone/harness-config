## WHAT WENT WRONG WHILE APPLYING IT — two real defects, both corrected

- **First version clobbered a good row.** An incoming record with no date and no
 total (`scrape_amazon_browser.py` emits `total_amount: null`) overwrote
 `D01-2770781-9853015` (2026-06-30, 5.82) with `''` and `0.0`. Restored from the
 backup, and the writer now leaves a row alone when the incoming record carries
 neither a date nor a total.
- **First version fought a hidden unique index.** The ledger has
 `UNIQUE(order_id, item_name, unit_price)` on `amazon_order_items`, which is not in
 the DDL; a delete-and-reinsert raised `IntegrityError` mid-run, after 42 business
 orders had already been written. Now **add-only**: `INSERT OR IGNORE` per item,
 existing rows are never deleted, and a count that falls is reported rather than
 hidden.

**Verify-don't-assume paid for itself twice here.** Both defects were found by
diffing before/after against a backup, not by reading the output.
