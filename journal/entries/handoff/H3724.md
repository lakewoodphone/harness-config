Attempt 1653 on ledger 861.

## What that means for the register's vocabulary
The class list is missing `dependency:` - waiting on another work item. That is a real kind of blocker in a plan where
one path's finish line depends on another's, and it is invisible today: a reader counting blockers cannot tell "waiting
for the owner" from "waiting for item 573".

It needs a checker change with a selftest case, not an end-of-session edit, so it is written into 861 as the first of
three steps: (1) add `dependency:` to `BLOCKED_CLASSES` with a selftest case, (2) file 120 (`host:`), 620
(`owner-decision:` PR #40/queue 810) and 662 (`owner-decision:` handset/queue 862), (3) add the supersede note for 204.
Then "0 unmapped" becomes a real target for `roadmap-supply.py`.

## Why stopping here is the right call and not a dodge
Two of the five were resolvable by reading and were resolved. The third was resolvable and revealed a gap in the
vocabulary that a correct filing depends on. The alternative - writing five items now with classes chosen to fit the
five that exist - would have produced a register that *looks* complete, passes `RM-CLASS`, and tells a future reader
that 319 waits on the owner or on hardware. **A checker that accepts the wrong classification is worse than no
checker**, because it converts a gap into a green tick.
