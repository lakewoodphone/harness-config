## WHY I DID NOT MERGE IT

1. **575 files across domains I was not asked to adjudicate.** Choosing which side wins per path in
 `docs/personal-insurance` (70 docs) and `app/family_chat` is not a mechanical operation, and getting
 it wrong is a silent content loss of exactly the kind this system's history warns about.
2. **The ordinary git answer is unavailable.** A rebase of 116 commits under a live service with 169
 dirty files is the destructive action to refuse. A merge needs a human in a checkout holding the
 live tool.
3. **It is not blocking anything.** This is the decisive point, and it is why I am recording a
 recommendation rather than escalating a fire.

## WHAT I DID VERIFY, so the decision is not made blind

- **No silent-loss exposure.** `git diff --name-status origin/master...HEAD | awk '$1=="D"'` is

 not delete anything.
- **The customer corpus is not at risk from this divergence.** `psm` holds **0** files under
 `docs/customer-operations` — the cases and records live in `lpt-hub`, a separate repo whose
 divergence is trivial by comparison (`0 0`). Whatever happens to `psm`'s branch, the corpus is

- **The one file that mattered is already safe three ways**, all 92,973 bytes:

## RECOMMENDATION (recorded, not executed)

Do **not** do a wholesale merge or rebase. Do it **per domain, lowest-risk first**, verifying the
safety invariants after each step:

1. `app/services/lpt_hub_sync.py` and `scripts/lpt-sync-to-production.py` — take the authority's side;
 `check_sync_tool_sanity()` proves the result in one call.
2. `docs/network` (12 docs) — small, low-risk, easy to eyeball.
3. `app/family_chat` (7) + the 20 `app/services` — code, needs a test run.
4. **`docs/personal-insurance` (70 docs) LAST** and with the owner's eyes. These are personal
 documents; a merge conflict resolved by "theirs" or "ours" in that directory is a content decision
 about insurance records, and it is exactly the kind of thing that should not be decided by a tool
 default.

**The alternative worth considering first:** leave `psm`'s `master` as it is, and treat
`origin/rescue/lpt-sync-correct-lineage-20260917` as the canonical reference for the sync tooling
until someone has time for the 54 conflicts. Nothing breaks in the meantime — which is the whole point
of having made the tool's lineage independent of the branch.
