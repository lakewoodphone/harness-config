## 2026-09-25 04:17 UTC · ZABZ-YOGA · Drove a real sale through the counter screen: found and fixed a 400 that made card capture impossible, and the chain now ends at the honest refusal - no card-entry surface for the no-reader path

**CHANGED - I TOOK A SALE THROUGH THE COUNTER SCREEN IN TEST MODE. It found a real blocking bug, I fixed and deployed it, and the chain now runs to its honest end.**

**THE BUG, found only by driving the screen (four agents, 148 tests, a typecheck and a full audit all missed it):** every **"Start card capture"** returned **HTTP 400** and the page rendered a **raw Zod error blob** as its alert text:
`[{"expected":"string","code":"invalid_type","path":["readerId"],"message":"Invalid input: expected string, received null"}]`
- **Cause:** the counter screen sends an explicit **`readerId: null`** for the no-reader (counter-device) path, and the router declared `readerId: readerIdSchema.optional()`. **`.optional()` accepts an ABSENT key but REJECTS an explicit `null`** - even though the service's own contract is `readerId?: string | null`. One modifier wrong, at the router/service boundary.
- **Fix:** `.optional()` -> **`.nullish()`**, with the reasoning written into the comment. Committed and pushed to `origin/test`; the test backend rebuilt from it.
- **Consequence before the fix: a card could never be put on file, therefore a sale could NEVER be committed.** The entire counter-sale flow was dead at step 3 of 5 while every test passed.

**WHAT I THEN DROVE, AND WHAT IT PROVED:**
1. **Customer search works.** Typed `john` -> *"1 customer matched. Nothing is chosen for you - pick the record that is actually this person."* Found **John Doe, customer #992, +1-555-1001**. Clicking chose him deliberately; the step went **Done**.
2. **Serial + DRN work.** Serial `F17TESTWAZE0001`, DRN `9001` -> *"3 of 5 steps done - next: Card on file."*
3. **`startCardCapture` now works**: *"A card capture was started (**SetupIntent seti_1UJQZaQ9X2ijIMADkvPPXzXa**) and is NOT confirmed. There is no card on file yet - the plan cannot collect until you confirm it."* **A real Stripe test-mode SetupIntent exists.**
4. **`confirmCardCapture` then REFUSED, correctly and precisely**: *"The card capture was REFUSED at the payment_method step (`payment_method_not_collected`): SetupIntent ... is "requires_payment_method" and carries no payment method; no card is on file - **Take the card again - the customer must complete the tap or the form**."* **That is the fail-closed behaviour working: it refused rather than pretending a card was on file, and it named the exact reason and the fix.**

**THE REMAINING GAP IS PRECISE AND REAL: THERE IS NO CARD-ENTRY SURFACE FOR THE COUNTER-DEVICE PATH.** The backend is complete and correct - it creates the SetupIntent, refuses honestly without a payment method, and would accept a confirmed one. But a staff member has **no way to enter a card** unless a physical Stripe Terminal reader is present. **So without a reader, a sale cannot be completed**, and that is a hardware/product decision rather than a coding gap: either the shop gets a Stripe Terminal reader, or the counter screen needs a Stripe Elements card form (the page's own copy already promises both: *"on the shop's Terminal reader, or on the counter device"* - the second half was never built).
