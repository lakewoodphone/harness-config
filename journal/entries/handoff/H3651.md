WHAT WAS LEARNED
- **`lpt-write order create` refuses any status but the create-time one while its own plan line validates against the full live enum** (L3845). Create at the default then `status set`; that is also what Zombeck did, so it is the route, not a workaround.
- **PowerShell heredoc to `ssh 'bash -s'` truncates its tail** (L3846). I chased it as a filesystem problem and re-tested a readable file five times before seeing it. base64 the whole command into one argv.
- A phone number with **no** customer row can still have a real history: 7 `communications` rows on `+17329983106` (2026-10-15 to 2026-10-18), every one `customerId` null and `orderId` null. Matching on `contactPhone` found them; `phoneNumberFrom`/`phoneNumberTo` returned 0 and would have read as "new customer".
- **P137G is a regulatory number, not a model.** Dell's own declarations of conformity attach it to the **Latitude 5420/5421** family - which carry two USB-C (TB4) ports and charge over USB-C at ~19-20 V. That makes "5 V instead of 19 V" the signature of a PD/charge-path fault and corroborates the owner's two-USB-C detail. Reasoning, not measurement; the service tag is what confirms the SKU.
