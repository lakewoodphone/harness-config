## 2026-09-25 15:55 UTC · ZABZ-YOGA · IA AUDIT: the tabs destroy an in-progress sale, the split follows our routers not the staff member's job, and the intended user cannot open the screen

**INFORMATION-ARCHITECTURE AUDIT - three structural findings, and the first two are bugs, not taste.**

1. **THE TABS UNMOUNT EACH OTHER MID-SALE.** `frontend/src/components/ui/tabs.tsx:202-204` returns `null` for the inactive tab, and the ENTIRE in-progress sale lives in that subtree - customer, serial, `setupIntentId`, `paymentMethodId`, amount (`WazeCounterSalePage.tsx:226-237`, `:250-282`). **One click on `Fleet` in the middle of a sale destroys the whole thing and can orphan a live SetupIntent.** The tab structure I introduced yesterday to tidy the sidebar created a data-loss bug, and nobody could have seen it without clicking around during a sale.
2. **THE FOUR TABS ARE THE FOUR tRPC ROUTERS, NOT FOUR STAFF INTENTIONS.** `CounterSalePage` / `ProvisioningPage` / `FleetPage` / `BillingPage` map one-to-one onto the backends we happened to build. **The object a staff member actually works on - the DEVICE - is the join key across all four and has no tab at all.** The split follows our system boundaries, not his job.
3. **THE SALE SCREEN'S INTENDED USER CANNOT OPEN IT.** Its own header says it is written for *"a staff member with a customer in front of them"*, but **all five routes are `ADMIN`-only** (`MVPRouter.tsx:514-553`) and **an EMPLOYEE's sidebar is Work Orders alone** (`navigation.ts:199-201`). The counter person who would sell a Waze phone cannot reach the screen that sells it. **This is an owner question about who sells, and it is flagged as his, not mine.**

**Its recommendation: THREE tabs by object - `Sell - Devices - Money` - with `Sell` as its own route rather than a tab body** (which also fixes finding 1, since leaving the route is then a deliberate act). Plus deletions worth doing under any split: duplicated readiness/progress renderings, "Plan agreed" as a step nobody performs, the arrears ledger living on the sale screen, and **six pieces of deployment config sitting inside the counter flow.**

**Also delivered:** a 4-surface x 9-treatment consistency table (shared notice primitive vs two hand-rolled copies; four primary-action variants; four Refresh variants; `px-3` vs `px-4`; two `thead` treatments inside Billing alone; two rails with opposite accessibility treatment); a vocabulary table **naming the winners** (`DRN` for the device number, `cap` for the ceiling); and nav leftovers including `navigation.ts:5-18` contradicting the Waze entry it sits directly above, plus a dead `WazeProvisioningQueueHeading`.
