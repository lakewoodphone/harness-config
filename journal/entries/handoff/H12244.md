## 2026-10-22 15:00 UTC · ZABZ-YOGA · Dictionaries re-download on sold phones: both network gates ruled out by measurement — resolver and PAC do not see the flow

Follows **H12227** (same investigation, that entry was written before the decisive tests). Full step log with every command and result: `personal-secretary-mvp/deploy/waze-mdm/docs/2026-10-22-dictionary-redownload-investigation.md`, commit on master `51c10f4cf` and its successor.

### The answer, arrived at the hard way
The dictionary fetch is a network fetch (airplane mode stops it) and it **never asks our resolver and does not honour our PAC**. Evidence, in order:

- Resolver allowlist cut to a **42-name skeleton refusing every Apple name**, phone **rebooted**, tap → **download completes**.
- Same cut held **ten minutes** (longer than any Apple CDN TTL), tap → **completes**.
- Served PAC with all eight non-location Apple names removed (curl-verified `0` remaining, 10 location names still in), **reboot** to force a PAC refetch, tap → **completes**.

So the 2026-10-21 location work (resolver 51 → 89 names; PAC rewritten at 00:46 to `DIRECT` the Apple CDN, moving `iphone-ld.apple.com` off a block list it had been on since `d4cfd61a34`, 2026-06-20) explains why the *behaviour changed*, and cannot be the mechanism, because closing both gates did not bring the old behaviour back.

### Why host lists cannot fix this
Both gates are lists consulted by the device's own stack: the resolver only sees names something asks to resolve; the PAC only sees URLs that go through a proxy-aware API. An iOS MobileAsset (a dictionary is one) is fetched by a system daemon that does neither. Blocking more names cannot stop a flow that never asks.

### The only MDM-deployable control left
A Network Extension content filter (`com.apple.webcontent-filter` `FilterType=Plugin`, signed extension + Apple entitlement) — already listed as a project in `KIOSK_OPTIONS.md` under "Managed URL filter app". This is a **funding decision, not an engineering one**, and it is the owner's: build/entitle the filter app, or accept that a customer can restore a dictionary and rely on the bench step.
