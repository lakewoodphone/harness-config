**State of the actual work.**

has rows for `zabz-tech-linux` (line 39, `writable`, normal host) and `linux-pc-ts` but **no MAC address

**Watcher re-armed for 24 h, and improved.** `_rearm-if-needed.sh` now mounts **both** the archive
(`/dev/disk/by-label/72029 - Transfer`) and the stick (`LPT`) **by label before it does anything else** —
after a reboot neither is mounted, and without the archive the verify has no source to compare against and
would fail for the wrong reason. That was a real gap in the first version of the recovery: it checked for
mounts and refused, which after a reboot means it would have refused forever.

**The lesson, for the second time and now with teeth:** the delivery's *evidence* lived on one machine, and
that machine is the least reliable thing in this job. The right design is to hash the medium itself against
a manifest held on the medium, so the proof travels with the stick — the shop's own doc says "look at the
MEDIUM", and what I built instead needs a second machine to be alive.

**Blocked on one physical action:** power on `zabz-tech-linux`. The watcher will then mount both devices,
re-run the verify, and write the checksums without any further instruction.
