## The measurement that changes the leading hypothesis

**The battery is healthy: DESIGN 45,000 mWh, FULL CHARGE 45,880 mWh (102%), 283 cycles, at 86%.** A machine with a healthy charged battery **does not** shut down when the barrel jack loses contact — it runs on the battery. So the August shutdowns are **not** explained by an intermittent jack; they point at the main power path failing underneath both sources.

The jack stays the best candidate for the **no-power** symptom and a weak one for the **shutdowns**. Two separate questions now, and the flex test decides only the first. **Nothing in a Windows-side capture identifies a component** — the bench-plan candidates (`PQ5400`/`PQ5401`, `PR5425`, `PR5436`/`PR5437`, pin-25 BTST, `PU5400`) are untouched by it.
