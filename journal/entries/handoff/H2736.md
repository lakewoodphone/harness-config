**THE DESIGN DECISION I MADE (this was the thing I was going to ask about - no longer):** authenticate the first payment **AT THE COUNTER, where the customer is physically present.** Concretely: create the subscription with **`payment_behavior: 'default_incomplete'`** and **`expand: ['latest_invoice.payment_intent']`**, return the first invoice's PaymentIntent `client_secret` to the screen, confirm it with Stripe.js (**this is where 3DS happens, with the customer standing there**), and only then treat the subscription as active and record the sale. Plus: create the SetupIntent with **`usage: 'off_session'`** so RENEWALS can be charged later without the customer - a card that can only be charged on-session fails on the first renewal, which is a silent revenue leak.
**Why this and not a trial:** a trial defers the problem rather than solving it and changes when the first money moves; `default_incomplete` authenticates at the only moment the customer is present. **Why not off-session authentication:** off-session charges cannot be interactively authenticated.
**The refusal path stays.** If authentication fails or is abandoned the sale must still be refused and the subscription cancelled - **that behaviour is exactly what made last night's failed run safe, and weakening it is the one thing that must not happen.**
