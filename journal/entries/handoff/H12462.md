## 2026-10-23 22:00 UTC · ZABZ-YOGA · Monthly receipts: opt-in list, idempotent ledger, detector - and the cron fired at 22:00 by itself

## 2026-10-23 22:00 UTC · ZABZ-YOGA · Monthly receipts are a mechanism now: opt-in list, idempotent ledger, and a detector that names the customer who did not get one

Owner: "we don't need all of them to get an email, she's the one who requested it, we can set it up for
customers who request, but once I tell you they requested they should be added to a list our system
has and it should be automated." That is a STANDING RULE, not one customer's fix. Receipts are opt-in.

### WHAT HE GOT
Heichal's October receipt SENT as a PICTURE: message `1a16ac02c6cf2137`, label SENT, read back -
`To mwasserlauf@heichalhatorah.org`, `multipart/related > alternative{text,html} + image/png 112,054 B`
with cid. He asked "isn't it supposed to contain a nice picture receipt?" and he was right: an HTML
receipt is not a picture of one.

### WHY IT WAS NEVER AUTOMATED (the three real causes, measured)
1. No list existed. "She requested one" lived in conversations.
2. `invoice.payment_succeeded` IS delivered to the shop backend. `webhook.service.ts` in
 phone-and-tech-full subscribes it and `handleInvoicePaymentSucceeded` is ONE log line under the
 comment "for future invoice system" - and even that line is a single-quoted string, so `${invoice.id}`
 never interpolates. A subscription to an event is not an implementation.
3. Stripe cannot send it: 6 of 8 subscriptions have `email: null` on the customer; 11 of the last 15
 paid invoices had no address. This is the whole book, not one customer - Heichal is just the one who
 complained.

### THE MECHANISM
`lpt-hub/tools/receipts/lpt_receipts.py` + `lpt_receipt_mail.py`, runtime `/root/receipts/`, cron on the
authority via `cron-wrap.sh`:
- `lpt-receipts-run` every 15 min - paid SUBSCRIPTION invoices not in `receipt_log` are rendered and
 emailed, then read back and verified.
- `lpt-receipts-status --flag` hourly - detector: missing / failed / stuck-pending / runner older than
 26 h -> names the invoice, exit 3, raises a wake flag.
- `receipt_log.invoice_id` is a PRIMARY KEY: a duplicate receipt is structurally impossible. A `failed`
 row with NO message id is retried; a row WITH one is never re-sent.
- `receipt_optin` in `/root/authority.db` is the list. Adding a customer is one command.
- September and October backfilled, so the next thing it sends her is 2026-11-22, unattended.

### THREE DEFECTS ONLY TESTING FOUND
1. `gmail_tokens_dir` is RELATIVE ("data/gmail_tokens"). A job run from `/root/receipts` reported
 "Gmail account ... not authenticated" - the L12414 false negative, in production, from the cron
 directory. Fixed by resolving the account with cwd forced to the app root.
2. A failed receipt was skipped forever -> a transient Gmail error meant NO receipt, not a retry.
3. The picture printed a tofu box for U+2713 (no font on this host had it; installed
 `fonts-dejavu-core`) and the item line read `6 x Monthly Service (at $11.00 / month)` - Stripe's API
 talking to a customer. Both fixed, re-rendered, looked at.

### PROOF, IN THE ORDER IT WAS OBTAINED
- Planted the failure (deleted the October ledger row): detector named it and exited 3.
- Dry run with real rendering: picture 152,470 B, eml 209,239 B.
- Real send branch, end to end, against the SHOP'S OWN INBOX (`1a16ac4ce4b9004e`) so no customer got a
 test; verified the part tree. Then restored the real row and address.
- Wrapper invoked by hand: `lpt-receipts-run 0` in `~/.cron-status/runs.tsv`.
- **The unattended run fired at 22:00:01Z, rc=0**, and correctly did nothing because both invoices are
 already receipted.

### THE RULE I WANT TO KEEP
An "automatic" thing that has never executed is a sentence, not a mechanism. The test that matters is
the one that plants the failure and watches the detector name it - not the one that proves the happy
path. Same shape as the ledger reaper that printed count: 0 all evening and had never freed anything.

### OPEN
- `lpt-hub` on ZABZ-YOGA is 581 behind / 17 ahead of origin/main; the receipt code is committed locally
 and NOT PUSHED. `deploy.sh` pulls the shared renderer from origin/main to work around it.
- The other 7 subscriptions get nothing, by instruction.
- The callback she asked for on 2026-10-07 is still owed; a receipt does not replace it.
