## Addendum 6 to H9513 (rounds 6-7) — a guard that deletes, and an instrument that flattered

### The defect I introduced, and how it was found

The internal-prose guard went live and correctly refused bodies written as agent instructions. The
next day I counted the cost per class: **of 34 distinct reminder-class texts ever delivered, 9 would
now be refused** — the Weinberg cash settlement, the Fisher SD-card search, the Weitman bench job,
the Yaakov refund. A refused reminder is a reminder he never receives, and unlike a cron duplicate
it is never resent.

Fixed with `_rescue_reminder()` in `owner_text_relay.py`, running before the guards, for
owner-directed reminder-class bodies only. It strips the scaffolding (paths, branch names, `.md`,
work-order and task ids, "Record … in", "append … to", "Do NOT", "not approved", "production note"),
caps at a sentence boundary, and sends the readable remainder. If nothing readable survives, or the
rewrite still reads as internal prose, the ORIGINAL body goes to the guard so the loss is deliberate
and recorded in the attempt row.

**Measured through the gate: 9 rewritten and kept, 1 still refused** — and the one refused is
`"Reminder: VS Code session: present ONE numbered owner-approval ask"`, an instruction addressed to
a coding session. Suppressing that is correct.

### The instrument flattered, twice

`reminders-at-risk.py` first called `internal_prose()` on the raw body and reported 9 lost when the
gate refuses none. Then `owner-text-scorecard.conformance()` — the number that decides whether this
whole overhaul worked — had the same flaw, charging the channel for reminders the gate rewrites:

| | before the fix | after |
|---|---|---|
| refused | 18 of 77 (23.4%) | **11 of 77 (14.3%)** |
| rewritten and sent | not shown | **7** |

The census now prints both halves, because a census that shows only refusals cannot tell a rewritten
reminder from a deleted one. **A probe that measures around the gate reports the defect it was
written to find** — twice in two days is a pattern, and it is the lesson of record (L9790).

### The next concrete item

The **digest composer** is the last body class that is still *written* badly rather than *refused*
well. `"10-13 14:21 UTC 36 items, 20 urgent\n- …full: data/owner-attention/latest.txt"` is exactly

time"* — and the guard now stops it, which means he currently gets **no** daily attention summary at

muted. `app/services/owner_attention_sweep.py` and `/opt/company/scripts/owner_attention.py` are the
files; the 2026-10-15 audit records that a concurrent session reverted a previous fix there, so the
