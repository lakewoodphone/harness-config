`lpt ticket 9177572354` and `lpt customer 9177572354` returned **nothing in both production

customer 1318, +17329306285, 2026-04-21. A third, unrelated Goldstein.

**Cause, measured:** the **2026-10-03 link backfill matched on the SURNAME ALONE** and wrote that

**Fixed and pushed: `3de0e202d` on lpt-hub main.** Both records annotated rather than erased, per
the house pattern set on `surface-laptop-4-1950-greenfeld` on 2026-10-12:

is now `false`, the copied `workflow.ticketState: closed` / `workOrderState: completed` are reset

verifier reads the false link as true. Case files carry a dated 2026-10-13 entry.
