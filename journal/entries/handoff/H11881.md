## 2026-10-21 00:03 UTC · ZABZ-YOGA · Text channel audit 2026-10-21: transport healthy, judgement broken - nine root causes, seven fixed, one decision is the owner's

Session 2026-10-20 ~23:35Z to 2026-10-21 00:05Z, ZABZ-YOGA, engine restarted twice mid-session. Owner asked whether the text system still works and is still evolving, why the CFO stopped texting, and for a full audit + 20 rounds of optimisation.

## THE ANSWER IN ONE PARAGRAPH

The transport is healthy; the JUDGEMENT was broken. He received 4 texts today from the AI line and replied twice. What stopped is AUTONOMOUS delivery: 38.9 h with nothing but direct replies, 13 refused messages, and a CFO whose question engine (a) has almost nothing left to ask because the books are 99.86% clear, and (b) is structurally unable to ask at all after 06:30 UTC. Underneath both: three independent producers and no deliverer, and a policy that reported "held" forever while every monitor wrote green.

## MEASURED ROOT CAUSES (all verified on secratary-cloud)

1. QUEUE GATE. settings_overrides.owner_sms_min_urgency="urgent" since 2026-05-04, and app/config.py:1082 has the same default, so removing the override changes nothing. Escalation exists only for _OWNER_ESCALATION_REASONS=("ask_owner","reminder:"), so urgent_email (6 rows), sync_breaker (2), freshness_watchdog (1) and card_due-via-"unspecified:" (1) had NO path to the phone at any age. The refusal is written upstream of owner_text.gate() (app/database.py:10908 at enqueue, autopilot.py:11876 in flush) and writes no sms_log row and no owner_text_decision row, which is why text-health.py's 20 checks read 0 attempts for a row refused 20 times.
2. BACKOFF STOPS ESCALATION. The flush selects held rows with due_only=True, i.e. next_attempt_at <= now. Every refusal pushes next_attempt_at further into the future, so a row that has been refused enough times to earn an escalation can never be selected to receive one. This is the defect behind "escalation is decorative".
3. CFO ENGINE STOPS AT 06:30. cfo-shift-cron.sh MAX_PER_DAY=14 on */30 means all 14 slots are consumed by 06:30Z and 17.5 h is dark. Its "daily ceiling reached" line goes to /root/.cfo/shift-cron.log (via exec >>), NOT /var/log/cfo-shift.log, so the documented log has been 0 bytes since Oct 13 and an operator reads silence.
4. NO DELIVERER FOR THE DECISION QUEUE. 114 owner questions pending in owner_decision_queue, oldest 820 h (34 days). owner-ask.py is the only program that turns such a row into a text and it is in no crontab and no systemd unit - it has never run on a schedule.
5. CFO QUESTION QUALITY. All 8 audited bad asks had an answer already on the box: statement descriptors ("Fuel", "Pass", "API CREDIT"), an already-categorised twin, or his own texts of 10-07/10-08. The gate (ask_quality.review) checks amount/card/date, never "can we answer this ourselves", and its ASK_RE does not match "say what it was", so three asks went out in three hours with no question mark and the date rule skipped.
6. LOST ANSWERS. ask 44 was answered by the owner 35 minutes after it was sent; nothing on a clock calls the reply matcher (cfo_clarify.answer_from_reply), so the answer was never recorded and the clarification sat "asked" for five days. The re-ask is deadlocked by open_cluster_keys() returning every state='asked' row with no age filter, so a sent question suppresses its own retry forever.
7. CUSTOMER TEXT HIDDEN AS A TEST. wake row 1257 (sms-desk:8482457112:2026-10-19) had kind='selftest' because sms-desk-watch.py set that for ANY subject passed to --selftest; wake.py excludes kind='selftest' from every count, so a real customer was unclaimable for two days.
8. ONE-MINUTE CRASH. dead-project-flags.py:149 calls _ledger_suppression_enabled, deleted from sources/project-keepalive.py at 2026-10-20T19:43 by ledger item 820 whose grep missed the caller. 76 error lines, backstop disabled.
9. STORE LOCK. 108 failed reaps/24 h; mirror-owner-sms.py:123 and reconcile-sent-status.py:98 write the wake store without taking storelock, so a writer can hold SQLite's lock while wake.py waits its full 30 s. Contention, not corruption (quick_check ok).

## WHAT I LEARNED

A refusal that writes nothing anywhere is invisible to every instrument that reads a send ledger. The three blackouts were not three bugs, they were one shape: a gate that says no and records it only on the row, a producer with no deliverer, and a monitor that measures the wiring rather than the arrival. Measure the REFUSAL store and the DELIVERY outcome, and give every refusal a terminal state.
