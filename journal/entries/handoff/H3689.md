## Lessons from running this fleet (each cost real time)
1. **A child commits at the end, and nothing before.** I read `git log` at 25 minutes, saw four branches at
 +0 commits and clean trees, and concluded the mesh had landed off-target. It had not: the children were
 working on ZABZ-TECH the whole time and committed at minute ~8. **Commit count is not a progress signal
 for a subagent.** The signal is the completion notice. Do not launch a duplicate fallback on that reading.
2. Remote `subagent` children here land on `zabz-tech` and run in-process; ~490 s per docs workstream. Four at
 once was fine (no pressure: 82.4% committed, below the 85% line).
3. Generated documents (`docs/INDEX.md`, `docs/audits/dead-citations-*.md`, `REGISTER.md`) conflict on every
 merge, by construction. Resolve by taking either side, then run `docs_check.py --write` **on the integrated
 tree** — that is the only correct resolution, and it needed a second regeneration pass because the
 dead-citation count depends on the new documents.
4. The gate caught my own new document twice: two citations of authority paths (needed `secratary-cloud:` /
 `harness-config:` prefixes) and links written relative to the repo root instead of to `docs/audits/`. The
 mechanism works on its author.
