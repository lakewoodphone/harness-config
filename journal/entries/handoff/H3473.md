## FOUND AND FIXED THIS ROUND

The **daily reprogram was failing silently on transient plug timeouts** with a
24-hour retry. From the machine's own log, three mornings out of seven:

```
Oct 03 10:00 ERROR NOT programmed: ConnectTimeout
Oct 04 10:00 ERROR NOT programmed: ConnectTimeout
Oct 06 10:00 ERROR NOT programmed: ConnectTimeout
```

That job writes the **ON** schedule onto the plug — the only thing that brings
power back on motzei once the secretary or HA is down. A miss on a **Friday**
would leave the coming Shabbat with no ON schedule and power would not return,
with nothing having alerted anyone. Full record: pain **P3004**.

Fix, two layers: `program_week` retries the connect 4× with a 5 s gap, and the
scheduler job became a 15-minute interval behind a window guard
(`_within_shabbat_program_window`, open `shabbat_program_window_hours` = 3 from
`shabbat_program_time`) instead of a one-shot daily cron. Idempotent inside the
window, so the first success of the day takes effect.

## ALSO WORTH KNOWING

The new retry silently made a **pre-existing** test the slowest in the suite
(15 s): `test_program_week_returns_error_when_plug_unreachable` now waits through
the retry delays. Fixed with an autouse no-real-sleep fixture in
`test_shabbat_orchestrator.py`, and the same guard in the new
`test_shabbat_reprogram.py`. Suite is back to ~2 s. A retry you add is a delay
every existing caller inherits.
