### WHAT THE AUDIT FOUND (each re-derived by the manager, not taken from a child's report)

1. **The Plaid pull fails most days and nothing notices.** 6 of the last 8 nightly runs failed; the last complete
 success was 2026-09-29 04:30Z. Root cause, verified from source: `plaid-python 42.0.0` is installed and
 `dir(plaid.api)` contains no `ApiException`, but `plaid_service.py` catches `plaid.api.ApiException` at six
 sites — so the handler raises `AttributeError` and destroys the real error. Second cause: 13.2 GiB SQLite write
 contention at 04:30; `plaid_ledger_ingest` sets no `busy_timeout` while `plaid_service` sets 10 s.
 Newest Plaid-attributable ledger row at 15:50Z is dated **2026-09-27 — 3.66 days behind**.
2. **Nothing schedules the accounting pipeline.** `books/report/__init__.py:21-24` says `automate.py` and
 `deploy/` are "not yet built". The only `ws3-classify.run` row in `run_log` is 2026-09-20T15:38Z. `books.db`
 is 10 days stale; `live/accounting.db` is written daily and is what the owner-facing paths actually read.
3. **The `cfo` project is on the autonomy loop and idle:** `todo=0 running=0 done=7`, state file
 `"work_remaining": false`. The carrier is healthy (45 releases, 0.60 of 25.00 USD spent, no caps in force) —
 the queue is simply empty, so nothing will ever be filed.
4. **No learning loop.** Verified by grepping all of `books/**`: the only `created_by='owner'` rules are two
 seeded from a 2025 rulebook; nothing turns an owner answer into a rule. Answering about a merchant changes
 nothing and the same merchant returns to the same unclassified pile.
5. **The ask path is throttled to near-silence and cannot match an answer to a question.** The kernel's own
 reading: `owner_send_refusal_note: "held: urgency 'normal' is below the send threshold 'urgent'"`. Caps:
 `--max-sends 2` per 5-minute tick on the AI line, 2 owner SMS per UTC day on the app path. And the thread
 matcher closes *the oldest open promise* for a ≤40-word reply — two outstanding questions means the answer
 lands on the wrong one.
6. **The needs-attention badge can carry a count, not a question.** `phone-gate.py:781` filters kernel findings
 on `needs_attention`; the badge renders failing health checks only. The kernel's `CHECKS` table in
 `ck/sentinel.py` is where a `cfo_money` check goes, and it then reaches every DSH window within 5 minutes.

### RESEARCH ANSWER

**Do not switch bank-data providers.** Plaid already covers Chase, Bank of America and Amex (all OAuth); no
competitor documents better swipe-to-posted latency, because the issuers set the ceiling. Teller
($0.30/enrolment/mo) and Quiltt ($100/mo) are *cost* plays, not freshness plays; Yodlee, MX, Finicity and Akoya
are enterprise sales motions; Flinks has a $500/mo floor and a 1-year term; Basiq, Powens and GoCardless are not
US. The once-a-day cron is the defect. Adopt instead: transaction + item webhooks (`PENDING_DISCONNECT` fires 7
days ahead), `/transactions/refresh`, live Balance polling (30/hour/Item = one every 2 minutes — the real-time
asset already paid for and unused), Statements for month-end tie-out, Enrich for merchant identity, and Stripe
primary for everything the business charges.
