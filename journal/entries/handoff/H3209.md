Objective 4 said "make every queued item answerable by reading its case file, creating one where
missing". Creating a case file for all 154 uncased items is the WRONG instrument: a bare ring
from a stranger is not a case, and 154 near-empty files would bury the ~200 real case files.
The correct instrument is a ranked ACTION list, and case files only where there is substance.
`build_worklist.py` flags `needs_case_file` only when the item is substantial - a voicemail, a
real text over 60 chars, or three or more rings. That flags 56, not 154.

no customer who SPOKE OR WROTE should lack a case file. A single zero-second ring is traffic,
not a customer, and cannot be made answerable by adding a file.
