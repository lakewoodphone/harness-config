### The change that moved rank one from 6 to 9

**A document states its subject at the top, and BM25 cannot see that.** Every chunk is a bag of words,
so a spec that mentions the question's words once in its opening lines lost to a journal lesson that
repeats them forty times further down — `SPEC-v2.md` never appeared in the top fifty for "journal v2
specification" while lessons that repeat the words did. A match in a document's **first chunk**
(`lines` starting `1-`) now breaks the tie inside the store: after name coverage, before the raw bm25
order. Cost: nothing measurable (median 191 ms).
