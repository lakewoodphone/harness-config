## IN FLIGHT

- **The last verification step needs a human and has not been done.** Open Settings on DRN 2002 and
 look at the Wi-Fi pane. Everything machine-readable agrees the new profile is installed, but
 `PayloadUUID`s are hard-coded and identical before/after, so no MDM read can prove the device is
 running the *new* restrictions rather than the old one. See the lesson entry of this date.
- `forceWiFiPowerOn` is left `true`, so Wi-Fi still cannot be switched OFF. If the owner wants true
 two-way switching, it is one value in three files + a re-lock. Owner has not been asked; the
 decision is recorded with its reason instead.

## BROKEN

- Nothing newly broken. Every device returns `MCInstallationErrorDomain 4027` for the
 `layeredkiosk.phase1` install in every lockdown batch - pre-existing, cosmetic: both variants share
 a `PayloadDisplayName` so iOS refuses the second, and the phase-1 profile is on no device. Not
 touched here; it is a separate cleanup.
- `allowWiFiModification` and `allowWirelessModification` are **not Apple keys** and do nothing. They
 sat in these profiles for years reading as levers. Left in place (harmless) but flagged in the docs
 and in the deployment-process table.
