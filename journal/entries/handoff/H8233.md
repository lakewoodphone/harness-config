### `/health` is green — and every line of it is true for its own reason

```
ok= True problems= []

```

Getting there needed three distinctions that had been collapsed into one:

| class | what it means | example |
|---|---|---|
| **own clock** | this host produces it; judged by its ingest row | comms 18 min |
| **pushed** | arrives from a workstation; judged by the newest document delivered | chat:vscode 9 min |
| **local only** | `push: false` by decision D-C; it never crosses, so its age here is not staleness | db:localdb |
| **not here** | bookkeeping from an early run; the corpus arrives under another id | files:local → files:zabz-yoga |

Two of those were permanent false alarms in the previous rule, and one of them (`db:localdb`) would
have stayed red forever because nothing was ever going to arrive.
