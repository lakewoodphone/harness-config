### I FOUND AND FIXED THE PRODUCER OF `Cannot operate on a closed database.`
`app/database.py`'s idle-TTL loop closed connections whose owning thread was **still alive**;
the owner's next use of the reference it was holding then raised. The dead-thread pass 20 lines
above already reclaims handles legitimately (its own test:
`test_dead_thread_connections_are_closed_not_just_forgotten`), so the eviction bought nothing.

**The fix:** `if owner_alive: continue`. Connections whose owner cannot be determined are still
swept, so the loop keeps doing work.

**Verified, not asserted.** The counter ran at a steady **11–17/hour** all day. Last event
**16:46:07**; fix deployed **16:51**; **0 events since** — while `closed_on_handout` (2) and
`closed_in_use` (1) kept firing in the same hour, which is what rules out "the recorder died".
152 events between 06:12 and 16:46.

- `test_db_connection_hygiene.py::test_idle_connections_are_reaped_by_ttl` asserted
 `pytest.raises(sqlite3.ProgrammingError)` on the owner's next use — it pinned the defect.
 Rewritten as `test_idle_connections_are_not_reaped_while_their_owner_is_alive`: the owner's
 connection must still WORK and may not be dropped from the cache.
- `test_co_w7_acceptance.py::test_the_cache_records_when_it_closes_a_live_threads_connection`
