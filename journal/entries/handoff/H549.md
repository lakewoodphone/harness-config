## 2026-09-18 03:19 UTC · ZABZ-YOGA · The wake chain fired by itself: Weinberg replied, the server released a headless session with nobody in the loop - and it exposed that the session answered nobody

# H-new — The wake chain fired by itself on a real reply, and the flaw it exposed

This is the first time the system acted on a live event with no human in the loop.

## WHAT HAPPENED, IN ORDER, WITH NOBODY TYPING ANYTHING

1. **02:41:53** — I texted Yisroel Weinberg from the AI line asking for his wife's three facts
 (`SM5acd7335d569f2d9bb2da79a728c75c3`, delivered).
2. Registered an **await**: *"take her business name, WhatsApp number and message choices, then publish
 her page."* That is the mechanism that turns a reply into work.
3. **03:02:55, 03:03:06, 03:03:12** — **he replied**, three times.
4. The cron reconciler pulled them from Twilio. The responder matched the await, filed a wake row
 (`await:8482102477:SMb2205782317`), and marked the texts `woken`.
5. The dispatcher claimed it and ran a **real headless DSH session on the desktop**.
6. The session read the thread, concluded correctly that the two facts were not there, and reported.

Nobody opened a GUI. The server saw a reply and revived an agent to handle it. **That is the thing the
owner asked for, and it works.**

## THE FLAW, FOUND BY WATCHING IT HAPPEN

**The session did the work and answered nobody.** Weinberg was left waiting a second time — his reply
was: *"Not sure what you mean with the messages. She wants a way that people can be automatically
added to be able to see her status"*, *"Thank you!"*, *"Don't spend too much time on it"*.

The await prompt said *"do WHAT when they reply"* and never said *"and reply to them"*. A system that
can release a session but leaves the human on the other end unanswered has moved the silence, not
removed it. Fixed: the responder's wake prompt now leads with **"DO THE WORK BELOW, AND THEN REPLY TO
THE PERSON … do not finish without sending one"**, names the exact send command, and repeats the
signed-Daniel rule.

I answered him myself the same hour (he had asked a real question, and a confused customer of a favour
deserves a human answer, not another experiment):

> Thanks - let's do the simplest version. I only need two things from her: the business name and the
> WhatsApp number customers should message. I'll use four ready-made messages she can reword any time.
>
> On the automatic part: WhatsApp does not allow a link to add someone to a Status. They have to save
> her number, and the page does it in one tap with a "Save our number" button - that is as automatic
> as WhatsApp permits, and it does work.
>
> Not spending much time on it.
> - Daniel

His expectation is now the *documented* one: she wants people **automatically added** to see her
Status. That is the exact thing that cannot be linkified (L1668's research), so the two-step flow is
the honest product, and he has been told so plainly rather than sold the impossible.

## A SECOND FLAW, MINE, FOUND THE SAME WAY

The first version of the dispatcher **filed its own wake row on a timer** ("weinberg-whatsapp-details").
It was released and burned a session to be told there was nothing to do. Removed. A dispatcher
releases; it must never file. A row is only filed by something that knows there is work.

## A THIRD, SMALLER ONE

The outcome came back **empty** while the desktop file was fine: `scp` was given a Windows path with
backslashes and read them as escapes, and `2>/dev/null` hid it. Forward slashes fixed it; the chain now
returns the session's own words (`WAKE_OUTCOME_BACK_OK`). **A silent failure with a correct-looking
remote file is the shape to watch for here.**

## STATE

 `wake-run.ps1` (desktop), cron every 5 minutes: sync → responder → learn → wake-dispatch.
- Await re-registered on Weinberg for the two facts, so his next reply releases a session that is
 explicitly told to reply and then publish.
- The wake store is still the **v0 schema** — no leases, no attempts, no cost. A four-way fleet is
