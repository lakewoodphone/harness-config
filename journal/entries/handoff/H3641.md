## 2026-10-15 (evening) · ZABZ-TECH · HANDBOFF: outbound-MMS fault reframed as a RECENT CARRIER-SIDE CHANGE across a population

**Read, in this order:** `lpt-flip-phone/docs/incidents/CASE_615387849_E4610_OUTBOUND_MMS_2026-10-15.md` (the full
bench record for one handset) then `.../E4610_E4810_MMS_DIAGNOSIS_AND_AB_PLAN_2026-10-15.md` **§0b**, which revises
the earlier sections. Also L3821, L3824, L3825, L3826.

### What changed today — the two facts that matter
The customer, once he clarified properly: **receiving always worked, texts and pictures; only SENDING fails.** And:
**it worked outbound until a few days ago.** The owner adds that **many customers have reached out in the past few
days**. So the fault is outbound-only, recent, and population-wide. That is the signature of a carrier-side change,
not a device defect — and it means every note in this estate describing picture MMS as an inbound failure is wrong.

### What was measured on serial 615387849 (E4610), and stands
- Message+ is the default, enabled, permitted, **and is the pristine factory APK** (hash-identical to
 `tools/apks/message-plus-e4610-v6.4.5.apk`). Not our rebuild. `com.android.mms` is absent from E4610 by design.
- Three SIMs across two carriers — US Mobile, a second US Mobile, and **direct Verizon** — all fail to send.
- Sending was measured against Twilio `+17324447361`: **zero inbound with media across the account's last 50
 messages.** Inbound into the handset works (3/3 delivered, one with `num_media: 1`).
- **The MMS device profile is empty on both carriers:** `userAgent=` and `uaProfUrl=` empty in the MmsService bundle;
 `dumpsys carrier_config` shows `mConfigFromCarrierApp=null` and a default bundle carrying `uaProfTagName=Profile`
 but **no `userAgent`/`uaProfUrl` keys**. In `MmsConfig.smali:1880-1882` the hardcoded `vzmmms1.0` fallback is
 reached **only on a non-Verizon device**, so on this Verizon device nothing sets it. **This is a vulnerability, not
 the change** — it was empty before it broke.
- **Different carriers fail differently:** US Mobile released its MMS network request in **~1.05 s** (once *before*
 the VZWAPP agent registered); direct Verizon held **16.7 s** and past **50 s** against a 60 s socket timeout.
 Record the carrier on every future measurement or the numbers are not comparable.
- **Orphaned MMS connections:** **10** `VZWAPP` agents at the worst point vs a healthy 1. `am force-stop` did NOT
 clear them; a **reboot** took it to 4. Symptom to count, not a cause.
- Ruled out with evidence: radio/data plane, the MMS APN, the SIM, our filter's disables, our rebuilt app, a stuck
 app, mobile_data. The `com.android.smspush` "no receiver app found" line is **once per MMS notification** and is
 the legacy path — Message+ still issues its own `requestNetwork … MMS`. Not the cause.
- **`pm enable com.qualcomm.qti.CdmaCallOptions` is still applied on that handset** and it left the bench that way.
 It changed the on-screen error but sent nothing. **Restore it on any phone that comes back.**

### The one-sentence working hypothesis
The handsets submit outbound MMS without a device profile, and **something on the carrier side changed in the last
few days to start refusing those submissions** — which produces exactly the observed shape: many customers, several
models, receive fine, send dead.

### Next actions, in priority order
1. **Does a second E4610 on the same MVNO send a picture?** That single question decides handset-versus-carrier and
 nothing else I can do substitutes for it.
2. **Build the onset record:** pull every complaint about picture/group messaging from the business record with its
 date, model and carrier, and find the boundary date. An onset date is the fastest route to whatever changed.
3. **Outside question:** did Verizon (or US Mobile's Verizon profile) change MMS handling in the last two weeks —
 MMSC device verification, MMS user-agent enforcement, band or APN changes? Forum and carrier-status evidence is
 the only source.
4. `pm enable`/debug-panel route for Message+'s Custom User Agent, if the panel can be reached in-app — a test *and*
 a candidate fix.

### Honest state
Not solved. Two candidate causes published earlier in the day were refuted by the next measurement (the smspush
line, then the user-agent theory I called weakened) — both are recorded. What is established is the *shape* of the
fault and a long list of eliminations; what is missing is the wire-level reason the submit is refused, and an onset
date. No phone is attached to ZABZ-TECH now.
