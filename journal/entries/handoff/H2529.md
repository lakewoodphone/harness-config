## 2026-09-23 23:10 UTC · ZABZ-TECH · Shlock: nine-angle mesh audit - the rope-loop hold-downs are dead on five grounds, the pole tow survives with six fixes; four measurements gate everything

 All nine returned; two found errors in my own work, recorded rather than patched silently.
- Wrote `2026-09-23-shlock-audit-nine-angles.md` (the synthesis), `2026-09-23-shlock-bench-tests.md` (gates + tests),

 the 6-in-bushing stack trap).

WHAT THE AUDIT SETTLED
- S2 (loose rope loops on the rails) is DEAD on five independent grounds: capstan physics (drag = mu x pi x load,
 ~74 lb per point at design load, so the hold-down and the brake are the same friction); a rope wicks water so the
 rubber washer cannot seal it; 14 of the 20 grommets are 22-24 in outboard of any rail; a UHMW sleeve cannot pass
 a loop on its own rail (the loop sits inside the sleeve's wall); and the knot is as thick as the collar the owner
 rejected.
- S1 (tow the pole ends) survives with six fixes, four mandatory: pin the swaged joints (2 x 1/4 in pins per joint -
 the July doc says swaged joints hinge under lateral load); through-bolt the tow (957 lb, 19x) instead of a rope
 eye fenced by hose clamps; add a mid-edge point at x = 171 (the pole's horizontal bending capacity is only
 19.6-50.7 lb per end against 21-150 lb of tow load); one continuous line to both ends (a rigid bar transmits
 force, not equality). Plus: pulley at the fabric plane, never low (78% of allowable otherwise), and a travel stop
 on the fabric side of the pocket mouth (1.0-2.6 in of dead-band).
- Retraction is not the showstopper it was called, but it is still bad: the stroke is 108 in not 144; with the
 anti-lift rings fitted the beam-clamp envelope caps it at ~30 in; and a gather dams the only drainage the design
 has (water lands inside the sukkah - the exact failure US6024153 exists for). Retract only dry.

BROKEN / OPEN
- Four measurements gate everything and none is done: the installed rail count and spacing (the grommet grid is
 built for 42 in o.c. but the owner's "no overhang" implies ~48 in - only the two corner grommets then survive);
 the grommet bore (#4 0.5 in vs #5 0.875 in - the spec and every buying record disagree); the in-service point
 count (36/22/20/16 gives 57/94/103/129 lb per point); and whether the pocket ends are open or stitched closed.
- The house-edge cam-buckle straps were never ordered, so the fixed edge has nothing to gather against.
- Queue #79 (no permit on the frame) is still open and still his.
