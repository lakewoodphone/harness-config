## THE GAP CLOSED THIS ROUND

`_power_up_critical` returned early and logged loudly when HA did not answer
within its ~6-minute window — and **nothing ever retried**. The interior door
stayed RELEASED and `automation.phoenix_interior_maglock_tamper_restore` stayed
switched off until the following Friday. A one-shot failure with no retry, on the
one step whose entire purpose is not leaving the building open. The mirror-image
failure (an OFF that never happened) already had a recovery; the ON did not.

Fix: `exec_due_power_action` consults two recoveries when nothing is due —

 1. `recover_missed_off` (existing) — run open, plug still ON. FIRST, because
 it is the door-SAFETY case and must not queue behind an extra HA call;
 2. `recover_incomplete_power_up` (new) — run closed, plug on, door still
 released. Acts only when the calendar says we are outside a run, the relay
 reads released, and HA answers. Refuses outright on an unreadable calendar,
 never touches the door inside a run, and treats an unreadable relay as
 incomplete rather than done.

Also: `power_up_is_complete()` (the cheap every-minute question) and
`_power_up_critical(settle_before_relock=False)` so a retry does not block the
scheduler for the full grace window again.
