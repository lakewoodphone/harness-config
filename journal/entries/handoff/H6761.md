## 2026-10-16 · the Waze device business, decided end to end

### The decisions

1. **Device 285 with the case a 20.00 optional add-on** (was 299 with the case inside it) — his words, journal
 `D4042`. Code: PR #452. Only `PRICING-AUTHORITY` in the divergent `lpt-hub` clone still says 299.
2. **Fixed plans plus add-on data. Metered overage is gone.** *"we'll just sell plans and addon data and
 thats it i think."*
3. **Starter 7 / 150 MB · Standard 11 / 250 MB (recommended) · Plus 16 / 500 MB.** My 6/9/13/17 and my
 two-rung versions were both worse: on the same 50-device fleet his ladder earns **243.60/month (46.8%)**
 against my 154.85 (32.9%) and 202.35 (38.2%).
4. **The subscription is always Starter; extra data is a step-up for the rest of the cycle**, priced at the
 tier difference (Starter→Standard 4.00 for +100 MB, Starter→Plus 9.00 for +350 MB). This is the best
 structural idea of the day: it removes proration entirely, because a step is a one-time charge and not a
 plan change, and the SIM fee is already paid so every step is 51–69% gross.
5. **+250 MB for 5.00 past 500 MB, repeatable, 1 GB ceiling, and past 500 MB it is manual** — his "stop data
 burn" rule. The mechanism: a SIM stopped at its limit recovers by **raising the limit**, and never by an
 enable call (Telnyx `sim-lifecycle`; enable/disable is what hangs on this account, DRN 2001).
6. **The warranty is ONE YEAR and it is a WORKING warranty** — if Waze stops working, a fault or an update,
 we put it right for the first year. That supersedes the "8 months / NEEDS OWNER" dispute, and it is a
 *service* warranty, not damage cover. **Nothing tracks the term** (no start date, no expiry) — open.
7. **International: the plan's allowance travels with the customer.** Telnyx's own Israel page: data **"as low
 as 0.0625/MB in Israel"**, 650+ networks, 180+ countries, no roaming fees. That makes the Standard plan
 cost-neutral at **176 MB in Israel** — and he said *"more like 175 MB"* before the rate was fetched. Cost
 is bounded at **4.63 for a travelling month**; disclosed in fine print in the customer's terms.
8. **The sign: green, price-led, service-led, branded.** `15-business/sign/waze-device-sign-letter.html`,
 US Letter so he prints it himself. Eight rounds of his corrections, all recorded in
 `shop-sign-and-benefits-2026-10-16.md`.
9. **Every plan option must be settable by an operator through the API, not only in a web form** — his
 *"much of the device provisioning you will do … make sure that's all robustly built"*. Specified as
 workstream **W5** in `plans-build-spec-2026-10-16.md` §8.
