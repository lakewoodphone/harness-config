## 2026-09-18 02:21 UTC · ZABZ-YOGA · ZABZ-YOGA · The text line is instrumented: 70 inbound SMS that reached Twilio and never reached the app are recovered, and nothing new can be lost silently

## CHANGED

**The AI text line is instrumented, and nothing on it can be silently lost any more.**
`~/bin/sms-inbox.py` on secratary (frozen copy: `harness-config/scripts/sms-inbox.py`, commit
`a99fa0c`), cron `*/5 * * * *`. It reconciles against Twilio — the actual log of record — into its
own SQLite store at `~/.sms-inbox/inbox.db`, keyed on MessageSid, and cross-checks the app's own
`sms_log` so a text that reached Twilio but not the app is a visible GAP rather than silence.

Its own store, not the app's, on purpose: the authority's deployed tree is **119 ahead / 176 behind
origin/master with uncommitted edits to `app/main.py`**, so an additive sensor could ship today while
the in-app writer waits for a deliberate fix. It never sends anything.

## THE FINDING (this is the point of the entry)

**70 inbound texts on the AI line reached Twilio and never reached the app.** 325 inbound total,
15 correspondents. Not spam — this: *"Call me now"*, *"I tried to call you and you were just quiet"*,
*"Open the front door for Mattis Klein for 10 minutes"*, *"Is this an automatic ai generated text or
legitimate questions?"*, *"My car is not starting"*, *"So what did it close at?"*, and two people
separately asking the system to stop its auto-reply.

Mechanism, from `journalctl -u secretary-api`: `Unhandled error on /webhook/twilio/sms: database is
locked` → `HTTP/1.1 500 Internal Server Error`. Twilio does **not** retry an inbound SMS webhook
after a 5xx, so each one was gone at that instant. Six confirmed on 2026-09-07/08/09 alone.

Two failures stacked: the text was lost, and nobody was told it was lost. Recovering the messages
was only possible because Twilio keeps them.

## IN FLIGHT

The permission model and the responder. `sms_inbox.py` already carries the ledger —
`permissions(phone, name, relationship, allow auto|queue|never, note, learned_from, updated_at)` —
seeded so far with the owner only (`+17325691594` = queue: he talks **to** the system, never
auto-reply to him). 15 correspondents are recorded and unidentified; the next round resolves them
from `comms_identity_link`/`contacts` and sets a real `allow` per person.

## BROKEN / NOT DONE

2. **Nobody answers a text.** No responder exists yet. That is the whole second half of the ask.
3. The authority's divergence (owner question #89) is now in the way of any in-app fix.
