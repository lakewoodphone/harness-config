## THE TWO BUGS THIS ROUND CAUGHT (both would have sent garbage)

1. **The model ignores the output format.** Asked for the bare token `QUEUE` it returned
 `**QUEUE**\n\n### Reasoning\n…` plus two suggested drafts. A plain `startswith("QUEUE")` check
 fails on the bold markers, falls through to "it answered", and **texts the whole scratch pad to
 the owner's father.** `classify()` now normalises, and refuses to send anything containing a
 heading, an "Option" list, or over 400 chars.
2. **Declining everything made `auto` a slower queue.** The first dry-run queued all six texts,
 including the exact case the owner named as answerable. Hence the HOLD tier.
