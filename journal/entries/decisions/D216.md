**D216 · 2026-09-16 · Android/LPT: a factory reset on an FBE phone (incl. Duoqin/Qin) is a crypto-erase - no chip-off lab referral.**

DECISION (2026-09-16, owner-adjacent case: Yocheved Hirschfeld 848-299-9657, Qin phone, deleted videos of her late mother).

If a factory reset actually happened on a File-Based-Encryption Android device, the data is unrecoverable and we do NOT refer it to a chip-off lab.

WHY: FBE key destruction is a crypto-erase - the flash holds ciphertext and no key. This is not a difficulty judgement, it is terminal. Our own BROM-level research confirms Qin (Duoqin) F22 Pro runs FBE AES-256-XTS with hardware-bound keys (lpt-hub/directives/completed/2025-12-26-qin-f22-pro-adb-authorization-bypass.md L116, L1833). Qin F21 Pro/F30 are MediaTek and BROM-accessible, which does NOT help - the keys are not in the dump.

MONEY CONSEQUENCE: a lab referral here is a near-certain 100-400 USD for ciphertext (300DDR charges a \ labour fee when sectors read but no files are recoverable - lpt-hub/docs/customer-operations/300ddr-full-customer-referral-workflow.md). Do not sell hope.

THE EXCEPTION THAT MATTERS: do not close on the customer's WORD 'factory reset'. It is customer vocabulary, not a diagnosis. Gallery/media-store corruption, storage-mount failure and SD-card faults are routinely described that way and ARE recoverable in-house. Also check the free paths first: Google Photos/Drive, a copy sent to family by WhatsApp or email (on-disk WhatsApp media is NOT encrypted), a microSD card, or the older phone the media came from. Stop the customer using the device immediately.
