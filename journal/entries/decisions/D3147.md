**D3147 · 2026-10-13 · Ullman IdeaPad 3 (NM-D471): one price, not a menu - 150 all-in with the 50 already paid credited, data-only at 75 plus media if the board is dead.**

Decided 2026-10-13 on WO26101312747 (order 3073, customer 1725). The owner's standing rule is that a price is worked out, not offered as candidates, and that the shop's own documented convention beats a new invention.

THE NUMBER: 150 all-in for a board-level repair of the charging/power input path, with the 50 diagnostic fee he already paid credited against it, so 100 to collect on pickup. If the trace shows the board is dead rather than the input path, the number changes category: 75 plus media for a data-only job (pull the drive, hand back his files), and no board is bought.

WHY 150 RATHER THAN ANYTHING ELSE: (a) parts are under USD 5 - the charger IC is 3.20, the input FETs, the PR5425 shunt and the ACDET divider resistors are dollars each - so the labour is the entire cost and roughly two hours of bench work is what the job is; (b) 150 is the figure the shop already set on this identical board the last time it was in, WO2607290002 (Kreisman, same presenting fault), so it is the shop's own convention rather than a new invention; (c) it is wrong only in the safe direction - if the fault turns out cheaper, the shop still made a good repair, and the price is never raised after the fact because nothing is quoted to the customer until the trace is done.

VALUE REALITY THAT CONSTRAINS IT: a working used 11th-gen IdeaPad 3 with i5/8GB is a 150-250 machine (one hard US sold point at 150). The exact replacement board PN 5B21B85082 asks 379.95, which is more than double a whole working used machine, so the exact PN is never the right buy; the cross-compatible 5B21B85187 (same NM-D471 PCB, i5-1135G7) was seen at 91.11, and a Windows OEM licence travels with the board so that must be disclosed before any swap.

TERMS ALREADY SET BY THE OWNER ON THE 2026-10-13 CALL, which the price must respect: the 50 goes toward the repair, the price is told to the customer BEFORE any repair, and the customer may decline.

NOT DONE, AND DELIBERATELY: orders.total is NULL, because nothing has been quoted. No part ordered. No message sent to the customer - outbound needs the owner's per-message approval.
