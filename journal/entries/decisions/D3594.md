**D3594 · 2026-10-14 · The Dialpad office SMS auto-reply STAYS - it is on purpose; the defect is that OUR tooling counts it as a human reply.**

Owner ruling, 2026-10-14 ~23:58 UTC, verbatim: 'it's dialpad auto reply and it's there on purpose.'

Do NOT propose removing or disabling the Dialpad office SMS auto-reply again. This is now the SECOND owner ruling in this area - H356 records a prior session already asking what a caller should get - and the answer is that the instant reply is intentional shop policy, not a defect. A successor who reads P2936b ('Either remove the auto-reply, or gate it') and acts on the first half will be overriding the owner on his own customer experience.

WHAT IS ACTUALLY WRONG, and therefore what the work is. The harm P2936b measured does not come from the reply existing. It comes from two facts about how the estate READS the thread:

1. ps_comms_waiting and the waiting detector treat ANY outbound row as a reply. The auto-reply lands one second after the customer's message, so it CLOSES 'waiting on us' on a thread where no human ever read the question. The bot manufactures health. Measured by child C4 on 2026-10-14: of the 24 rows ps_comms_waiting claims, 8 are false positives, AND 8 genuine questions that the bot talked over are FALSE NEGATIVES - dropped from the worklist entirely. The worst verified instance, read by me directly from /root/.fsearch/comms.db: (848) 245-7112 on 2026-10-07 texted 'Hi it's Mrs spritzer don't see my money in the box can you please open the door I was told you are there thank you' and the only reply was the hours bot. Seven days, unanswered, about CASH IN THE DROP-OFF BOX.

2. The local gate isFirstTimeTexter (a2p-auto-reply.service.ts:150-185) reads only its own 24h auto-reply log, never the thread - so it can also fire mid-conversation.

SO: the fix is to teach the estate's own instruments to recognise the auto-reply as a MACHINE acknowledgement and not as a human reply, so the questions it talks over surface on the worklist where a person will see them. That is a read-side change in our tooling. It does not touch Dialpad, it does not change what any customer receives, and it is ours to make without asking.

Evidence for the text-matching: the live text is the OLD walk-in-hours variant as of the most recent send (2026-10-14T23:08:56Z, (848) 373-6364): 'Thanks for texting Lakewood Phone & Tech. Our walk-in hours are Sunday 12-4, and Tuesday and Thursday 10:30-5:30...'. At least one other variant exists in the history ('We're closed right now. Our hours are Mon-Thu 10:30-5:30, Fri 10:30-1:00', (848) 333-6341 style, 2026-09-03). Do not hardcode one string - match the shape, and say which variants you matched.
