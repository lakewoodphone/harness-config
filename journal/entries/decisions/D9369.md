## The design problem this created, and how it is solved

Giving the temp code a keypad slot makes it a credential that outlives its own switch unless
something clears it. So live HA `house_access_pin_control_timer` now publishes slot 6 with the
temp code when `input_boolean.phoenix_house_access_pin_enabled` goes on, and clears slot 6 when
it goes off. The script writes slot 6 only when the switch is on, and records
`not_written_switch_off` when it is not.

**Firmware fact, measured:** this lock REJECTS clearing an empty slot (`SetPINCodeRsp status 1`,
first recorded in D3148 for slots 2/3). A clear therefore emits no programming event and cannot
be verified — measured 2026-10-22 22:57Z, the slot-6 clear timed out at 45 s and rolled back an
otherwise complete rotation. That is why an OFF switch means "do not touch slot 6", not "clear
it".

## Second measured defect: the owner text can veto a finished rotation

At 22:58Z the owner's copy was refused by `owner_text` with `rate_floor` ("one text every 20
minutes") and at 23:08Z with `similar_hold` ("he has not answered the message sent 0.35h ago").
Both times every door was already correct. The owner message is now **best-effort**: a refusal is
logged with its code, recorded as `owner_sms` in `last-success.json`, and never rolls the codes
back. The parents' send remains the hard gate, because they are the ones who can be locked out.
Neither refusal enqueued anything in `owner_message_queue` — the word "queued" in the gate's
refusal is prose, not a row; verified by reading the table.
