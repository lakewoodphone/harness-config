**D2647 · 2026-09-25 · The Family Chat is mine to run organically - the owner instigates, Chip holds the voice, and I never hand him a plan to approve**

**His words, 2026-09-25, after I asked him to approve four drafted Chip messages:** *"I don't plan the Family Chat messages that
intensely. I usually have nothing to do with it altogether. I just wanted to instigate our conversation... send it, be organic
about it, just like you're supposed to be on the [group] chat. If somebody's talking to you, sometimes respond, sometimes don't."*

**What that settles.**
1. **I run the family chat.** The owner does not want per-message approval for Chip's participation, does not want a schedule to
 review, and instigates rather than authors. The four hand-authored awning messages of 2026-09-25 were an exception he asked
 for, and even then he answered my approval request with "just do it all".
2. **Organic, not clockwork.** Chip speaks on his own presence model (dormancy wakes, burst rolls, cooldowns, the 3/day
 unsolicited cap) - never a mechanical drip of queued messages.
3. **Sometimes respond, sometimes don't.** That is the randomizer's job for ordinary chatter. It does NOT undo H2469/D2390:
 a message ADDRESSED to Chip is still always answered - the owner asked for exactly that two days earlier, and the classifier
 now returns CHIP on five of five realistic reactions to a hand-authored message (measured 2026-09-25 with the real prompt,
 context stubbed, nothing sent).
4. **This is a standing delegation, not a hole in the outbound rule.** Chip is the owner's own voice in his own family chat, and
 the autonomy was his design. What still holds absolutely: Chip never writes a price, a date, a commitment, a legal, medical,
 financial or contractual posture, and never speaks for the owner about the business or about money. `chip_enabled` (kill
 switch) and the monthly budget stay as the hard stops, and either one turns him off completely.

**Technical rider, same session.** `FAMILY_CHAT_AI_MODEL` was `deepseek-v4-flash`, an id that is NOT in the provider catalogue
(`api.deepseek.com/v1/models` lists exactly `deepseek-flash` and `deepseek-v4-pro`), so every call was silently falling back.
Set to the validated chain `deepseek-flash,deepseek-v4-pro` - the provider's own alias first, so a future flash generation is
inherited with no edit (the exact rule the owner gave on 2026-09-15). Verified in a fresh process: chain resolves to
`deepseek-flash` with no warning. **Deliberately not restarted** - the effective model was already the same, and production is
not being bounced hours before Yom Tov for a warning that changes no behaviour.
