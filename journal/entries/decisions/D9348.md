**D9348 · 2026-10-21 · Standing rule: no outbound customer message without a human preview and explicit allow - enforced in the pay-notice flow.**

**The owner's rule, verbatim (2026-10-21):** *"we never send out outgoing messages unless a human
previews and explicitly allows"*, followed by *"i'm not saying you messed up, i'm just saying to be
careful not to wire up something that will text customers by itself"*.

**It is a rule about autonomy, not about speed.** A human seeing the exact body and saying yes is
the gate. The send I made at 14:20:35Z that day was inside it - the body was shown in a fenced
block, he replied "send it", and the sha256 was re-read to prove the body was unchanged. What he
was warning about is the next piece of work: a sweep, a watcher, a retry, or a tool that an agent
can drive end to end on its own judgement.

**How it is enforced now, so it does not depend on anyone remembering:**

1. **Drafting cannot send, by type.** `PayNoticeDraftDeps` in
 `backend/src/services/payments/pay-notice-draft.service.ts` has no sender member; the sender
 appears only on `ApprovePayNoticeDraftDeps`. Any change that adds a transport to the drafting
 path breaks the invariant at compile time. Keep it that way.
2. **The send is one human action.** The only caller of `approvePayNoticeDraft` in the deployed
 backend is the staff tRPC procedure `payNotices.approve` (`/payments/notices`). Measured
 2026-10-21: no cron on `lpt-apps` or the authority touches it, no systemd timer does, and the
 authority's scheduled work is harvesters and guards.
3. **`lpt-pay-notice send` needs the human's words**, not just a hash:
 `--apply --confirm-sha256 <hash> --human-approval "<their words>"`, and the words are written
 to `~/.lpt-pay-notice/approvals.jsonl` with the draft, the hash and the actor *before* the
 transport is touched. A hash alone is a machine gate an agent can satisfy alone; the approval
 sentence is not.

**What this forbids in future work.** No scheduled sweep, reconciliation job, status-change hook,
or "order is ready" trigger may call `approvePayNoticeDraft` or any other sender. Such a job may
create a `PENDING` draft and stop. If a customer is to be texted, a human reads that body and says
yes, every time, and the message that carries it is the one they read.
