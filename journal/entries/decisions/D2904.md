**D2904 · 2026-09-30 · Never pay the lab before the customer has said yes - and size the risk on the decline fee, not the success fee.**

**D - 2026-09-30 - NEVER PAY THE LAB BEFORE THE CUSTOMER HAS SAID YES (owner, after correcting the seat).**

WHAT WAS DECIDED. No lab payment goes out before the customer has seen the recovered file listing and
said they want the data. Sequence, always: the lab issues the listing -> **we review it** -> **the
customer sees it and says yes** -> **then we pay** -> they ship -> we collect. Approval in the lab's
portal is not payment and commits nothing.

WHY, AND WHAT THE SEAT GOT WRONG. I had recommended approving and paying on **74115** (Fishman, 2TB) and
**73459** (Kogel, chip-off) while knowing that neither customer had been shown a listing, that 74115 is
missing 2,239 files / 89.17 GB (mostly large video), and that on 73459 the customer had paid **nothing at
all** - not even the diagnostic. The owner: *"why would you tell me to lay out money that isn't refundable
to the lab, if the customer may not pay us back ... isn't that absolutely terrible misleading business
advice that would get any bookeeper or cfo fired?"* He is right. The advice spent the shop's cash on a
customer's unconfirmed appetite and called it prudence because "the fee is owed anyway".

THE MECHANICS THAT MAKE IT FREE. 300DDR has **no card on file and no authority to debit us**. Every charge
- deposit, open-cover fee, success fee, return shipping - is a **JotForm link we choose to open**. So
payment is our last move, not our first, and "approve" only asks them to send a link.

SIZE THE RISK ON THE DECLINE FEE, NOT THE SUCCESS FEE. The lab's own letter on 74115: *"We charge a $100
labor fee if you don't want the data we recovered."* Paying first risks 500; asking first risks 100 - a
400 swing.

RETRACTED IN THE SAME BREATH: the claim that *"at 99% or better the flat fee is owed whether or not we take
the data"*. That is our paraphrase of their terms page, and the lab's own conduct the same week contradicts
it. If it ever matters, ask one human sentence, not a numbered list.

ALSO RULED: **an uncollected deposit is not a deposit.** 73459 ran at full chip-off risk with 0 collected,
which is what the DEPOSIT POLICY exists to prevent and it was never applied.
