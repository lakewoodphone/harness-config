**Determined from the owner's 39 photographs, 2026-09-17, with the evidence in the frame.**

1. **The panelled enclosure on the deck is a COMMERCIAL SUKKAH, not the rain cover and not the tarp.** Native-resolution crops of the vendor plates read, character for character, `SUKKAHOUTLET` / `(973) 778-5624` and `SUKKAH OUTLET` / `(973) 778-5624`. Four vision agents had independently logged the tan panelled surface above the deck railing as "cannot tell whether it is the tarp or a rigid panel system"; the plate settles it. The owner was asked to explain that panel; he no longer needs to.

2. **Pipe-on-pipe crossings on this frame ARE positively clamped — "the rails simply rest on the beams" is contradicted.** `IMG_0896` is certain: a formed galvanised saddle plate with **two U-bolts, two hex nuts, and individually countable threads** clamping two crossing pipes. Corroborated at `IMG_0885` (saddle + two U-bends), `IMG_0887` (threaded shank + hex nut on a flat plate) and `IMG_0895` (two nuts). Consequence for the wind check: the roof-leaves-at-~40-mph case assumed the beam merely sits in a saddle; where crossings are tied, that mode starts at the ~70-76 mph bound instead. **It is not yet established for a pipe ENDING into another pipe (a tee)** — the parts of a tee are visible in 0896 and 0887 but never its fastener — so the post-to-beam and rail-to-beam* joints still need one square-on photograph of a collar fitting at a tee.

3. **The white collar seen on the rails is a white split sleeve held by two worm-drive hose clamps over black rubber bands on a single pipe** — not a pipe-to-pipe fitting, and not identifiable from pixels as the UHMW 8705K23 sleeve. The earlier statement to the owner that "the UHMW sleeves are on the rails" is overstated and was corrected.

4. **Overhead lumber exists alongside the pipe** (crops show longitudinal grain, a squared arris, a flat face with no cylindrical specular highlight, and a knot — where every pipe in the set has that highlight). Whether any of that lumber belongs to the cover frame or is a pre-existing member is an owner question.

5. **Pipe stencils corroborate the specified material**: `ASTM A5[?]` (A53 almost certainly, digits cut off by the frame edge — not asserted) plus `PIPE`, and `SCH` schedule fragments.
