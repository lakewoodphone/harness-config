## Decision 2026-10-21 · A child's phone: the parent's floor, a band the parent sizes, the child's choices inside it (Q3)

Owner's answer to Q3, verbatim:

> "Option 3 makes sense, it keeps the child's autonomy decisions but the rules are bounded and the
> parents decide the overarching rules and the kid's decisions are within the parents' allowance rooms,
> and the parents can set if it's totally theirs or they just provide the rooms and leave some space for
> the kid to decide."

Recorded as `kosher-filter-ai/docs/decisions/D-20261021-02-floor-and-band.md` (commit `64f70c8`, gate
17/17). Three things now exist on every sealed phone and the product must tell them apart:

1. **The floor** — the parent's rules, immovable at the phone by anyone, including the shop. Hard
 categories, the reset anchor, the partner requirement, the accountability surface.
2. **The band** — how much discretion the parent grants, from zero ("totally the parent's") to everything
 above the floor ("here are the rooms"). Choosing its width is the parenting decision the product
 exists to make concrete, and it belongs in the interview.
3. **The child's own choices** — everything inside the band, chosen in the child's own interview and
 movable again without asking, because the range was already authorised.

Consequences that are now requirements, not options:

- **A rule must carry its provenance** (parent floor / household default / child's own choice). Today
 every rule collapses into one list with no origin, so the product cannot say who chose what.
- **Delay depends on the layer, not the category.** Inside the band: instant. Crossing the floor: a
 request with the class delay. Narrowing the band: the longest delay, because it removes autonomy.
- **Child-facing copy stays honest**: the hard lines are your parents', everything else is yours. That is
 how D-20261021-01 ("it is mine") stays literally true for the part of the phone the child feels.
- **The parent must see what the child did with their room**, or the delegation is a lottery.
- **An adult phone is the same model with one holder in both roles**: self-set floor, the whole phone as
 the band, the partner as the only outside party. Whether an adult may lower their floor to nothing is
 open — it is Q4.
