**D9309 · 2026-10-21 · D-37 the product is LPT Filter and the shop's name is the collateral.**

D-20261021-37 records the owner's answer to Q37 on 2026-10-21 ("LPT Filter, I think."): the product is
sold under the shop's name. One brand, one reputation, one entity to trust. The repository name
(lpt-filter-ai) and the Android applicationId (com.lakewoodphoneandtech.filterai) already agree with it,
so this is a decision recorded rather than a rename ordered.

The consequence worth remembering is the risk accepted deliberately: the shop's name is now the
collateral. A product called LPT Filter that fails reflects on Lakewood Phone & Tech the same day;
there is no distance between product and business, which is the point.

What that forbids: the name on anything the shop would not defend - a beta invite without the seal, a
lesser platform, a discounted tier with a weaker filter (D-13); a second name for the same thing, no
"kosher mode" sub-brand for the child case; one spelling and one capitalisation everywhere; no partner
shop selling it (D-30 already refuses partner benches). Every claim carries the name, so the two scoped
claims travel with it: best effort and always improving (D-33), nothing refunded for a miss (D-32). If
the shop stops, the name stops with it (D-36).

Committed 0d85850.
