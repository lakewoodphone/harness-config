**D3156 · 2026-10-14 · Kamera: device, casing, app shape, and cloud design decided; price deliberately NOT set.**

**D · 2026-10-14 · The camera product ("Kamera") — dev decisions taken, one commercial decision deliberately left open.**

Owner asked for a full research and audit of a "camera that does not look like a phone" for the Orthodox
market, to be built about a month out. Research complete; record is `lpt-hub`
`research/product-research/kosher-camera-device/`, branch `research/kosher-camera`, commit `4d6d5fbd1`,
15 files, 2,736 lines.

**Dev decisions I took (mine, per the standing rule that dev questions are not owner questions):**

- **Device: used iPhone 11, 64 GB.** Same device the Waze product already buys at 145.00; confirmed bulk
 lot at 125.00 for 521 units. Ultrawide + Night mode. Fallback is the **iPhone 12 mini**, and only if the
 casing work proves a 150.9 x 75.7 mm board will not fit.
- **Casing: a "rangefinder" in MJF PA12 with the phone mounted SCREEN-INWARD** so the phone's screen becomes
 the camera's rear LCD and the object has **no exposed glass front** — the one cue every product on the
 market leaves showing. Hollow short barrel (nothing in front of the lens; bore >= 3.46 x depth so the
 120-degree ultrawide is not vignetted), aluminium plate bonded to the hotspot as a heat spreader, no
 metal on the frame rails, Volume-Up plunger shutter.
- **App: ONE custom app (camera + gallery + uploader)**, not stock Camera + Photos. Reason, verified from
 Apple: iOS **cannot hide Settings or Phone**, and **no iOS key strips the share sheet**. One app makes
 "the customer cannot send a photo anywhere" true by construction. Locked with `allowCamera=false` +
 `allowedCameraRestrictionBundleIDs`.
- **Cloud: no Apple Account on the device at all** (`SkipCloudSetup`) — the only configuration where
 "you can only see what you shot" holds by construction, because with no account there is no library to
 merge into or pull down. Upload **Wi-Fi only** into **Cloudflare R2** (1.35/device/month at 100 GB, zero
 egress), with a read-only per-device web gallery. **The eSIM carries no photo bytes.**
- **Distribution: App Store + VPP device-based assignment**, reusing the mechanism that already delivers
 Waze (adamId 323229106). Not the Enterprise Program; ABM Custom Apps flagged VERIFY-OPEN.
- **Pilot: five units.** Not 500.

**The one thing NOT decided, deliberately: the price.** The research stream proposed 349. It does not
survive the corrected casing cost — the market model used 12/unit for a shell, which is the price of a
silicone case, not a camera-shaped enclosure (95-140 at 100 units per the casing research). Landed cost is
245-290 at 100 units and 345-460 at pilot volume; the shop's standing rule is 100 gross profit per device
sale, which puts the floor near 365 at volume. **The price is settable only after a shell is quoted.**

**Also decided: the ABM question is not mine.** Apple's Apple Business Manager Agreement section 2.5
prohibits reselling devices with the enrolment settings enabled, which reaches the **already-selling** LPT
NAVIGATION product, not just the camera. Queued to the owner as **#510 (critical)**; the halachic question
about a cloud-uploading camera is **#511**.
