**D3988 · 2026-10-19 · The estate search corpus is repaired by backfill, guarded by coverage floors, and content-addressed.**

Decided after the authority resize (2 vCPU/7.7 GB/75 GB → 8 vCPU/30 GB/225 GB, measured this session),
against the owner's standing requirement: everything searchable from one place, current within three
hours, an answer in a few seconds.

**(1) Coverage is an assertion, not an inference.** The central index held 1.3 % of the corpus
(18,682 of ~1.4M documents; mail 39 of 118,617; comms 0) and `/health` was green, because freshness
was judged by the newest row in the whole store. `config/sources.json` now carries `coverage.min_docs`
— 14 per-corpus floors — and `esearch /health` fails per corpus below its floor. A floor is set well
under measured capacity; it alarms on a reset or a lost push, not on a quiet afternoon.

**(2) A rebuilt index is repaired by backfill, never by waiting for the next sync.** Adapter
watermarks survive an index rebuild, so every source reports `ok` and emits nothing.
`scripts/backfill.py` reads a host's own index and pushes it; it is idempotent, resumable, honours
`push:false`, and prints what it excluded and why. This is now the documented recovery step
(`docs/OPERATIONS-ESEARCH.md` §3b), and the first use took the centre from 18,682 to 334,630+.

**(3) Every document carries a content hash.** The files adapter emits no `sha`, so identical copies
could not be recognised: one query returned the same test file from four worktrees as hits 1–4.
`add_document` now hashes the text when the adapter did not — one pass over bytes it is already
chunking. The surface collapses identical content to one hit carrying `copies` and `also_at`.

**(4) The authority may do index work, deliberately and bounded.** The old "never build on the
authority" rule assumed 2 cores and 22 GB free; it is now 8 cores, 30 GB and 97 GB free, and the
corpus work was done there. Unchanged: it is the sole writer of `authority.db`, `company-api` shares
the box, and `esearch.service` keeps a 4 GB `MemoryMax`.

**What is NOT decided here** (owner-level, queued separately): what counts as "a document" for the
push allow-list (screenshots and images are currently excluded — 2,401 of ZABZ-TECH's 3,027 docs
corpus), whether git history belongs in the centre at all (98,000 commit documents available), and
pruning ZABZ-TECH's 16.7 GB local index.

Refs: `docs/SEARCH-AUDIT-2026-10-19.md`, commits `4fa334b`, `b0e6052`, `c0d808a`, `b0d9109`.
