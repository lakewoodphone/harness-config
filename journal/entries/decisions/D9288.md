## Cost model 2026-10-21 · what one sealed phone costs, and where the price ladder breaks

Measured on lpt-apps-01: 4 vCPU, 7.76 GB, 75 GB disk, 13 containers, broker DB 81 MB, 377 GB in / 107 GB
out since boot (7 weeks). Hetzner's comparable plan lists at 19.99 for 2 vCPU / 4 GB; the box is costed at
35 a month deliberately pessimistically.

Three costs: inference is pennies per phone because the design keeps decisions on the device (3,000
escalated frames a month is about 6 dollars for a whole fleet); screenshot sampling at one per cent is
pennies; support labour is the business at 0.42 a minute fully loaded (2.10 for five minutes, 4.20 for ten,
8.40 for twenty).

Owner's ladder: 7 monthly, 20 quarterly (6.67), 70 annual (ten months paid, 5.83). Fixed cost about 45 a
month, so break-even is 17 phones at five support minutes and 72 phones at ten. Half-price second phone
contributes 0.90 at five minutes and loses money at ten. Kept as docs/audits/COST-MODEL-2026-10-21.md.
The number to re-measure after the first ten customers is support minutes per phone per month.
