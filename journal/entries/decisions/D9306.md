**D9306 · 2026-10-21 · The parent sets the age at which authority passes, the child sees it, and the ceiling is twenty-four.**

aliases: Q34

Owner, verbatim (2026-10-21):

> "The parent can set the age at setup, with the child seeing, and it can go up to 24."

What it decides, condensed:

1. The parent names the age at which authority passes, at setup, as a line on the form he signs.
2. The child sees it from day one, on his own screen next to the floor and the band — supervision, not a
 sentence.
3. The ceiling is twenty-four: nobody is supervised past his twenty-fourth birthday, at any setting.
4. At that age authority passes in a deliberate event, not a flag: the form re-issued in his name, the floor his,
 the chaver his choice, the parent told.
5. The handover is real — a new form, a new signature, and where the shop holds the anchor a fresh anchor
 decision, because an adult anchored to a parent cannot recover his phone.
6. The date is visible to both and cannot move quietly: moving it is a floor change, the slowest class, with the
 child told.
7. A parent may bring it earlier, never later than twenty-four, and the delay on that loosening is deliberate; an
 unset age still gets the default, shown to the child.
8. A phone that passes to an adult may keep the filter and its release rules are his; billing stays separate.
