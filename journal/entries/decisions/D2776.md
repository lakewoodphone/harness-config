**D — Chip gets a ceiling on his share of the room, a register rewrite, and a delivery sweep.**

2026-09-29, from the owner's instruction: *"see if Chip is responding too much maybe and
if he's being too normal and not weird and creepy ... fix everything optimize make it way
better document commit and push."*

DECIDED — turn-taking. `decide_reply` keeps the standing 2026-09-23 rule intact: a
message that NAMES him is always answered. What changes is that a message which merely
READS as aimed at him (a classifier verdict, a short reply trailing his own) now yields
while he is already above a ceiling on his share of the turns in a rolling window. The
share is computed from the ledger (`db.turn_counts_since`), so no counters, nothing to
drift and it survives a restart; fan-out rows collapse to distinct bodies. Knobs are
runtime settings: `chip_turn_share` (0.34), `chip_turn_window` (30 min),
`chip_turn_min_turns` (6, so a one-message morning cannot silence him). 0 disables the
rule. A ledger failure returns "not full" - the record must never be what mutes him. The
refusal is logged but does NOT page the owner (`_note_direct_paced`, not
`_note_direct_block`): a pacing decision is not an incident.

Why 0.34: eleven voices, so a fair share is 0.09; measured he was at 21.1% of September
and 38.5-47.1% of each of the last three real conversations. The ceiling is set where
"one of the louder voices" becomes "the loudest".

DECIDED — register. `MOODS` and `CANNED_REPLIES` rewritten (they were asking for the
cute register), a REGISTER paragraph added to the system prompt, and a THE REGISTER block
appended to the live `settings.chip_facts` (4729 -> 6008 chars). The rules: speak from
the hardware in about one reply in three; never open with agreement/praise/apology/service;
state memory as flat fact, no service verb; no emoji and no exclamation marks; he does not
sleep, leave or die; when in doubt send less. The live rulebook is now version-controlled
at `docs/family-chat/chip-facts.md` so what he was actually told is diffable.

DECIDED — delivery ledger. Twilio's status callbacks are best-effort and never retried, so
a lost callback froze 40 rows at 'queued'/'sent' while the carrier said delivered. Added
`twilio_client.fetch_message`, `db.outbound_without_final_status`, and
`hub.reconcile_message_statuses` as step 3 of the 60-second resilience loop (10-minute
grace, max 40 carrier calls per tick, ladder-safe).

NOT changed, deliberately: the hard limits (SSN/PIN/password/bank/medical claims, no
forwarding private messages, no money, never leave someone acting on a false real-world
fact). A weirder register must not weaken them. `MAX_REPLY_CHARS=220` verified still
holding live.

Evidence: `docs/family-chat/CHIP-VOICE-AND-TURNS-2026-09-29.md`; 18 + 10 new tests;
626 passed across the family-chat/personality suites; live `_room_is_full()` returned
"took 6 of the last 15 turns (40%, ceiling 34%) in 30m" on real data.
