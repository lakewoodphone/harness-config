**D2715 · 2026-09-28 · Volume is not the limit: the count cap becomes a 500/day runaway backstop and the real limit is 70 USD/day of API spend; stupid or repeated work is blocked by the ledger, not by a counter.**

THE OWNER'S CORRECTION, 2026-09-28, verbatim:
 "I feel like it's more important to make sure it's producing than limit how much it does. Meaning
 if it wakes 50 times a day, but all of that is honest, real work and not made up money spending
 stuff, then it's also fine. There's no reason to limit how much you could do per day. I mean,
 obviously we can't have overall more than $70 of spending, let's say, on the deepseek API daily.
 But it's not like how many times. It's more like if it wakes, if it's stupid and it works the same
 project 50 times and each time it's already a little doing that project or it wasn't necessary,
 then it's stupid. But if there's legitimate reasons to wake more than 12 times, then why not? I
 want to be really powerful and autonomous and able to actually finish projects really fast and
 move on and evolve and do the next thing. The point of stopping you isn't to make you do less work
 is to make sure you're not doing stupid work or doing the same work over and over. Or wasting
 money."

WHAT THIS CHANGES, and it is a correction of my design and not a tweak of a number:
 * THE COUNT IS NOT THE LIMIT. `DEFAULT_MAX_PER_DAY` becomes 500 - a runaway backstop in the same
 spirit as the WAKE_SESSION recursion guard, not a budget. Volume is unlimited by design.
 * THE SPEND IS THE LIMIT: `DEFAULT_MAX_USD_PER_DAY = 70.0`, his number. At the MEASURED 0.068 USD
 per autonomous shift (38 priced releases, 2.5825 USD total in ~/.sms-inbox/wake-cost.jsonl) that
 is ~1,029 shifts/day of headroom, so it binds only when something is pathological - which is
 exactly what a ceiling is for.
 * STUPIDITY IS THE THIRD LIMIT, and it is enforced by the LEDGER, not by a counter: an item is
 refused when the same title is already open (`work.py add` -> `already-filed`), `claim-next`
 leases so two shifts cannot take one item, a `close` with no --proof/--result is RECORDED AS NOT
 DONE, and a completed item stays completed.
 * The old number was also inert AS a money guard: every release line logs `cost=none` because
 `dsh --profile headless` reports no cost, so `spend_today()` has always read 0.0. A money guard
 that reads zero while money is spent is worse than no guard - filed as ledger work to bind it to
 the real per-shift measurement.

WHY THE OLD DESIGN WAS ACTIVELY HARMFUL: every day 2026-09-20..09-28 hit the 4/4 cap, and from 09-25
it was hit by 04:17Z, so the operation idled roughly 20 hours a day against a 70 USD budget it was
never close to touching. Four releases a day costs about 0.27 USD.

STANDING RULE FOR FUTURE SESSIONS: when tempted to add a ceiling on HOW MUCH, add a guard against
WASTE instead. Ask "would this wake be work that is not already done?" - not "have we woken too many
times today?"
