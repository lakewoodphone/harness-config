**D2928 · 2026-10-06 · Shop CP14+ separator: repaired by covering, not replaced - owner confirmed, nothing to order.**

2026-10-06 09:2x ET, Lakewood. Owner asked whether to bin the shop MECHANIC CP14+ separator after a plastic bag melted onto it. Decision (mine, recommended; owner confirmed by choosing 'order nothing'): keep the unit. Clean the burnt residue with heat + plastic scraper + melamine/IPA, then lay a PTFE (Teflon) cover sheet over the silicone mat so the device never touches the plate surface again. Rationale: the surface is silicone, the whole tool is USD 40, there is no bag-proof model in the class, a replacement costs a 2-week wait more than money, and the 'warn me when it has finished heating' feature the owner wanted already exists on his unit as a settable countdown. If he ever does spend, the right purchase is a second CP14+ as a shelf spare (never stalls the bench), not a 'better' one. Concrete cover: a 12 x 16 inch PTFE heat-press sheet (for example HTVRONT 3-pack, USD 5.99 on Amazon) fully covers the 350 x 225 mm mat with margin when the 16 inch side runs along the 350 mm. Related: L3347.
