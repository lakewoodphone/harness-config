**D2649 · 2026-09-25 · 2026-09-25 - field usage measured from real rows: hide the dead fields, treat priority/queue as dead inputs, and note that 117 of 119 recent payments have no order attached.**

Measured 2026-09-25 against the REAL production rows (595 orders / 551 customers / 332 payments), pushed and
merged to `test` as `docs/business-workflows/lpt-repairs-portal-payments/FIELD-USAGE-2026-09-25.md`. The
owner asked for this: "use all the data you actually have... what people actually put in... what fields
don't get filled out."

WHAT MUST CHANGE IN THE UI BECAUSE OF THE DATA
1. **Charging is happening WITHOUT being attached to a job.** 77 orders are CAPTURED, but
 `chargeAmount`/`chargeDescription` are 3/595 all-time and **1 in the last 90 days**; the money lives in
 `payment_transactions` (staffNote filled on 86% - a heavily used field), and **only 2 of 119 payments in
 the last 90 days carry an `orderId`**. So the shop takes money every day and cannot say what it was for.
 This is the strongest argument yet for the owner's instruction that a charge IS a work order.
2. **Hide or stop leading with**: `scheduledDate`, `attachments`, `serviceTemplateId`, `statusId`,
 `passcode` (0 in 90d), `discountPercent` (0/595), `discountAmount` (1), `warranty_start_date`,
 `backBurnerImportance`, `callbackRequestedAt`, `credit_amount`, `customers.address` (3/551),
 `dateOfBirth`, `preferences`, `emergencyContact`, `loyaltyPoints` (0/551), `stripeCustomerId` (0/551).
3. **`priority` and `queue` are dead inputs wearing a full column** - 100% non-null by default, but
 `priority` differs from NORMAL on 22/595 (0 in 90 days) and `queue` on 7/595 (0 in 90 days).
4. **Keep first-class, they are load-bearing and were missing from the September list**: `ticketId` (95%),
 `contactPreference` (76%, 3rd most-used non-key column), `serviceDetails` (63%), `repairPartsSnapshot`
 (65%), `customers.notes` (77%).
5. **`selectedRepairPartIds` was called "CORE - keep" in the September analysis and is 0 non-null in all
 three databases.** The real parts field is `repairPartsSnapshot`. Any UI built on the old claim is built
 on nothing.

TWO FINDINGS THAT ARE NOT UI AT ALL, and are the owner's business
6. **There are two live production databases and they disagree**: the task-directed path resolves to RDS
 `d5oq83veii4rlb` (332 payment rows) while the container `lpt_prod` used by the September analysis holds
 359. Orders and customers track within a row; payments differ by 27. Any payment conclusion must say
 which instance it read.
7. **Stripe is used but no customer is linked to it**: 276 payment rows carry a `stripePaymentIntentId`
 while `customers.stripeCustomerId` is set on **0/551** customers. Saved cards, repeat charging and
 proper receipts are impossible today regardless of what the UI offers.
8. **Notes are split across two stores**: `customers.notes` 423/551, plus a `customer_notes` table with
 976 rows over 504 customers (394 in the last 90 days). The console shows the smaller half.
9. **SMS consent is not recorded while SMS is**: 123,592 `communications` rows (119,291 in 90 days) against
 consent fields set on **1/551** customers. The consent record and the messaging volume are out of step.
10. **Two vocabularies for "paid"**: `payment_transactions.status` is PENDING/SUCCEEDED while
 `orders.paymentStatus` is PENDING/CAPTURED.
