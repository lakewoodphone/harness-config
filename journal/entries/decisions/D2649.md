**D2649 · 2026-09-25 · Two guards on the Shabbat power path: a mid-run power-up needs force, and the door gate cuts on a named basis.**

**D · 2026-09-25 · Two guards on the Shabbat power path: a mid-run power-up needs the word `force`, and the door gate may cut on a named basis.**

1. `POST /shabbat/power-up` now refuses while a Shabbat/Yom Tov run is under way
 unless called with `?force=true`. Reason: powering up mid-run re-locks the interior
 door and restores the guard-rail automations — it undoes the run — and the failure
 mode that invites it is an agent seeing only "Home Assistant unreachable". The
 scheduled power-up goes through `exec_due_power_action`, not this endpoint, so the
 guard cannot block the real one. Verified against the live calendar:
 `current_power_down_run(settings)` → `inside: True, block: 2026-09-26..2026-09-27`.
2. The pre-cut door gate now cuts on a *named basis* and refuses on the others:
 `relay_reads_unlocked` and `ha_down_after_requested_stop` pass;
 `relay_reads_locked` and `relay_unreadable` (with no stop requested) abort. The
 second passing basis is new and necessary: once the graceful HA stop actually
 works, HA is down at the pre-cut read, and treating that as a lockout would abort
 every power-down forever.
3. Dev decisions taken without asking (they are mine): try the known HA stop service
 names in order rather than hardcoding one; keep the `.env` override as the operator
 knob; commit the authority's service files locally and **not push** (journal L1928:
 a journal push once reverted `origin/master`).

Related: D2686 records that tonight's 3-hour offset change was the owner's own
instruction, applied and executed.
