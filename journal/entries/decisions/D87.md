3. LADDER (engineering, mine): preference order deepseek-flash -> deepseek-v4-pro -> deepinfra/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731. Fail DOWN to the lowest tier that answered in the CURRENT probe, after N=2 consecutive unhealthy probes; climb UP only after N=2 consecutive healthy probes. Hysteresis is asymmetric on purpose: speed matters when the current tier is dead, safety matters when it is alive.
