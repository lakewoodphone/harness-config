### Inbound paper lives in `lpt-books/inbox/`, and its git remote never leaves the machine

Decided 2026-09-25, mid-intake, and recorded because a future session will ask
where the owner's scanned paper belongs.

- **Where:** `/home/zabz/lpt-books/inbox/<batch>/` — a batch folder per intake
 (`desk-2026-09-25`, `nj-dol-2026-09`), each with `originals/` (mode 400,
 byte-for-byte, hashed in `INDEX.tsv`), `RECORD.md` (the subject-organised
 record), `records.json`, `CATALOG.md` (intake log with corrections) and
 `transcripts/`. `lpt-books/README.md` now registers it, so the next session
 finds the convention instead of inventing a fourth place for paper.
- **Why there and not a new repo:** `inbox/` already existed for exactly this
 (the NJ DOL photos of 2026-09-22), and `lpt-books` is the repo that owns the
 entities, the cars and the filings that this paper is about.
- **Remote:** `/home/zabz/repos/lpt-books.git`, a bare repo on `secratary`. Checked
 before committing that nothing mirrors it offsite (no cron, no hook, no second
 remote). This matters because the pile holds a marriage certificate, two partial
 SSNs, a bank card PIN, a tax-portal passcode and about eighty neighbours'
 contact details; a hosted remote would have been a data incident, not a backup.

**Related decision, same session:** credential values printed on paper moved to
`/home/zabz/.secrets/desk-2026-09-25-documents.env` (mode 600, outside git, the
established convention on this box), and the prose references them instead of
repeating them. The photographs still show the values, so those specific items are
marked as secret material in the record.
