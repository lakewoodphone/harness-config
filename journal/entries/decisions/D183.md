**D183 · 2026-09-15 · Batch the remaining production guard patches into one restart - the price card is High, not Critical, and restarts are the riskiest routine op on the live site.**

Decision: do NOT restart production once per guard finding. Batch them.

searchMobile.global was patched immediately (image searchmobile-guard-20260915-221641) because it leaked
CUSTOMER PII - names, emails and phone numbers, matched by substring, to anonymous callers.

Its successors leak PRICING, not customer data: repairJob.list returns the shop's full model x repair-type
x labor-tier rate card (verified 200 with real rows, anonymous), motherboardSwap exposes basePrice and part
credit rules, settings.getDefaults exposes defaultMarkupPercent. That is High, not Critical - no customer
data, no money movement, no staff capability reached. Every one of these needs the same treatment: a
container edit, a derived image, a compose repoint and a recreate with a ~50s outage.

So: source-guard them all as the parts land, then apply ONE dist patch covering the whole set with a single
restart and a single verification pass (guard-check.sh + an anonymous probe per path). Source is already
guarded for repair-job.router and motherboardSwap; settings.getDefaults is next.

WHY THIS IS THE RIGHT TRADE: a live backend restart is the riskiest routine operation on the customer site.
Doing it once with a verified list is strictly safer than doing it N times, and the residual exposure is
business-competitive rather than private. If any of them had returned customer data, it would be patched
immediately regardless of the restart count - that is the line.
