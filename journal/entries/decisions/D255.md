**D255 · 2026-09-17 · 300DDR/lab recovery deposit policy: deposit must cover the lab's OUTCOME-INDEPENDENT cost (160 base + 100 open-cover + the model's donor average), as a credit not a fee.**

DECISION (owner, 2026-09-17, in his words: "write it in now for all future recoveries so we remember this")

The LPT lab-referral deposit policy is now: the customer's up-front deposit must cover the lab's OUTCOME-INDEPENDENT cost, sized against the FAILURE case, never against the success fee.

- Base deposit: $160 per drive (unchanged).
- + $100 per drive open-cover fee whenever the metal HDA cover was or may have been opened. Non-refundable to us; refundable to the customer if the lab confirms it never opened the drive.
- + the lab's own average donor figure for that exact model whenever donor parts are possible or likely (chances reply states it - $195 for ST2000LM007, $168 for ST1000LM035). Pays for the donor if the head swap happens; returned or credited if it does not.
- + outbound shipping (~$25).
- Quoted to the customer as ONE number and as a CREDIT against the agreed recovery price - never a price increase. The quoted price for a successful recovery does not move.
- The flat recovery fee ($300/$500/$700) and the PCB/encryption/large-drive fees are SUCCESS-ONLY and must never size a deposit. On a no-data drive 300DDR charges nothing except the open-cover fee if the cover was opened, plus shipping.
- A donor is never bought without the customer's yes; 300DDR allows declining the head swap at no charge.
- Never quote a donor-inclusive flat price - a donor inside the flat price nets $150 on the 2TB drive at the bottom of the range, under the owner's >=$200/drive floor.

WHY: the Fishman 347-534-6208 case collected $160/drive and left an uncovered outcome-independent exposure of $168-$195 per drive (the donor) plus up to $100/drive (open cover). His case was quoted and collected before the rule existed and stands as agreed; the rule is for all future recoveries.
