**D153 · 2026-09-15 · Shlesinger X201 flicker: diagnose the cable before ordering a panel; laptopscreen.com does not carry 2010 ThinkPads.**

Task evidence: customer Shlesinger (848-245-7112), shop task 25404, case
`docs/customer-operations/cases/shlesinger-thinkpad-x201-screen-flicker-2026-09-15.md`, 2026-09-15.

1. DECIDED: cable before panel. The manager reported "screen flickering, everything attached
 securely". On the ThinkPad X200/X201 the display cable runs through the hinge and breaks INSIDE the
 insulation, so a seated connector proves nothing. ThinkPad-Forum.de (X201, intermittent LED
 backlight) states the pattern: it starts with the ThinkLight and spreads; remedy is a cable matched
 by the FRU on the connector sticker. So the order of work is: 20-minute non-invasive tests (BIOS,
 lid-angle sweep, ThinkLight/webcam, flashlight, external monitor, battery-vs-AC) -> cable -> panel.

2. SOURCED: panel is `LP121WX3(TL)(C1)`, matte LED, 30-pin LVDS, X200/X201/X201i, FRUs 42T0710 /
 42T0711 / 27R2453. Live price 2026-09-15: newlaptopscreen.com "Lenovo X201 12.1 inch", new grade
 A+, $65.62 incl. tax, free shipping 5-12 days, 18-month warranty, 60-day free returns.
 Quote by the shop formula (cost x 1.4 + $65) = $156.87 -> $155-160.

3. IMPORTANT NEGATIVE: laptopscreen.com, the shop's usual screen supplier, does NOT carry this
 machine. Verified: `/English/model/Lenovo/X201/` and `/English/model/Lenovo/THINKPAD%20X201/` both
 answer "404 - no items found". Their catalogue effectively starts a few years after 2010, so for a
 2010 ThinkPad do not start there.

4. ECONOMICS: a working used X201 is worth about $50-100, so even a cable-only repair at ~$105-120
 exceeds the machine's value. Give the customer the number before doing the work rather than
 quietly repairing a machine that cannot justify the bill.

Nothing was ordered and no customer message was sent.
