**D9295 · 2026-10-21 · A parent may keep frames, encrypted so that only his account can open them and we never can.**

aliases: Q24

Owner, verbatim (2026-10-21):

> "Parents can set whatever settings they want, including getting screenshots or questionable material from
> their children, however even if that means the content is in our servers, it will be encrypted, and only
> the parent's account can see it — we can never see it."

What it decides, condensed:

1. Retention is a household setting, default off, and the parent may switch it on; the asking is recorded.
2. What is kept is encrypted so that only the parent's own credential opens it — end-to-end, not "encrypted
 at rest with a key we hold", which is encryption we can undo.
3. No staff route, no admin route, no support exception. A member of staff seeing a frame is impossible by
 construction, not forbidden by policy.
4. The AI classification path is a separate act: transient, consent-gated, and a household that stores
 images is not thereby sending them to a provider.
5. Retention has an expiry the parent sets, and deletion is real — ciphertext and the household's key
 material destroyed, and a D-18 deletion request takes the ciphertext too.
6. Key loss is stated out loud at the moment retention is enabled: lose the credential and the recovery
 path and the images are unrecoverable, including by us. That is the price of the sentence.

Nothing here is a promise about our behaviour; it is a property of the system or it is a lie.
