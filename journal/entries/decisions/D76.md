**D76 · 2026-09-14 · Archiving is right for our own audit records and wrong for a record about a child.**

`screenshot_reviews` was in `_ARCHIVE_TS_TABLE_COLUMNS`, so the hourly retention loop wrote a **gzipped copy** before deleting the rows. That is correct for access requests, decisions and tamper alerts — their value is historical and the shop should be able to account for what it did. It is wrong here on two counts: COPPA's deletion test is that the information is not maintained "in retrievable form" and cannot be retrieved "in the normal course of business", and a `.jsonl.gz` on disk is retrievable; and it **defeated a parent's deletion request**, which removed the row and left the archive untouched. The table is out of the archive registry and now has exactly one retention mechanism — the dedicated purge keyed on `received_ts`, keeping anonymous label counts — and that purge is on the hourly schedule as well as the upload path, so "180 days" holds for a phone that has gone quiet. Two tests pin it: the table is absent from the registry, and a fresh row carrying a wrong device clock survives the purge.
