**D3133 · 2026-10-13 · The device ships in a two-piece box, not a corrugated shipper (owner correction).**

**D — 2026-10-13 · The LPT NAVIGATION device ships in a two-piece box (lid lifts off a base), black or white. Never a corrugated shipper.**

**The owner, on seeing the box the spec had chosen:** *"that box is terrible, it's a cardboard shipping
box — you can't give devices in such a box. It needs to be white or black and open with the top half
coming off the bottom half, every phone comes in such a box."*

**How the wrong box got chosen is the part worth keeping.** `artifacts/kosher-waze-box/BOX-SPEC.md` and
`box-dieline.svg` had already designed the right thing: rigid two-piece, 180 × 105 × 26 mm base
interior, EVA insert, ~330 g filled, "rigid, quiet, obviously deliberate". It was displaced by a stock
Uline kraft mailer, and BOX-SPEC's own revision note gives the reason verbatim: the two remaining
numbers — *a rigid-box quote and a pad-print quote* — *"both needed a supplier contacted, and a
published-price route existed that needed nobody."* **A supplier phone call was avoided by shipping the
product in a shipper.** The failure mode is not carelessness; it is choosing the path that needs no
human, and it should be watched for wherever an agent picks a route.

**The decision now:** two-piece lid-and-base box, black (design ground `#111111`) or white; brand with a
printed label on the **lid**, never print the box for a first run (7–15 business days after artwork
approval). The dimensions are already in BOX-SPEC §2 — nothing needed inventing.

**Ordered for the first ten units (priced 2026-10-13, owner places the order):** a 10-pack of matte-black
7 × 5 × 2.5 in two-piece boxes at 1.60 a box, an 8-pack of 16 × 12 × 1 in cuttable closed-cell foam for
the insert, and a 50-pack of 8 × 10 in clear bubble sleeves — about 52 dollars for ten units, about 5.20
each. Rejected and recorded: magnetic-closure boxes at 4.80/box (nice lid, 3× the cost, sold in 2s and
5s), and the custom-printed rigid box at this quantity (right answer at 100–250 units).

**Honest limit:** nobody has held one of these boxes. The sizes are the sellers' published figures and
the fit follows from arithmetic (161 × 86 × 17 mm device in a 178 × 127 mm footprint), so **the first
article is the test** — build one, close it, confirm the phone cannot move. If it is wrong it is a 16
dollar return, not a custom run.

**Record:** `lpt-hub/docs/customer-operations/waze-device-launch/packaging/box-correction-and-order-2026-10-13.md`,
with a reversing note at the top of `packaging-spec.md` §1 and a row in `FLOWS-INDEX.md` (packaging had
no row there at all).
