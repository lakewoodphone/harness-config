**D2981 · 2026-10-09 · The diagnostic fee is 50 non-refundable, credited to the repair - the kiosk is right and the marketing copy plus the Terms are wrong.**

DECIDED 2026-10-09, ZABZ-YOGA, while reading the LPT storefront.

QUESTION: the site states a diagnostic fee in two contradictory ways. The intake kiosk says 50
non-refundable per item, credited toward the repair. Eleven other source files - including
`frontend/src/pages/TermsPage.tsx:222` - promise a "free diagnostic". Both are live on production.
Which is the shop's actual policy?

THE RECORD ANSWERED IT, SO IT WAS NOT ASKED:
- `KioskIntakePage.tsx:25-27` calls the fee sentence "fixed owner copy... rendered verbatim as
 KIOSK_FEE_SENTENCE" and states that no other claim about price appears on that page.
- journal `L3322` (owner directive, 2026-10-05): "50 non-refundable diagnostic fee per item, credited
 toward the repair. Any draft promising 'no charge to check it' now contradicts current pricing."
- journal `P2854` cites `lpt-hub/docs/operations/lpt-pivot-2026-10-05-program.md` for the same.

DECISION: **the fee is 50, non-refundable, per item, credited toward the repair.** The marketing copy
and the Terms of Service are the wrong side and get corrected. The kiosk sentence does not change.

WHY THIS IS MINE AND NOT THE OWNER'S: he set the pricing himself on 2026-10-05 and had the kiosk
copy written as fixed owner text. Nothing since contradicts it. Correcting a public surface to match
a price he already set is engineering, not a policy choice - and leaving a false statement in the
Terms while charging the fee is the worse option in both directions.

SCOPE: the 11 files listed in the handoff, the Terms wording, and the tests that currently pin the
free claim (HomePage.test.tsx, TermsPage.test.tsx, brand-assets.test.ts).

WHAT WOULD REOPEN IT: a 2026-10-05-or-later instruction from the owner changing the fee, or a
decision that the fee is waived for some class of customer. Neither exists today.
