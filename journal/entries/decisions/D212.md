**D212 · 2026-09-16 · D198 · Customer-facing fixes first; the portal flag is the last step of the redo, not the first.**

**D198 · 2026-09-16 · The LPT website redo does its customer-facing half first, and the portal flag is the last step, not the first.**

Decided by Zabz (development decision, not an owner decision), after the 2026-09-16 website-redo audit.

**The order:** (1) stop publishing falsehoods - the fabricated FAQ address, the Google sign-in 404, the hours;
(2) make the computer-finder funnel work end to end, including the prod webhook and a staff view; (3) make the
portal's own sign-in reachable by widening the customer allowlist and moving the five refused actions to
customer-scoped procedures; (4) then, and only then, turn `VITE_FEATURE_CUSTOMER_PORTAL` on for production.

**Why, and what was rejected:** the obvious move was to flip the portal flag, since the account build is already
deployed to production behind it. Rejected because turning it on today would hand customers a login page that
403s (the allowlist refuses every `auth.customer*` procedure), a portal where order/pay/message/logout are all
FORBIDDEN, and a Google button that 404s. That is a launch that behaves like a dead end, and the damage would be
to customer trust rather than to uptime. **A feature flag is a launch control, not a way to defer a broken
path.**

**Also decided:** the promotion of `test` -> `main` (102 commits, never attempted) happens only after the
`systemLog` guard is ported into source, because promoting before that ships a public write endpoint to
production. And the mock-data fallback in `useFaqs` is to be treated as a defect class across the repo, not as
one bug: a catch that renders invented content must not be reachable in production.
