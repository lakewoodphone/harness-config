**D152 · 2026-09-15 · Owner-queue shelf life, alert lifecycle bound to subsystem success, SMS off with the app as the surfaced channel.**

**Decision (owner's, 2026-09-15): owner SMS stay off, and the urgent ones are surfaced in the app.**
His words: "Keep SMS off, and I surface the urgent ones in the app." `data/OWNER_SMS_KILL_SWITCH`
stays. The badge is the surface: it now carries the *live* set -- 20 held rows, 2 critical + 11
urgent, with headlines naming what is waiting -- instead of 42 items of which 40 were dead
briefings. Nothing may be sent outbound; the delivery check now says so in words.

**Decision: owner messages get a shelf life by reason.** briefing 36 h (superseded by the next
one), comms_freshness/sync_breaker 24 h, ask_owner 7 d, urgent_email 14 d, anything unknown 7 d.
Applied only to `held`/`queued` rows; the change is `status` + `status_note`, never a delete.
Rationale: an unbounded queue is not conservatism, it is noise that blinds every reader of it, and
the reason-class horizons are the narrowest rule that keeps money mail (the one class where hiding
a row is worse than carrying it) while dropping text whose value expired the same day.

**Decision: an alert clears when its subsystem succeeds, at the single point every outcome
passes** (`_record_subsystem` -> `clear_owner_sync_alerts`), rather than at each call site. Two
call sites would have drifted; one cannot.

**Decision: `check_delivery` measures the channel the owner chose, not a channel he closed.**
CRITICAL is reserved for "no channel to him at all" (SMS off AND no/!stale badge payload). A closed
channel is reported as a decision, with the switch file's own path and mtime as evidence.

**Decision: `_run_reminder_poll` delegates to `personal_ops.process_due_reminders`.** Satisfying
an existing import is additive; rewriting agent_bus.py while another session edits it is not. Safety
rests on the implementation's own defaults: `dashboard` marks without sending, and only a reminder
whose channel is `sms`/`both` reaches `send_owner_update`, which passes the kill switch.

**Decision (mine, recorded): patches are committed path-scoped while a second session is live in
the same repo** -- anchors validated, hunk headers read, explicit `git add <paths>`, never `-A`.
