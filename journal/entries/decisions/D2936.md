**D2936 · 2026-10-05 · The question card owns ONE progress display and the save chip moves fine-grained, but the card does NOT claim account storage from a view it cannot see.**

DECIDED 2026-10-05, branch overhaul/question-card, while rebuilding participant-column.tsx.

1. ONE progress display, two labelled halves, read from the view.
 `QuestionProgress` renders `view.moduleLabel` (already "Question 7 of 15") beside the module name,
 and `describeProgress(view)` ("Question 34 - at least 88, up to 174") on the same line, over one
 bar driven by `view.percentOfFloor`. Two labelled halves of one display, not two displays: FIXLIST
 rows 15/16 ask for exactly this pair and the card may not compute either number.

2. TWO different numbers may still be on screen, and that is deliberate.
 `assessment.tsx` renders its own "0 / 88 answered" badge in the participant overview above the
 card. That is a second definition of the total and it is that file's to fix. The card renders no
 second count of its own any more; the duplicate that remains is one file up and is reported as such.

3. The save chip takes an explicit mode, and defaults to the account wording.
 `describeSaveChip(view, mode)` writes "Not saved to your account yet - keep this tab open." for the
 account mode (the sentence the brief requires) and "Not saved on this device yet - keep this tab
 open." for device mode. The mode exists because the app runs without DATABASE_URL by default, where
 every write is device-local and an account claim would be a fresh lie in the other direction. THE
 MODE IS CURRENTLY ALWAYS "account", because `assessment.tsx` passes only an `isDraftSaved` boolean
 and no persistence mode, and the component may not import that file's sync state. A one-field
 additive change to assessment.tsx (or `getSessionView`) would let it be truthful in local-only mode.

4. `isDraftSaved ? "synced" : null` is the whole of the mapping into `SyncStatus`.
 `isDraftSaved` is `Boolean(currentRecord)` - "a saved record exists" - which is NOT "the server
 acknowledged the last write". The chip can therefore say "Saved to your account" one write earlier
 than strictly justified. It maps to `synced` rather than being ignored because the view's other
 value for an answered session, `null`, would print "Not saved to your account yet - keep this tab
 open" on a screen where the header says "Saved to your account", which is the contradiction of
 FIXLIST row 6 rebuilt inside the same viewport. The residual optimism is recorded here rather than
 papered over.

5. Nothing was deleted to make the layout shorter.
 The About-you form, the why-this-question panel, the adaptive follow-up trail, the four generated-
 artefact statuses, the AI diagnostics and the emerging-picture cards are all still rendered - moved
 into three collapsed disclosures below the answer flow. The only strings removed are internal
 classification labels ("Question Guidance", "Construct focus", "Shared baseline") from the always-
 visible chrome, and `getParticipantGuidance` from this file because it re-derived a progress count.

6. The scale stays five stacked options. DO NOT "fix" it into a horizontal row.
 A horizontal five-up row would save about 240px and is the only change that could bring the first
 option meaningfully closer to the fold inside this file, and it was rejected: FIXLIST section 5
 item 8 says the question typography is the best design decision in the product and should not be
 restyled, and five long scale labels ("Strongly unappealing", "Not important to me") cannot sit
 side by side at 1280px without wrapping into a worse layout. The fold is a shell problem; compressing
 the answers to fix it would trade a real readability win for a number.
