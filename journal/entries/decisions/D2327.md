**D2327 · 2026-09-22 · Household thermometers: weekday = Exergen temporal artery (~); Shabbos = non-electric only, Geratherm Galinstan glass + FeverScan strip (~).**

Owner asked 2026-09-22 to buy two thermometers for the house: one "very accurate and fast" for weekdays, one usable on Shabbos. He said "like you recommended" - no such recommendation existed anywhere (journal search: no match; secretary memory search: 0 rows; .dsh/sessions grep: no match; VS Code workspaceStorage: none found). So the pick is made fresh and recorded here.

SHABBOS PICK (the halachically load-bearing half):
- Halachic position, three independent sources: R' Ovadia Yosef (halachayomit.co.il HalachaID=3868) "there is no room to take one's temperature with an electronic/digital thermometer"; Israeli Rabbanut answer via yeshiva.org.il (id=88116) "use a chemical thermometer that changes colour... there is a halachic problem with turning on a digital thermometer"; asktherav.com 4759 "always best to use a non electric thermometer, such as a forehead thermometer strip or an oral glass thermometer"; anash.org / R' Chaim Hillel Raskin: measuring is generally prohibited on Shabbos (uvdin dechol, Mishna Berura 306:34) but permitted for health needs, and a NON-electric thermometer may be used. medicalhalacha.org 2024-03-07: a digital thermometer is not muktzeh (it is a keli with permitted functions), so the problem is the electricity/measurement, not carrying it.
- Therefore: non-electric only. Buy (a) Geratherm mercury-free glass oral thermometer, Galinstan column, item 20010, $8.50, macgill.com - the accurate one, ~3 min oral; (b) Veridian FeverScan forehead strip, liquid crystal, 15-second readout, no batteries, reusable, ~$6-10 (Amazon B09SHBMN6W) - the fast screen. ~$15 total.
- Optional upgrade if accuracy on Shabbos matters: TempaDOT 5122 chemical dot-matrix oral/axillary strips, +-0.2F, 60 s oral, box of 100 = $52.75 sterile / $37.75 non-sterile (medonthego.com); NexTemp 1112 box of 100 = $37.75. This is literally the "chemical thermometer that changes colour" the Rabbanut names, and it is far more accurate than the forehead strip.
- Rejected: "ModChom Shabbat" (nirshamti.co.il/en/modchom-shabbat) - a real Shabbos-approved infrared unit, but it is built for institutions (shul/hospital/mikveh entrances), fires every 4 s regardless of who is there, and is not a home fever thermometer. Rejected: any digital/ear/infrared device for Shabbos - mainstream pesak says no.

WEEKDAY PICK: Exergen TemporalScanner TAT-2000C temporal-artery, ~2 s, all ages, no probe covers, ~$30-45 (amazon.com B0FXN4S46H listed at $29.98). Runner-up if he wants ear: Braun ThermoScan 7 IRT6520, 2 s, age-precision, ~$45-60. Evidence: PMC4008772, 294 children - mean difference vs rectal was 0 degC for temporal artery, 0.49 degC tympanic, 0.34 degC infrared skin; but all three had only 22-41% sensitivity for >38 degC, so no non-rectal device is a substitute for rectal when it matters. Rectal digital (Vicks SpeedRead / Braun PRT2000, ~$10-15, 8-10 s) remains the reference standard.

DECIDED (not an owner question): Shabbos devices are non-electric; weekday device is temporal artery. Owner question raised in-session: only whether he orders them himself or I place the order (money = his).
