**D157 · 2026-09-15 · No in-house soldered iPad charge-port repairs (A1893 job declined).**

2026: owner declined the A1893 bent-charging-port job after the pin-count/difficulty findings (L1517, L1518). Standing read: soldered iPad charge-port flexes are OUT of scope on the Lakewood bench - the part is ~\ but the job is a glued, soldered 12-16 pad array under a display removal, with a real pad-ripping risk, and it is not worth the bench time.

Practical consequences:
- When a customer brings an iPad 5/6/7/8/9, Air 1-5 or Pro 9.7/10.5/12.9 with a dead/loose/bent Lightning port, do NOT route it as an in-house repair. Either decline the port itself, or quote the whole device (trim/refurb floor) instead.
- Do not re-research this and do not re-raise it as an option unless the owner asks. The outsource path (mail-in board lab, e.g. VCC Board Repairs advertises B2B shop pricing) stays on file if he ever wants it.
- Still worth doing in-house and unchanged by this: diagnosis (rule out Tristar/PMIC and dirty/loose port), and the no-board-work case - if only the outer shell is bent and pins/pads are intact, straighten and retest.
