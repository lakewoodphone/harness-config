**D2330 · 2026-09-22 · Shabbos thermometer hechsher: none exists from OU/Star-K/CRC (verified on Star-K's own device list); the governing sources are SA OC 306:7 + MB 306:36 and Halachipedia, not the Rabbanut.**

Owner pushed back on my Rabbanut citation (2026-09-22): "rabbanut is a drop to the left, find approval from ou, star k, crc or some other rabbinic authority a drop to the right of them." He is right, and the correction is about SOURCING, not about the conclusion.

FINDING 1 - no such hechsher exists, and I verified it at the source. Star-K's own Sabbath Mode page (fetched live via firecrawl 2026-09-22 ~14:3x EDT, https://www.star-k.org/sabbath) states: "There are two STAR-K certified companies that make such devices: GramaChip Technologies and Movinglife... GramaChip - Stairlift, Platform Lift, Ceiling Lift, Stair Climber, Recliner Chair, Suction Machine, Nebulizer, Hospital Beds, and Nurse Call Systems. Movinglife - Shabbatto Folding Scooter." NO THERMOMETER. Star-K's device program exists for ELECTRIC devices used by cholim on the principle of grama - a completely different problem from a passive thermometer. OU and CRC certify Shabbos-mode APPLIANCES only (GE ovens/fridges via Zman Technologies' "Shabbos Keeper", OU & CRC certified; CRC's own Shabbos Mode page at consumer.crckosher.org is entirely appliances). So there is no OU-, Star-K- or CRC-certified Shabbos thermometer to buy, because no agency certifies one - there is nothing in a battery-free instrument to certify.

FINDING 2 - the right sources were available all along and I did not use them:
- Shulchan Aruch O.C. 306:7 + Mishna Berura 306:36: measuring on Shabbos is an issur d'rabbanan, permitted for a mitzva such as refuah. This is the PRIMARY source and the strongest anchor.
- Halachipedia (the standard American yeshivish reference), "Medicine on Shabbat", Fever section, verbatim: "For a fever, all oral medications may be taken. A mercury thermometer may be used."
- medicalhalacha.org (American medical-halacha project), "Measuring a Hospital Visitor's Temperature on Shabbos": taking a temperature for refuah is generally permitted; "the use of battery-powered digital thermometers may well involve the Melachos of Boneh and Makeh bPatish, and causing the numbers to appear on the screen of the device may violate the prohibition of Koseiv." i.e. the DIGITAL device is the problem, not the measurement.
Net: mainstream American yeshivish pesak lands in exactly the place the Rabbanut letter did - because it is SA 306:7 + MB 306:36, not because the Rabbanut said it.

FINDING 3 - the one product that DOES carry an explicit approval from right of the Rabbanut: The Key Belt "Shabbos Thermometer", $12, gallium/Galinstan glass, no mercury, no batteries, "approved for use on Shabbat when necessary by the rabbis of the city of Bnei Brak, Israel" (thekeybelt.com/product/shabbos-thermometer, verified live). Ships from Israel, +972548444697, no US distribution - cannot meet a 3-day window, which is why it was not the answer on Tuesday.
DECISION: keep the NexTemp 100-pack (B08BNMVLKX, battery-free chemical indicator) as the Shabbos unit - it needs no hechsher precisely because it has no electric circuit. If he wants a product bearing an explicit approval, the Bnei Brak gallium unit is the only one that exists.
