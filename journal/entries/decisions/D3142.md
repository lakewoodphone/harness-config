**D · 2026-10-18 · The waived-labour job's media is 30 to keep and free to borrow, and the machine's fate is the customer's call, not a parts harvest.**

**Owner, verbatim:** *"give her the usb tell her its' 30 if she wants it or she can just borrow it for free and ask if we should give back the computer or throw it away, we have no use for any parts"*

- **The labour stays waived** — `price: null`, `payment_state: waived`, no work order. That is unchanged from
 D3135.
- **The stick is 30 if she keeps it, or free to borrow.** This **supersedes the 10 adder** recorded in
 **D3135** (which came from the Greenfeld case: *"data-only 75, 85 with our USB stick"*). **30 is the
 owner's number for this job** — quoted from him, not derived, and it applies to the medium only.
- **"No use for any parts"** — the machine is not to be stripped, so the only question to put to her is
 **return it or discard it**. That is a customer decision and the shop has no preference; the secretary is
 asked to put it to her rather than the shop deciding it.
- **The label, printed** (Brother QL-820NWB serial **H3G354844**, 29 mm DK-2210 roll, **25.3 mm of tape**,
 transport `exit 0`, printer observed going **READY → PRINTING**): heading `TANTE PEREL`, then `RAITZIK` /
 `GET HER NUMBER` / `USB IS READY` / `$30 TO KEEP IT,` / `OR BORROW FREE` / `ASK: GIVE THE PC` /
 `BACK, OR BIN IT?` / `NO PARTS NEEDED`.
- **`GET HER NUMBER` is on there because we do not have it**: the case record's `phone` field is empty, and
 it has been since intake on 2026-10-14. The label is the instruction to close that gap.

**Why 30 and not the 10 already on record:** a formula would have said 10. The owner said 30. When the
owner states a number, the record takes his number and the earlier derivation is superseded by name —
not quietly averaged with it.
