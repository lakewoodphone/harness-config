**D2939 · 2026-10-05 · Never-offline design: package verified, offsite copy is step 2, survivor needs an 80 GB-class disk.**

**Decided 2026-10-06 ~03:3x UTC, ZABZ-YOGA, during the office outage (H3250/H3252).**

**1. The resilience package is verified before it is relied on.** `F-verification-and-errata.md`
re-derives the load-bearing claims of `00`-`E` independently and records four errata; the one that
mattered was the false "nightly offsite to R2" claim (corrected in B by the sibling instance and, by
me, in C, which still carried the false row after B was fixed). Standing practice from this: **grep a
finished package for every claim that would change a decision if false**, because a correction in one
annex does not propagate.

**2. The offsite copy is step 2, not round-off.** An encrypted restic copy of the 12.17 GB authority
to the existing R2 bucket, driven from an already-paid-for Hetzner box, is the cheapest mitigation of
the largest exposure in the system (a single disk holds the only copy, including the owner's
irreplaceable 805,275-row session corpus). It does not depend on the survivor host, the lease, or the
DNS switch, so it must not be sequenced behind them.

**3. The survivor host must be a dedicated VPS with an 80 GB-class disk.** Measured: the two existing
Hetzner boxes have 20-21 GB free, and one complete snapshot is 16 GB with "no small variant" — so
neither can hold a snapshot plus a working copy, and a shared host could take the shop's website down
during a survivor restart. This supersedes annex C's "single-digit EUR" without a size.

**4. The corrected order of work:** external heartbeat **and** backup-freshness alert off the box →
offsite copy → rehearsed whole-server restore with a recorded pass/fail → survivor VPS + 1-minute
replication + DNS-switch promotion (with the lease/arbiter/fencing before any split) → one shop visit
(UPS, switch/strip inspection, Tailscale on the HA host, HA on permanent power, the plug's schedule
read to settle the maglock question).

**5. Two things stay mine and are not owner decisions:** the heartbeat, the freshness alert, the
restore rehearsal, and committing the box-only config. The money and the visit are the owner's.
