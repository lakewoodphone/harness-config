## D215 — the owner's car-insurance folder is a start-here index plus a decision paper, not a pile of findings

**Decided 2026-09-16.** `personal-secretary-mvp/docs/personal-insurance/` now holds:

- `README.md` — the start-here page: the 60-second state of the policy, the one open decision, the five
 questions to ask the agent, a map of every file, and where the raw evidence lives. Written so a
 future session answers "where do we stand on the car insurance" in under a minute.
- `car-insurance-case-2026-09-16.md` — the case file: the three questions he asked, the policy, the
 money trail, the NJ inspection law, the timeline, the evidence index, the action list.
- `coverage-decision-2026-09-16.md` — the decision: collision+comprehensive on or off, with costs,
 pros, cons, the arithmetic, and a recommendation.
- the declarations page, the NJ insurance ID card, and the recovered May 2026 binding/research set.

**Why an index and a decision paper as separate files.** He asked to "document everything fully so we
can always just pull it back up again easily". The case file is the record of what HAPPENED; the
decision paper is the record of what was DECIDED and why, and it has to stand alone, because the
reasoning is what gets relitigated. The README is what a fresh session reads first, so neither of the
other two has to be found by guessing a filename.

**The recommendation on the record** (so it is not re-derived): put collision and comprehensive back
on. ~$110–130 to reinstate for the remaining 56 days, then ~$420 per 6 months — about 4–5% of the
car's value a year, which is a fair price, not a bargain and not a rip-off. Expected value is
negative, as for any policy; the decision turns on the balance sheet (≈$2,027 liquid, four
consecutive loss months, $52,021.91 of owner loans, $4,325 still owed to his mother on the car) and
on a $70/month premium being budgetable where a $17,000 loss is not.

**Raw evidence stays at `~/phone/insurance-plymouth-rock/evidence/`** — that directory is explicitly
non-load-bearing (it is the phone desk). The record is the git-tracked folder.
