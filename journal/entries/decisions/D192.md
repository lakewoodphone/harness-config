**D192 · 2026-09-15 · Payroll arrears are Gusto's own stated number, not a reconstruction; the final week's run is not optional; and filing ownership goes to the queue.**

**The decisions taken in this session (2026-09-15, ZABZ-YOGA), each with the rule that produced it.**

**1. The arrears figure is $2,208.32, and it is Gusto's number, not mine.**
Gusto's 2026-08-28 confirmation states a $2,208.32 debit ($1,685.15 net + $523.17 tax) for the run that was
then blocked and canceled on 2026-09-04, and the bank feed proves no debit ever posted. The decomposition
($1,780.91 net wages + $427.41 IRS/NJ withholding) is that same money, not a second estimate. *Rule kept:*
when a payroll processor states in writing what it will debit, that is the number of record — do not
reconstruct the pay periods from labels. My first reconstruction of which week was "missed" was wrong and
the confirmation emails fixed it in one read, because Aug 24–30's total = the missed week ($1,472.21) plus
the half-week shortfall ($736.11) exactly.

**2. The Sep 7–13 run scheduled to debit 2026-09-16 is the employee's final week of pay, not an optional
expense.** Canceling it does not save the money; it converts a payroll into an unpaid wage. New Jersey's
rule (N.J.S.A. 34:11-4.3) is the regular payday for the period containing the termination, and quit and
fired are identical in NJ — so a Sep 14 firing on a Thursday cycle means Sep 17/24.

**3. The catch-up must be a real, taxed payroll, not an off-platform payment.** Paid as a Zelle or cash the
2026 W-2 is wrong and a wage goes unreported. Because the unpaid weeks are all in the still-open Q3 2026,
Gusto's correct mechanism is an off-cycle payroll.

**4. The owner question is filing ownership, not the money, and it went to the queue as row #64** — one
question, three options, one recommended — rather than a document handed to him. The recommendation on the
record: let Sep 16 run, dismiss him in Gusto, catch-up next week, elect Gusto to file 2026 (~$205), and pay
the bookkeeper only for what Gusto refuses to do.

**5. Documentation is the deliverable that survives; the conversation is not.** The numbers and their
sources are in `docs/finance/gusto-closeout-and-weinberg-final-pay-2026-09-15.md` and the rules in
`gusto-closeout-research-2026-09-15.md`, so the next session does not re-derive a $2,208.32 that has already
been proven twice.
