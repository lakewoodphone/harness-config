**D2335 · 2026-09-23 · Carried: D163 landed-cost rule applied to the case - the comparison benchmark is the cheapest comparable US unit, not the incumbent, and not list price.**

Standing rule D163 (2026-09-15) required landed cost rather than list price after the owner paid ~\ of DHL duty. Applied to the rugged case on 2026-09-23. One sharpening worth keeping: **the benchmark for a landed-cost comparison is the cheapest comparable unit already available in the US, not our incumbent price.** Comparing an Alibaba landed cost of \.30/unit to our \.95 incumbent shows a \.65 saving and makes the import look compelling; comparing it to the \.55 two-layer case already on Amazon shows \.25 and makes it look pointless. The first comparison is arithmetically true and decision-useful only if no US substitute exists. Whenever D163 is applied to a commodity part, price the US substitute first and compare against that.
