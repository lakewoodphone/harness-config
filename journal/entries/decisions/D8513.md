Written down at **23:53Z** as `kept=7 / deferred=25`. Matched at **01:25Z**. Both numbers, first in-peak tick,

### All four conditions checked, not assumed

- **deferred**: `deferrals_db=1`, `deferrals_total=1`, `ticks_30m=1` in the observer at 01:26:23Z;
- **work continued**: 62 LLM calls and **2,129,675 input tokens** between 01:00:03Z and 01:26:44Z, `rows_30m=66`;
- **no claim churn**: `lifecycle_30m=0`, `claim_rel_journal=0` — the release-after-10-minutes worry did not
 materialise, because a department that has not ticked is holding no claim to release;
- **no errors**: zero error-level lines since the tick, and the surrounding machinery (healing sweep, error

And the policy is right at the hour it runs, not only at a rehearsal instant: at 01:15:31Z the deployed code
returned `run_now=False defer=164` for `dept_engineering` and `dept_world_index`, and `run_now=True` for
`orchestrator` and `dept_comms_relationships`.

### What this proves and what it does not

**Proves**: the mechanism works end to end in production — gate fires, magnitude as predicted, exempt set
intact, work continues, and the deferral lands in `activity_log` where it can be queried rather than merely
printed.

**Does not prove the money.** One deferred tick is a mechanism, not a saving. The saving is week-against-week
against the frozen baseline, with the success test fixed *before* the week: cost down, peak share down,
`tokens_per_task` unchanged, `tick_error_pct` not above 9.65 %.

**Also untested**: that it keeps deferring for the whole block, and that the 06:00–10:00 block behaves the same.
`peaksnapshot` writes the verdict at 04:06Z, and the two-snapshot design exists because "deferred for two ticks
then stopped" is invisible in a single end-of-block reading.

### The lesson worth keeping

Four hours between the prediction and the event, with the number written down and the observers detached from
the conversation. That is what made this result mean something: a **pre-registered** prediction cannot be
explained away afterwards, and an observer that outlives the session is the only kind that can report on a
seven-hour window.

The two failures on the way — a watcher that expired before the first in-peak tick, and a verification tool that
would have called an empty window a failure — were both *instrument* failures, and both were caught because the
instrument reported its own coverage and its own incompleteness. **Build the observer so that a quiet result
carries its provenance.**
