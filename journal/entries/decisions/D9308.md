**D9308 · 2026-10-21 · D-36 a written continuity plan, a named successor, and a published thirty-day release.**

D-20261021-36 records the owner's answer to Q36 on 2026-10-21 ("2"): a written continuity plan, a
named successor, credentials documented in a sealed envelope, and a published process for releasing
every phone over thirty days if the business stops. No automatic loosening built into the product.

The load-bearing parts, because they are easy to drop and the file names them: the envelope holds the
broker's administrative secret, the anchor account's recovery path and the account holding the shop's
default anchor - a plan without the keys is a letter of intent; the successor is a person with a name
in it, not "the estate"; the escrow has a review DATE in the yearly cycle, because an envelope nobody
checks is a paragraph; the published procedure has three steps a customer can read.

The rejection of the automatic sunset is recorded WITH its cost: if nobody acts, phones stay sealed
and no customer recovers them. Accepted, because an automatic release turns any long outage or missed
hosting bill into a field of unfiltered phones, which is the failure the product exists to prevent.
Degradation is to a local filter, never to nothing (D-06).

Committed 0d85850.
