**D238 · 2026-09-15 · I own the shop lights - the map, the truthful names, and the dead room-lights automation.**

**Decision (2026-09-15): I own the shop lights. Here is the map, the naming, and the dead automation that was fixed.**

The owner handed over the lights directly: "you have to manage the lights, so the main light is on zigbee, and i just plugged in the 2 zigbee plugs for the other 2 lights". He then confirmed, from the Zigbee2MQTT frontend, which physical light each plug drives, and confirmed both "blinked" when I switched them — so the mapping is his statement and my test agreeing, not an inference.

| physical light | Zigbee2MQTT name (now) | IEEE | Home Assistant entity | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| main light | Control Room Main Light | 0x1cc089fffe24f54b | `switch.deserted_house_lights` | live, SONOFF ZBMINI R2 relay |
| light on top of the desk | **Desk Light** | 0xffffb40e06003a17 | `switch.smart_plug_waiting_room` | live, Third Reality 3RSP019BZ plug |
| larger room light | **Larger Room Light** | 0xffffb40e0600382b | `switch.control_room_strip_lights` | live, Third Reality 3RSP019BZ plug |

**Names made truthful, safely.** The desk plug was called "Smart Plug Waiting room" — wrong room entirely — and the larger-room plug had no name at all, so Home Assistant displayed `0xffffb40e0600382b`. Both renamed via `zigbee2mqtt/bridge/request/device/rename`, which was first proven not to move HA entity_ids (see the lesson), so the eleven references in `phoenix_security.yaml` needed no change.

**A whole lights automation was dead.** `access_control.yaml` contains a ROOM LIGHTS (ON/OFF) section that turns the control-room lights on when someone is present and off when nobody is. It targeted `light.control_room_main_light` and `light.control_room_strip_lights`. **Neither entity has ever existed** — the lights are Zigbee devices exposed as `switch.` entities. Each call sat behind `{{ states('light....') not in ['unknown','unavailable'] }}`, which is `False` for a non-existent entity, so the block silently did nothing, forever. Measured before the fix: `states('light.control_room_main_light')` = `unknown`, same for the other. After repointing all four guards and targets at the real entities and reloading: all three guards resolve `True`. This is the third instance of the same failure shape in one day, alongside the two camera still streams and the whole clip path.

**Availability enabled for the seven mains devices** so a lamp cannot masquerade as on. Immediately exposed two phantom lights HA had been reporting as `on`: "Control room smart light" (0x7cb94c78515c0000, silent 76 days) and the plug at 0xffffb40e060040c4 (silent 361 days). Deliberately NOT enabled for the Kwikset lock or the three Samjin door sensors' batteries.

Not touched, and not mine to assume: what the lights should actually *do* over a day. The occupancy block already turns two of them off when the shop empties; whether the desk light belongs in that group, and whether there should be a closing-time sweep, is the owner's call and is the question I asked.
