## D103 · 2026-09-14 · Owner rejected $135.80/unit as "not cheap sourcing" and commissioned a sweep; the QC gate was corrected for a cased, car-powered device

**Owner instruction, verbatim:** *"find better deals, this isn't what i meant by cheap sourcing. scour the internet,
maybe check alibaba and more and do serious research where people get good deals on this from"* — plus *"update the
case files and docs and sync everything with all the new info."*

**He was right, and the arithmetic says so.** The 5-lot looked cheap per unit and is not:

| Option | Per unit | Grade | Returns |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5-lot (Sage Sustainable Electronics) | $135.80 | Fair — minor screen flaws allowed, battery only ≥80%, third-party parts possible | 60 days, seller pays shipping |
| 2-lot | $164.50 | Good | — |
| single | $159.00 | Fair | — |
| single | $189.00 | Excellent | — |
| his previous seller | $129.99 | "good condition" | **none — last one** |

**The deciding number is cost per *usable* unit, not per unit:** one failure in five makes the lot
**$679 ÷ 4 = $169.75 per usable unit**, worse than every alternative. Its one real advantage is the 60-day
seller-paid return — the $129.99 single takes no returns at all.

**QC gate corrected (a real error of mine).** The first version demanded **battery ≥85%** and treated cosmetics as a
grade factor — written as though this were a handset for a shop window. It is not: the device **lives inside a
rugged case** (back and all four edges hidden, so scratches, case cracks and missing covers are invisible in
service) and **lives on car power**. Revised to **battery ≥80% plus a swelling check** (swelling is a safety item in
a hot car, not a spec item), with **body cosmetics explicitly excluded**, and the **screen**, the **eSIM install**
and the **function sweep** as the checks that actually decide a unit. Recorded with the old rule beside the new one
so the change is visible rather than silent.

**Commissioned, in flight at the time of writing — three parallel research agents:**
1. Asia-direct: Alibaba, Made-in-China, 1688, DHGate, HK/Shenzhen trade lists — published per-unit prices, MOQ,
 whether anyone advertises US-warehoused stock, and a worked landed-cost example (de minimis is gone; duty,
 freight and formal entry all apply).
2. US dealer/ITAD channels: where professionals actually buy — ITAD sellers, B2B marketplaces and their gates,
 dealer forums for first-hand per-unit numbers, and the NJ/NY/Brooklyn wholesale distributors.
3. The shop's own advantage: buying cracked, no-power, iCloud-locked and carrier-locked units and repairing them —
 with screen/battery/port parts costs, a worked example, and an explicit ruling on which routes are **legally
 closed** (activation-locked devices can never be legitimately returned to service) so they are never revisited.

**Files synced with the new state:** the pipeline doc (rewritten, then §6 replaced with the open-purchase status and
the comparison above), the QC gate (revised end to end), the box spec (revision note: the stock Uline box plus
printed sticker replaced the custom rigid box), the quote drafts (now optional), and a **new pointer page in the
deployment repo** — `personal-secretary-mvp/deploy/waze-mdm/docs/product-state-2026-09-14.md` — so a session working
on the MDM side cannot contradict the naming, the single tier, or the price, and knows that dropping tier 2 removed
the per-model engineering rather than leaving it outstanding.
