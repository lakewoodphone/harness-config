**D331 · 2026-09-20 · What the badge may go red about (session_archive + attention_debt severity), and how owner row #79's lost amounts were restored.**

**D · 2026-09-20 · ZABZ-YOGA — What the badge is allowed to go red about, and how a corrupted owner row is repaired**

Three decisions, all made on measurement, all recorded here so they are not relitigated.

**1. `session_archive` severity.** HIGH only when a machine is **UP** (presence inside 4h from
`next_stage_node_reports`) and its last **heartbeat** export is older than the 3h window. A fleet that is
off is INFO and says so. A machine that has never posted a heartbeat is reported `unmeasured`, and if
nothing is fresh the check keeps the old conservative HIGH with the missing signal named — an unreadable
signal is never allowed to look like health. Change age (`dsh_sessions.updated_at`) is context and metrics,
never severity. Commit `a75f601` on `secratary:~/ceo-kernel`.

**2. `attention_debt` severity.** Fresh critical → CRITICAL; fresh urgent, a question past the 336h
window → HIGH; `normal`-urgency waiting items are counted and named in the summary and never set severity.
Rationale and evidence in L2086.

**3. Owner queue row #79 (sukkah frame, unpermitted).** P265 item 3 says the lost amounts must be
re-derived from the cited sources and the row rewritten — never guessed. Done exactly that: every figure
now in the row comes from `_scratch/shlock-permit/PERMIT-RESEARCH.md` §3-§5 and
`lpt-hub/research/miscellaneous/2026-09-17-shlock-permit-research.md` — $2,000 per violation
(N.J.A.C. 5:23-2.31(e)2), each week after notice a separate offense, the permit $60 min + $85 subcode min
+ $40 min plan review + $35 zoning + $0.016/cu ft surcharge ≈ $150-300 all in, the $1,500-$4,000 stamping
figure explicitly marked as corroborated by nothing, and the facts-first step given as **about $50-75**
(the $35 zoning application named in the code plus the written inquiry) rather than the damaged `$`-lost
string. Written with **bound parameters** only, and the row was read back to prove 16 dollar signs landed.
Row #33 (same defect, a price band) and #38/#86 are left as found, with their stored strings, for a
separate decision.
