**D2655 · 2026-09-28 · Withdrawn: age-based pruning of dsh_session_events; exclude the FTS shadow tables from snapshots instead.**

On 2026-09-28 this seat analysed a full disk on secratary, correctly found dsh_session_events (805,275 rows + FTS5 shadow tables) to be the largest growing table, and was about to propose age-based pruning.

The owner then stated that VS Code and DSH session data is the most valuable data the business has or will have -- training corpus he intends to keep for years.

The pruning proposal is WITHDRAWN. Decision: keep every row. Reduce the cost of the COPY, not the size of the record:
 1. dsh_session_events_fts / _fts_data / _fts_content / _fts_docsize are FTS5 shadow tables, derivable from dsh_session_events.raw, which app/services/dsh_session_ingest.py rebuilds -> exclude them from backup snapshots and rebuild on restore.
 2. Or snapshot the database daily rather than every 6 hours, keeping the 6-hourly cadence for non-session data.
 3. Or move the corpus to dedicated storage and address it there.
None of these deletes a row. Evidence for the table sizes and the disk incident: journal P2553 and personal-secretary-mvp/docs/operations/disk-analysis-2026-09-28.md.
