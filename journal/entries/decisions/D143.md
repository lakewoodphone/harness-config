**D143 · 2026-09-14 · Fios home network record lives in personal-secretary-mvp/docs/network/ and the fact in memories 19173.**

**Decided 2026-09-14** (mine to decide — file layout and store choice are development decisions, not
the owner's): the evidence record of the owner's home installation is a **directory per installation**
under `personal-secretary-mvp/docs/network/<isp>-<place>-<date>/`, holding `README.md` (facts split
into measured / quoted / inferred), `MANIFEST.json` (sha256 + dimensions per photo) and `photos/`
(originals, commit-ready). The machine-readable fact goes to `memories` on the authority (id 19173),
dated follow-ups go to `tasks`, and nothing durable is kept only in the conversation.

**Why here and not `~/phone` or `equipment/`:** `~/phone` is a deliberate non-git desk for
phone-started sessions, `docs/` is what other machines actually read and sync, and `*.jpg` is not
gitignored (only `*.png` is), so the photos survive as real files. A future session adding another
site (office, Yisroel's mini) should copy this shape rather than invent one.
