**D79 · 2026-09-14 · Refreshing a sign-off snapshot is an attestation, so it has to say what it actually is.**

The checked-in security sign-off snapshot is a **manual**** attestation**: its fields include `reviewer`, `review_method` and `review_notes`, and the committed version read `reviewer: copilot-ci, method: automated, review_notes: "Refresh digests after S-BACK-021 …"`. Regenerating it after a digest change is the established workflow (three previous commits are exactly that), but the script's *default* notes claim a completed manual checklist — so my first refresh signed my name to a security review I had not performed. Corrected immediately: `reviewer: zabz-agent`, `method: automated`, and notes that name exactly what moved and state that **no manual review was re-performed** and that the supply-chain and outbound-HTTP reviews are unchanged from the previous signer.
*Rule:* when a tool fills in an attestation for you, read what it says before you keep it. `git show HEAD:<file>` is how the previous signer's honest wording is recovered — and matching their convention is both easier and truer than matching the default.
