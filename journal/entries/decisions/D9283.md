## Decision 2026-10-21 · The adult phone: integrity is the fixed floor, content and delays are his, and the product advises (Q4)

Owner's answer to Q4, verbatim:

> "3 for sure, that's our whole filter, he sets exactly what can be allowed, what can never be allowed,
> what takes time delays how long they are and so on. obviously we need to advise users in certain
> directions and when they pick things that undermine the filter, we warn them as such."

Recorded as `kosher-filter-ai/docs/decisions/D-20261021-03-integrity-floor-and-advice.md` (commit
`4039555`, gate 17/17, register 51 rows).

1. **The fixed floor on every phone is integrity, never content**: cannot be uninstalled, cannot be
 switched off, cannot be reset around, anchor belongs to the person and never to the shop.
2. **Content, allow lists, deny lists, which changes carry a delay and how long** — all the customer's.
3. **New requirement: an advisory layer.** The product recommends, and warns when a choice undermines the
 filter. Never a refusal, never a silent tightening; the warning is dismissed and the choice plus the
 acknowledgement are recorded and shown back in his own summary, so "I set every last thing myself"
 stays literally true.
4. **The warning catalogue is product copy with an owner**, not a code detail: each entry needs a
 trigger, a sentence in the owner's voice, and a place (setup, change, or the summary), and it is
 versioned.
5. Known triggers already measured in the audit: private browsing inside an allowed browser, VPN and
 proxy apps, installs from anywhere, a loosening delay of zero, no partner at all.

Engineering consequence for me, not a question for him: rules and choices must carry provenance and
acknowledgement so the summary can show "you chose this, we warned you, you kept it".
