**D7430 · 2026-10-18 · The bootloader reset: Enhanced FRP is already in the app but shipping empty - arm it, and add Knox Guard on Samsung.**

Follow-up research, four streams: what stops a recovery/bootloader factory reset from removing the filter?

WHAT A RESET DOES: kills device owner and every policy by design; a system APK survives the userdata wipe
but its DO status does not (OwnersData lives in /data/system/device_owner_2.xml). The child is left with
FRP: the wizard asks for the previous Google account OR the screen lock. Satisfy it and they own an
unfiltered phone; fail and they own a brick.

THE FINDING THAT MATTERS: the lock is ALREADY IN OUR CODE AND SWITCHED OFF. PolicyApplier.kt:533-550
arms DevicePolicyManager.setFactoryResetProtectionPolicy (Enhanced FRP, API 30+) naming the accounts that
may provision the device after a reset. It only fires when R.string.frp_google_account is non-blank; that
string ships EMPTY (res/values/strings.xml:735) and is filled only by the build property LPT_FRP_ACCOUNT
(build.gradle.kts:63,206-207). Every APK built so far has Enhanced FRP inert. Turning it on is one build
property, zero cost, and it converts a reset from an escape into a brick only we can revive.
Caveat to test on a device: plain FRP also accepts the previous SCREEN LOCK, so the lock must be the
parent's or the shop's, not the child's - a provisioning rule, not code.

THE ONE CONTROL THAT BLOCKS THE WIPE ITSELF: Samsung Knox Guard - blocks factory reset, USB, Bluetooth,
NFC, FOTA and binary flashing, and "Factory reset the device. When the device boots up and connects to a
network, Knox Guard activation automatically begins" (docs.samsungknox.com/admin/knox-guard/kbas/kba-1733).
Activates on a phone already in use via a browser URL; release path documented with a 2-day cancel window;
Samsung only, Android 11+; one seat covers 3 years and resellers list it around five dollars per device;
30-device 3-month trial; no enterprise minimum published.

ZERO-TOUCH: "the device automatically provisions itself on first boot, or next factory reset" and "you
can't factory reset a device to remove it from your organization's management". Free, but devices must be
bought from an enterprise reseller (retail cannot be converted), the DPC "must be available from Google
Play" and on Google's compatible list - ours is neither - and Google's permissible-usage policy is
written for enterprise management, so a consumer parental product is a question for Google, not an
assumption. Offline gap: with no network the flow is skipped and the phone self-resets once it reaches
Google.

CUSTOM IMAGE: the only product where the filter literally survives a reset (this is what KosherOS and
KosherCell sell). Costs Play Integrity DEVICE_INTEGRITY -> banking and Wallet stop working, no OEM
updates, warranty void, and on Samsung the Knox e-fuse kills Secure Folder and Samsung Pay permanently.

DETECT IT: the broker already has app/main.py:_run_phone_offline_monitor + partner.send_partner_offline_alert
(soft/hard levels), so a reset makes the phone silent and the parent is told - the same tamper-evident
posture Covenant Eyes sells. The alert channel needs configuring to be worth anything.

RECOMMENDED STACK: (1) Enhanced FRP armed with a shop/parent account - free, already coded; (2) screen
lock owned by the parent/shop - free, a rule; (3) offline alerts - free, coded; (4) Knox Guard on Samsung
- ~5/device/3y; (5) zero-touch only if we start buying devices through a reseller; (6) custom image as a
separate premium tier. Documented in kosher-filter-ai docs/research/ANDROID-ENROLMENT-NO-WIPE-2026-10-18.md
Part 2, pushed b2d41e4.

OPEN DECISION: whose Google account goes into LPT_FRP_ACCOUNT - one shop account for every phone
(recommended: the child can never have it, and the shop can always recover a device) or the parent's
account per device (more private, but a parent who forgets their password loses the phone).
