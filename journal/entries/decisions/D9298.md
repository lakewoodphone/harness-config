**D9298 · 2026-10-21 · The setup is a conversation that produces a form, and the form is what applies.**

aliases: Q27

Owner, verbatim (2026-10-21):

> "3"

Which is: a conversation that produces a form. The assistant talks the customer through the questions, and
what it heard becomes a written summary he reads, edits and accepts before anything applies.

What it decides, condensed:

1. The conversation is how the settings are produced, not how they are applied. Nothing takes effect from
 what the customer says out loud.
2. The form is the contract: the level, floor and band, the chaver and his dials, every delay, the retention
 setting and any image retention, with the red markings on the choices that cost accountability.
3. He may edit any line before accepting, and editing returns him to the relevant question rather than
 starting over.
4. The accepted form is stored, dated, and shown to him again — in his own account and on the phone — when
 he asks why something is blocked or wants to change one line.
5. The parent's signature sits under that form for a child's phone, so the page the shop files and the page
 the customer accepted are the same page.
6. The assistant proposes; the form applies. The form is generated from the same data the phone enforces, so
 page and behaviour cannot drift — which makes the level that currently reaches nothing (work item 933) a
 prerequisite, not an improvement.
