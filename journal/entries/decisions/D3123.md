**D3123 · 2026-10-12 · Chezky Shwartz WO26081312596: set it out for pickup or disposal, print a label, no chase - the shop's parts delay is the reason.**

Owner decision, 2026-10-12, on the Chezky Shwartz case (WO26081312596, LG G3 D852 that could not be revived). Verbatim: *"basically it took us too long to get the parts to be honest, so maybe just mark down that we are putting it out for pickup or disposal and print a label"*.

**What it decides.** The device goes out on the shelf for pickup, and to disposal if it is not claimed. Nothing is sent to the customer — I had drafted a pickup reminder and asked whether to send it; this answer closes the chase rather than postponing it, so the draft was never sent and must not be sent later without a new instruction.

**Why, in his own framing:** the shop's own delay on parts. That matters for how the record reads — the 22 days since the customer was told is not his silence to answer for — so it is written into note 3142, the case file and the sync record in those terms.

**The label**, printed 2026-10-12 18:08 UTC on the Brother QL-820NWB (29 mm roll, no barcode): `#2337 / Chezky Shwartz 732-620-4948 / LG G3 D852 / NOT REPAIRED / READY FOR PICKUP / BALANCE 50`. Deliberately NOT printed on it: "or disposal" (the device is customer-visible and the disposal path is an internal default) and the parts apology (that is context for staff, not for the tape). Wording choice recorded so it is not re-litigated.

**What was deliberately left undone:** waiving the 50. He said mark it down and set it out; he did not say forgive the balance, so `orders.total` still reads 50.00, no payment row exists, and the record names the waiver as an open counter decision rather than assuming either way. Forgiving a written commitment is his to give at the counter.

**Transferable rule:** when the owner names a disposition ("put it out", "dispose of it"), that is the record's job and the label's job, and it is still not permission to send a message. A decision about a device and a decision about outbound words are separate approvals.
