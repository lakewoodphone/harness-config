**D9304 · 2026-10-21 · No money back for a miss - the mechanism is guaranteed, the classification is best effort and said so.**

aliases: Q33

Owner, verbatim (2026-10-21):

> "No filter is guaranteed foolproof. We do our best and constantly improve but can't actually give money
> back."

What it decides, condensed:

1. Nothing is refunded because the filter missed something: a page that got through, or one wrongly blocked, is
 answered with a fix and the release path.
2. Classification is best effort — the product blocks what the customer asked, improves continuously, and
 claims no perfection.
3. The fourteen-day refund is a change of mind, never a remedy for failure, and the copy must not conflate the
 two.
4. The limitations page says it plainly, because the honest sentence is also the defence: we do not guarantee
 nothing gets through, and here is what we do instead.
5. The copy must never merge "it cannot be removed" with "it catches everything"; the legal posture already
 forbids an accuracy percentage.
6. A reported miss becomes a corpus entry, an acknowledgement and a fix in the next update — stated as the
 remedy before he buys.
7. Support never argues what the filter should have caught: record, thank, say when he will hear back.
8. The counter's words match the page: sealed so it cannot be taken off, no promise it never misses, and we fix
 what you report.

Note: the clause this decision first called the mechanism "guaranteed" was narrowed the same night by
D-20261021-33.
