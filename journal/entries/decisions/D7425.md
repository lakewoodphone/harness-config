**D7425 · 2026-10-18 · No published prices on the LPT website - from-pricing only at intake or on request.**

Owner decision, 2026-10-18, owner queue row #558.

His words: "We can't promise pricing on devices because it's so different per device and per month and whatever. So we give up from pricing when you bring your device in, or when you ask gospel. We're not publishing it on the website."

THE RULE, binding from now on: no repair price is ever published on the LPT website. From-pricing is given at intake (device in hand) or when the customer asks. Prices vary per device and per month, so a published number is a promise the shop cannot keep.

WHAT THIS CHANGES
- Queue row #558 asked whether to publish iPhone 13 154.32 / 13 Pro 173.12 as from-prices: answered NO, row answered.
- The live site's iPhone screen page promises "upfront pricing" and shows none. That promise is now the wrong promise: it must say pricing is quoted when the device is brought in or on request. Same correction applies to the public terms copy held in #557.
- Any future request to "publish prices" is a defect, not a proposal: this decision already answers it.

Source of the numbers: owner_decision_queue row 558, answered 2026-10-18 (journal D7425).
