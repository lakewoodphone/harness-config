## D3040 — The public door moves by SUBSET, not whole: dialer. and secretary-ssh. stay on the office box

DECIDED 2026-10-11 during the authority cutover to secratary-cloud. MINE, not the owner's: it is a
sequencing and tooling decision inside work he already directed.

THE FACT THE HANDOFF DID NOT CARRY. `company-dns-failover.py` moves ALL FOUR of the company's public
hostnames in ONE act - its HOSTNAMES list is [ai., api., dialer., secretary-ssh.]. MEASURED from
/etc/cloudflared/config.yml on the office box, those four are NOT one surface; they are a path-based
dispatcher onto four backends, and two of them cannot leave the office box:

 api.abletelsolutions.com -> localhost:8002 company API MOVES
 ai.abletelsolutions.com -> localhost:3000 dashboard MOVES
 ai.abletelsolutions.com ^/phone -> 127.0.0.1:3087 phone redirector MOVES (copied)
 dialer.abletelsolutions.com -> 127.0.0.1:8899 live-voice dialer STAYS
 secretary-ssh.abletelsolutions.com -> ssh://localhost:22 ssh escape hatch STAYS

A move-all would point dialer. and secretary-ssh. at the new tunnel, whose ingress has no rules for
either, and Cloudflare answers 403 for a hostname with no ingress rule - the failure mode
company-dns-failover.py's own header records from its round-4 test. So the subset is not a
convenience; moving dialer. before its backend has moved is a certain outage of the dialer.

WHAT WAS DONE. Two files in harness-config/scripts/mesh/, derived from the survivor's proven copies
and unchanged in their default behaviour:
 company-dns-failover.py gained --hostnames, validated against the known four so a typo cannot
 point a hostname this tool was never written for at a new tunnel.
 company-promote.py gained --hostnames (passed through) and --seed-stamp "" .
The seed checks exist because lpt-survivor-01 was seeded from a development snapshot; they read a
stamp a survivor-seed run writes. secratary-cloud is not a recovered standby, it is the new primary
carrying the live database, so it has no stamp. --seed-stamp "" skips those two checks AND SAYS SO
IN THE OUTPUT, because a check that vanishes silently is how a guard becomes decoration. It is only
safe when the operator has just shipped a compared copy of the live database (Stages 4-5 do exactly
that). It must never be used for a failover onto a standby.

CONSEQUENCE, DELIBERATE AND RECORDED: after the cutover, dialer. and secretary-ssh. remain on the
office box and so remain dependent on its power. That is a smaller and more honest dependency than
the company API and database having one, and it is the next piece of work rather than a defect of
this one. The handoff mentioned the /phone redirector as "no - does not move"; it wrote that to mean
the BACKEND stays, but the route had to come along or ai./phone would 403. It was copied.
