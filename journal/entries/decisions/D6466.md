**D6466 · 2026-10-17 · The CFO may text the owner or his wife without asking; the Audible question is sent.**

Owner, 2026-10-18: *"The cfo doesn't need my permission to send things like that to me or my
wife."*

I had just asked him to approve sending a finance question about an Audible membership to his
wife. The question was unnecessary, and it is the exact friction the corpus names
(`asked-unnecessarily`, 20 sessions): asking a man to authorise something he has already
authorised, and making a person wait hours on a question he asked for.

charge, a subscription nobody remembers, a membership with unused credits -- is authorised to
the OWNER and to his WIFE. No per-message yes, and no "may I send this" back to him.

Recorded at `/root/.owner-text/standing-rules.json` `permissions.cfo_to_owner_or_wife`
(approval_required: false, recipients [owner, wife], with his sentence and the boundaries).
That file is read on every submit by the relay's standing-rule reader, so it is the durable
home rather than this entry. Backed up at `standing-rules.json.bak-20261018`; the reader was
re-verified after install (allows owner_attention_item, still closes the retired digest,
allows family_chat).

WHAT IT DOES NOT COVER, and must not be stretched to: a price, a date, a commitment or a legal
posture in the body (still his, per the outbound hard stop); a message to anyone other than the
owner or his wife; a body composed and then changed.
