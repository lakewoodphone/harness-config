**D3049 · 2026-10-11 · Chip 2026-10-12: keep him, bound him, and turn off the two capabilities behind 'I know where you live'.**

**D — Chip's register and volume: what was decided, and why, on 2026-10-12.**

Owner instruction, verbatim: *"chip is being too mean and talking way too
much."* The family chat is mine to run (D2647, 2026-09-25), so the tuning is
mine to decide; these are recorded so they are not relitigated.

**1. Keep Chip; do not turn him off.** The 2026-09-30 fix for the same class of
complaint was to pause him (D2844). That is not what the family asked for here:
they are still teasing him by name, which is an invitation, and the one member
who says "do the job nothing more" is still reading his messages. What was
wrong was the register and the count, not the presence. `chip_enabled` stays 1.

**2. The mean register was designed in, by instruction, twelve days earlier —
and both instructions are now in the rulebook with their dates.** 2026-09-29 the
owner asked for Chip to be *less* normal and *more* "weird and creepy"; the code
was tuned toward it (`basement dweller` archetype whose text is "complains about
heat, dust, and being ignored"; a `sleepy` mood that complains about the
basement; a register rule to speak from the hardware as his own body one reply
in three; a system prompt that told him he has NO body and to "write the stranger
one instead" whenever a line could have come from a helpful assistant). Every
rule added on 2026-10-12 carries the owner's words and the date, so a future
session can see the dial and which way it was last turned.

**3. The two capabilities that produced "I know where you live" are disabled.**
`chip_secretary_tools` is now `""`. Chip had `secretary_ask_ceo` and
`secretary_calendar` on by a hardcoded default in `app/config.py:329`, so a
family group chat of eleven had a live channel into the CEO agent, and the
calendar half — asked with `all_accounts=True` — answers with business entries
(the live probe returned "Global Direct Parts dispute deadline (#161898,
$167.96)"). A tool that has never once worked (it raised AttributeError on every
call) and would leak business data the moment it did is not a capability worth
keeping switched on. Reversible in one setting; the fix to the hardcoded default
is recorded as NEXT, not done from here.

**4. Naming Chip no longer guarantees an answer.** The 2026-09-23 invariant
("if someone speaks directly to it, it can respond") was being spent: naming him
bypassed the turn-share ceiling *and* the addressed daily allowance was 30, so
five messages naming him produced five replies and ~45 % of the turns in a room
where he is one voice of eleven. A burst ceiling of 2 addressed replies per 30
minutes now applies to named messages too, and the daily allowance is 6. The
2026-09-23 instruction is not reversed — detection still ignores the randomizer
and the cooldowns — it is bounded. Setting either to 0 restores the old
behaviour.

**5. Being told to stop is a silence case, above the addressed branch.** No
prompt could fix this: the addressed path hands the model "Answering NONE is not
allowed here", so before the gate the only possible response to "Chip, shhhhh"
was a comeback — and on 2026-09-30 there were three. It is now the same shape as
the sensitive gate, on the same reasoning: a missed joke costs nothing, an
answer to "be quiet" costs the family's patience.
