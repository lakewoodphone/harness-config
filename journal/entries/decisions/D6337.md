**D6337 · 2026-10-16 · 2026-10-16 DECIDED: one plan - 8 a month including 150 MB, 2 cents a MB past that, a customer-set cap enforced on the SIM, billed in arrears on the 1st.**

The owner chose the usage model over the three-tier ladder (7/11/16) and over the whole step-up mechanic, which are now RETIRED. One plan: 8.00 a month including 150 MB (about 25 hours of driving at the measured 6 MB/h); 0.02 per MB past that; the customer sets a cap, enforced on the SIM itself by the carrier data limit with an 80% warning first, so the promise is 'you will never pay more than your cap'; billed in arrears on the 1st in ONE charge, base plus usage, with the first bill a prorated stub (8.00 x days/30, under eight days absorbed - which is exactly where the prorated base covers a full SIM month whether or not Telnyx prorates). HIS REASONING, verbatim: charged at the end of the month so we don't have to double charge them when they use more, they set a cap, and we charge for data at cost plus a markup. MARGINS at a 50-device fleet, Zone-1 blended cost 0.0153/MB: 100 MB -> pays 8.00, we keep 4.47 (56%); 250 MB -> 10.00, keep 4.17 (42%); 500 MB -> 15.00, keep 5.35 (36%); 1 GB -> 25.00, keep 7.70 (31%). Fleet of 50 at a 250 MB average: 500.00 in, 291.50 out, 208.50 a month profit, about 2,500 a year. The ladder would have earned about 50 a month more and that was traded deliberately for one charge a month instead of two, one price to say instead of three, and a cap that is a promise a plan cannot make. WHAT IT CHANGES IN BUILT WORK: waze-plans.ts (PR 455) shrinks to one plan plus a usage rate; the Stripe objects (456) become one recurring price plus a month-end usage amount WE compute, not Stripe metered billing; the data bucket (457) becomes the billing source, not just a cap; the faces (458) turn the plan picker into a cap picker; the sign's data line is now WRONG and must change. AND THE NEW DEPENDENCY: the cap is the enforcement, so 'raising a SIM data limit restores service' is load-bearing - documented by Telnyx, untested on our account (the operation that hangs is enable, not the raise).
