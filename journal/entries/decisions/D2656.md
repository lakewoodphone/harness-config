**D2656 · 2026-09-28 · Household bathroom drains: no tub catcher (lift-and-turn plug), cheapest sink catchers, Zip-It for upkeep.**

Owner reported 2026-09-27 evening: both bathroom sinks and the bathtub draining very slowly; wife suspected hair. Resolved with a Zip-It the same evening.

**Fixture types were established mid-session, and they decided the purchase.** Bathtub = a **lift-and-turn knob** (the stopper sits in the drain hole). Both bathroom sinks are **never filled** by the owner, so a stopper function there is worth nothing.

**Decided — no hair catcher on the tub.** An in-drain catcher and the lift-and-turn plug compete for the same hole, so it would mean a swap twice per bath. The owner bathes regularly and refused that outright ("it can't be a 27 step process"). Upkeep is instead a Zip-It down the tub drain every 2-4 weeks — 60 seconds, a few times a year, nothing to remember per use.

**Decided — sinks get a 2-pack of pop-up stopper + strainer basket, $13.99**, covering both sinks. Rejected the SinkShroom at $10.57: better product, but its coupon is capped at one item, so two sinks would be $23.56.

**Decided — the Zip-It 3-pack ($16.95) stays in every scenario.** It is the only item that clears a clog that already exists; the catchers only prevent the next one.

**Decided — no chemical drain cleaner, ever.** No lye/gel product (slow on hair, damages older pipes, leaves caustic water in the trap that burns whoever opens it next) and no baking-soda-and-vinegar, which does nothing to hair.

Order # 112-7603884-6857046, $32.99, placed 2026-09-27.

**Full record with the diagnosis tree, the tenant rules, the maintenance schedule and every product evaluated:** `~/code/home/maintenance/2026-09-27-bathroom-drain-slowness.md`

**Evidence:** TubShroom's own drain-type guide (tubshroom.com/blogs/tips/types-of-bathtub-drains-a-complete-identification-guide), read 2026-09-28 — only a *trip lever* drain (lever on the overflow plate, plunger hidden inside the pipe) accepts an in-drain catcher "without any modification"; lift-and-turn, push-pull, pop-up, toe-touch and flip-it all require removing the stopper first.
