**D2330b · 2026-09-22 · CORRECTION: the device is sold AT THE COUNTER, not online - what must be built is the data tracking and automatic Stripe billing, not a storefront checkout.**

**CORRECTION FROM THE OWNER, 2026-09-22, and it re-scopes the software.** In his words: *"what do you mean online checkout? we sell them at the counter, you don't order them. but the data tracking and auto stripe and all needs to be fully built."*

**What I had wrong:** I asked "for the first 10 devices, do we sell them at the counter or wait for the website?" - treating the website as a possible sales channel. **The website is NOT a sales channel for the device.** There is no customer-facing purchase. I had a whole workstream (W2, then the completeness pass) polishing `WazeStorePage.tsx` and the Stripe Checkout session for a purchase flow that should not exist.

**The correct model, which is also what the ORIGINAL settled plan said and I drifted from it:** the pipeline doc from 2026-09-14 records it plainly - *"Sold: **in the shop, in person.** The portal shows status and lets the customer change the data cap - it takes no money."* What must be fully built is the **data side**: per-device usage tracking and **automatic monthly Stripe billing**.

**So the real requirement is a COUNTER SALE that starts a metered subscription, and then bills itself:**
1. Staff sell the device in person and **capture the customer's card** at the counter.
2. That creates a **real Stripe Billing subscription** with a base monthly price ($9.00) and a **metered per-GB price** ($18.00), attached to the customer.
3. The **device is linked to that subscription** so the metering engine can find it by serial.
4. Usage is metered from Telnyx automatically and **billed in arrears, automatically, forever** - no staff action per month.

**What already exists and is the right shape:** the metered billing engine (W1: usage -> Stripe Billing Meters, idempotent deltas, fail-closed), the billing-grade usage ledger on the fleet API (W3), the scheduler (W-A: runs the sync, off by default), and `terminal.service.ts` for Stripe Terminal card capture. **The billing half is largely built; the missing half is the counter sale that creates the subscription and attaches the card.**

**What does NOT exist:** anything that saves a card for off-session billing and creates a Stripe *Billing* subscription against a metered meter (a grep found no `SetupIntent` / `off_session`). The repo's own `subscription.service.ts` is a home-grown renewal system, **not** Stripe Billing, so it is NOT what the Waze plan uses.

**What is still needed before ANY device goes out:** a Stripe **Billing Meter** and two **Prices** (base + metered) created in the account, plus `STRIPE_WAZE_METER_EVENT_NAME` and `WAZE_PLAN_METERED_PRICE_ID` set. Those are account objects; creating them needs Stripe access. **Nothing bills until they exist**, which is exactly why the owner said not to sell anything yet.

**What should happen to the storefront page:** it is not a sales channel and must not take orders. It can remain as an informational page (it is honest, it discloses refurbished/battery/lock, and it says "we are not taking orders for the Kosher Waze device online yet - call or visit the shop"). **Checkout stays disabled permanently for the device.** The `WAZE_STORE_CHECKOUT_ENABLED` flag should be understood as permanently false rather than "not yet on".

**Also still mine and small:** the page prints "0.25 GB" where the owner says "250 MB" - same number, but 250 MB is how a person reads it.

**NEXT, in order:** (1) create the Stripe meter + prices; (2) build the counter-sale flow (record sale, capture card, create the subscription, link the device); (3) a staff view of who owes what and which charges failed; (4) enable the scheduler and prove one real billing cycle end to end.
