**D2914 · 2026-10-04 · Meals gets two files per dish: an evidence file for the record and a cook file for the kitchen, and lahmajin is the worked example.**

The meals tier now separates two artefacts that have opposite needs:

- YYYY-MM-DD-<dish>.md - the EVIDENCE file. Every source, every dispute, every rejected option, and
 why each decision was made. For the record and for a future session.
- YYYY-MM-DD-<dish>-recipes.md - the COOK file. Recipe cards only, no prose, no sources, nothing that
 is useless with wet hands.

The lahmajin package is the worked example and is complete: 2026-10-04-lahmajin.md (37 KB of evidence,
nine sections) plus 2026-10-04-lahmajin-recipes.md (13 KB of cards). The cook file carries five
versions - the simple lachmagine on frozen dough rounds (make this first, per rule 10's outcome
evidence), the paper-thin lahmacun, a cooked-filling version that cannot get a wet bottom, a one-tray
version for a crowd, and a Pesach cauliflower-crust version - plus a failure card, make-ahead, the five
published spice families, and two shopping lists.

Split when the evidence gets in the way of cooking, not by default. The lasagna dip stays as one file
because 11 KB of evidence does not obstruct anyone.

Pushed to origin and authority. Commits: 04096b8 (rebuild from tasting evidence), d12fec8 (rule 10),
6222756 (the split).
