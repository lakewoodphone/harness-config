**The kiosk fee sentence: the owner chose to QUALIFY it, 2026-10-14 — so the last surface that promised the diagnostic credit unconditionally no longer does.**

This closes the open half of D3154.

THE QUESTION AND THE ANSWER. `frontend/src/brand/brand-assets.ts` held
`DIAGNOSTIC_FEE_SENTENCE` — "There is a 50 dollar non-refundable diagnostic fee per item. It goes
toward the cost of the repair." — and D2981 records that sentence as the owner's OWN fixed copy. Under
the 2026-10-14 rule ("it is only credited to repair on non difficlut diagnostics") it was no longer
unconditionally true, and `what-the-owner-actually-wants.md` §7 says a conflict with a quoted owner
instruction means **the quote wins or the owner is asked**. He was shown the conflict with three
options and chose **"qualify it on the kiosk too"** — over leaving his own words untouched, which was
my recommendation and which he overruled. **My recommendation was wrong and his answer was better:
the sentence is seen by a walk-in customer at drop-off, and it is a money claim, so it must not promise
something the shop will not always do.**

WHAT CHANGED (PR 440, commit `688c2fd1b`):
- `DIAGNOSTIC_FEE_SENTENCE` -> "...It goes toward the cost of the repair on a non-difficult
 diagnostic; a difficult diagnostic is charged on its own."
- `DIAGNOSTIC_FEE_CLAUSE` -> "...credited toward the repair on a non-difficult diagnostic".
- Those two constants are read by HomePage (twice), PhoneRepairPage, ContactPage, ScreenRepairPage,
 TermsPage, DataRecoveryPage and the homepage FAQ fallback — so all of them moved together.
- `KIOSK_FEE_SENTENCE` in `pages/KioskIntakePage.tsx` is now an **ALIAS** of the brand constant, not a
 second literal. Two literals for one money sentence is how the website and the tablet came to tell
 customers two different things before, and it is the same drift PR 439 had to fix between the two FAQ
 seed files.
- Two new assertions make it stick: brand-assets.test.ts fails if either constant omits
 "non-difficult diagnostic"; KioskIntakePage.test.tsx fails if the alias ever stops being the same
 string.

**LESSON FOR THE NEXT SESSION: when the record says "the quote wins or the owner is asked", ASK. I
wrote that sentence into `what-the-owner-actually-wants.md` §7 myself and then spent a long time
arguing myself out of obeying it. He answered in one line and chose the option I had argued against.
The rule was written because this estate has a history of agents deciding owner copy.**

Still not defined anywhere, deliberately: what makes a diagnostic difficult. D2921 — the owner decides
at the bench, case by case. Do not add a threshold.
