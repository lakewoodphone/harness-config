**D9359 · 2026-10-22 · The resale test must need nothing from the owner - the key sweep is out of it.**

Owner, 2026-10-22, after I had asked him three times in one session to press keys on a
handset during a bench window:

 "do you realize i never do the presses cuz i'm not watching your screen and they
 should probley be out of the test, the test should not be doing anything that
 requires my interaction"

BINDING. The condition/resale flow asks him for nothing. Implementation: key sweep is
opt-in (--key-window-seconds 120, default 0), the keypad check reports n/a "not run -
needs a press" and does not block, and the verdict can reach SELLABLE without any human
step. Consequence accepted and recorded: with no press the grade treats the keypad as
untested, which caps a handset at B; every handset in the register is already B or C,
so nothing changes in practice.

The general lesson, not just about keys: I built an interactive step into a test that
runs in an agent session he cannot see. A test that waits for a human who is not
watching is not a test, it is a hang. Before adding any step, ask who is looking at the
console.
