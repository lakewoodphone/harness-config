**D6657 · 2026-10-17 · The sign's layout is unfrozen: the 10-16 'keep the structure' brief is revoked, and the copy selection is open.**

Owner, 2026-10-18, on the Waze Device sign (`lpt-signs/waze-device-sign-letter.html`, v12): "the graphics and the
layout are just terrible" and, separately, "even if the information is correct, how to present and which
information to include".

**This revokes the constraint that produced v12.** `_waze-fleet-sync/HANDOFF-2026-10-16.md` line 149 (read this
session) told the v12 builder to "Keep the structure the owner preferred (the logo lockup, the two price columns,
the icon rows, the plan panel, the contact grid)". That line is why v12 changed only the glyphs and the font and
still drew the same complaint: nine layouts exist since 2026-10-16 -- variants A/B/C, N1, N2, v9, v10, v11, v12 --
and all nine share one skeleton (masthead -> headline -> sub -> rule -> two prices -> icon rows -> panel -> phone).
**The structure is now open. Do not freeze it again.**

Second decision implied by the same message: the *content* selection is on the table, not just the presentation.
Until this session every brief treated the copy as settled ("Copy unchanged, character for character").

Two findings that make the redesign substantive rather than cosmetic:
- The sheet carries **262 visible words** (measured this session by stripping the body text of the built HTML).
 The project's own sign research, `sign-and-benefits-2026-10-16.md` line 210 (VA Signage Design Guide, cited),
 says **7-10 words, one concept**. The sheet is ~26x over its own standard.
- The whole sheet is a CSS background, so it prints blank cream-on-white unless "Background graphics" is ticked;
 `@page{margin:0}` with full bleed also loses an unprintable border on a home printer. Fix decided: hand the
 owner a **PDF**, not the HTML, so backgrounds cannot be dropped.
