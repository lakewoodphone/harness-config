**D2917 · 2026-10-05 · Lakewood Rentals launch: the owner's admin identity is his gmail, photo screening is ON, and supply comes before demand.**

## 2026-10-05 · ZABZ-TECH · Lakewood Rentals launch decisions

D-1. **The owner's marketplace admin identity is `lakewoodphoneandtech@gmail.com`.**
`ADMIN_EMAILS` on lpt-apps-01 was `eliyahu@lakewoodphoneandtech.com`, but the Google account he
actually signs in with is `lakewoodphoneandtech@gmail.com`, so `ensureAdminRole()` correctly refused to
promote him. Appended, not replaced, so both work. Reason it matters: admin is the only route to the
moderation and export surfaces (`/api/admin/*` are all `role !== "admin" -> 403`), and a marketplace
with no admin can never clear its flag queue.

D-2. **Photo content screening is ON in production.** `PHOTO_CONTENT_MODEL_API_KEY` set (the Google AI
Studio key the system already holds — no new credential, no new spend commitment; cost is fractions of
a cent per upload and the spec already budgets "negligible at launch"), model `gemini-3.1-flash-lite`,
validated present in the live Gemini catalog at decision time. Reason: unconfigured, the screen returns
`flagged`, which per PHOTO-6 **publishes** the photo and merely queues it for a human — so an
unconfigured screen is not "safe by default", it is unscreened and public. Verified after by
`/api/health` -> `"image_screen_configured": true`.

D-3. **Password auth stays disabled and LPT Google SSO stays the only door.** No change made; recorded
because it was re-verified rather than assumed, and because it is the fact that decides whether
advertising can work: the authority auto-provisions an unseen Google identity
(`createdAccount: true`), so a landlord needs no invitation and no LPT account beforehand.

D-4. **Advertising sequence: supply before demand.** Recommend to the owner: advertise to landlords
first. Against `listings=0` a renter-facing click lands on an empty map and converts at approximately
zero, while "list your basement unit free" is a complete, true pitch today. This is the one item in
this entry that is the owner's to overrule — it costs money and it is his market.
