**D3047 · 2026-10-11 · The phone app runs on the authority, not the owner's laptop - and the migration was already in flight, so I did not touch it.**

**Decision (2026-10-11, ZABZ-YOGA, surface audit 146): the owner's phone app is to be served by the
authority, not by his laptop — and because that migration is already half-built, this session did not
touch it.**

**What was decided, and by what right.** The estate's own rule is that anything the owner reaches from
outside must survive a laptop being closed. `/phone` currently terminates at
`zabz-yoga-1.tail93e6e6.ts.net` → the laptop's `phone-gate.py` (pid 9128, `127.0.0.1:3086`) → engine on
`127.0.0.1:3099`. That dependency is **not** a decision I need the owner for: it is an engineering
fault with an engineering fix, and the fix is written out as five numbered steps in
`docs/mesh/146-AUDIT-SURFACES.md` §3.

**Why I did not apply it.** `/opt/dsh-phone` on `secratary-cloud` was created 15:35–15:37Z and
`/usr/local/bin/phone-gate.py` + `phone-gate-allow.txt` at 15:35Z — i.e. another session is *building
this right now*, and `phone-redirector.env` was changed at 15:33:49Z with a `.before-target-fix` backup
beside it. Two writers on the same migration is the exact condition this estate's rules exist to prevent,
and the migration brief forbids restarting the authority's units without saying so. So the correct action
was to measure, record the current state precisely, and leave the in-flight work alone.

**The two public findings that ARE someone's decision, and where each went.**
- `dialer.abletelsolutions.com` — a permanent public `502` in front of a port nothing has ever served
 (`refused` at 05:43:46Z, 06:17:58Z and 15:38:42Z; no unit, no source, no crontab). The earlier sweep
 (`135-PARITY-AND-ROBUSTNESS.md`) reached the same conclusion. **Queued as owner question #446**, with
 the recommendation to delete the DNS record, because deleting a public hostname that shares an ingress
 with `secretary-ssh.` is irreversible enough to be his.
- `https://secratary-cloud.tail93e6e6.ts.net/` — no listener, no `tailscale serve`. Part of §3's fix,
 not a separate question.

**What is NOT decided here, deliberately:** whether the phone app should exist at all, what it should do,
and what it should look like. Those are the owner's taste and product call. This entry decides only
*where it runs*.
