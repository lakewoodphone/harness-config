**D75 · 2026-09-14 · I audited my own parent notice against the code and three sentences were untrue.**

The notice said: *"before anything is sent, the face and skin are painted out to a plain colour, it is cropped to the clothing being judged, the background is removed"*. `prepare_frame_for_egress` does four things — EXIF orientation, RGB conversion, downscale to 1024px, re-encode without metadata — and **none of them is any of that**. The face-painting pipeline exists only in `docs/research/024` and D36-02; it was designed and never built. Two more: *"it does not report, score, or rank your child"* needed qualifying, because a partner the owner names receives flagged-review alerts containing the phone id and the site tag (`partner.py::send_partner_screenshot_flagged_alert`); and *"we do not allow that partner to keep the picture or train on it"* is a **requirement we have not obtained** (C-7), not a fact, because no vendor has answered the clause set.
*The rule:* a promise document drafted from the *design* rather than from the *implementation* will always overstate, and in this area an overstated claim is the exposure itself — the FTC's NGL theory was an overstated AI-filtering claim needing no privacy law at all. Every sentence of the notice now carries the code or decision that makes it true in `docs/compliance/notice-claims-vs-code-2026-09-14.md`, so the next audit is a diff, not a re-read. The unbuilt half is recorded as **C-9**, and it belongs on the phone (where the detectors already are) rather than the server.
