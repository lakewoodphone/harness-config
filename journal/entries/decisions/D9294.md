## Decision 2026-10-21 · Frames are ephemeral - the verdict is kept, the picture is not, and nobody at the shop ever sees one (Q23)

aliases: Q23 · D-20261021-22 · docs/decisions/D-20261021-22-frames-never-stored.md

Owner's correction, verbatim: "Aren't we building it in a way that frames do not reach us? They are held in ephemeral memory only or something? We don't keep user data from what I understand."

He was right, and the code says so: config.py:435 sets screenshot_retention_days = 0, and frames.py:133-158 stores the image only when that value is above zero — otherwise image_b64_to_store stays None, the review row is written with image_base64=None and deleted_ts=now, and the response reports "image_retained": false. The question I asked about staff reviewing a frame described a configuration we do not run.

1. Frames are ephemeral by design and by default: the broker keeps what was flagged, when, with which scores and which rule — never the image. Retention stays at zero.
2. Nobody at the shop ever looks at a picture: no staff review queue, no support view, no exception for a technician investigating a complaint.
3. A review record is not a picture: the row lives 180 days, is purged outright, and only anonymous label counts survive the purge.
4. The only way an image leaves the phone is a model asked to classify it, and both provider settings are off in production; that egress is governed by D-03 and the consent recorded at setup.

Now required: turning retention on is a product decision, not a setting; support is answered from the verdict, rule, level, decision row and the customer's words, so false-block reports carry the rule id; the privacy sentence stays narrow and strong — "We never see your screen. We keep what was blocked and why; the picture is deleted the moment it is judged."
