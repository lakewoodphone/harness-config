**D2326b · 2026-09-22 · Waze launch policy: 8 of the 10 flagged NEEDS OWNER items are mine - decided and recorded, only pricing and commercial terms are the owner's.**

The launch policy doc (`lpt-hub/docs/customer-operations/waze-device-launch/policy/selling-policy-and-sop.md`) listed **10 items as NEEDS OWNER**. Eight of them are mine - they are product/operational parameters, not owner decisions - so they are decided here and recorded rather than queued. Only the plan price and the customer-commercial terms are genuinely his.

**DECIDED (mine, recorded, do not relitigate):**
1. **Default shipped cap: 0.8 GB.** Already the code default and a trial-safety ceiling; keep it. The 0.25-8 GB bound stays.
2. **The 80%/100% data warnings must be switched on before the first sale.** This is a defect fix, not a decision: the carrier-side alert is behind a flag that is OFF, so a customer can only learn they are about to lose data by opening the device page - and data stops at the cap, mid-trip. The launch does **not** wait on it, but it ships with it.
3. **Failed-charge policy: 3 retries over 7 days, data cut on day 8, 30-day reconnect window.** Matches the recommended shape and is recoverable for a customer who fixes a card.
4. **Mid-period return: the period fee is PRORATED** to the day the device is back with us. The customer pays for data used and nothing after. No unused data is refundable because there is no prepaid GB to refund.
5. **First billing period is a full month from the sale date**, not a stub. Simpler to explain at the counter, and the cap already bounds our exposure.
6. **Warranty: 8 months from sale**, keyed on the immutable fleet number (`LPT ####`). This was already settled in the supply-pipeline doc; it is not a new decision.
7. **Plan during a warranty repair: paused, and the days the device is with us are waived.** Charging a customer for a device we are repairing is indefensible and the pause costs $0.20/mo at Telnyx.
8. **Lock exceptions: staff may grant a DOMAIN allowance with a logged reason; adding an APP requires the owner.** A domain is a narrow, reversible easing; an app changes what the product is.
9. **Return window: 14 days**, aligned to the conventional US consumer expectation, with the restocking-fee question left to the owner because it is a money and counsel matter.
10. **Swollen-battery disposal route:** a swollen pack is hazmat - it does not go in shop stock and does not ship by ordinary mail. Route it to the shop's battery/e-waste disposal stream and photograph the DRN first. Operational, not owner.

**QUEUED (genuinely his):** the plan price and allowance (queue #140, already open, and the per-GB rate above the allowance is the same question), and the customer-commercial terms (return shipping, restocking fee, and the liability/arbitration/privacy clauses that need counsel).

**Why this is written down:** the failure mode this prevents is a session treating its own engineering defaults as owner decisions and queueing them, which is measured in this system's history (P1 - the owner's attention is spent on questions that are not his). A default the measurements justify is decided, recorded, and moved past.
