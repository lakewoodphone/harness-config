**D7636 · 2026-10-18 · Monthly sponsorship asks never reach the owner queue.**

Owner, 2026-10-18: "i don't need you to bring monthly sponsorships to my attention at all"

STANDING RULE, binding: recurring/monthly sponsorship solicitations never reach the owner queue. Not as a decision, not as attention, not as a digest line. A cold ask for a recurring monthly commitment is answered NO by policy unless the owner has personally raised the organisation first.

Context: queue row #656 (Moshe Zirinsky, $500/month kollel sponsorship at BMG) was dismissed on these words. Do not re-file it and do not file its siblings.

If a sponsorship ask ever carries something the owner must know (an existing commitment of his being changed, a tax document, a name attached without permission), that is a different matter and is filed as NEWS with the contract detail, never as a sponsorship request.
