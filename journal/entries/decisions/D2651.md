# Owner decision, 2026-09-25 17:00 EDT (ZABZ-YOGA session)

He said: *"i want the shutdown to happen 3 hours before sunset from now on, not so close to
sunset, do you understand?"* — about the Home Assistant / Shelly plug Shabbat + Yom Tov
power-down (`personal-secretary-mvp` `app/services/shabbat_*`, executed by the secretary).

## The decision

- **Power OFF = sunset − 180 min** (was sunset − 60 min, owner spec 2026-09-11). Power ON
 stays sunset + 100 min — it does not depend on the OFF offset.
- It is the whole power-down that moved (the shutdown *is* the cut): door hold → graceful HA
 shutdown → re-verify → plug off.
- Rationale is his and was not asked for: the old 60-minute-before-sunset cut landed in the
 middle of the pre-Shabbat rush. Earlier is what he asked for; earlier is what it does.

## How it was applied (authority = secratary, the machine that runs it)

- `.env` `SHABBAT_CANDLE_OFFSET_MIN=180`, `app/config.py` default 180 (with a comment naming
 the owner and the date), `app/services/shalom_zmanim.py` `DEFAULT_CANDLE_OFFSET_MIN=180`.
- Backups first: `secratary:~/shabbat-offset-backup/20260925-210138/`.
- 49 Shabbat tests pass with the new value; the running process's own environment was read
 from `/proc/<MainPID>/environ` to prove it, not assumed.
- The repo now matches the authority for the four service files (they were untracked on the
 authority and stale in the repo — P47) plus the offset; commit `f436387c9` on
 ZABZ-YOGA, `feat/owner-direct-request-sms`, not pushed.

## The consequence I accepted rather than asked about

Setting a 3-hour offset at 17:00 on erev Sukkot put the OFF (15:48) **in the past**, which
`recover_missed_off` is written for: "an offset change moved the deadline into the past". So
tonight's run fired ~1 minute after the restart instead of at 17:48. I let it, and did not
ask, because (a) the direction is unambiguously his, (b) the only alternative was to keep
the exact "so close to sunset" behaviour he had just told me to stop, and (c) by the time he
read it the old 17:48 deadline was inside the hour anyway. The delta was 41 minutes of
camera/HA coverage. It was stated in the reply, not hidden.

Dev decisions made without asking (his rule: dev work is mine): keep the setting name
`shabbat_candle_offset_min` (renaming it would churn `.env`, config and tests for no
behavioural gain); patch the code default *and* the env, so neither alone can revert it.
