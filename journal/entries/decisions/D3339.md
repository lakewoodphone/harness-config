**Asked**: Should an LPT NAVIGATION device be able to take a destination by voice? It cannot today, and the reason is ours: on 14 June the microphone and dictation were switched off on DRN 50 as a temporary rollback, written down as "defer Waze/keyboard voice input until a controlled physical retest", and the retest was never run in four months. The research is done and committed; what is left is one policy decision and a 20-minute test on a phone.

**His answer**: DIRECTION 2026-10-14 (owner, in session): on-device/offline dictation is to be working on the six LPT NAVIGATION units (DRN 2002, 2004, 2005, 2006, 2007, 2008). His words: "on the 6 shop waze devices can you just put the offline talk to text model". So voice destination entry SHIPS on these units, OFFLINE ONLY: allowDictation=true, forceOnDeviceOnlyDictation=true, guzzoni.apple.com and *.smoot.apple.com stay refused, and nothing new is opened for speech. Two facts that shape the work: (1) no MDM mechanism installs a speech asset - Apple exposes no asset inventory and no install verb, and the only supported lever is an app calling AssetInventory.assetInstallationRequest(...).downloadAndInstall(), which a one-app kiosk (com.waze.iphone, 133 bundles blocked) does not have; so the ~330 MB EmbeddedSpeech model is pulled by the device itself and the Enable Dictation tap stays manual - about 2 minutes per unit, then Wi-Fi + charger + idle 15+ minutes. (2) The asset CDN is already open as of 2026-10-14 16:26Z, so the download now works; the open risk is that the same door admits an iOS update, which the PAC path rule (docs/voice-input/10) fixes. NOT answered by this: whether com.apple.shortcuts may be unblocked for the Shortcuts -> waze:// deep-link fallback. That stays blocked and is still the owner's call on its own.
