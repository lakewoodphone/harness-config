**D6465 · 2026-10-17 · Owner-text channel: one front desk, a mandatory ask ladder, one ask ledger, weekly self-audit.**

Decisions of record for the owner-text channel, 2026-10-18. Full reasoning:
`harness-config/docs/owner-text/FRONT-DESK-2026-10-18.md`.

1. ONE FRONT DESK. Every text to him, and every text sent on his behalf, is composed by
 `owner_text_relay.py`. A door submits an ITEM; it may not write a body. Measured today:
 `compose()` has one caller and `submit_item()` has none, so this is a target, not a state.

2. THE ASK LADDER IS MANDATORY. An item may not open an ask without a `preask` receipt. Rungs in
 order: owner's own past answers -> ledger (amount-anchored `plaid_transactions` + `purchases`)
 -> comms (his texts, calls, mail) -> docs -> web. Verdicts: `answered` (act, do not ask),
 `deflected` (he told us to work -- never re-ask), `partial` (ask, carrying the evidence found),
 `unknown` (ask, saying what was checked). `merchant_resolve` is used only for a merchant-only
 question, never as the solver of an amount.

3. ONE ASK LEDGER: `relay.db ask`. `owner-ask.py` is RETIRED (zero rows, no cron line -- a store
 with no writer and no reader is a trap for the next session). `cfo-ask` keeps its own store as a
 projection and files with the relay. The suppression key becomes the charge
 `(account_id, external_transaction_id)`, not the merchant.

4. THE CEILING IS PER RECIPIENT. 320 chars for him, 1600 for anyone else. His reading limit is not
 a transport limit, and applying it to a family member is what stranded attempts 74/75/76.

5. A MESSAGE'S RECIPIENT IS PART OF ITS IDENTITY. `attempt.to_number` is recorded and required;
 "two sids, one body" is only a duplicate when the recipient is the same.

6. A REPLY NEVER BINDS ON TIME ALONE. `textdecide`'s "newest open ask, inferred" fallback is
 removed; ambiguity is surfaced as one numbered question with the two or three candidates.

7. THE CHANNEL AUDITS ITSELF WEEKLY, IN THE WAKE SYSTEM. `17 13 * * 0`, eight metrics, and a wake
 flag ONLY when a threshold breaches -- nothing on a timer may file work merely because time
 passed. The woken session fixes one defect at the door that owns it, proves it, records it.

8. THE LIVE STACK IS VERSIONED. `scripts/text-channel/mirror/` holds the byte-for-byte copy of
 `/root/bin` plus a hash manifest and verify/deploy scripts, because `/root/bin` is not a git
 repository and P4254 can restore a file under a session that has just verified it.

WHAT IS GENUINELY HIS, and stays in the owner queue: money, family, external sends, legal posture,
ground truth only he holds. Everything else in this document is an engineering decision and was
taken without asking.
