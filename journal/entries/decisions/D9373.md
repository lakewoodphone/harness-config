**D9373 · 2026-10-27 · The shop's security priority is the record, not the alarm - every person, pictured, timed, kept.**

**The answer (owner, 2026-10-27, in his words).** Asked Q1 of
`harness-config:docs/security-and-evolution/ha-security/SECURITY-DISCUSSION.md` — what are we actually
defending against — he answered that burglary protection is wanted but is not the fear: *"it's a pretty
secure neighborhood, I'm not so scared of that. Really important is extreme tracking. I want to know every
person who came and want a little picture or video of them. I want to know when they came, when they left.
And very good tracking of everything that ever happened there is more important to me even."*

**What this decides.** The product of this system is the **record**, not the siren. Priority 1 is total
observation — every person, a likeness, arrival time, departure time, complete history, searchable.
Priority 2 is the intrusion alarm, which stays as built (credential disarms, presence never does).

**Why it changes the build order.** Six documents in that folder are ordered alarm-first: stage 1 fixes the
disarm, stages 2-5 tune the reflex, and observation waits for `50-BUILD-PLAN.md` stage 6. Under his ranking
that order is wrong. The parts that make a person appear in the record with a picture and a correct
timestamp now come before any further alarm work.

**The measured blockers to his requirement** (all read on 2026-10-26, sources in the folder):
1. Two cameras are **sixteen minutes apart** in time, so any cross-camera timeline is wrong
 (`05-LIVE-FINDINGS.md` L4, `from-zabz-tech/reports/R2-cameras.md`).
2. **No snapshot has ever been stored.** The evidence chain never reached the capture step, and pruning
 runs at fourteen days (`05-LIVE-FINDINGS.md` L5/L6, `OPEN-THREAD-nonblocking-evidence.md`).
3. **A snapshot call can return another camera's frame with HTTP 200** — evidence that lies
 (`from-zabz-tech/security-audit-2026-10-26.md` S62-S66).
4. **No machine vision exists on the estate**; the Dahua detectors are switched off and 54 detectors have
 been zero for 30 days (`from-zabz-tech/reports/R2-cameras.md`).
5. **Only five physical cameras**, channels 2/3/4 with no lens, one channel dead, 25 camera entities mostly
 substreams and placeholders (`05-LIVE-FINDINGS.md` L4).
6. **The company cannot see an event in real time** — `events` and `routing_log` are at 0 rows and the
 dispatch TypeError is swallowed (`05-LIVE-FINDINGS.md` L6).
