**D2455 · 2026-09-23 · Delivery truth: unreachable vs unrouted; owner questions route via the sweep, not the ask path.**

## Decision

`ck.sentinel.check_delivery` now separates **unreachable** from **unrouted**. Only
the first can be CRITICAL, and it requires every channel to be dead: the app
surface (badge payload) stale or absent AND no send inside the window. A live
surface plus a question no channel will carry is HIGH and named.

Rationale, with the measurement: on 2026-09-23 the check went CRITICAL saying
"nothing is reaching the owner" while the badge payload was written every five
minutes and the badge was carrying that very finding to him. It measured one
channel and made a claim about the owner. That is the same failure as the
stale-database crisis and L1500.

## What changed in behaviour

* `owner_sms_off` no longer decides whether the surface is examined; the surface
 is always measured (it was computed and then ignored unless the kill-switch
 marker happened to exist).
* `reason='ask_owner'` is measured separately from other sends, because a daily
 briefing reaching him can no longer mask questions that reach nobody.
* `proactive_update` rows are excluded from the unrouted count: the app's own
 `_OWNER_SMS_REASON_DENY` refuses them by design (his 2026-09-22 instruction),
 so counting them would be an alarm that can never go green.
* CRITICAL now requires BOTH dead; the "quiet but armed SMS line" case is INFO.

Evidence: `~/ceo-kernel` commit 402a506 + merge 09c1a88, pushed; 6 cases in
`tests/test_check_delivery.py`, all passing; live run now reports delivery INFO
and the total attention count fell from 2 to 1.

## Decision

Owner questions are routed to `owner_decision_queue` by the lifecycle sweep, not
by the ask path -- for now.

`app/services/owner_question_queue.py` files any `held`/`queued` `ask_owner` row
onto the queue (idempotent on `source = owner_message_queue#<id>`) and marks the
swept row `routed_to_decision_queue` with the queue id. Called from
`app/services/lifecycle_sweep.py::_file_owner_questions` ahead of the expiry
phase. Rationale: routing at the sweep covers every producer, and the ask path's
168-hour expiry was the only thing that used to happen to a question -- silence.

The first-party fix (have `_exec_ask_owner` write the queue row itself) is
recorded as the next step, not done: it means editing a call site in a repo whose
working tree has 216 uncommitted entries and restarting the API, and the sweep
closes the leak today with no behavioural change to the send path.
