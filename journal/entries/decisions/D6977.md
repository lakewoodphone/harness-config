## D — 2026-10-18 · The owner queue: one table, a judged email channel, a working set, and it learns

**His requirement, verbatim (2026-10-18):** *"make sure it's one queue that has everything that I
need to know or needs my either one ... any email or text that you deem worthy of my attention ...
once in a while I'm going to ask, hey, let's go through the queue or let's knock out the queue and
you just tell me things one at a time."* Then two additions the same session: *"the queue needs to
learn over time what to put in it and what to prioritize based on my responses ... when we go
through a session knocking it out together"* and *"all information about it must be gathered ...
so only put it in the queue after all info is gathered about it fully so I can just quickly knock
out the queue ... once every other day or so."*

Standing rule set: **`docs/owner-queue.md`**. This entry is the decision record behind it.

### Decided

1. **One table stays the one queue** — `owner_decision_queue` on the authority, `kind ∈
 {decision, attention}`. No second store. `owner_message_queue` (held rows) and
 `agent_questions` are still READ and merged at read time, because their lifecycle belongs to
 the app; `urgent_email` held rows are dropped when the mail judge already holds a verdict on
 that very subject.
2. **Email is judged, not filtered.** A model call per batch of 10 against a written rubric, one
 verdict cached per message with its reason (`owner_mail_verdict`). `email_catalog.needs_owner`
 is not the signal (347 of 812 rows flagged, sample: UGG, Uber Cash, AAA). Measured: 205
 messages / 7 days → **134 drop, 66 attention, 6 decisions**, and every decision carries a
 recommendation.
3. **Dialpad never feeds it** (his correction). Google Voice via its forwarding mail does.
4. **The working set, not the archive.** Decisions always; attention only while it is fresh (≤3 d),
 asked of him, ≥ $100, or critical/high. The rest is counted and named, never deleted. Measured
 before this rule: 100 pending items, 53 of them older than 3 days — not knockable.
5. **It learns, deterministically.** Every answer/dismiss/dismiss/snooze writes
 `owner_queue_feedback`; ≥2 dismissals with more dismissals than answers demotes a sender (−40
 each, cap −120, printed in the arithmetic); his last 12 answers/dismissals are handed to the mail
 judge as few-shot. No numeric weight is auto-tuned and no model touches the ranking.
6. **Every item arrives briefed**: why, what he would do, my recommendation, the full text, history
 with that sender, and POSSIBLY MOOT when the row named a day that has passed.

### Proven, this session, on the authority

* `ingest all --days 3` → exit 0; `health` → every source OK; timer `lpt-owner-queue.timer` enabled,
 first run `status=0/SUCCESS` for both ExecStart and the health ExecStartPost, NEXT 14:36Z.
* Learning end-to-end on a scratch copy: 3 dismissals from one party produced `learned=-80` in the
 arithmetic and moved the score 440 → 360; a fourth dismissal did not exist because the threshold
 is 2 and the key must be a real identity (the first run demoted `party=several`, i.e. every
 digest, which is why `NON_PARTIES` exists).
* Moot flag: a 92-hour-old "dinner tonight" row renders `POSSIBLY MOOT — said "tonight" 92 hours
 ago`, score 951 → 751.

### Not done, and named

1. **Resolved premises still sit in the queue.** Telnyx "balance −$0.01" stayed critical and top for
 two days after the account was re-enabled; the payment confirmation is a different fact and
 nothing links them. `owner_premise_check` is the hook.
2. `scripts/owner_attention.py` still ranks independently for phone delivery.
3. The digest and a standalone row can both mention the same bank balance when the bank sends two
 messages that day.
