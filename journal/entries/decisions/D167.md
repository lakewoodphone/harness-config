CORRECTION TO THE COST, FROM STRIPE'S OWN DOCS
The queue row and my own earlier framing said the secret-key roll needs the reader re-paired at the counter. That is wrong. Reader registration lives in the account and is done once by registration code or serial from the Dashboard; a smart reader then talks to Stripe over the internet on its own credentials, and our server only mints short-lived connection tokens with the secret key. Rolling the secret key therefore costs: update the key wherever it lives, restart those services. No counter work, no re-pairing, no downtime beyond a container restart. Source: https://docs.stripe.com/terminal/payments/connect-reader (register reader -> Dashboard/registration code, then connect with connection tokens).
