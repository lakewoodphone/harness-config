## 2026-10-14 — Waze voice input: decide by test, never by opening `guzzoni`, and do not unblock Shortcuts without the owner

**Decided** (dev-level, mine to make):
1. **The first move is a test, not a change to shipped devices.** The 2026-06-14 note deferred voice input "until a
 controlled physical retest"; that retest is now written as `deploy/waze-mdm/docs/voice-input/05-test-plan.md` and
 runs on DRN 2003 (a `test`-group unit), swapping one profile file and calling two endpoints on one device.
2. **On-device dictation is the only variant worth testing**: `allowDictation=true`, keep
 `forceOnDeviceOnlyDictation=true`, keep `guzzoni.apple.com` and `*.smoot.apple.com` blocked, and keep the DoH
 profile prohibited from disablement. Apple documents dictation as on-device with no internet connection in many
 languages, so this opens no network path and needs no allowlist change.
3. **`guzzoni.apple.com` is never allowed.** Apple labels it "Siri and dictation requests" — one host serving both —
 so allowing it for speech-to-text admits the whole Siri service, which filtering vendors document as a content
 bypass. There is no documentable way to scope it.
4. **The profile variant is generated by `make_waze_layered_kiosk_profile(allow_dictation=…, allow_microphone=…)`,
 never by hand-editing a copy**, and the generator script refuses to write into `deploy/waze-mdm/profiles/`.
 Reason: the DRN-50 postmortem records what stray profile variants next to production ones cost.
5. **`allowMicrophone` is to be treated as inert** until a device test says otherwise, and either removed or
 replaced by the documented declarative per-app permission default rather than left claiming a protection.

**Left to the owner** (not mine): whether a sold LPT NAVIGATION device may have a working microphone at all, and
whether `com.apple.shortcuts` may be unblocked for the Shortcuts → `waze://?q=` fallback. Both change what the
product is, not how it is built.

EVIDENCE: `deploy/waze-mdm/docs/voice-input/04-filter-safety-and-options.md` (master `f585413b9`).
