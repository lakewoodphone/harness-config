**D3155 · 2026-10-19 · The computer floor is 500 dollars, enforced by the budget bands and a failing test.**

**D~ · 2026-10-19 · The computer floor is $500, and it is enforced by the budget bands themselves — no band may start below it.**

Owner's instruction, 2026-10-19, verbatim: "We also switched that we don't sell computers for less than $500. If you want to pay less than 500 we don't do it."

How it is implemented, and why this way:
- A customer states money only by choosing a budget band (`LaptopQuestionnaireForm.tsx` Section 5). The band enum is therefore the only thing that can enforce a floor, and it does: `MID_600_750` is the lowest, starting at $600.
- `backend/src/trpc/routers/laptop-preference.ts` now exports `MIN_COMPUTER_BUDGET_USD = 500` and `BUDGET_TIER_LOW_USD`, and `__tests__/laptop-preference.budget-floor.test.ts` fails the build if any band ever starts below the floor. Before this, the $600 lowest band was an accident of D72, not a policy.
- The questionnaire now says the floor to the customer, above the bands: "We don't sell computers for less than $500 — that is the floor for a machine we can stand behind."
- A fifth band below $600 was deliberately NOT added. The gap $500-$599 cannot be expressed today, and closing it needs another value in the Prisma `BudgetTier` enum — but the 2026-09-14 tier-rename migration is HELD (D2659) and production was last measured still carrying the OLD three labels (H2794, 2026-09-28). Adding a value on top of a held, drifted enum is how a config drift becomes an outage. Close that gap only after the prod enum is reconciled.

Still contradicting the floor on 2026-10-19, and NOT in this repo's gift:
1. The live Google Form offers "Under $400" and "$400-$600" and is open. Its closure is the owner's call — it is a public endpoint of the business.
2. `lpt-hub/non-device/Computer-Sourcing/SPEC-GUIDE.md:63-67` still lists 2025 bands starting at "Under $400"; that is the procedure the sourcing agent follows.
3. `phone-and-tech-full/docs/pricing/` lists three laptops at $350/$400/$450 and the case record shows real sub-$500 sales in the last six weeks (Brodie $400, Pinter $309.21, Weitman option $390). Historical records must not be rewritten; the floor applies going forward.
4. The secretary's intake has no floor at all: `plan-laptop-research-from-form.py` parses `Under $N` with `low = 0.0` and only ever computes a ceiling; `research-laptop.py:354-358` parses `sell_min` and discards it.
