**The raw transcript archive's home is the authority, the pull stays driven from the always-on host, and the safety rules do not move with it.**

Decided 2026-10-11 during the authority cutover, recorded so it is not relitigated:

- The raw archive lives at `secratary-cloud:/opt/company/archive/lpt-transcripts/`, with the office box's `/home/zabz/lpt-transcripts/` kept as the frozen second copy. Two hosts, two copies.
- The hourly pull runs from the always-on host, because AGENTS.md section 1 trap 2 measured a workstation-side scheduler reporting success for 21 days while the archive stood still. On the authority it is `transcript-archive-pull.timer` (`OnCalendar=*:23`, `Persistent=true`, journald), not cron, because cron on the authority has an empty crontab and the office box's `cron-wrap.sh` convention does not exist there.
- Exit 0 = current, 1 = GAP, 2 = silence (not a defect, does not fail the unit), 3 = the pull failed (fails the unit, so `systemctl --failed` carries it).
- The archive's machine IDENTITY is the name in the manifest and is never the ssh alias. Moving the archive host must not invalidate a cursor.
- `REFUSE_NAMES`, card/credential redaction and the `source_sha256` cursor keying are NOT negotiable and were not touched. Where the cursor code was changed it was changed TOWARDS keying on the source hash.
- The authority's raw-archive dependency on the tailnet is real and was NOT worked around by inventing a workstation-hosted tunnel as the permanent path: the 2026-10-11 catch-up ran that way ONCE, as a labelled one-shot, and the durable path waits for the tailnet key (owner question 445).

EVIDENCE: `docs/mesh/134-ARCHIVE-CONTINUITY.md` sections 2, 3.
