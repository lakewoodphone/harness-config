**D9358 · 2026-10-22 · Two products, not one: the filter has a release path, the sold Kosher Waze device never does - corrects my #935 note.**

The owner drew a line I had collapsed, 2026-10-22, immediately after my #935 note. Two products, two different answers, and my correction to #935 overreached.

**1. THE FILTER** - installed on a CUSTOMER'S OWN DEVICE. There IS a path to unblock/remove it. `request_release`, `ensure_release_allowed` and `release_eligible_at` belong to THIS product, and they are legitimate. His words: "the filter is the customers device that we installed something on, so there's a path to unblock it."

**2. THE DEVICES WE SELL** (Kosher Waze units) - sold as Waze-only handsets. **No path toward release, ever.** His words: "the devices we sell, we are selling them as Waze-only devices with no path towards release."

WHAT THIS CORRECTS. Queue row #935 was resolved with text saying "any request_release / ensure_release_allowed path must refuse or be removed rather than compute a date". That sentence is RIGHT for sold devices and WRONG for the filter, and it must not be applied to the filter code. The queue table has no edit, so this entry is the correction of record; the row's own premise (a thirty-day clock) is still open for the filter product only, and should be re-asked scoped to it rather than dropped.

WHAT IS STILL TRUE from the #935 resolution: a sold Kosher Waze device carries no release date, so the parent-facing page must not print one for it.

CONSEQUENCE FOR ANY FUTURE READER: never write "we do not release phones" flatly. Say which product. A rule stated for one product and applied to the other either breaks a customer's legitimate unblock or promises a release the shop never gives.
