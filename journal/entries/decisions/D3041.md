## Decisions taken building the Yanofsky profile — 2026-10-11

Recorded so they are not relitigated. All of these are development decisions, taken without asking.

1. **The profile lives at `C:\Users\ezabz\code\noson-yanofsky-profile\`** — its own directory, not
 inside `docs/` or a repo, because it is a family/private-research artefact with a book, 24 PDFs,
 audio and eight research files in it, and because the owner asked for "a little file directory".
2. **The extracted text is the ground truth, and it is kept** (`_raw/`, 21 spine files). Every
 statement about the book is checkable against it, and the whole directory can be rebuilt from the
 epub in seconds.
3. **Two planned files were folded in rather than duplicated**: the career timeline into `PROFILE.md`
 (year by year) and the consolidated publication list into `PROFILE.md` plus
 `research/research-01-bibliography-papers.md`. A second copy of a list is a second place to go
 stale.
4. **Web work to the mesh; file-local work locally, and said out loud.** The six research streams ran
 on `zabz-tech` / `zabz-tech-linux`; the book analysis and all synthesis ran on this laptop because
 the text exists only here and the broker failed under burst. The split is written into
 `SOURCES.md` §2 instead of being presented as one uniform fleet.
5. **A worker's out-of-brief work was kept when it was real, moved when it was dangerous, and
 recorded either way** (`SOURCES.md` §5): the correspondence folder was kept (the recordings were
 verified on the authority server), a one-line README edit was kept, and three fetched HTML files
 were moved out of `_raw/` because they contaminated the corpus other workers were checking against.
6. **Verification is a deliverable, not a step.** `SOURCES.md` publishes the verification numbers and
 the four corrected claims, including that the directory cannot vouch for anything that depends on
 a figure or a table lost in the text extraction.
7. **Nothing outbound.** No message, email or form was sent to him or to anyone about him. The only
 contact that exists is the owner's own voice messages, which are archived, not answered.
