**D8323 · 2026-10-19 · Whole-bill baseline at 462.54 per 30 days; two instruments with two questions deliberately.**

## 2026-10-19T23:25Z · ZABZ-YOGA · the whole bill, and why two instruments is the right count

`bill_report.py` prices every provider per model and reports what it cannot price as **unpriced token
volume — counted, never costed, never zero.** Frozen whole-bill baseline for the pre-change week:
**107.9259 / week = 462.5394 / 30 days.** DeepSeek is **93 %** of it (100.43), Google ~32.6/month,
and six OpenAI calls are honestly unpriced rather than silently zero.

### The two instruments disagree by ~1.25 a week, and that is correct

`weekly_report.py` prices *every* DeepSeek row at the `deepseek-flash` card, because it answers the
peak/off-peak question and one card keeps the band comparison clean. `bill_report.py` prices each row at
its **own** model, so the 1,618 `deepseek-v4-pro` rows cost their real 1.32/3.96. The 1.25/week is
exactly those rows.

Neither number is wrong; quoting either as the other would be. **Two tools with two clearly-named
questions beat one tool with a flag**, because a flag is where a reader stops checking which question
they asked.

### Google is ~32.6/month, not ~48

Consistent with round 11: `gemini-3.5-flash` was priced at the 5.00 unknown-model fallback instead of
1.00. The audit's ledger-derived figure was an upper bound on a number that was never a rate.
