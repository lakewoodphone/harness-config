**D69 · 2026-09-14 · Any read that can fail to determine returns that failure as a field, not as an empty list.**

Established by the `device_profiles` defect: a table NanoMDM never populates made every device report *no profiles*, and a safety check then printed "MISSING" for profiles that were installed. The contract is now `determination: "known" | "unavailable"` plus a `reason` when unavailable, and callers branch on `determination` — never on an empty array. A genuinely empty list is `known, []` and is a different fact from "the device has never answered". Paired with `observed_at`, so a stale observation can never be presented as current.
*Second half of the decision:* the act of asking a device is a separate endpoint (`POST /profiles/refresh`) from reading what it last said (`GET /profiles/observed`), because waking a phone is a deliberate action with an operational cost, not a side effect of rendering a page.
