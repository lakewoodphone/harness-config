**D - 2026-09-30 - approve both live 300DDR tickets at a >=99% result, because the fee is owed either way (owner's own portal approvals).**

WHAT WAS DECIDED. 300DDR tickets **74115** (Fishman 2TB, 98.12% of files, listing reviewed) and
**73459** (Kogel 256GB flash, chip-off pass 2, >99% of sectors, partition corruption) are both
**approved**. The owner posted both approvals himself, as portal comments in the lab's RepairShopr
portal: 74115 at 09:51, 73459 at 09:59, verbatim *"Approve ticket <n>. Please send me the payment
link."* No message was sent by the agent; nothing was authorised by proxy.

WHY. On the lab's own written terms, a **99%-or-better result has no decline branch** - the 100-per-chip
decline fee is expressly conditioned on recovering *less than* 99% (Nathan, ticket 73459, 2026-08-20,
verbatim in the case file). So declining returns the same bill and no data, while approving returns the
data. The alternative reading - "we can always decline for 100" - was in our own 2026-09-18 policy and
is now marked VOID for a >=99% result.

WHAT IT COMMITS US TO. 74115: 500 to the lab, about 495 net (customer 900 plus the 160 deposit that is
not a credit, less the fee and shipping). 73459: 500 plus the transfer medium and return shipping
(charges exclude all three and we supplied no transfer drive), against the customer's approved 700
tier - roughly 160 net, under the 200-per-drive floor. The customer conversation on Golembick's price
(row #128, recommendation 450) remains the owner's and is still open.
