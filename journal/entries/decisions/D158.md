## What was decided

**The owner's own queue now reaches the surface he actually reads, and the parts of it
that were never his are gone.**

1. **`check_owner_queue` added to the CEO kernel** (`~/ceo-kernel`, commit `9962993`).
 The sentinel now reports exactly ONE decision — the next one — with its true age,
 severity, whether it is blocking, and the recommendation that goes with it. Ordering
 mirrors `~/bin/owner-queue.py next` (blocking, then severity, then older, then id) so
 the badge and the CLI cannot disagree about what is next. 15 checks now, payload
 verified at 2026-09-15T17:14Z.
 - Severity is bounded deliberately: attention only past 7 days, or past 3 days when
 the item is blocking other work. A queue is never empty, so "pending > 0" would be
 a countdown to numbness. What makes it green is answering it or me resolving it
 because it turned out to be engineering — an act, not the passage of time.
 - Age is computed from `asked_at`; the unrefreshed `age_days` column is a fallback and
 a ≥1 day drift is reported (L-new, `age_column_stale_ids: [15, 14]`).
 - Why this and not something else: the queue, the CLI and the journal mirror all
 existed, and **nothing the owner opens rendered any of it**. He had asked for a queue
 he reads from "when we have time", one at a time, with a recommendation. Before this,
 a blocking decision aged on a count nobody saw.

2. **The queue was drained 14 → 11 pending**, on the rule that anything solvable by me is
 in the wrong place. Resolved with recorded reasons:
 - **#39** (cheap-first semantic routing): a dev/config question, never his. The two
 stores disagreed; the row was aligned to `.env`, so both now say `false`. Enabling
 cheap-first is a cost/quality default that is mine to set, and it goes on as a

 Closed as deferred rather than left occupying a slot in a queue he reads one item at

 no engineering content in it. The other half — the company routed a medical task to
