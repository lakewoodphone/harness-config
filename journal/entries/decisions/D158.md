**D (2026-09-15) - Two secrets, not one: the notification image needs the LAN URL and the tap-through link needs the public one. They are different transports with different authentication.**

Follows L1525, where repointing a single `phoenix_external_base_url` at the Cloudflare-Access-gated
tunnel would have broken the picture *inside* notifications **at the shop**, while fixing only the
link.

**What exists now:**

 secrets.yaml
 phoenix_external_base_url: "http://192.168.50.34:8123" # LAN -- the in-app image
 phoenix_public_base_url: "https://ha.abletelsolutions.com" # public -- tap-through

`automations/access_control.yaml`, 4 notification blocks, each now sets both:

 image: '{{ snapshot_url }}' <- app fetches it silently; must NOT need a login
 clickAction: '{{ snapshot_url_public }}' <- the human taps it; a Cloudflare login is fine

**Why the split is necessary and not a preference.** `image:` is downloaded by the phone app in the
background with no session, so a login-gated URL returns a login page instead of a JPEG. Clicking is
a human in a browser, who can authenticate once and keep the cookie. One secret cannot serve both.

**The bug in my own edit, caught before it could fire.** My regex matched `snapshot_url: "..."` and
inserted the public twin after it -- 3 of 4 blocks. The fourth used **single quotes**
(`snapshot_url: '...'`), so it was missed while its `clickAction` was still switched, leaving a
notification that referenced an undefined variable at runtime. Found by asserting the count of
*definitions* against the count of *usages*, not by trusting the edit; fixed, and the check now
reports **0 blocks with an undefined public link**. YAML parses, `automation.reload` returns 200, the
hallway-guard automations are `on`, and the log carries no template errors.

**VERIFIED as far as it can be without sending:** the config, the templates and the reload are all
proved. **The final step is a real notification, which is the owner's to trigger or authorise** --
nothing outbound is sent on my initiative.

**Backups:** `access_control.yaml.bak-publiclink-20260915-133550`,
`secrets.yaml.bak-baseurl-*`, `secrets.yaml.before-revert-*`.

**Still the honest limitation:** from home, tapping the link opens a Cloudflare Access login before
the snapshot. Making it load with no login at all means an Access bypass for `/local/snapshots/`,
which would put camera stills on the open internet behind nothing but a guessable filename. That is
the owner's risk call and I have not made it.
