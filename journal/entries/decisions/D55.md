**D55 · 2026-09-14 · The journal has three tiers - append-only log, rewritten state, generated index.**

*Decided:* the journal is three tiers with different rules, because it was one tier doing three jobs badly.

- **`log/`** — history. Append-only, sharded by kind and month, oldest first. **Never read whole**: reads go
 through the index and take a slice. An entry is never edited; a correction is a new entry citing the old one.
- **`state/`** — what is true now: `NOW.md`, `open-pain.md`, `owner-questions.md`, `in-flight.md`. Small,
 rewritten, each carrying `Updated:`. This is where the "read everything to find out what is open" tax is paid
 once instead of per session.
- **`index/entries.tsv`** — generated from the shards, never hand-edited. It is what makes a 198 KB shard
 cost 4 KB to query.

*Reasoning:* the previous design conflated them, so a reader who wanted present tense read 1,067 lines of
`PAIN.md`, most of it past. The third tier exists because the second and first are derivable from each other
only expensively, and `check` verifies the state tier is not older than the newest handoff.
*Consequence:* `journal/README.md` is now the map; the old README's six-file table is in `archive/`.
