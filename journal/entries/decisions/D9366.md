**D9366 · 2026-10-22 · Authority disk phase 3 done: session events archived to R2 and pruned to a 14-day window, nightly job installed.**

Phase 3 of the disk strategy is implemented and verified, 2026-10-22 19:20-20:18Z, on his conditional approval ("as long as you research it and understand it fully and know all the variables").

WHAT WAS MEASURED BEFORE ANY ROW WAS TOUCHED:
- dsh_session_events 13,916 MiB + its FTS data 4,655 MiB + two indexes 421 MiB = 19.96 GB, 85.7% of the database.
- The file is not growing (7.9 MB in 3.4 days); the FREELIST drains at 942 MB/day, 174k rows/day.
- Three FTS5 external-content triggers (ai/ad/au) exist, so deleting a row removes its index entry automatically. No FTS repair step.
- The esearch adapters (localdb/sync.py, secretary/sync.py) contain NO DELETE: they copy into usearch.db and never prune it, so archiving source days does not remove them from search. This was the capability risk that would have forbidden the whole plan, and it is not real.
- dsh_session_events_archive.py already existed and does the right thing: one zstd JSONL per day, re-read, sha256 + row count compared against the table, prints ARCHIVED+VERIFIED, never deletes. Used as-is. Do not write a third archive tool.

THE RUN: keep 14 days, archive the 24 older ones.
- 1,647,127 rows, 7.20 GB raw JSONL -> 1.14 GiB zstd, every day ARCHIVED+VERIFIED with its sha256.
- New tool `/usr/local/bin/prune-archived-days.py` is the only thing that deletes. It refuses any day whose archive does not decompress to exactly the row count the database holds (all 24 matched), and deletes in 25,000-row rowid-range batches with short transactions so company-api's 10 s busy timeout is never hit. 207 seconds total.
- Result: freelist 2,599,701 -> 4,365,432 pages = 9.92 -> 16.65 GiB of reusable space; file unchanged at 31.75 GB; rows 3.37M -> 1.73M; live window 2026-10-08..2026-10-22.

WHY THIS IS THE FIX: the live set is now a fixed window. Each night the day that ages out is archived, verified and deleted, and the freed pages are reused by that day's ingest, so the file stops growing. VACUUM INTO remains available to return the 16.6 GiB to the filesystem (needs ~23 GB scratch and a quiet window) - it is now housekeeping, not rescue.

DURABILITY: the archives are the only copy of those days. They are in R2 under authority/session-archive/<day>.jsonl.zst - 25 objects, each size read back after upload (uploaded=25 skipped=0 failed=0) - and locally in /root/archive/dsh-session-events.

SCHEDULED: /usr/local/bin/dsh-session-archive-nightly.sh at 03:30 via cron-wrap.sh. Refuses under 12 GB free, aborts the prune if the archive step exits non-zero, logs each step. Tested end to end as a no-op the same evening: archive exit=0, prune exit=0, offsite exit=0, OK.

NEVER AGAIN: re-archive an existing day without --force; delete a day whose archive has not been proven by decompressed record count. The delete is irreversible and the archive is the only copy.

Also recorded as L12266: the fills were never caused by disk size (interval flat at 1-3 days across 76 -> 152 -> 225 GiB), and two of five research children died from their own unbounded du on the box they were measuring.
