**An activity that needs a harness, form-fitting clothing, or a stranger's hands on your equipment is out when the sisters are coming — and the constraint comes before the research**

2026-09-28, Chol Hamoed. The owner's mother asked for a Chol Hamoed outing for him and his brothers. By the
time the plan was built, **Chaya (19) was coming too, and she said rock climbing is not tzniyusdig.**

She is right, and three concrete things make it so:

1. A climbing **harness** is worn around the waist and thighs, over athletic clothing, for the whole session.
2. It is a **mixed public gym**; bouldering means sitting and lying on shared mats in front of adult
 strangers, and the sport is inherently stretch-and-contort.
3. **The first-timer packages put an instructor on your rope.** Staff Belay (70 for one or two climbers) and
 the Introduction to Rock Climbing (35 per climber, groups of 5+) both mean a male staff member belaying a
 19-year-old up a wall. That one alone ends it.

Only bouldering avoids the instructor — no rope, no harness — and it still leaves 1 and 2.

**THE RULE:** before recommending an outing to a mixed family group that includes the sisters, check whether
the activity requires a harness, form-fitting clothing, or a stranger's hands on your equipment. If it does,
it is out. **Ask who is coming and what they can do BEFORE researching the venue**, not after.

**What it cost not to ask first.** By the time she raised it I had a complete recommendation built — 16,000
sq ft of climbing, verified day-pass prices, drive time, minimum ages, a sensible team split, and a phone call
to make a reservation. All of it for a building she would not walk into. Had she not said it herself, that
recommendation would have gone out and the trip would have been planned around a venue the family would not
use. The research was correct and useless, and one question at the start would have reordered the entire
shortlist.

**What was chosen instead:** *Activate Games, 893 St Georges Ave, Woodbridge NJ* — 45 minutes away, 24.99 per
player on a weekday, open until 10 PM, twelve game rooms, teams hard-capped at five, each group alone in its
room. No harness, no heights, no instructor, ordinary clothes. It clears the rule on the merits rather than
by talking her past it, and it is analysed in full in the household record.
