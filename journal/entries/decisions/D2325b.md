**D2325b · 2026-09-22 · Kosher Waze data billing: postpaid Stripe subscription, base + metered GB reported as idempotent deltas.**

**Decision (mine — engineering, not owner):** Kosher Waze data is billed as a **postpaid Stripe subscription with a base monthly price plus a metered per-GB component**, matching the owner's already-stated model ("they pay per usage they don't pick an amount, they just change the cap"). No prepaid top-up, no prepaid GB. The device is a separate one-time payment.

**The correctness rule:** Telnyx reports *cumulative* consumption, so `billable_mb_target = clamp(consumed_mb - allowance_mb, 0, cap_mb - allowance_mb)` and only the **delta** is reported, with `already_reported_mb` advanced **only after Stripe acknowledges**, under a deterministic idempotency key of (serial, subscription_item, period_start, billable_mb_target). This is what makes a crash-retry safe.

**Where it runs:** on the LPT site backend, NOT the fleet host. The site holds customer identity and the Stripe account; the fleet host only knows usage. The site pulls; the fleet does not push.

**Fail closed:** `coverage.complete == false` from the usage source means REFUSE to bill that device and record why - never bill zero and render it as a $0 month. An empty result is a refusal, not health.

**Why this needed saying at all:** there was no billing-grade usage source. `/fleet/telnyx/usage` returns a rolling 100-row window and the Telnyx WDR endpoint 404s on this IoT account (fleet_api.py L1848-1850). A per-customer bill could not be computed or defended from what existed on 2026-09-22.
