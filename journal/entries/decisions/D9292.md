## Decision 2026-10-21 · The number from one to five is a bundle - content first, friction with it - and every part stays adjustable (Q21)

aliases: Q21 · D-20261021-20 · docs/decisions/D-20261021-20-level-is-a-bundle.md

Owner's answer, verbatim: "3". Which is: the number is a bundle — choosing it pre-fills the content and the friction together, and the customer then adjusts individual knobs from there.

1. A level sets everything at once: picking four gives a complete, defensible phone in one choice — which categories are blocked, the picture thresholds, how long changes wait, how much accountability is on by default.
2. The headline meaning of the number is content. "Level four" answers the question a parent is actually asking — what does this block — and the friction comes with it rather than defining it.
3. Every part stays adjustable afterwards. The number is a shortcut, not a cage: four's categories with one's delays is a legal setup.
4. Leaving the bundle is visible, not silent — the summary says "you are on a custom setup, based on four".

Now required: five bundles authored as data, with the two existing CommunityContentProfiles thresholds as the bottom half of that table; the bundle is a snapshot at the moment of choosing, so a later redefinition never rewrites a household, which is offered the update explicitly; the interview shows the bundle before applying it; a custom setup is first-class with its own accountability line; the level must reach the device (owner_level.py writes a value nothing reads — work item 933).
