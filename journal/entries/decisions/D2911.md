**D2911 · 2026-10-04 · The meals record exists: kashrus sets a dish's shape before nutrition does, and both lasagna dip and lahmajin are real meals only in specific builds.**

The household now has a meals record at ~/code/home/meals/, with nine rules in its README that decide
every recipe in it. The load-bearing ones: kashrus decides the shape of a dish before nutrition gets
a vote; in a MILCHIG dish the protein must come from the dairy base because removing the meat removes
the protein; in a FLEISHIG dish the dairy is usually in the SERVING SUGGESTION rather than the
recipe; and fat is a technique constraint in a kosher kitchen, not a taste preference.

Two dishes written up on 2026-10-04:

LASAGNA DIP (owner and his wife). Verdict: a real meal only on a rebuilt base. The published recipe
is 617 kcal / 28 g protein / 50 g fat per serving and is treif, because every version puts Italian
sausage or ground beef in a cheese base. Rebuilt MILCHIG on cottage cheese + part-skim ricotta it is
413 kcal / 34 g protein / 17 g fat. The decision tool is protein per 100 kcal, not per 100 g: cottage
cheese 13.1, part-skim mozzarella 9.4, parmesan 9.2, ricotta 8.3, cream cheese 1.8, sour cream 1.2.
I corrected myself in-file: halving the parmesan to cut sodium costs 1.5 g protein per serving to save
64 mg - keep it, and fix the sauce instead, which is worth minus 385 mg at no protein cost.

LAHMAJIN (asked for as "the little breads with meat on top"). Verdict: yes, and it is naturally
correct for the house - meat on bread, no dairy - but the dairy is in eight separate published
serving suggestions (ayran, tan, labneh, yogurt) and the official Turkish Ministry of Culture dough
carries 330 g of yogurt per 500 g flour. Recommended recipe is 480 kcal / 20 g protein per flatbread;
a classic paper-thin one is 13 g and needs three of them to make a supper. The topping loading is the
protein lever.

Files pushed to both remotes (origin + authority). Journal entries: D2911 and the lessons below.
