**D9347 · 2026-10-21 · Describe-the-picture models: cloud APIs blocked on contract, CPU misses by 100x, on-device 348MB; geometry covers 3 of 5 with no training; the real question is whether a frame may leave the phone.**

## "Models that just describe picture content" — analysed against our own evidence

Owner asked what about models that simply describe the picture, so nothing needs training. This is the
estate's fourth judgement engine (V, vision-language) and docs/research/016-options-on-device-and-off-device
already priced it three ways on 2026-09-11. Conclusions, with the numbers that decide them.

**The describing-model path is real, and none of the three placements is free.**

- **Cloud consumer VLM APIs — blocked on contract before legality.** OpenAI retains image inputs for manual
 CSAM review *even under ZDR*; Anthropic mandates 30-day retention even for ZDR organisations; Google and
 Anthropic bar the capable tiers from consumer use; xAI is 18+; DeepSeek disclaims children's data
 (016:96, OFF-5). The obvious "just call GPT" route cannot carry screenshots of a child's screen.
- **CPU VLM on our own server — measured and misses by ~100x.** InternVL2-2B one yes/no on a Core Ultra 7:
 8.89 s/frame at 448x448, 44.15 s/frame at 768x768 (016:95, OFF-4). Resolution, not parameter count, is the
 lever. Verdict in the doc: CPU is a classifier fallback, not a VLM path.
- **On-device small VLM — impossible on the phones we sell.** SmolVLM2-256M is a 348 MB floor against a
 ~512 MB per-app ceiling on the low tier, and its accuracy sits below GPT-5-Nano's 53.7% (016:74, ON-5).

**The one genuinely promising describing model.** Shieldstral 1.0 3B (Apache-2.0, released 2026-08-04) is a
policy-adaptive multimodal safety classifier: the policy is written in natural language and judged at
inference with a single-token yes/no, re-targetable without retraining, on one GPU (016:97, OFF-6). It is the
cleanest way to make "tight clothing", "hemline", "is a woman visible" prompts rather than models. Two
load-bearing caveats: its accuracy on our five attributes is unmeasured, and its continuous score must not be
used as a gate because calibration error is measured up to ECE 0.496 (016:41-43).

**What needs no describing model at all.** Geometry covers three of the five attributes by measurement —
Pose lite + selfie multiclass + hair segmenter, +22 MB, Apache-2.0, and MediaPipe is already a shipped
dependency, so no new runtime (016:70, :81-84). Only tightness needs a trained head.

**Economics, if we ever do leave the device.** Serverless GPU: 1.11 dollars/month at 50 users, 22 dollars at
1,000, assuming 10 escalations/user/day on an L4 (016:93). A 24/7 GPU is idle 91% of the time at our volume
and the crossover is 3.3-7.8M frames/month — do not buy one (016:94).

**The lever worth more than the model.** Deny-first: blur first, reveal only on a positive safe verdict, which
converts a wrong model from an exposure into an annoyance (016:131, X-1).

**Therefore the decision is not "which describing model".** 016 §6 lists one dependency underneath ON-1,
ON-2, OFF-2, OFF-4 and OFF-6 alike: **A1 — may a frame leave the phone at all.** Every architecture choice,
cost and legal posture keys off that single answer, and it has never been put to him. That is the question.

Recommendation: **nothing leaves the device.** Local-only is also the market's advertised feature in this
sector (owner-queue #511 records competitors selling the absence of connectivity as the kosher differentiator),
and our own compliance work says making the vendor call unnecessary dissolves the COPPA and EU Chapter V
problem entirely rather than mitigating it. Hold the serverless policy model as a measured upgrade, not a launch
dependency, and revisit only if the on-device measurement shows the gap is real and large.
