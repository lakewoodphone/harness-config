**D9313 · 2026-10-21 · Sticker review: ghost stays, teal stays, no managed-device line (device cannot be reset).**

**Sticker review, decisions taken 2026-10-21 (owner, one question at a time):**

1. **Name on the device: KOSHER Waze.** His word: "Kosher Waze". Against BOX-SPEC 5.1's recommendation; recorded in D9312.
2. **The Waze ghost mascot stays.** His word: "with gohost". Shown A/B against a ghost-free re-layout (same type sizes) at artifacts/kosher-waze-sticker/variants/compare-ghost.png.
3. **Palette stays teal -> near-black with chartreuse type.** His word: "so keep it". The box keeps its near-black/off-white/gold; the two palettes are deliberately not harmonised.
4. **No managed-device warning line on either sticker.** Reason, his words: "the device cannot be reset we blocked that". A warning about a failure the customer cannot cause is noise. The same wrong premise was in BOX-SPEC 4's warranty copy ("Do not erase, restore, or remove the management profile") and is corrected there to say the device cannot be erased or factory reset.

Consequence for the print run: the artwork is the existing design with layout fixes only, so nothing is being redesigned at the last minute. Still mine to decide and to show: the phone number format, and the portrait panel's thin keyline.
