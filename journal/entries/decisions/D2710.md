**2026-09-28 · The household record is a real remote-backed repo, and trip records live in `home/trips/`**

Decided and executed 2026-09-28 15:35 ET.

**What was wrong.** `~/code/home` — the household record repo, which the journal itself cites as the full
record for household decisions (D2656: *"Full record … `~/code/home/maintenance/2026-09-27-bathroom-drain-slowness.md`"*)
— **had no git remote and existed on one disk: the ZABZ-YOGA laptop.** Every journal entry pointing at it was
pointing at a file with no copy anywhere else, in a business that has lost data twice.

**Decision.** The household record is version-controlled and remote-backed like `harness-config`:
- bare origin **`secratary:/home/zabz/home.git`** (created 2026-09-28, the same idiom as
 `/home/zabz/harness-config.git` and `/home/zabz/ceo-kernel.git`)
- branch `main`, cloned at `~/code/home` on **ZABZ-YOGA** and **ZABZ-TECH**
- pushed from ZABY-YOGA (`b3d6828`), then ZABZ-TECH committed and pushed (`48c137a`); both machines verified
 at the same revision
- **`home/trips/` is the folder for trip and outing records**, one file per trip or holiday period,
 date-prefixed, same rules as `maintenance/` (record the sources, record what was rejected and why)

**Why not GitHub.** Nothing here belongs in the business org, and it is household material. The authority
already hosts the other bare repos and is reachable from every node over Tailscale, so this adds no new
credential, no new service and no new failure mode.

**Why not leave it in `reports/`.** `~/code/reports` on ZABZ-YOGA is not a repo at all, and on ZABZ-TECH it
is a repo with no remote. Anything written there is machine-local by construction — which is exactly how a
document survived a night of work and was then found to be wrong with nowhere authoritative to correct it.

**Consequence.** The loose overnight file is now a one-line SUPERSEDED pointer to
`home/trips/2026-09-28-chol-hamoed-5787-sukkos-trips.md`, so the two cannot drift.
