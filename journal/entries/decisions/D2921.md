**D2921 · 2026-10-05 · D: LPT charges a 50 fee for any time spent, always kept, and never credited.**

OWNER INSTRUCTION, 2026-10-05, verbatim: "okay 50 is a fee for me doing anything that takes
time with it, be it labor or diagnosing. when it''s either, it''s non refundable cuz i did the
diagnosis or labor. when i don''t fix something that let''s say i was going to charge 100 dollars
for then we only keep the 50 dollar fee i take for even trying."

THE MODEL
A 50 fee is charged for anything that takes the owner''s time - labour or diagnosis. Because work
was done, it is NEVER refunded. What differs is whether it is INSIDE the quoted number or ON TOP
of it:

 50 was an ATTEMPT at the repair, no separate diagnostic work
 fixed -> the quoted repair price (the 50 is already inside it)
 not fixed -> 50. That is all that is kept.
 there WAS extra diagnostic work that took real time
 fixed -> repair price + 50 (the diagnosis is its own job)
 not fixed -> 50

WHY THE TWO CASES DIFFER
The attempt IS the first part of the repair, so charging separately would charge twice for one
job. Diagnosis is NOT the repair - it is a second piece of work - so it stands alone and adds.

WHICH CASE APPLIES: the owner''s judgement, case by case at the bench. Owner''s words: "case by
case i think". The customer NEVER hears this distinction; explaining it is the sentence that
makes a fee sound arbitrary.

STARTING WORK: when the 50 is toward the repair, NO work starts until the customer approves. So
there is no "declined after we began" case to price.

THREE WRONG VERSIONS I PRODUCED AND WAS CORRECTED ON (each sounded defensible and was
commercially wrong - this is why the model must be read, not re-derived):
 1. "the 50 is credited toward the repair" - taken from the 2026-10-05 pivot plan doc. Wrong as
 a general rule; that doc described only the attempt case. Nothing is credited.
 2. "if the 50 paid for diagnosis it comes off the repair" - BACKWARDS. Diagnosis adds.
 3. "50 on top of every job" - wrong; in the attempt case it is already inside the price.

CUSTOMER-FACING PATTERN, derived from what worked:
 lead with the PRICE not the fee; give the 50 a factual reason ("that is us opening it up");
 state the failure outcome ONCE and LAST ("you are only out the 50" beats "non-refundable");
 say what is included so no fourth number appears later; do NOT write both outcomes into every
 message or you invent a risk that is not there.
