**D3145 · 2026-10-18 · T-Mobile: a DISPUTE, not a debt - its balance mail must never be queued as a payment decision.**

## The correction, verbatim

Owner, 2026-10-18, dismissing owner-queue row **#636** ("T-Mobile service suspended — $105 overdue"):

> "i told you a few times already i tried to cancel the service and it didn't work so i just stpped paying, we dealt with this already."

## The standing facts

1. He requested cancellation of the T-Mobile account.
2. T-Mobile did not action the cancellation.
3. He deliberately stopped paying in response.

The reason the account is unpaid is (1) plus (2): a cancellation he asked for and did not get. The
balance is **DISPUTED**, not owed-and-forgotten. It is not a bill he forgot or refused to look at.

## The instruction

**T-Mobile balance mail must never be queued as a payment decision.** Not "pay $105", not "pay the
overdue bill", not "restore your service by paying". It is a dispute with a named history, and
presenting it as money he owes is the mistake he has now corrected more than once.

## What was built so it cannot repeat

`/root/bin/owner-queue-suppress.txt` — a data file of suppressed senders, read by
`/root/bin/owner-queue-ingest-mail.py` **before** the model judge sees the message. A match is
recorded in `owner_mail_verdict` as verdict `drop`, class `dispute`, `money` NULL, with the rule's
reason in `why`; it is never written to `owner_decision_queue`. Two rules: `financialservices.t-mobile.com`
(any subject) and `@t-mobile.com` with a billing subject. A non-billing T-Mobile message (for example
a password change) is still judged normally, so account security is not lost with the billing noise.

## The rule this leaves behind

A reversal is not a debt (L3124). A balance on an account he asked to close, and deliberately stopped
paying, is a dispute. Before queueing any money item, read the decision that produced it.
