**D175 · 2026-09-15 · Sign corrected: no Closed wording, 4 walk-in days only, call-or-text band; supersedes D173's sign content.**

Corrects the sign section of **D173**, written earlier in the same session before the owner saw the
first render.

The first version listed seven rows and printed "Closed" for Wednesday and Saturday, with "Sunday —
by appointment". The owner's reply, verbatim: *"don't write saturday, its shabbos, and anyways its
for sure clsoed then, leave that day off, and dont write closed for wednesday and sunday, leave
wednesday shabbos and sunday off, and write on bottom that for sunday wednesday and night hours you
can call or text to see if we are avaialble"*.

**Final sign content — four rows, no "Closed" anywhere:**

```
MONDAY 10:30 AM – 5:30 PM
TUESDAY 10:30 AM – 5:30 PM
THURSDAY 10:30 AM – 5:30 PM
FRIDAY 10:30 AM – 1:00 PM
```
then the teal band *FEEL FREE TO CALL OR TEXT ANYTIME / (732) 655-2355*, then a khaki band:
**"Sunday, Wednesday & evening hours / Call or text to see if we're available."**

Rows are now 330px tall with ~120pt type, sized by a `fit_font()` helper that shrinks until the day
and the time both fit inside the card, so a future copy edit cannot silently overflow the border.

**And the same rule now governs the website, not just the sign** (commit `5adc55903` on
`fix/walkin-hours-20260916`, on top of PR #142):

| Surface | New wording |
|---|---|
| backend closed-hours greeting | four open days, then "For Sunday, Wednesday or evening hours, call or text to see if we are available." |
| HomePage hero badge line 2 | `24/6 Secure Drop-off • Sun, Wed & evenings: call or text` |
| landing ContactSection note | `Sun, Wed & evening hours — call or text to see if we're available` |
| landing FAQSection + FAQ data hook | same offer instead of "We're closed Wednesday and Saturday" |

**The one place that cannot follow this rule: Google Business Profile.** Google renders all seven
days and only accepts hours-or-closed per day, with no per-day custom text. Wednesday and Saturday
will read *Closed* there regardless. That is a Google constraint, not a choice — recorded in
`docs/marketing/google-business-profile-content.md` so a future session does not "fix" the mismatch
by reintroducing "Closed" onto the shop's own materials.

Verified after the change: backend `business-hours` 37/37; frontend `ContactSection` + `HomePage`
43 pass / 3 fail — the same three failures that exist on pristine `origin/test`. Both new
call-or-text assertions pass by name.
