**D · 2026-09-25 · The Shabbat power-down moved to sunset − 180 min on the owner's own instruction, and it fired the same minute it was applied.**

The owner asked, in a ZABZ-YOGA session at ~17:00 EDT (quoted verbatim in that
session's transcript): *"i want the shutdown to happen 3 hours before sunset from now
on, not so close to sunset, do you understand?"* — about the HA + Dahua camera
power-down executed by the secretary's Shabbat orchestrator.

At 21:01:38 UTC the yoga session applied it on the authority: `.env`
`SHABBAT_CANDLE_OFFSET_MIN=180` (line 478), `app/config.py`
`shabbat_candle_offset_min: int = 180` (was 60), `app/services/shalom_zmanim.py`
`DEFAULT_CANDLE_OFFSET_MIN = 180` (was 60), with the pre-change files kept at
`~/shabbat-offset-backup/20260925-210138/`. `secretary-api` was restarted at 21:02:32.

Because the new OFF time (15:48 EDT) was already in the past when the change landed,
the every-minute watchdog's `recover_missed_off()` treated it as an OFF that had
never happened and completed it at **21:04:09 UTC** — 1 h 16 min after the new
deadline, 43 min before the old one. The sequence was the safe one: interior door
held released and proven (8/8 samples over 15 s, tamper automation disabled), then
the cut, with `reverify_door: unlocked: True` immediately before it.

So the power-down itself was correct and the door was never at risk. What the owner
experienced as an "emergency shutdown" was the catch-up path doing what it is
designed to do, four minutes after a config change, with nobody told. Recorded so
that the next session does not read the 21:04 event as a fault it has to atone for —
and so that "apply the new offset, then watch the watchdog catch up" is understood as
the expected consequence of editing that value mid-window.
