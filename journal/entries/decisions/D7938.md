**D7938 · 2026-10-19 · Computer finder: inspected before sale, 30 days to return a machine with an issue.**

His words, verbatim, 2026-10-19, third dictation on the computer finder (extends D7938 and D7937):

"We inspect them before they go out. However, if there's an issue, you have 30 days to return it"

WHAT THE PAGE MAY NOW SAY
- Every machine is inspected before it goes out.
- If there is an issue with the machine, the customer has 30 days to return it. This is the single
 exception to the no-returns rule, and it is conditional on there being something wrong with the
 machine — not on the customer changing their mind.

STILL NOT STATED, and it cannot be written until he does: what the customer gets when they return a
faulty machine inside the 30 days — the machine repaired, a replacement, or their money back — and
whether the 75 deposit is included in what comes back. Recommendation put to him 2026-10-19: the
deposit is part of the price, so a refunded machine returns the whole amount including the 75.
