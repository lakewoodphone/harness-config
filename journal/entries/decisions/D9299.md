**D9299 · 2026-10-21 · The chaver is invited, must accept, and is told he holds the phone's recovery.**

aliases: Q28

Owner, verbatim (2026-10-21):

> "2"

Which is: he is invited, he accepts, and the acceptance covers both the role and the reset anchor.

What it decides, condensed:

1. No chaver exists without his own yes. An invitation is sent, he reads what it means, he accepts or
 declines; a nomination alone creates nothing.
2. The invitation states the anchor plainly: his account is what a wiped phone asks for, he may be the only
 one who can bring it back, and losing it sends the phone to the bench and erases it. A responsibility, not
 a notification setting.
3. It states what he will be told and what he may do, from the dials the owner set.
4. He may leave at any time, and leaving is the customer's right too; the seven-day offboarding delay applies
 and both sides are told.
5. The acceptance is recorded with a date and the text he accepted, so a chaver can be shown the page and a
 household cannot claim he agreed. Declined or unanswered is a visible state: "invited, not yet accepted",
 the trust cap behaves as unpartnered, the household is prompted.
6. The anchor cannot be armed on an unaccepted chaver. Widening his powers is a fresh consent, not an edit.
 One invitation, one reminder, then nothing.
