**D9351 · 2026-10-21 · Five rulings on the kosher filter: rungs 9-10 retired, rung 2 is skin not swimwear, modes chosen by the family, per-device certs, no patent spend yet.**

## 2026-10-21 · ZABZ-TECH · Five rulings on the kosher filter, taken in conversation with the owner

All recorded in `kosher-filter-ai` on branch `research/on-device-cascade-20261021` (pushed, head
`08e50e7`). Each one here so a future session does not re-litigate it.

### D1 — Levels 9 and 10 are retired

Owner: *"okay we'll leave out 9 and 10 then"*, after being shown neither has a model, dataset or benchmark,
and that 10 is a composition judgement ("is this frame built around her") with no model in existence.

Written into `docs/corpus/LADDER.md` as a dated ruling. **The numbers are not renumbered** — 165 ratings in
`corpus/labels/` are labelled against that numbering and shifting it would corrupt the corpus. 9 and 10 stay
vacant.

Scope consequence, which is a promise and not a backlog cut: **the ladder no longer claims to catch a
romantic or intimate scene.** That must not appear in customer-facing copy.

Architecture consequence: every surviving rung is a garment, coverage or exposure judgement, so two model
families collapse into one.

### D2 — Rung 2 is a skin measurement, not a swimwear classifier

Owner: *"2 doesn't have to be swimwear, it could just be skin showing like low neckline."* Correct, and it
unblocks the rung.

Every surviving garment rung is the same primitive over a different region: skin pixels over region pixels,
region from pose landmarks. Rung 2 is torso+hips, 5 is below the collarbone, 6 is distal limb segments, 7 is
above the knee, 8 is chest and thigh. This is Lyu et al., MDPI BDCC 8(4):35, 2024 — F1 0.82 long sleeve /
0.87 short sleeve / 0.80 trousers, skin step under 0.1 ms — whose only blocker was OpenPose at 2.9 s, and
our pose runs at about 20 ms.

So no swimwear dataset is needed, and `research/08`'s non-commercial iMaterialist finding stops mattering.
Buildable-now rose from four rungs to five: **1, 2, 5, 7-hem, 8.** Rungs 4 and 6 are household settings.
Rung 3 and the opaque-sleeve half of 7 are still blocked on opacity, which nothing labels.

**With it, a defect to avoid:** the published skin window is lighter-skin biased (Jones' Bayes ~F1 47 on
Fitzpatrick VI). Ship the learned colour-augmented classifier (Xu et al. CVPRW 2022, F1 90.9), never a
colour-threshold rule.

### D3 — The filter is not one transport. The family chooses the mode.

Owner: *"Anything we can do on device we do but anything not then we do through proxy or different things.
So we have multiple filter options depending what you want and it's like a cascade."*

Recorded as `docs/corpus/FILTER-MODES.md`. Four layers — network; device-without-certificate;
device-with-a-per-device-key (default); device-plus-broker — each with what it sees, what it is blind to,
and what the family must trust. The mode is installed by us and chosen by the household.

This is the same principle as rungs 4 and 6 being household settings: **where the answer depends on what a
household will tolerate, expose the dial instead of hiding the trade inside a model's error rate.**

### D4 — Every device gets its own certificate. Never a shared root.

Not a new owner instruction but the engineering constraint D3 requires, recorded because the honest answer
to the certificate objection depends on it entirely. A shared master key is exactly as bad as the fear
says; a per-device key generated at provisioning, never held centrally, is not.

**If this is done lazily later, we inherit precisely the fear Emunet is exploiting, and we will deserve
it.**

### D5 — Compete on learning, not on anxiety. No patent spend yet.

Owner: *"I'm not so scared of them... we didn't invest money into this, like even if they end up being
better, it's fine... keep designing, learn from their filter, take the good parts, leave the bad parts."*

So the patent position is **deferred, not dismissed**. The free mitigation is already the plan: emit a
structured attribute vector rather than a score, and evaluate the ladder as policy on the handset — which
is also better technically. **Trigger to revisit: before we ship per-region blur or any customer-facing
build.** Until then no attorney, no spend. Do not queue it.

### The Emunet assessment, in one line

Scudio LLC (Delaware) trading as Emunet; founder Shlome Baum (sold FieldOne to Microsoft 2015); Geder is
the distribution partner; public early access 2026-09-21. **Windows desktop only — no Android or iOS app
exists**, and their own FAQ scopes it to Windows 11 hardware. Same thesis as ours (TrueSight claims gender,
skin+hair exposure and full-body contours), which is our ladder independently derived, with the same
unresolved weak link — gender recognition — and no published accuracy.

The finding worth keeping: **their own Terms of Service reserve every technique their marketing disclaims.**
§5.4 permits remote processing when a device lacks capacity; §6.1 lists proxies, certificate stores, VPN
profiles and packet filters; §6.7 says filtering may require certificates. *"No proxy technology"* is a
current state, not an architectural commitment. Their marketing is running ahead of their architecture, and
that is the mistake to avoid in our own copy.
