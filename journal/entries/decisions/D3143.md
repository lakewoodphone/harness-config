**D3143 · 2026-10-13 · Replace Soylent Complete Meal with Kate Farms Nutrition Shake (pareve OU).**

DECIDED 2026-10-13 (my call, dev/ops level, owner may overrule on taste or price):

- The owner stops relying on Soylent. Not because the product is unsafe — no recall ever for the RTD
 shake, no evidence of harm — but because the supplier is failing: Starco Brands (its owner) filed
 going-concern doubt in its Q2-2026 10-Q, lost its primary supplier, exited retail, and every
 Complete Meal ready-to-drink flavour has been out of stock for months. His own ledger shows no charge
 since 2026-08-05. The July 2026 Custom Bakehouse acquisition is a powder plant, so the RTD line has no
 obvious path back.
- Replacement chosen: **Kate Farms Nutrition Shake — Chocolate**, 12-pack, about 51 USD (4.25/bottle),
 OU Pareve, bought from Amazon. It is the only OU-verified pareve ready-to-drink shake that is a genuine
 complete meal (330 kcal, 16 g protein, 6 g fibre, full micronutrient panel), against Soylent's 400 kcal
 and 3 g fibre. Cost delta about +0.75/bottle, roughly +15 to +22 USD/month at his observed 20-30
 bottles.
- Cheaper labelled alternative, if he prefers it: OWYN chocolate 11.15 oz (OU Pareve, roughly half the
 price) — accepted as a 180 kcal protein shake rather than a meal. Bridge if he wants no change today:
 Soylent Complete Protein chocolate, still in stock, OU-DE, 3.25/bottle.
- Rejected: cholov-yisroel powder + CY milk (about 75 USD/month, more friction, and unnecessary because
 OU-DE/pareve already clears his rule); Soylent powder (not kosher certified); Ensure/Boost/fairlife/
 Muscle Milk (plain OU-D = real dairy); Huel, Vega, Ka'Chava (kashrut unverifiable).

Why recorded: so a future session does not re-run this research, does not re-ask him the cholov yisroel
question he already answered, and does not accidentally recommend a plain OU-D product.
