**D3143 · 2026-10-18 · Raitzik label shipped as v2 (32.7mm): CALL MOM FOR HER NUMBER, WE DON'T NEED ANY PARTS - WE THINK IT'S GARBAGE; supersedes the v1 text quoted in D3142.**

**The Raitzik label that ships is v2, not the text quoted in D3142.** The first version was too terse and the owner corrected it on sight — a correction to the record, not a new decision (the money, the loan and "no parts harvest" are unchanged).

Owner, verbatim: *"a drop too concise, write we don't need any parts and think it's garbage / write tante perel and she should call mom for her number"*

**Tape v2, printed 2026-10-18 13:46 EDT, 32.7 mm, exit 0, printer observed `READY → PRINTING`:**

`TANTE PEREL` / `CALL MOM FOR` / `HER NUMBER` / `USB IS READY` / `$30 TO KEEP IT,` / `OR BORROW FREE` /
`ASK: GIVE THE PC` / `BACK, OR BIN IT?` / `WE DON'T NEED` / `ANY PARTS -` / `WE THINK IT'S` / `GARBAGE`

**What the correction actually taught, and it is the reusable part:** both edits were about *who does the
work* and *what we think*, not about wording. "Get her number" was an instruction with no route; **"CALL MOM
FOR HER NUMBER"** names the source. And **"WE DON'T NEED ANY PARTS - WE THINK IT'S GARBAGE"** is a position,
where "NO PARTS NEEDED" was a neutral fact. A note for a person should say who to ask and what we actually
think, or they will have to come back and ask.

v1 (25.3 mm) is superseded. D3142 quotes the v1 words and is otherwise still correct.
