**D154 · 2026-09-15 · Vendor mail signs under the account-holder name (not the customer alias); the DMP-BD84 case is filed as a device log, not a customer case.**

Two small calls made while logging the DMP-BD84 / RattleByte case. Both are mine to make (process/dev, not the
owner's), both reversible, both recorded so they are not relitigated.

**D1 — Vendor and counterparty mail signs under the account-holder name, not the customer alias.**
The shop's customer-facing identity is intentionally `Daniel` (`app/config.py:399 agent_sms_signature = "Daniel"`;
`app/main.py:14081` — "Customer-facing identity is 'Daniel' only in customer contexts"). That is correct for
customers. It is wrong for vendors: RattleByte knows this account as **Eliyahu Zabrowsky** — the order, the
PayPal receipt and the ticket are all under that name — yet the 2026-09-09 and 2026-09-11 emails to RattleByte
were signed `Daniel`, and the vendor's ticket is addressed to "Eliyahu Zabrowsky" while the body is signed by
someone they have never heard of. For suppliers, banks, RMAs, warranty claims and wholesale accounts the sender
identity should match the account-holder name on their books.
*Scope:* business counterparties only. Customer-facing comms keep `Daniel`. Not a bug in existing code — an
extension of an existing rule to a class of counterparty it did not cover.

**D2 — The DMP-BD84 case is an owner device project, not a customer case, and is filed as a device log.**

ezabz68@gmail.com. Verified empty against the case index first: `lptcase.py find BD84` → no match
(180 cases searched); `find panasonic` and `find bluray` → no confident match.
Filed instead at `hardware-diagnostics/panasonic-dmp-bd84-rattlebyte-diagnostic-2026-09-12.md`, per
