**D154 · 2026-09-15 · Shelf life and severity constants: questions 14 days, question debt 14 days, fresh critical 48 hours.**

**Decision: three shelf-life and severity constants, recorded so they are not re-argued.**

- `AGENT_QUESTION_MAX_AGE_HOURS = 336` (14 days) -- a pending agent question older than that
 becomes `expired` with a `status_note`, applied by the lifecycle sweep every 120 s. Fourteen
 days because a question the shop has not answered in a fortnight is no longer the question it
 was, and the owner's real decisions live in `owner_decision_queue`.
- `QUESTION_DEBT_HOURS = 336` -- the kernel counts only questions inside that window as live
 owner-attention debt; older ones are reported as a note, not as work.
- `FRESH_CRITICAL_HOURS = 48` -- `attention_debt` is CRITICAL only for a critical-severity item
 observed within 48 h; otherwise HIGH with the age stated.

All three are status changes or readings, never deletes: 507 question rows survive with their
text and 313 carry the reason they left the pending set.
