**D229 · 2026-09-16 · The phone gate is the only door; the engine's /api fence stays closed.**

Decided 2026-09-16 on ZABZ-YOGA, against the shape proposed in docs/mesh/50-transport.md 2.1.
THAT PROPOSAL: launch the engine with `--trusted-host <node>.tail93e6e6.ts.net` and publish it.
REJECTED because `--trusted-host` is a STARTUP setting (dsh-web-app/lib/index.js:37), so applying
it means restarting the engine and ending every live session on the node -- including the session
doing the measuring. It also permanently widens what the fence accepts.
TAKEN INSTEAD: scripts/phone-gate.py runs loopback-only in front of the engine and presents Host
AND Origin as 127.0.0.1:<engine port> (--engine-authority). No restart, no name added to
trustedHosts, and the fence still refuses a foreign Host: measured through the tailnet name,
`GET /` 200 with a 30-day cookie, `GET /healthz` 200 application/json, `GET /api` with no
cookie 401, and the engine reached DIRECTLY with `Host: <tailnet FQDN>` still 403.
It is also the only shape that signs a cold visitor in, which a home-screen Web App needs.
