**D9312 · 2026-10-21 · Owner keeps KOSHER Waze as the device sticker name, against BOX-SPEC 5.1.**

**Owner decision, 2026-10-21, in the sticker-design review:** the device sticker keeps **KOSHER Waze** as the product name.

Context: BOX-SPEC.md §5.1 says do not print "Kosher Waze" or "LPT Waze", citing live US Reg. No. 3713203 (Google LLC, Class 009, standard character mark), and §6 settles the product name as LPT NAVIGATION. I put that to him with the two costs and recommended LPT NAVIGATION. His answer, verbatim: **"Kosher Waze"**.

So: the box keeps LPT NAVIGATION (§5.1 unchanged for box artwork), and the device sticker carries KOSHER Waze by explicit owner decision. The exposure is his to hold and it is now recorded as his, not inferred by me. Any future session that reads §5.1 and proposes renaming the sticker is re-litigating a settled decision — point it here.

Still open at the time of writing: whether the Waze ghost mascot artwork stays on the sticker, the colour system (teal/chartreuse vs the box's #111111 / off-white / gold), the phone-number treatment, and whether the "managed device, do not erase" line goes on the portrait panel.
