**D268 · 2026-09-17 · The Bachrach Galaxy filter case ships as the free EDGE method (ADB disable), and the paid managed-filter tier is queued as owner question #97 rather than decided here.**

CONTEXT: Moshe Bachrach'"'"'s Galaxy. Family Link cannot block Google search because the Google app is a
system app and Google does not allow app limits on system apps - verified at source 2026-09-17.

DECIDED (mine, as engineering):
- The procedure documents `pm suspend` first (most reversible), then `pm disable-user`, then
 `pm uninstall --user 0`, with the reversal command written next to each - and says to disable ONE
 package at a time because our own 2025-11-24 incident boot-looped a device by disabling the
 Package Installer.
- The deliverable carries a pass/fail verification checklist that ends in a REBOOT, on the principle
 that a block which does not survive a reboot is not a block.
- Steps 3-6 are written as NOT YET RUN ON THE PHONE, because they have not been, and the document
 says which claims are vendor-documented, which are our own incident record, and which are verified.
 I corrected an unverified Circle-to-Search settings path to say UNVERIFIED rather than ship a guess
 that looked like a fact.

ESCALATED, deliberately (owner question #97, queued on the authority):
- Whether "block Google search completely" vs "block it completely AND he cannot undo it" becomes a
 PAID MANAGED FILTER offer (Livigent or equivalent). This is service-and-price, not engineering, and
 it decides whether Galaxy-class filter setup becomes a sellable line.
- RECOMMENDATION GIVEN: ship the free fix now (answers him today, already pushed) and vet a managed
 filter separately. Do not hold his phone for the paid option.
- Why it is his and not mine: money and the service catalogue. Why it is NOT a blocker: his phone can
 be done today with the free route either way.

NOT DONE, deliberately: no SOP was added. An SOP should be promoted from a procedure that has actually
been executed and verified on a device, and this one has been executed by nobody yet. Promoting it now
would put an unverified procedure where future technicians would trust it. Promote after steps 3-6 run.
