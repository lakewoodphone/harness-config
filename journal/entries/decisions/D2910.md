**D2910 · 2026-10-04 · The house has a meals record now: kashrus decides the dish's shape before nutrition does, and lasagna dip is a real meal only on a cottage-cheese base.**

The meals tier is new. ~/code/home/meals/ now holds one file per supper idea, date-prefixed, with
the verdict first, plus meals/README.md which carries the house food rules that decide every
recipe: kashrus decides the shape of the dish before nutrition gets a vote; a milchig dish gets its
protein from dairy, not from a meat side; a milchig dish needs milchig bakeware; protein target 30 g
per person and some fibre; sodium is the health number to watch in this category, not fat; record
what was rejected.

First dish: lasagna dip (owner and his wife, 2026-10-04). Verdict: a real meal, but the published
recipe is 617 kcal / 28 g protein / 50 g fat per serving AND treif, because every version of it puts
Italian sausage or ground beef in a cheese base. Rebuilt MILCHIG on a cottage-cheese+ricotta base it
is 413 kcal / 34 g protein / 17 g fat, and about 760 kcal / 43 g protein on the full plate with
garlic bread and a big salad. Cream cheese was the specific fault: 6 g protein and 34 g fat per
100 g, against cottage cheese at 11-12 g protein and 2.3 g fat - the base was the protein problem,
not the portion size. "Cook the noodles first" is correct, but for hydration reasons, not because it
makes a dip; cooked noodles folded in turn it into a skillet lasagna.

Recorded so the arithmetic is not redone next time a cheese-forward supper is proposed.
