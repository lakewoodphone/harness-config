**D3152 · 2026-10-14 · A family relay to the owner is not rate-floored.**

**D · 2026-10-14 · A family relay to the owner is not rate-floored: the gate bounds the machine, not his family.**

**Decision (dev decision, mine to make — D2647 says the family chat is mine to run).**

`app/owner_text.gate` exists to bound what the AI says to the owner; its docstring
is explicit that "no producer and no urgency gets past it". D3144b item 7 then
routed the Family Chat's owner leg through it, which was right while the leg
carried machine-authored text and wrong the moment it carried a family member's
words. Measured consequence, 2026-10-14T04:00Z: the first owner-classified leg
(wife's line, `OWNER_ALT_PHONE_NUMBERS=+18483897895,+18482245096`) recorded turn
#96 and burned the one-text-per-20-minutes budget, so the leg to the owner's own
iPhone 8 ms later was refused, and the hub retired his number as "unsubscribed".

So: a verbatim relay of an inbound member's message (`verbatim_relay=True`, passed
by `_process_inbound`'s fan-out only) skips the owner leg guard entirely — kill
switch, quiet hours and gate. Everything the machine authors to him stays gated:
Chip's own family-chat lines, `_notify_admins` broadcasts, the owner-text relay.

Rejected alternatives: (a) exempting only the primary number and gating the
alternates — leaves his wife's phone eating his budget; (b) raising
`owner_text_min_gap_minutes` — weakens the limit he set for the AI to fix a relay
problem; (c) removing +18482245096 from `OWNER_ALT_PHONE_NUMBERS` — changes who
counts as the owner on inbound, a bigger change than the defect needs.

Residual, recorded as pain: an outbox *replay* of a relay does not carry the flag
(the outbox has no such column), so a queued relay that is retried later can be
gated; it can no longer deactivate anyone.
