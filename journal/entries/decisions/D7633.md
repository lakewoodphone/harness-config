**D7633 · 2026-10-18 · CORRECTED: sealed is the only product install - device owner is not optional, and the reset is required before the customer account exists.**

CORRECTION of D7234, same day, by the owner. D7234 said the counter flow was "install first, Standard
default, device owner an attempt". That is wrong and the owner said so:

 "Absolutely not. You cannot build that. Our filters have to be foolproof 100 percent. We can't have
 stuff that determined user can get rid of. No way."

SUPERSEDING RULE: device owner is not an optional extra and a removable install is not a product mode.
counter_install.ps1 now defaults to -Mode Sealed, claims device owner SECOND (right after install), and
on a refusal exits non-zero saying the phone is NOT FOR HANDOVER - it never falls back to a removable
install. The removable path survives only as -Mode Diagnostic behind -IUnderstandThisIsRemovable, and it
reports NOT FOR HANDOVER and exits 2. 12 contract tests green on zabz-tech.

WHY THE RESET, answered properly: Android only hands out device owner in the window before anybody has
been given control of the phone - no accounts, and on modern Android not past first-run setup. That is
an anti-takeover rule: otherwise an app installed later could claim the phone. Deactivating other device
admins does NOT unlock it: an app can be a device admin alongside others, and removing another app
admin requires already being the device owner (circular). The gate is the account state, not the admin
list.

CONSEQUENCE FOR THE COUNTER, which reframes the customer conversation: the seal has to exist BEFORE the
customer account does. After the seal is taken the customer signs in and restores their own backup -
accounts added post-seal are normal and do not weaken device owner. So the honest offer is "reset, then
restore from your backup", not "we destroy your phone", and the cleanest offer is a phone supplied by us
that has never been set up at all.

BUILT TO SETTLE THE REST WITH MEASUREMENT, NOT ARGUMENT: scripts/seal_probe.ps1 reads the gates
(accounts, device_provisioned, user_setup_complete, existing owner, active admins) and, with -Attempt,
tries the only non-destructive escape: claim, clear the two setup flags, claim again, restore the flags.
It reports SEALED / REFUSED: accounts / REFUSED: already set up / REFUSED: owner already set / REFUSED:
other, with the device model and Android version, so the answer becomes per-model fact. Read-only by
default; never deletes an account, never resets, never uninstalls.
