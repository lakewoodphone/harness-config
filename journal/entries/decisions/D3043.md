**D3043 · 2026-10-11 · Port decisions for the cloud authority: marker-delimited crontab blocks, /home/zabz aliases, one authoritative DB, and nothing outbound.**

DECIDED (workstream 139, 2026-10-11):
1. The crontab on secratary-cloud is marker-delimited. Every workstream that installs lines must keep its block inside `# >>> <name>` / `# <<< <name>` and read-modify-write the crontab, because the boxes crontab started EMPTY and two workstreams writing `crontab -l | ... | crontab -` concurrently lose one of the two sets. Why: no coordination mechanism exists yet, and this costs nothing.
2. Deploy tool bodies to /root/bin (inside root's 0700 home, root's actual $HOME) and publish them at /home/zabz/bin by SYMLINK, rather than recreating /home/zabz with real directories. Why: 11 of my payloads hard-code /home/zabz/bin and 16 resolve $HOME/bin, and root's HOME is /root; one symlink satisfies both, is reversible, and does not commit the authority to a 15 GB /home/zabz copy before that move is decided. Reconcile `bin` first if /home/zabz is ever recreated for real.
3. The owner's authority database for owner-facing tools is /root/authority.db. Any office tool defaulting to the app data dir (which on this box is a stale 1.8 MB stub) gets its default repointed, with the office original preserved beside it as *.office-default. Why: the stub has no owner_decision_queue and the failure mode is a confident `nothing to ask`.
4. Job names in cron-wrap.sh stay identical to the office names wherever the job is the same job, so monitors that keyed on the name still read true. Where only part of an office chain is safe to port (L59 sms-inbox), the NAME is kept and the report says which parts are missing -- rather than inventing <name>-2.
5. Nothing outbound is enabled. owner-ask/cfo-ask run in their composed-only modes until the owner approves the exact words of one message; sms-responder is not installed at all. Why: docs/outbound-comms-hard-stop.md and THIS WORKSTREAMS OWN RULE, which is stronger than convenience.
6. hours-drift is NOT dead. 132-CRON-INVENTORY.md filed it DEAD at "100% non-zero"; the tool reserves exit 3 for CONDITION-not-fault, its office non-zero rate was 20/20 rc=3, and on the cloud box it reads four surfaces and correctly names a real Google Maps drift. A non-zero exit code is not a broken job when the tool documents the code.
