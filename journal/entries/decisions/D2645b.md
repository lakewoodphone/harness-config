**D2645b · 2026-09-24 · 2026-09-23 - LPT staff console target: Work Orders and Customers only, charging inside the work order, Settings out of the nav.**

Owner direction, 2026-09-23, after looking at the staff console. Recorded because it is the shape the next
sessions build to, not because he asked for a document.

HIS WORDS, in substance: the AI workspace is being taken away for now; the two floating buttons (report,
bottom-left; help, bottom-right) must be gone; the left navigation must be **just Work Orders and
Customers**; work orders must be far easier to fill out; charging happens **through the work order**, and
an on-the-spot sale is "just a sale type of work order"; the Customers tab shows the customer, their work
orders and their whole communication history, including Dialpad; Settings needs an overhaul, including how
customers relate to the user list. Waze tabs are another agent's work.

TARGET IA - decided by me, and why:
- **Primary navigation: exactly `Work Orders` and `Customers`. Nothing else.**
- **Settings moves out of the left nav** into the header/account menu: it must stay reachable (he wants it
 overhauled, which requires reaching it) but it is not a daily tab.
- **Routes stay mounted even when navigation entries disappear.** Removing a nav item is not removing a
 capability; the one exception is the parked AI workspace, which is unmounted but whose code is kept in
 place, marked as deliberately parked.
- **Charging is a work-order action.** The standalone Charge / Payments / Price-Lookup entries go; the
 price-lookup UI survives inside line-item entry, and the payment path is reached from the work order.
- **A counter sale is a work order with sale intent** - same record, same payment path, no repair fields.
- **Customers: one page** - search (name/phone/email/serial, ambiguity always shows every match), then the
 customer with their work orders, one unified comms timeline (calls, texts, voicemails, transcripts,
 notes), and their portal account status with create/attach/invite/revoke.
- **The customer-account question is settled in the product, not in a memo:** a customer given a login is a
 user with a customer role linked to the customer record; the per-customer controls live on the customer
 page, the staff-side list of linked accounts lives in Settings, and both call the same backend.
- **Floating buttons removed from the staff shell**; the customer-facing marketing site keeps its own.

DELIVERY SHAPE: four isolated workstreams on the mesh, each its own worktree and branch off `origin/test`
(`66b81278e`), partitioned by file so no two touch the same path - `lpt/shell-clean-20260923`,
`lpt/workorders-simplify-20260923`, `lpt/customers-20260923`, `lpt/settings-20260923`. Integration is mine,
serially, into `test`, then a test deploy for him to look at. Waze paths are excluded from all four briefs.
