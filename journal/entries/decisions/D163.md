**D163 · 2026-09-15 · Device Parts is out of favour - the owner paid the DHL duty himself and is not happy with the cost.**

2026-09-15, owner's own words: 'i just paid myself, and i am not happy about how much it cost, mark down that we don't like device parts.'

FACTS
- Device Parts order 62415 (9 Qin screens) was held by DHL for ~70 USD import duty. The queue row #34 asked the owner to choose; he paid it himself rather than have the company pay and reclaim it.
- The same vendor had already raised its unit price 47.7% on this line, so the duty is the second cost surprise from one supplier in one order.
- Cause of the duty is structural, not vendor malice: China/HK parcels lost the 800 USD de-minimis exemption, so duty is now ordinary landed cost on every parcel from that lane.

DECISION
- Device Parts is NOT a preferred supplier. Treat it as a fallback until a better-sourced lane exists; before ordering anything from it, compute and state the landed cost (unit + duty + freight) and compare it to whole-unit alternatives, not to list price.
- No Device Parts charge, fee or dispute goes to the owner again: it is answered from the landed-cost number in the quote, or it is not ordered.
- Follow-on work: find a supplier lane where the landed cost is knowable before the order (this is the same work as the Kosher Waze fleet sourcing question, queue #20).

EVIDENCE
- owner_decision_queue row 34, answered 2026-09-15 by the owner in conversation on ZABZ-TECH; owner paid DHL directly.
