**D9314 · 2026-10-22 · Disabled the Windows Search index service on ZABZ-YOGA 2026-10-22 - it was filling the disk unattended.**

While the owner was away for 10 hours and a whole-volume audit was running, free space on ZABZ-YOGA fell from 24.2 GiB to 4.7 GiB in about 40 minutes. Perf counters named SearchIndexer writing 33-87 MB/s; every other process was under 2 MB/s. The Windows Search database itself (Windows.db) stayed fixed at 39.59 GiB, so the space was being churned in its working set, and it was released the instant the service stopped - free space jumped from 4.72 to 17.85 GiB. Stopping WSearch alone was not enough: it restarted itself (StartType Automatic, service recovery) and resumed at 86.6 MB/s, taking free space from 17.92 to 12.37 GiB in six minutes. ACTION TAKEN: sc.exe config WSearch start= disabled, then the SearchIndexer process was ended. Result: service Stopped / Disabled, free space stable at 17.91 GiB. REVERSAL: sc.exe config WSearch start= auto ; Start-Service WSearch. This is on a host whose posture is 'writable' (settings/machines/host-posture.tsv, zabz-yoga-1). It is also the single largest reclaimable item already on the delete list (40.84 GiB), so leaving it off is the intended state, not a temporary workaround. Nothing was deleted.
