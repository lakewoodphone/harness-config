**D8258 · 2026-10-19 · Baseline frozen at 425.06 per 30 days; the week is one command, and a day is noise.**

## 2026-10-19T22:45Z · ZABZ-YOGA · the baseline is frozen and the week is one command

### The instrument

`scripts/cost/weekly_report.py` reproduces any window's numbers and compares it to a frozen baseline.
Its one important decision: **it never reads `estimated_cost_usd`.** That column was written by the
retired 0.44/1.32 card for months, so using it would compare the broken instrument's opinion to a
corrected one and manufacture a saving out of a bug fix. It reads the stored *token counts* — which were
always right — and prices them per UTC hour against the dated book. Tokens are the fact; cost is a
derived opinion.

### The frozen baseline

`docs/cost/baseline-2026-10-19.json`, the week ending before the pricing fix (2026-10-12T22:00Z →
2026-10-19T22:00Z):

- input 511,142,458 / output 3,639,743 / cached 4,121,088 (0.806 %)
- **cost 99.1796 = 425.06 per 30 days**
- **peak share 26.79 % of input, 42.21 % of cost**
- 298 company ticks, 719 tasks completed, 839 telemetry ticks
- **81 failed ticks = 9.65 %** — the ceiling the proof must not breach

Change timestamps: pricing fix live **22:08:46Z**, schedule enabled **22:31Z**.

### The caution the instrument produced about itself

A smoke test over the last 24 hours reported a peak share of **38.6 %** against the week's 26.79 % — a
**44 % swing with no change behind it.** So a one-day comparison is not weak evidence, it is *noise
wearing evidence's clothes*, and anyone who quotes a 24-hour "saving" from this will eventually quote a
24-hour loss with the same confidence. The proof must be week against week.

That is worth stating plainly because it is the failure mode this whole project exists to avoid: a number
that looks like the answer, produced by a window chosen after the fact.

### What "proven" will mean, fixed in advance

All four in one report, or it is not proven: `cost_usd_per_30_days` down; `peak_input_share_pct` down;
`tokens_per_task` unchanged within noise (same work, less money); and `tick_error_pct` not above 9.65 %.
Stating the success test before the week runs is the only thing that stops a later session from picking a
different test.
