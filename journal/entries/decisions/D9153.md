DECISION (mine, from his correction - this one matters)
He said: "if you do 100 songs a day, I probably won't get around to listening to most of them. So then they don't count as listened to, just there because they're put in my playlist."
That kills the daily-dump design. The playlist is a ROLLING QUEUE: on refill, tracks he played leave, tracks he never reached stay, new tracks only fill the empty slots. Consequences:
- unplayed is NEUTRAL, never a negative signal, never scored as rejection;
- only three learning signals: played (ledger), deleted-from-playlist by him, replay;
- the container self-sizes to his real consumption once the ledger has a few days.
