**D9302 · 2026-10-21 · Walk-in only - the phone has to be on the bench, so the market is where the bench is.**

aliases: Q31

Owner, verbatim (2026-10-21):

> "1"

Which is: walk-in only, Lakewood and nearby, everything at the counter.

What it decides, condensed:

1. Sold and installed in person: no mail-in, no remote seal, no partner technicians in other cities.
2. The market is the distance a customer will drive to the counter, and that is a feature — the person who sets
 the floor stands in front of the technician who seals it.
3. No shipping of customer phones at any price, either direction. A phone in the post is a phone nobody can
 support and a liability nobody priced.
4. The website sells an appointment, not a product: it explains and prices and gets him into the shop, because
 nothing completes online.
5. A failed seal costs a handover, not a shipping label, which keeps the refusal procedure honest; and the
 five-minutes-a-phone support target is only reachable in person.
6. Growth is a second bench, not a second channel: another city means a shop there with its own technician and
 its own signature.
7. A customer who moves away keeps his filter and loses his counter — support becomes the app and the queue, and
 release still works because it is on the phone.
8. The counter says it plainly, so nobody buys expecting a national service.
