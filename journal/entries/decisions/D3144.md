**D3144 · 2026-10-13 · The owner text line: ONE relay, and the router is the missing piece.**

**Decision (2026-10-13, dev decision, no owner question needed).**

The owner text line (Twilio 732-444-7361 <-> 848-389-7895) is served by ONE relay, not by
rebuilding anything. Five independent read-only workstreams audited it; the measured findings are
in `personal-secretary-mvp/docs/owner-text-line/AUDIT-2026-10-13.md` and
`/root/sms-audit-20261013/` on secratary-cloud.

1. **Extend, do not rebuild.** The gate `app/owner_text.py::gate()` (`:2435`), the ledger
 `owner_text_turn` and the outbox `owner_message_queue` (2,591 rows) already exist. 12 prior
 attempts on this line, 9 landed. A grep for a unified router returned ZERO hits - the router is
 the single missing piece.
2. **One source of truth per thing.** `/root/bin/textsend.py` resolved a STALE 1.8 MB copy
 (`/root/personal-secretary-mvp/data/secretary.db`: sms_log 0 rows, owner_text_turn 1 row) while
 the live app writes `/root/authority.db` (4,103 sms_log, 92 turns). Every rate limit, turn check
 and quota in that sender was enforcing itself against an empty file. Fixed to resolve the app's
 own resolver; `textsend.py doctor` proves both sides agree.
3. **Routing key, not a label.** `owner_text_turn` gains `handler` + `ref`. A turn records WHO asked.
 `handle_owner_reply` gains its third job: dispatch the answer to that handler. Before this,
 `record_owner_reply` closed every open turn with the same body and `answer_body` was read by
 nobody - that is the whole "two brains" failure.
4. **A refusal is terminal or transient, never a queue condition.** `Verdict.terminal` is True for
 formatting faults (the same body can never pass) and False for transient ones. A terminal refusal
 DISMISSES the row.
5. **Length belongs to the composer.** One ceiling, checked before enqueue. 618 of the storm rows
 were a 321-character body against a 320-character ceiling - one character over.
6. **A provider outage fails fast.** DNS/connection failure opens a breaker and alerts once; it is
 not a per-message failure to be retried every 60 seconds for 14 hours (841 rows).
7. **Every path to his phone passes the gate**: Family Chat's owner leg, the `notify_owner` raw
 Twilio fallback, and the owner escalation call. `_escalate_family_emergency` carries a written
 exemption rather than a silent one.
