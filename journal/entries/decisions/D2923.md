**D2923 · 2026-10-05 · D: The 50 fee does NOT apply to trivial jobs — and the Nano went 160 to 150 because the parts are cheap.**

TWO ANSWERS FROM THE OWNER, 2026-10-05, closing the open questions on D2921.

1. TRIVIAL JOBS ARE OUTSIDE THE 50 RULE
 Verbatim: "does not apply to trivial jobs".
 A job that costs no real time - a 15 battery, a phone bought outright, a straight swap -
 carries no 50 fee. The 50 pays for time, so where there is no time to pay for there is nothing
 to charge.
 This CORRECTS the earlier blanket statement "we are always doing non refundable 50 dollar fee
 from now on for everything fixed or not". The accurate rule is: anything that takes real time,
 labour or diagnosis. Trivial jobs are outside it.
 Recorded because a flat fee on a 15 battery would make it 65, which loses a customer over
 twelve dollars - the owner closed this the moment it was put to him.

2. WHY THE NANO SCREEN WENT FROM 160 (MAY) TO 150 (2026-10-05)
 Verbatim: "just because 160 seemed like charging too much for labor if parts don't cost so
 much".
 So it fell ON PURPOSE. This is a principle, not an adjustment:

 When the part is cheap, the labour figure has to be defensible on its own. A big number on
 top of a small part invites the customer to feel overcharged, even when the labour is
 genuinely hard.

 On the Nano the LCD and digitizer together are under 9 from the shop''s own supplier, so the
 price is almost entirely labour. The job did not get easier; the price got fairer.
 A future session comparing the two quotes must read this as a deliberate correction, NOT as
 inconsistent pricing. See P2854, where the absence of a written rate card makes exactly that
 mistake likely.
