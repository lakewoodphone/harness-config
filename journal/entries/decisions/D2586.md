**The owner's instruction: *"now update your docs to never ever screw me over like that again."* Four rules were added or hardened in the agent persona, and the detail was written to a referenced doc. This entry records what changed and why, so a future session knows the rules are load-bearing rather than decorative.**

**1. The outbound hard stop was rewritten and made explicit** (`presets/zabz/agent.cordis.yml`, the "Never send anything outbound" rule, and the new `docs/outbound-comms-hard-stop.md`). It now carries the five things that let me walk past it on 2026-09-24:
- a **task-level directive is not approval** ("get this straight with 300dr" authorised the work, not words);
- **approval is per message** — a singular yes covers one message, never the batch;
- **my own offer is never my permission** ("say send and they go" was mine, not his);
- **no price, date, commitment or contractual posture goes into a message unless he set it in that conversation**;
- **read the `To` header back after sending** — `SENT` describes what we did with the message, not who received it (P2477: `reply_to_message` on our own message self-addressed, `ok:true`, right thread, label `SENT`, recipient us).

His words: *"I could've answered many of those questions, you are being annoying... you are ruining business relationships."*

**3. New rule 9: do not make him think for you.** One number, worked out, with the arithmetic and the reason — never a menu of candidates. This case's own file already contained the answer ($500/chip → quote ~$800) while I was offering him 660, 750, 800 and 860 across four turns. His words: *"can you actually damn think about this instead of proposing all different ideas and making me think for you."*

**4. Rule 8 (money survives the renderer)** was added earlier in the same session: two dollar signs in one paragraph render as inline maths in his GUI — serif, spaces eaten, word-joined.
