**D6017 · 2026-10-15 · CFO P3 corroboration stays at 18 percent, and the reason is structural, not a queue of unlinked documents.**

Decided 2026-10-16 after measuring both levers that looked like an easy provenance gain on the CFO ledger. The bank exports in /root/finance-fleet-20261013/statements/ are NOT independent corroboration: of 1,311 matches against ledger rows on account+date+amount, 1,300 match rows whose source_system is the same CSV family the ledger was imported from and only 11 match a plaid-api row - the ledger's card rows largely CAME from those documents, so recording them as corroboration would be circular. Vendor billing is not row-linkable either: Twilio's 316 rows are per-component usage dated the 1st for the previous month while the card charges are the payments, so the monthly sums diverge (2026-06 vendor 170.31 vs card 60.74) and a row-level link would be a modelled inference dressed as evidence. Both were therefore NOT built, and P3 stays at 1,137 of 6,249 (18.2 percent) with that as the stated reason rather than a number that quietly rises. Checked at the same time and NOT a defect: lpt-twilio-spend, lpt-openai-spend and lpt-stripe are classified mirror by cfo-entity-statements.py and excluded from the statement, so they do not double count - the module's own evidence is Stripe gross receipts 7,347.69 against BoA payout clearing 7,256.22 with the 91.47 difference being Stripe's fee. The same round moved P2 from 77.8 to 83.0 percent by appending the resolver's own recorded notes (188 rows) to rows no rule could explain.
