**D3154 · 2026-10-14 · Waze kiosk: unblock the Wi-Fi/Bluetooth Settings panes, keep forceWiFiPowerOn true, six LPT units only.**

**D · Waze kiosk Wi-Fi/Bluetooth: remove the three Settings panes from the block list, keep forceWiFiPowerOn = true**

Decided and applied 2026-10-14, on the six LPT NAVIGATION units (DRN 2002, 2004-2008) only.

**The decision.** `com.apple.wifi`, `com.apple.wifiSettings` and `com.apple.BluetoothSettings` come
out of `blockedAppBundleIDs` in the DRN kiosk profiles (136 -> 133). `forceWiFiPowerOn` stays `true`.
Every other Settings pane stays blocked; `allowListedAppBundleIDs` stays `["com.waze.iphone"]`.

**Why the panes had to come out.** There is no other way to give the customer Wi-Fi. The allow-list
makes Waze the only launchable app, so there is no second route into any setting; the pane is the
control. Without this, the 2026-10-13 Bluetooth decision (`allowBluetoothModification = true`) was
inert, and a customer with bad cell service had no fallback.

**Why `forceWiFiPowerOn` stays true - the reasoning, not a menu.** Apple's definition and the two
behaviours are mutually exclusive *by construction*: `true` means exactly "the customer cannot turn
Wi-Fi off". So:

- `true` = Wi-Fi is always on, so the device keeps whatever fallback network we provisioned. This is
 what the owner asked for ("what happens if they have bad service"). It does not prevent choosing a
 network, and Airplane Mode cannot defeat it.
- `false` = the customer may switch Wi-Fi off and on freely - including off, in a car, and then never
 rejoin.

Chosen: `true`. A navigation device whose fallback connectivity can be switched off and not restored
does not answer the question that motivated the request. This is the reason to relitigate, not a
preference - if the owner wants two-way switching, it is one value in three files plus a re-lock, and
that is the whole change.

**The cost, recorded rather than glossed.** A reachable Wi-Fi pane is reachable Settings, so this
widens the kiosk's surface beyond Waze. Accepted because the alternative is a device with no Wi-Fi and
no control at all.

**Explicitly out of scope, per instruction.** No gemach device (DRN 13, 26, 48, 54) and no
shop-counter device (2001, 2003) was pushed to. `waze-layered-kiosk-drn13.mobileconfig` was patched on
the host so a future gemach re-lock picks it up, but nothing was sent to DRN 13.

**What this corrected.** `allowWiFiModification` and `allowWirelessModification` are not Apple keys
and never did anything; they stay in the files as harmless noise but are flagged in
`deployment-process.md` so no future session tunes them again.
`deploy/waze-mdm/docs/bluetooth-policy.md` §3 was wrong that flipping `layered-kiosk` alone would have
been defeated by `layered-kiosk-phase1` - every device rejects that install (error 4027, shared
`PayloadDisplayName`), so phase-1 is on no device. Corrected by addendum, not by editing §3.

Record: `deploy/waze-mdm/docs/wifi-bluetooth-switching.md`; commit `ee3c5e979`.
