## Decision 2026-10-21 · Deletion erases the person and keeps the behaviour under a pseudonym that points at nobody (Q19)

aliases: Q19 · D-20261021-18 · docs/decisions/D-20261021-18-deletion.md

Owner's answer, verbatim: "Like you said, 1 and 3." Which is: delete on a verified request, everything except the billing record the tax authority requires, and keep the behaviour under the pseudonym because it no longer points at anybody.

1. A verified deletion request is honoured: his identity record, contact details, his household's names and the mapping between him and his phone are erased. No argument, no retention window used as a delay.
2. The behaviour survives, stripped of the person — decisions, blocks, verdicts, complaints and their outcomes stay in the corpus under the pseudonymous subject, because with the mapping gone they describe a phone rather than a man.
3. The billing record stays: invoice history, amounts and dates for the period the tax authority requires, and nothing else kept with them.
4. The erasure is provable — a deletion writes its own record: when asked, when done, what was removed, what was kept and under which obligation.

Now required: the pseudonym must not be reversible from the behaviour data (not the phone id, the install time, a fingerprint, or free text with a name in it); free text is the leak, so the scrubber runs before storage and a deletion sweeps the bodies too; the parent deletes for a child's phone; a deletion does not release the filter; the support script gets one sentence. Scrubber and deletion are one piece of work, not two.
