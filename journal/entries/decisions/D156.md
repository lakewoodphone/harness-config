**D156 · 2026-09-15 · Case record written on the authority; only the app the customer needs re-enabled; flash judged by applied carrier config.**

Recorded 2026-09-15 while closing the bench side of the Eli Green S22 Ultra flash case.

1. Customer case records are written where the sync engine reads them: `~/repos/lpt-hub/docs/customer-operations/` on the authority, both the case markdown and its sync-record JSON. Backups of the previous versions were kept at /tmp/eli-case.md.bak-20260915 and /tmp/eli-sync.json.bak-20260915. The workstation copy of lpt-hub is not the one the sync reads.

2. On the customer's phone, only the app the customer actually needs was re-enabled (Play Store). The other 19 disabled preloads were left disabled; Samsung Messages is the enabled default SMS app, so SMS works. Mass re-enabling preloads is not default shop practice — it is done per request.

3. The 2026-09-10 Odin flash was judged successful on the applied carrier config (FKR = Visible, WFC enabled, IWLAN up) rather than on the CSC code the plan named (XAA).
