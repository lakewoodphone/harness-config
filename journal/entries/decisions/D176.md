**D176 · 2026-09-15 · Hours sign header restyled to the business-card lockup; note band moved from khaki to navy.**

Owner feedback on the v2 sign, verbatim: *"make the logo nicer, more like the business card logo / leave out these words Walk-in phone, tablet & computer repair / the location info and the sunday and wednesday are too much the same color"*.

Source of truth found for "the business card logo": `personal-secretary-mvp/docs/finance/lpt-tax-docs/business card 7-13-25.pdf`
(two pages; rendered with PyMuPDF for reference). The card's identity is the **mascot character**, a
wordmark that splits PHONE (white) from TECH (khaki) with LAKEWOOD letterspaced small, and a navy
service strip. The flat favicon mark the sign was using was the wrong thing.

Three changes to `scripts/make-walkin-hours-sign.py`:

1. **Header now follows the card.** The mascot (`assets/walkin-hours-logo.png`, the same character
 as the card) sits on the teal band; the lockup is LAKEWOOD in letterspaced khaki, then
 `PHONE &` white with `TECH` khaki. The favicon vector mark is kept in the script only as a
 fallback if the mascot asset is missing.
2. **Tagline deleted** - "Walk-in phone, tablet & computer repair" no longer appears under the
 wordmark.
3. **The availability note moved from khaki to navy.** It and the location footer were both khaki,
 which the owner read as the bands blurring together. Now: teal = call us, navy = other hours,
 khaki = where we are. Three bands, three colours, no repeats. This is the rule to keep for any
 future band added to this sign.

Still on the mascot's original pale-yellow disc with the khaki ring -- that is the site logo as it
exists, and it reads as a badge on teal. Bare character cut-out is possible if the owner wants it
closer to the card still.
