**D67 · 2026-09-14 · A portal may push a profile only as one of two named intents, never as a raw profile picker.**

The owner said DRN staff need to *"push a profile"* (his three DRN needs), and settled that the portal is the entry point for managing the fleet (`D19`). Taken literally that would mean exposing the profile registry: 10 types, of which `hard-kiosk`, `soft-kiosk` and `vpn` all resolve to the *same* `waze-layered-kiosk.mobileconfig`, and `layered-kiosk` has per-DRN identifier variants. An operator picking the wrong one, or re-installing without removing first, bricks a phone.
*Decision:* the portal exposes the two intents the business actually has — **re-apply lockdown** and **lift lockdown for a service window** — and delegates sequencing to the fleet-api's tested `/lockdown` path, which already handles the DRN variants and the remove-then-install order iOS requires. Applying is the safe direction and needs no token; **lifting** un-restricts a device, so it requires typing `SERVICE WINDOW`, enforced in the router (`adminProcedure`) and not only in the UI — same treatment as Lost Mode's `LOST MODE`. The raw registry stays in the MDM console.
*Consequence:* this amends the earlier reading in `wazeFleet.ts`'s header ("anything deeper links out"), which was wrong for DRN specifically; the comment is corrected in place with the owner decisions that changed it rather than left contradicting the code.
