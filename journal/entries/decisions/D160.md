**D160 · 2026-09-15 · The security evidence viewer audited end to end and rebuilt - the evidence was fine, the presentation was not.**

**D (2026-09-15) - The security evidence viewer audited end to end and rebuilt. The evidence was never the problem; the presentation was, in five specific ways.**

Owner: *"it looks old, I don't see full snapshots or full playable clips or security history clear
enough… it is terrible and unhelpful, audit it end to end and overhaul the system."*

**The audit's first result is the one that mattered: the chain is healthy.** 2,931 events; 2,863
snapshot references; 164 clip references; **0** events with no media; **0** missing files; **0**
zero-byte clips; **0** clips without an MP4 `ftyp` header; clip sizes 565 KB–3.0 MB, median 801 KB;
and a clip opened in a real browser returned `readyState 4`, **8.02 s, 2560×1440**, no error. 27 days
of coverage. So "the system is broken" would have been the wrong conclusion and the wrong repair.

**The five defects, all in presentation:**

1. **Every snapshot cropped to a 150px band** — `.imgs img{height:150px;object-fit:cover}` on a
 2560×1440 frame. Direct cause of "I don't see full snapshots".
2. **Clips rendered as a bare `<video>`** — no poster, no caption, no duration, and the camera label
 the generator already emitted was *ignored*. A perfectly good 8-second clip looked like a broken
 black rectangle. Direct cause of "I don't see full playable clips".
3. **No grouping, filtering, searching or date jump** — 2,931 flat cards, 49 clicks to reach the
 oldest. Direct cause of "history not clear enough".
4. Clicking a snapshot opened a new tab instead of a lightbox.
5. No coverage view, so the **4 days with no events** were invisible.

**And the structural point that decided where to fix it:** the page is a `PAGE_HTML` string **inside**
`timeline_tools/phoenix_timeline_generate.py`, rewritten on every capture. Replacing
`/config/www/timeline/index.html` would have looked fixed until the next event and then silently
reverted. **Fix the generator or you have fixed nothing.**

**v3 rebuild** (committed, deployed, regenerated): uncropped snapshots with `auto-fit` so a single
still fills the card (most events hold exactly one — `auto-fill` had left two-thirds of every card
empty); full-resolution lightbox with ←/→ and Esc; **clips get a poster borrowed from their own
camera's nearest still** plus caption and a duration read from the metadata; day grouping with sticky
headers; composable filters — camera (with counts), clips-only/snapshots-only, day picker, free-text
search; a **per-day coverage strip** that sizes each day by volume and marks a gap; and `iso`,
`n_img`, `n_clip` added per event so the view never re-parses a human date string.

**Verification: `scripts/timeline-check.py`, 19/19.** It launches a private headless Chrome — the
shared Playwright browser is in use by another session — and drives it over the DevTools protocol
with the standard library only. Asserted, not eyeballed: a snapshot measuring **1393×784 from a
2560×1440 source, ratio 1.78** (uncropped); a clip **having a poster**, controls and a caption;
**each filter genuinely narrowing the list** and Reset restoring it; the lightbox opening with the
full-size image and closing; the coverage strip showing **4 gap days**.

**Two defects found in my own audit tooling, both of which would have produced a confident false
report** — recorded because the wrong number is worse than no number:
- media URLs are stored **relative to the page** (`../snapshots/…`) and resolve correctly in a
 browser; my first path-mapper treated them as root-absolute and reported **2,859 missing files**
 that were all present.
- a gap check asserted a cell by its **hex colour**, which browsers normalise to `rgb()`, so it read
 0 gaps where there are 4.

**Not verified, stated plainly:** his phone (layout checked at 1500 px and by CSS rules only); whether
the filtered views are pleasant to scan, as opposed to correct; older coverage (only recent files
were opened); and whether a 2,931-event single-page index is the right shape at 10,000.
