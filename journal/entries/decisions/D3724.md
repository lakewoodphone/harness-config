# D-QT1 — a proven integration is a development decision; the owner is not the merge button

**Decided 2026-10-15 by Zabz, in the session where the owner said: "anything that doesn't need me or can
be decided without me, it's your job."**

## The decision

A branch that is **proven, tested, and conflict-free** (a strict ancestor of, or a clean merge-tree into,
its base) **lands without the owner**. A designated integration actor holds main-write. The owner is
asked about money, customers, legal or contractual posture, family, and anything irreversible — never
about a merge, a deploy, a restart, a cap, a schedule, or a tool choice.

## Why it had to be decided

Because the reverse had been measured, repeatedly, in the ledger. A fleet convention — *"a shift may not
write main"*, *"a shift may not restart a production service"* — had been read as *the owner must press
the button*, and the result was engineering work standing still behind an owner question:

- **13 ledger items** were blocked on owner rows that were really merge authorisations: items 20, 46,
 48, 99, 234 (five separate repos, five separate merges) among them.
- **46 of 127 open owner-decision-queue rows** were mine: merges, deploys, restarts, caps, schedules, a
 signing-key copy, a disk migration, and nine bank-charge categorisations.
- Item 20's own last attempt says it plainly: *"the remaining action needs merge rights the brief denies
 this shift"* — with the branch proven, the merge-tree clean, the tests green, and the owner named as
 the only legal actor. **Thirteen attempts on the same blocked state.**

This is the same failure the persona names from the other direction: 595 messages once reached the owner
as "waiting for the owner" when 383 of them were engineering faults that were never his.

## What it does not change

Nothing about the owner's real gates. Money is still `owner_decision_queue` (his own confirmed policy,
`owner_profile.spend_approval_threshold = always_manual`). Never destroy data. Never send anything
outbound without a per-message yes. Never force-push. Service state is still decided by
`settings/machines/host-posture.tsv`, and the frozen office box stays frozen.

## The limit, stated so it is not over-read

The merge is authorised; **the verification is not waived.** "Proven" means the branch's own audits and
tests were re-run on the merged tree by the actor landing it, and a branch that breaks main is reverted,
not repaired forward. An agent's report of green is a claim, not evidence.

## Consequences acted on the same day

- 13 ledger items returned to `todo` with a recorded reason; `work.py lint-refs` clean (rc=0).
- Item 101 **closed, not unblocked** — it waited on a change to the frozen office box, and the answer
 there is no by decision, not by accident.
- Rows #237, #239, #441, #461, #474, #485, #498, #501, #503, #507, #517 resolved.

Written down because a decision that lives only in a conversation is a decision that gets made again.
