**D2837 · 2026-09-28 · The phone is for questions, warnings and deadlines only. A finished job is recorded in the ledger; 'it finished' is never a reason to text the owner.**

THE OWNER, 2026-09-28, after being shown the completion message I proposed to send him:
 "why would it text me this, whats the point, is there a question or clarification you need from me
 or a warning you need to give me?"

THERE WAS NO POINT. The message existed to prove that my plumbing worked, which is not a reason to
spend his attention. It was also the second time in one day that I had proposed sending him something
nobody needed - the first was a completion report that carried no question.

THE RULE, now installed in the shift contract that every woken session reads:
 A TEXT HAPPENS ONLY WHEN IT CARRIES A QUESTION, A WARNING, OR A DECISION WITH A DEADLINE.
 A FINISHED JOB IS RECORDED IN THE LEDGER. "IT FINISHED" IS NOT A REASON TO TEXT.

 The three legitimate cases, as the contract states them:
 (1) A QUESTION ONLY HE CAN ANSWER, with the recommendation on the same line.
 (2) A WARNING HE MUST ACT ON TODAY - something about to break, be lost, or cost money, where
 waiting for him to look would be too late.
 (3) A DECISION WITH A DEADLINE - a reply is genuinely needed before time runs out.

 Explicitly forbidden: that a job started; that a job finished; that tests passed; that a branch was
 pushed; that a queue row was filed; or texting to prove the channel works.

WHY THIS IS BETTER THAN "SHORT TEXTS ONLY", which was my previous rule: a short text can still be
worthless. The test is not length, it is whether the message asks him for something or warns him of
something. A completion report fails that test no matter how short it is; a one-line question passes it
no matter how small the decision.

STANDING INTERPRETATION FOR THIS SEAT: "keep me informed" is what the LEDGER and the journal are for.
The phone is for the things only he can do - decide, unblock, or be warned in time.
