**D3149 · 2026-10-13 · Battery chargers: PhoneLCDParts at 2.99 is the supply; resale at 10 (2026-10-13).**

Owner asked 2026-10-13 where to buy more of the two-pin universal battery chargers he holds
(4.4V 600mA pins / 5.2V 1000mA USB) and which ones are good.

**Decided:**
1. **Supply = PhoneLCDParts, Wayne NJ, `LCD-SS-5`, $2.99 each.** Verified by the session itself
 (`itemprop="price" content="2.99"`, in stock, add-to-cart, no account needed, ground-only, free
 shipping over $350). Buy 24. Not the eBay bulk lots ($4-5/unit, single lots) and not an import.
2. **Import rejected at this size.** Alibaba UC-01 is $0.67-0.92 goods, but duty is 37.50% for HTS
 8504.40.95 from China, de minimis is dead for every country since 2025-08-29, and the 50 kg ocean
 minimum makes small orders worse. Landed 200 pc = $1.49 + broker + bond + 3-6 weeks. Revisit at
 1,000+ a year.
3. **Bench step-up = a charging/activation board with a V/A display** from the shop's existing parts
 accounts (MobileSentrix `.../repair-tools/testing-devices/external-chargers`: JCID BT01, AiXun,
 MaAnt Dianba), or a KORAD KA3005D settable supply at $129.99 for a calibrated tool.
4. **Resale at $10 out the door**, NOT the `cost x 1.4501` house rule ($4.34). The 1.4501 rule is a
 device-resale rule; this is a $3 accessory whose identical twin is $9.99 on Amazon and $10.82-14.95
 on the next listings, and the customer can price it. Flagged in the doc as a documented exception
 to be folded into PRICING-RULE.md if the owner agrees.
5. **Spec that actually matters:** the charge-target voltage (4.2V for 3.7V cells, 4.35-4.40V for the
 3.8V cells most phones use since ~2016), real termination rather than a float, the sliding pins
 (the wear item), and a volts/amps display rather than a percentage bar. No $10 unit in this class
 has a true voltage readout.
