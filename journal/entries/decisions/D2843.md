**D — 2026-09-30 · Chip's pacing retuned: at most one self-chosen line a day, a calmer room share, and the addressed path a little more generous**

Owner instruction, verbatim: *"make sure chip doesn't respond too much people are getting annoyed, more like once in a while pretty weird and creepy, and a drop more when addressed directly"*.

MEASURED BASELINE (live database, before the change)

- Ambient Chip replies, from his own counters: 08-19: 3, 08-21: 2, 08-23: 1, 09-04: 1, 09-23: 4, 09-25: 2, 09-28: 2.
- Utterances he actually sent (distinct minutes holding a `Chip:` relay row): 09-25: **12**, 09-28: 7, 09-29: 6. Each fans out to ~12 phones — 12 utterances is ~144 SMS to the family in one day.
- Of those, the *addressed* path carried 9 on 09-25 and 9 on 09-28 (`settings.chip_direct_*`). So ambient was already about every second or third active day; the volume that annoys the family is mostly nine addressed answers in a day stacked on top of a self-chosen one.
- Chip's room share before the 2026-09-29 rule: 21.1 % of all September turns, and 38.5-47.1 % of the turns in each of the three most recent real conversations. Fair share with eleven members is 0.09.

THE CHANGE (live `settings` keys; every knob is read per call, so this needs no restart and no deploy)

| Knob | Was | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `chip_daily_max` | 3 | **1** | hard ceiling on self-chosen (ambient) lines per day |
| `chip_min_dormant` | 24 h | **36 h** | after any reply, 36 h of guaranteed quiet |
| `chip_cooldown` | 45 min | **90 min** | floor between ambient replies |
| `chip_rate` | 0.02 | **0.015** | chance per group message while dormant |
| `chip_wake_factor` | 0.10 | **0.05** | halve the "he has been quiet a while" boost |
| `chip_burst_rate` | 0.18 | **0.10** | calmer inside a burst window |
| `chip_burst_cooldown` | 30 min | **60 min** | quieter inside a live conversation |
| `chip_turn_share` | 0.34 | **0.20** | ceiling on his share of a rolling 30-minute window |
| `chip_direct_max` | 25 | **30** | the addressed allowance rises: being spoken to is not chiming in |
| `chip_direct_quiet` | (unset = answer at night) | **respect** | 23:00-07:00 stays quiet even when addressed; one 2 a.m. text should not wake eleven phones |

WHY THIS IS SAFE AND DOES NOT MUTE HIM

- An addressed reply is recorded with `unsolicited=False` and does not consume the daily allowance (`personality.py:399-402`), so `chip_daily_max=1` cannot silence the addressed path — checked in the code, not assumed.
- A message that **names** Chip still bypasses the room-share rule entirely (`personality.py:979`); only messages that merely *read* as aimed at him yield while he is already the loudest voice. So "a drop more when addressed directly" survives the tightening.
- Every value was read back through the same accessor functions the running app calls (`config.ai_daily_max()` etc.) — the AFTER block of the applied run shows 1, 36.0, 90, 1, 30, 0.2, and `ai_direct_quiet_ok = False`.

REVERSIBILITY: one command per knob (`db.set_setting(key, value)`). The previous values are the code defaults, so deleting the rows restores exactly the old behaviour.

STILL TO COME FROM THE SAME INSTRUCTION: *"pretty weird and creepy"* is a register change, proposed as replacement text for the live `settings.chip_facts` by the `fc/brain` workstream and gated on the reply-quality harness (`chip_eval.py`, 33-case golden set) so it is not shipped on taste.
