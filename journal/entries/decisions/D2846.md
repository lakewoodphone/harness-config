**D — 2026-09-30 · Removal now costs an hour: a removed member is told the wait, five times, and then ignored until he is back in.**

Owner instruction, verbatim: *"once you are removed you can only come back in 1 hour later … so now when shabsi texts the chat say he is muted, and when texts #start you should say how much time he has left, and then every time he texts it should respond with how much wait time (up to 5 times then it should just ignore him until he's actually let back in)"*.

WHAT IT DOES (all verified live on a copy of the database, 2026-09-30 16:06 UTC, `chip_resume`-style script at `/tmp/fc-shabsi-removal.py`)

- `hub.mark_removed(phone)` is the removal path: it mutes AND stamps who removed him and when. This is the difference between a removal and a self-mute.
- While removed, **anything** he texts — a plain message, `#start`, `#help`, `#status`, anything — is answered with how long is left:
 `You're muted: you were removed from the group chat, so nothing you send goes to the group. You can come back in about 45 minutes — text #start then.`
- The fifth reply ends with *"This is the last reply until you're back in."*; the sixth text produces **nothing at all**.
- After the hour, `#start` puts him back in and clears the state; before the hour it only reports the wait.
- Nothing a removed member sends ever reaches the group, at any point.
- **A SELF-mute is deliberately exempt**: `#mute` then `#start` still works instantly, because a person must never be able to trap themselves. Only an outside removal starts the clock.

STATE AT THE TIME OF WRITING

Shabsi: muted, removed stamped `2026-09-30T15:50:40Z` (the moment he was last taken out), so he can return from **16:50:40Z**; his reply counter is at **0 of 5** untouched by the preview, which ran entirely against a copy.

REVERSAL

`db.set_member_muted(phone, False)` plus clearing the three `fc_mute_*` keys readmits anyone immediately; `mark_removed` with a backdated `fc_mute_at:<phone>` shortens or removes the wait.
