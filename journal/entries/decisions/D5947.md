**D5947 · 2026-10-14 · Owner answer 2026-10-13: no quiet hours, night delivery is not a harm.**

**Owner answer, asked directly 2026-10-13 during the owner-text-line audit.**

The audit reported that 27% of what reached his phone arrived between 00:00 and 05:59 and counted it
as a failure. Asked whether the company should hold a quiet window, he answered:

> "doesn't matter, i just open texting when i see it doesn't matter when it comes in"

**Decision: no quiet hours. `owner_quiet_hours_enabled` stays False.** A quiet window would withhold
messages he wants delivered whenever they are ready.

**Two consequences, recorded because the estate has re-asked the same question before:**

1. **Do not ask this again.** The arrival hour is not a cost to him. Any future audit of this channel
 must not present "x% arrived at night" as a defect.
2. **I was wrong, and the record says so.** The measurement was sound; the interpretation was mine and
 it was wrong. `AUDIT-2026-10-13.md` keeps the 27% number with an explicit correction rather than
 deleting it, because a deleted mistake is a mistake that gets made twice.

**The generalisable lesson:** a metric about the machine's behaviour (when a message was sent) is not
evidence about the recipient (whether it bothered him) unless he has said so. The audit conflated the
two. The night-delivery column is a fact about the scheduler, not about his experience - and the only
way to know which is to ask the person, once, before writing it down as harm.
