**D7630 · 2026-10-18 · Owner queue audit 2026-10-18: one fact one row, a dossier gate, learning with evidence, the whole mailbox.**

**D · 2026-10-18 · The owner queue is one table, one order, one dossier per item -- and the rules that decide what enters it.**

Decided in the audit round of 2026-10-18, after measuring the live queue and finding five defects that
reading the code had not shown. Recorded here because a decision that lives only in a session is a
decision thrown away.

**What the queue is.** `owner_decision_queue` on `secratary-cloud`, read through
`/root/bin/owner-queue.py`. Everything that needs Eliyahu: a decision only he can make, and anything
from his personal texts, calls, voicemails or email he would want to know about. Dialpad is never in
it. Google Voice reaches it through the forwarded mail. The rule set is `harness-config/docs/
owner-queue.md`; the procedure is the `owner-queue` skill.

**The five rules this round added or corrected:**

1. **One fact, one row -- collapsed at READ time** (§8.1). By message identity when proven
 (account+thread), by a decision absorbing the notice that is the same matter, and by a digest
 losing any member a real row already owns. Probable-but-unproven siblings are *shown* and closed
 with `--with`, never silently merged, because a wrong merge hides an item.
2. **A dossier gate** (§7.1). A queue-surface decision with no recommendation, or with no amount, date
 or named party, is held out of the working set and counted -- never presented. Rows citing
 `Evidence: <table> row <id>` are joined live and printed on the card; a template recommendation
 ("investigate it and bring me a recommendation") is replaced by one the record supports. A person's
 text with no recommendation is demoted to attention: a message is not a formal decision.
3. **Learning reads four dimensions, signed, with per-dimension floors** (§6). party, domain, class,
 channel; a kind he dismisses sinks and a kind he always acts on rises; a channel needs 10 closes
 before it may move at all and a sender needs 3. Only his verbs count -- `resolved` contributes
 nothing, because 442 of 559 closed rows were closed by me under the autonomy rule, not by him.
 `learning --backfill` recovered the history; the tool must print its evidence base.
4. **The mailbox is read, not the inbox** (§4.2): `newer_than:Nd -in:spam -in:trash`. And **health
 proves completeness, not just freshness** (§12.0), from each ingest's own `fetched / dropped /
 written / folded / errors`.
5. **Two Google Voice rules corrected** (§4.1): a community announcement is not marketing, and an
 unknown missed call is not "nothing to act on" -- it folds into one weekly row that names the
 numbers.

**What is still not true.** The learning has no live evidence yet: of 111 answered or dismissed rows,
0 come from a channel that still writes. The mechanism is proven by simulation and by a 5-close test
run, but the first real teaching is the first knock-through session. The queue says so itself rather
than claiming otherwise.
