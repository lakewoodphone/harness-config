**D9303 · 2026-10-21 · Alpha and beta testers get it free for the first month or two, and then they are customers.**

aliases: Q32

Owner, verbatim (2026-10-21):

> "I will have a free release to alpha and beta testers for the first month or 2, I think."

What it decides, condensed:

1. Alpha and beta testers pay nothing for one to two months — the universal trial extended to the people
 deliberately finding faults.
2. A tester has a job: filing what breaks, in his own words, and being reachable.
3. Free ends on a date told at install; then he pays the ordinary price or takes his release. No silent
 conversion, no permanent discount tier.
4. Testers are enrolled with a record: installed date, free-period end, reported faults, whether they converted.
5. Wider in one way, narrower in another: diagnostics (verdicts, decisions, support threads) may be read to
 diagnose a fault; nothing about images, which stay ephemeral unless the household opts in.
6. The pilot is measured, not felt: support minutes per phone per month, escalated frames, false blocks, release
 requests — the first ten testers are the only source, so they are written down.
7. Same seal, same signature, same floor as a paying customer; free is the service, never the standard, and the
 cost is a recorded pilot cost.
8. A tester who leaves is a result, not an insult, and his reason is the first churn data.
