**D68 · 2026-09-14 · The API owns timezone correctness; consumers are never asked to guess.**

Every fleet-api timestamp is a `timestamp without time zone` holding UTC, and `::text` returned it as `2026-09-08 22:54:13.245131`. Browsers parse that as **local** time (measured: `2026-09-09T02:54:13.245Z`, four hours off on a UTC-4 box), so every consumer computed a wrong age with no error anywhere. Formatted in SQL as ISO-8601 with an explicit `Z` (`to_char(col, 'YYYY-MM-DD"T"HH24:MI:SS.MS"Z"')`), which is also session-TimeZone independent.
*Rule this establishes:* a serialised timestamp leaves a service with its zone attached, or it is a bug. Applies to any future endpoint, not just these three columns.
