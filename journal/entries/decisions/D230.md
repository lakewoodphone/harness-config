**D230 · 2026-09-16 · Node durability is the one-minute watchdog hook plus a logon entry.**

Registering a scheduled task on ZABZ-YOGA is DENIED without elevation, and I will not ask for a UAC
prompt for something I can do another way. Measured 2026-09-16: Register-ScheduledTask fails with
"Access is denied" both with a principal and without -- because BUILTIN\Administrators is present
but marked "Group used for deny only", i.e. the account IS an administrator holding a UAC-filtered
token. So scripts/Install-PhoneGate.ps1 is written and checked in but not installed, and instead:
 * `dshw.ps1 ensure` now checks the gate FIRST, independently of engine health, and that already
 runs every minute as the user through the DSH Engine Watchdog task;
 * a Startup-folder entry covers logon.
Verified by killing the gate by hand: `dshw ensure` -> listening again -> tailnet `/` 200 and
`/api` 401, with result=clean in ~/.dsh-sync-status/phone-gate.json.
