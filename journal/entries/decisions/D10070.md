**D10070 · 2026-10-26 · The agent can see its own context: dsh-plugin-context-awareness shipped, and persona rule 8 corrected.**

## The problem

The engine had a token meter, three context projections and `/cost`; the AGENT could read none of them.
Its belief about its own context was a guess, and the guess was pessimistic and acted on: sessions
announced a limit and proposed a fresh window at a fraction of real occupancy. The persona made it
worse — rule 8 asserted the harness "does not tell you when either happened", which is false for
compaction (the checkpoint preamble is a visible message) and true only of the pruner.

## What shipped (2026-10-26, ZABZ-YOGA)

1. `packages/plugin-context-awareness` — a host-plane bundle row, `dsh-plugin-context-awareness`:
 * `context_status` tool: tokens / window / percent / band / when compaction fires / compaction count.
 * `CONTEXT …` notice at the first measurable step and at each band crossing
 (`comfortable <35% · working <55% · tightening <70% · critical`), stating the real fire point
 (700,000 of 1,000,000 at the preset's thresholdRatio 0.7).
 * `COMPACTED …` notice once after `compaction/summary` or `compaction/prune`: what a checkpoint keeps,
 what is at risk, that nothing was destroyed, where the transcript is (`ps_search what=sessions`),
 do not re-do recorded work, do not restart.
 * The number comes from `ctx.tokenMeter.measure(session)` — the engine's own — deliberately NOT a
 second fold. An absent or throwing meter is REPORTED, never estimated.
2. Persona rule 8 replaced with "You can see your own context. Read it; never guess at it", plus a bound
 on the cost rule: start a fresh session because the bill says so and the handoff is written, never
 because the window might be full.
3. `scripts/mirror-persona-block.py` — the same rule lives in FOUR surfaces and was fixed in one;
 the script mirrors it and refuses when a target has zero or several matches.

## Why the design is what it is

Claude Code tells the model plainly that the session "ran out of context" and conceals the mechanic only
from the user. arXiv 2610.20116 measured the cost of summaries that do not declare what they removed:
confident errors, +22-42 points of planted-fact recall when they do. arXiv 2606.22528: in-context
constraints honoured 100% before compaction, 70% after. So the notices state what was lost and what
survives, not just that something happened.

## Evidence, not assertion

* 23 assertions pass (`node --test test/logic.test.mjs test/wiring.test.mjs`).
* Live: the orientation notice rendered in the shipping session at `208,587 of 1,000,000 (21%)`, and the
 `context_status` schema registered in the same step — the delivery path is measured, not assumed.
* `dsh --profile web --dump-config` exit 0 with the new rule present and the old one absent.
* `scripts/preset-drift.py`: `zabz/agent.cordis.yml` MATCH.

## The gap left open, on purpose

`dsh-compaction-basic` still tells its summariser "Do NOT mention this summarization request or that the
context was compacted", and its template has no section for what was dropped. The wording is hardcoded;
patching node_modules dies at the next upgrade. The honest next step is our own compaction plugin,
built against the benchmark harness in arXiv 2610.20116 — not done on faith.
