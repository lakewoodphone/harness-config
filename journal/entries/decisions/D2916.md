**D2916 · 2026-10-05 · Owner-attention sweep design: one question per run, which channels are question-worthy, dedup as a union, no send path.**

﻿DECIDED 2026-10-05, app/services/owner_attention_sweep.py. These are engineering
calls I made and record rather than route to the owner.

1. AT MOST ONE QUESTION PER RUN, and none at all when nothing is new. The ranked
 list is the artifact; owner_decision_queue is the scarce channel. A run that
 has nothing new to ask is a correct run, not a failure.

2. WHICH FINDINGS MAY BECOME A QUESTION. Only three channels are treated as
 decisions that are the owner's: obligation, owner_task, provider_payment_
 failure. A waiting customer is WORK the shop does -- it goes on the ranked
 list with a recommended action, not into the decision queue. Narrow exception:
 a customer message matching refund/dispute/chargeback/complaint/attorney/
 fraud/lawsuit/overcharge is a decision and may be asked.

3. DEDUP IS A UNION, BIASED TOWARD SUPPRESSION. app.owner_question_dedupe OR a
 supplementary entity/identifier signal. A false positive costs one skipped
 candidate; a false negative asks the owner twice. See L3257.

4. THE TASK-OVERLAP CROSS-REFERENCE WAS REMOVED, NOT TUNED. It produced a false
 claim in front of the owner and could not be thresholded honestly. See L3260.

5. FAILED RUNS DO NOT STAMP THE CLOCK. _last_owner_attention_at is set only after
 success, so the next 300s tick retries instead of sleeping 30 minutes on a
 failure -- the second half of why email_catalog stayed empty for so long.

6. THE STALENESS WITNESS RUNS ON THE AUTOPILOT'S CLOCK, NOT THE SWEEP'S. A run
 record cannot detect its own absence: if the sweep never runs, no sweep code
 executes to notice. The tick is the independent witness. Throttled to one
 warning per hour so a real alarm does not become wallpaper.

7. NO SEND PATH EXISTS IN THE MODULE. There is no mail/SMS/call call anywhere in
 it. Its only outbound surface is a decision-queue row, which is a table the
 owner reads, not a message anybody receives.
