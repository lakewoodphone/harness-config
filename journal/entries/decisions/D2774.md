**D2774 · 2026-09-28 · Household bought a HobbyZone Sport Cub S 2 RTF trainer, 2026-09-28.**

**2026-09-28, Chol Hamoed. Bought: HobbyZone Sport Cub S 2 615mm RTF, model HBZ444000, 159.99, at HobbyTown Toms River.**

Reported by the owner in the same session, from the store. Tag stated 206.99 retail, so it came in **47 below** the retailer's own stated retail — consistent with the other check that day (Traxxas T-Maxx 3.3 at 349.95 against a 549.95 list), which together establish that **this shop sells at or below list, not at a hobby-shop premium.**

**Why this aircraft and not the alternatives on the same wall:**
- It was the **only complete, real 4-channel trainer of usable size** there. 615mm — roughly **40% more wingspan** than the 99 P-51D — three flight modes, no assembly, **"Needed to Complete: Nothing!"**
- **Fifteen E-flite models on that wall carried the bold "2" = BNF Basic**, meaning **no transmitter in the box**. Two of them (T-28, Apprentice class) also lack battery and charger. See **L3050**.
- The **99 P-51D Mustang 450mm RTF** was the cheaper complete alternative and is the right buy if the flying matters more than the plane's headroom. **The 199.99 RAGE F4U Corsair** is the brushless step up.
- The **Traxxas T-Maxx 3.3 nitro** was rejected despite a ~200 discount: fuel at 30-40/gallon, glow plugs, tuning every session, on top of the price. Electric 4x4 at 229.95-329.95 is the right shape for a family.

**Contents:** Spektrum MLP6DSM transmitter, 4x AA, E-flite 150mAh 1S LiPo, 1S USB charger, spare prop, **plus a set of floats** — which matters because this household has water.

**Recorded as NOT bought:** a kite, kite line, an extra battery, or any other RC item. Left explicitly unknown rather than assumed.

**The operational consequence for future sessions:** there is now an RC aircraft in the house, and **it is a light foam plane that flies in calm air only, roughly 5 mph and under.** A maiden flight at dusk with the wind already dead is how a new plane breaks. **Fly it in daylight.**
