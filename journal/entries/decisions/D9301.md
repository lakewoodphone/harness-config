**D9301 · 2026-10-21 · Shabbos and Yom Tov lock the phone by default, on sunset at the phone's own location, and only calls get through.**

aliases: Q30

Owner, verbatim (2026-10-21):

> "User can decide this, but the app pushes for and defaults to the phone locks for Shabbos and Yom Tov based
> on sunset at location and only allows calls — and you have to bear in mind that Israel can have a slightly
> different calendar."

What it decides, condensed:

1. Default on: a newly sealed phone locks for Shabbos and Yom Tov unless the customer turns it off at setup.
2. Sunset at the phone's own location to nightfall, computed on the device with no network.
3. Only calls pass — browsing, messaging, the app store and the filter's settings are unavailable.
4. Emergency numbers are always reachable, including the country's; a lock that stops a child calling for help
 is the failure this must never have.
5. Israel is a different calendar, not a shifted clock: one day of Yom Tov there, differing readings, chosen by
 location rather than a stored flag.
6. Nightfall is a household setting, the 42 and 72 minute conventions both available and shown by name; travel
 moves the location and the customer is told first.
7. The customer can turn it off, the child cannot — the parent may make it part of the floor.
8. The locked screen says why and when it unlocks; the calendar ships as a versioned data file; off in the broker
 today (`shabbos_curfew_enabled` false), enforced on-device.
