**D3153 · 2026-10-14 · Desk food replaces the shake as the office lunch plan.**

DECIDED 2026-10-14. The office lunch stops being a shake. The shake was solving the wrong problem: he
wanted something at the desk that tastes like food and carries a meal's calories, and a shake is a
convenience product priced as convenience that tastes like a supplement.

The plan, all shelf-stable, all no-fridge, all clearing his kashrut gate (no dairy ingredient: OU-pareve,
OU-DE or certified meat/fish; plain OU-D is out because he keeps chalav yisroel):

A. **Tuna lunch — the default.** 1 StarKist Chunk Light in Water 2.6 oz pouch (OU Pareve) + ~6 Wasa
 crispbread (OK Kosher pareve, Pas Yisroel) + 1 oz roasted almonds (OU Pareve) + 1 apple.
 about 570 kcal, 25 g protein, **about 3.40** = 0.60 USD per 100 kcal.
B. **Peanut-butter lunch — the cheap day.** 2 Tbsp Skippy/Jif (both OU Pareve) + ~6 Wasa + 1 banana +
 1 oz Biena chickpeas (OU Pareve). about 655 kcal, 13 g protein, **about 2.36** = **0.36 per 100 kcal**,
 the cheapest lunch on the board. Lower protein, so not five days a week.
C. **Zero-prep fallback.** Aloha Peanut Butter Chocolate Chip bar, OU Pareve (OUV7-VAFNTH2), 240 kcal,
 14 g protein, 1.88 a bar in a 12-pack. Bigger alternative: GoMacro MacroBar PB Chocolate Chip, pareve
 under cRc, 290 kcal, 2.52 a bar.
D. With a kettle: 2 plain Quaker Original oats + 1 Tbsp peanut butter + 1 oz pistachios + 1 banana,
 about 555 kcal for 1.75.

**The arithmetic that settles it:** five desk lunches cost roughly 13-17 USD a week against 21.25 for five
Kate Farms bottles — and the peanut-butter lunch is a third cheaper per calorie than the OWYN carton the
shake decision landed on, while being real food. The shake stays available as the fallback on a day with
no time.

Traps recorded so they are not walked into later: **Chomps meat sticks carry no hechsher at all**; Triscuit
variety packs are OU-D (only Cinnamon Brown Sugar is pareve); **Justin's nut-butter CUPS are OU-D while the
jars are pareve**; KIND nut bars and KIND Protein are OU-D; Clif, RXBAR, Quest, Barebells, Perfect Bar,
Pure Protein bars and Nature Valley Protein are dairy or uncertified; Quaker Essentials and Kodiak oatmeal
are OU-D; Blue Diamond is OK Kosher, not OU. Kosher beef jerky exists (Ben's Best, Yoni's, Old
Williamsburg, Graze Smokehouse — all OU meat) but runs about 85 USD a pound, so it is not a desk staple.

Where to buy: Wasa 3.49 a box at Seasons Lakewood; StarKist pouches 0.94-1.28 at Walmart (1.99 locally);
Klein's/Wonderful almonds about 10 a pound; Skippy 2.00 at Kosher West on the current discount; Bingo
Wholesale has no web storefront at all but free membership and is the go-and-look bulk option; Super Stop
delivers in Lakewood with no minimum; KosherKart has paused deliveries.
