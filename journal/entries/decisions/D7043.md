**D7043 · 2026-10-18 · VOID D7041 and D7042 — a test run wrote two fake owner answers into the journal.**

**These two entries are VOID. They record a test, not a decision.**

D7041 and D7042 were written at 2026-10-18 15:19 UTC by an audit session running
`owner-queue.py answer 631 --with 645` **against a throwaway copy of the queue at /tmp/oq-e2e.db**,
to prove that one answer can close two rows that are the same matter. The answer text
"paid it today, both alerts same card" is mine, written to see the mechanism work. The owner never
said it, and nothing about Chase account ...9730 has been decided or paid.

The mechanism it was testing does work: `--with 645` closed #645 as "answered together with #631" and
wrote a feedback row for each, with `channel=email` and `party=Chase` populated -- which is the first
evidence that the learning loop receives attributable dimensions.

**The defect this exposed is real and is fixed with it.** `_journal_copy()` wrote to the live journal
whatever database the row lived in, so any test, backfill or replay against a copy wrote fake owner
answers into the permanent append-only record. It now refuses unless the database is the authority's
own `authority.db`, and says so on stderr when it refuses. Verified by re-running the same test after
the fix: no journal entry appeared.
