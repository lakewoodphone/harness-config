**D2333 · 2026-09-23 · Cases on Alibaba: yes and worth ~\/unit - but D163 governs, landing the first 10 at retail and sampling the import lane in parallel.**

**Owner asked, 2026-09-23: "for cases, do you think we can order very good but cheaper cases on alibaba or something?"**

**My answer: yes, materially - and there is a standing rule about exactly this lane that I checked before answering.** The case is the single biggest non-device cost line (**$21.95/unit** SUPCASE Unicorn Beetle Pro from Amazon, roughly 20x the entire per-unit packaging cost). Alibaba-class two-layer rugged iPhone 11 cases look like **$1.50-4.00/unit at 50-250 MOQ**, so maybe **$3-7 landed** - a saving of roughly **$15/unit**, about **$1,800 on 100 units**.

**BUT THE RULE GOVERNS, and it exists because this cost the owner money: D163 (2026-09-15).** His own words after paying ~$70 of DHL duty by hand on a small parts order: *"i just paid myself, and i am not happy about how much it cost"*. D163 records the structural cause - **China/HK parcels lost the $800 de-minimis exemption, so duty is now ordinary landed cost on every parcel from that lane** - and sets the standing method: *"compute and state the landed cost (unit + duty + freight) and compare it to whole-unit alternatives, not to list price."* **So "cheaper on Alibaba" is a claim about list price, and the number that decides it is landed cost.**

**The three real blockers are not money** (an MOQ order of 100 at ~$3-4 is only ~$300-400, so working capital is fine):
1. **Lead time 2-6 weeks by air** - which kills a 1-2 week launch outright. This, not cost, is why the answer is NO for the first 10.
2. **Quality, on the thing that physically protects a $299 device sold to a customer, with no returns.** Needs a sample before any bulk order, and the sample itself takes a week or two.
3. Duty is now ordinary cost on that lane - a surprise only if uncomputed.

**RECOMMENDATION GIVEN: buy the 10 at retail ($219.50) for the launch, and run the Alibaba lane in parallel for the real batch**, because the saving is worth a sample order and a real quote but is not worth delaying the first sale.

**Dispatched `subagent-98`** to price it properly: Alibaba/AliExpress/DHgate two-layer rugged iPhone 11 cases at MOQ/100/250/500 with pad-print or deboss options, **plus whether US-warehoused stock exists** (a US warehouse removes the duty lane AND the transit time, which may beat Alibaba outright), plus a US-wholesale lane with no import at all, plus **the landed cost computed with the HTS classification and duty rate including any Section 301 add-on, brokerage amortised** - and a recommended single sample order. It was told explicitly that **lead time is a first-class result, not a footnote**, and that a supplier's own "drop tested" claim is not a finding.

**Also live:** `subagent-97` pricing the 250-unit packaging run and, most importantly, checking whether the 7x5x3 box actually fits an iPhone 11 **inside a rugged case** - because the spec sized it by estimation and ordering 250 boxes that do not fit is the expensive mistake available here.
