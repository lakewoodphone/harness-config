**D8386 · 2026-10-19 · Backtest predicate pinned by test, and the cheap-share target derived rather than copied.**

## 2026-10-20T00:01Z · ZABZ-YOGA · closed the two loose ends that could hide a repeat of the drift

### The backtest predicate now has a test

`is_deferrable` drifted from the gate once and produced a **16.1 %** projection against a real **10.0 %**. The
cause was two implementations of one question; the fix was to make the backtest call the gate's own
`is_exempt`, but a fix without a test is a pause, not a guard.

Two tests now load `scripts/cost/schedule_backtest.py` by path and assert it defers exactly what the gate
defers: `dept_engineering` yes; `comms_relationships`, `business_ops`, `scheduling`, `home_life`, `research`,
`devices` and `orchestrator` no; and the aggressive scenario still excludes the orchestrator. 65 tests in the
file.

**A projection is a claim about a decision, and a claim about a decision needs the decision's own predicate
rather than a similar-looking one.**

### The target is set, and it was derived rather than copied

`SCHEDULE_CHEAP_SHARE_TARGET=0.86`, verified in the running process's environment at 00:00:22Z. The handoff
suggested 0.8; the replay puts peak input share at ~13.96 % under the live exemption set, so 0.86 is the
reachable figure.

0.8 was not merely optimistic - it was **unfalsifiable**, because the baseline already met it. A target that
the status quo satisfies cannot detect anything. The replacement is derived from what the gate actually does,
so it can fail.
