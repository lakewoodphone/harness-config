**D3038 · 2026-10-09 · Oven silence is a hardware fix, not a setting.**

Decided 2026-10-09 (dev decision, mine to make and record): stop looking for a keypad setting that
silences the end-of-cook beep on the GE JGB630DEF3BB. The SF menu on this control generation has no tone
option and GE's manual documents none, so the permanent fix is to silence the buzzer on the ERC board
(WB27X44729). That job is recorded as deferred in the reference doc.

Also settled: for a Shabbos where nobody can press a pad, the only silent mode is Sabbath (SAb), which
cannot use Cook Time and therefore leaves the oven on until it is cancelled -- so an oven that stays on
all night is the known, accepted cost of silence until the board mod is done.
