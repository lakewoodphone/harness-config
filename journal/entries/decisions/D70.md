**D70 · 2026-09-14 · A `retired` device is exempt from staleness in every surface, and the monitor is the authority for that rule.**

Measured this session: of 65 devices, 9 are `retired`, all in the DRN fleet, and of the 62 the staff banner called "silent", those 9 were retired phones working as intended. The monitor had already encoded this (`retired_exempt`); the UI had not, and reported the opposite. The fleet-api now returns `retired` explicitly on the single-device read (falling back to `state`), and the UI excludes those devices from the silent count and labels them neutrally.
*Rule:* when a policy has been encoded once, a second implementation must read the same field rather than re-deriving the rule from raw state.
