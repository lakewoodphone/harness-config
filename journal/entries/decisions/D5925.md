**2026-10-09 · The Weinberg 1,000.00 payment is booked whole as owner funding of LPT, and the composition discrepancy is carried in both directions rather than resolved.**

The owner paid 1,000.00 on 2026-10-09 by Zelle from his personal Chase College *9730 to Yisroel Weinberg, and said "the extra $400 is a bonus" and that it came from his personal account "so it's a loan to the business".

**Decided, and why:**
1. **The whole 1,000.00 is owner funding of LPT** (an A8 movement, method `on-behalf`), not a part-business part-personal split. His instruction was about the payment, not about a component of it, and the payment discharged an LPT obligation. The consequence — LPT owes him 1,000.00 and both parts are LPT wages — is recorded, not hidden.
2. **Both parts are 2026 wages** (a bonus to a person who worked for LPT in 2026), so his 2026 W-2 and the NJ-W-3 / 941 carry 1,000.00 more. Gusto was cancelled 2026-10-07, so LPT files them itself. This was not put to the owner as a question: it is the treatment, not a preference.
3. **The discrepancy is recorded, not resolved.** His words give a settlement element of 600.00, which is 63.46 below the 663.46 gross-basis figure the owner himself decided on 2026-10-05; the total paid is 336.54 above it. I did **not** ask which he meant, because the answer changes nothing material: the obligation is discharged in full, nothing further is owed, and the W-2 shows 1,000.00 either way. What it *would* change is a future session's reading of the case file, so both readings are written down in both directions (case file and books record) with an explicit "do not read 63.46 as an unpaid balance".
4. **Nothing outbound.** The written acknowledgment from Weinberg (the one thing the 2026-10-05 plan asked for) is queued as an owner question with the wording ready; it needs his per-message approval, like everything else.
5. **The obligation keeps its amount (66346).** The extra 336.54 is not a second obligation, and seeding one would inflate the register.

Rejected alternatives: hand-inserting an `owner_loan_movements` row (double-counts — see L3412); a manual journal entry (double-posts the cash account — same lesson); asking the owner to re-derive the 600/663.46 question (attention for no consequence — rule: do not make him think for me).
