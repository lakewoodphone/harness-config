## P2888 — Every owner reminder SMS is silently held by the urgency gate, and the row still reads sent

WHAT HURTS: no reminder the owner schedules ever reaches his phone, and the record says it was sent.

MECHANISM, measured on the authority 2026-10-09:
- app/services/personal_ops.py line 741 calls send_owner_update(f"Reminder: {msg}", settings=settings) with NO urgency argument, so urgency falls back through _infer_owner_update_urgency(reason="proactive_update") to "normal".
- app/config.py line 947 sets owner_sms_min_urgency = "urgent" (no .env override on the authority; the prod .env only sets OWNER_SMS_DAILY_QUOTA=20 and OWNER_SMS_REPOINT_MONITORED_INBOX=true).
- app/services/owner_notifications.py::owner_sms_allowed returns order(current) >= order(threshold), so normal < urgent is False for every reminder.
- send_owner_update then writes owner_message_queue status='held' and logs activity owner_sms_held. It returns ok=True with held=True, and personal_ops counts that as sms_sent.
- Worse, mark_reminder_delivered() is called BEFORE the send attempt (line 722), so reminders.status flips to 'sent' even when nothing left the building.

EVIDENCE (the case that found it):
- reminders row 564, "Sign up for gov programs: SNAP/food stamps ... NJ FamilyCare + LIHEAP", scheduled 2026-10-07T15:30Z, status='sent', delivery_channel='both', delivery_attempts=1.
- owner_message_queue row 2493, same text, status='held', sent_at NULL.
- activity_log row 324548: action=owner_sms_held, reason=unspecified:b8fe599f, urgency=normal, 2026-10-07T15:30:15Z.
- Grep of dialpad_sms_cache for the text and for the owner number 8483897895 around 2026-10-07: nothing sent. The dashboard row and the 'sent' status were the ONLY delivery. The owner found out by asking, on 2026-10-09.

BLASThisk WHEN IT IS REPAIRED: every past-due pending reminder becomes due at once. 33 reminders are pending; the 2026-10-09 09:00 and 13:00 batches are in the near future. Re-date or drain the backlog BEFORE the gate is opened, or the repair becomes a text flood.

WHAT WOULD FIX IT: pass an explicit urgency for owner-created reminders (an owner-scheduled reminder is an owner-authorised interruption, unlike proactive chatter), and stop marking a reminder delivered before the send outcome is known.
