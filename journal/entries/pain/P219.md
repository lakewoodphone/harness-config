## P219 — The mesh's own processes are the top disk reader, and a purchase rested on the opposite claim

Measured 2026-09-16 by the disk pass on ZABZ-YOGA, over two 120 s windows with GetProcessIoCounters:
python.exe is the dominant consumer by three orders of magnitude -- one python process had read
3.15 TB and one ripgrep invocation read at p50 61.6 / p95 250 MB/s, while SearchIndexer.exe and
MsMpEng.exe never entered the top twelve and measured 0 B/s read and 0 B/s write during deliberate
churn. So the "scanners are the disk problem" thesis (docs/mesh/30 3) was wrong, and with it:
 * the 107.3 GB / 29.2 GB figure -- not reproducible AND not measurable from an unprivileged seat,
 because GetProcessIoCounters cannot open a LocalSystem handle;
 * "\PhysicalDisk(_Total)\% Disk Time" as a headline -- it read 354 % at p50 while the disk queue
 was 2, i.e. mostly NVMe command overlap. Use queue length and bytes/sec.
 * the Phase 2 proposal to buy a dedicated NVMe volume: its only justification was that figure.
 ZABZ-YOGA shows 71 GB free, pagefile 2 % used, 3,000 MB written against 3.22 TB read.
WHAT WOULD FIX IT: a clean baseline on the administered node (ZABZ-TECH) with the fleet stopped,
then the same with the search scope exclusion applied, read from queue + bytes/sec. That needs
elevation, which I cannot do from this seat. The open lead is the 3.15 TB read itself.
