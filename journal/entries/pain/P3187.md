## P3187 — A queued family relay loses its relay flag when replayed from the outbox

**A queued family relay loses its relay flag when it is replayed from the outbox.**

The 2026-10-14 fix exempts a verbatim relay from the owner's gate by passing
`verbatim_relay=True` from `_process_inbound`'s fan-out. The family chat's
durable outbox (`data/family_chat.db`, table `outbox`) has no column for that
intent, so a relay that was queued — the fan-out time budget ran out, or the
first attempt hit a network error — is replayed by `flush_outbox` through
`send_and_record` without the flag, and can then be refused by the owner gate.
It can no longer deactivate anyone (`OwnerLegDeferred` is caught in
`flush_outbox` and only fails that row), so the harm is one delayed family
message, not a silent removal.

What would fix it properly: either a `verbatim_relay` column on `outbox` (a safe
`ALTER TABLE ... ADD COLUMN`, matching the two migrations already in
`app/family_chat/db.py:291-297`, plus a line in `_assert_column_order`), or make
`enqueue_outbox` refuse to queue an owner-bound relay at all, since a late relay
is noise anyway. The second is smaller and matches the "drop it, do not replay
it" choice already made for deferred sends.
