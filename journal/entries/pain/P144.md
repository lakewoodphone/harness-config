## P144 — What still rots: agent_questions, a badge that cannot go green, two live writers, reminders sent to a dashboard nobody renders

**P-new — `agent_questions` rots exactly the way `owner_message_queue` did, and the new policy does
not cover it.** 313 pending questions, oldest 134 days, none created since 19 July; the badge line
today ends "oldest question 134d old". Same disease, same fix: a horizon by kind, aged on the
observation clock, applied by the sweep that already runs every 120 s. Evidence: the kernel's own
`attention_debt` metrics on 2026-09-15. Fix: one more phase beside `_expire_stale_owner_messages`.
(Cross-ref P6 "Held messages and unanswered questions rot silently" -- the *message* half is now
fixed and applied; this entry is the *question* half.)

**P-new — the badge can be permanently red.** `attention_debt` goes CRITICAL whenever any
critical-urgency row is held, so 2 six-day-old Google security alerts keep the whole surface red
even though 11 of the 13 live items are ordinary. Severity should follow what is live *and fresh*,
not a count alone: a critical item inside 48 h is CRITICAL, an older one is HIGH and says its age.
Otherwise the badge trains its reader to ignore it, which is the exact failure the shelf-life work
just repaired one layer down. Fix: one severity function, replayable over the stored findings.

**P-new — two live writers in one repository.** A second session committed `5a6c3b66` at 14:02 today
while I held ~17 modified files that were not mine; earlier the same pattern cost a commit in this
repo ("a timeout once cost a commit") and in `harness-config` a concurrent writer hard-resets. What
worked today, and should become the rule: validate anchors, read `git diff` hunk headers to confirm
every hunk is mine, `git add` explicit pathspecs, never `-A`, and `git commit` immediately --
uncommitted work in a repo with a live writer is work waiting to be lost. `~/ceo-kernel` held 123
lines of unattributed uncommitted work for 21 hours; I committed it with attribution rather than
leave it to die. Fix: a short-lived-working-tree discipline, and this rule written where patches are
written.

**P-new — reminders are marked `sent` into a "dashboard" nothing has been shown to render.**
396 rows, 14 pending, 0 due; the recent ones carry `delivery_channel='dashboard'` and
`status='sent'`. `process_due_reminders` marks a dashboard reminder delivered without sending
anything. If nothing renders that surface, the reminder feature reports success while reaching
nobody -- the same shape as the owner-message queue. Fix: find the reader, or make "delivered to
dashboard" mean something the owner can open.
