## P70 — The badge's pill sits in the bottom-right corner of the phone, where a composer control can be

## P70 — the attention badge's tap target occupies the corner a bottom-anchored control may need

**What hurts.** `#dsh-attention-badge` is `position: fixed; left: 16px; right: 16px; bottom: 14px;
z-index: 2147483000`, and its `.pill` child carries `pointer-events: auto` (the container is
`pointer-events: none`). Its box on a 393px-wide phone is `x=189 w=188 h=36` at the bottom-right,
ending 16px from the right edge and 14px from the bottom.

**Measured consequence.** In the question-card reproduction, a tap aimed at the card's Submit button
landed on the badge's `.count` span instead — the badge was the reason a control that was inside the
viewport could not be clicked. The same corner is where a control sits when the composer is anchored
to the bottom of the screen.

**What is NOT true.** It is not currently covering the composer in a real session: measured
2026-09-14 at 393x852 and 393x700, the composer's Send button sits at y=470 / y=394 and the badge at
y=802 / y=650, with zero vertical overlap, and a tap at Send's centre lands on Send. So this is a
risk in a corner, not a live defect — and it should not be "fixed" by moving the badge without
knowing what every phone layout does in that corner.

**Already mitigated for the one control that must never be unreachable.** `assets/question-card.css`
gives the question card `z-index: 2147483600`, above the badge, so a question is always answerable.

**What would settle it.** A check that, for each phone state, asks which element a tap at each
visible control's centre reaches — the same `document.elementFromPoint` test that found this.
`scripts/phone-layout-check.py` and `scripts/question-card-check.py` already do that for their own
surfaces; the composer half does not yet assert taps.

**Do not touch this casually.** The badge was audited and hardened on 2026-09-14 by another session
(**H78**, 130 + 55 checks, with `scripts/verify-badge.js`). Any change to its geometry has to keep
those passing.
