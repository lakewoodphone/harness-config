**3,728 of 26,291 rows (14%) have a title that is not a title** - the heuristic is
`title like '%"actions"%' or title like '"I''ll%' or title like '"I need%'`.

It is not historical. By month:

Producer is the department layer, not the orchestrator:

 dept_scheduling 249 | dept_comms_relationships 245 | dept_business_ops 154

 reliability_verifier 70 | proposal_evaluator 65

All of them are `status='done'` (1,681 by the narrower heuristic that includes `"I need`). So the
company diligently worked and closed 1,681 tasks whose titles were model chatter - roughly a
thousand sessions of real budget spent processing its own malformed output.

This is the read-only conclusion. **What I did NOT establish: the exact line that stores it.** The
strongest lead is a `create_task` action whose `args` were never parsed as JSON - the title field
receives the raw reply text beginning with agent prose. `app/assistant_chat.py` parses model replies
with `json.loads` in at least six places (lines 1097, 1425, 1773, 1819, 1994, 2059) and a
`json.JSONDecodeError` fallback in one of those paths would produce exactly this shape. Next action:
grep those six call sites for which one falls back to the raw string on parse failure.

Cost of leaving it: 14% of the task table is noise, every task query over-counts, and the boss's
task list can show him machine chatter as work items.
