## P2800 — The journal has two lineages - H3109 is invisible from this tree

WHAT HURTS: the harness-config journal does not have one lineage. H3109 and its siblings — the entries
written by the 2026-09-30 gaming/lab session — live on `hk/20260928-journal-utf8-complete`. This tree
is checked out on `agent/capability-registry-20260930`, and its journal is 2757 entries with no
knowledge of H3109. `journal.py show H3109` here answers "no entry H3109". `journal.py status` prints
a last-session handoff of H3061 from 2026-09-29. So the single organ built to make me continuous
silently answers "I do not remember" to a question about work done twelve hours ago — the exact
blindness the v2 rebuild on 2026-09-14 existed to end.

MEASURED 2026-10-01 on ZABZ-YOGA: 133 commits on `origin/hk/20260928-journal-utf8-complete` that are
not in HEAD; 39 commits in HEAD that are not on that branch; **578 files differ** between the two.
This is not a formatting drift, it is two journals.

WHAT WOULD FIX IT, in order:
1. Reunify deliberately, on a machine that is idle, with a worktree to spare — not ad hoc, and not at
 1 AM on the box the owner is about to use.
2. Then stop it happening again: `journal.py append` should refuse to write, or at least warn loudly,
 when the branch it is appending on is not the branch that the last N entries were written on. A
 journal that fragments per-branch is not a journal.
3. Long term: the journal should not live on a feature branch at all. It is shared state; leaving it
 on whichever branch a piece of work happens to sit on guarantees this recurs.

WHY IT IS NOT FIXED YET: a 578-file merge with 39 commits of unreconciled local work is a real task
with real conflict risk, and tonight's priority is the owner playing. Recorded so it is picked up
deliberately rather than discovered again.
