## P214 — 185.112.144.0/22 (1984 Hosting shared range) is blackholed from the T-Mobile home line

**185.112.144.0/22 (1984 Hosting's shared range, Iceland) is silently dropped from the T-Mobile home line on ZABZ-YOGA.** Every port times out; the whole /22 and 1984hosting.com are dead, while 1984.is (same hoster, 93.95.226.16) answers 200 8/8 and google/cloudflare are perfect. The office line, Hetzner, the mac-mini and 8/8 check-host.net nodes all reach it, and the working paths cross the same Zayo routers the home trace dies on.

Not fixable from our side: an external peering/filtering fault between T-Mobile and the site's upstream. Worked around by tunnelling only rattlebyte.com through the office server (see the 2026-09-16 ZABZ-YOGA handoff).

**What would fix it properly:** the block clearing upstream, or an ISP-side ticket. Only worth doing if it starts affecting other destinations - right now it is one vendor's prefix and the workaround is invisible.
