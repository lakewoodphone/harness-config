## P76 — 2026-09-14 - P50 explained (test pollution, 37 rows, dormant) and P57 re-measured as still live

**P50 explained: the non-status values in `work_sessions.status` are test pollution from old runs, not a live writer**

P50 says "`work_sessions.status` holds 40 non-status values, and nobody noticed — *something* writes into
this column without going through `update_session`, which is the same class of defect as the completion
lie". Measured 2026-09-14:

- **12 distinct values**, down from 40: `paused` (47,084), `completed` (26,682), `failed` (1,463),
 `expired` (21), and then `5` (21), `2.5` (11), `4.5` (2), `3.67`, `4.43`, `4.56`, `4.81`, `4.9`.
- **Every numeric row is a test artefact.** Row 12796–12803 have `title` of `"5"` or `"1"`, `started_at`
 of `"0"`, `updated_at` of `""`, `progress_pct` NULL and `steps_completed` of `1.8`/`1.4` — floats in an
 integer column. Their ids are ~12,800 against today's ~75,400, so they are old.
- The counters are large because the **four real statuses are correct and dominant**; the 37 garbage rows
 are 0.05% of the table.

**So the inference in P50 — "something writes without going through `update_work_session`" — is half
right and half wrong.** Something did write 37 rows with a float in `status` and a float in
`steps_completed`, and it was a test run pointed at the production database rather than a temp one. It is
not a live writer: nothing has appended to that set recently.

**What that leaves open, named rather than assumed.** The *guard* does not exist. A test suite that
writes to `settings.database_url` without overriding it can pollute the production tables again, and
nothing would notice — which is the actual defect P50 is pointing at, and it is the same shape as every
other finding this session: **the mechanism is silent, so pollution and health look identical.** I have
not deleted the rows (destructive, and they are the only evidence of the class) and I have not written a
guard, so P50 stays open with the cause identified and the residue quantified.
