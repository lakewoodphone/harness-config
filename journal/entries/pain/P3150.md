## P3150 — parts_inventory can only be appended to or have its quantity changed - so every correction becomes a zero, a duplicate or a stale name

The shop's parts table can only ever be appended to or have its quantity changed, so every correction it needs turns into a zero, a duplicate or a lie.

Measured 2026-10-12, while correcting four rows the bin catalog had created wrongly. `app/services/shop_ops.py` exposes exactly four routes: `POST /api/v1/shop/parts` (add), `GET` (list), `PATCH /api/v1/shop/parts/quantity` (quantity and min_quantity only), and `POST /api/v1/shop/parts/link` (link to a repair, decrementing). There is **no delete and no field update** - not the name, not the part number, not the notes, not the category.

What that cost in one afternoon:

- **A row that named a saleable Sony ZV-1F** could not be relabelled as a broken parts unit; it was set to quantity 0 and a second row was posted. A reader now sees two rows for one object, and only their names say which is which.
- **A row naming a bare "iPhone 13 mini display assembly"** - which had the value of a screen, several times the handset - is now a zero-quantity row whose *name still says display assembly*. Nothing stops the next person from reading it as a part we hold; the number 0 is the only signal and it is easy to miss in a list.
- **The QIN handset** the owner said to get rid of leaves a row behind for ever (`QIN AI Life F21 Pro kosher-variant keypad phone`, qty 0). There is no way to say "was in stock, disposed of, do not re-list".
- Notes are the one place provenance lives ("from bin photo IMG_1136", "identification is medium; not confirmed by hand") and they are **write-once**. So a row's note ages into a claim nobody can correct - the exact "stale reading believed because it is written down" failure this estate keeps paying for.
- The parts unit I added by hand could not be given the manufacturer part number, because `plan()` dedups on part number first and would have folded it into the saleable type; so its part number is blank and it is invisible to the export's dedup (see L3699).

Fix direction: a `PATCH /api/v1/shop/parts/{id}` accepting the same field whitelist as `website_db` already enforces (`ORDER_WRITABLE_FIELDS` is the pattern to copy), an append-only `notes` action so provenance can be added without rewriting, and a `status` field - `active | consumed | disposed | returned` - so "we no longer hold this" is a fact rather than a quantity of zero. Until then, a zero is doing three different jobs: never stocked, consumed, and duplicate-record.
