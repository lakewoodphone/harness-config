## P2538 — Two of four mesh children died on the ssh transport at ~15 minutes with NOTHING pushed - install-heavy fleets need early pushes and a per-node cap of two

Measured 2026-09-23, ~20 minutes into the first fleet I dispatched this seat.

Four mesh children were dispatched in one message for the LPT staff-console rework (shell, work orders,
customers, settings), all four cut from the same `origin/test`. The broker placed **all four on
`zabz-tech`**. Two (`subagent-3` work orders, `subagent-5` settings) died at roughly the same wall-clock
point with:

 exit 255 and produced no final message; stderr tail: Read from remote host
 zabz-tech.tail93e6e6.ts.net: Connection timed out / client_loop: send disconnect: Connection reset

Subagent-3 was mid-reasoning, about to run its `pnpm install`. **Neither had pushed anything**: no
`lpt/workorders-simplify-20260923`, no `lpt/settings-20260923` on the remote. Both slices are total losses
and must be re-run.

WHAT IT COSTS: two of four workstreams lost, ~15 minutes of fleet time each, and the re-coordination.
WHAT IT IS NOT: a code failure. The other two children kept running on the same node, so the machine was
alive; the transport was what broke.

TWO FIXES, both mine to apply in the next briefs:
1. **Push early, not at the end.** A brief must say: commit and push your branch at the FIRST coherent
 increment, then again when finished. A child that pushes once at the end has everything riding on one
 ssh channel, and today's failure mode loses the whole slice - including work that was already correct.
2. **Cap concurrency per node at two for install-heavy workstreams.** Four simultaneous `pnpm install` +
 vitest runs on one desktop is what the node looks like when sshd itself starts missing keepalives.
 The mesh broker decides placement; the manager decides how many exist at once. Two at a time, re-dispatch
 as slots free, rather than four and a coin flip.

NOT YET EXPLAINED, and not to be papered over: whether the transport died because the node was starved or
for an unrelated network reason. The evidence is the two failure lines and two survivors, both on the same
node - consistent with starvation, not proof of it.
