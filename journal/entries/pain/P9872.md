## P9872 — The reset anchor is never armed on the adb seal route, so a factory reset frees every phone the shop seals

ResetProtectionAnchorStore.arm is called at AdminReceiver.kt:97 inside onProfileProvisioningComplete only. The shop seals with dpm set-device-owner (counter_install.ps1), which does not deliver that broadcast - the app's own comment at AdminReceiver.kt:31-33 says so - and instead fires ACTION_DEVICE_OWNER_CHANGED and onEnabled, both of which call applySealPolicy and neither of which arms the anchor. PolicyApplier.kt:575-579 then finds no anchor, falls back to the empty strings.xml:735 frp_google_account, and sets no FRP policy; the app logs 'FRP policy not set: no reset-protection anchor was armed at setup'. dpm set-device-owner also requires no accounts on the device, so there is no generic FRP either. Consequence: every phone the shop seals today can be factory-reset and set up again by anyone. D-20261021-05 has no implementation on the only route in production. Verified by reading the source on 2026-10-22, not from a lane report. Fix: arm the anchor on the seal path from a supplied account (installer payload or the household's chaver account from the broker) and refuse-and-say-so when none is supplied.
