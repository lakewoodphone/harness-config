## P253 — MacBook repairs have no shop knowledge base and no parts-pricing path - every one costs a fresh research pass

WHAT HURTS: this is the first MacBook display case since the A1932 backlight job, and nothing reusable exists for
it. grep for A2338 / EMC 3578 across lpt-hub returns NOTHING; personal-secretary-mvp/memories/repo has no MacBook
entries at all; the only MacBook-adjacent research (A2337 display assemblies, A1932 no-backlight) is one model
over and a year old. So every MacBook costs a fresh research pass on the same six things: model/EMC identity,
ranked failure modes, whether the part is a bare panel or a COMPLETE TOP LID, the supplier row, the pairing /
True Tone caveat, and the discriminator sequence - all of which ARE cached for phone repairs.
Compounding it: the laptop parts path is built around bare LCD panels for Windows laptops. A MacBook needs a
complete top lid, and the price swings by COLOUR and by used-genuine vs aftermarket, which the current workflow
has no field for. On this case the colour alone was not even captured at intake and decides $5 of cost.
WHAT WOULD FIX IT: one page per chassis family under research/hardware/apple/ (A2338, A2337, A1932, A1706/A1708)
holding exactly those six fields with the date each price was read. Then the next MacBook is a lookup, not a
research pass. The A2338 entry already exists in draft inside this case's diagnostic log and can be lifted
straight out: hardware-diagnostics/macbook-pro-13-a2338-goldstein-diagnostic-2026-09-17.md.
