## P150 — Three of the nine pre-existing test failures are one root cause: .env.example is missing 24 declared settings

**P-new — three of the nine pre-existing test failures are one thing: `.env.example` is missing 24 declared settings.**

Measured 2026-09-15. `tests/test_config_contract.py` fails three tests and they share a single
cause -- `report["backend_env"]["missing_keys"]` has 24 entries, and the profile tests fail on the
drift warning `"Root .env.example is missing 24 keys"`. The keys:

 COMPUTER_FORM_WEBHOOK_SECRET, HA_PTT_KEY, SECURITY_ALERT_EXTRA_RECIPIENTS, SECURITY_API_KEY,
 SHELLY_PLUG_HOST,
 SHABBAT_AUTOMATION_ENABLED, SHABBAT_CAMERA_ENTITIES, SHABBAT_CAMERA_SERVICE,
 SHABBAT_CANDLE_OFFSET_MIN, SHABBAT_DOOR_LOCKDOWN_AUTOMATIONS, SHABBAT_GRACEFUL_SHUTDOWN,
 SHABBAT_INTERIOR_LOCK_ENTITY, SHABBAT_KEEP_HA_RUNNING, SHABBAT_LOOKAHEAD_DAYS,
 SHABBAT_OFF_LEAD_SEC, SHABBAT_ON_AFTER_OFFSET_MIN, SHABBAT_ON_LEAD_SEC,
 SHABBAT_POWER_DOWN_ENABLED, SHABBAT_POWER_DOWN_HOLIDAYS, SHABBAT_PROGRAM_TIME,
 SHABBAT_RELOCK_GRACE_SEC, SHABBAT_SHUTDOWN_PAUSE_SEC, SHABBAT_TIMEZONE,
 SHABBAT_TZEIT_OFFSET_MIN

Why it matters beyond the test: a fresh deployment following `.env.example` would not know 24
settings exist, sixteen of them the Shabbos automation that turns the shop's power and locks on and
off. The contract test is the only thing that has noticed.

Reproduction (11 s, no full-suite run needed):

 .venv/bin/python -m pytest tests/test_config_contract.py -q

Two things must be read before writing the fix, which is why it is not done in this round: how the
contract's parser counts a key (does a commented `# KEY=` line satisfy `missing_keys`, or must it be
an assignment?), and whether the *defaults* for the `SHABBAT_*` booleans trip
`test_effective_config_summary_reports_safe_baseline_profile`'s `high_risk_note_count == 0`. Both
are in `app/config_contract.py`; guessing either would produce a green test and a lie in the
template.
