## P244 — An inbound customer enquiry can sit unanswered for a day while every scan reports success

A real customer enquiry sat unanswered for 19 hours while the shop was actively texting other customers - and every automated scan that should have caught it reported success.

MEASURED 2026-09-17 from the authority (`dialpad_sms_cache`, `dialpad_call_full`, read 15:27-15:35 UTC):
- Levi, **7325754823**, new customer (0 prior cases in the lpt-hub case index; 0 rows in `contacts`).
- Contacted **8 times**: 3 inbound calls 2026-09-16 16:44-16:45 UTC (voicemail left) and 5 inbound texts
 between 16:47 UTC 09-16 and 11:46 UTC 09-17. Same one-line question every time: does the shop fix old
 Sony Video8 / Hi8 players with tape-deck and playback issues.
- **Inbound 5, outbound 0 in that thread** (`ptf-thread-7325754823` 4 rows, `4981260336799744` 1 row - all
 inbound). The outbound side of the table is NOT blind: `dialpad_sms_cache` holds **66,183 outbound rows**,
 newest 2026-09-17 15:04 UTC, and the shop texted **other** customers at 11:46, 14:14 and 15:04 UTC on the
 very morning Levi was re-sending. So "0 outbound" is real absence, not a harvest gap.
- `seed_ops_inbox` ("Daily scan for new work orders and incoming requests") reports **last_run_status
 success** at 2026-09-17 08:20:51 UTC. H399 fixed the *storage* of unmatched inbound (website previously
 `continue`d and dropped them silently); **surfacing them to a human is a separate, still-open job.**

WHY THIS CLASS KEEPS HAPPENING: the failure is a job that reports success while producing no human-visible
alert. It is the same shape as the store-and-forward "ok" H399 found, and as the earlier `ps_health`-style
green readings that meant nothing. The system could see the message; nobody was told.

WHAT WOULD FIX IT: an explicit, measurable inbound-enquiry SLA - a new inbound customer SMS with no outbound
reply in the same thread after N hours becomes an interrupt, not a digest line. Measure it as a number
(oldest unanswered inbound, per shop hour) so it is falsifiable, and let it go red. Naming the alert channel
is the work; the detection data already exists in `dialpad_sms_cache`.

RELATED: H399 (the storage fix), P1 (owner attention spent on things that are not his - this is the mirror
image: customer attention that never reached anyone).
