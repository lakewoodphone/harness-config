## P3203 — The Waze filter has two contradictory states — the repo's and the running one — and nothing compares them

## P · The Waze device's filter has two states — the repo's and the running one — and nothing notices when they diverge

WHAT HURTS, measured 2026-10-14. `deploy/waze-mdm/dns/doh_filter.py` and `docker-compose.yml` describe one filter;
the container on `waze-mdm-01` runs another. The live one blocks no broad Google roots and allows three Apple hosts
the repo blocks. Both are "the Waze filter", no test compares them, and reasoning from the repo produced a wrong
first diagnosis of a real customer-visible defect (Waze voice input) in this very session.

WHY IT MATTERS BEYOND THIS BUG: the filter is the product. A prospective buyer's question and a parent's question are
both "what does it block?", and today the honest answer depends on which of two contradictory documents you read.
Nobody has been lied to, but nothing in the estate can currently answer that question with one number.

WHAT WOULD FIX IT: a scheduled comparison that reads both sides and alarms on the difference — the container's
`ALLOWED_DOMAINS`/`BLOCKED_DOMAINS` against the repo defaults — with the live side as the authority and the repo
brought to match it, plus the dated reason for each deliberate difference. The pieces already exist: the DoH filter
is one container with two env vars, and `tests/test_waze_dns_filter.py` already exists to assert the repo half.

SECOND, SMALLER PAIN IN THE SAME PLACE: `allowMicrophone=false` has been in every shipped profile since
2026-06-14 asserting a microphone block that Apple provides no key for. Fixing it is a one-line profile change;
*noticing* it required downloading Apple's schema. A quarterly "every restriction key in our profiles exists in
Apple's published schema" check would have caught it in June.

EVIDENCE: `deploy/waze-mdm/docs/voice-input/evidence/live-dns-filter-20261014.md`; `journal` L3822.
