## P58 — 56 of 57 pain entries carry no proof of being finished, so the open list cannot be trusted as a to-do

**Symptom.** `state/open-pain.md` lists **56 of 57** entries as open, so the "what should I work on" view is
honest but useless: it cannot distinguish a live problem from one fixed two days ago.

**Evidence.** Searching every pain entry's text for a completion word finds one in only 19 of 57; a
line-initial `DONE`/`FIXED`/`RESOLVED` in **1**. Status was never a field — for example P2 says "DONE
2026-09-11 (later session)" in a paragraph, and P3's fix shipped while its entry stayed open.

**Cost.** A future session either re-derives what is open by reading 1,067 lines of prose (the exact tax the
rebuild removed), or trusts a list that is 98% noise and stops reading it — which is how the previous
occupant accumulated 55 unapplied proposals.

**Fix.** `state/status.tsv` is append-only and `journal.py resolve <id> --status done --why "..."` writes to
it, so the log stays immutable and the status becomes a dated fact. Closing these 56 requires evidence per
entry, so the work is bounded and mechanical: the ones with a recorded fix (P2, P3, P7, P10-retracted) first,
and a `resolve` only where a measurement exists.
