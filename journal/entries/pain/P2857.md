## P2857 — No out-of-band path to the office LAN - the shop can only be fixed by walking in

**What hurts.** There is no out-of-band path to the office LAN. When the four machines that share
the shop's upstream device go down together, nothing I can run from home can reach the shop, can
power anything, or can even see inside it — and the company (18 agents, ~300 ticks/day, the
authoritative database) is stopped until a human walks in.

**Ranked by what is actually lost.** Every hour a customer message, a call, an email and a
scheduled job goes unanswered with no one triaging it; the owner's phone is the only channel and
he is the only one who can use it.

**Why each existing candidate does not cover it (measured 2026-10-06, H3250).**
- **Wake-on-LAN from home** — impossible. Magic packets are L2 broadcast and do not route; the only
 hosts on the shop's segment are the ones that are down.
- **The office Home Assistant host** — alive and on the shop LAN, i.e. exactly the hands that would
 fix this, but its tunnel exposes only `ha.abletelsolutions.com` behind Cloudflare Access, and
 neither the service token in `.env` nor the account token can get through. It is a sensor I can
 see the heartbeat of and not a hand I can move.
- **Cloudflare tunnel** — outbound from the down machine; provides nothing when the machine is off.
- **A smart plug** — the shop's one Shelly (192.168.50.103) powers HA and the cameras, not the
 machines, and it needs the office internet to reach its cloud; it cannot fix a dead switch.

**What would fix it, in cost order.**
1. **Tailscale on the HA host** (free, ~10 min, needs one physical visit) — turns the shop's one
 always-on box into a real mesh peer: then I have a shell on the shop LAN from anywhere, can ping
 the machines, and can send WoL. This is the single cheapest change that would have made tonight
 a five-minute fix.
2. **A second, un-gated tunnel ingress on the HA host** (free) — e.g. a `/wake` route, or a scoped
 service token added to that Access app's policy, so HA is usable without an interactive login.
3. **UPS on the router + switch + machines** (~100-150 USD) — covers a blip; does nothing for an
 ISP outage or a dead switch.
4. **Second WAN (LTE failover)** — covers an ISP outage only, and the owner's T-Mobile gateway
 question (queue #207) suggests hardware may already exist.

**Note.** None of 1-4 can be done while the shop is unreachable; all of them need one visit that
also fixes tonight. They should be done in the same visit, not after the next outage.
