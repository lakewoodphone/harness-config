## P2857 — Remote subagent provider is unreachable from ZABZ-YOGA when Tailscale is down - no fan-out possible

2026-10-06 09:23 ET: two background subagent calls both failed with 'remote-ssh: broker-unreachable: the placement broker could not be reached at secratary-ts:http://localhost:3091/place - ssh exited 255: ssh: connect to host secratary.tail93e6e6.ts.net port 22: Connection timed out'. A direct 'ssh secratary-ts' from the same laptop reproduced it at the same moment, so it is Tailscale/network, not the broker. Cost: any fleet or fan-out is silently impossible until the tunnel is back, and a session that does not check will grind serially instead. Fix: check reachability before planning a fleet and say so in one line; consider a local fallback path that is labelled LOCAL rather than silently absent.
