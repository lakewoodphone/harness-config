## P326 — The ask-a-question tool does not render on the owner's phone — plain text instead, until it is verified on his handset

## P · The ask-a-question tool does not render on the owner's phone, so it is unusable for the exact thing
it exists to do — and I used it four times in one session before he stopped me.

**His words, 2026-09-18:** *"Don't use the ask a question tool, you still haven't resolved and made it work
on this phone, I can't read it normally — just ask the question plain text."*

That is decisive, and it should have been known before the first use. The tool presents a question with
labelled options and a recommendation — which is **precisely** the "one question, options, one
recommended" format he asked for on 2026-09-11. If it renders badly on the phone, the single most
important communication primitive in this seat is broken end to end, and no amount of correct reasoning
upstream of it matters. He read nothing.

**What happened this session:** four consecutive `ask_user_question` calls. He never selected an option.
Instead he typed free-text replies into it ("What is transmission fluid?", "Would the oil change place do
it also", "don't use this tool"), which means the question and its options were not legible to him —
he could only reply blind.

**Two things to fix, and they are different:**
1. **The tool's UI on a phone** — whatever the harness renders on mobile. Until that is verified on his
 actual handset, the tool must not be used with him. **Plain text, question last, options as numbered
 lines, recommendation marked.** That is the standing format.
2. **The default.** A tool that is right in principle and broken in delivery should default to OFF for
 this owner, not be trusted because it is the theoretically correct channel.

**Fix:** verify the rendered output on `iphone-15-pro` (on Tailscale as `100.85.105.93`), or stop offering
the tool. Either way, the plain-text fallback is not a workaround — it is the requirement.

**Evidence:** four `ask_user_question` calls in the 2026-09-18 car session, all answered with free text
instead of a selection; his message quoted above; the 2026-09-11 standing instruction that owner questions
be one at a time with options and one recommendation.
