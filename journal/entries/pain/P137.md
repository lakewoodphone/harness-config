## P137 — A session believed it closed task #25118 while the task stayed open, then died at the budget

PAIN: a session can believe it closed its task while the task stays open, then dies at the budget

EVIDENCE, session 75413 on task #25118 "Check CI/CD pipeline status and open PRs"
(secratary, 2026-09-14). The log reads:

 Step 1: [ACTIONS: 3 (github_list_prs, github_list_prs, github_list_prs)]
 Step 2: [ACTIONS: 4 (github_list_prs x3, github_list_issues)]
 Step 3: [ACTIONS: 4 (github_list_issues, github_list_prs x3)]
 Step 4: "I've gathered enough from the CI/CD scan. Let me take a mutating action:
 record the findings and close the task with a concrete summary."
 [ACTIONS: 1 (update_task)]
 Step 5: "I've completed the CI/CD scan... Let me record the findings and close the task."
 [ACTIONS: 1 (update_task)]
 -> [STEP_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED: reached planned 5/5 work steps without [WORK_DONE]]

The task row afterwards: status=open, progress_pct=99, completed_at=None. So the two
update_task calls did not set a terminal status, while the session's own narration
believed they had closed it. The task was then re-claimed (75412 failed 5/5, 75413
failed 5/5 on the same task) and the loop is burning the full budget each time.

WHAT I DO NOT KNOW AND MUST NOT GUESS: why the calls were inert. The tick notes that
would carry each action's outcome ("Work action update_task: ...") are not persisted
anywhere I could find -- proactive_actions is passed around in memory and I could not
locate a table holding it (proactive_events, tick_activity, tick_telemetry do not
match on 'Work action'). So the parameters the model sent are unknown. Two candidate
explanations, neither verified: it called update_task without a usable task_id, or
with a status outside the terminal set ('done'/'completed'/'closed').

WHY THIS IS PROBABLY ALREADY MITIGATED: steps 4 and 5 were mutating, so the budget
extension deployed the same night (a23cb985) should extend this exact session shape by
3 more steps and give it room to finish the close. That is the first thing to check in
the live extension evidence before adding anything else. Do NOT stack another fix here
until the extension's effect on this shape is observed.

IF IT RECURS AFTER THE EXTENSION: the cheap diagnostic is to persist the rendered
[TOOL RESULTS] block into output_log (or a debug column) for update_task calls only,
which would show the parameters and the failure reason. That costs a bounded log size
and ends the guessing.
