## P334 — The Shabbat automation has only two modes, unsafe and inert, so it silently defaulted to off and stayed there for a week

## P· — The Shabbat automation has only two modes, *unsafe* and *inert*, so it silently defaulted to off and stayed there for a week — through a Shabbos, with Yom Kippur next

**Symptom.** The owner's Shabbat/Yom Tov power automation — "only the cams and ha get shut off" (his words, owner-confirmed 2026-09-06, Option C) — has not run since Rosh Hashana 2026-09-11/13. It did not run for the Shabbos of 2026-09-18/19 either: HA and all five cameras ran straight through. Nothing errored, nothing logged, and nothing said so.

**Why it is a *design* pain and not a bug report.** There is no operating point between dangerous and dead:
- **Cut the plug** = HA + the Dahua (all 5 cams) lose power. This is the owner's spec and it is the *only* thing on this install that stops the cameras — no HA switch stops a camera (only per-camera `motion_detection` / `disarming` / `event_notifications` toggles exist, and none of them stop recording or streaming). It is also the mode that released the interior maglock for 91 ms and locked the household out of the control room on 2026-09-11.
- **`keep_ha_running`** = the mitigation. It leaves HA powered, so the door stays openable — and its camera path is `SHABBAT_CAMERA_ENTITIES`, which is **empty**, because the only way to stop these cameras is the plug. So the safe mode is not a mode: it does nothing at all.
So the system is forced to choose between a cut that has already locked people out once and a no-op. It chose the no-op, turned the master flag off, and stopped being visible.

**The silent half.** `SHABBAT_AUTOMATION_ENABLED=false` makes both scheduler jobs `return` on their first line. There is no heartbeat, no "not armed" line, no dashboard row. A dormant safety automation and a working one produce the identical log. The owner only found out by asking — twice: once on 2026-09-18 14:05 EDT, when a session investigated and asked whether to arm it, and got no answer, so Shabbos ran uncovered; and again 2026-09-20 02:1xZ.

**What would fix it.** (1) Arm-or-report: one scheduled line a day that says either "armed for the next window, OFF at T, ON at T" or "NOT ARMED — and here is why", so a dormant holy-day automation is a visible state rather than an absence. (2) Resolve the mode question for real: either the Dahua gets its own switch (then `keep_ha_running` becomes a true mode) or the design accepts the cut with the door gate as its only protection. (3) The wiring assumption under all of it — HA and the Dahua on the *same* plug — is documented as *planned* and has never been verified; at ~27.5 W flat day and night, with no IR step, that is genuinely in doubt.

**Evidence.** See handoff H604 for every read; `Schedule.List` -> `{"jobs":[]}`; power history one gap in ten days (Rosh Hashana, 50.86 h) and none at all across 2026-09-18/19; `SHABBAT_AUTOMATION_ENABLED=false` in `/proc/3232884/environ`.
