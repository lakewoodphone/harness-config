## THE META-COMPLAINT, AND IT IS THE MOST IMPORTANT LINE HE WROTE

> *"Why is it that I have to keep pointing them out so many times? And also, I shouldn't have to point
> them out at all with your walkthrough should discover all these problems by yourself. You're more
> capable than this. Why do you keep making me find everything?"*

**He is right.** On 2026-10-13 the persona walk reported **0 blockers / 20 majors** while the live
screen had all eight of the above. The walker was built to assert that things *render* — it counts
controls, measures boxes and takes screenshots — and it has **no concept of a screen being wrong**.
It never:

- checks that a control **does anything** when pressed (the dead tax checkbox),
- checks that a value and a placeholder **agree** (100 vs 180),
- checks that a number is **shown once** rather than twice under two names (Awaiting parts / Waiting on parts),
- checks that **nothing is clipped or overlapped** (the table under the side panel),
- checks whether a **search finds a record that exists**,
- checks whether a **settable-looking field is editable** (device type),
- treats **developer text in the UI** as a defect (an env var name shown to a shop owner).

**Every one of those is a checkable property, and none of them is checked.** Adding them is worth more
than any single fix below, because it is the difference between him finding the next eight and the
walk finding them.
