## P2549 — The Shabbat power-down hard-cuts Home Assistant - homeassistant.shutdown returns HTTP 400 every run

# The Shabbat power-down hard-cuts Home Assistant, and has for two weeks

**What hurts.** `_power_down_critical` asks HA to shut down gracefully and is refused **every time**:

```
WARNING app.services.home_assistant: Graceful HA shutdown failed: 400 Client Error: Bad Request
 for url: http://homeassistant.local:8123/api/services/homeassistant/shutdown
```

HA 2025.10.3, `POST /api/services/homeassistant/shutdown`. The orchestrator treats it as
non-blocking and cuts the plug 10 s later, so the sequence *works* — but "graceful" is a lie: the
recorder database, the Zigbee/MQTT stack and the timeline tooling are all power-cut cold, every
Shabbat and Yom Tov. It has been in the docs as a known wrinkle since 2026-09-11 (that doc's words)
and nobody has looked at why.

**Why it matters beyond tidiness.** Every other failure in this chain is gated and aborts; this one
silently degrades the only step meant to protect HA's own state. It also hides a second question:
is the 400 the service name, the auth scope, or the `/api/services` path changing shape — and would
the *right* call actually stop the unit before the plug cuts (HAOS shutdown needs the supervisor, and
a 10 s pause may be far too short)?

**What would fix it.** Read HA's own log for the 400 at the moment of the next cut (Sun 2026-09-27
20:23 EDT), then either call the correct service (`homeassistant.stop` vs the supervisor's
host-shutdown endpoint), or make the graceful step **verifiable**: poll HA until it stops answering,
bounded, instead of sleeping 10 s and cutting. A cut that cannot be proven graceful should say so in
the report, not in a WARNING nobody reads.

**How it was skipped (so it is not skipped again).** 2026-09-25: found while making the owner's
timing change; deliberately left alone because it is not the timing change and the machine was
minutes from Yom Tov — recorded here rather than fixed in a hurry.
