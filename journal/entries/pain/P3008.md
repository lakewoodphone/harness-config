## P3008 — The owner's phone app terminates on the owner's laptop; the authority is one step short

**The pain.** The owner's phone app — the link on his iPhone home screen — terminates on an **ephemeral
Windows laptop** (ZABZ-YOGA), while the always-on authority sits one step short of being able to host it.

**Measured 2026-10-11 15:38–15:47Z:**
- `ai.abletelsolutions.com/phone` → `302` → `zabz-yoga-1.tail93e6e6.ts.net` → the laptop's `phone-gate.py`
 (pid 9128, `127.0.0.1:3086`) → DSH engine `node` (pid 10668, `127.0.0.1:3099`). Confirmed by the gate's
 own log and by `tailscale serve status` on the laptop.
- `/etc/cloudflared/phone-redirector.env` on the authority carries
 `PHONE_TAILNET=zabz-yoga-1.tail93e6e6.ts.net` — an override that forces another node. `phone-redirector.py`
 would otherwise name **its own** node (`tailscale status --json` → `Self.DNSName`).
- The authority's own tailnet door answers nothing (`tailscale serve status` = `No serve config`; no
 listener on 443), and its `/opt/dsh-phone` engine is installed (373 MB, `@deepseek-ai/dsh` 0.2.0-rc.2)
 but not running: no listener on 3089, no systemd unit. It was created 15:35–15:37Z, i.e. mid-audit.
- `phone-redirector.env` was changed at **15:33:49Z** and a `.before-target-fix` backup left beside it, so
 the migration target was reverted to the laptop about an hour before this audit looked.

**Why it hurts, not just why it is untidy.** A phone app that depends on a laptop fails *silently from the
public side*: hop 1 still returns `302 Found` when the laptop is asleep or away, so every status-only check
reports the surface healthy while the owner's icon opens nothing. This is the same defect class as the miss
that commissioned this audit. Nothing fires when the laptop is closed; the owner simply finds his app dead.

**What would fix it (exact, and already half-built).**
1. Finish `/opt/dsh-phone` on `secratary-cloud` and start the engine on `127.0.0.1:3089`, under a systemd
 unit (`phone-engine.service`) so it survives a reboot. The disk is fine: 24 G free of 75 G.
2. `tailscale serve --bg --https=443 http://127.0.0.1:3089` on `secratary-cloud` — this is the piece that
 makes the authority's tailnet name answer at all.
3. Point `PHONE_TAILNET` at the authority's own name (`secratary-cloud.tail93e6e6.ts.net`) or delete the
 line entirely so the redirector self-names; `systemctl restart phone-redirector`.
4. Remove the stale 124128-byte `phone-gate.py` from `/usr/local/bin` on the authority — the current gate is
 the 4745-byte `scripts/phone-gate.py` in the repo.
5. A monitor, because the failure is otherwise invisible: assert from outside that following
 `ai.abletelsolutions.com/phone` ends at **HTTP 200 whose body contains the phone layer**, not at a 302.

**Secondary pain, same audit:** `dialer.abletelsolutions.com` answers `502` because nothing on the office
box listens on 8899 and no dialer unit exists — the program is not installed anywhere I could find. That is
an owner question (customer-facing voice), recorded in the owner queue rather than fixed here.
