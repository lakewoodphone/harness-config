## P6314 — Telnyx arrears killed the fleet's SIMs for a week and nothing escalated it

## 2026-10-13 · ZABZ-TECH · The Waze fleet's SIM lifecycle is broken at the money end, and nobody looks

**P — two years of the fleet's cellular connectivity depends on an account nobody watches, and it has
been dead for a week without generating a single alarm anyone acted on.**

Measured 2026-10-13 19:15 UTC through the fleet's own billing endpoint: the Telnyx account is at
**-0.01 USD** with a **0.00 credit limit**, and **both SIMs in the fleet are `blocked`** with the
reason string Telnyx itself gives: *"The SIM card has been blocked due to lack of funds."*

The fleet's own cost ledger shows when it stopped being able to happen: `fleet_telnyx_cost_ledger`
recorded **2 active SIMs and an estimated 400 cents/month on 2026-10-01 through 2026-10-06**, then
**0 active / 2 blocked from 2026-10-07 onward** — so the SIMs have been dead for a week. The ledger
kept recording the truth every night at 04:30 UTC. Nothing raised it to the owner. The devices it
disables are the ones the shop sells.

**What this costs:** five units are onboarded and cannot take a SIM, because provisioning needs
funds and the account has none. The fix is small and is the owner's, because it is money — a
top-up. **What is not the owner's, and is the actual defect: a nightly ledger that records
`blocked_sims: 2` and never escalates it.** A monitor whose output nothing reads is not a monitor.

**Second, smaller, and the same shape:** the fleet control plane's `fleet_devices.state` reported
`deployed` for all five new units while wallpapers were `NO` and no profile had been installed.
A state label that reaches its happy value without the work being done is indistinguishable from a
working device to anything that reads it.

**Third:** the Telnyx portal login was down (503 on `magic_link_requests` and `gsi_login`) while the
API was fine — so the one path the owner has to fix the money problem himself was the path that
broke, and the machine path that could have told him kept working and saying nothing.
