## P2478 — The quote-SMS path re-sent the same two customer texts ~50 times in 17 hours (2026-09-20) and nothing in the estate noticed

The LPT quote/status SMS path sent the SAME TWO CUSTOMER TEXTS ~50 TIMES IN 17 HOURS and nothing in
the estate noticed. Measured 2026-09-24 in dialpad_sms_cache (read 22:19Z):

- to +1 845-659-8807 (a number that has NEVER had one inbound message - see lesson L2525):
- 2026-09-20 03:17Z to 20:09Z: the "We looked into your Microsoft screen..." (310 chars) and
 "We found the issue with your Dell..." (148 chars) texts, re-sent in pairs every ~30 minutes.
- message ids ptf-comm-132629 / 132636 through ptf-comm-137140 / 137147 - distinct ids, distinct
 created_at, so these are 50 separate SENDS, not one message harvested 50 times.
- 72 outbound rows on that number between 2026-09-08 and 2026-09-20 in total.

Why it matters beyond the noise: (a) the customer saw nothing (wrong number), and had the number been
right this is 50 texts to a real customer; (b) the loop stopped by itself on 2026-09-20 20:09Z and has
not fired since, so whatever retried was never observed, caught or fixed - and it will fire again on
the next quote.

NOT YET DONE (this is the pain): find the sender. The ids are `ptf-comm-NNNN` (phone-and-tech-full
origin). Searched phone-and-tech-full *.py, lpt-hub for "Want us to go ahead" / "ptf-comm" - no hit,
so the composer is elsewhere (candidate: a standing work order or a CEO tick that re-sends until a
quote is accepted, with no idempotency key and no per-conversation send cap).

WHAT WOULD FIX IT: (1) locate the composer; (2) make the send idempotent per (case, message body /
quote id) - a re-send of an identical body inside a window must be a no-op; (3) a per-conversation
send cap that refuses and LOGS rather than silently queueing; (4) a monitor that alerts when any
single phone gets >3 identical outbound bodies in an hour. (4) alone would have caught this in 90
minutes. Nothing in the estate currently looks at outbound message volume per contact.
