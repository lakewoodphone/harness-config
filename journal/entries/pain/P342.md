## P342 — P339 - secratary is I/O-starved: io full 95.6 percent, and it breaks mesh placement with a false broker-unreachable

## The authority is I/O-starved, not CPU-starved, and it silently breaks mesh placement

Measured 2026-09-20 15:00-15:15Z on secratary (4 cores, 23.4 GB RAM):

 /proc/pressure/io some avg10=99.77 full avg10=95.60
 load average 22.07 (4 cores), 576 MiB free, 1.8 GiB swapped
 ssh to secratary:22 timed out on 4 of 9 attempts

`io full = 95.6%` means 95.6% of the time EVERY runnable task was stalled on disk. It is not high
load; it is a box that cannot make progress. Causes, all measured via `ps -eo` + `/proc/<pid>/io`:

1. `/tmp/oq-mkcopy.py` running `VACUUM INTO` of the live 9.8 GB `secretary.db` to make a copy for
 testing `~/bin/owner-queue.py`. It had written 7.7 GB over 11 minutes.
2. `~/.fsearch/commsindex.py` re-indexing the same 9.8 GB database (494 MB read, D-state).
3. `secretary-backup.service` - 24 min wall, 5m28s CPU, 6.3 GB memory peak, every 6 h.

**Consequence to remember: the mesh provider's placement calls reach the broker THROUGH
secratary:22. When the authority is saturated, `subagent` fails with `broker-unreachable` - an error
text that is FALSE. The broker was healthy the whole time (GET / -> 200, POST /place -> 200 in
0.24 s, pid 3077721, up 1d18h). The casualty was the transport hop, and the message blamed the
broker.** Two mesh dispatches were lost to this and misdiagnosed as "mesh down" before I queried
the broker directly on its own host.

How to tell the difference: `ssh secratary-ts "curl -s 127.0.0.1:3091/"` and
`journalctl -u mesh-broker -n 15`. If the broker answers, the mesh is fine and the real failure is
the laptop-to-authority SSH hop.

Fix applied this session: killed nothing (the VACUUM finished on its own), removed the 9.1 GB stale
copy, and installed two systemd units. Result: `io full` 95.6% -> 1.89%, load 19 -> 4.8,
`/health` 200 in 7 ms.
