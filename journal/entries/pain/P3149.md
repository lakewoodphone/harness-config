## P3149 — The daily reconciliation can mark a job finished with no evidence any work happened - 64 orders carry it

## P — The daily reconciliation can mark a job finished with no evidence that any work happened

**What hurts.** A status is a claim about the world. The automated reconciliation pass is allowed to
write one to production, and it has no way to check whether the claim is true.

**Measured 2026-10-12** against the `operational` Postgres (the live store):

 select to_jsonb(h)->>'toStatus', count(*) from order_status_history h
 where h.reason like 'Auto-updated by secretary reconciliation%' group by 1

 READY_FOR_PICKUP 64 | AWAITING_PARTS 34 | UNFIXABLE 9 | CANCELLED 9
 AWAITING_PAYMENT 6 | AWAITING_APPROVAL 6 | AWAITING_DIAGNOSIS 3

**The mechanism.** `app/services/daily_reconciliation.py` proposes a change; the write goes through
`app/services/website_db.py::update_order`, which hardcodes the history reason
`f"Auto-updated by secretary reconciliation (confidence: {confidence})"` (website_db.py:772) and
accepts any status the caller hands it. Nothing between the model's proposal and the UPDATE asks
whether the status is supported.

**The concrete harm, one case.** Order 2880 / `WO26091512715` (Shlesinger, Lenovo ThinkPad X201,
flickering screen) was moved `AWAITING_DIAGNOSIS -> READY_FOR_PICKUP` on 2026-09-18 11:00:55 at
confidence 0.9. No bench diagnosis had been run, no part ordered, no money charged, and the customer
had never been told anything — he had been quoted about 110 by phone on 2026-09-15 and had said he
would get back to us. The ticket then read as finished business for **24 days**. Any staff member
opening it would conclude the customer simply had not collected the machine. Corrected by hand
2026-10-12 (production note 3150, status `AWAITING_APPROVAL`).

**What would fix it.** Either (a) a guard at the writer: refuse a transition to a handover or
terminal status (`READY_FOR_PICKUP`) unless the order carries evidence of completed work — a note on
the order (`customer_notes."workOrderId"` is set), or a non-zero `orders.total`, or a `SUCCEEDED`
row in `payment_transactions`; or (b) upstream and stricter: the reconciliation is never permitted
to set a handover status at all, and may only propose diagnostic/intermediate ones. (b) is the
better shape — the honest fix is that an automation which cannot see the bench must not be able to
assert that the bench is finished. Its failure mode is that a genuinely completed job would then
need one human status change, which is the correct cost.

**Do not treat "0 groups with a guard mismatch" as "the guard is working."** Same trap, different
field: see tools/case-index/DOUBLES.md.

Files: `app/services/website_db.py:759-774`, `app/services/daily_reconciliation.py`.
