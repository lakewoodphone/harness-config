## P3929 — Nothing monitors whether an LPT device can actually reach the internet

## P — Nothing monitors whether an LPT device can actually reach the internet

**What hurts.** On 2026-10-14 the LPT Waze fleet was found to have **never** had working cellular
data, and no signal anywhere said so. Every indicator we have was green:

- Telnyx: SIM `enabled`, "Ready to connect to networks and consume data", account funded,
 `live_data_session: connected`.
- MDM: device `deployed`, queue depth 0, `push_ok` 1, checking in every 6 minutes.
- The device's own profiles: the correct APN (`data00.telnyx`) installed.

All of it true, on a phone with no data. Six weeks. It surfaced because the owner drove with a
device, and again on 2026-10-14 because he held three phones and photographed one.

**Two causes, and only one of them is now fixed.** Data Roaming is now settable from the fleet
(route deployed, D4129). The second — a SIM pinned by hand to AT&T (310-410), a network that is
not one of the five IMSIs on the Telnyx profile and on which **no LPT SIM has ever carried
payload** — cannot be set from here at all: Network Selection is not an Apple MDM `Settings`
item. It is a finger, on each device.

**What would fix it, in order of cost.**
1. **A real "has a data bearer" check.** The cheapest honest one: alert when a SIM's
 `current_billing_period_consumed_data` is flat over N days *while the device is checking in
 with the MDM*. Both halves are already available — Telnyx per SIM, and MDM `last_seen` — and
 neither is joined to the other today. Nothing else in this estate can catch this class.
2. **Onboarding cannot ship a device without it.** The device-side step (§2 Step 8) must be part
 of the prep checklist, and the fleet route called in the same pass, so a device that leaves
 the bench has roaming ON and the network on Automatic.
3. **Assert the network, not just the SIM.** `current_mnc` should be an expected value in
 whatever check exists. 410 on this fleet means "attached where it cannot work".

**Guard against the wrong fix.** Do not answer this by adding another dashboard field. Three
separate green indicators already existed and all three were about the SIM, not about the
payload. The measurement has to be *bytes moved*.
