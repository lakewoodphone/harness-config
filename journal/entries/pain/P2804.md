## P2804 — A claim is a pool operation, not a named target: my own selftest marked a real work subject done

ROUND 5, 2026-09-30. I broke a rule the secretary-wake skill already documents, in a different store. In round 3 I verified the retryable classifier by running wake.py claim --by retryable-selftest, expecting it to claim my own test subject. A claim takes the NEXT ELIGIBLE ROW, not the one you name: it claimed #158 project:housekeeping:20260930#3, a REAL work subject, and my next two lines finished it as done. A genuine subject was closed with a selftest outcome and the work was never done; the script also left two selftest subjects live for the dispatcher to spend releases on. Likely cost: one housekeeping release slot plus up to two wasted releases. CORRECTED within the hour: #158 restored to new with an outcome line naming the error and the reason; both selftest subjects closed as failed with a deliberately non-transient outcome so the classifier would not refund them; verified 0 claimable selftest subjects and 0 real subjects sitting done by my hand. FIX FOR THE MECHANISM ITSELF: verify a claim or finish path against a COPY of the store, or claim by something that can only see your own row - never against a live queue that other actors are drawing from. And any script that writes a production store cleans up after itself, or it manufactures the junk work this system exists to prevent.
