## P247 — One repair job, two case files and two sync records - only one carrying the idempotency guard, so a push fires a second order; no detector exists

**Duplicate case-file + sync-record pairs for ONE job can fire a duplicate paid order, and nothing detects them.**

**What hurts.** The production sync tool keys its idempotency guard on
eferences.websiteOrder inside a sync record. When one job has two records and only one carries the guard, the unguarded one reads as a new job: the tool's answer is CREATE, and a customer who already accepted a price gets a second order. D194 (2026-09-15) named this exact hazard and the reconciliation that day did not clear it - on 2026-09-17 the same job was still duplicated, and the correct record was the unguarded one.

**How common.** Not rare, and systemic rather than one bad session: each case is written by whichever session touches it, under whatever slug it picks, so the same repair accumulates device-customer-phone-date.md, device-customer-phone-date-the-same.md, and a -sourcing- variant. Measured 2026-09-17 on the real corpus: **3 of 186 case files have no front-matter at all**, one of those three belongs to a job that also has a *conforming* case file, and there is at least one further pair (shlesinger-thinkpad-x201-screen-flicker-2026-09-15.json with ...-2026-09-15-2026-09-15.json). The real count is unknown - which is the point.

**Why the existing checker does not cover it.** lptcase.py check has a duplicate-phone class and it is the wrong signal: 18 groups, most of them one customer with several genuinely different jobs (Chaim Margulies has 4). Grouping by phone either floods with false positives or, as happened here, misses the pair entirely - the duplicate case file has no front-matter, so it has no phone to group on.

**What fixes it.** A detector that keys on the JOB (phone + device/MTM + dates + the caseId/sourcePath cross-references between records and case files), reports a reason and a confidence per group, distinguishes "same customer, different jobs" from "same job twice", and refuses to merge anything. In flight 2026-09-17: subagent building a read-only ools/case-index/doubles.py on branch doubles-detector in its own worktree.

**What must not be the fix.** Deleting the duplicate. The duplicate is the only place some facts live (in this case the production link lived only on the *wrong* record) - the fix is copying the truth into the surviving record, then retiring the other with a pointer. Deleting either one is how a link or a customer's money record vanishes.
