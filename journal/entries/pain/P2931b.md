## P2931b — The unattended kosher-filter loop is 1 shift/day; the product only moves in fleet bursts

## The unattended kosher-filter build loop runs at 1 shift/day, and the product only moves in fleet bursts

**Symptom, measured on the authority 2026-10-09** (`~/bin/kaf-loop-status.py`):

| day | shifts | minutes | commits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-02 | 12 | 190.2 | 27 |
| 2026-10-05 | 12 | 141.0 | 51 |
| 2026-10-06 | 6 | 123.6 | 1 |
| 2026-10-07 | 1 | 36.9 | 3 |
| 2026-10-08 | 1 | 18.0 | 1 |
| 2026-10-09 | 1 | 27.2 | 0 |

The 12-shift days are the days a **live session with the owner open** ran a fleet (kf1002, kf1005 —
the commits are authored `zabz@local` / `ezabz@abletelsolutions.com`). The 1-shift days are the
unattended keepalive alone, and they produce 0–3 commits. So the honest shape is: **the daily
automatic builder is a trickle; the product advances when a session is open.**

**Consequence for the owner.** He asked "is it getting fully built out for android and iOS", and the
unattended answer is: the loop spends its one shift a day and, until 2026-10-09, it spent it on
Windows and integration work. Android had 3 attempts in 14 days; iOS had none ever. `origin/main`
did not move between 2026-10-05 17:50 and 2026-10-09 05:21.

**What is already fixed (2026-10-09).** The queue now leads with the eight Android/iOS items; the
never-satisfiable `seed_eng_filter_ai_daily_push` is retired; `origin/main` moved to `2d9585d`.

**What still hurts.** One 20–30 minute shift per UTC day is not a build-out rate for a project with
39 open items (40 todo, 5 blocked) across two phone platforms. With the queue fixed the loop will
now spend that shift correctly, but a phone release still needs sustained sessions. The lever the
owner's own rule already names is the fleet: fan out the Android and iOS items across worktree-
isolated subagents and integrate serially, which is what produced the 27- and 51-commit days.

**What would fix it.** Scheduled fleet windows: a bounded, governed fan-out over the phone-platform
ledger items on the machines that hold the toolchains (Android SDK on ZABZ-YOGA `C:\Android`, .NET
and the Windows services on ZABZ-TECH, Xcode on the Macs), with the same integration discipline
used on 2026-10-05.
