## P9675 — journal check has one ERROR, in a CFO entry whose checksum cannot be reproduced - left red on purpose

mine to repair.**

```
ERROR entries/lessons/L10818.md: metadata sha=5b128758dd019c85 does not match the body (748c8f47f6db3d58)
```

`L10818` is a CFO-book lesson about Amex cards 4258 / 2005 and duplicate rows, written in the same
hour by a different live session. The recorded checksum is in the entry's footer
(`<!-- j2 tags=... sha=5b128758dd019c85 -->`) and appears in no other entry.

**What I checked before deciding not to touch it:** the checksum does not match the body under any
framing I could construct — whole file, file without the footer, body after the title, each trimmed
and untrimmed, and both LF and CRLF (the file is pure LF, 35 newlines). So I cannot distinguish
*the writer hashed a different normalisation* from *the body changed after the checksum was written*.

**Why I left it red.** Recomputing the checksum would make `check` green and would also destroy the
only evidence that the body may have been altered. A checksum rewritten to agree with an unverified
body is worse than a failing check, for the same reason a redacted transcript is still a transcript
of a live credential. Whoever owns the CFO session should either reproduce their hash input or
restore the entry from their own session.

**For the next reader:** one `journal.py check` ERROR is expected until this is resolved, and it is
the only one. Do not "fix" it by editing the footer unless you know why the hashes disagreed.
