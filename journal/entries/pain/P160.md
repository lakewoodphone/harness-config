WHAT WOULD FIX IT
1. Rotate or delete the AWS key pair and disable the idle phone-tech-dev-user after taking a full IAM inventory. Owner's yes needed: deleting a key is irreversible and it is his cloud account.
2. Rotate both Twilio auth tokens after finding and updating every consumer (secratary env, lpt-apps, Home Assistant, Dialpad bridges); Outworq's needs Shimon, which makes it an owner conversation.
3. Strip the secrets from the working trees, add the patterns to .gitignore, and add a pre-commit/git-grep gate so a new one cannot be committed. History rewrite is NOT the fix and is not proposed: rotation is.
4. One secret store for the fleet (this is the durable fix; the repos cannot hold production secrets on 6 machines).
