## P9876 — harness-config is split across two lineages - Windows commits cannot be pulled by the authority or the laptop

harness-config has at least two git lineages and they are not supersets, so harness-config work done on
Windows cannot be pulled by the authority or the laptop.

MEASURED 2026-10-22 while installing fetchkit:
 C:\Users\ezabz\Code\harness-config (ZABZ-TECH) remote secretary-ts:/home/zabz/harness-config.git
 - a bare repo on the FROZEN office box. My commits 17fd462e, 76d64a28, 6da58f84, 38a59519 land here.
 secratary-cloud:/home/zabz/harness-config remote journalpub -> github.com/lakewoodphone/harness-config
 secratary-cloud:/home/zabz/code/harness-config remote journalpub -> same github repo
 - both at 49a34ad4, a journal-autocommit lineage, and NEITHER has my commits (git cat-file -e fails).
 laptop-ts (ZABZ-YOGA) remote secratary-cloud:/home/zabz/code/harness-config
 - i.e. it pulls from the github-side checkout, so it is behind too.

Consequences, so the next session does not mistake copies for sync:
 * `git pull` on the authority does NOT get Windows-side harness-config work, and says "Already up to date".
 * fetchkit is therefore INSTALLED on all four hosts by copying the file, not by pulling it. Those copies
 will be replaced the moment the lineages unify - which is correct, but it means "installed" is not yet
 "tracked" everywhere.
 * This is the P3164 shape: two mains that are not supersets. Do NOT merge them by guessing, and do not
 force-push either side - the authority's checkout is what its running configuration is read from.

WHY IT MATTERS BEYOND fetchkit: every harness-config change made on a Windows box this estate believes it
has made is invisible to the two hosts that do the work. That is a silent divergence in the layer that
holds the rules.

NEEDS: one decision from the owner on which repo is canonical for harness-config, then a recorded merge.
Until then, a change is only really deployed when it is copied AND the copy is noted as a copy.
