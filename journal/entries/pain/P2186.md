## P2186 — Nothing can watch a time-boxed owner-health risk overnight - wake.py has no not-before, tasks fire on tick

**P328 · 2026-09-21 · Nothing can watch a time-boxed owner-health risk overnight.**

WHAT HURTS: A risk with a hard deadline — a 25-hour fast starting at 18:39, a medication course, an observation window — is exactly the case where an agent's good advice is worth least, because the failure happens while no session is open. Two mechanisms exist and **neither can be scheduled**: `wake.py flag` is released by the dispatcher as soon as it is filed (no "not before" field), and the reminder/task system fires when a tick happens to run, not at a time. So the one thing a health risk needs — *start a session at 20:00 and check* — is the one thing the tooling cannot express.

MEASURED CONSEQUENCE: 2026-09-20 the risk was identified and documented (P resolved to advice only). On 2026-09-21 the owner met the red flag on his own list — laboured breathing, dizziness, severe dehydration — and nobody knew until 75 minutes after the fast ended, when he happened to type an update. His wife is the reason it ended well.

WHAT WOULD FIX IT: a `--not-before <ISO>` (or `--at`) field on `wake.py flag` so a flag can be parked until its moment, honoured by the dispatcher and the dedup/cooldown logic; plus the standing habit (recorded as L2186) of creating the dated check in the same breath as the advice. Verify by filing a flag 10 minutes out and observing that it is NOT released before then.
