## P6347 — dedupe pairs entries that are not the same entry, and MOVES the loser: it retired 14 live bodies on the landed union

**`dedupe`'s "same entry" judgement is not the no-loss identity, and it MOVES the file it judges.**

Measured 2026-10-16 on the landed union, `/root/code/harness-config/journal`.

**1. It pairs entries that are demonstrably different.** `journal.py dedupe` printed:

 L6270 == L3764 (same entry: body) — keeping L3764, moving entries/lessons/L6270.md

Read from the two files:

 L6270 **L6270 · A credential move is a permission change: two bugs a probe caught that would
 have failed the first real deploy** (2026-10-16, secratary-cloud)
 L3764 **L3764 · journal.py append with an inline --body can fail silently; use --body-file**
 (2026-10-12, ZABZ-YOGA)

Different entries, written four days apart by different hosts, on entirely different subjects.

**2. Its predicate disagrees with the acceptance gate's on the same pair.** For `P2561bc`, which
dedupe called `== P2561b`:

 archived P2561bc : gate body_sig 97a6007f7310021c journal entry_hash 1f807751f6a013ee
 live P2561b : gate body_sig 43b43d514eaccb28

Two different bodies by the gate's own definition, which is the definition the whole union is
verified against. `L3767` shows the same shape (`410e3b82` archived vs `cf974802` live).

**3. The consequence is data-loss-shaped, and it is not theoretical.** `dedupe` does not decide —
it MOVES the loser into `archive/duplicates/`, out of the live view. On the landed tree it moved 34
files; the cross-tree gate then went from `NO BODY LOST` to **14 bodies missing**, every one of them
live in at least one source tree and live in no entry afterwards. Running
`r7-restore-archived.py --from-sources <trees>` put them back and the gate returned to
`BODIES MISSING 0`. **This is also the mechanism behind the round-6 seven**: those were bodies
dedupe had retired, and round 6 patched the symptom with a hardcoded list of ids.

**RULE, until dedupe's predicate is fixed:** `dedupe` is NOT the inverse of the no-loss gate.
Never let it be the last step over a union. Run `r7-restore-archived.py --from-sources <trees>`
AFTER it, then re-run the gate, and treat a `check` of 0 errors as saying nothing about this —
`check` is intra-tree and `dedupe`'s own report is the only place the pairing is visible.

**CHECK** (0 = no source-live body has been retired out of the live view):

 python3 ~/code/harness-config/scripts/evolution/journal-union/r7-verify-body-loss.py \
 --trees /root/work/r8/trees --merged /root/code/harness-config/journal --normalise-ids \
 | tail -3
 # expect: BODIES MISSING from merged : 0 / VERDICT: NO BODY LOST
