## P165 — The family chat DB silently loses outbound rows, and nothing notices because the chat still works

WHAT HURTS
Twilio is the only complete record of the family group chat. The hub's own
data/family_chat.db held 3 inbound rows in the month to 2026-09-15 (1 Sep, 1 on
6 Sep, 1 on 15 Sep) while Twilio shows 2, 1 and 19 messages on those days. On
2026-09-15 specifically, Twilio shows 19 sends between 15:48 and 15:53 and the DB
contains exactly one row (id 99576, the 15:50:45 send). chip_last_reply_at in the
DB is still 2026-09-04 (1788536881) even though Chip sent messages on 09-09 and
09-15.

The chat keeps working because sending and recording are separate, so a record
that never lands is invisible: no error, no retry, no alarm. The same shape as
the body-swap bug -- the system looked healthy while its memory was wrong.

WHAT WOULD FIX IT
1. Reconcile the last N hours of the family hub between Twilio's message list and
 the messages table, the way the Dialpad and text harvesters already reconcile.
 Report drift (count and ids) rather than silently accepting it.
2. Make every send path write via one function that cannot skip the record.
 _notify_admins already calls twilio_client.send_message directly with no
 db.log_message -- a known unlogged path and the first place to look.
3. Alert when the newest row in messages is older than the newest Twilio message
 by more than a few minutes.

RANK: after the body-swap fix. Not customer-facing, but it is the reason a
"why did Chip say that" investigation cannot be answered from our own data.
