## P147 — eBay, Amazon and Cloudflare-walled parts shops are unfetchable from this harness; only plain-HTML shops can be priced

Sourcing an older laptop panel from this harness is close to half-blind, and it cost most of the time
on the Shlesinger X201 job (2026-09-15).

Measured, not guessed:
- `web_fetch https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw=LP121WX3` -> HTTP 403 ("Something went wrong on our end").
- `web_fetch https://www.amazon.com/s?k=LP121WX3` -> HTTP 503.
- `empowerlaptop.com/p/lcd-ltn121at07/` -> 403 Cloudflare "Just a moment...".
- `extension.thinkpads.com` forum threads -> 403 Cloudflare.
- blisscomputers.net product URLs found via search -> 404 (their slugs have changed), and their
 `search.php` page returns the header but the result list is cut off before the prices.
- laptopscreen.com search takes `?q=` but answers "Please enter at least 3 letters into search field";
 their model pages are the only reliable entry point.
- parts-people.com search DID work (plain HTML) and returned "0 Dell Parts" - useful, but Dell-only.

What worked: web_search snippets to find exact product page URLs, then fetching those pages directly.
That is how the $65.62 newlaptopscreen.com price for the X201 panel was obtained.

What would fix it: a browser-based fetch path for sourcing (the company already has browser_use
actions, and lpt-hub has scripts/procurement-assistant with Playwright). Failing that, expect every
eBay/Amazon price to come back as "unverified" and say so in the case file rather than guessing a
number. A guessed price in a customer quote is worse than an unpriced part.
