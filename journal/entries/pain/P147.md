## P147 — A counterparty reply that only exists on a vendor portal never enters the system, and the needs_response flag that would catch it is unread

**Symptom:** a counterparty's substantive reply sat unseen for three days and the work item was closed anyway.

**Cause:** the reply existed only in the vendor's support portal. Our pipeline is mail-shaped, and the mail never
came — osTicket addressed it to `info@lakewoodphoneandtech.com`, which is **not one of the four connected Gmail
accounts** (`lakewoodphoneandtech@`, `eliyahuzabrowsky@`, `ezabz68@`, `zabzgpt@`). So nothing ingested it, nothing
drafted against it, and task `24658` was closed on the notification instead.

**The cheap alarm that already exists and nobody reads:** `email_thread_state.needs_response = 1`.
Thread `1a094aafde65d7fb` has sat at `message_count=1, needs_response=1, status=triaged` since 2026-09-12T08:38:50Z.
A query over `needs_response = 1 AND last_message_at < now-24h` would have flagged it on 2026-09-13.

**What would fix it:**
1. A daily sweep over `email_thread_state` for `needs_response=1` older than 24h with no draft — surface or
 auto-draft. See the lesson entry from the same session for the full reasoning.
2. Connect `info@lakewoodphoneandtech.com`, or make it forward into an account that *is* connected. It is the
 address printed on vendor orders and on the customer-facing website, so it is not going away.
3. When a support flow is portal-based, say so in the case record. "The vendor never replied" and "the vendor
 replied into a portal" look identical from the mailbox and have opposite responses.
4. Do not let a task auto-create-and-auto-close around a single inbound notification. Closing needs evidence of
 resolution, not evidence of ingestion.

**Severity:** high for anything with a real counterparty waiting (vendor RMA, warranty, supplier dispute). Low for
newsletters. The blast radius is "we look like we ignored someone, or we lose a warranty window".
