## P2562 — A misfiled wake row cannot be withdrawn without writing the store by hand

WHAT HURTS. wake.py offers flag/add/claim/heartbeat/finish/reap/list/stats/pause/
resume/await(s) - and no way to withdraw a row that was filed by mistake. On
2026-09-28 I filed one by omitting SMS_INBOX_DB from a test command (see the
lesson entry), and the only way to remove it was a raw
`sqlite3 ~/.sms-inbox/inbox.db "delete from wake where subject=... and source=...
and prompt=..."`. That is the one write the store's design otherwise keeps out of
reach, and it had to be done under time pressure because the row would have been
claimed after 00:00Z and spent a release on nothing.

WHAT WOULD FIX IT. A `wake.py retract <subject-or-id> --why "..."` verb that
requires the row to be unclaimed (state='new', claimed_at IS NULL) and writes the
reason into the row (outcome/context) before marking it cancelled - so a
withdrawal is itself on the record instead of an unlogged DELETE. Cheap, and it
removes the temptation to reach for sqlite3.
