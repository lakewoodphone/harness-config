## P151 — Case sync records are only 'minimally validated', so out-of-schema keys live in production records unnoticed

Customer case sync records are validated "minimally" by the sync engine (app/services/lpt_hub_sync.py: "Parse and minimally validate a single sync record JSON payload"), while the published contract docs/customer-operations/CUSTOMER_CASE_SYNC_RECORD.schema.json declares `additionalProperties: false` on operational and workflow. Result: a live case record (Eli Green, 2026-09-09 → 2026-09-15) carried `operational.quoteStatus` and `operational.diagnosis`, which the published schema rejects, and no tool ever said a word.

What would fix it: have the sync/ingest path validate each record against the schema and fail (or warn loudly) on a violation, the same way journal.py check fails on a malformed entry. Until then, anyone writing these records has to remember the contract by hand — and the two keys I removed had already survived a week.

Cost when it bites: a consumer that does validate strictly rejects the record silently, and the case disappears from whatever feed the record was meant to populate. Cheap insurance: one schema check at write and ingest time.
