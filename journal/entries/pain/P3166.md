## P3166 — secratary-cloud is at 92% disk above a 26 GB database that is the company's only books

Measured 2026-10-13T14:5xZ: `/dev/sda1` 78,397,936 KB total, **6.29 GB free, 92% used**. The live
company database `/root/authority.db` is **26,157,563,904 bytes** and grows ~2.3 MB/h. Local snapshot
directories `/var/lib/authority-snapshots` (4.0K) and `/var/lib/authority-backup` (40K) are effectively
empty.

Top consumers under `/`: `/root` 42 GB (`/root/work` worktrees 4.9 GB, `git-rescue-20261012` 2.4 GB,
`repos` 1.9 GB, `finance-fleet-20261013` 1.3 GB), `/opt` 16.7 GB.

**Why this hurts:** this box is the sole writer of the company database. Filling the disk does not
degrade the company, it stops it. A 14-hour DNS outage on 2026-10-05 already produced 841 failed
writes to this store.

**What would fix it:** a measured, ordered reclaim - worktree reaping (`agent-fleet rm`/`rmall`
already exists), the `git-rescue-*` and `finance-fleet-*` directories after confirming they are
superseded, and VACUUM/archive ageing on the database itself. Then a hard floor: refuse heavy work
above 90% rather than discovering it after a job is killed. `disk-guard` runs every 15 min and has not
prevented this, so read what it is actually guarding before adding a second one.
