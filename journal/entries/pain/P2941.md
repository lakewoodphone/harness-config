## P2941 — The cloud authority has a run ledger and nothing that reads it

WHAT HURTS: On the cloud authority, nothing reads /root/.cron-status/runs.tsv. cron-wrap.sh writes a row per run (timestamp, name, rc, seconds, first 160 chars of stderr) and that ledger is the estate's entire "did it actually run" mechanism — but the guards that consumed it on the office box (family 10: cron-failures.py, guard-registry-check.py, cron-target-guard.py, pain-check.py) are another workstream's port and are not installed. So the first five families I just ported (owner channel, wake, asks) are scheduled, will run, and a job that fails every tick would be indistinguishable from a channel nobody uses. That is the exact defect cron-wrap.sh was written on 2026-09-30 to end.

WHAT WOULD FIX IT: port the family-10 guard pair (cron-script-guard: does every cron target still exist; cron-failures: has any job failed N runs in a row), point them at /root/.cron-status/runs.tsv and the authority crontab, and — per 132-CRON-INVENTORY.md §2 #32 — make cron-target-guard DECODE the base64 payloads, because the office version reported `ok / 0 missing` while two crontab targets were genuinely absent.

EVIDENCE: /root/.cron-status/runs.tsv existed and was EMPTY at 06:27Z; the crontab had no root crontab at all until 06:38Z; no consumer of the ledger exists anywhere on secratary-cloud (checked /usr/local/bin, /root/bin).
