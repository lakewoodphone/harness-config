## P2667 — Mesh subagent runs are rejected and their output discarded when the child omits the MESH-HOST marker

Measured 2026-09-29 on ZABZ-YOGA. Five subagent children were dispatched to the mesh (all placed on ZABZ-TECH, lease-granted, exit 0). Three reported normally; TWO (the boardwalk-bike-rules lane and the rail-trails-and-bike-rentals lane) settled as failed with diagnostic: 'the child did not begin its report with MESH-HOST: <hostname> - its location is unproven, so the run is not reported as complete'. job_output for both returns '(no new output)': the child's entire answer is DISCARDED, not merely flagged. Cost: two of five research lanes produced nothing after ~2.5 minutes of wall clock each, on a time-critical task. What would fix it: (a) put the literal instruction 'Begin your report with the line MESH-HOST: <hostname> as your first line' into every mesh brief, which the three that passed evidently received from their preset; and (b) make the harness PRESERVE and surface the child's text on an unproven-location failure instead of dropping it, since the content is usually still useful and only the provenance is in doubt.
