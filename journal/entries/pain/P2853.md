## P2853 — US Mobile reseller lines are recorded nowhere: no line-to-customer, no line-to-payment, so the shop cannot tell which line spend is recoverable

## P· The shop's reseller phone lines are recorded nowhere, so "who pays for this line" is unanswerable

**What hurts.** Lakewood Phone & Tech runs a US Mobile **reseller** account. Lines are activated,
ported, suspended and expired on it every month (mail trail: activations on 2026-07-24, 2026-08-14,
2026-08-18; expiries 2026-07-30 and 2026-08-24; SIM moves 2026-08-11; ~11 account-update mails on
2026-10-02). Yet there is **no record anywhere** that ties a line to a customer, to a plan, to a price
or to a payment. Measured 2026-10-05: the shop customer mirror (542 customers) matches exactly one of
the nine known line numbers — the owner's own old line; no Stripe line item, no incoming payment
memo'd for a line; no case file for the one customer who asked about his line.

**Cost.** The shop pays the carrier on card 3929 (55 charges, 957.60 since 2026-04-07) and cannot tell
which of that spend is recoverable. Cancelled customers keep being paid for. A customer asks about his
line 36 times over two weeks and nobody can even look it up.

**What would fix it.** One table of record — line number, carrier network, plan, monthly cost,
who it is for, who pays, since when, status — fed by the mail trail (the per-line notifications already
arrive and already name the line), reconciled monthly against the card charges, and surfaced as an
exception list: lines with no payer, lines with no customer, lines still billed after a cancel. First
step is the owner's portal read on 2026-10-06 (reminder 567, task 27681); that gives the line list the
rest hangs off, and the account login then belongs in the vault so no future read needs him.

**Related:** P1 (owner's attention spent on questions that are not his) — this is one of the few that
genuinely is his, because the portal is behind his passkey.
