## P9871 — I broke both shop store phones by sweeping by device_group instead of device_class

## 2026-10-22 17:14 UTC · I broke the two shop store phones by treating a device group as a device class

**What happened.** At 16:52:10 and 17:03:24 I pushed the `AllowFailover=false` DNS profile to DRN 2001 and DRN 2003. Both are `device_class = store_device` — the shop's own phones, not kiosks. That profile makes our DoH resolver the only one they have, and our resolver is default-deny over 78 names, so everything else — general browsing, app traffic, connectivity checks — stops resolving. The owner noticed within twenty minutes: "the LPT store phone and the iPad started saying no internet."

**The mistake, in one line.** I selected devices by `device_group='test'` and read "test" as "safe to change"; `device_class` said `store_device` the whole time and I never looked at it. A kiosk policy was applied to a general-purpose phone.

**Fixed.** Per-DRN variants `waze-siri-filter-dns-drn2001.mobileconfig` and `…-drn2003.mobileconfig` created with `AllowFailover=true` (the resolver prefers a per-DRN file, so the fleet default stays strict), pushed and **Acknowledged at 17:13:30 and 17:13:56**. Both store phones are back to their previous behaviour. DRN 2004 — a real kiosk — stays on `false`.

**Rules this adds.**
1. Never sweep by `device_group`. Sweep by `device_class` and by an explicit DRN list, and print both before sending.
2. `store_device` and `owner_personal` must never receive the kiosk DNS payload; they are general-purpose devices and the allowlist makes them look offline.
3. Before any fleet push, state what the device loses if the change is right: with `AllowFailover=false` a device loses every name not on the allowlist. If that is not the intent, the change is wrong.

**Not yet explained:** an iPad also showing "no internet". There is no iPad in `fleet_devices` (no iPad serial or product anywhere) and no command was sent to any non-fleet device tonight. It is either on a network that resolves through our filter by another route, or it is unrelated. Needs one fact from the owner to trace.
