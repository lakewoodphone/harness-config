## P2189 — P — The ask_user_question widget is unreadable on the owner's phone, so a rendered question is a dead end

## What hurts

On 2026-09-23 the owner replied to a rendered ask_user_question with: "I can't see the question when you use the answer a question tour on the phone." He works late from a phone as often as from the machine, and the widget gives him a question he cannot read and options he can only answer by typing blind - the exact opposite of "ask rarely, ask well, ask one at a time".

Cost: one full round trip lost per question, and the risk that a question silently goes unanswered and the turn stalls - which is the failure mode rule 1 exists to prevent.

## What would fix it

- A one-line rule in the operating instructions: **ask owner questions in plain chat text, never as a widget** (or: widget only when the session is a desktop GUI). Cheaper than any code change and it works on every machine.
- If the widget is kept, its question text + option labels must render as plain markdown in the transcript so a phone reader still sees them.
- Verify on the phone: open the GUI at the tailnet URL from a handset and confirm a rendered question is legible before trusting the widget for anything that matters.

## Where the fix belongs

~/code/harness-config (the preset/operating instructions shipped to every machine) - it is a harness-default behaviour, not a family-chat detail.
