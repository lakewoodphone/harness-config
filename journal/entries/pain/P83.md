## P83 — No automatic outage alert reaches the owner: SMS kill switch, GV dead, badge client unbuilt

Nothing automatically alerts the owner when a system breaks, which is why 2026-09-14 was discovered by him being blocked at his desk rather than by a message. Three separate causes, all measured tonight: (a) OWNER_SMS_KILL_SWITCH (mtime 2026-07-20) is still active and the company logged "owner SMS queue flush blocked by kill switch" at 21:32 today, so the SMS path is deliberately off; (b) Google Voice has delivered nothing since 2026-07-02 with 6,284 consecutive auth failures (631 today, both accounts status=failed, Chrome profile renamed account_0.broken-1789361480 today) and is already owner question #15; (c) the attention badge was chosen as the delivery channel in owner question #11 but its client half was never built (journal 2026-09.2.md, H55 area). Net effect: the failover watch can flip the fleet's model and the owner will not be told - he will only notice the model name changed.
WHAT WOULD FIX IT: the failover watch should surface its own moves (the chosen channel is the badge, so a flip must be visible there), and the standing P40/P55 question - findings with no consumer - needs one channel that is not the kill-switched SMS path. This is the difference between "the system heals itself" and "the system heals itself silently".
