## P4251 — journal.py dedupe retires a colliding body to archive/ instead of renumbering it - 7 live entries would leave the live view in a five-tree union

`journal.py dedupe` retires a colliding body to `archive/duplicates/<id>.md` instead of renumbering it to a fresh LIVE id, and its write has no collision-avoidance loop (unlike `repair-ids`, which the R7-JOURNAL-MERGE builder says does). Consequence, measured on the five-tree union candidate built 2026-10-15:

 H2729 live in the mac tree as "Daily note from LakewooechsMini" (344 chars)
 -> the candidate keeps YOGA's body live under H2729 and puts the mac body in archive/duplicates/
 L3767 live in cloud+yoga as "A whole-file comparison..." (168 chars)
 -> the candidate keeps a DIFFERENT yoga body live under L3767 and archives that one too
 same shape for H2781, H458, H514, P2561bc, H3401

Seven bodies in all. Nothing is deleted from disk, and `0 invented` holds -- but "the union keeps every body" and "the union keeps every body LIVE" are different sentences, and only the first is true. At switchover those seven would leave the live view: `list`, `search` and `status` would stop returning them.

The builder warned about this in its own report (its §4.2: a fixture where `dedupe --apply` took three copies of one id in and produced two, 630 bytes before and 420 after, one heading gone).

WHAT WOULD FIX IT: a renumber-to-live step for these cases, which is what `repair-ids` does and `dedupe` does not.
