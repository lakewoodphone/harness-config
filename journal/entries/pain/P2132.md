## P2132 — P2139 - the wake dispatcher is capped and its last three releases all died in the ssh hop

Measured 2026-09-20 ~18:37Z on the authority. The wake system is the half of this operation that works
with nobody at a keyboard, so its health matters more than any single defect fixed today.

**Liveness - and it is alive.** The skill is explicit that a dispatcher silent for hours "is not peace,
it is death", so this is the first thing to check:

 wake.py stats: {"new": 12, "claimed": 0, "done": 7, "failed": 4}
 released_today: 5 failed_today: 4
 caps: max_per_day 4, paused: false, in_session: false
 caps_in_force: ["daily_cap: 5/4 releases today"]
 wake-dispatch.log mtime: 18:35 (2 minutes old), "daily cap reached (5/4) - not releasing"
 every 5 minutes since 18:10

So the heartbeat is healthy and the dispatcher is running - **it is simply out of budget for today.**
That is the governor working as designed, not a fault. The cap resets at 00:00 UTC (~5.5 h away).

**The real defect is the failures.** `failed_today: 4`, and three of the four share one signature:

 id=8 mesh-offload-program:2026-09-18 "ssh hop failed (rc=1) after 843s"
 id=10 dshw-proxy-restart:2026-09-20 "ssh hop failed (rc=1) after 843s"
 id=20 selftest-hoplevel:2026-09-20 "ssh hop failed (rc=1) after 843s"

**843 seconds, three times, and the same number every time** - that is a fixed timeout being hit, not a
transient network blip. Each consumed one of the day's four release slots, which is why the cap is
exhausted: **three of today's five releases bought nothing.** The `attempts: 1` against `max_attempts: 2`
means each subject gets exactly one more try and then is muted for good.

**What this means for the overhaul.** The remaining objective items are measurement over hours (the
long-run rates) and a multi-session design fix (`lifecycle_sweep`). Both are exactly the kind of work
that should happen in a woken session while no human is present - and the wake path is currently buying
nothing three times out of five. This is now the highest-leverage defect in the operation, higher than
anything left on the company audit, because it gates the mechanism by which everything else continues.

**Also noted, and NOT acted on:** 12 flags in state `new` waiting, including `wake-cost-tuner:20260920`
and `dormant-archive:20260920`. They are not stuck - they are queued behind the cap. I did not touch
`wake.py`, `wake-dispatch.sh`, `run-wake-sources.sh` or cron, per the standing instruction in those
flags' own prompts.

**The 843 s number is the thread to pull.** It is suspiciously exact and suspiciously equal across three
different subjects, which points at the dispatcher's own ssh timeout rather than at any subject. The next
step is to run the dispatch hop in isolation and time it, not to re-file the flags.
