## The Shabbat automation's only route into the shop LAN is one workstation's `tailscale serve`

Measured 2026-10-22 22:16–22:25 UTC. The authority reaches the Shelly plug and Home Assistant
through **`zabz-tech.tail93e6e6.ts.net`**: `tailscale serve` on ZABZ-TECH forwards `:8080` to
`192.168.50.103:80` (the plug) and `:8123` to `192.168.50.34:8123` (Home Assistant). The app's own
env is `SHELLY_PLUG_HOST=zabz-tech.tail93e6e6.ts.net:8080`, `HA_URL=http://…:8123`.

That was the correct fix on 2026-10-18 — before it, the authority had no route at all and 1,835
consecutive "Shelly unreachable" warnings. But it makes a Windows workstation the single point of
failure for a safety automation:

- if ZABZ-TECH is powered off, rebooting, or its Tailscale service is down at 13:02 Friday, the
 door release, the light and the cut all fail. The interlock then refuses the cut (correctly) and
 the cameras stay on for Shabbat — the 2026-10-16/17 outcome, arriving by a different road;
- the motzei power-**on** is unaffected, because it is a device-side schedule on the plug itself.

What softens it tonight: ZABZ-TECH is up since 2026-10-15 19:49, no pending reboot, sleep-on-AC
disabled, Tailscale Automatic; the owner SMS alert on a failed action (2026-10-18); and a new
15-minute pre-flight cron on the authority that asks the question 3 h ahead and alerts once per
cut (commit `1c56b939f`).

What it still needs, and it is a real piece of work, not a tweak:

1. a **second gateway** on `zabz-tech-linux` (192.168.50.23, on the same LAN, tailnet present) with
 the app resolving the plug/HA through something that can fail over — an `ssh -L`/serve pair plus
 a host-chain setting, or a Tailscale **subnet router** advertising `192.168.50.0/24` from two
 nodes so the private addresses work directly and either node being up is enough;
2. the subnet-router route is the better end state: it removes the per-port serve config, lets the
 app use the plug's real address, and makes "the gateway" a thing with two carriers instead of
 one workstation.

Do not trade a proven path for an unproven one the day before a run: tonight's job was to verify
and to make failure loud, not to re-architect nineteen hours before the cut.

Also found, same area: `shabbat_interior_lock_entity` defaults to `lock.0x002446fffd0a4705` — the
Kwikset on the apartment door — while the code hardcodes `_MAGLOCK_ENTITY = "switch.smart_switch_l4"`
and nothing reads the setting. It misled this session for one test run. Dead config naming the
wrong door is worse than no config.
