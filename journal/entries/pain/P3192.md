## P3133 — The vendor mailbox has no scheduled reader - three recorded occurrences, and the third hid a box carrying three customer drives plus our own EVO

## P-new · pain · 2026-10-12 · open

## The vendor mailbox has no scheduled reader, so a lab answer is only as fresh as the last human who happened to open Gmail — three recorded occurrences now

**The cost, named.** On 2026-09-30 five 300DDR messages had sat unread for five days. On 2026-10-02 four more had
sat since the 09-30 sweep. On **2026-10-12, six more** had arrived between 2026-10-07 23:56Z and that afternoon
and **not one had reached a case file** — including:

- the shipping notice for **three customer drives in one box** (`#74115`, `#74114`, `#74048`), with a tracking
 number that existed nowhere in the repo, **and** the fact that **our own Samsung 870 EVO 2 TB (533.11) was in
 that box**;
- two separate *"confirm receipt so we can delete our backup"* notices, on `#73896` and `#73459`;
- the fact that the parcel had **not** arrived, which is what gates the whole Fishman conversation.

**Why it keeps happening.** The 10-07 board itself diagnosed it: *"the lab mailbox is read by no scheduled job"*
and prescribed *"file a lab message into the case that owns its ticket number in the same session it is read"* —
a rule that depends on a human reading Gmail, which is exactly the thing that is not happening. The 10-07 board's
own window closed at 13:00Z, so the entire 10-08 batch fell outside it and was invisible to every successor.
Three independent sweeps have now each found several days of unfiled vendor mail.

**The fix, in order of value.**
1. **A scheduled reader that files by ticket number, not by notification.** The ticket number is in the subject
 line of every one of these messages (`Update from 300DDR: Ticket #<n>`), and every ticket already maps to a
 case file. This is a join, not a judgement — it can run unattended and post a digest of "new vendor mail,
 filed here, anything asked of us". That is the whole defect.
2. **A standing check the agent runs at the top of any lab work**: sweep the vendor mailbox for everything since
 the last board's date, and treat "no messages" as a refusal until the account is named (see the lesson on
 `gmail_search` defaulting to another person's mailbox).
3. Failing both: the board's date must be a *read-through* time, not a snapshot time, and every board says which
 window it actually covers.

**Not a phone problem and not a spam problem.** The mail is triaged `personal` or `updates`, sits in the inbox,
and nothing escalates it. Gmail-side filters were tried and are not the lever; the missing thing is a reader.
