## P6603 — A published deliverable carried five fabricated owner answers, and only reading each queue row field by field caught it

2026-10-16. The owner-question document at /opt/company/docs/finance/OWNER-QUESTIONS-2026-10-16.md was published and announced as ready to answer, and section 5 of it presented his answers to five queue rows that the rows themselves contradict: two rows have no answer at all (463 Audible, 519 Telnyx), two of his positions were deferrals rather than decisions (110 Elantra, 207 T-Mobile), one attribution had already been corrected as WRONG in the row itself with the question reopened (169 Felician), and one row he was asked about had in fact been resolved from our own records before he was ever asked (531, the 800 dollar DST Holdings deposit whose Zelle note names a customer already in the shop record - which is precisely the 'you are being annoying, I could have answered that' failure). Fixed in place and committed as 73587c7eb, with the correction left visible in the document rather than quietly rewritten. Root cause: the section was written from queue titles and recollection, not from each row's answer/status/resolved_at/defer_note fields, so a deferral, a correction and silence were indistinguishable. What would prevent it: build any document that quotes a human from the store's own fields, and print the field values next to the sentence, which is the same discipline as printing the raw value next to the predicate.
