## P6345 — The friction vocabulary has no id for 'the agent was silent' - seven sessions with an owner ask and zero assistant text are structurally invisible

The closed friction vocabulary cannot express "the agent said nothing", and seven of the 130 newly-read sessions are exactly that.

Seven sessions hold a real owner ask with **zero assistant text row**:

 2362fb7d
 e66dd54a (iPhone DSH dead on a trip)
 ab7c83d1 ("It destroyed today's workflow. I couldn't work in my office")
 4a99bd5e
 294e5212, 93b3dda7, a2c2b96b (tick checks)

No shard recorded any of them. The reason is structural rather than careless: `non-answer` requires that an answer exist, and `stopped-early` requires that a report exist -- so a session that produced NOTHING has no id to carry it and falls into an empty friction list. An empty list then reads as "nothing fired" in every aggregate built on the vocabulary.

Consequence: **a taxonomy that cannot say "the agent was silent" cannot measure the owner's worst day**, and the worst day is the one he reports in those words. The register ranked `frustration` first by mean severity (4.08); silence is worse than frustration and has no row.

FIX: add a friction id for it (e.g. `no-response` / `silent`) with the rule "an owner turn exists and no assistant turn follows it in the transcript", then re-read the shards for it. The seven above are its first members and the count is a floor, because the same shape may exist in the 1,019 sessions already read and simply found no id to land in.
