## P2798 — The owner text channel: a second brain owns the send, three ceilings, and blocked obligations that never escalate

RANKED BY WHAT THE OWNER FEELS. Measured 2026-09-30.

1. THE TEXT RESPONDER IS A SECOND BRAIN, AND IT OWNS THE SEND. It has its own model
 resolution (`textdecide.ModelCatalog`, its own `PREFERRED` alias list), its own nine-tool
 agent loop (`textwork.py`, `TOOLS = web_search, memory_search, owner_state, owner_profile,
 contacts, sms_thread, comms, app_db_query, queue_lookup`), its own send path
 (`textsend.send` straight to Twilio) and its own anti-repeat store (`offered.db`). It
 never calls `app/owner_text.py`, so the one-at-a-time invariant is enforced on one path
 only. What it costs: the owner can be asked two questions at once and answered in two
 unrelated conversations, and 117 texts in 7 days against his 14 replies.
 FIX: route its owner-bound send through `POST /sms/send`; have the app's reply handler
 resolve the turn. Definition of done: `text-health.py` D1 stops failing.

2. THREE DAILY CEILINGS, ONE CONCEPT. `config.py` default 20, `.env` 20,
 `owner_sms_flood_guard` hardcoded 8, and the message the owner actually saw on 2026-09-29
 said "quota reached (3/0 distinct messages sent today)". FIXED for the flood guard
 (L3109 session); the `3/0` text and the `owner_sms_min_urgency = urgent` default still
 need one pass. Definition of done: `text-health.py` C1 stays green.

3. AN AUTONOMOUS SEND THAT IS REFUSED LEAVES A ROW THE OWNER NEVER SEES AND NOTHING THAT
 TELLS HIM. Seven rows sit `held`/`queued`, several with 200-600 recorded send attempts
 (id 1991 has 676, id 2034 has 266). Each is a real obligation -- a car inspection, a
 tuition balance. The guard is right to refuse a text; the failure is that a blocked
 obligation never escalates into the ONE question that could clear it.
 FIX: an escalation sweep that promotes the oldest blocked autonomous obligation into the
 owner-queue as one question with a recommendation.

4. SIX ONE-SHOT MUTATORS IN `~/bin` with 3-7 backup siblings each (`owner-queue.py.bak-*`,
 `sms-responder.py.bak-*`, `patch-shift-contract.py.bak-*`). SIDE-CARS.md names the owner
 of each primitive, and 21 files touching `owner_discussion_queue` were cut to 8, but the
 backups themselves are now the confusion.

5. FOUR INVESTIGATIONS were commissioned as mesh subagents and both first attempts died on
 prompt quoting before doing any work (apostrophes and quotes arrive at the remote one-shot
 as shell arguments). Two hours of wall clock for zero findings, twice.
 FIX, now proven: put the brief in a FILE, make the prompt one argument-safe sentence, and
 verify the child wrote its report before believing it ran.
