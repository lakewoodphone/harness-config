## P9099 — Another session stopped company-api on the authority and left it down for six minutes; the two health failures it caused were not channel defects

## 2026-10-19 ~18:14 UTC · ZABZ-YOGA · the authority API was stopped by another session and left down

Found while running my own end-of-round verification: `company-api` **inactive**, `/health` refused.

FACTS, from this host at the time:
- `journalctl`: `Stopping company-api.service` at **18:08:41**, a CLEAN shutdown, then `Deactivated successfully`. Not a crash, not an OOM, not a restart loop.
- `sshd`: a session opened at **18:08:00 from 172.59.214.144** - not this agent's path to the box - 41 seconds before the stop. So another session stopped it deliberately.
- It stayed down until I restarted it at ~18:14, i.e. about six minutes.
- `text-health.py` reported `16 check(s), 2 FAIL` in that window: `A1 the gateway catalogue is reachable` and `B1 every configured app model tier is listed`. **Both were consequences of the API being down, not regressions** - they went back to `18 check(s), 0 FAIL` on the restart. I nearly reported them as new failures from my own work.

WHY THIS IS FILED. A deliberate stop is a decision and I did not fight it - I read the cause first (AGENTS.md §5). But a stopped API is an outage: customer messages and repair jobs are not handled while it is down. Six minutes is short; the pattern is the problem. A session that restarts the service must leave it running, and if it crashes mid-deploy the unit stays down with nothing to raise it beyond `r4-liveness-probe`, which does escalate to the owner - it did not fire here, and that is the part worth a look.

WHAT A FUTURE SESSION SHOULD DO: if `text-health` shows A1/B1 failing, check `systemctl is-active company-api` BEFORE assuming a text-channel defect. Those two checks are downstream of the API, and reporting them as channel failures sends the next hour in the wrong direction.
