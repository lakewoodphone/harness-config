## P173 — Walk-in hours live in 6+ hand-written strings and drift silently; the schedule is not one source of truth

The owner changed walk-in hours once (Wednesday closed, 2026-09-15) and that same fact was written
by hand in six-plus places: the backend closed-day schedule, the after-hours phone greeting, the
homepage hero badge, the landing contact card, the landing FAQ, the FAQ data hook, the
`businessHours` message template, plus two more docs that describe the hours as current fact
(`dialpad-ivr-audio.md` and the Bachrach after-hours plan). Two of the surfaces I did NOT reach
this session (Dialpad's live closed-hours script, the Google Business Profile) are exactly the ones
customers hit when they are deciding whether to drive over.

**What would fix it:** one exported hours constant (days -> open/close or null) that the backend
greeting, the frontend badges, the FAQ answers and the message templates all render from, so a
schedule change is a one-line diff. The telephony and GBP surfaces can then be generated from it
instead of restated.

Cost when it is not fixed: a wrong door-hour costs a customer a wasted trip, and the shop has no way
to notice it happened.
