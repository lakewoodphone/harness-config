## P3000 — The CFO program's second condition - reconciliation - was never measured at all

Found 2026-10-13 while looking for who owned it. Nobody did.

THE CONTRACT (`~/phone/cfo-program-2026-10-02/00-PROGRAM.md`): the program is done when, for every
account and month, transactions are (1) explained, (2) RECONCILED - "the sum of the account's
transactions equals the account's own balance movement for that month (`balance_snapshots`), or the
difference is written down with a reason" - and (3) carry "no exception without a name".

MEASURED. `cfo-scoreboard.py`'s own `definitions` block has UNEXPLAINED / THIN / EVIDENCED and
`has reconciliation key? []`. So only condition (1) has ever been measured, by anything.

THE TOOL ALREADY EXISTED AND WAS NEVER RUN. `cfo-balances.py` (written 2026-10-02) builds a
month-by-month anchored-vs-derived balance history with provenance per reading and reports where
they disagree. Nothing scheduled it. Run today:
