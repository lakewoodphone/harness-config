## P3160 — The mesh broker -- the only dispatch control plane -- runs on the FROZEN office box

MEASURED 2026-10-13 13:2xZ. The estate's placement broker lives on the FROZEN office box:
`packages/plugin-remote-fanout` resolves `brokerUrl` to `http://localhost:3091` reached over
`ssh secratary-ts`. `settings/machines/host-posture.tsv` marks `secratary` frozen, and the
authority is `secratary-cloud`.

WHAT HAPPENED: the office box's ssh timed out mid-fleet and a dispatch HARD-FAILED:
 remote-ssh: broker-unreachable: the placement broker could not be reached at
 secratary-ts:http://localhost:3091/place -- ssh exited 255: Connection timed out during
 banner exchange. Connection to 100.84.72.88 port 22 timed out.
Commit `a813fab2` is titled "the broker becomes an optimisation, not a veto - a dead control
plane no longer stops a dispatch". It did not hold on this path.

WHY IT MATTERS: the estate deliberately froze the office box and moved the authority to the
cloud, then left the only dispatch control plane pointing at the frozen box. Every fan-out --
the estate's own DEFAULT for large jobs -- has a single point of failure on a machine that is
supposed to be switched off.

WHAT WOULD FIX IT: point the broker URL at `secratary-cloud`, and make a broker failure fall
through to direct per-node probing instead of aborting the dispatch. A fix already exists and
is UNMERGED: `origin/mesh/broker-host-cloud-default-20261013`, commit `89ffd242`
(2026-10-13 06:08 EDT), which retargets fleet-profile-gate.ps1, mesh-e2e.ps1, mesh-hygiene.ps1
and mesh-restart-when-idle.ps1 off the frozen box.
