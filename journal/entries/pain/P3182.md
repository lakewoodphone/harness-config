## P3014 — A customer reply on the Dialpad business line never fires an await - the wake path still watches the retired Twilio line

A customer who replies on the shop's **Dialpad business line** never fires a registered `await`, so the reply sits unseen until someone searches for it.

MEASURED 2026-10-11, end to end. A confirmation text went out at 19:14:08Z (Dialpad id 4557723212226560) and `wake.py await +18454995076` was registered as the flagship pattern says. The customer answered at 19:16:41Z, 2m33s later (inbound id 6507535285395456: "Yes confirmed once we extract the data I'm throwing out the computer so I don't care about it"). At 19:43 the await was still listed as active by `wake.py awaits`, and no flag had ever been filed. Root cause, read from wake.py's own header: layer 1 CAPTURE is `sms-inbox.py`, which reconciles inbound/outbound texts **from Twilio**, and the responder reads `~/.sms-inbox/inbox.db`. The shop's business line is Dialpad, so the message is not in that store at all - `grep -a -c 6507535285395456 ~/.sms-inbox/inbox.db` returned **0** while the same phone appears 3 times (from the await row itself). The comms index does have it (ps_comms_search, indexed 179,483 rows) - that is how it was found.

Consequence: the whole customer channel moved to Dialpad but the wake path still watches the retired Twilio line, so "a promised answer is the one signal that is unambiguously work" silently does not apply to customers. Four days of customer texts would fire nothing.

FIX, and it has a precedent: `mirror-owner-sms.py` already bridges the OWNER's texts from the app's `sms_log` into the inbox store on a */5 cron, read-only, insert-only, deduped on `twilio_sid`, limited to 7 days. The same bridge is needed for inbound Dialpad customer messages (dialpad_sms_cache, direction=inbound) - dedupe on Dialpad message id - so the responder and the awaits see the channel the shop actually uses. Second, smaller gap: `wake.py` has no way to close an answered await (no `await --close`), so this one will keep reporting the customer as "waiting" until it lapses on 2026-10-18.
