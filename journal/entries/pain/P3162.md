## P3162 — A counterparty's approval deadline reached the inbox, was classified twice, and was told to nobody (Sticky Brand order #828330)

**What the owner asked (2026-10-13, verbatim, angry):** *"we got emails about needing to approve the proof. For the stickers we ordered... if we don't approve it in the just printer without approval... you're in charge of the emails, in charge of the ways. Why did you bring nothing to my attention? Why did you not discuss this with me? Why did you not look it up yourself at least?"* He is right on every count.

### Why it was silent — four independent failures, each enough on its own

**1. The deterministic classifier has no rule for "someone is asking you to approve something", and its unknown branch is silent.** `app/services/email_intake.py`, `classify_message()`, is an ordered if-chain.
- `Approval Needed #828330` matches *no* rule and falls to the terminal fallback (rule 11, ~line 455): `{"category": "review", "priority": "p2", "needs_owner": 0, "confidence": 0.4, "reason": "default"}`. The single most important message of the set hit the "I don't know" branch, which reports `needs_owner=0` — indistinguishable from "nothing to do here".
- `Final reminder Review Proof for order #828330` matches `_ORDER_RE` (~line 231) through the alternative `order\s+#` → rule 8 → `{"category": "order", "priority": "p2", "needs_owner": 0, "reason": "order/shipping"}`. **A printing deadline was swallowed by a shipping rule.**

**2. The LLM triage path dropped the other copy on the floor.** `email_triage_log` id 3791 (`Approval Needed #828330`, 2026-10-05T19:24:21Z) = `category='personal'`, `sub_category='needs_review'`, `confidence_tier='low'`, `action_taken='none'`, `action_status='skipped'`. Since 2026-10-01 there are **177 rows with `action_taken='none'`, all `needs_review`** — a dead pile that currently holds repeated `[Telnyx] Low Balance Alert`, `[Action Required] Mark AI agent OAuth clients as agentic for Google Workspace MCP by Nov 11, 2026`, Dialpad voicemail notices and real customer mail (`chevedy@gmail.com`). Worse, **every one of the 1,114 triage rows since 2026-09-20 is `category='personal'` except two** (`receipts`) — the shop's own business mailbox is being labelled personal mail, and 164 of those rows were `action_needed`/`urgent_action`.

**3. The task that was raised for him was closed as done, empty, 66 minutes later.** `tasks` id 27694 `Review Sticky Brand print proof for order #828330 (auto-print in 2 days)` created 2026-10-06T14:32:46.093Z, delegated 14:37:07 (`delegations` id 6065 → `dept_mashgiach`), `status='done'` at 15:38:26.853Z with **`result_summary=''`**. No proof was reviewed. No approval was given. A task that existed *because a decision was owed* was satisfied by an empty string.

**4. The one channel that could still have reached him was suppressed and only logged.** `activity_log` 2026-10-06T14:33:42Z: `proactive_sms` — `"Failed owner SMS: [suppressed] owner_text gate refused (awaiting_reply): he has not answered the message sent 0.17h ago (turn #53)"`. The one-at-a-time gate is correct policy, but it dropped the item instead of queueing it, and the failure is a log line on a machine he does not read.

### Consequence

A pre-print check that the shop paid for (the proof) was silently forfeited, on the branded stickers for a product launch, and the owner found out only because he went looking. Nothing has shipped, so the loss is bounded — but the same four holes are still open for every counterparty that ever asks him to approve something.

### Fixes required (in flight, see the handoff that cites this entry)

1. `email_intake.py`: a high-precedence ACTION-REQUIRED / deadline rule before the money and order rules, covering "approval needed", "review proof", "action required", "final reminder", "auto-print", "if no feedback", "respond by", "expires"; and the silent `reason="default"` branch must not report `needs_owner=0` with no flag.
2. The `needs_review` → `action_taken='none'` dead end must route somewhere a human or agent will read.
3. A task whose origin is an owner-facing approval or deadline must not be closable with an empty result.
4. An owner-notification suppression must queue, never drop.
