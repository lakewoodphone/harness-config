## P2730 — Our spend-ceiling plugin fails to activate on 0.1.7 with no diagnostic, and a dead guard is invisible

## OUR SPEND CEILING PLUGIN FAILS TO ACTIVATE ON 0.1.7, SILENTLY, AND A DEAD GUARD LOOKS EXACTLY LIKE A CEILING THAT IS NOT BEING HIT

MEASURED 2026-09-28 (ZABZ-TECH) in isolated staged boots of the candidate `0.1.7-rc.2`.

The engine reports, as one line among many:
```
dsh: warning: 1 entry did not activate
spend-guard (dsh-plugin-cost/guard): failed to import
```
`spend-guard` is OUR plugin (`harness-config/packages/plugin-cost`, row `dsh-plugin-cost/guard`) and it
enforces the owner's money ceilings -- warn at 35, stop new fan-out at 80, refuse a step at 150, and a
concurrency cap of 12. When that entry does not activate, **none of those limits is enforced**, and the
only trace is a warning line on a boot the owner never reads.

OBSERVED RATE: 2 of 3 staged boots.
```
15:15:59 anchor absent failed
15:20:47 anchor present ok
15:24:34 anchor present, settings.yaml present failed
```
`anchor absent` is explained and is an artefact of MY staging: `lib/guard.js` resolves
`@deepseek-ai/schemastery` through a hand-rolled `createRequire` anchored at
`<DSH_HOME>/profiles/web/package.json`, so a staged home without `profiles/node_modules` cannot resolve
it. But the THIRD run had the anchor and still failed.

WHY IT IS NOT SIMPLY MODULE RESOLUTION: importing `guard.js` directly succeeds in every condition I can
construct -- `DSH_HOME` set to the staged home, `DSH_HOME` unset, `createRequire` from the staged
`profiles/web/package.json` -- and always reports `exports=apply,inject,name`. So the engine's load path
fails where a hand-made one does not, and the engine gives NO reason: `failed to import` is the entire
diagnostic. **Root cause NOT established.** What is established is that it is not reproducible by
resolution alone, so it is timing-, order- or context-dependent, and I will not guess.

WHY THIS IS A STOPPER FOR THE UPGRADE, and not a detail: a spend guard that silently stops running is
worse than no guard, because the owner believes he is protected. This is the same class as the
`hardcoded model name` incident that hid for a week behind a silent deterministic fallback -- a
protective mechanism that fails quietly. It must be fixed before implementing, and the fix does not
depend on knowing the exact root cause:
 1. resolve `schemastery` through MULTIPLE candidate roots with shape validation, the way
 `scripts/merge-settings.mjs`'s `loadYaml()` already does, instead of one hand-rolled anchor;
 2. move fragile work out of module top level and into `apply()`, so a load-time hiccup cannot kill
 the entry;
 3. make a failure to activate IMPOSSIBLE to miss -- surface it on our own health surface, not as one
 line of engine stderr.

NEXT STEP to establish the cause, if wanted: boot the candidate with the guard row `disabled: true` and
again enabled, same staged home, several times, and diff the engine's stderr; and check whether the
loader's error is swallowed by design (`dsh: warning: N entry did not activate` is a summary, and no
per-entry reason was printed anywhere I could find).
