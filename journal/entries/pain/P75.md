## P75 — 2026-09-14 - CORRECTION: tasks are plentiful (3265/14d); a third of them are the company working on itself, and the same problems are re-tasked and closed unfixed

**CORRECTION + measurement: a third of the work queue is purely about the AI company, and the same problems are re-tasked and closed without being fixed**

**Correction first, and it is the fifth of the same kind.** P74 said "the goal generator created eight tasks in two days while the ticker ran 389 times". Wrong. `activity_log` does not log every task creation: the `tasks` table shows **135 created on 2026-09-14 and 82 on 09-13**, and **3,265 in fourteen days**. Tasks are produced in volume. I wrote, in the very same entry, that `activity_log` "records what the code chooses to log" and is "weak evidence about absolute output" — and then read a count from it as an absolute number one sentence later. The `self_management` *share* of logged rows stands; the task count does not.

**The measurement, bounded honestly.** Classifying 3,265 task titles from the last 14 days by keyword:

| label | tasks | share |
|---|---|---|
| purely about the AI system itself | 1,084 | **33.2%** |
| touches the phone repair business | 1,182 | 36.2% |
| both | 509 | 15.6% |
| unclassified by keyword | 1,000 | 30.6% |

The first run of this classifier put 44.5% in the unclassified bucket; inspecting it showed the misses were themselves system work (`ghost_agent`, Home Assistant sensors, "3 improvement proposals", "reliability and integrity"), so the self-share rose from 19.3% to 33.2% after one refinement. **The honest claim is bounded: at least a third of the queue is purely about the company, at most ~36% touches the business, and ~31% cannot be classified by keyword at all.** This is a crude instrument and it is described as one.

**What the samples show, which matters more than the percentages.** The queue contains the *same* problems repeatedly:

- `ghost_agent` appears as tasks **#24955, #24965, #24975, #24982** — four tasks for one defect;
- the reconciler sprawl appears twice: **#24976 "PRUNE DUPLICATE STALE-IN_PROGRESS RECONCILER TOOL SPRAWL"** and **#24987 "PRUNE THE STALE-IN_PROGRESS RECONCILER TOOL SPRAWL"**;
- and several tasks are literal self-references: *"Based on the live snapshot and cross-domain signals, here are 3 synthesis proposals"* (#24984), *"Based on the live snapshot and my scan, here are 3 specific improvement tasks for today"* (#24980).

**And the completion is not real.** **1,831 tasks were closed as `done` in nine days** against 7 `open` and 3 `in_progress` — while the `ghost_agent` defect those tasks named was still producing error rows four tasks later, and the four finance syncs (`boa_sync`, `amazon_sync`, `ebay_sync`, `spending_report`) were still failing. **A task is being closed without the problem being closed.** That is not my inference from a field: it is the same defect and the same persistence across four task ids.

**Which means the owner already told us this and it is in his queue.** **Question #8**, asked 2026-09-11, quotes it exactly: *"the same four data syncs have had at least 6 separate tasks created to fix them ... and every one is marked completed while the failures are unchanged."* His observation is now confirmed with numbers, and its own recommendation — *"let me dig into what these four syncs actually need, find the real root cause"* — is **my work, not his decision**. It is being removed from the queue as a mis-filed item and taken as the next engineering job.

**Why this is the finding that matters to the money.** The company is not idle and not obviously broken: it creates work, runs it, and reports it done. It is *re-proposing the same problems and closing them without resolving them*, at ~$13/day, with roughly a third of the effort aimed at the AI company itself. That is a different and more expensive failure than a stalled loop, and no completion-signal fix addresses it.
