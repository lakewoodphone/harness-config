## P3176 — P — The LPT Waze fleet has never had cellular data, and nothing would have noticed

**What hurts.** The LPT Waze fleet (DRN 2001-2008, Telnyx IoT eSIMs) has **never had working
cellular data** — 0.8-0.9 MB per SIM over 44 days, i.e. signalling only. Every LPT kiosk device
therefore only works on WiFi, which is the opposite of the product.

**Why it went unnoticed for six weeks.** Three separate misses:
1. The symptom (bars, no data type, "no network connection" in the car) was reported to nobody
 until the owner drove with a device on 2026-10-13.
2. The MDM's own data was believed: SIM `enabled`, account funded, `live_data_session: connected`.
 Every one of those was true, and the phone still had no data.
3. No monitoring looks at the *data indicator* or at per-SIM consumed bytes trending to zero.

**The fix, in order:**
1. On each device: Settings → Cellular → Cellular Data Options → **Data Roaming → ON**.
 (Immediate, per device, needs a human finger.)
2. Durable and fleet-wide: teach the MDM a `DataRoaming` **Settings command** — Apple's valid
 `Settings` items include `DataRoaming` and `VoiceRoaming` (see L1873), and `fleet_api.py` only
 ever sends `DeviceName`, so the route must be written. Then onboarding sets it automatically and
 no device ships without data.
3. **Add a real check**: "does this device have a data bearer" — a DoH/HTTP probe from the device
 that fails loudly, or alert on per-SIM consumed-bytes staying at 0 for N days. Today the fleet
 can be 100% broken and every dashboard says healthy.
4. **DRN 2001's SIM is `disabled` in Telnyx** (not just roaming): `POST
 /fleet/devices/2001/telnyx/enable` requires a JSON body — it 422s with `{"detail":[...Field
 required]}` when sent without one, so a bare POST looks like a server fault.
5. Clean the invented `allowDataRoamingModification` key out of the kiosk profiles the next time
 they are rebuilt (harmless, but it advertises a lock that does not exist).
