## P2936 — 73 percent of the owner decision queue is three automated generators, one of which asked the same question six times

## 2026-10-12 05:29 UTC · ZABZ-YOGA · 73 percent of the owner decision queue comes from three automated generators, and one of them asked the same question six times

**Measured 2026-10-12 05:20Z on the authority, `python3 /root/bin/owner-queue.py stats` and a
`GROUP BY source` over `owner_decision_queue`.**

The queue exists for one purpose, in the owner's words: *"the ones that absolutely need me and you
can't solve you bring up with me one at a time."* Its only currency is his attention. Tonight it holds
**162 pending** rows (446 all-time: 190 resolved, 59 dismissed, 35 answered), and this is what is in
them:

| source | n | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| `lpt-work-order-sweep`, one night (2026-10-09) | **90** | per-order "was this paid / did this get collected" questions |
| `lpt-board-walk-20261008` | **10** | board hygiene from one walk |
| `wake-balance` | **6** | **the same low-balance question, six times** |
| everything else | 56 | of which 14 are high, and **9-10 are genuinely his** |

**Three failure modes, each with a different fix.**

1. **One bulk ingest becomes N items.** A sweep that files one row per order turns a single shop rule
 ("a finished order in READY_FOR_PICKUP with no payment row is collected unless the case file says
 otherwise") into 90 questions the owner has to answer one at a time. **A sweep must file a
 classified question or nothing - never a raw row.**
2. **An emitter with no dedupe.** `wake-balance` fired six times across four days for one condition.
 The queue has no uniqueness key on (question, source), so a recurring condition accumulates rows
 instead of updating one. **Dedupe on the emitter's fingerprint.**
3. **A question with no measurement behind it.** All six low-balance rows ask him to decide without
 carrying the balance. He cannot answer that either. **A question whose recommendation cannot be
 computed from a cited reading does not belong in the queue.**

**The reference fix is already in the table.** Of the 40 high-severity items, several are
*per-order* questions that the business's own indexed comms archive (179,608 records,
`ps_comms_search`) and the order database can answer without him - and rule 3 of the estate's own
`AGENTS.md` §2 says a solvable item in this queue "is in the wrong place: solve it, then resolve the
row."

**Correction made tonight, as the worked example:** #445 ("the cloud authority is not on the tailnet")
had been sitting as a high-severity blocker since 2026-10-11 06:19Z. The systems register §1 already
said the blocking note was stale and the authority had been on the tailnet since before 21:35Z the
previous evening. **Resolved as false when written.** It was never his.

**What it costs:** 118 of 162 rows (73 %) are one of the three generators, and the queue's head -
#228, the subsidy question, genuinely his and blocking - has been at the front of that pile for 10.6
days. **A queue that is 73 % noise is a queue nobody reads, including the owner.**
