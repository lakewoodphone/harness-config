## P3003b — Nothing watched the tax calendar; it found five obligations inside 21 days, two already past

2026-10-13. NOTHING on this estate looked at a tax calendar. Measured: no crontab entry and no
systemd timer matched 'tax'; `scripts/tax/tax-reminders.json` was read only by
`estimated-tax-calculator.py`, which is itself unscheduled; and `docs/finance/NJ-TAX-AND-PAYROLL-
MASTER.md` carries the whole schedule WITH THE WARNING IN CAPITALS - "Q3 2026 (due 10/20/2026) needs
to be filed from actual Q3 sales - do NOT forget" - and nothing was making anyone remember.

BUILT: `/root/bin/tax-deadlines.py`, on the clock at 06:17 daily via cron-wrap (`tax-deadlines` in
~/.cron-status/runs.tsv, exit 0). It computes the recurring obligations FORWARD FROM A RULE rather
than from a list that goes stale -
 NJ ST-50 quarterly, 20th of Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct
 NJ-927 quarterly, 30th of Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct (+ WR-30)
 NJ W-3/EFW-2 annual ~Feb 15
 Federal estimated Apr 15 / Jun 15 / Sep 15 / Jan 15
- merges the one-off dated items from tax-reminders.json, and writes
`/root/.cfo/tax-deadlines.md`.

WHAT IT FOUND ON ITS FIRST RUN, obligations inside 21 days:
 2026-09-15 28 days PAST Federal estimated tax (1040-ES) <- already missed
 2026-09-15 28 days PAST Q3 Estimated Tax Due
 2026-10-15 IN 2 DAYS 2025 Tax Return Due (extension: file or pay balance)
 2026-10-20 IN 7 DAYS NJ ST-50 Q3 2026 (sales tax)
 2026-10-30 IN 17 DAYS NJ-927 + WR-30 Q3 2026 (payroll)
plus an un-actioned item dated 2026-08-15: "Enter NJ employer number into Gusto".

THE TAX TOOLING CANNOT PRODUCE THE QUARTER THAT IS DUE, verified three ways:
 * `scripts/tax/calculate-sales-tax.py` prints only through 2025-Q3..2026-Q2. No 2026-Q3 section.
 * `manage-taxes.py calculate --quarter 2026-Q3` ACCEPTS the flag and IGNORES it - it shells out to
 the same script and prints the same fixed table.
 * that script ends with "NO SALES TAX PAYMENTS RECORDED" while the ledger holds 1,874.00 of NJ
 GOV TAX payments on 2026-07-01/02. It is not reading payments at all.
 * and `scripts/tax/tax-payments.json`, the hand-maintained record `manage-taxes.py status` trusts,
 was last written 2026-07-14 - so it cannot see the NJ Division of Taxation payment of 78.00 the
 bank recorded on 2026-10-06. A status that stops at the last time someone edited a JSON file is
 not a status. The new digest therefore reads payments out of the LEDGER and labels them MEASURED.

AND I ALMOST PUBLISHED A WRONG NUMBER. Replicating the script's own revenue query for 2026-Q3 gave
sales 23,166.83 and tax 1,534.80 under the 6.625% rate. Then the control: run the same query for the
four quarters that were actually filed and compare.
 2025-Q3 computed 1,110.01 filed 202.00 DIFFERS
 2025-Q4 computed 432.79 filed 227.00 DIFFERS
 2026-Q1 computed 683.23 filed 327.00 DIFFERS
 2026-Q2 computed 2,290.08 filed 1,118.00 DIFFERS
Every one is roughly double, which is the duplicate-transaction problem (W2743) showing up in a
tax calculation: the same money posted on two Amex cards sums twice over. So 1,534.80 is NOT the
figure to file, and no correct Q3 figure exists yet - the number needs the dedup resolved first.
Recorded rather than guessed, because a tax figure that is double the truth is worse than none.

NEXT, in order: (1) dedup the sales-revenue query so it reproduces 202.00/227.00/327.00/1,118.00 on
the known quarters, (2) THEN compute 2026-Q3, (3) have the digest carry the computed figure beside
the deadline instead of only the date.
