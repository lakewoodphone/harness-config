## P3161 — The authority is one hourly job away from a full disk, and its alarms cannot escalate

MEASURED 2026-10-13 by the manager, then confirmed by a mesh child's own failure.

READING TAKEN 13:2xZ on secratary-cloud, by me: disk /dev/sda1 66G/75G used, **6.1 GB free
(92%)**; inodes 13%; swap **3829 MB of 4095 used (93.5%)**; Mem 2929 used / 12676 available;
`/root/authority.db` **25.86 GB**; `/root/work` 4.4 GB; `/root/git-rescue-20261012` 2.4 GB;
`/root/repos` 1.9 GB; `/root/finance-fleet-20261013` 1.3 GB; `/var/log` 232 MB.

CLAIMED by a second agent and NOT reproduced by me: `disk-guard` logged CRITICAL at
**98.7% / 0.0 GB free** at 13:00:03Z while `df` read 92% at 13:08Z; `prune-backups` logged
`FLOOR BREACHED ... Pruned NOTHING`; `oom_kills_24h=11`; `api_rss` 5.1-5.8 GiB; the hourly
`transcript-archive-pull` needs ~6.27 GB of temporary space; `company-api` runs with
`MemoryMax=infinity`.

WHY IT MATTERS: the database grows ~2.3 MB/h. At 6.1 GB free that is ~111 days to full if
nothing else changes -- but the hourly archive temp is 6.27 GB, i.e. ONE hourly job can fill
the disk entirely, on the box that is the sole writer of the company database. And
`estate-monitor` computes `verdict=CRITICAL crit=4` every two minutes while its escalation
allow-list is hard-coded to a single condition (`estate-monitor.py:1355-1359`), so
`escalated=0` -- the estate has a detector and no transport.

WHAT WOULD FIX IT: a bounded/streaming archive temp that does not stage gigabytes on the same
volume; a `MemoryMax` on `company-api`; schedule the already-written `/root/bin/cron-liveness.py`;
and one line letting `estate-monitor` escalate disk, swap and OOM conditions it already computes.
