## P2879 — The launcher logs launch attempts, not outcomes - fifteen no-op clicks are on record as successful opens

WHAT HURTS: `Open-SlotWindow` in dshw.ps1 logs `open slot=... origin=... pid=...` after Start-Process
returns. Start-Process returning proves an Edge stub was created, NOT that a window exists. On
2026-10-08 windows.log carried fifteen consecutive "open" lines (20:45 on the 7th through 20:32 on the
8th) for clicks that opened nothing at all, and the click path is `dsh-new://open` ->
`pwsh -WindowStyle Hidden -File dshw.ps1 new`, so there was nothing on screen either. The owner's
report was "i just tried to open another window, i still have the same problem, nothing opens", and
the launcher's own record said it had succeeded fifteen times.

PARTLY FIXED 2026-10-08: the proxy-down fallback now REFUSES instead of launching a URL that opens
nothing, and writes the refusal to windows.log and watchdog.log (a hidden path needs a file, not a
console line). The general hole is untouched: no launch is ever verified against a window that exists,
and `dshw status` reported "1 window(s) open" while a census found 5, because it derives the count from
origin liveness in a shared profile.

WHAT WOULD CLOSE IT: after Start-Process, take one window census (EnumWindows, geometry matched to the
slot's position/size) and write `opened` or `NO WINDOW APPEARED` to windows.log; have `dshw status`
print that same census rather than the origin-derived figure; and make the +=/new control surface the
refusal to the owner in the GUI rather than only in a log.
