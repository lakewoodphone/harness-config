## P2825 — ZABZ-YOGA: the dock takes the 7EC2 left-hand USB-C port and lights no external display

## WHAT HURTS

The dock's DisplayPort tunnels fail to come up on the **7EC2** left-hand USB-C port of ZABZ-YOGA
(Yoga 9 2-in-1 14IMH9, type 83AC), while the **7EC3** port works. It is the owner's work laptop;
when it fails he loses two external screens, and the working fix (move the cable to the other
port) is only known because he found it himself.

**Why it is hard to get right.** While it was failing, every comfortable reading said the monitors
were fine: `Get-PnpDevice -Class Monitor` showed both Dells `OK`, and
`WmiMonitorBasicDisplayParams` showed them `Active=True`. Both come from registry last-known state
and survive disconnection. Only `Win32_VideoController` (internal panel mode, 2880x1800) and
`Screen.AllScreens` (count 1) and the non-OK device list (a code-45 phantom
`Unknown USB Device (Port Reset Failed)`) told the truth. A session that trusted the monitor
classes would report "the displays are connected but dark" and go looking in the wrong place.

## WHAT WOULD FIX IT

1. One full power drain (shutdown, hold power 15-30 s, boot) with the dock on 7EC2. That is the
 only test that separates *port stuck in a bad state* from *port physically dead*, and it needs
 the owner to take the machine down. Until it runs, neither claim is proven.
2. If it survives the drain, it is hardware and a Lenovo warranty question (type 83AC).
3. Confirm the BIOS/USB4 firmware generation actually offered for 83AC. Installed is NNCN37WW
 (2026-04-30); Lenovo Vantage is not installed and the support page is JS-rendered, so the
 current available version is **not** established.

## WHAT WAS DONE ABOUT IT

- Diagnosed from live readings; worked state restored by moving the cable (owner's action).
- Recorded: `L3233` (the two ports are separate USB4 systems; presence is not a link check) and
 the full record at `docs/hardware/zabz-yoga-usb4-dock.md` in harness-config, including the
 two-command diagnosis and the recurrence procedure.
