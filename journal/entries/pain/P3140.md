## P3140 — The cloud authority has 15 GiB and two jobs that each want 7.4 GB - it oom-kills about hourly and hard-reset itself on 2026-10-12

## P — the authority is memory-bound, and that is a reliability ceiling for everything on it

Measured on secratary-cloud 2026-10-12 (4 vCPU AMD EPYC, 15 GiB RAM, 4 GiB swap, 75 GB disk 80% full):

- transcript-archive-pull.service reached 7.4 GB RSS (total-vm 9.2 GB) and was the oom-kill victim
 FOUR times in one day: 08:43, 09:33, 11:30, 12:33.
- company-api.service reached 7.4 GB RSS and was the victim three times: 15:17, 07:52, 09:05.
- 14 oom-kills in 24 hours. The kernel's oom-killer is GLOBAL, so a victim is chosen across the
 whole host - and cloudflared, the mesh broker, the phone engine and the API all live here.
- At 13:09:02Z the journal simply stops and the next boot starts 13:13:08Z with no shutdown
 sequence: an unclean reset. That is 4 minutes in which the company, the phone and every public
 hostname (Cloudflare 530) were dead, and nobody was told.

MEASURED GROWTH of company-api from a cold start after that reboot:
 1.5 GB -> 2.8 GB (first ~5 min), then ~37 MB/min, settling toward ~15-20 MB/min:
 13:19 2826 MB, 13:21 2917, 13:23 3210, 13:25 3209, 13:27 3246, 13:29 3282, 13:31 3297, 13:33 3298.
 It is a large resident working set plus slow growth, not a runaway - but on 15 GiB, with a 7.4 GB
 archive pass beside it, the two together exceed the machine.

STOPGAP APPLIED: MemoryMax=3.5G on transcript-archive-pull so a runaway pass dies by cgroup
instead of killing the host. The first pass under it ran clean (313 MB, Result=success) and there
were zero oom-kills in the hour after the reboot.

WHAT WOULD ACTUALLY FIX IT (one of, in the owner's hands because it is money):
 (a) more RAM on the instance, or
 (b) the archive pull taught to stream instead of hold (its 7.4 GB pass is the single largest
 allocation on the box, and it is 7.2 GB of archive on disk), and
 (c) the API's working set understood - ~3.3 GB at rest is what it needs.

WHY IT MATTERS BEYOND THE BOX: the phone, the shop dashboard, the public site and every agent tick
share one host, so this is not one service's problem.
