## P248 — The sync idempotency guard exists on 9 of 383 records, so duplicate-order protection is a habit rather than a mechanism

**The idempotency guard exists on 9 of 383 sync records, so duplicate-order protection is largely not in place - and the fix is a decision nobody has made.**

**What hurts.** The production sync tool decides CREATE vs SKIP on `references.websiteOrder` inside a sync record. Measured 2026-09-17 by a read-only detector over the whole corpus: **only 9 of 383 sync records carry that field at all**. So for 374 records the tool has no per-record proof that the job was already pushed, and any one of them that is re-pushed can create a second order for work already paid for. It has not happened yet: 0 of the 69 sync duplicate groups has a guard mismatch *right now*, so the damage is latent, not armed. Latent is the right word for a hazard that is one link away from firing.

Why it looked safer than it is: the 82XM00LMUS job (customer 848-480-5115) *was* one link away - one record held the production link and said the part was never ordered, while the record holding the truth had no link at all - and it was only closed on 2026-09-17 by hand. **A guard that is present on 2% of records is not a mechanism; it is a habit.**

**How the duplicate half became ordinary.** 77 duplicate groups across the corpus (75 unclosed): 8 case-file groups and 69 sync-record groups, 62 of them plain two-record `same-caseId` pairs. The shape is always the same - the same repair written twice under two slugs by two sessions. The detector is read-only and reports evidence per group; it merges nothing.

**What would fix it, in the order that matters.**
1. **Make the guard a written invariant, not an optional block.** Either the sync tool's writer always writes `references.websiteOrder` on a create, or the tool refuses to consider a record pushable until it has one. One of those two, decided once.
2. **Retire the 68 mechanical `same-caseId` pairs**, hardest-first, adding each reconciled group to `known-closed-doubles.json` with its reason.
3. Keep `doubles.py` in whatever check runs before a push, so a pair that develops a guard mismatch fails loudly instead of being discovered by a customer receiving two invoices.

**What must not be the fix.** Deleting the unguarded duplicate. In the 82XM case the duplicate was the only place the production link lived, and the unguarded record was the only place the truth lived - deleting either one loses an order link or the customer's money record. Copy the truth into the survivor, then retire the other with a pointer.
