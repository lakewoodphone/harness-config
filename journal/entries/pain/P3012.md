## P3012 — 'Unidentified' is 183 rows and 45,737.48, holds deposits in a spending bucket, and names its own source

2026-10-13. `Unidentified` is not a small residual. It is 183 rows and 45,737.48, it contains deposits in
a SPENDING bucket, and 24,863.52 of it names its own source.

HOW I GOT HERE. Following clause 5 ("owner questions about unclear current or recent purchases") through
the forward view's own category list, which reported `Unidentified Personal Spending 1045.00` and
`Unidentified Receipt Clearing 5209.00` in its last six months.

THE POPULATION, measured in the ledger rather than the report:
 Unidentified Receipt Clearing 109 rows 29560.97 108 of them CREDITS, i.e. deposits
 Unidentified Disbursement Clearing 52 rows 8119.57 all debits
 Unidentified Personal Spending 18 rows 2679.88 9 debits AND 9 CREDITS
 Unidentified Card Credit Clearing 4 rows 5377.06
 ---------------------------------------------------------------
 total 183 rows 45737.48
`Unidentified Personal Spending` holds nine credit rows. A spending category receiving deposits is not a
residual, it is a bucket that has lost the thread of what it is counting - and the forward view, the P&L
and every "unidentified" figure the owner has ever been shown are downstream of it.

AND 62 OF THE 183 ROWS NAME THEIR OWN SOURCE, 24863.52 of them:
 ABLETELSOLUTIONS TRANSFER <- a named payer, many times, 2024 through 2026
 ATM CHECK DEPOSI TW04C379 1601 MADISON AVENUE <- a named place, with a deposit id
 BKOFAMERICA MOBILE 03/10 XXXXX24583 DEPOSIT <- a named bank and a card's last four
These are not unidentified. They are rows the CLASSIFIER does not match, which is a different problem with
a different fix - extend the pattern list - and one that does not need the owner's attention at all.
**An "unidentified" report that includes ABLETELSOLUTIONS TRANSFER is measuring the classifier, not the
world.**

A SUSPICION I TESTED AND WITHDREW. Two identical credits of 1045.00 `ATM CASH DEPOSIT 07/01 1700 MADISON
AVE STE 6 Lakewood NJ` on 2026-07-01 looked like a duplicate charge. They are correctly handled: one is
`ingestion_status='duplicate'` from `Chase Credit Card CSV` pointing at the `active` `plaid-api` copy, so
exactly one is counted. Verified before reporting rather than after.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR CLAUSE 5. The ask loop works at three purchases a day against a backlog of 183 rows
holding 45,737.48 - and at least 62 of those rows should never have been candidates, because they say what
they are. Before the CFO asks the owner another question, the unidentified bucket needs to be split into
(a) rows the classifier should match and does not, (b) genuine unknowns worth one question each, and
(c) rows that are unidentified because they are cash, which no question will ever resolve.
