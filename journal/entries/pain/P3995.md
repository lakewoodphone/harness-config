## P3995 — Ledger item 216 was closed done on a DoD that asked for screenshots nobody took

## Ledger item 216 was closed "done" on a definition of done that asked for screenshots nobody took

Found 2026-10-14 while picking up the handoff. Item 216 is the owner's own report about the
counter search; its DoD has four parts, and part (3) reads:

> (3) A before/after screenshot of the exact thing he pointed at, committed under
> docs/website/walkthroughs/<date>/owner-feedback/, plus a screenshot of at least ONE other
> place in the same class.

**No such screenshots exist for item 216.** The close recorded a live before/after RUN of
`e2e/personas/verify-search-reaches-finished-jobs.mjs` on the test site — which is a better
instrument than a screenshot for that question, but it is not what the DoD asked for, and the
close did not say the DoD's third part was being satisfied by something else.

**Why it matters more than a missing PNG.** A `done` close is read by every later session as
"nothing here needs doing", so an unmet part of the DoD becomes invisible rather than open.
The estate has a name for this shape already: a claim that is not the claim that was earned.
The next session found it only because it re-read the item before acting on it.

**What to do instead:** when closing an item whose DoD names evidence you did not produce,
either produce it, or say in the close **which part you are not producing and why** — the same
rule as an empty search result being a refusal and not health.

**State after my attempt:** item 216 is back to `todo`, the instance fix is recorded as done
with its test evidence, and the remaining class work is named (the search-box half of the
class is unmeasured, four lists silently cap how far a filter can reach — 20/50/100 rows — and
`receipt.list` is unproven because the handler throws before any query is built).
