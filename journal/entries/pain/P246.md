## P246 — A second Zabz session moved lpt-hub main under me mid-session - two writers, one repo, no lease

**What happened.** 2026-09-17, working the Weitman case in ZABZ-TECH's `lpt-hub`, `main` moved under me
mid-session. At the start the checkout was on the desktop's own diverged line (tip `86cfcde24`, 48
commits ahead of `origin/main`, 2775 behind). By the end, local `main` carried `origin/main`'s commits
plus the desktop's — `058b8e73c "case: three customers had no production order"`, which had been
`origin/main`'s tip twenty minutes earlier, was now a *local-only* commit in my own log. Levi and
Czermak case commits appeared in my history with subjects I had never written, authored `zabz` and
`zabz68`, and a second copy of the same work (same subjects, different hashes) existed on
`origin/rescue/zabz-tech-lpt-hub-20260917`, pushed by someone else between my pushes.

**Conclusion:** a second Zabz session is operating the same `lpt-hub` checkout on this machine at the
same time. Nothing was lost — every push I made landed, and `origin/main` (`0ec8f74dc`) and the working
tree agree on every file I touched — but that was luck, not process. Two sessions rebasing and
mirroring the same repo concurrently is how one of them silently reverts the other's commit.

**What would fix it.** A lock: `lpt-hub` claims a lease before a session mutates it, the way
`laptop_sourcing.py` and the case-index already lock what they own, and a session that cannot get the
lease reads and reports instead of writing. Failing that, at minimum: **never `git fetch` + move a
branch out from under a working session** — check for other live writers first, and if `git status`
or the log shows commits you did not author, stop writing and hand off through the journal rather than
racing.

**Measured, not assumed:** `git log HEAD..origin/rescue/zabz-tech-lpt-hub-20260917` returned 7 commits
whose subjects I did not write; `git log origin/rescue/…..HEAD` returned 9 of mine the branch lacked;
and `058b8e73c`'s repo position changed from remote tip to local-only inside 25 minutes.
