## P6275 — The owner's iPhone dictation has been broken for 13 days with nobody driving it to a close

**P · The owner's iPhone dictation has been broken for 13 days with nobody driving it to a close**

HIS WORDS, 2026-09-30: "I find that the talk to text on my iPhone is exceptionally bad... it skips out some
words and it gets garbled very easily... I hate it." Then, after installing what I told him to install:
"I think I did all the instructions, but I'm not sure what happened... it looks like talk to text isn't
working at all through Apple or the other one."

WHAT I DID: diagnosed it as one cause rather than two — the orange mic dot in his screenshot meant
something was holding the microphone, most likely a Superwhisper recording left running, which locks out
Apple's dictation entirely — gave four ordered steps, and asked for one screenshot.

WHAT WENT WRONG: **the session ended there and nobody followed up.** No session on 1-12 October returned to
it. He is dictating every day on a phone whose dictation is worst-case broken, and the last thing on the
record is a request for a screenshot he never sent. This is the "flag it and stop" failure in rule 2c: a
discovered problem was treated as a finding to report instead of a thing to drive to done.

WHAT WOULD FIX IT: (a) the most likely single cause is still a stuck audio session — force-quit Superwhisper
then restart the phone; (b) Apple's mic key does not exist on third-party keyboards, so "Apple's is gone
too" is expected, not broken — switch the globe key back to Apple before concluding anything; (c) check
`Settings ▸ General ▸ Keyboard ▸ Dictation ▸ Enable Dictation` is actually on. It closes when one real
sentence dictated into a real message comes back correct — not before.

COST IF LEFT OPEN: he re-asks, or he stops using voice input to the machine that runs his business, and
every dictated message to me arrives garbled — which the journal already records as a real tax (L2338, the
"jockish" search that cost a session).
