## P3170 — A device can sit in the shop for a day with no production record, and nothing detects it

Measured 2026-10-13 on the Ullman case. The customer walked in on 2026-10-12 and left a laptop; the owner wrote him a sticky note, took it, and no work order was ever created. The device was invisible to production until a session filed it retroactively 29 hours later, and it was only found because the owner happened to send the photographs.

WHY IT IS INVISIBLE: every check the estate runs is order-first. sync-health-audit's reverse gap counts 'recent active prod orders with NO lpt-hub case' and the case index counts cases without records. A device with NO order and NO case is in neither population, so no report can ever surface one. The failure mode is exactly the one the shop has already been bitten by twice - customer data with no record - and it is structurally undetectable today.

WHAT WOULD FIX IT: a physical-custody reconciliation. The shop has a counter, a shelf and a queue; the record has orders. Anything on the shelf with no AWAITING_DIAGNOSIS/AWAITING_PARTS order is a gap, and it needs either a human walk-list or a photo-of-the-intake-pile routine. The honest cheap version is a printed daily 'orders open right now' sheet compared against what is physically in the lockup - which is a real job for the shop's own staff, not a query.

RELATED: this is the third case in a week filed retroactively from the owner's photographs (WO26100812735-12740 batch, 2026-10-08). The batch is evidence that walk-in intake and production entry are not connected, and that agents are the ones closing the gap by hand.
