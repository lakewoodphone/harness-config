## P336 — Customer card numbers land in plaintext in dialpad_sms_cache - no redaction on any ingest path

# Customer card numbers land in plaintext in `dialpad_sms_cache` and nothing strips them at ingest

Found 2026-09-20T07:2xZ while answering "what needs my attention". A customer texted us a card to charge:

 phone 8482993187, 2026-09-18, body: "Pls charge 5129 9252 3138 5389 Exp 05 30 cvv 378 zip 08701 Ty Yoel Weissman"

That is a live PAN + expiry + CVV sitting in plaintext in the authoritative DB (62,598 inbound rows in
`dialpad_sms_cache`). The customer sent it, so this is not an internet exposure - it is a hygiene and
liability problem: cardholder data in a table that agents read into their context, and a PCI-DSS
violation the moment we claim to process cards.

**There is no redaction on any ingest path.** The write paths, found by
`grep -rn "dialpad_sms_cache" app scripts tests` on the live tree (2026-09-20):

 - `app/services/dialpad_webcrawler.py` ~3834 - `INSERT OR IGNORE INTO dialpad_sms_cache` (Playwright
 UI crawler, driven every 30 min by `scripts/dialpad-harvest-cron.sh`)
 - `app/services/dialpad_service.py` ~812 - `INSERT OR IGNORE INTO dialpad_sms_cache`
 - `app/main.py` ~6066 - the inbound webhook path via `persist_sms_event`

**The fix, designed and NOT yet built.** A pure `app/services/pii_redaction.py::redact_payment_data(text)`,
called on the body at each of those three writes, masking a card to its last four. The crux is the gate:
13-19 digits alone is WRONG because IMEIs are 15 digits and would be mangled, so the candidate must pass
a **Luhn check**, and CVV / expiry are masked only next to a `cvv`/`cvc`/`csc`/`exp` keyword. Tests must
include the negative cases: a phone number, `$120`, a date, a 15-digit IMEI, and a 15-digit string that
passes Luhn with no card context.

**Existing rows are deliberately untouched.** Masking history is a data modification; the owner has not
been asked, and "never destroy data" applies. Only ingest, going forward.

**Why it is not done tonight.** My attempt to fan this out over the mesh failed on my own brief - see
lessons L#### "A mesh child's host is the broker's choice": the child landed on ZABZ-TECH while the brief
pinned it to the authority's Linux tree, and it died at the 30-minute cap. Hot-patching the live comms
pipeline on erev Yom Tov is also the wrong risk for a no-deadline hygiene fix.
