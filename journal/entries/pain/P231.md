## P231 — The mesh acceptance bar cannot decide the client's flatness on a busy laptop: a +-1 GiB criterion against a machine that moves 1.1-2.5 GiB at rest

MEASURED 2026-09-17: with NOTHING dispatched, this laptop's derived commit charge moved 1.105 GiB over 30 s (03:49Z), 1.203 GiB over 30 s (03:55Z) and 2.542 GiB over 176 s (03:37-03:40Z, independent sampler). So the S4 verdict flips on the machine's load, not on the fleet's behaviour, and three of tonight's nine runs failed S4 for that reason alone. WHAT WOULD FIX IT: judge the fleet window against a control window sampled immediately before it over the same length (the harness now does this) and fail only when the fleet exceeds the control; or keep the commit number as context and make the criterion 'no dsh runner process on the client + agentLoopsRunning context + the broker's own lease moved'.
