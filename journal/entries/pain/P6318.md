## P6318 — I put the gemach fleet's wallpaper on six units for sale

## 2026-10-13 ~20:00Z · ZABZ-TECH · I put another organisation's wallpaper on six units for sale

**What I did, precisely.** Running `onboard.py` for the five new LPT NAVIGATION units, I called
`/render-wallpapers` and then pushed the result to their lock **and** home screens — and to DRN 2002,
which I had just converted to a sale unit. What went onto those screens was the **"waze gemach"**
artwork: the gemach logo with the black hat, a Hebrew dedication, *"a project of DATING RESOURCES
NETWORK"* with its logo, the gemach's line 732.523.4827, and "Powered by LAKEWOOD PHONE & TECH" —
with **`LPT 4` … `LPT 8` drawn into the middle of it**. Verified by fetching the pushed images back
from the fleet API and looking at them (sha256 recorded below).

**Why it happened, and it is not the code's fault.** Production's own comment on the LPT wallpaper
path says it is *"the shop's own art, the shop's own words, no DRN number and no DRN artwork anywhere
on it"* — and that path is gated on `device_class = 'store_device'`. Our five units have
`device_class = NULL`, so `_render_home_wallpaper` fell through to `DRN_HOME_SOURCE` — the gemach
BMP — and `_wallpaper_label` drew a site-rank number on it. **I ran the gemach fleet's onboarding
path on LPT product units without ever opening the LPT product's own wallpaper pipeline**, which has
been sitting in the same directory the whole time:

 scripts/waze/wallpaper/home.html → render.py → upload.py (verify → stage → push)

**And the reason it has no number on it** is in `upload.py` itself: `stage_home(png, drns)` stages
**one image to every DRN**. The LPT wallpaper is identical on every unit because a sold unit carries
its identity in its **name**, not printed on the screen. That is the distinction I collapsed.

**What the correct art is, measured:** the LPT template renders a teal scene with the white Waze line
logo, the KOSHER / Waze wordmark, "Lakewood Phone & Tech", "For all your tech needs" and
732.655.2355 — and it **matches the customer sticker that was already produced and paid for**
(`lpt-hub/artifacts/kosher-waze-sticker/kosher-waze-round-2in-600px.png`). Rendered, layout-verified
by the pipeline's own pixel check, staged to the wallpaper volume for 2002 + 2004-2008, pushed, and
`ota.py device-name` applied so each unit reports `LPT 2002` … `LPT 2008` — SOP step 7, which I had
skipped and wrongly queued as a question.

**An environment landmine that made the documented path silently impossible.** `render.py` cannot run
under this repo's `.venv`: that venv is a **Microsoft Store Python**
(`WindowsApps\PythonSoftwareFoundation.Python.3.13`) whose child processes **cannot create a file or a
directory anywhere** — `repo-root-write-test.txt` was denied in the repo root, in `$HOME` and in
`$TEMP`, and Playwright died at `mkdtemp ... EPERM`. **`C:\Python313\python.exe` is a normal CPython
3.13.5 that already has playwright, PIL and requests, and it can write.** Use it for anything that
writes a file until the venv is rebuilt on a non-Store interpreter.

**Two contradictions in our own record, which are the owner's to settle — I am not guessing again:**

1. **Does a sold unit's wallpaper show the fleet number?** The bench SOP (§7 step 6) and the
 packaging spec (line 75 and §5.1: *"a wallpaper that displays `LPT {drn}`"*) say **yes**. The LPT
 template and `upload.py` (one image for all DRNs) say **no**. Both cannot be true, and the
 packaging spec is the older of the two.
2. **"Kosher Waze" is on a customer-facing screen.** The LPT template renders the wordmark
 KOSHER / Waze, which is exactly what the shipped sticker carries — but journal **D96** rules that
 WAZE is a live Google trademark and that customer-facing material carries **LPT NAVIGATION™ only**
 ("Kosher Waze" is internal shorthand and stays internal). A wallpaper is customer-facing.
