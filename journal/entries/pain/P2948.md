## P2948 — run-sentinel reported rc=0 for 2,991 runs and nothing ever read what it sensed

WHAT HURTS (ranked 3)
A ported-but-unmonitored job is indistinguishable from a silent one.
- run-sentinel always exits 0 by design (a finding is the signal), so cron-wrap.sh records rc=0 forever
 whether the kernel senses something, senses nothing, or cannot see at all. The office record is
 2,991 runs / 0 non-zero, which proves nothing about whether it was ever useful.
- Nothing reads the state dir it writes. The honest monitor is the ABSENCE of a new history.jsonl row
 in 15 minutes, which is exactly the absence-reporting monitor 131 sec 7 asks for and which does not
 exist for any office job.
- Same shape as pain P2477's family: a job that reports success while doing nothing is this estate's
 most expensive recurring defect (132 sec 2, AGENTS.md sec 1 trap 2).

WHAT WOULD FIX IT
- One cron-check job that reads /root/.cron-status/runs.tsv and raises ONE owner question when a name
 that ran 15 minutes ago has no row now; and that treats an empty result as a refusal, never as health.
