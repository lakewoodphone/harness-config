## P2854 — P: The shop's money model lives only in the owner's head - no written pricing rule exists

FOUND 2026-10-05, while trying to word one customer message about a 50 fee.

WHAT HURTS
The shop has no written pricing model. It has fragments in four places that disagree:
 - lpt-hub docs/operations/lpt-pivot-2026-10-05-program.md: "50 non-refundable diagnostic fee
 per item, credited toward the repair" (true only for the attempt case, and it reads as the
 general rule)
 - actual sent messages: "We charge 30 for filtering and apps" (2026-02-20) vs 15 chip filter
 today; 160 for a Nano screen in May vs 150 tonight
 - case files: every price is a number with no rule behind it
 - the owner''s head: the only complete copy.

CONSEQUENCE
Anyone - a new employee, an agent, a future session - has to derive the rule from examples and
will get it wrong. I produced three wrong versions in one sitting, all defensible, all
commercially wrong (L3341). A customer-facing message about money was drafted three times and
scrapped three times, on a live customer, while she stood at the counter.

WHAT WOULD FIX IT
One page: the 50 rule as now written (PRICING-MODEL.md), plus the going rates for the shop''s
standard jobs - screen tiers by device class, battery, port, data recovery, filter, diagnostic
- each with the date it was last confirmed. Then any new quote is checked against it rather than
asked about.

COST OF NOT FIXING IT
Every new job is a fresh negotiation with the owner, and the answer drifts. Two examples from
2026-10-05 alone: the same Nano screen was 160 in May and 150 tonight, with nothing recording
why it moved.
