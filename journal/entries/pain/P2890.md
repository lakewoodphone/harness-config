## P2890 — 117,602 SMS rows carry an undateable created_at, so any date-bucketed outreach number built from dialpad_sms_cache is inflated 2-3x for Jul-Sep 2026

MEASURED 2026-10-09 on secratary (ZABZ-YOGA session, while answering "biweekly
customer interactions for the past 6 months").

WHAT HAPPENS. dialpad_sms_cache.message_id splits into two families that look
alike and behave completely differently:

 ptf-comm-* 117,602 rows, 450 threads, ids assigned in insert order
 Dec 25 / Jan 41 / Feb 23 / Mar 7 / Apr 61 / May 61 / Jun 78
 Jul 56,655 / Aug 50,366 / Sep 10,343 / Oct 3
 numeric the live harvest, ~2,000-2,600 rows/month, steady
 ui-feed-* 107 rows (Apr), part of the same live harvest

The bodies are real shop conversation ("Ok thanks how much do I owe?", "Hi, your
device is out for pickup", real work-order numbers), so these are NOT junk rows.
The defect is that their `created_at` CANNOT BE A MESSAGE TIME. On 2026-07-19 five
different customers' threads carry created_at 04:53:37.179 / .646 / .982,
04:53:38.362 / .710 - five separate conversations inside 1.5 seconds - while
`fetched_at` steps ~90 microseconds per row, i.e. insertion order. The real
family on 2026-06-10 shows natural spacing (18:02:56, 18:08:30, 18:18:56,
21:22:04, 22:58:00) with fetched_at ~5 s BEFORE created_at.

CONSEQUENCE. Bucketing these rows by created_at manufactures 14,497 messages on
one day (against a ~120/day norm) and 30 days over 1,500 rows totalling 106,751
rows. In the biweekly series it tripled "distinct people who contacted us" for
July-September: 211/416/276/316/270/195 with them, versus
210/238/242/264/213/191 without. Anything that reads this table by date - the
outreach_people and first_time metrics in ~/.lpt-reports/tracking.db, and the
`outreach` column of every LPT report - is wrong for that window.

WHAT WOULD FIX IT. (1) Exclude `message_id LIKE 'ptf-comm-%'` from every
date-based calculation until the timestamps are repaired, and say so in the
report rather than silently dropping 117k rows. (2) Recover the true message time
for those rows - the sequence per thread is ordered, and `ptf-thread-<phone>`
names the counterparty, so a repair pass can date them from the live Dialpad feed
or from production `communications` for the same phone. (3) Re-run
`lpt-analytics.py track --backfill-history` afterwards so the long-term series is
recomputed rather than patched. (4) Add a guard: a day with more than ~5x the
trailing median row count fails the freshness check instead of printing as a
record day.

C:\Users\ezabz\code\phone-transcripts\biweekly-final.py and sms-probe3.sql.
