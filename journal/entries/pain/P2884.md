## P2884 — About thirty orders say paid with no payment row, and 83% of payment_transactions is attached to nothing

## About thirty orders say "paid" with no payment row, and 83% of the payment table is attached to nothing

`payment_transactions` holds 349 rows: **290 have no `customerId` and 291 no `orderId`**. So an order
can show `collected 0` while the money was in fact taken, and the reverse shape — a payment row that
belongs to no order — cannot be attributed at all.

On top of that, the sweep found about **thirty orders closed with a status note reading
`paid $X via card/cash/zelle` and no payment row behind them** (e.g. WO2607200010 "paid $150 by
zelle", WO2607200004 "paid $50 by card", WO2606180002 "$80 via cash and $20 via zelle"). Each was
recorded on its order by the 2026-10-09 sweep, and the policy question is question S2 in
`owner-questions.md`.

The 2026-10-08 settlement run (H3339) is the reason the table grew from 56 SUCCEEDED rows to 319 —
and it is also why **4 orders now have settled payments exceeding their own total**: a settled Stripe
charge and a manual "offline payment" row describe the same money. Measured, not guessed:
`orders_where_settled_exceeds_total = 4` (orders 75, 199, 202, 728).

**What would fix it:** make a close note unable to say "paid" without a method and an amount (the
closing flow in the counter), and have the Stripe reconciliation match a settled charge to an
existing offline row for the same order and amount before inserting a second one. Both are code, and
neither needs the owner — the policy question S2 does.
