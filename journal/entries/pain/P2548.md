## P — The lab's system of record is a portal we cannot read, and every lab fact we hold comes from email

**What proved it, 2026-09-25.** I built a complete audit of the 300DDR relationship from **216 emails,
all time, both directions**, and concluded that ticket 73896 (Golembick) was blocked because the lab
was waiting on our written *"I agree"*. **The owner replied that he had already answered**, and pasted
the ticket page. It carries a comment the mailbox never saw:

> **Thu 09-24-26 08:24 PM — Update by: `customer-reply` — Subject: Customer Reply**
> *"okay please go ahead / I agree"*

and the portal's own progress block reads **Work Approved: Yes, Work Completed: No.** The approval had
been made **about 21 hours before** I declared it outstanding. Nothing in the mailbox — not the
`helpdesk3@` notifications, not the `chances@` thread, not Gmail's own thread state — carries it.

**Why we are blind.** 300DDR's system of record is a **RepairShopr portal** at
`300ddr.repairshopr.com` (portal key `wa1zrdt3rqoxeg7z4jue`, published at the bottom of every lab
email).
- **No integration exists.** `grep repairshopr` over `personal-secretary-mvp` returns **zero matches**.
- **No stored credential.** The `credentials` table has no RepairShopr/300DDR row.
- **The portal key is not access.** Fetching
 `https://300ddr.repairshopr.com/my_profile/v2/index?portal_key=wa1zrdt3rqoxeg7z4jue` returns the
 **Sign In** page ("Email Address / Password"). It needs the account login.

**This is not a one-off.** Portal-only material already sitting in the case files, every line of which
reached us because a **human opened the portal**: this approval; the *"unsent model draft sitting in
the ticket thread"* noted in the Wilner case file; and the ticket-73459 comments quoted in
`usb-files-disappeared-tbd-2026-08-06.md`. Each was a manual read that no process repeats.

**The consequence to hold onto.** Any statement of the form **"the lab has not replied" or "the lab is
waiting on us" is unproven until the portal has been read.** On 2026-09-25 that claim was wrong on a
live money ticket, and it was wrong in a case file, an index row, a git commit and two journal entries.

**What would fix it.** A logged-in session for the portal (credential in the `credentials` table,
service `repairshopr-portal`, used only to **read**), polled for the open tickets' comment feeds, with
each new comment written into its case file and sync record by ticket number. Until that exists, the
cheap manual check is: **before asserting a lab state, open the portal for each open ticket** — and the
portal's progress block (`Diagnostic` / `Work Approved` / `Work Completed`) is the fastest read there
is.
