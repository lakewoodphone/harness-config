## P2817 — A real reminder held forever over one character, and a machine notice in his queue forty-three times

A REAL REMINDER WAS HELD FOREVER OVER ONE CHARACTER, AND A MACHINE NOTICE SAT IN HIS QUEUE
FORTY-THREE TIMES.

MEASURED 2026-09-30 in personal-secretary-mvp/data/secretary.db, table owner_message_queue:

 id 2057 status=held send_attempts=25 reason=reminder:488
 last_send_error: "[suppressed] owner_text gate refused (too_long): is 321 chars"
 id 2036 status=held send_attempts=43 reason=comms_freshness
 last_send_error: "held: urgency 'normal' is below the send threshold 'urgent'"

TWO DIFFERENT DEFECTS WEARING THE SAME STATUS.

1. ONE CHARACTER. Row 2057 is a reminder the owner created (300DDR/Fishman tickets
 74114/74115). Its body is 321 characters and app/owner_text.py refuses anything over 320.
 `fit_for_one_text` exists and is applied to *escalated obligations*; this one never
 escalates, so it keeps the 320 default and can never fit. Twenty-five attempts, zero
 delivery, because a body is one character too long and nothing trims it.

2. A MACHINE NOTICE IN THE HUMAN'S QUEUE. Row 2036 ("dialpad_sms comms capture is stale") is
 addressed to us, not to him. It was placed in the owner queue and then held 43 times by a
 threshold it can never pass. A row that can never be sent is not a pending message; it is
 a stuck counter, and it occupies a slot in the only queue he reads.

WHAT WOULD FIX IT: (a) any owner-bound body over the limit is TRIMMED at a sentence
boundary, not refused -- a body that cannot fit is a formatting problem, never a reason for
silence; (b) machine-facing notices are routed to an internal channel and never into
owner_message_queue, and an assertion in the enqueue path refuses a body that names our own
infrastructure as if it were his business; (c) a row with N send attempts and no delivery is
promoted to ONE question rather than retried -- which is what owner-ask.py exists to do, and
which for row 2057 is now queue #211.

Provenance note: neither row has produced a text to the owner today. Row 2057's
"suppressed" attempts and the sixteen Elantra reminder attempts earlier today were all
refusals (status=suppressed, twilio_sid NULL), which is why the duplicate check reads zero
delivered repeats over 24 h. The channel is quiet; the queue is the thing that is stuck.
