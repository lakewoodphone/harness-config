## P2933 — The SMS gate does not see MMS media: five voice clips arrived and the mirror recorded num_media=0 / ignored

**P (2026-10-12, secratary-cloud)** - On 2026-10-11 the owner sent five voice recordings from his iPhone to the secretary's Twilio line (+17324447361). They arrived and were transcribed, but two records disagree about them, and both understate the truth:

- **company DB** (`sms_media`): 5 rows, `content_type=audio/amr`, `status=saved`, matched to `sms_log` rows 4092/4095/4096/4101/4103.
- **the owner-line gate** (`/root/.sms-inbox/inbox.db`, table `messages`): the same five MessageSids are present with **`num_media=0` and `state='ignored'`** - the gate cannot see media, so a message whose entire content is a photo or a voice clip is silently ignored.

**What that means:** a customer (not just the owner) who sends only a picture or only a voice note gets no reply and no wake. The gate decides on text it can read; media-only inbound looks empty. Measured 2026-10-05 too: two `image/jpeg` MMS rows, one with `download_failed: 307`.

**Also uncovered:** the audio files exist only in `~/evidence/owner-mms-2026-10-11T001236Z/` and `data/sms_media` **on secratary**. The transcript archive (`/opt/company/archive/lpt-transcripts`, 43.5k objects) pulls only dsh-sessions, dsh-attachments and copilot-chats - it does not carry that evidence tree, and Twilio's media URLs expire. Nothing but that box held the bytes until 2026-10-12, when the five originals + TRANSCRIPTS.md + manifest.json were mirrored to `/root/evidence/owner-mms-2026-10-11T001236Z/` on the authority (sha256 of each verified against the manifest).

**Fix, in order:** (1) make the gate read `NumMedia` and decide on media, not on an empty body; (2) either pull `~/evidence/` into the transcript archive or add the media store to it, so carrier-transcoded customer audio has a second copy.

**Verified:** `sqlite3 /root/.sms-inbox/inbox.db "select sid,num_media,state from messages where sid like 'MM%'"`; `ps_db_query` on `sms_media`; `sha256sum` of the five files, both sides.
