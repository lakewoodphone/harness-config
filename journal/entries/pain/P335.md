## P335 — The website has no holiday-closure surface, so it advertises Monday open on the day the shop is closed for Yom Kippur - Google and the IVR know, the site does not

# P — The website has no holiday closure, so it advertises Monday open on the day the shop is closed for Yom Kippur

Measured 2026-09-20T02:47Z, while verifying the owner's Google hours edit.

Three customer-facing surfaces carry the shop's hours. Two of them now know that Monday 2026-09-21 is
Yom Kippur and is closed; **the website does not.**

| Surface | What it says about Monday 2026-09-21 | How it knows |
|---|---|---|
| Google Business Profile | **Closed** (`regularOpeningHours` + a Monday exception; pane reads "Opens 10:30 AM Tue") | owner set it / holiday hours |
| Phone IVR | closed — Yom Kippur is one of the configured holidays | `docs/operations/dialpad-ivr-audio.md` lists Erev Yom Kippur Sep 20 / Yom Kippur Sep 21 |
| Our own calendar | Yom Kippur 9/21 present as a holiday | `app/services/shalom_zmanim.py`, `docs/operations/walkin-hours-2026-09-15-change-plan.md` A7 |
| **Website** (`lakewoodphoneandtech.com`) | "**Mon**, Tue & Thu 10:30 AM–5:30 PM" — nothing about the holiday | a generic weekly string, hand-carried |

Evidence for the website column: fetched 2026-09-20T02:47Z; the page's Business Hours block and its
`openingHoursSpecification` both state the plain weekly schedule, with no per-date or holiday field
anywhere on the page.

**Why it matters.** A customer who checks the website rather than Google drives to a locked door on a
Yom Tov. That is the same class of harm as the Wednesday error the owner just corrected — the surface
that says "open" when the door is locked — and it is invisible to every scan we run, because the string
is not wrong, it is merely incomplete.

**Why this is P173 again, in a new place.** The fix is NOT to hand-edit the website's hours string each
time: that is exactly the six-hand-written-copies failure the September hours work was about. The
website needs **holiday/special-hours support fed from the same source the other surfaces use** — the
calendar's holiday list in `shalom_zmanim.py` — rendered as a dated notice ("Closed Monday Sep 21 for
Yom Kippur") when today or tomorrow is in that list, with the generic weekly schedule left as the
default.

**Cheapest correct step:** expose the holiday list the IVR already reads, and render a dated one-line
closure notice on the site's hours block from it. One source, three surfaces.

**Not blocking anything tonight** — the shop is closed Sunday anyway and Google, the surface customers
actually check, is right.
