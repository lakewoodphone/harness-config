## P3007 — His answer cannot teach anything: the guess the question carries is not a category that exists

2026-10-13. Traced the whole answer path end to end on copies and found the question he is about to
answer cannot teach anything, because the guess it carries is not a category the ledger has.

HOW I GOT HERE. After fixing the matcher (W2759) I ran the full path - match, record, rule - on
COPIES of the asks store, the alias store and rules.jsonl, so neither live question was touched.
Both replies matched correctly:
 "the polka dot one was shop supplies" -> via=merchant-named -> ask-1791867617
 "ask-1791862240 yes, shop supplies" -> via=reference -> ask-1791862240
and both returned `ok: True` - but `rule_created` was False and the rules file did not grow.

WHY, verbatim from the code. `cfo_clarify.category_from_answer` answers a "yes" by taking the
clarification's own `suggest_category`. For the newegg question that value is **"shop supplies"**,
and the module's own words are:
 "he agreed with the suggestion 'shop supplies', but entity lpt holds no such category, so the
 cluster is marked answered with no rule"
Measured against the ledger: `select count(*) from categories where lower(name)='shop supplies'` is
**0**. The lpt categories whose names mention supplies at all are "Business, Industrial, & Scientific
Supplies Basic" and "Office & Shipping Supplies". The phrase was never a category.

WHERE IT CAME FROM. `cfo-ask.py:1038-1041` parses the guess back OUT of the composed body with
`re.search(r"My guess:\s*([^.\n]+)", body)` and stores it as `suggest_category`. At 03:30 the body
came from the BARE-BODY FALLBACK at line 605, which hardcodes "My guess: shop supplies. Reply 'yes'
...", because `charge_msg` did not exist on this box yet. So the suggestion was defaulted by a
fallback, parsed back out, and offered to him as an agree-with-me. When he agrees, the answer names
a category the ledger cannot file and the rule is skipped.

FIXED AT THE SOURCE. The fallback line no longer asserts a category - it now reads "No guess from me
- say what it was and I'll file it for good", matching what `charge_msg` already does when it has no
basis for a guess. So the situation cannot recur through this path.

WHAT I DID NOT DO, and why it matters. The LIVE newegg clarification still carries
`suggest_category='shop supplies'`, so if he replies "yes" his answer will be recorded, the ask row
stamped, the merchant not re-asked - and NO RULE learned. I left it rather than editing the row to a
real category, because picking one is a classification of his money: the evidence points at
"Merchandise & Inventory" (lpt's dominant category for supplier purchases - 126 Amazon rows) while
the phrase itself points at the Supplies categories, and those disagree. His answer being recorded
without a rule is honest; a rule I chose for him would not be. Recorded here so a future session does
not read "answered" and assume a lesson was learned.

WHAT THIS SAYS ABOUT THE OBJECTIVE'S LAST CLAUSE. "Answers persisted as durable rules so nothing is
asked twice" has two halves and only one of them is automatic. **Not asked twice** works - the ask row
is stamped and the merchant is skipped. **Persisted as a durable rule** requires the answer to
resolve to a category the entity actually holds, and nothing converts the owner's plain words
("shop supplies") into one. That resolver is the missing piece, and it is the next unit of work.
