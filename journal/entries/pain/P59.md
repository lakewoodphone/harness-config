## P59 — The phone's gate died once and nothing on the host says why

**Symptom.** At 05:48:40 the gate was serving normally (its last log lines show a document signed
in and returned, 38,729 bytes). A browser navigation at ~05:49 failed with
`ERR_HTTP_RESPONSE_CODE_FAILURE`; `ss -ltn` showed **no listener on 3086** and Tailscale Serve
answered **502**. Within ~90 seconds the 10-minute cron watchdog had restarted it, and the 5-minute
probe at 05:50:04 was green — so the outage was short, and the owner would have seen a broken page
if he had opened it in that minute.

**Evidence.**
- Serving: `~/.dsh-phone/gate.log` 05:48:40 `-> signed in in flight, returning 38729 bytes with 1 cookie(s)`.
- Gone: loopback `curl` on 3086 → `http=000`; `ss -ltn` → only 3087 and 3089; Serve → 502.
- Restarted: `cat ~/.dsh-phone/gate.pid` → a new pid, `ps -o etime` **00:16**; probe at 05:50:04
 `ok True 15/15`.
- **No cause on the record.** `gate.log` carries no traceback (the process writes its own stderr
 there), `dmesg` shows no OOM line, memory at the time 5.5 GB used of 23.4 GB with 17.9 GB
 available, load 1.3, and the gate's own supervisor (`serve-phone.sh`) never kills a live pid —
 it returns early when the pidfile's process is alive.

**Cost.** Small tonight by luck: a phone opened in that minute shows a 502 the owner cannot act on.
The class is not small — this is the one path the owner depends on from his pocket, and its recovery
depended on a schedule that happened to fire.

**Fix, in order.**
1. **Applied:** the watchdog cadence is now `*/2` instead of `*/10`, so a dead gate is restored in
 under two minutes rather than up to ten.
2. **Open:** give the gate a heartbeat it cannot silently stop writing (an append-only line with a
 monotonic counter) and have the probe alarm on a *gap*, not on the current state — absence is the
 signal, which is what the kernel was built for (P2).
3. **Open:** log why the process exited. A signal-death leaves nothing; a wrapper that records the
 exit status would turn "it died once, unexplained" into a reading.
Do NOT add a second supervisor: the port bind is the lock, and two supervisors fighting is worse than
a two-minute gap.
