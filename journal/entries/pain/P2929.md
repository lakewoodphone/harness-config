## P2929 — Today's card takings are invisible to every internal record for up to a day

## Today's card takings are invisible to every internal record for up to a day, and the report therefore understates the day

MEASURED 2026-10-08, read-only, against the vendor's own API.

The shop's card money is taken at the counter all day and reaches no internal record until later.
Measured for 2026-10-08:

| source | today's card money | lag |
|---|---|---|
| **Stripe API (vendor truth)** | **960.64**, 8 succeeded charges | none |
| production `operational` payments | 704.73 SUCCEEDED (5) + 112.30 PENDING (5) = 817.03 | partial, real-time-ish |
| accounting ledger `stripe-charges` | 1.00 (1 row) | last sync **04:30** — a daily job |
| accounting ledger `stripe-payouts` (bank side) | 0.00 | payouts trail charges by days |

So the report I ran tonight told the owner "money landed 0.00 (PARTIAL)" and "money recorded 712.00",
while the shop had actually taken **960.64 by card that day**. Everything labelled PARTIAL was honest,
but nothing in the report NAMED the true figure, and the owner's literal question — how much money is
coming — has a correct answer that was available in near-real-time and was not being read.

Same day, the vendor's own records confirm the counter-workflow defect recorded in journal H3335:
**not one of the 8 succeeded charges carries an `orderId`** — they carry free-text staff notes instead
("Cleaning for a kyerser", "Lab work", "Contacts", "Labor fee"). Charges of 543.79 and 117.29 carry
no identifying note at all. That is why production holds 5 of today's settled charges as PENDING and
why money cannot be attributed to a job without a human.

WHAT WOULD FIX IT: add a **card takings (Stripe, live)** line to the `today` view in
`scripts/finance/lpt_analytics/today.py`, labelled with its own freshness and placed beside — never
summed into — money landed and money recorded. The credential is the same `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` in
`~/personal-secretary-mvp/.env` that the application already uses; the call is read-only
(`GET /v1/charges`). Deliberately NOT done in this session: putting a live payment credential into a
scheduled job is a decision that deserves to be made explicitly, and the one-day-lagged ledger figure
is already exact for every completed day.

WHY IT MATTERS MORE THAN IT LOOKS: this is the only measure in the whole reporting set that answers
"how much did we take today" with the shop still open. Every other money figure is either a booking
(not money) or a bank movement (days late).
