## P2254 — Newegg resale tax exemption is REJECTED, so every Newegg sourcing buy pays ~6.6% tax it should not pay

Every Newegg order this business places is charged NJ sales tax (~6.6%) that it should
not be paying, because the resale-tax-exemption on the Newegg account is REJECTED.

Measured 2026-09-22 from the owner's own account pages:
- CERTIFICATE MANAGEMENT row: jurisdiction New Jersey, form "Resale Certificate",
 reason "Resale", effective 2026-08-12, expiration blank, STATUS **Rejected**.
 Rejection text: "Please re-upload your tax exempt document. Please add your company
 name to your Newegg account settings."
- Live cost of the rejection: Newegg order #419077624 (placed 2026-09-22 10:35 ET,
 refurbished Dell Latitude 7430, item 9SIB32ZKWF9970, subtotal $339.99) was charged
 **$22.52 tax** on a machine bought purely to resell, taking the total to $362.51.

So the tax is not a rounding error: it is 6.6% of every sourcing buy, on a business
whose whole model is buying refurbished laptops and reselling them. It also corrupts
the margin figure the sourcing flow computes, because the 1.4501 multiplier is applied
to a pre-tax source price while the till actually pays the taxed one.

WHAT WOULD FIX IT: add the company name to the Newegg account settings, then re-upload
the NJ resale certificate, then re-file the exemption. Two fields and a document. The
rejection reason names both blockers, so it is mechanical rather than a judgement call.

Owner instruction 2026-09-22 was a reminder, not a fix ("remind me to deal with this
one day"), so a reminder was set (reminders id 471, 2026-09-24, dashboard, ack
required) and the period-specific credit was routed to the CFO as task 26579
(dept_finance). Recorded here because a future sourcing session must know the source
price it reads is NOT the cash cost - add ~6.6% until the exemption clears.
