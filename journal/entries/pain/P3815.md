## P3815 — 252 LPT pickup orders were closed today by an actor the record does not name, before the owner asked for any of it

Measured 2026-10-20 on the operational store via order_status_history.

At 14:31:02-14:31:05 and again at 15:05:27-15:05:31 - BEFORE the owner's 'close anything untouched for 50 days' instruction later that hour - 252 work orders went to CLOSED with changedBy NULL and a per-order reason in natural language: 'Auto-closed by the assistant after N days in Out for pickup'. Top transitions that day: READY_FOR_PICKUP->CLOSED 272, UNFIXABLE->CLOSED 92. All 376 CLOSED orders in the book got CLOSED today; not one CLOSED predates 2026-10-20.

Attribution attempted and FAILED, not skipped: grep for the reason string matches only app/services/lifecycle_sweep.py, whose 'Auto-closed by' lines are about TASKS not work orders; data/tools/stale_in_progress_reconciler_executor.py is also task-level. 'Out for pickup' appears in neither. No standing work order on the authority matches (34 listed; the nearest, seed_phone_daily_owner_run, ran 14:53 and FAILED). ps_search what=sessions returned did_not_answer: 'authority:sessions OperationalError: unable to open database file' - a REFUSAL, not a negative.

So: an unknown actor closed 252 customer-pickup orders with no actor and no note, and the session index that could have named it is down. Two consequences: (1) a NULL-actor close is indistinguishable from a data corruption and must never be the shape a policy produces - the sweep should write changedBy and a note, as lpt-close-stale does; (2) the session index needs fixing, because 'who did this' is currently unanswerable.

Also confirmed today: 483 of 660 orders carry a machine-written status (225 by changedBy 1/168 or reconciliation reasons, 252 the NULL sweep, 6 by accounts 299/300). 376/376 CLOSED have pickupReleasedAt NULL because that column is not in ORDER_WRITABLE_FIELDS at all, so no sanctioned path can write it.
