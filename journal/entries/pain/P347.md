## P347 — P339 - the AI text line answers nobody, and the owner stopped using it in May because of it

**Ranked pain, measured 2026-09-20.**

1. **A real person texts and nothing ever comes back.** 13 inbound texts have no reply after them;
 Weinberg has 7 inbound and 5 of them sit `queued` since 2026-09-18 03:29. Full audit:
 `harness-config/docs/ai-text-line-audit-2026-09-20.md`, lesson **L2099**.
2. **The owner cannot use his own assistant.** His number is `allow=queue` on his own line, so his
 texts are filed, not answered. He last tried 2026-05-11.
3. **"I'll look into it" has no implementation.** `HOLD:` acknowledges and then raises an
 owner-queue row; there is no *do the work, then reply* path. The one thing the owner named as the
 point of the system (2026-09-20: *"should be able to say, hey, I'll look into and then actually
 look into and get back to them"*) does not exist.
4. **Nothing reports whether the system works.** `responder.log` prints `0 to decide` and that is
 the whole observability story.
5. **The line is dormant at the wrong end.** The personal line has 0 inbound in 11 days; the
 customer line (Dialpad `+17326552355`) takes ~2,600 customer texts a week and has no AI at all.
 Out of scope by the owner's instruction — the AI line is personal, customers are Dialpad — but it
 is the largest unclaimed opportunity in the business and should be re-raised only with a concrete
 proposal, not as a question.

**What would fix it:** the v2 rebuild in flight this session — context, decision, tracked work with
follow-through, a send gate, and a `report` verb. Close this pain when a real text from a real person
gets a real answer without a human in the loop.
