## P3021 — Sync records cannot satisfy the schema the contract tells every session to validate against

Both Weitman sync records fail `CUSTOMER_CASE_SYNC_RECORD.schema.json` with **8 errors each — the same 8
before and after an edit** (measured 2026-10-11, ZABZ-TECH: `git show HEAD:<file>` validated against the
working copy). They are not alone. The schema sets `additionalProperties: false` at the top level and
inside `customer`, `device` and `operational`, while the records the shop actually writes carry
`communications`, `evidence`, `source`, `sourceNote`, `productionCustomerId`, `dialpadContactName`,
`budgetUsd`, `purchase`, `shouldSyncToWebsite` and more. `references.workOrderIds` holds
`"WO26082712634"` strings where the schema wants integers.

**Why it matters.** The case-sync contract instructs every session, as step 1 of finishing a case, to
*"Validate the record against `CUSTOMER_CASE_SYNC_RECORD.schema.json`"*. No record carrying the shop's
real fields can satisfy that, so the instruction is either ignored in silence or would block the work.
And the push tool (`lpt-sync-to-production.py`) does not validate either — measured 2026-10-11:
413 records, CREATE 2, SKIP 411, ERROR 0 — so nothing anywhere catches it. A validation step that can
never pass is worse than no validation step, because it teaches sessions to skip the step.

**What would fix it, and the decision it needs.** Either the schema is the contract and ~411 records are
wrong (a migration), or the schema is stale and should be widened to the fields in use (an edit plus a
re-validation run). That is a judgement about what the contract is FOR. Cheapest first step: count how
many of the 413 records fail and on which properties, so the choice is made on numbers rather than on
the two files that happened to be in front of me.

Related, same family: `lptcase check` reported 224 `sync-record-without-case` rows and 99 genuine
`case-without-sync-record` rows on 2026-10-11. Those may be the same underlying rot seen from the other
side.
