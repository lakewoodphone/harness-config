## P7243 — I sent a duplicate question to the owner's wife by reading the wrong store for delivery

## P — I sent a duplicate question to the owner's wife, and I caused it by reading the wrong store

**2026-10-18, 02:09:24Z, on the authority.** A question about an Audible membership charging 15.94
a month went to `+18482245096` twice. The first copy was delivered at **01:39:03Z** (`family_chat.db`
`messages` id 173133, sid `SM93fa012417ea2560bb9ca46f09400809`, status `delivered`). The second was
sent by me at **02:09:24Z** (sid `SM6ec485e94cb83e4db3b757dfb443045a`, 3 segments). The owner's wife
received the same question twice, thirty minutes apart, at two in the morning.

**How I got it wrong, exactly.** I queried `/root/authority.db` `sms_log` for the sid, found nothing,
and concluded "the message was never delivered". `sms_log` holds the *owner-leg audit* rows — the
family leg writes a `suppressed` row there when a gate refuses, and nothing on success. **Family
Chat's record of delivery is its own `family_chat.db` `messages` table**, which had the delivered
row all along. I then reported the absence as a defect (`work.py` 535, "an outbox row is marked sent
with no attempt and no sid") and sent the message again to "complete his instruction".

`flush_outbox` calls `db.outbox_mark_sent` at `app/family_chat/hub.py:2213` **only after**
`send_and_record` returns, so `status=sent` implied a real send. The outbox was telling the truth and
I called it a liar.

**What made it possible, in order of blame:**

1. I had a *story* (the outbox lies) before I had the store that could confirm or refute it, and I
 went looking for evidence of the story rather than for the record of delivery.
2. `sms_log` is the obvious-looking store and it is the wrong one. The estate's own rule —
 *"before quoting a count, ask what WRITES the row"* — has a twin I had not written down: **before
 claiming an ABSENCE, ask which store would hold the presence.**
3. I was operating under a fresh permission ("the CFO needs no approval to text me or my wife") and
 the permission removed the one step — asking him — that would have caught it.

**Cost.** One duplicated text to a family member, from an assistant, at 2am, about money. The
relationship cost is the owner's to judge; `work.py` 535 was withdrawn with the proof, the send is
recorded in `sms_log` 4284 and relay 80, and the permission's `does_not_cover` now says a body must
not be re-sent on a delivery claim that came from a store which cannot carry it.

**Fix that would have prevented it:** read `family_chat.db messages` for family delivery, always.
The general form: a delivery claim needs the *sender's* ledger, not the *audience's*.
