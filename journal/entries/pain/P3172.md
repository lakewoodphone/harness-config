## P3001 — The shop's order status vocabulary has no value for delivered-and-paid, so finished jobs sit at READY_FOR_PICKUP and read as waiting

MEASURED 2026-10-08. Read live at runtime with 'lpt-write vocab' against the operational store: the statuses are exactly AWAITING_DIAGNOSIS, AWAITING_APPROVAL, AWAITING_PARTS, AWAITING_PAYMENT, READY_FOR_PICKUP, CANCELLED, UNFIXABLE. There is no DELIVERED, COMPLETED, PICKED_UP or CLOSED. Consequence: a job that is paid in full and physically collected by the customer - Golembick, WO2608180004, 2026-10-08, 703.79 collected - can only be left at READY_FOR_PICKUP, which is indistinguishable on the board from a device still sitting on the shelf waiting for its owner. That is 425 rows in that state, so the board cannot be read as a worklist. It is at least the third row recorded against this gap (WO2608060003, and the 2026-10-08 02:26 note on this same order). WHAT WOULD FIX IT: add the missing terminal status to the vocabulary and let the shop's board show finished-and-collected separately - that is a schema/enum change on the operational store plus the website's own status rendering, which is an owner-visible product decision, not an engineering one. Until then every closed job leaves a note explaining itself, which is the workaround this session used.
