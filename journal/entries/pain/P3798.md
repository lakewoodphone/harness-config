## P3798 — A session's *"we searched and found nothing"* note reads as a measurement, and I reported it to the owner as one

WHAT HURTS. On 2026-10-14 I told the owner *"no payment from Pruzansky appears in Stripe"* while
answering a 187-hour customer queue. The claim came from **note 3181 on work order
`WO2608060002`**, written by another session, which said it had searched *"77 payment intents,
2026-08-01 to 2026-09-01"* and that *"no payer name exists anywhere in Stripe"*.

The payment is in our own store: `/root/accounting-data/live/accounting.db` → `transactions` id
`873cd124-760e-4f95-b12b-1794eec08099`, `stripe-charges`, **170.60** on 2026-10-13,
`description` reading literally **`Pruzansky`**, charge `ch_3UQD9yLnlgYj1gmT1BOxhIjw`. One query
by amount and date finds it.

**Three ways the note was unfalsifiable and read as fact anyway:**

1. **It named no field.** It reasoned from Stripe Terminal *metadata* — `readerId`, `staffNote` —
   and concluded no payer name existed anywhere. Measured in that same store: **0 rows contain
   either string**, so it was describing Stripe's raw payload while concluding about our rows. The
   name was in `description`, a field it never mentions.
2. **It named no store.** "Hunted in Stripe" could mean the API, the accounting store, or the
   production payments table. Those answer differently, and the note's own scope
   (*2026-08-01 → 2026-09-01*) **excluded the month the payment was made in**.
3. **The store could not have held the answer yet.** All nine Stripe charges of 2026-10-13 have
   `created_at 2026-10-14 04:40:05Z` — ingested by `stripe-sync` after the note was written. The
   honest word was **UNVERIFIABLE**, and the note wrote **none**.

**WHY IT MATTERS BEYOND ONE CASE.** This is the `wrong-data` failure (11.2% of sessions, the most
common in the 2026-10-13 audit) wearing a lab coat: a negative written in the register of a
measurement, by an agent, and then *inherited* by the next agent because it is a note on a work
order and notes look like facts. The owner's words when he found the payment himself:
***"why couldn't you find this yourself"*** — and then, when I brought him the arithmetic instead
of doing it, ***"isn't it tax? are you stupid"***. 170.60 / 1.06625 = 160.00; the tax convention
was already in `lpt-ops/docs/writes.md`.

**WHAT WOULD FIX IT.** A money claim must carry: the **store and path**, the **fields searched**,
the **last write time of the store**, and the **query**. Anything less is a hypothesis and must
say so. `lpt-hub/_scratch/respond/find-stripe-payment.py` is the concrete fix for this class —
it searches every text column rather than the ones a session remembers, so the next agent cannot
repeat the shape.
