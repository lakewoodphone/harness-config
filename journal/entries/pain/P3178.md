## P3010 — A shutdown that MASKS cron can run again at 04:00, and a dead scheduler pages nobody

**What hurts.** Something on the authority, running as `zabz` with sudo around 04:03Z on 2026-10-11,
stopped the entire company and **masked `cron.service`** — and nothing noticed for 15 hours. The
owner found it by asking a question. Every scheduled job on the box was dead the whole time,
including the Dialpad text harvest (18.5 h stale) and the lpt-hub corpus refresh (900 min stale).

**Why it is worse than a normal outage.** Stopping a service is recoverable and a supervisor
restarts it. Masking is not: `/etc/systemd/system/cron.service -> /dev/null` means the estate
**cannot** heal itself, and it did not. The pattern that produced it was database-lock surgery —
`lsof` on `secretary.db`, `kill` the holders, then stop and mask everything to free the database.
So a tool built to clear a lock took the company off the air as its method.

**What would fix it, in order of value.**
1. **Find the actor.** I have the exact command burst and its timestamps but not the script. The
 journal shows repeated short `zabz` sessions (`session-827` ... `session-830`) at ~15 s intervals
 doing `lsof`/`kill`, which reads like a loop in a shell script rather than a person. Candidates
 found so far are `harness-config/scripts/mesh-hygiene.sh` and `mac-mini-audit.sh` (both contain
 `lsof`), plus `bin/ha-shutdown-replay.py` and `bin/patch-kill-switch-at-choke-point.py` by name
 alone. Until it is named, this repeats at ~04:00 tomorrow.
2. **Make a dead or masked `cron.service` on the authority page immediately.** There is a freshness
 check for the Dialpad harvest and for the lpt-hub refresh, and both went red and sat red — but
 neither says "the scheduler is dead". A masked unit is a one-line, unambiguous signal and it was
 visible to `systemctl is-enabled` the whole time.
3. **Forbid masking in any automated remediation.** Stopping is the reversible action; masking is
 the one that removes the estate's ability to recover. Any script that stops services to clear a
 lock should be allowed to stop, never to mask.
4. **Treat the hung offsite push as the real fault.** At 04:00:08Z `estate-monitor` reported
 `offsite_push_incomplete_run=1.0`, a run started 01:40:08Z with no 'push finished' line after 139
 minutes. That hang, plus a database locked hard enough to defeat the harvest's five retries, is
 what the shutdown was reacting to. With the disk at 92 percent (37G free of 467G), the push is a
 plausible first thing to check.
