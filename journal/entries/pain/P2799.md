## P — The authority's disk is 99% full (7.6 GB free of 467 GB), and the family chat writes to SQLite on it

Measured 2026-09-30 14:5x-15:1x UTC, secratary:

```
/dev/mapper/ubuntu--vg-ubuntu--lv 467G 440G 7.6G 99% /
inodes: 6% used (not the problem)
```

Largest consumers, measured with `du -sh /home/zabz/*`: **secretary-backups 298 G**, `personal-secretary-mvp` 30 G (of which `data/` is 26 G), `quarantine-debris` 28 G, `phoenix-evidence` 17 G, `repos` 14 G, `accounting-data` 6.2 G, `recon-20260915` 5.2 G, plus roughly twenty registered git worktrees at ~2 GB each (`lpt68-wt`, `lpt60-wt`, `lpt51-wt`, `item4-wt`, `int28-wt`, ...).

WHY IT MATTERS HERE

The family chat's entire record is one 1 MB SQLite database on that filesystem, written by the same single process that handles the webhook. A full filesystem does not fail loudly: SQLite raises on write, the hub's send-and-record path raises after the carrier has already accepted the message, and the family keeps talking while the record stops. The 2026-09-15 incident (117 of 140 stored inbound rows unreadable, a month undetected) is the same shape.

WHAT WOULD FIX IT (ordered, cheapest and safest first)

1. **Reclaim test artifacts inside the repo**: `_pt_full` 372 M, four `_pt_deploy_*` 181-238 M each, `_pt_w5_live` 41 M, plus `__pycache__`. `agent-fleet clean -r <repo>` is the sanctioned mechanism (it drops `_pt_*`, `__pycache__`, `.pytest_cache` by design, and nothing else). ~1.3 GB, no risk to data. Not done on 2026-09-30 because a pytest was running on the authority at the time and `_pt_full` had not yet been proved to be a stale basetemp rather than a live staging directory - **prove which, then clean**.
2. **The stale worktrees** (~40 GB): `git worktree list` shows about twenty, several `prunable`. Removing a worktree is destructive to any uncommitted work inside it, so it needs per-worktree inspection, not a sweep.
3. **`secretary-backups` at 298 G** is the real number and is a retention policy decision, not housekeeping. Whoever holds it needs to say how many days or generations are worth keeping. **This one is the owner's**: it is deleting backups, and it is irreversible.
4. **A disk alarm at 90%** that pages someone, so the next 99% is noticed before a write fails.
