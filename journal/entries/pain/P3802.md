## P3802 — lptcase-derived money is a PROSE SCRAPE and it reported a customer as owing another shop's quote - 2100 instead of 740

The case index (lptcase) derives a case's money by scraping prose, and on Joe Fishman's case it scraped ANOTHER SHOP'S QUOTE and reported it as ours.

MEASURED 2026-10-14 on data-recovery-347-534-6208-2026-09-01:
 lptcase show -> 'money quote \ seen: \.0, \.0, \.0' with its own 'warn multiple-prices:3'
 index/cases.json -> quoted_price: 2100, money_owed: 2100
 AUTHORITATIVE: front matter 'price: 1060'; sync record stillDue 740, totalCollectibleFromCustomer 1060.

WHERE 2100 CAME FROM: case file lines 535/548/551/995 - the quote the CUSTOMER arrived with from an outside shop: 'He arrived with an outside shop's verdict: drive recoverable, laptop not recoverable, ~\,000-2,100 quote', plus a proposed \ diagnostic fee that was never adopted. The extractor took the customer's REJECTED external quote and made it the shop's price, and it IGNORED the front matter, which has said price: 1060 since 2026-10-07.

WHY IT MATTERS: 'lptcase money --uncollected' lists this customer 1,360 too high. A session reading that view and calling the customer would quote a number 1,360 wrong, on a case where the correct figure (740) is already recorded in two authoritative places. That is the shop's most expensive failure class (wrong-data) with a direct path to a customer.

THE GENERAL RULE: on any case where the customer arrived with somebody else's quote, the largest number in the prose is probably NOT ours. Prefer front matter and the sync record; treat the index as a hint, never as the money.

NOT FIXED, only recorded: the extractor still does this. A fix must make it prefer front-matter price over body scraping, and must not treat a quoted external figure as the shop's price. Case file and sync record both now carry the warning.
