## P3154 — The mandated transcript archive is OOM-killed hourly at :29 and has not completed in days

WHAT HURTS: the permanent archive of every DSH session and every Copilot chat is being OOM-killed
EVERY HOUR and has not completed a run in days. AGENTS.md section 1 makes a copy of every session and
chat mandatory and permanent ("those a copy always needs to exist of this should be documented"), and
that rule is currently not being honoured while every surface looks green.

MEASURED BY ME, secratary-cloud, 2026-10-13T01:4xZ:
 journalctl -k --since "24 hours ago" | grep -iE "Out of memory|oom-kill|Killed process"
 Oct 12 22:29:58 oom-kill:constraint=CONSTRAINT_MEMCG oom_memcg=/system.slice/transcript-archive-pull.service
 Oct 12 23:29:10 Killed process 213028 (python3) total-vm:9260160kB, anon-rss:6262188kB
 Oct 13 00:29:44 Killed process 223093 (python3) total-vm:9260160kB, anon-rss:6273032kB
 systemctl show transcript-archive-pull.service -> MemoryMax=6442450944 (6 GiB)
 So: the job is capped at 6 GiB, it needs ~6.27 GiB, and it dies at :29 every hour. It is a
 self-inflicted cap miss, not a hardware fault.
 SEPARATELY AND WORSE: company-api.service -> MemoryMax=infinity, MemoryCurrent 5.69 GiB,
 MemoryPeak 8.58 GiB, and 21 OOM-or-killed lines in `journalctl -u company-api --since "7 days ago"`.
 `MemoryMax=infinity` means the kernel picks the victim.

WHY IT IS INVISIBLE: the kill is scoped to the job's own cgroup, so the company stays up and nothing
alarms. The unit's own status is "ran". Only the kernel log records the death.

THE RIGHT FIX IS NOT A BIGGER CAP. Raising MemoryMax to, say, 10 GiB would collide with company-api's
5.69 GiB on a 15.6 GB box (measured available 12.3 GB) and simply move who gets killed. The puller is
unbounded in memory per run; the fix is to bound the WORK PER RUN (process a bounded number of files /
pack per machine rather than in one pass), so a run always finishes. Two candidate shapes, to be
chosen after reading scripts/transcript-archive-pull.py:
 (a) a per-run file/byte budget with a durable cursor so successive runs make progress; or
 (b) stream/ingest in chunks instead of materialising the whole pack.
Also worth doing in the same change: set a finite MemoryMax on company-api (it currently has none) so a
runaway cannot take the kernel's choice of victim.

EVIDENCE: the kernel log lines above; AGENTS.md section 1; docs/mesh/175 section 2.2 item 3 (the mac
mini has never contacted the archive). Found incidentally while verifying W1's report, which is the
only reason it is known.
