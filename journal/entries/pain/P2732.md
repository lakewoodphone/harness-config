## P2732 — Settings readback: 14 BREAKS on 0.1.7, two explained and corroborated, the remainder not yet separated from a loader-timing artefact

## The settings READBACK works and reports 14 BREAKS on 0.1.7: at least two are real and EXPLAINED, the rest are not yet separated from a timing artefact

MEASURED 2026-09-28 (ZABZ-TECH). `dsh-update/lib/settings-effective.mjs` boots the candidate web
profile against an isolated home and reads the engine's own effective settings through
`POST /api/settings/describe`. First successful reading:

```
 candidate: ran=true verdict=BREAKS
 keys=81 consumedEntries=81 distinctKeys=33 activeKeys=23 inactiveKeys=10
 present=2 absent=11 different=3 unverified=17 BREAKS=14 INFO=10
 namespacesRegistered=18 namespacesOurs=9
```

WHAT IT TOOK TO GET A READING AT ALL, and both were real defects rather than configuration:
1. The credential was resolved from `--dsh-home`, so a STAGED run looked in the staged home, where the
 engine writes its own credential file carrying no deployment key, and the gate refused with
 `unverified: credential: <staged>\.credentials.yaml holds no DEEPSEEK_API_KEY record` -- correct, and
 useless. Fixed by adding `--state-home` (explicit, then `$DSH_STATE_HOME`, then the operator's real
 home when `--dsh-home` is plainly staged), the same split `verify.mjs` already had.
2. It then refused because a leftover `dsh web` from an earlier test held port 3400. Correct refusal,
 no reading. The port is free again, and the module should try the NEXT free port rather than give up
 on the first one -- still open.

THE FINDINGS, split by whether they are explained:

EXPLAINED, and each is independently corroborated:
- `agent-presets` -- "the engine registered no settings namespace 'agent-presets' at all in this boot".
 On 0.1.7 that entry was RENAMED to `agent-preset-registry` (P2731), so a settings section named
 `agent-presets` has no entry to update. This is the settings-side proof of why change #4 of the
 migration -- patching the REGISTRY ROW's `config.default` to `zabz` -- is necessary and not optional:
 `settings.yaml`'s `agent-presets: default: zabz` cannot reach the engine at all.
- `spend-guard` -- same message. That is the activation race of P2730 / L2979 seen from a completely
 different angle. Two independent detectors, one plugin failure. A nice cross-check.

NOT YET EXPLAINED, and they matter: `llm-deepseek`, `agent-loop` and `ui-onboarding` also report no
namespace. Those are UPSTREAM entries that should exist, so either 0.1.7 really stops registering them
under these names, or the readback queried BEFORE the loader settled and saw a partial namespace list --
the same post-loader timing that P2731 shows the settings import is sensitive to. 7 of the 17
`unverified` entries carry the "no namespace" reason.

THE CONTROL DID NOT RUN, and its failure is itself informative: `--baseline-engine <0.1.5>` returned
`ran=false` because the 0.1.5 engine CANNOT MOUNT THE MIGRATED STAGED CONFIG -- it names packages that
do not exist on 0.1.5. That is the version coupling demonstrated from yet another direction. The
control that would separate the two explanations must be run against a 0.1.5-SHAPED home (the live one,
copied), not the staged one.

NEXT TEST, in order: (1) baseline readback against a 0.1.5-shaped home -- if 0.1.5 ALSO reports no
`llm-deepseek`/`agent-loop` namespace, the readback is querying too early and 12 of the 14 findings are
artefact; if it registers them, they are REAL and the upgrade silently drops our retry policy, concurrency
cap, agent-loop parallelism and ui-onboarding state; (2) make the readback wait for the loader to settle
and retry `describe` until the namespace set is stable, recording how long it waited; (3) only then
decide whether any of it blocks the upgrade.

DO NOT IMPLEMENT on top of this reading until (1) is answered. A `BREAKS` that is an artefact would block
a valid upgrade; a `BREAKS` that is real and waved away would silently revert money and retry policy.
