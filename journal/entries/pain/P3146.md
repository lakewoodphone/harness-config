## P3146 — Drop-offs with no work order: 848-525-8975 (Levy), 848-330-4317, 347-525-7284

Devices are dropped off at the shop and discussed on the phone with the owner as "we have
it" while no work order exists in either production store. Looked up 2026-10-12 because the
owner asked about "levy 848 525 8975".

Evidence (all read 2026-10-12, production `operational` via the authority and the retired
`app` store via lpt-apps):

- 848-525-8975: zero customers, zero orders, zero notes/tickets/quotes/intake rows in both
 stores; the ONLY trace is one outbound Dialpad call 2026-09-24 19:39 UTC (112 s), comms id
 137850 on `operational`, whose customerId AND orderId are both null. In that call the owner
 says the caller "dropped off your phone", diagnoses a blown motherboard fuse, quotes 65 to
 fix it (with relapse risk) or a new phone at 215 plus 45 for the contacts transfer.
- 848-330-4317 (the caller names himself Levi Levy, same day 2026-09-24, "I dropped off the
 phone an hour and a half ago", owner answers "I'm working on it right now"): zero customers,
 zero orders in either store.
- 347-525-7284 (Yochanan Levy, 2026-10-06, owner tells him the repair "is finished so we have
 it, it's good, come in"): zero customers, zero orders in either store.
- Production holds no customer spelled "Levy" at all - only Sarah Levi (732-557-1217,
 WO2609030001) and Yochana Levi (609-385-8579, WO2608190001).
- 3 orders were created on 2026-09-24 (Ribowsky, Gewirtz, Goldberg). The two Levy drop-offs
 that day are not among them.

Why it hurts: a device that exists only in a call recording is a device the shop cannot find,
cannot price, cannot call about, and cannot prove it returned. This is the same class of loss
that has already bitten twice. It is invisible to every health check because the absence is of
a row, not the failure of one.

What would fix it: a drop-off intake that is impossible to skip - the moment a device is
accepted, a work order plus a customer row exists, keyed on the phone number, with the tag
printed from that row. Cheapest version: one screen with device, phone, symptom, quoted price,
and no save without a phone or a name; plus a nightly reconciliation that lists calls
containing "dropped off" with no work order created within 24 h.

Reproduce: lpt ticket 8485258975 (no match); lpt sql --store operational "select ... from
communications where to_jsonb(communications)::text ilike '%5258975%'"; ps_comms_search exact
"5258975" -> 1 call. Trap hit while doing this: comparing regexp_replace digits to a 10-digit
literal without right(...,10) returns 0 rows for an 11-digit stored number - that is a query
bug that reads exactly like "no record".
