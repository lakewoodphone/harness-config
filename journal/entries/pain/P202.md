## P-new — The company's own systems have no idea whether the shop is open, and three of them assert that it is

Found 2026-09-15 auditing the walk-in-hours change (surface `W3-internal-systems.md`).

There is **no shop-hours predicate anywhere in the codebase**: zero matches for
`is_business_hours | shop_is_open | is_shop_open | closed_today | open_now | business_hours |
hours_of_operation | shop_hours` across `app/**/*.py`. So:

- `app/services/shop_ops.py:69-73` / rendered `:417-422` — the pickup-ready SMS says *"We're open today
 until {closing_time}."* and `closing_time="{closing_time}"` is passed **as a literal**, so the message
 ships with raw braces and **no hours value is ever computed**. It asserts the shop is open on any day it
 is sent, including a closed Wednesday, and nobody would notice because the placeholder never rendered.
- `app/services/shop_ops.py:934-940` — the uncollected-pickup reminder says *"Please stop by at your
 convenience!"* with no open/closed check. It invites a walk-in on a day nobody is staffed.
- `app/services/voice_realtime.py:243-262` — the voice agent's `web_search` tool description lists
 **"business hours"**, so a caller asking "are you open?" makes our own agent search the web and read
 back the stale Google Business Profile listing ("Opens 10:30 AM Wed").
- Every standing work order is Wednesday-blind (`seed_ops_inbox` 08:00, `seed_comms_front_door_status`
 08:30, `seed_devices_inventory` 09:30, `seed_gbp_weekly_reputation`, `seed_gv_daily_digest` 19:00,
 `seed_phone_daily_owner_run` /180 min).

**What would fix it:** one hours module — days → open/close or unstaffed — plus a gate on outbound
customer sends and one authoritative memory/doc that supersedes the stale ones. This is P173 seen from the
other end: P173 is the fact living in six hand-written strings; this is the fact being *acted on* by code
that never had it at all.

Cost when it is not fixed: a customer is texted that the shop is open on a day it is shut, and our own
voice agent reads them a schedule Google still has wrong.
