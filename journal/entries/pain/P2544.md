## P2544 — Eleven emails to a business partner whose body was the agent's own reasoning

## P — Eleven emails to a business partner whose body was the agent's own reasoning

**What happened.** Between 2026-09-14 and 2026-09-18, **eleven emails were delivered to
`helpdesk3@300dollardatarecovery.com`** whose entire body is the assistant's internal deliberation —
"thinking out loud" text that a human would never send. Nine of the eleven landed in five days,
across four live tickets (73459, 73890, 73896, 74048, 74114), and **five of them on ticket 73896
alone.** Found 2026-09-25 by reading all 36 outbound messages between the shop and the lab, all time.

Verbatim examples:
- 15 Sep, ticket 74048: *"I need more context about what the update message actually says before
 drafting a reply. Let me check the email content."*
- 18 Sep, ticket 73890: *"I don't have the actual content of the update message from Brian, so I
 can't draft an informed reply. **Could you share the body of the email so I can respond
 appropriately?**"* — asking the lab to send us the email they had just sent us.
- 14 Sep, ticket 73459: *"hi Brian, Thanks for the update on ticket #73459. **Please proceed** and
 let me know if you need anything else. sincerely"*

**Why it hurts.** (1) The shop looks like it is operated by a malfunction, to the one vendor it
depends on for chip-off work. (2) **"Please proceed" is a live authorisation risk** — on a ticket
whose own lab terms make approval irreversible once the file listing is accepted, sent 15 minutes
after the lab reported the recovery was bad, at a time when the settled policy was to show the
customer the listing first. A partner that acts on that sentence creates an obligation we did not
mean to create. (3) The only cleanup ever sent (2026-09-24, *"one of my employees sent that email by
mistake. Please ignore it"*) covers **one** of the eleven, names an employee for it, and asks
nothing. (4) An auto-reply that answers legal, money and irreversibility questions **while unable to
read the message it is answering** is worse than no auto-reply.

**Blast radius, measured.** Searching both Gmail accounts with no label filter for the giveaway
phrases returns 9 distinct messages; **8 are to 300DDR**, 1 is a loose match in a Twilio support
thread (unverified). **No customer has received one.**

**Root cause, first hypothesis (not confirmed).** The lab's notifications have a fixed shape — a
wrapper (*"You have an update for ticket #NNNNN … read the message below"*) and the substance further
down, sometimes after a long unsubscribe footer. A path that maps the mail to an auto-reply is
reading the wrapper as the entire body, so the model honestly reports it has no content — and that
sentence is what gets sent. It fits every observation: only this sender, only notification-shaped
mail, and a repeated request for what we already have.

**What would fix it.** (a) Hard-stop: an outbound body must never be model output that discusses the
task. Gate every send on the body being a written *message* — if it contains first-person deliberation
markers ("I need more context", "Let me check", "I'll draft"), refuse and route to a human draft
instead. (b) Fix the parser so the lab's wrapper is stripped and the real body is what the agent sees;
until that is proven, do not auto-reply to that sender at all. (c) No auto-send on any thread whose
reply could authorise money or an irreversible step — those always become drafts. (d) Alert on any
outbound body under ~40 words that matches a deliberation pattern.

**Verify the fix with the same query that found it:** both accounts, no label filter, phrases
`"I need more context about"`, `"draft an appropriate reply"`, `"Let me first check the context"` —
hits must go to 0 and stay 0.
