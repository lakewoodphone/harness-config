## P6311 — wrong-data: the 2026-10-03 link backfill gave at least 2 sync records another customer's production order; 7 more candidates unfixed and 10 unverifiable

The 2026-10-03 sync-record link backfill matched on surname and I cannot yet say how many records
it got wrong.

MEASURED 2026-10-13, lpt-hub `origin/main`, 414 sync records.

- 294 records claim a production order.
- 19 of those disagree with the record's own customer phone.
- 9 are true record-versus-order last-10-digit mismatches.
- **2 are provably another customer's order** and are FIXED (`3de0e202d`): the Sendy Goldstein
 MacBook A2338 record and the sd-card Goldstein record, both given Perel Goldstein's Sunbeam
 order `WO2604210130` / id 1430.
- **7 are candidates and NOT fixed**: abramczyk-sd-card (7326786417 vs 8482857640),
 flash-drives-newman (3477680413 vs 9087874138), lenovo-yoga-c930-rosenberg (2484162533 vs
 8485258790), sony-video8-levi-7325754823 (7325754823 vs 2163171864),
 unknown-yael-herskowitz-2026-07-16 (7325691594 vs 8483897895), weissman-ssd
 (8482993187 vs 8482020007), plus one 9-digit-phone false positive (hp-15-fd0095wm-shimmy-weichbrud,
 482612269, same digits both sides).
- 10 more carry no phone at all and this test cannot see them. Four of those point at the SAME order
 `WO26082712642` (Chaim Margulies) from four different cases, which is either one customer with
 four devices or the same surname bug - unresolved.

WHY IT IS A PAIN AND NOT JUST A BUG. A record that claims the wrong order reads as *linked*, so the
reverse-gap count under-reports and the case disappears from exactly the list built to find it. That
is not a cosmetic error - it is the mechanism that hid `weissman-ssd` for three weeks, and it will
hide the next one. And the obvious guard did not exist: nothing in the sync tooling compared a
record's own customer phone against the phone on the order it claimed.

WHAT WOULD CLOSE IT: for each of the 7 (and the 10 with no phone), read the order's customer NAME
and phone from production and compare it to the record's customer NAME as well as its number - a
spouse or an old number explains a phone mismatch, a different name does not. Then, whether or not
the links turn out wrong, add the phone-vs-name comparison to the sync verifier so the next
backfill cannot do this silently. The method is already written down in L3740 and the scripts are
in `C:\Users\ezabz\Code\_scratch\check-sync-links.py` and `sync-link-check.sql`.
