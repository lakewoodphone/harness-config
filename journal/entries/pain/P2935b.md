## P2935b — owner-ask has no fallback: one un-sendable row deadlocked the owner channel for 8 days

WHAT
bin/owner-ask.py picks the single best candidate, composes a <=160-char body, and refuses to
send if ask_quality.review() rejects the body. It writes no `ask` row and does NOT try the
next candidate. So ONE un-sendable candidate at the head of the queue halts the ENTIRE owner
channel, silently - the only trace is a line in ~/.owner-ask/cron.log that nothing reads.

MEASURED 2026-10-10T12:45Z
~/.owner-ask/cron.log holds 136 consecutive "chosen: decision:248" and 136 "GATE REFUSED this
body - nothing was sent, nothing recorded". #248's composed body is a machine instruction
("Sign in to Amazon once in the browser on the ZABZ-YOGA laptop...") with no question in it, so
ask_quality rule 3 refuses it - correctly, and the wake contract forbids sending him a machine
instruction anyway. The last successful send in ~/.owner-ask/asks.db is id 36 at
2026-10-02T17:00Z. Nothing from this channel has reached the owner in 8 days.

SCALE - NOT A ONE-ROW ACCIDENT
Composed and gated every one of the 147 eligible decision candidates: 37 pass, 110 fail. The
failures are ordinary decisions whose recommendation sentence is an instruction rather than a
question ("Option 1, because...", "I fix it", "Treat it as..."). The head-of-line block is the
normal shape of the queue's wording, not an outlier.

CONSEQUENCE FOR THE HOURS DRIFT
owner_decision_queue #310 (Google Maps advertises Monday 10:30-5:30 when Monday is a
no-walk-in day) was filed 2026-10-07 and could not reach him for two compounding reasons:
(a) the asker's ALREADY_READABLE regex matched the words "Google Business Profile" in its
question (separate entry, P2934), and (b) even once that was cleared, its composed body failed
ask_quality because the recommendation was an instruction. Both are now fixed ON THE ROW -
#310 is clean at both gates, proven 2026-10-10T12:50Z. But it is medium and would still sit
behind ~25 high rows, and the channel cannot advance past #248 at all.

THE FIX OWED (deliberately NOT done in this session)
owner-ask.py should fall through to the next candidate when ask_quality refuses the composed
body, and record the refusal so the same row is not recomposed every 30 minutes forever. This
is a change to the LIVE send path (owner-ask runs every 30 min with --send), so it needs a test
proving: (a) a refused head does not stop the next candidate; (b) no duplicate or out-of-order
ask; (c) the refusal is recorded rather than silently dropped. A source that alarms when the
asker refuses N consecutive picks would have caught this on 2026-10-03 instead of today.

WHAT WAS DONE HERE
owner-ask.py was NOT edited. The hours row was made clean at both gates; the diagnosis and the
measurement are recorded; the channel is left as it is - blocked, not silenced, cron intact.

REFS
bin/owner-ask.py (pick/compose/send); bin/ask_quality.py (review); ~/.owner-ask/cron.log;
journal P2934; owner_decision_queue #310.
