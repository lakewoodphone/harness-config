## P155 — The Waze fleet's sourcing evidence lived on one laptop, and the handoffs pointed at paths that did not exist elsewhere

Found 2026-09-15 while being asked to "pull up the Waze iPhone sourcing docs": the four research reports for the most
active product decision in the business were unreachable from ZABZ-TECH for a full day. The handoff entries pointed at
paths that did not exist here, so the machine with the most repos could not read the work.

Why it hurts: this is the second time a deliverable has been believed-but-missing (the flat-file journal had the same
shape). It costs a retrieval session and, worse, invites a future session to re-research settled ground.

What would fix it:
1. A rule in the writes, already journaled as a lesson: anything produced for the business goes into lpt-hub (or the
 appropriate repo) and is pushed in the same session. Machine-local scratch is not a deliverable.
2. A cheap verifier on the report/handoff path: when a handoff names a repo-relative path, check it exists at write time.
 `journal.py append` could do this for paths that look repo-relative and warn - it already validates its own ids and shas.
3. Reconcile ZABZ-TECH's lpt-hub main (2,446 behind origin, 31 unpublished commits) so pushing from this machine stops
 failing and the next deliverable written here reaches the authority by the normal route instead of a detached worktree.
