## P215 — Binding a personal policy has no compliance checklist, so a mandatory NJ inspection was never scheduled

RANKED: high. Cost: the owner's 2022 Hyundai Elantra lost collision and comprehensive coverage mid-term and it went unnoticed for two months.

What happened: on 2026-05-09 the agent bound Plymouth Rock/Rider policy HPA70002908356 for a used 2022 Elantra. New Jersey requires a pre-insurance physical-damage inspection within 7 days (N.J.A.C. 11:3-36.5(b)1); the 2022 model year is inside the 7-model-year waiver so no waiver applied, and the owner had just left Allstate so the 4-year tenure waiver did not apply either. Nobody told him, no date was set, and the binding checklist ('Binding Script / Checklist', car-insurance-decision-2026-05-07.md) has 14 items and none of them is the inspection.

Measured extent of the gap: 'inspection' appears ZERO times in the 53 KB Plymouth Rock deep-dive (039), ZERO times in the 62 KB policy decision doc (036), ZERO times in the 50 KB coverage-optimisation doc (040), and ZERO times in either car doc. Five documents, about 170 KB of research, and the single mandatory post-binding obligation was not in any of them.

WHAT WOULD FIX IT: a per-state, per-line 'after you bind' checklist that the binding step cannot close without producing evidence — for NJ auto that is: (1) inspection scheduled or a written waiver recorded, (2) declarations page downloaded and filed, (3) ID card downloaded and filed, (4) pay plan and its next due date recorded, (5) renewal date recorded with a reminder at T-45 days. The 2026-05-09 policy record even had the ID card and the declarations page as unticked checkboxes; that is the same failure one level down.

Related: P94-class 'open loop with no owner and no date'. This is the concrete instance.
