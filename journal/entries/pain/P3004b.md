## P3004b — The LLC has a dissolution risk sitting in a JSON file since August, and 148 queue rows never mentioned it

2026-10-13. Chasing what the ST-50 taxable-sales figure actually is, and finding three live
tax/legal items the owner had never been shown.

THE FILE THAT HAD THE ANSWERS. `scripts/tax/nj-portal-state.json` - the NJ Premier portal state
captured 2026-08-12 - holds the real filing record, and it disagrees with the tool that reads it:
 quarter portal says FILED tax-payments.json says paid KNOWN_TAX says
 2025-Q3 268.23 (FR1100883158) 202.00 202
 2025-Q4 295.23 (FR1100883245) 227.00 227
 2026-Q1 384.20 (FR1100883297) 327.00 327
 2026-Q2 1118.04 (FR1100883353) 1118.00 1118
The filed figure carries interest and penalty for the late periods - the portal notes "Interest was
18.49 not 15.70 as originally recorded; 2.76 remains to zero the period. Status shows In Balance."
So TAX is 202/227/327/1118 and the FILED amounts are those plus late charges. Both records are
right about different things, and nothing on the box reconciles them.

AND Q3 2026 IS CONFIRMED UNFILED, from the portal rather than from inference:
`sales_tax_account.last_period_filed` = **2026-06-30**, current_balance 2.76.

THE BASIS IS STILL NOT ESTABLISHED, and here is what I ruled out. The Q2 2026 tax paid was 1118.00,
which at 6.625 percent implies taxable sales of about 16,880 - and the raw ledger query for that
quarter returns **34,567**, almost exactly double. So the transactions table double-counts (W2743)
AND the figure 16,880 is not gross revenue. NJ taxes retail sales of tangible property, and a repair
shop's labour may well sit outside "taxable sales", which would explain a large gap; whether that is
how LPT files is not recorded anywhere I can find. The v9 FINAL P&L that `KNOWN_TAX` cites does not
exist on the box (estate index: NO MATCH), and the tax master document does not state the derivation.
I have not guessed a number for a state filing.

THREE ITEMS, now in the OWNER QUEUE as #464, #465 and #466, all high, all passing the P265 guard -
and the first is the one that actually matters:
 #464 **NJ shows the LLC with "Registration Status: Missing Annual Reports", and two consecutive
 missed years can dissolve it administratively.** 75 per report. Filed via Registry Services,
 which needs his login. This is the cheapest possible fix with the largest possible downside,
 and it had been sitting in a JSON file since 2026-08-12 with nobody told.
 #465 ST-50 Q3 2026 due 2026-10-20, portal's last filed period 2026-06-30.
 #466 NJ-927 returns show NOT FILED for 06/2026 and 09/2026 although 106.97 and 108.34 were paid -

Checked before queueing: `owner-queue.py list` holds 148 rows and NOT ONE mentioned the annual
reports, the sales tax deadline or the payroll returns.

THE POINT WORTH KEEPING. All of this was already written down - in `nj-portal-state.json`, in the
tax master document with a warning in capitals - and none of it was reachable by anything that runs
on a clock. A fact in a JSON file is not a fact anybody has. The tax clock built earlier this round
reads those files; that is the difference between knowing and having.
