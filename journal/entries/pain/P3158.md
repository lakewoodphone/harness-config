## P3158 — The persona harness hand-copies DOM assumptions in three places and each goes stale silently - the same false blocker fired twice, five months apart

MEASURED 2026-10-13 in `e2e/personas` on ZABZ-TECH.

**The same false blocker has now been reported twice, five months apart, and both times it was the
harness rather than the product.**

- **2026-09-25:** the harness looked only inside `table tbody tr` while 14 valid work-order links sat
 on the same page. It was fixed to click `a[href^="/work-orders/"]`, and the comment above that line
 records the incident in full.
- **2026-10-13:** the finding is back, because the Counter's open queue was rebuilt as
 `ul[data-testid="staff-open-rows"]` of `button[data-testid="staff-open-case-…"]`. **There is no
 anchor any more**, so the September fix matches nothing and the walk reports a blocker again.

**Three places in the harness encode the hub's shape by hand**, each with no test of its own:

- `openFirstWorkOrder`'s candidate list — `journeys.ts:135`
- `rowPricing` — `journeys.ts:462`
- the evidence block — `journeys.ts:483-490`

So a UI change makes a **working** screen look broken, and the false finding lands in the day's report
as a blocker.

**WHAT IT COST:** the owner was told, as a serious finding, that the shop cannot see its work orders.
It renders twenty-five of them, as 80px `touch-manipulation` buttons — good phone UI. He was told the
opposite of the truth, and it went to the top of the roadmap before it was caught.

**WHY IT WASN'T CAUGHT BY A SECOND OPINION:** the "independent" probe written to check it made the
same two assumptions (the list is a table; the rows are work orders) and so agreed for the wrong
reason. Recorded separately as L3763.

**FIX DIRECTION:** the harness must assert its own assumptions before it reports a blocker — fail
loudly when its locators match nothing **while the screen declares rows**. The Counter already carries
`data-row-count="25"`, which is exactly the signal that distinguishes *"nothing there"* from *"did not
match"*. Read that declared count in **one** place and have all three call sites use it, so a stale
assumption fails in one named place instead of silently inventing findings.
