## P9995 — Offered to delete the last copy of an open customer's WhatsApp data

## P— I offered to delete the last surviving copy of an open customer's WhatsApp data

2026-10-27, ZABZ-TECH storage audit. Found 24.1 GiB in
`personal-secretary-mvp\_scratch\whatsapp-backup\`: `msgstore.db.crypt14` x4 plus a 6.66 GiB tar.gz.
Dated 2026-07-22, which is **97 days** — past the shop's 30-day window — and outside the one approved
home. I wrote it up as "expired, duplicate (tar.gz + extracted), in the wrong place", put a
recommendation on it, and asked for a yes.

**Every one of those adjectives was wrong.** The case record
(`lpt-hub/docs/customer-operations/sync-records/s24-ultra-whatsapp-corruption-zevi-braun-8482105287-2026-07-22.json`,
updated 2026-10-03) says ticketState **open**, workOrderState **checked-in**, WO26082712682,
"Verify status and reconcile". It is the salvaged data from the 2026-07-22 pmclear incident, for a
customer whose phone is still in the shop. The 30-day clock measures *completed* jobs; this job is
not completed.

The owner's answer when offered: *"nope, i delete them, you don't ever"* — now the first rule of
`docs/shop/customer-data-retention.md`.

**The generalisable failure**: age and location are not expiry. Before any customer data is even
*named* as a deletion candidate, read the production order it belongs to — open vs completed is the
only fact that turns a copy into debris, and it is one join away. A folder that is old, misplaced
and duplicated can still be the only copy that exists.
