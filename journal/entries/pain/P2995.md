## P2995 — 326 answered customer conversations since 2026-07-01 have no customer row, and 2 are waiting on replies nobody knows about

WHAT HURTS
The shop's conversations live in Dialpad; the shop's record lives in production. Nothing joins them.
Measured 2026-10-08 on the operational store (the shop's real one), against communications.
createdAt > 2026-07-01:
 - 914 distinct inbound numbers
 - 466 of them have NO row in customers (last-ten-digit match)
 - 326 of them had a real two-way exchange - we answered them - and still have no row
One of those 326 is Douglas 'D' Heber (443) 610-7535: three contacts (2026-05-05, 2026-05-27,
2026-07-29), two quotes of 50 he accepted, one day he was physically in Lakewood, and no customer,
no work order, no note in either store. His 2026-10-07 inbound voicemail was never answered; the
last thing ever sent to him was the address on 2026-05-27.

THE SECOND HALF OF THE PAIN: A TICKET LOOKUP CANNOT TELL 'NO ORDER' FROM 'NOT A CUSTOMER'
'lpt ticket 4436107535' on 2026-10-08 returned 'No work order matches in either store'. The query
reaches a customer only through an order (order_by_identifier_sql), so a person with conversations
and no order is invisible - and the message reads like the person does not exist. I nearly reported
'no such customer' from it. What settled it was ps_comms_search, not the case tool.

WHAT WOULD FIX IT
1. A nightly reconciliation: every distinct inbound number that received at least one outbound reply
 and has no customers row -> one row in a work queue for the counter, with the last inbound text
 pasted in. The 326 is the backlog; a nightly diff keeps it from growing silently.
2. A first-class 'unanswered inbound' view: last message per party is INBOUND and older than N hours.
 Heber's 2026-10-07 voicemail is a day old with no reply; the same query would have surfaced it on
 2026-10-08 without anyone asking.
3. 'lpt ticket <phone>' should say which it is: no order for this number, but N communications and a
 customer row found / not found. Never a bare 'no work order matches'.

STATUS
Open. Both measures are read-only and reproducible with the SQL in the case file
