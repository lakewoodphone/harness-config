## P3155 — 10 case files declare no work order in front matter while naming one in the body - the prose harvest makes the standard un-enforceable

MEASURED 2026-10-12 on 284 lpt-hub case files. `CASE-FILE-STANDARD.md` §2 says front-matter is authoritative and `work_order: ""` means "there is none". `lptcase.py` did not honour that: it appends every `WO\d{8,}` found in the PROSE to the case's work orders. So "the case declares no work order" and "the case declares no work order but its narrative mentions one" are indistinguishable.

 work_order set in front matter : 135
 work_order explicitly EMPTY ("") : 148 <- 10 of these name a WO in the body
 work_order key ABSENT : 1

Those 10 are the whole blast radius of making an explicit empty front matter authoritative, so the change itself is small - but the 10 are not one thing, and that is why it is NOT done yet. Adjudicated against the operational store:
 * 2 are the known-wrong-link class (this case's own body naming the order it must NOT be linked to): macbook-air-a1932-no-display-yanofsky-..., surface-laptop-4-1950-greenfeld-... . Correct as they are; removing the prose claim is the fix.
 * 3 have an unambiguous same-customer-same-phone order that simply never made it into the front matter, and filling it in ALSO closes a measured false gap in sync-health-audit.py (which reported WO26100812727 "Niasoff" as a recent active prod order with NO lpt-hub case, while that case file exists and names the order in its body):
 hard-drive-enclosure-niasoff-7324924311-2026-10-07.md -> WO26100812727 (cid 2799, AWAITING_DIAGNOSIS)
 kyocera-flip-phone-verizon-branded-pohrille-8482857198-2026-10-07.md -> WO2606180003 (cid 1621)
 oneplus-nord-n30-mizrachi-3474540014-2026-10-07.md -> WO2608200003 (cid 925, "one plus 2nd time")
 * 3 name several WO numbers that all belong to the SAME CUSTOMER but to other jobs (14in-laptop-weitman lists 4 whiteman orders, none of them a laptop-sourcing job; dell-inspiron-15-3000-hinge-kokis lists 3 orders for three other people). For these the empty front matter is arguably CORRECT and the prose is context.
 * 1 is a phone match with a DIFFERENT customer name - text-john-frankl-9178482026-2026-09-03.md names WO2602160001, which production attributes to "Spitzer" at the same phone (+19178482026). Same-phone-different-name is exactly the Yanofsky pattern; do NOT set it without checking with the owner.

NEXT (clear, small): make an explicit `work_order: ""` suppress the prose harvest (keeping body hits as `work_orders_mentioned`), and while doing it fill the 3 unambiguous front matters, leave the 3 multi-customer ones empty, and leave text-john-frankl alone. Do NOT simply make front matter authoritative without adjudicating those 10, or 8 cases silently lose a `wo` line that `open`/`show` readers rely on.
