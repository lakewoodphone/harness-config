## Why these two

**The problem.** The August BAMBOO COOL V-necks and the October 2025 David Archy bamboo
rayon undershirts brown under the armpits. Daytime sweat volume is **not** the cause — the shirts
end the day dry, and the brown appears only after several washes.

**The cause.** The aluminum salts in the antiperspirants in use — Right Guard Xtreme Defense gel
and solid, and Dove Men+Care dry spray, all ordered 2026-06-23 — accumulate in the fabric and
oxidise over successive wash cycles. Bamboo rayon holds that residue and does not release it;
cotton and modal do. That is why the bamboo garments are the ones that discoloured.

**The consequence for sizing and choice.**
- No sweat-blocking garment is needed. A person whose shirts end the day dry does not have a
  sweat-breakthrough problem.
- Undershirt: **cotton**, slim fit, because a 42" jacket and 38" waist is what slim fit is cut for
  and it is what prevents the bunching the David Archy tops had. Sizing by measured waist, not by
  the retailer's profile nudge, which read "Small is recommended" on a 38" waist.
- Underwear: **modal trunk**, because it gives the softness of the bamboo rayon already owned with
  better wash behaviour, and because a 3.5" trunk with a 1.38" stay-put waistband is the specific
  fix for the ride-up.

## Care, which matters as much as what was bought

- **Wash warm with oxygen bleach (OxiClean). Never fabric softener** — it binds the aluminum
  residue into the fiber and is what makes the browning permanent.
- **Never chlorine bleach on the armpits** — it sets protein stains instead of lifting them.
- **Do not tumble dry the modal trunks or the bamboo garments.** Heat destroys the spandex
  recovery and the modal softness, which is the whole reason to buy them over cotton. Air dry.
  Same for the existing BAMBOO COOL pairs — this is the likely reason they have degraded.
